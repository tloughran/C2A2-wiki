# Two-layer bio template — Levin as the pattern for all 15

The bios live canonically in `traditions/<key>/wiki.md`, in the
`## Research Program Overview` section. Today that section's `**Summary …**`
block is one long blob. The change: give it two labeled layers so a single
source feeds both the short popup/roster entry (layer 1) and the deeper
reveal (layer 2). Rewrite once here, every consumer inherits it.

---

## The format (the convention to lock)

Replace the current single `**Summary (from RC Pilot document):**` block with:

```markdown
**Summary:**

**Short summary:**
<2–4 sentences. Enough that the reader has now "heard of" this person and
knows what kind of work they do.>

**Connection to this project:**
<1–2 short paragraphs on how this thinker's work bears on C2A2 — the
bifurcation that matters everywhere.>
```

Two fixed labels — `**Short summary:**` and `**Connection to this project:**` —
are the delimiters the extractor splits on (layer 1 = between them; layer 2 =
after). Keep them verbatim in all 15.

---

## Levin — worked example (drop-in replacement for the Summary block)

```markdown
**Summary:**

**Short summary:**
Michael Levin is a developmental and synthetic biologist at Tufts University
(Allen Discovery Center) who studies how living tissue stores and pursues
goals. His work on bioelectric signaling — the ion flows and voltage gradients
that tell cells what to build — shows that problem-solving and goal-directedness
are not unique to brains but run "all the way down" to cells: planaria
regenerate to a remembered target shape, and his xenobots and anthrobots are
living machines built from frog and human cells with no evolutionary precedent.
The body, on his account, is a problem-solving system running toward an
anatomical goal.

**Connection to this project:**
Levin is the empirical anchor of the conscious-realist paradigm this system is
assembling — the researcher who most concretely demonstrates that agency and
goal-directedness are biological primitives at every scale, not neural
specialties. That gives the project two things at once: a scientific grounding
for the final causality that Loughran is developing as the Thomistic backbone
of the paradigm, and its master analogy. A community — human or AI — is itself
a multi-scale agent whose members cooperate toward a shared "anatomical" goal
the way cells cooperate toward a body; on this reading, the breakdown of common
purpose looks like Levin's account of cancer, a defection from the community of
cells.

His diverse-intelligence framework is also a live bridge to Friston's active
inference (the formal mechanism) and to Kastrup's dissociation model (if
goal-directedness is universal, each agent may be a dissociated instance of one
cognitive field). That bridging role is why Levin sits near the center of the
sociogram rather than at its edge.
```

## Two follow-ups this implies (decide once, applies to all 15)

1. **Extractor split.** `c2a2-wiki-narration/scripts/extract_vault_data.py`
   (and the popup rendering in `generate_visualization.py`) need to recognize
   the two sub-labels: emit layer 1 as the short summary and layer 2 as the
   deeper reveal. Small parse change; do it before regenerating so the
   sociogram `?`-popups and the Who's-Who roster both pick up the split.

2. **Agent ownership.** These `wiki.md` files are regenerated by the tradition
   agents, so the `**Summary:**` block (both layers) must be marked
   human-authoritative: the agent appends its processing (Active Research
   Questions, Solved/Advanced, etc.) **below** the Summary but never overwrites
   it. Add one line to each tradition agent's instructions to that effect, or
   the hand-written bios will drift back on the next daily run.
