# Sean Carroll — Physicalism Member

*Part of the [[rival/physicalism/wiki|Physicalism tradition]]. Written from inside the tradition, as a mature member would present him. Carroll also has a full C2A2 tradition wiki at [[traditions/carroll/wiki|Carroll Tradition Wiki]]; this page presents him as the anchor of a rival tradition rather than as one program among fifteen.*

## 1. Who he is and why he anchors the tradition

Sean Carroll is a theoretical physicist and philosopher of science, Homewood Professor of Natural Philosophy at Johns Hopkins University (a joint appointment in physics and philosophy), Fractal Faculty at the Santa Fe Institute, and the host of the Mindscape podcast. He anchors this tradition for three reasons. First, he states the physicalist claim at the level where it is strongest: not a vague claim that matter is all there is, but the specific claim (paraphrased) that the laws governing everyday phenomena are known, have been tested, and leave no room for new forces acting at everyday scales. Second, he pairs that claim with a generous account of higher levels (poetic naturalism) that lets the tradition say minds, choices and values are real without reading them into fundamental physics. Third, he is the tradition's most active public interlocutor with its rivals: Mindscape has hosted many of the people this vault tracks, so his positions are tested in open conversation rather than defended in isolation.

## 2. Core positions

1. **Core Theory completeness at everyday scales.** The Standard Model plus general relativity, treated as an effective field theory, specifies its own domain of validity; within that domain (chemistry, biology, brains) the underlying laws are effectively known. Any new mind-related force strong enough to matter would already have shown up. Carroll, *The Big Picture* (2016); "The Quantum Field Theory on Which the Everyday World Supervenes" (arXiv, 2021).
2. **Poetic naturalism.** There is one world, but many useful vocabularies for describing it. Talk of people, reasons and purposes is legitimate when it earns its keep at its own level, even though it adds nothing at the level of fundamental physics. *The Big Picture* (2016).
3. **Everettian quantum mechanics.** The wave function evolving by the Schrödinger equation is the whole story; measurement needs no collapse and no special role for observers or consciousness. *Something Deeply Hidden* (2019); C. Sebens and S. Carroll, "Self-Locating Uncertainty and the Origin of Probability in Everettian Quantum Mechanics," *British Journal for the Philosophy of Science* (online 2016; print 2018); S. Carroll and A. Singh, "Mad-Dog Everettianism: Quantum Mechanics at Its Most Minimal" (arXiv 2018; collected in *What is Fundamental?*, Springer, 2019).
4. **Against mind-first modifications of physics.** If fundamental reality had intrinsically mental aspects, either they alter the known laws (which our physics gives strong reason to doubt) or they leave the laws untouched (in which case it is hard to see what explanatory work they do). Carroll, "Consciousness and the Laws of Physics," written for the *Journal of Consciousness Studies* special issue responding to Philip Goff's *Galileo's Error* (2021).
5. **Compatibilism as the poetic-naturalist account of choice.** Free will is a real feature of the agent-level description, not a gap in microphysics. *The Big Picture* (2016); developed in conversation with [[members/list]] on Mindscape episode 354 (May 2026).

## 3. Where he disagrees with fellow members

- **With [[members/paul-churchland]] and [[members/patricia-churchland]]:** Carroll is not an eliminativist. Beliefs, desires and choices are not a false theory awaiting replacement; they are a legitimate emergent vocabulary. Poetic naturalism keeps folk psychology where the Churchlands expected to retire it.
- **With [[members/sapolsky]]:** Carroll's compatibilism treats choice as real at the level of people; Sapolsky argues that the full causal history leaves no room for free will or desert. Within the tradition this is the main live dispute about agency.
- **With [[members/tegmark]]:** Both are Everettians, but Carroll does not adopt the mathematical universe hypothesis. For Carroll the fundamental thing is a physical quantum state obeying a physical law, not a mathematical structure as such.
- **With [[members/weinberg]]:** Weinberg ended his career unsatisfied with every interpretation of quantum mechanics, Everett included; Carroll regards Everett as the clear front-runner.
- **With [[members/list]]:** Carroll adopts List's account of levels and free will, but List has moved to a non-physicalist view of consciousness (see List's page). Carroll has not followed him there.
- **With [[members/frankish]]:** Carroll does not commit to illusionism. His stated position is that consciousness is real and emergent; whether phenomenal properties are an illusion is a question he leaves to the philosophers of mind in the tradition. [UNVERIFIED: no single Carroll text located that explicitly accepts or rejects illusionism by name.]

## 4. What he offers against the conscious-realist rivals

- **Against [[traditions/kastrup/wiki|Kastrup]]:** Kastrup leans on quantum measurement as evidence that observation is constitutive. Everett removes that support: no collapse, no observer-dependence at the fundamental level. If the formalism needs no observer, measurement cannot be the doorway through which mind enters physics.
- **Against [[traditions/hoffman/wiki|Hoffman]]:** Hoffman argues that spacetime and particles are interface icons. Carroll's effective-field-theory argument asks what new physics conscious agents would add at everyday energies, and points out that any mind-first derivation of spacetime must still reproduce the Core Theory's verified predictions. The tradition treats this as a confirmation standard every rival has to meet. The C2A2 Carroll wiki records that Carroll has been asked about Hoffman's "Trace" mathematics on air but that his answer is not on record.
- **Against [[traditions/levin/wiki|Levin]]:** Carroll grants that higher-level causal descriptions can be the right ones to use; that is poetic naturalism. What he denies is that any such description violates or supplements the microphysics. Levin's goal-directed morphogenesis is welcome as a level of description, but not as evidence against physical completeness. The C2A2 Carroll wiki records this as an open test: whether manipulable causal emergence (Pigozzi and Levin) is more than bookkeeping.
- **Against panpsychism generally:** "Consciousness and the Laws of Physics" (2021) is the tradition's standard reply to Goff-style Russellian monism.

## 5. The weak point a fair rival would press

Poetic naturalism says higher-level talk is legitimate when it is "useful." A rival can ask: useful to whom? The criterion appears to presuppose agents with purposes and standards of explanation, the same features the tradition claims to explain as emergent. Carroll's account of *why* the phenomenal character of experience accompanies certain physical processes is also thin. He relocates the hard problem to the emergent level rather than solving it. A MacIntyrean will add that "many vocabularies, one world" leaves open which vocabulary has authority when they conflict, such as the moral and the microphysical.
