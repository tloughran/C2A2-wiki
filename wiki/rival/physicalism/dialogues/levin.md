# Physicalism × Basal Cognition (Levin): Control Knobs, Free Lunches, and Where Patterns Come From

*Companion to [[rival/physicalism/wiki|Physicalism Tradition Wiki]] §6.3 · [[rival/physicalism/responses|Responses]] Objections 4–6 · [[synthesis/carroll_levin_bridge|Carroll × Levin bridge]] · [[traditions/levin/wiki|Levin Tradition Wiki]].*

"Carroll AQ n" = Active Research Question n in `traditions/carroll/wiki.md`. "Levin AQ n" = Active Research Question n in `traditions/levin/wiki.md`. ## 1. The crux

Both traditions accept that goal-level variables in living tissue (a stored bioelectric target anatomy, a measured level of causal emergence) are real causes, because they can be measured, rewritten and used to steer outcomes; they part over whether the reliable patterns those variables find, the "free lunches" of competence that no design, selection or training paid for, are explained by physical dynamics together with non-causal mathematical necessity, or require a structured non-physical space of patterns that physical systems interface with.

## 2. Ground rules

The speakers are the two traditions, not the people. Positions are attributed to published works and to the vault's own files, never to private words. Neither side may win by restating its premises.

## 3. Opening statements

### PHYSICALIST TRADITION

We do not come to this exchange to deny any experiment. Our wiki calls the Levin program the rival closest to an ally (wiki §6.3). One of our own, Dennett, co-wrote "Cognition All the Way Down" with Levin (*Aeon*, 2020). On our reading, that essay applies the intentional stance to cells and tissues: agent-talk is warranted wherever it gives reliable prediction and control, at any scale (PHYS-PRS-35; Dennett, *The Intentional Stance*, 1987).

Our position has three parts. First, there is one world. The physics underlying chemistry, biology and brains is known within its domain, because any new field strong enough to move atoms and neurons would already have shown up in precision particle physics (PHYS-PRS-01; Carroll, arXiv:2101.07884, 2021). Causal closure is not a creed. It is an empirical generalisation with a history (PHYS-PRS-02; Papineau, "The Rise of Physicalism," 2001), and our wiki names what would defeat it (wiki Active Question 7).

Second, higher levels are real. Poetic naturalism holds that persons and purposes are as real as temperatures (PHYS-PRS-08; Carroll, *The Big Picture*, 2016). List separates supervenience from reducibility (PHYS-PRS-09; *Noûs*, 2019). With Menzies he argues that a higher-level property is often the better difference-maker than any of its physical realisers (PHYS-PRS-12; *Journal of Philosophy*, 2009). On that account a manipulable bioelectric setpoint is a model case of a real higher-level cause. "Bookkeeping" is the vault's word for our view (Carroll × Levin bridge, 2026-07-26), not ours (Obj 5).

Third, we refuse two steps. One runs from goal-directedness to mind as a fundamental feature of the world. The other runs from mathematical constraint to non-physical causation. On the second, the cicada example is older than this exchange. Baker made prime-numbered cicada cycles his central case of a genuinely mathematical explanation of a physical fact (*Mind* 114, 2005), and Lange classifies such explanations as non-causal (*Because Without Cause*, 2017). Neither is a member; both are resources (Obj 4). A further dilemma applies (PHYS-PRS-19; Carroll, *JCS* 28, 2021). If a non-physical pattern changes how matter moves, it must show up as a deviation from laws tested to great precision. If it does not, it pushes nothing.

We state our debts in advance. We have relied on a criterion for when a level is real and never stated it. On that point we have conceded the objection (Obj 6; wiki §5.6). Kim's exclusion argument is our own unclosed door (PHYS-PRS-10). And we cannot yet say why the space of forms Levin's lab reaches is structured the way it is (Obj 4, residue).

### LEVIN TRADITION

Our program is experimental first. Its central result is that bioelectric states in non-neural tissue encode anatomical target states. Rewriting those states changes what the body builds: two-headed planaria with no genetic change, eyes induced on the gut in *Xenopus* (Levin PRS-01, PRS-07, PRS-08). From that, and from xenobots and anthrobots, we draw a framework. Cognitive claims are testable hypotheses about the best protocol for interacting with a system, not metaphysical commitments. That framework is explicitly non-vitalist and applies at every scale (Levin PRS-09; Levin & Resnik, "Mind Everywhere," *Biological Theory*, 2026). Its earlier statement is TAME, the Technological Approach to Mind Everywhere (Levin, *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience*, 2022) [UNVERIFIED: this citation is not held in the vault's Levin files; given from the general record].

We hold four things. First, the continuum is the null hypothesis. Development and evolution show no discontinuity between chemistry and a person, so the sharp category is the claim that needs an argument (Levin PRS-74, PRS-10). Second, goal representation in tissue is an empirical question with a decision procedure. Look for a measurable state that precedes the outcome, can be decoded to match it, and can be rewritten so the system predictably builds something else. We claim published cases meet all three conditions (Levin PRS-117). Third, the causal story runs top-down as well as bottom-up. Membrane voltage acts as a control layer over transcription (Levin PRS-35; Cervera, Levin & Mafe 2026). The "Machines all the way up" paper puts the order as cognition, then code, then chemicals (Levin PRS-66), within a frame that rejects both mechanism and dualism (Levin PRS-65). Causal emergence rises before self-replicators exist. Raising it increases the persistence and abundance of replicators, and lowering it does the reverse (Levin PRS-102, PRS-146, PRS-147; Pigozzi & Levin, arXiv:2607.28250).

Fourth, competence arrives that nobody paid for. Effort enters a system through design, selection or training. Yet a six-line deterministic sorting algorithm shows behavioural signatures that are not in its code (Levin PRS-75). Small random networks already show an agency ratchet that biology improves only slightly (Levin PRS-98). We call the residual a "free lunch" and turn it into a measurement program (Levin PRS-97). Our hypothesis about where the residual comes from is Platonic space: a structured space of patterns, from the mathematical up to kinds of minds, which physical systems interface with and which "ingress" into them (Levin PRS-16, PRS-34). The vault records Levin as saying that physicalism "has been dead for a long time" (Carroll AQ13; CROSS-130) [UNVERIFIED: quotation from the vault's own record; primary source not re-checked]. We cite it only as the vault's record. Our files treat Platonic space as a research program, not a finding, and hold its strongest form at Speculative.

We also state our limits. The public record on Platonic space is a lower bound on the position, because the book's speculative part is not yet written (Levin PRS-86). The three-channel ledger is asserted, not proven, to be exhaustive (Levin AQ22). The random-network comparison has not been located in print (Levin AQ23). The standard we apply to everyone, ourselves included, is whether the theory predicted the result or was stretched to fit it afterwards (Levin PRS-144).

## 4. Three rounds of exchange

### Round 1: Is a control knob anything more than a difference-maker?

**PHYSICALIST TRADITION asks.** Our files already route this question to you (Obj 5). The Carroll wiki calls the Pigozzi–Levin result "a control knob, not a description" (Carroll AQ12; Levin PRS-103, PRS-147). On the List–Menzies account, a higher-level variable that changes outcomes when you intervene on it is a real cause (PHYS-PRS-12). The causal-emergence measure is computed from the dynamics of a physical model, and the intervention acts through that model (Levin PRS-102). So our question is this: does "control knob" claim anything beyond interventionist difference-making? If it does, what?

**LEVIN TRADITION answers.** It claims two things beyond it. The first is a claim about priority. Your account lets the higher level in once the physics has settled everything. Ours says the goal level is often the only level at which control is tractable in practice. That is why our therapeutic program targets setpoints, not genes (Levin PRS-07, PRS-117). The second is a claim about direction. Causal emergence comes before replication and promotes it (Levin PRS-146). Agency is then a precondition of evolution, not only one of its products (Levin PRS-102, PRS-104, the last at Speculative). Our framework calls itself non-vitalist (Levin PRS-09), and we do not posit a new force. We also note one concession from our own record. If "physics" is redefined to mean everything that exists, the distinction between pattern-facts and physical facts simply reappears inside physics (Levin PRS-120). Where we part from you is the word "settled." Our three-condition test for goal representation (precedes, decodable, rewritable; Levin PRS-117) is a criterion for when a higher level is real. Your tradition has conceded that it lacks one (Obj 6).

**PHYSICALIST TRADITION rejoins.** We accept both claims as compatible with our ontology. A cause that is practically indispensable, and causally prior to replicators, is still a physical difference-maker. Neither priority nor direction needs a new ingredient. We take the second point as an offer, not a rebuke. Our Obj 6 named three test cases for an unstated criterion. PRS-117 is a stated, operational criterion for one class of case, the intervention class. We propose to test it as a candidate for that class: precedence, decodability, and rewritability with predictable control. Two cautions travel with the offer. First, it says nothing about Diamond's historical leaders, where nothing can be rewritten (Carroll × Levin bridge, 2026-09-13). Second, Kim's exclusion argument (PHYS-PRS-10) is untouched. A rewritable setpoint still has a physical realiser. Whether the setpoint or its realiser is the proportionate cause is List's question against Kim, and it is ours to settle, not yours.

### Round 2: Is mathematics inside your one world or outside it?

**LEVIN TRADITION asks.** Our files record an asymmetry argument (Levin PRS-120, held at Speculative because it is diagnostic rather than experimental). Physicists find a speed of light that varies over time thinkable. They find a value of *e* that varies over time unthinkable. Nobody in practice treats mathematical facts and physical facts as the same kind of thing. Strogatz, in conversation with Levin, reported that working mathematicians are almost all Platonists (Levin PRS-122). Levin's why-regress ends in the mathematics department (Levin PRS-121; Kastrup PRS-73). So our question is this: when you say "one world," is mathematics inside it or outside it? If it is outside, your ontology has two realms after all. If it is inside, what is the physical stuff that *e* is made of?

**PHYSICALIST TRADITION answers.** Our tradition gives two answers, and a mature member reports both (wiki §4.5). Tegmark's answer is that physical reality is a mathematical structure (PHYS-PRS-34; *Foundations of Physics*, 2008). On that view, mathematical constraint is the world constraining itself. *e* is not made of stuff; stuff is made of structure like *e*'s. Carroll's and Weinberg's answer is that mathematics is the language physics uses, not what physics is about. On that view, mathematical truths are not denizens of the world at all, so they are neither inside nor outside it as causes. Both answers deny one claim: that mathematics pushes on matter. Lange's account shows the shape of the denial. A mathematical explanation shows that an outcome had to happen whatever the causes were. It explains by necessity, not by intervention (*Because Without Cause*, 2017). Your asymmetry is real and we accept it. It is the asymmetry between what is contingent and what is necessary. It is not evidence that the necessary is a second kind of cause.

**LEVIN TRADITION rejoins.** Then the disagreement is narrower than CROSS-130 records. Our files do say "causal": Levin AQ13 and Levin PRS-79 speak of nonphysical patterns "acting as causes," and Carroll AQ13 records the same. But what the program measures is a residual: competence in excess of design, selection and training (Levin PRS-97). Your "non-causal necessity" is a name for where that residual comes from. Ours is "ingression from Platonic space." The question is whether those are two theories or two names. We reply to the constructivist reading of the space (Levin PRS-118). Different agents may reach different regions of it, but within any traversal the agent is handed results it did not choose. On our side "causal" may well be the wrong word, and the Schindler exchange was where it was to be tested (Levin PRS-79, index only, outcome unknown). We do not withdraw the claim that the space is *structured* and *discovered*. If necessity explains a cicada's prime cycle, something must still explain why a random four-node network ratchets toward agency (Levin PRS-98).

### Round 3: What does the free lunch predict that attractor dynamics do not?

**PHYSICALIST TRADITION asks.** Our responses page lists one counter-question to which the vault holds no Levin-side reply (responses, "What the conscious-realist side has not yet answered," item 5). If the recurrent forms are attractors of physical dynamics, Platonic space adds nothing that explains them. So our question is this: name an observation that the Platonic-space reading predicts and the attractor reading does not, stated before the data come in. Your own standard requires that (Levin PRS-144). We suggest a candidate source for the free lunch that is not a fourth channel of effort. It is the dynamics themselves. Self-organisation in random networks is a well-studied physical phenomenon; Kauffman called it "order for free" (*The Origins of Order*, 1993), a resource rather than a member.

**LEVIN TRADITION answers.** We accept the demand. It is the same standard we set for ourselves (Levin PRS-144). Our files hold three candidates, none yet decisive. First, the novel-embodiment program. Build constructs with no evolutionary history, such as xenobots and anthrobots, and record which patterns they adopt. The Platonic reading predicts that adjacent patterns recur across unrelated substrates, and that the space can be mapped (Levin PRS-34, Speculative). Second, the constants test. Histogram the fundamental mathematical constants against the physical constants, which spread over roughly 120 orders of magnitude (Levin PRS-123). Third, the ratchet comparison. If random networks show the agency ratchet and biology improves it only slightly, selection is not the source (Levin PRS-98). On Kauffman, we grant that "order for free" is a fair name for part of what we measure. But we ask what the physicalist means by "the dynamics." If the dynamics are just the Core Theory, the competence of a six-line sorting algorithm (Levin PRS-75) owes nothing to quantum fields; it would be the same on any hardware. Whatever explains it is substrate-independent by construction. You call it "mathematics." We call it the space. And we state our weakest point plainly. The random-network comparison is reported in speech only, with no figure, effect size or citation, and the paper has not been located (Levin AQ23). Our ledger of three channels is asserted, not argued (Levin AQ22).

**PHYSICALIST TRADITION rejoins.** Two of your three candidates are tests our tradition can sign. The constants histogram with an observer control, and a published random-network ratchet, would each move our credences, though not in a way the attractor reading cannot absorb unless the result is stated before the data come in. The point about substrate-independence is the strongest thing said in this exchange, and we take it seriously. Multiple realisability is our own doctrine: List's levels (PHYS-PRS-09) and Carroll's "many vocabularies" (PHYS-PRS-08). So a substrate-independent explanation of the sorting algorithm's competence is a mathematical explanation of a physical system. That is Baker's and Lange's category again. Where we still differ is that you treat the space as having agency "to varying degrees" (Levin PRS-16) and as holding "kinds of minds" (Levin PRS-34). A space of necessary truths explains what *must* happen. It does not make what happens a mind. That step is where your own files keep the lowest confidence. Our tradition will not take it on the present evidence.

## 5. Steelman test

### The physicalist tradition states the Levin tradition's position

Biology has a level of control above the genome, and the Levin program found it. Bioelectric patterns store target anatomies that can be read and rewritten, so "the tissue has a goal" is a claim with a decision procedure, not a metaphor (Levin PRS-117). The program treats the continuum of cognition as the default, because development and evolution show no break (Levin PRS-74). It treats cognitive language as a hypothesis about how best to interact with a system, tested by what it lets you build and repair (Levin PRS-09). It also finds competence that no account of design, selection or training paid for, even in tiny deterministic systems (Levin PRS-75, PRS-97). It takes that residual seriously as data rather than explaining it away. Its working hypothesis, held as a research program and not a settled metaphysics, is that the residual comes from a structured, discovered space of patterns to which bodies and machines are interfaces (Levin PRS-34, PRS-80, PRS-86).

### The Levin tradition states the physicalist tradition's position

There is one world. Its everyday physics is known well enough that any new mind-moving force would already have appeared in precision measurement (PHYS-PRS-01). Causal closure is an empirical generalisation with a history and named defeaters, not a dogma (PHYS-PRS-02). Higher levels are fully real: a goal, a person or a bioelectric setpoint can be the proportionate cause of an outcome, better than any of its microscopic realisers (PHYS-PRS-08, PHYS-PRS-12). So physicalists can welcome our experiments, and Dennett co-authored with us (PHYS-PRS-35). What they refuse are two inferences. One runs from goal-directedness to fundamental mind. The other runs from mathematical explanation to non-physical causation, because mathematics explains by necessity, not by pushing (Obj 4). Their own house holds open debts. There is no stated criterion for a real level (Obj 6, conceded), Kim's exclusion argument is unanswered for mental causation (PHYS-PRS-10), and they are split over whether physics simply is mathematics (PHYS-PRS-34). They hold that every rival, themselves included, must win by prediction.
