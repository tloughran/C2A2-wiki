# Physicalism × Conscious Realism (Hoffman-Prakash): Who Restricts the Trace?

## 1. The crux

Both traditions accept the same mathematics. Take a Markov kernel Q and a subset A of its states. The Trace Chain Theorem gives a unique kernel Q_A on A. Information about the states outside A is lost irreversibly. The relation "is a trace of" orders Markov chains in a way that is locally Boolean, globally non-Boolean, and leaves some pairs strictly incomparable (Hoffman PRS-34, PRS-36, PRS-37; physicalism wiki §6.2). On this the Hoffman-Prakash tradition builds a boundary concept, the trace blanket. It claims the trace blanket strictly generalises Friston's Markov blanket from acyclic to cyclic systems (Hoffman PRS-10). The physicalist tradition grants that the formalism is ontology-neutral. It also grants that the generalisation, if proved, is a real formal gain (PHYS-PRS-37). The dispute that remains is narrower and deeper: **who restricts?** In trace logic, A is by definition the observer's accessible states (Hoffman wiki, Question 19). For the physicalist, a blanket is a physical boundary picked out by conditional independence, and it is there whether or not anything observes it (PHYS-PRS-36). The theorems hold under both readings, so the theorems cannot decide between them. This dialogue asks whether anything else can.

## 2. Ground rules

The speakers are the two traditions, not the people. Positions are attributed to published works and to the vault's own files, never to private words. Neither side may win by restating its premises.

## 3. Opening statements

### PHYSICALIST TRADITION

The tradition starts from a bounded claim. Within their domain, the laws that govern atoms, cells and brains are known. Any new field strong enough to act on neurons would already have shown up in precisely measured particle physics (Carroll, "The Quantum Field Theory on Which the Everyday World Supervenes," arXiv:2101.07884, 2021; PHYS-PRS-01). Higher levels are real as patterns whose variables evolve, to a useful approximation, without the microscopic details (Carroll & Parola, "What Emergence Can Possibly Mean," arXiv:2410.15468, 2024; wiki O3, which is the tradition's working gloss).

Seen from here, the trace is familiar. Tracing a chain onto A integrates out the time the chain spends outside A. That is a form of marginalisation, a close relative of the coarse-graining the tradition already uses to explain emergence. "Relative" is the honest word, because tracing eliminates states instead of lumping them together (wiki §6.2(a)). The whitepaper credits Revuz (1984) for the theorem (Hoffman PRS-34), so the construction predates any metaphysics laid over it. Markov blankets are already in physicalist hands. The tradition reads Friston's free-energy framework as physical theory, and Seth's account of the self as interoceptive inference works inside that framework (Seth & Friston, "Active Interoceptive Inference and the Emotional Brain," *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B*, 2016; Seth, *Being You*, 2021; PHYS-PRS-36).

So the tradition does not attack the mathematics. It asks one question: what licenses reading A as "an observer's access" and not as "a subsystem's boundary"? Uniqueness, irreversible loss and incomparability hold under both readings. A population of neurons, the conformations of a molecule or a market can be traced exactly as a conscious agent can.

The tradition also brings a criterion it uses on itself. A theory that cannot be true and justifiably believed at the same time should be rejected (Carroll, "Why Boltzmann Brains Are Bad," arXiv:1702.00850, 2017; PHYS-PRS-32). The program's own theorem says that infinitely many larger kernels are compatible with any trace (Hoffman PRS-37). If so, an observer confined to a trace can have no evidence that the hidden states are conscious agents and not physical states. The theory does not undermine itself. Its positive ontology, though, is underdetermined by its own lights.

The tradition concedes what it should. If trace blankets strictly generalise Markov blankets to cyclic dynamics, that is a gain physicalists need, because organisms are cyclic throughout (PHYS-PRS-37). It concedes that the vault records this claim from an April 2026 talk and holds no written proof of it (Hoffman PRS-10). That is a fact about the vault, not a charge against the claim. And both traditions hold that spacetime is not fundamental (Carroll × Hoffman bridge, 2026-08-02).

### CONSCIOUS-REALIST TRADITION (Hoffman-Prakash)

The tradition is no longer the fitness-beats-truth argument of *The Case Against Reality* (2019). It is co-led by Hoffman and Prakash through the TRACE Institute (Hoffman PRS-19, PRS-20), and its foundation is a theorem. The Trace Chain Theorem gives, for any subset A of a Markov kernel's states, a unique kernel Q_A in closed form (Hoffman, Prakash & Chattopadhyay, *Traces of Consciousness*, preprint doi:10.20944/preprints202410.1305.v1; the vault records a 2024/2025 date discrepancy at Hoffman wiki Question 17; Hoffman PRS-34). Information about unobserved states is lost exactly, and any trace has infinitely many possible precursors (PRS-37). That is an independent route to the interface conclusion (PRS-37).

On this foundation the tradition records four further results. First, the trace order is a logic: locally Boolean, globally non-Boolean, with some pairs of kernels strictly incomparable (PRS-07, PRS-36). Second, as recorded in the Hoffman tradition wiki, the trace logic is homomorphic to the Lebesgue logic of probabilistic belief, the basis of the "observer mechanics" of Bennett, Hoffman & Prakash (1989) (PRS-35, PRS-45, PRS-46). Third, Recursive Trace Logic lets an observer change its own state space (PRS-30). Fourth, the trace blanket: the boundary structure the trace order induces on interacting agents. The tradition claims it strictly generalises the Markov blanket from acyclic to cyclic graphs, with the free-energy principle as a special case (Hoffman & Prakash, "A Multiscale Logic of Collective Intelligence," Thoughtforms Life, April 2026; PRS-10). The 2026 whitepaper extends the claim from boundaries to dynamics. Surprise minimisation is recovered without a material substrate, without a rigid internal/external split and without a true world state (*A Science of Reality: Observers, Interfaces, and Traces*, Trace Institute whitepaper, 2026; PRS-31).

On who restricts, the tradition argues from its own results, not from its premises. The physicalist's "physical boundary picked out by conditional independence" needs a joint distribution over the whole system, inside and outside the boundary. That is a view from nowhere, and PRS-37 shows no restricted view can recover it. Conscious realism does not add an observer to an ontology-neutral formalism. It declines to add the one thing the formalism does not contain, a God's-eye whole. What remains is the restricted views and the order among them, and an observer is what a restricted view is. "Just marginalisation" also understates the formalism. A Trace Institute working paper argues that hidden Markov models and probabilistic automata cannot jointly express many observer-relative views of one computation (*Trace Logic and Theory of Computation*, Thm 7.12; PRS-56, held at Medium; the vault records the paper as undated, unattributed and unreviewed).

Finally, the program has accepted the burden of proof in writing. The whitepaper's Eight Conjectures bind Recursive Trace Logic to named derivation targets, from Minkowski space to the Born rule and scattering amplitudes. Each can fail on ordinary mathematical grounds (PRS-28).

## 4. Three rounds of exchange

### Round 1 — The physicalist tradition asks

**Question.** The whitepaper withdraws the internal/external decomposition. It replaces the perception/decision/action 6-tuple with a single kernel, C = ⟨(X, 𝒳), Q, n⟩, and says, in a footnote, that it takes a more cautious stance on whether kernels and state spaces decompose into internal and external constituents (whitepaper §3, fn. 1, as recorded in Hoffman PRS-29). But a blanket is a boundary concept. It is defined by splitting states into inside, outside and the boundary that screens them (cf. Friston × Hoffman bridge, 2026-09-20). How can a framework that has withdrawn the internal/external split also claim to generalise the very concept that is that split? Doesn't PRS-10 need what PRS-29 gave up?

**Answer (conscious-realist tradition).** What PRS-29 withdraws is a *fixed, built-in* decomposition. The old 6-tuple wrote a world/agent split into every agent's definition, and the whitepaper calls the 6-tuple a recoverable "sufficiently decomposed variant" (PRS-29). What the trace supplies is a *derived* decomposition. Choose any subset A, and the theorem gives A, its complement U, and the trace kernel that summarises what U contributes to A (PRS-34). So the split is not primitive; each trace generates one. A trace blanket is the boundary structure that this relational partition induces between interacting agents (PRS-10). On this reading PRS-29 and PRS-10 do not conflict. They are the same move seen from two sides: take the split out of the foundations, then get it back as a theorem about restriction. A boundary that is derived, not built in, also need not assume acyclic dependence, which is why the claim reaches cyclic systems. PRS-31's "without a rigid internal-external decomposition" means not fixed in advance, not absent.

**Rejoinder (physicalist tradition).** The tradition accepts the answer: it removes the charge of inconsistency. But it sharpens the question. If every subset yields a trace, and every trace yields a derived split, then a split costs nothing: one exists for every choice of A. A Markov blanket is not like that. Most subsets of a system are not blankets, and finding the partition under which a blanket exists is hard work. The Friston × Hoffman bridge (2026-09-20) states the asymmetry: a trace is a *construction*, a blanket is a *discovery*. So if trace blankets generalise Markov blankets, they must be traces that meet some further condition. Naming that condition is the technical content of the claim, and the vault holds no statement of it (Friston × Hoffman bridge; Hoffman wiki, Question 13). Whatever the condition turns out to be, it will be a fact about Q: about which states screen which. It will not be a fact about who is looking. The tradition has not checked whether Friston's random-dynamical-systems blankets already allow cyclic coupling [UNVERIFIED] (wiki §6.2(c)).

### Round 2 — The conscious-realist tradition asks

**Question.** You say a blanket is a physical boundary, picked out by conditional independence, that exists whether or not anything observes it. Conditional independence is a property of a joint distribution over the whole system. Where do you stand to read that distribution? By PRS-37, any trace has infinitely many larger kernels as possible precursors, and no inference from the inside recovers which one is actual. Isn't "the physical boundary" a description of the whole that no restricted observer could be entitled to, and so the one reading the mathematics says no observer can confirm?

**Answer (physicalist tradition).** The trace theorem needs the whole too. Q_A is defined from Q: the formula sums over excursions through U using Q's own transitions (PRS-34). Without an ambient kernel there is no trace. So both readings posit a whole. Conscious realism's whole, a network of conscious agents, is as far beyond any single trace's evidence as the physicalist's. PRS-37 cuts both ways (PHYS-PRS-32). On how anyone is entitled to claims about the whole, the tradition's answer is that entitlement is graded, not all-or-nothing. We hold theories with degrees of credence, updated on total evidence and weighted by priors (Carroll, "Beyond Falsifiability," arXiv:1801.05016, 2018; wiki E1). Conditional independences in a cell or a circuit are found by intervening, measuring and comparing models, never by reading the whole distribution. The exact underdetermination in PRS-37 is a result about what one fixed trace determines. Science is not one fixed trace. It is many overlapping restrictions, revised and cross-checked. Even inside the program's own logic, that is a question about joins in the trace order, which PRS-36 leaves open for pairs that are comparable.

**Rejoinder (conscious-realist tradition).** The tradition grants the symmetry: both readings posit an ambient kernel that no single trace confirms. But the physicalist's answer depends on combining overlapping restrictions, which in trace-logic terms is the question of joins, and here the tradition has a result. Many pairs of kernels are strictly incomparable, and no universal order holds across all kernels (PRS-36; Hoffman wiki, Question 8). Where two views have no join, there is no common refinement. That leaves no place inside the order from which the two can be combined into one view of the whole. So the physicalist picture, many views converging on one observer-independent world, bets that the views science uses all have joins, and the trace order shows the bet is not guaranteed. Conscious realism treats incomparability as part of the structure. The physicalist owes an account of why science's own restrictions keep landing on comparable pairs.

### Round 3 — The physicalist tradition asks

**Question.** Grant all the mathematics, the trace blanket included. What observation, or what result inside the mathematics, would come out differently depending on whether A is "an observer's accessible states" or "a subsystem's physical boundary"? The Eight Conjectures (PRS-28) are derivation targets for known physics. Recovering known physics is a consistency check, not evidence for one reading of the kernels over the other (wiki §6.2(d); Carroll × Hoffman bridge, 2026-07-19). The Trace × Noonautics falsification conditions are recorded as "announced, not specified" (Hoffman wiki, Question 15; PRS-38, PRS-39). Name one result that the conscious-agent reading predicts and the physical reading of the same kernels does not.

**Answer (conscious-realist tradition).** First, the Eight Conjectures are not only consistency checks. The vault records neither Minkowski space from Markov n-cycles nor the Born rule from enhanced Markov chains as yet derived (PRS-28, Conjectures 1 and 5). If the program does so from a substrate with no spacetime and no Hilbert space, that is new physics, whatever reading one adds. The tradition admits it is not a discriminating test of the reading. Second, the program offers construction as a falsification surface. Modular AI built under trace logic, and spacetime physics recovered from the formalism, can each be attempted and can each fail (PRS-49). Third, the discriminating question belongs to a different domain from physics. The conscious-agent reading says the states of a kernel are experiences: the streamlined agent's measurable space is a space of "potential experiences" (PRS-29). It therefore makes claims about experience that the physical reading leaves silent. The Trace × Noonautics collaboration was set up to test those claims, by perturbing the interface pharmacologically (PRS-25, PRS-38). Its predictions have not yet been specified, and the tradition records that fact against itself (Hoffman wiki, Question 15; acquisition item 20).

**Rejoinder (physicalist tradition).** The tradition takes the first two parts as offered. A derivation of Minkowski space or the Born rule from Markov chains would be a real result (Carroll × Hoffman bridge, 2026-07-19). But a result that is real whatever reading one adds is, for that very reason, not evidence for one reading. The third part is the honest answer, and it shows where the dispute actually lives. The discriminating claims concern experience, not the boundary. So the trace blanket cannot be the place where the two readings are told apart. The relocation also has a cost for the conscious-realist side. On the program's own account, the observations in a pharmacological study are themselves traces (PRS-37). The physical reading can model the same reports as brain states under a drug, so what is needed is a pattern one reading predicts and the other forbids. Until the Trace × Noonautics predictions are specified (PRS-38), the question of who restricts has no test, in physics or in phenomenology. The tradition says the same of itself. It has not yet named a result that the physical-boundary reading predicts and the observer reading forbids (responses.md, Objection 8).

## 5. Steelman test

### The physicalist tradition states the conscious-realist position

The Hoffman-Prakash program begins from an exact theorem, not from evolutionary speculation. Any restriction of a Markov process to a subset of its states yields a unique effective process, and the loss of information about the rest is total and irreversible (PRS-34, PRS-37). The relation among such restrictions is a logic: locally classical, globally non-classical, and with incomparable pairs, which the Hoffman wiki reads as quantum logic (PRS-35, PRS-36; CROSS-033). The program does not posit a world and then add observers to it. It declines to posit anything no restricted view could reach. It takes the restricted views themselves, and the order among them, as what there is. An observer is just what a restricted view is. On this footing the boundary between agent and world is derived, not assumed (PRS-29). The program claims this boundary is general enough to contain Friston's blankets as a special case (PRS-10). It has also committed in writing to recovering spacetime and quantum theory as theorems (PRS-28).

### The conscious-realist tradition states the physicalist position

Physicalism, in its poetic-naturalist form, does not deny that every scientist sees from somewhere. It holds that the laws relevant to brains and organisms are known within their domain (PHYS-PRS-01). It holds that higher-level descriptions, persons and boundaries among them, are real wherever their variables evolve autonomously enough to be useful (wiki O3). On that view a trace is one more way of getting an autonomous higher-level dynamics. It is exact where coarse-graining is approximate, and so it is welcome (PHYS-PRS-37). A Markov blanket is a pattern of conditional independence in a physical system, found by intervention and model comparison, never by reading the whole distribution at once (wiki E1). Physicalism also agrees that spacetime is not fundamental. Its quarrel is only with the further step, from "this subset is restricted" to "this subset is a conscious observer." It holds that this step adds a claim no trace can test (PHYS-PRS-32), and it asks the same test of itself.

### (b) Questions to mature members of the physicalist tradition

- **P1.** The Round 1 rejoinder bets that whatever condition turns a trace into a blanket will be a fact about Q, not about who is looking. Is that bet a consequence of the tradition's account of levels (O3), or a further commitment the tradition has to defend?
- **P2.** The Round 2 rejoinder asks why science's own restrictions keep landing on comparable pairs. Does the tradition hold that the views science combines must always have joins? If two well-confirmed theories turned out to be incomparable in the trace order, what would the tradition revise: its realism about a single world, or its reading of the order?
- **P3.** By the tradition's own standard (wiki §7, Question 7), what observation would raise its credence that a restricted subset is an observer and not only a boundary? If the honest answer is "nothing", what is the status of "physical boundary"?
- **P4.** Graded credence about the whole is built up by intervention. Is an intervener itself a restriction, in the trace sense? If so, does the tradition's epistemology already presuppose an agent of the kind it treats as derived?
- **P5.** If PRS-56's separation result holds up under review, does "a relative of coarse-graining" still describe the trace? Or must the tradition's account of emergence be extended to include structure that no single-observation formalism can express?

### (c) Questions to mature members of the conscious-realist tradition (Hoffman-Prakash)

- **H1.** What further condition on a trace makes it a trace blanket that contains Friston's blankets? Can it be stated without reference to an observer, and does a written proof of the PRS-10 claim exist beyond the April 2026 talk?
- **H2.** PRS-36 makes some views strictly incomparable. Does the tradition hold that the views of different scientists, or of different traditions, are ever incomparable in this formal sense? Or is that a conjecture (Hoffman wiki, Question 14) that the tradition would decline to assert?
- **H3.** If one of the Eight Conjectures were proved, would the tradition count the proof as evidence for conscious realism, or only as a result of the mathematics? If only the latter, which of the tradition's own results bears on the reading itself?
- **H4.** What pattern in the Trace × Noonautics reports would conscious realism forbid? Given PRS-37, how does the tradition tell a report that is a trace of other agents from a report that is a trace of the reporter's own kernel?
- **H5.** Is every subset A of every kernel an observer, or only some? If only some, what distinguishes them? If all, is a trace of a thermostat's states a conscious observer?
