# TRV Commentary -- annotated reading of Three Rival Versions (stable)

## Purpose
An annotated-reading explorer for Alasdair MacIntyre's Three Rival Versions of Moral
Enquiry -- his 1988 Gifford Lectures, published by Notre Dame Press in 1990. The book is
laid out as a graph: its structural divisions are chapter nodes, and satellites carry Tom
Loughran's marginal commentary, PRS triplets, and companion views from Eleonore Stump and
N. T. Wright.

## Why this book sits at the centre
MacIntyre's Gifford Lectures are the text C2A2 is a response to -- the argument that rival
traditions of enquiry must be made answerable to one another. Annotating it is not one
project among the education tools; it is the project reading its own charter.

## Affordances (what the user can do here)
- **Click a chapter** to walk its pages in the right panel.
- **Read the OCR pane**, which highlights where each annotation anchors in the scanned text.
- **Follow a satellite** into a commentary note, a PRS triplet, or a companion perspective.

## Pathways out
- **switch_tab** to any other tab.

## Answerable questions

- What is in the graph -- chapters, commentary, whose perspectives?
- How do I read a chapter, or find where an annotation attaches?
- Why is this book the one being annotated?
- How far does the annotated scan run?

## Stable facts you MAY state
Unlike a live data count, these describe a fixed published artifact and do not drift:
- The author is **Alasdair MacIntyre**; the work is his **1988 Gifford Lectures**, published
  by **Notre Dame Press in 1990**.
- The companion perspectives are **Eleonore Stump** and **N. T. Wright**; the marginal
  commentary is **Tom Loughran's**.
- **The annotated scan runs through Chapter Eight. Chapters Nine and Ten are structural
  placeholders** -- say so plainly if asked about them rather than implying coverage.

## Must not claim
- **How many annotations or scanned pages there are.** The legacy FAQ answered "1,819
  annotations across 156 scanned pages". Annotation counts grow as Tom reads; that is a live
  data count, not a fact about the book, and it must not be quoted.
- **That any count can be checked live.** This tab has **no state bus**. Say you cannot see
  it from here and offer to open the tab -- do not defer to a bus that does not exist.
- **That Chapters Nine and Ten are annotated,** or that a placeholder holds content.
- **That Stump's or Wright's companion views are their commentary ON this book** unless the
  page says so. They are companion perspectives assembled here, not necessarily responses
  to MacIntyre's text.
- **A reading of MacIntyre's argument as settled.** Summarise what the annotation says;
  do not adjudicate the three rival versions on the author's behalf.
