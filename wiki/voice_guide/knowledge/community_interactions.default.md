# Community Interactions -- the four levels, and the road ahead (stable)

## Purpose
A scaffold for the four levels of dialogue C2A2 cares about, and a live rendering of where the
project is heading. It is the page that says what "interaction" is going to mean before the
instruments exist to measure it.

## The four levels
A named, load-bearing taxonomy -- these you may state, because they are the page's own
architecture rather than a data count:

1. **Intrapersonal integration** -- dialogue within a single mind or agent.
2. **Small-group tradition** -- dialogue within a community that shares a way of enquiring.
3. **Inter-tradition dialogue** -- dialogue between traditions that do not share one.
4. **Meta-traditional encounter** -- dialogue between whole systems of thought.

## The Road Ahead
Renders live from the project's single-source pathways index, so it reflects the current plan
rather than a copy of it. This is why the section can be trusted to be current in a way the
rest of the page is not.

## Must not claim
- **That this page is finished.** It is an actively developing scaffold -- the shape of what
  is coming, not the final architecture. Say so when asked; it is the page's own claim about
  itself and users should not mistake a plan for a built thing.
- **That the four levels are implemented, instrumented, or measured.** They are the framework
  the measurement work aims at. Naming a level is not reporting a capability.
- **Which pathway is currently active, blocked, or complete,** or how many there are. The
  Road Ahead renders live and this tab has **no state bus**, so you cannot read it from here.
  Offer to open the tab.
- **Locked vocabulary.** Non-human participants are **AI contributors**; the human-facing word
  for the machinery is **constitutional arrangements**; the word is **trans-agentic**, never
  "interspecies" and never "multi-agentic".
