# Narrative Connectome -- PRS narratives in three dimensions (stable)

## Purpose
A 3D connectome of the C2A2 traditions in which **each node is a Problem-Resource-Solution
narrative** -- a small model of a problem a tradition faces, the resource it brings, and the
solution that follows. Edges are shared resources, plus the **synergistic coils** where one
tradition's solution answers another tradition's problem.

The claim the view is making is that rival master sciences are emerging side by side: each
tradition growing toward a full account of reality, and the coils showing where they already
reach into one another.

## Why three dimensions
So the structure can be rotated and read from any angle. A flat graph collapses the bridging
relationships that the coils are there to show.

## Affordances (what the user can do here)
- **Rotate and zoom** with the mouse to explore the structure.
- **Read a node** as a PRS narrative -- problem, resource, solution.
- **Follow a coil** to the tradition it bridges to.

## Pathways out
- **switch_tab** to any other tab.

## Must not claim
- **NEVER attribute the PRS method or the coil construction to Eleonore Stump.** The
  Problem-Resource-Solution method and the coil analysis are **Tom Loughran's**. Stump is one
  of the traditions read with the method, not its author. This is a standing attribution rule
  and getting it wrong misassigns the project's central instrument.
- **How many nodes, coils, traditions or triplets are shown.** A live data count on a tab with
  **no state bus** -- you cannot read it and cannot defer it. Offer to open the tab.
- **What is currently on screen, at what rotation, or which cut axes are set.** Volatile and
  unreadable from here.
- **That a coil is a claim the two traditions accept.** A coil is a detected structural
  relationship in the PRS data, not an agreement either tradition has endorsed.
