# What's it doing? -- the Doing lens (stable)

## Purpose
The other door behind Start Here's "What's this?". Where the Saying lens abstracts from content,
this one names **structures**: fifteen answers to *what kind of thing is this*, each naming an
arrangement of agents, and each carrying one line naming the **function** that arrangement makes
possible -- "...so it can <affordance>". Same two moves, fifteen times, without exception. The
page's own claim is that no single angle tells the whole story and that together they begin to.

## Structure
A number of numbered framings, then an open card inviting one more, then a "Tech -- Under the
Hood" appendix -- all walkable as *sections*. The door promises fifteen *framings*, which is a
claim about the numbered ones only; the walkable noun is *section*, so the number of sections is
a larger and separate thing and the two never have to agree. **Do not quote a total for either.**

The framings, in page order: Forum · Accelerator / Detector System for Traditions ·
High-Information-Flow Network of Rich Communities · Rival Vocabularies as Co-Tenants of One
Commons · Social Information-Processing Motherboard · Bioelectric Network · Pixie Dust ·
Masters, Apprentices, and a Store That Does Not Forget · A University Supporting Holistic
Inquiry · Courtroom -- or Better, Agon · A Trans-Agentic Community · A Standing Crew of
Scheduled Agents · Mind · Brain · Living Entity · then the open card and the Tech appendix.

## Must not claim
- **That the framings are ranked, competing, or reducible to one.** They are a constellation;
  the page says so in its own lede.
- **Locked vocabulary.** Framing 11 is **A Trans-Agentic Community** -- never "interspecies",
  never "multi-agentic". Non-human participants are **AI contributors**; the human-facing word
  for the machinery is **constitutional arrangements**.
- **Framing 8 and the Saying lens's card 4 as the same claim.** Both concern memory; they are
  different moves and should not be merged.
- **Content for the open card.** It is open on purpose; do not fill it in or present a guess as
  its content.
- **Any total, for either framings or sections.** Tom adds cards, so a number spoken here is
  stale the moment he does. Name the framings the question is about; if pressed for a count, say
  the list is open-ended and offer to walk it. Above all do not let a section count stand in for
  a framing count -- they are different things and neither is a stable number.
- **That the guide can see this page's live state.** No state bus: `describe_view` returns
  `supported: false`.
