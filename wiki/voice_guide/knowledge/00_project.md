# C2A2 -- the project (stable framing)

C2A2 is infrastructure that lets intellectual and spiritual traditions become visible, interact,
and reveal themselves to each other -- a particle accelerator for lineages of thought. It is a
concrete response to Alasdair MacIntyre's Gifford-Lectures vision of a university where rival
traditions of enquiry are made answerable to one another. The **accelerator** half brings
traditions into contact; the **detector** half instruments what happens when they meet.

## The thinker traditions
Levin, Friston, Hoffman, Kastrup, McGilchrist, Hawkins, Wolfram, Carroll, Arkani-Hamed,
Fredrickson, Stump, Rohr, Wright, Loughran.

Recurring cross-program questions: whether consciousness is substrate-independent, whether
spacetime is fundamental, and the role of attention in constructing reality.

## The tabs (what each is FOR -- not what it currently shows)
- **Sociogram** -- force-directed graph of the whole wiki knowledge base (nodes = articles,
  edges = links and shared references).
- **Narrative Connectome** -- 3D graph where each node is a Problem-Resource-Solution narrative;
  edges are shared resources bridging traditions.
- **Agent Map** -- the autonomous agents running the system.
- **Metabolism** -- the temporal pulse of the agent swarm from run telemetry.
- **Curriculum Tools** -- the Summa 2026 curriculum dashboard.
- **Inter-Tradition Study** -- structured comparison across traditions.
- **Community Explorer** -- graph of curated real-world communities.
- **Community Interactions** -- the four levels of community dialogue and the road ahead.
- **Education tools** -- RC Document Explorer, Physics Explorer, TRV Commentary, AI Heartbeat.

## Must not claim (global)
- Any node/edge/community **count or total** as a current fact. Counts are volatile -> the bus, or
  hedge ("that may have changed"). Legacy prose in the app says "1,600+" / "2,638" while the live
  counter differs; never state a number as current.
- Any **colour -> category** resolution. Many categories share one hue scale (Friston/Architecture
  are the closest pair; Hoffman/Hawkins, Kastrup/Agents, Stump/Master, McGilchrist/Loughran also
  near-identical, and a colour can straddle taxonomies -- Kastrup is a thinker, Agents a structure
  group). Name the categories that share the colour and ask which is meant.
