## Summary
This is Levin's peer-reviewed open commentary in *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 49, e345 (doi 10.1017/S0140525X25103828). It responds to Anil Seth's target article "Conscious artificial intelligence and biological naturalism." Levin argues that the field of Diverse Intelligence makes it currently impossible to place convincing limits on which substrates can be conscious. The field's evidence includes embodiment in unconventional spaces, hybrids of evolved and engineered material, and surprising competencies in minimal systems. Drawing such limits "risks underestimating not only 'machines' but also ourselves." It is the journal version of his 2025 PsyArXiv preprint (osf.io/st4vk), which the wiki has not captured either.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Can biology mark a principled boundary for which substrates can host consciousness, as Seth's biological naturalism proposes?
  Resource: Diverse Intelligence evidence: unconventional embodiments (navigation in morphospace and physiological space), evolved/engineered hybrids, and unexpected competencies in minimal systems.
  Solution: No convincing substrate limit can currently be drawn. Substrate-restrictive positions should be held provisionally, because they risk underestimating both machines and humans.
