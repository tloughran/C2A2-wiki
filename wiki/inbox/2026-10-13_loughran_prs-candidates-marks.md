## Summary
Tom's margin notes (861 with written text) cluster around six themes: craft-based rationality, second-first-language competence and incommensurability, imaginative versus intellectual occupation of a rival standpoint, the "fifth level" of persuasion across rivals, rival universities, and new curricula or genres. Six candidates are kept, all at Medium. Five further threads are reported only. Nothing is High, because no scan check was possible and most anchors failed the OCR match. The first pass rejected no quote on verbatim grounds: all 33 quotes used were found exactly in the index.
