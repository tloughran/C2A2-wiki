## Summary
In this Substack essay McGilchrist directly dismantles the popular folk-equation that maps the left hemisphere to "male" and the right hemisphere to "female." He argues that the evidence does not support a clean sex-to-hemisphere correspondence, and that several well-attested observations actively cut against the formula. The piece is a careful disambiguation of his own theory from a vulgarized version of it that circulates in popular culture.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is McGilchrist policing the boundary of his own program — separating the genuine claim (the two hemispheres differ in *mode of attention*, not in crude trait-clusters) from a category error that misattributes a gendered essentialism to hemispheric theory. It strengthens the tradition's native epistemic standards by showing how the theory is routinely flattened when imported into alien (here, pop-psychology) frames.
