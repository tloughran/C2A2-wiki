## Summary
SPCK (256 pp, published 15 Oct 2026; announced at https://ntwrightpage.com/2026/10/14/into-the-heart-of-isaiah/) is a guided reading of Isaiah 40-55, billed as "The Fifth Gospel." Wright argues the chapters are one connected drama rather than isolated predictions: exile and homecoming, God's return to Zion, the vocation of the suffering Servant, covenant renewal, and new creation. These themes climax in Jesus's life, death and resurrection and shape the church's Spirit-led calling. (Based on the publisher page; chapter-level content not yet read.)

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Companion to *Into the Heart of Romans* (2023); gives the Isaianic backbone for Wright's "exile-and-return / God's return" account of salvation as inclusion in the covenanted new-creation people. Not yet in the wiki. Directly citable for Summa 2026 claims about salvation, vocation and the Servant as corporate-and-individual figure.
