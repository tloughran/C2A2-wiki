## Summary
A *With Reality in Mind* Q&A (30 June 2026) in which Kastrup addresses two young philosophers directly about entering the philosophical vocation. His warning is that the Daimonic path opens doors but does not reduce the sacrifice: "his own life is now like a movie — synchronicities stack up with such regularity that he now takes it for granted. But a life of meaning is not a life of ease." The session also covers how dreams differ from waking life, "why information confuses so many philosophers," the symbolism of diving, and what it means not to dream.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The wiki holds the Daimon as a *metaphysical* posit — the principle of individuation, the specific character of an alter — from the 2025 book and the Harpur dialogues. It does not yet hold the Daimon as a **vocational and formative** claim: what following it costs, and what a senior figure tells apprentices about entering. That is the missing register. A research program's account of how it forms its own mature members is first-order data for this network, not biography.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: If the Daimon is real and following it aligns one with the deeper structure of mind, does the aligned life become easier? The synchronicity literature invites the inference that it does.
  Resource: Kastrup's report of his own case — synchronicities now so regular he takes them for granted — paired immediately with the counter-claim that this does not diminish the sacrifice: "a life of meaning is not a life of ease."
  Solution: Alignment and ease come apart. The Daimonic path is evidenced by increased meaningful coincidence and *not* by reduced cost, so ease is not a criterion for having found one's Daimon — which removes the most obvious way of misreading the doctrine as a prosperity heuristic.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: "Doors will open for you on the Daimonic journey, but that doesn't diminish the sacrifice involved."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: What does a tradition owe those entering it? Philosophical formation is usually transmitted as doctrine, with the vocational cost left tacit.
  Resource: The session's staging — a senior figure explicitly warning two named apprentices "as they embark on their perilous journey," rather than instructing them, delivered publicly to the whole membership community.
  Solution: The warning *is* part of the curriculum. Making the cost explicit at entry is treated as a condition of honest formation, not an aside.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: "In this meeting Bernardo warns two young philosophers as they embark on their perilous journey..." The published summary is one paragraph; the recording is member-gated and was not retrievable.

## Cross-Tradition Signals

**C2A2-native — this is formation data, and the network needs more of it.** The accelerator's stated aim is to bring people to *mature membership* in a tradition quickly enough that cross-tradition dialogue becomes possible. That presupposes each tradition has a curriculum for maturity. Most sources this network ingests are a tradition stating its *conclusions*; this one is a tradition stating its *entry conditions and costs*. Recommend the Master agent treat "how does this tradition form apprentices, and what does it warn them about?" as a standing cross-tradition question to be asked of all fourteen, with this proposal as the Kastrup-side seed. Compare the McGilchrist commencement address (PROP-2026-07-22-002), which is the same genre from a different tradition — an educational tradition stating its telos to those it has just formed.

**Kastrup ↔ Rohr / Stump — discernment of vocation.** The claim that alignment does not reduce cost is a near-exact restatement of a standing commitment in the contemplative and Thomistic traditions: consolation is not the criterion of a true call, and the via is not eased by being the right one. That three traditions with incompatible metaphysics converge on the same discernment rule is exactly the kind of convergence the Pattern Detector should surface — a rule that survives translation across paradigms is a candidate for being load-bearing rather than local.

**Kastrup ↔ Wolfram / Friston — "information" as the contested term.** PRS-CANDIDATE-03, if it develops as expected, puts Kastrup in direct opposition to two programs in this network that treat information as fundamental or near-fundamental. Worth watching, but not actionable until the recording is heard.
