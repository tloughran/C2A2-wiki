## Summary
A With Reality in Mind session (held Tuesday 13 October 2026; page published 11 October) that opened with Kastrup's review of the metaphysical options now on the table: which ones he confidently discards, and his intuitions about where the field should go next. The page, written by host Amir Giles, sorts Robert Lawrence Kuhn's catalogue of nearly 500 theories of consciousness into five families: consciousness emerges from the physical; it is separate from the physical; it is a property of the physical; both emerge from something else; or the physical is an appearance in consciousness. It restates Kastrup's view that physicalism is the worst framework on offer. It also reports something new for this wiki: Kastrup does **not** regard analytic idealism as a full account of reality, only as "a step in the right direction", one "our culture might actually be ready for."

**Evidence caveat:** the recording is for members only. This card is built from the public session page and its framing. The page's own words were written by the host, not Kastrup. What he actually said in the session is not verified. As with PROP-2026-10-07-001, the reviewer may want to hold this card until a transcript or a public clip appears.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Until now the wiki has recorded analytic idealism as Kastrup's settled position ("the 21st century's only plausible metaphysics"). If he openly calls it provisional, that is a change in how the tradition sees itself. It moves from defending a finished system to running a research program with unfinished business. That fits the C2A2 framing of traditions as programs judged by the questions they generate. A list of the frameworks he rejects, and the directions he expects next, would also give the wiki a ready-made map of where Kastrup's program borders the other traditions.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **MacIntyre / C2A2 core:** a tradition's leading voice calling his own framework provisional is the kind of self-assessment MacIntyre ties to a living tradition: knowing where its present formulation runs out. Worth flagging to the master wiki if the recording confirms it.
- **Hoffman (conscious realism):** both Hoffman and Kastrup fall in the "physical is an appearance in consciousness" family. Which "future directions" Kastrup favors could show whether he moves toward or away from Hoffman's mathematically formal conscious-agent program.
- **Wolfram / Carroll / Arkani-Hamed:** a confident discard list will almost certainly include physicalist and emergentist positions close to Carroll's poetic naturalism. That gives a concrete point of disagreement to stage.
- **Stump (Thomistic hylomorphism):** hylomorphism fits none of the five families cleanly. That gap is itself a signal: the grouping may leave out the form/matter option.
- **McGilchrist:** McGilchrist holds that consciousness and matter are aspects of one reality, and resists calling that idealism. That puts him close to the "co-emergence from something else" family, a natural partner for any post-idealist direction Kastrup sketches.
