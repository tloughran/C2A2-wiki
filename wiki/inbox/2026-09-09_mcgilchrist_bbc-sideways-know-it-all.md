## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Fredrickson.** "How to stay in equilibrium while inquiring" is a flourishing question wearing epistemological clothes. If McGilchrist's answer is about breadth of attention, it meets broaden-and-build directly: the same widened attentional aperture, described from a clinical rather than an affective side.
- **Kastrup.** Kastrup treats dissociation as the mechanism by which individual minds arise. A psychiatrist warning that certain lines of self-inquiry carry risk is speaking about dissociation as a *clinical hazard* rather than a metaphysical structure. The two men are collaborators; a divergence here on whether dissociation is benign machinery or a live danger would be a high-value flag. Not yet verified — flagging the place to look.
- **C2A2 / MacIntyre.** The claim that some inquiries are riskier than others is a claim about what a tradition must supply to a novice before turning them loose in it — the curriculum-for-maturity problem C2A2 is built around. If McGilchrist frames the safeguard as *apprenticeship or company* rather than *method*, that is close to a direct hit on the second-first-language thesis.
