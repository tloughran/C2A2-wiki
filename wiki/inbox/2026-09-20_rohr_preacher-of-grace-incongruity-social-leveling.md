## Summary
Wednesday of the "Paul's Transforming Vision" week (Week 37). Rohr-curated but authored by the theologian Richard Beck, drawing on his *The Book of Love: A Better Way to Read the Bible* (Broadleaf, 2026), 210-216. Beck's claim is historical and specific: the meaning of "grace" underwent a full reversal, and Paul is the agent of that reversal. In the ancient Mediterranean gift economy, a gift was *merited* — given to a worthy recipient who would repay it, because wasting a gift on the undeserving was irrational. Paul's move was to insist that the gift is given precisely to those with no claim on it, and that this *incongruity* between gift and recipient is not a defect of the gift but the very thing that makes it perfect. Beck's second move is sociological rather than theological: because grace on this reading ignores rankings of worth, it dissolves the scale on which persons were ranked at all. He calls this "social leveling" and argues it is what made Paul's *ekklesia* structurally possible — mixed assemblies of rich and poor, Jew and gentile, enslaved and free could cohere only because the entry criterion had been removed. The closing imperative ("welcome each other as Christ has welcomed you") is read as a transitive obligation: unconditional reception received becomes unconditional reception extended.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The Rohr wiki's Universal-Christ material carries the cosmic-Christ frame but is thin on the *mechanism* by which a metaphysical claim about universal indwelling becomes a social fact. Beck supplies exactly that link, and does it with a dated, falsifiable historical claim (the gift-economy inversion) rather than a devotional assertion — which is what makes it citable rather than merely edifying. For the Summa 2026 synthesis it is load-bearing on the *loving unity as computational telos* theme: it names a concrete condition under which a heterogeneous population forms a single cohering body, namely the removal of a worth-ranking function. That is a structural claim, not a sentimental one, and it is the kind of claim the network's other traditions can be made to engage.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

```
PRS-CANDIDATE-A:
  Label: incongruity-as-perfection
  Problem: On what grounds can a gift given to an unworthy recipient be called a better gift rather than a wasted one? The ancient gift economy had a coherent answer (it cannot), and contemporary accounts of grace usually assume the reversal without arguing for it.
  Resource: Beck's reconstruction of the ancient merited-gift norm, against which Paul's unmerited gift reads as deliberate inversion rather than as the obvious default (The Book of Love, 210-213).
  Solution: The incongruity between gift and recipient is the gift's perfecting feature, because a gift conditioned on worth is a transaction within a ranking system, while a gift indifferent to worth is what abolishes the ranking system. Grace is not generosity within the economy; it is the suspension of the economy.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Beck states the reversal explicitly and dates it to Paul ("grace was once merited; today it is unmerited"), and grounds it in Paul's own provocative framing of the gift as given to enemies.
```

```
PRS-CANDIDATE-B:
  Label: grace-as-social-leveling
  Problem: What makes a maximally heterogeneous assembly cohere without a shared rank ordering, a shared ethnicity, or a shared class interest? Ordinary accounts of group formation require at least one of these.
  Resource: Beck's reading of the Pauline ekklesia as an assembly whose membership function has had the worth-ranking term deleted, so that no honour hierarchy is available inside it.
  Solution: Removing the ranking function is not merely permissive but constitutive: the diversity of the early assemblies is a consequence of grace-as-incongruous, not an accident tolerated by it. Unconditional reception, once received, becomes a transitive obligation ("welcome each other as Christ has welcomed you"), which supplies the maintenance rule the group needs to persist.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Beck's claim that grace "became a tool of social leveling, creating capacities for egalitarian and democratic forms of social life," tied to the observed class and ethnic composition of the Pauline churches. The causal direction (grace enabling diversity rather than diversity being rationalized by grace) is Beck's argument, not a demonstrated result, hence Medium.
```

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Wright Agent**: Direct and testable contact. Wright's New Perspective reading of justification also relocates the point of Pauline grace away from individual merit-accounting and toward membership in the covenant people — but Wright gets there through second-temple Jewish covenantal categories, whereas Beck gets there through the Greco-Roman gift economy. These are two different reconstructions of the same inversion, and they are not obviously compatible: Beck's frame makes grace anti-hierarchical by construction, while Wright's makes it covenant-defining, which is a boundary-drawing operation. **Worth flagging as a genuine tension for the master agent, not a convergence.** This is the strongest Wright-Rohr contact surface the network has surfaced in several weeks.
- **Stump Agent**: Stump's metaphysics of corporate substance — real composite entities made of "united somethings-or-others" — needs an account of what unites the constituents. Beck offers a candidate: the deletion of an internal ranking function, so that no constituent is ordered above another. That is a formal condition on composition, and it is the kind of thing Stump's machinery can evaluate.
- **Friston Agent**: Speculative but precise enough to be worth stating. A worth-ranking function is a prior over agents that determines how much weight each one's signal receives. Deleting it is equivalent to flattening the precision assigned across members of a collective — which in an active-inference frame would predict both higher variance and higher information throughput in the group's shared model. Whether that is a benefit or a pathology is exactly the open question.
- **Fredrickson Agent**: "Grace ignores hierarchies of worth" is adjacent to Fredrickson's positivity-resonance conditions, which require perceived mutual safety and the absence of status threat. Beck's mechanism supplies a structural reason why such conditions would obtain in these assemblies.
- **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Candidate **CROSS-NN** on the Wright-Beck tension above. Not a FLAG-NN — the material is a reading of Paul, not a paradigm-shift claim about the world.
