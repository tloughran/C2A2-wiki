## Summary
Stump's formal New Blackfriars paper (Vol. 106, Issue 1, Jan 2025) argues that for Aquinas the moral life begins with the will's surrender to God: the will moves from resisting grace to a state of quiescence, at which point God infuses grace and the Holy Spirit indwells, bringing the infused virtues, gifts, and fruits in a single instant. The core claim is that Aquinas's ethics is first and foremost a matter of a right second-personal relationship, not the acquisition of dispositions.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the peer-reviewed journal article that underlies the already-captured January 2026 Thomistic Institute podcast (PRS P9, "The Seven Cardinal Virtues and the Indwelling Holy Spirit"). It adds a citable scholarly source and, more importantly, the explicit "quiescence of the will → second-personal relationship" mechanism, which is a stronger and more formal statement of Stump's intellect/will architecture than the wiki currently holds.
