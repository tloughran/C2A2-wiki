## Summary
Ask NT Wright Anything episode (Premier Unbelievable, hosted by Mike Bird). Wright addresses whether Protestants "miss out" by misunderstanding the Orthodox tradition, whether the Greek Orthodox Church is "biblical," the theology of icons, and the identity of the "elect lady" of 2 John — alongside questions on judgment, holiness, and Christian witness. Wright's characteristic move is to relativize the Reformation-era Protestant/Orthodox framing by returning to the second-temple and New Testament context, treating icons, tradition, and church identity as questions to be adjudicated by the biblical narrative rather than by later confessional boundaries.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This extends Wright's ecclesiology-in-dialogue — how the "people of God" persists as a single tradition across the Orthodox/Protestant divide — and his reading of the Johannine letters (the "elect lady" of 2 John as a church addressed as a corporate person). Both bear on the network's interest in the church as a real corporate entity and on how a tradition transmits and adjudicates truth over time; neither is yet captured in the Wright wiki.
