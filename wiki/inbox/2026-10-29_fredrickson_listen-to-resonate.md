## Summary
Zhou & Fredrickson (*Current Opinion in Psychology* 53, 101669; DOI 10.1016/j.copsyc.2023.101669) propose that listening is an interpersonal route into positivity resonance. Positivity resonance is defined as shared positive emotion plus caring nonverbal synchrony and biological synchrony across people. Good listening works through two channels, real-time sensory connection (e.g. direct gaze) and perceived safety, and the review argues each is reciprocally linked to listening.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
