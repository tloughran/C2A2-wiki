## Summary
Carroll's Mindscape episode 351, published yesterday (2026-04-20), is a long-form conversation with Peter Singer covering metaethics (consequentialism vs. other frameworks), varieties of utilitarianism, the practical demands of ethics, animal rights, and end-of-life decisions. While Singer is the guest, Carroll's questions and reframings consistently reflect his own poetic-naturalism commitments — particularly the claim that a naturalist ontology can and must support robust normative structure, and the question of how welfare is individuated across sentient creatures.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Carroll's existing PRS triplets lean heavily on physics and emergence. This episode is one of the clearest recent examples of Carroll doing normative / ethical work *inside* his poetic-naturalism frame — exactly the territory where his program has been thinnest in the wiki. It also engages utilitarianism and animal ethics, which connect naturally to Levin's diverse-intelligence program (what counts as a subject of moral concern when agency is scalar and substrate-independent?).
