## Summary
Ask NT Wright Anything (Premier Unbelievable, with Mike Bird), three listener questions. (1) The creeds of the great councils remain important, but Scripture, not any theological system, keeps primary authority. (2) "The righteousness of God" carries a wide biblical meaning traced through Abraham, Phinehas, Romans and 2 Corinthians, and no single English translation captures all of it. (3) The Ascension should not be pictured as Jesus going "up above the clouds"; it points to the biblical joining of heaven and earth in Christ.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Item (2) is the core of Wright's covenant-faithfulness reading of *dikaiosynē theou*, which the wiki does not yet carry as a triplet. It is the load-bearing scriptural anchor for the Summa 2026 claim that salvation is inclusion in the covenanted people united with God. Item (1) states Wright's authority ordering (Scripture over system), useful whenever the network sets Thomist synthesis against biblical text. Item (3) overlaps approved PROP 2026-09-27 (ascension political theology); treat it as reinforcement, not a new triplet.

## Candidate PRS Triplets
PRS-CANDIDATE:
- P: What does Paul mean by "the righteousness of God," and does the usual forensic-only rendering lose content?
- R: Wright's covenant reading, traced via Abraham (Gen 15), Phinehas (Ps 106 / Num 25), Romans and 2 Cor 5:21.
- S: God's righteousness names God's faithfulness to the covenant promise to put the world right through Abraham's family; justification is God's declaration of membership in that family. No one English word carries the range.
- Confidence: Medium (the position is well established in Wright's books; this episode's details are known only from the episode description)
- Evidence: Episode description; *Paul and the Faithfulness of God* (2013) and *Justification* (2009) for the full argument.

PRS-CANDIDATE:
- P: How should a tradition weigh conciliar creeds against Scripture when they seem to diverge in emphasis?
- R: Wright's ordering: creeds as authoritative summaries, Scripture as primary authority.
- S: Creeds are kept as guardrails but are read back into the narrative they summarize, not the reverse.
- Confidence: Medium
- Evidence: Episode description, question 1.
