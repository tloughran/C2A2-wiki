## Summary
A Dutch magazine feature (VA Issue 3, 2026) translated into English and posted on McGilchrist's channel. It synthesizes his core thesis for a general readership: narrow-grasping (LH) vs open-broad (RH) attention, attention shaping perception (the "Gorillas in Our Midst" study), three Western flourishings each declining into LH dominance, and the claim that beauty, truth and goodness are discovered rather than invented and lose value when made explicit. Remedies are inner and communal — change of heart and mind, small committed communities, prayer/meditation — and hope is framed as a duty.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Mostly a consolidation, not a new advance; its value is the explicit pairing of "small committed communities" with "a few percent of humanity can start change" (Mead), which is the most direct McGilchrist statement yet of a community-scale remedy.
