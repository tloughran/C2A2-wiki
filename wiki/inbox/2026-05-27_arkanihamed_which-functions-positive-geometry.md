## Summary
Co-authored paper extending the positive-geometry program from polytope-like canonical
forms toward functions with non-trivial branch-cut structure — the analytic features that
characterize string amplitudes and other physically realistic S-matrix objects. The paper
asks the inverse question: rather than starting from a geometry and reading off a function,
which classes of physical functions are even candidates for a positive-geometry origin?

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the kind of move that determines whether the positive-geometry program scales from
toy models (amplituhedron, associahedron) to the actual analytic structures of real-world
QFT and string theory. If branch-cut-bearing functions admit positive geometries, the
program's claim to be a candidate for "post-spacetime physics" becomes substantially
sharper; if they don't, the program is bounded. Either resolution is high-value.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Whether positive geometry, as a candidate framework beyond spacetime/locality, can capture analytic structures (branch cuts) that physical amplitudes actually have.
  Resource: Criteria for "admissibility" — a precise mathematical characterization of which functions can be expressed as canonical forms of a positive geometry.
  Solution: A classification programme that lets practitioners check, for any candidate physical amplitude, whether a positive-geometry origin is in principle available.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Paper title and arXiv abstract framing the question as branch-cut → string-amplitude scope-extension.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: The amplituhedron program has been criticized as restricted to planar N=4 SYM — a beautiful but possibly idiosyncratic theory.
  Resource: Extending the admissibility analysis to string amplitudes (which have richer analytic structure than rational planar N=4 amplitudes).
  Solution: A bridge between the amplituhedron-style positive-geometry program and the broader S-matrix bootstrap / string-amplitude community.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Paper subtitle explicitly couples "branch cuts" to "string amplitudes."

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Wolfram:** Both Arkani-Hamed and Wolfram take spacetime to be derived from a more
  fundamental combinatorial/computational substrate. The positive-geometry route and the
  ruliad/hypergraph route are competing answers to the same family of questions about
  emergence of locality. Branch-cut-handling is a discriminating test case.
- **Carroll:** Carroll's Mad-Dog Everett (this same proposal batch, PROP-2026-05-27-005)
  takes Hilbert-space dynamics as fundamental. Positive geometry, by contrast, takes
  combinatorial geometry as fundamental and derives even Hilbert-space-like objects. The
  pair sets up a clean three-way contrast (Carroll / Arkani-Hamed / Wolfram) on what
  "fundamental" means.
