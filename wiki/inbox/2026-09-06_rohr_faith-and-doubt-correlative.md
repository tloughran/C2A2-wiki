## Summary
Opens the "Faith with Doubt" week (Sept 6-12), adapted from *Falling Upward* (2024 ed.), 70-71, and *The Naked Now*, 117. Rohr defines basic religious faith as a vote for coherence, purpose, benevolence and direction in the universe, and contrasts it with the Western drift toward faith as rational assent to propositions. Faith and doubt are called correlative: people of great faith suffer great doubt because they keep growing (Mother Teresa's decades of darkness as the example). The developmental claim is that certainty is a legitimate first-half-of-life survival strategy that must be given up for wisdom, which lives with mystery and, in living with it, partly resolves it. The diagnostic error named is making the object of faith ideas or doctrines rather than a person: faith is that Ultimate Reality is accessible and on our side, not that a dogma is true.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the sharpest recent statement of Rohr's account of what faith *is*, and the rohr wiki has no entry on it despite faith being the pairing point with Stump named in the wiki summary. It is directly citable for the Summa fidelity questions on faith (II-II Q.1-7), where Aquinas's faith-as-assent framing is exactly what Rohr is arguing against; the synthesis needs the contrast recorded by tradition path. It also connects the two-halves-of-life frame (Active Research Question 3) to an epistemic claim, which the wiki currently treats only developmentally.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-A:
  Label: faith-as-trust-in-coherence-not-assent
  Problem: If faith is assent to propositions, doubt is faith's contradictory, and any honest uncertainty counts as loss of faith.
  Resource: Rohr's redefinition of faith as trust that reality is coherent, purposeful, benevolent and going somewhere good, with a person rather than a doctrine as its object.
  Solution: Faith and doubt become correlative rather than contradictory: doubt is uncertainty about propositions, faith is trust in a direction, and the two can rise together.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Stated directly; sourced to *Falling Upward* 70-71 and *The Naked Now* 117.

PRS-CANDIDATE-B:
  Label: certainty-as-first-half-survival-strategy
  Problem: Developmental accounts of faith cannot say why early certainty is not simply a mistake to be corrected earlier.
  Resource: Rohr's claim that eliminating doubt is a good survival technique in the first half of life, that first-half worldviews are not true but are functional, and that elders are required to make the second half visible.
  Solution: Certainty is stage-appropriate rather than false-simpliciter: an agent without enough experience of wholeness cannot yet hold its parts, so the transition depends on testimony from those who have.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: The "control tower" and "without elders, a society perishes" paragraphs.
