## Summary
The July 4 weekly summary caps the "Everyone Is Chosen" week (June 28 - July 4) of the 2026 Good News for a Fractured World cycle. The week's argument, assembled from Rohr, Barbara Brown Taylor, Brian McLaren, and Diana Hayes: election is universalized without being evacuated — grace is "unaccountable gratuity" from God's side, chosenness is real but never exclusive, and (the load-bearing claim, repeated twice in Rohr's own words) belovedness only propagates through people who have first internalized it: "Only beloved people can pass on belovedness." The closing practice is Nouwen's Life of the Beloved passage on the blessed one who blesses — blessing as the natural transmission behavior of the internally blessed.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This week does conceptual work, not just devotion: it states a transmission model of love — belovedness spreads agent-to-agent and only through agents in whom it is already realized, never by curse, blame, or accusation. That is a testable propagation dynamic, and it is the mechanism-level complement to the Universal Christ claim already anchoring the rohr wiki (the pattern is universal; its communication is strictly local and embodied). Citable for Summa syntheses on grace, predestination (I Q.23), and charity's diffusion.

## Candidate PRS Triplets
PRS-CANDIDATE:
  Label: belovedness-transmission-model
  Problem: How does universal chosenness become effective in agents who have not experienced it — what is the transmission mechanism of grace between persons?
  Resource: Rohr's "only beloved people can pass on belovedness" + Nouwen's blessed-one-blesses dynamic (Life of the Beloved).
  Solution: Love propagates only through agents in whom it is internally realized; negative signals (curses, accusation, blame) transmit nothing constructive. Grace's universality is ontological; its propagation is strictly local, person-to-person.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: The Sunday and Thursday Rohr quotes anchoring the summary, plus the Nouwen practice text chosen as the week's capstone.
