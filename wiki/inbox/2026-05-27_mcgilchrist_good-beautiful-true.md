## Summary
Two-day in-person event at the Sheldonian Theatre in Oxford on May 2–3, 2026 in which McGilchrist delivered two new long-form lectures — "The Value of Value" and "So How Do We Remake the World (Because We Can)?" — alongside guest speakers Martin Shaw (mythography), Angie Hobbs (ancient Greek philosophy), Jude Currivan (cosmology), and David Lorimer (Scientific and Medical Network). The central thesis: the good, the beautiful, and the true are not optional cultural preferences but are woven into the fabric of the cosmos. Recordings are issued to in-person and livestream ticket holders a few weeks after the event; public availability date is not yet established but the event itself has occurred.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is McGilchrist's first sustained institutional positioning of his program at the Sheldonian — the same venue that hosts the Boyle Lectures — and it is co-authored with named guest speakers from theology, classics, cosmology, and the Scientific and Medical Network. That stages McGilchrist's program as an inter-tradition assembly in its own right, not just as a single-author thesis. The two new lectures are direct successors to "The value of value" Substack essay (PROP-2026-05-27-001) and the second lecture ("So How Do We Remake the World") is, on the available description, the program's most explicit attempt at a *prescriptive* outcome — the move from diagnosis (Master and Emissary, Matter With Things) to active reconstruction.
