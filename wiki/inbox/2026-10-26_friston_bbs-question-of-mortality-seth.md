## Summary
This is Friston's peer-reviewed open commentary in *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 49, e336 (doi 10.1017/S0140525X25103439; green open access via UCL Discovery). It responds to Anil Seth's target article "Conscious artificial intelligence and biological naturalism." Friston says Seth's piece left him "wondering whether artificial consciousness is an oxymoron." He uses the commentary to unpack the thermodynamic issues around *mortal computation*, meaning computation whose substrate has to keep itself in existence, and what follows for conscious artefacts. The reference list (Caticha on information geometry; Crooks on thermodynamic length) points to an information-geometric, thermodynamic argument.
