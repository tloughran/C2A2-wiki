## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Scale invariance is a basic test of the reference-frame thesis: if each column models objects in its own reference frame, it must be able to recognise "the same object, bigger" without relearning it. Hawkins' reframing toward deformation touches Active Research Question 8 (what happens to a model when its object changes).
