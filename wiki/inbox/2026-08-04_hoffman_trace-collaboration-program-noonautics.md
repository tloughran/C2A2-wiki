## Summary
The Trace Institute has published a dedicated page describing its **Collaboration Program** and its first output: a joint research program with Noonautics (led by neurobiologist Andrew Gallimore). The page lays out a division of labour that is new information relative to the preprint already in the wiki: Noonautics supplies the *empirical* arm (the extended-state DMTx protocol, sending trained scientific observers into hours-long rather than minutes-long DMT states to record structured phenomenological observations), and the Trace Institute supplies the *analytic* arm (trace logic for conscious observers plus the full conscious-agent formalism, used to build a quantitative interpretive framework). The stated joint deliverable is "clear predictions that can falsify, or lend credence to" the hypothesis under test.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The wiki already holds the Gallimore–Hoffman–Hermansson preprint (PROP-2026-06-23-001), but not this — which is a different kind of object. It is the tradition acquiring an **experimental apparatus and a partnership mechanism**, not another theoretical result. Conscious realism has been criticised for decades as untestable; here the program states a protocol, an observer corps, an analytic pipeline, and a falsification condition, and it does so through a repeatable institutional form ("the first collaboration to emerge from the Trace Institute Collaboration Program") that is explicitly designed to produce more such pairings. Per the tradition-scope note already recorded in this wiki (PROP-2026-05-05-005), TRACE-affiliated output counts as in-tradition rather than commentary.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Conscious realism predicts conscious agents of arbitrarily high complexity whose structure cannot be represented within our 3D-spacetime interface. Such agents are by construction imperceptible, which appears to make the prediction unfalsifiable in principle.
  Resource: The extended-state DMTx protocol (Noonautics) treated as a *pharmacological perturbation of the interface itself* — lengthening the altered state from minutes to hours so that trained observers can make repeated, structured observations rather than recall a brief episode.
  Solution: Converts an in-principle-imperceptible entity into a conditionally observable one: if the interface can be transiently retuned, agents outside its normal representational range may become perceivable, and the *structure* of what is reported can be tested against what conscious-agent dynamics predicts.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: "Noonautics… will deploy their extended-state DMT protocol, allowing experiences of minutes to hours, to send trained scientists and experts into the DMT state to record precise phenomenological observations" (Trace × Andrew Gallimore, traceinstitute.org).

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Phenomenological report is notoriously unusable as scientific data — it is unstructured, unreplicable across observers, and has no formal target to be checked against.
  Resource: Trace logic applied as the *analytic* layer over phenomenological data: "the Trace Institute… will apply the trace logic for conscious observers and the full mathematical apparatus of conscious agent theory to build a quantitative analytical framework capable of interpreting those observations."
  Solution: A two-arm design in which the mathematics is specified *before* the observations and generates the categories the observations are scored against — the standard remedy for unstructured report data, here applied to a domain where it has not previously been attempted.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: The stated joint aim of "the first rigorous, empirically testable model of the DMT experience, with clear predictions that can falsify, or lend credence to, the hypothesis."

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: How does a young research program with a heterodox metaphysics acquire empirical reach, given that it has no laboratory of its own and its claims cut across established disciplinary funding lines?
  Resource: The Trace Institute Collaboration Program — a standing mechanism for pairing the Institute's formal apparatus with an outside group's experimental capability, of which Trace × Noonautics is the first instance.
  Solution: Theory-side institutions can scale empirically by *renting* experimental arms through formalised partnership rather than building them; the mechanism is reusable, and the Institute has publicised it as such.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: "It marked the first collaboration to emerge from the Trace Institute Collaboration Program" (Traces of the Other event page, traceinstitute.org).

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Kastrup (flagged explicitly, per agent brief):** this is the sharpest available test case for the Hoffman↔Kastrup relation. Both hold consciousness fundamental, but Kastrup's analytic idealism treats altered states as *dissociative boundary* phenomena within one universal mind, while Hoffman's framework treats them as *interface retuning* revealing genuinely other agents. The same DMT data would be read incompatibly by the two traditions — a rare instance where two allied idealisms make divergent empirical predictions. High-value bridge/conflict candidate.
- **Levin:** the conscious-agents-of-arbitrary-complexity claim is the metaphysical twin of Levin's goal-directedness at unfamiliar scales; both traditions assert agency where our default perceptual categories deny it, and both are now reaching for protocols that would make that agency detectable.
- **Carroll / Arkani-Hamed:** unchanged from prior entries — the "spacetime is not fundamental" commitment is the shared ground, but this proposal adds nothing new on that axis and should not be double-counted.
- **Friston:** the interface-retuning hypothesis is expressible as a transient change in the generative model's priors; whether that reframing is a genuine bridge or a category error is itself a good inter-tradition question.
- **Methodological caution for the reviewer:** the falsification conditions are announced, not yet specified in this source. Confidence on PRS-01 and PRS-02 should not be raised above Medium until the preregistered predictions are published.
