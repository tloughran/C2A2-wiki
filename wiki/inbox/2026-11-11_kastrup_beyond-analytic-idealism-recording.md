## Summary
This is the recording page for the 13 October 2026 With Reality in Mind session (2h 13m). The wiki already holds that session as PROP-2026-10-14-001 ("The Great Contender"). That card came from the **pre-session announcement** and carried an explicit caveat that what Kastrup actually said was unverified. This page has since been published with **direct Kastrup quotations** and a full 35-chapter timestamp list. It turns several of the earlier card's Speculative claims into reported ones and adds new content. Kastrup names what could falsify analytic idealism (for example "seeing without eyes"). He says that if naturalism falls, logic is "the next domino to fall". He keeps dualism "on the table". He projects analytic idealism forward by joining it with IIT (integrated information theory) and Markov blankets.

Key quotes (host's write-up, quoting Kastrup):
- "I think today, analytic philosophy is the pointing finger, and I think there is no other pointing finger. That's the only one that has a chance to take us out of the madness of physicalism."
- "My education tells me we are going back. But my maturity ... whispers in my ear like a little demon that it may be the next turn of the spiral ... because the basis of our analytic reasoning are so tenuous."
- "Analytic idealism has just been started. It's not under my control, thank goodness, because it means it will continue if I'm not here anymore."

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
In C2A2 terms this is Kastrup publicly turning his position into a **research program** (Levin/Lakatos sense). He states defeaters ("could analytic idealism survive seeing without eyes?"). He separates "deepening analytic idealism" from "moving beyond it". He hands the program on beyond himself. A tradition that names its own falsifiers and its successor directions is easier to compare with rival traditions.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: What empirical findings would force analytic idealism to be abandoned, and how much of its value would survive if they did?
  Resource: Kastrup's distinction between a theory as destination and a theory as "pointing finger", plus named candidate defeaters (seeing without eyes, and anomalies of the UAP/abduction type) that would undercut naturalism itself
  Solution: Hold analytic idealism as the only current route out of physicalism while stating openly that it is provisional. A theory that is later discarded still keeps its value if it pointed in the right direction ("the value of a theory we may eventually discard", ch. 2:08:59)
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Direct quotes above; chapters 1:01:48 "Could analytic idealism survive seeing without eyes?" and 2:08:59.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: If the evidence ever forces us to give up naturalism, what goes with it?
  Resource: The argument that logic is empirically grounded (ch. 1:04:50, "Is logic empirical?"), so that losing naturalism makes "maths, science and logic ... tenuous guides to reality". Kastrup links this to Zen
  Solution: Kastrup openly leaves it undecided whether a post-naturalist turn would be a regression or "the next turn of the spiral". He reports a tension between his formal training and his "maturity"
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: "the next domino to fall is logic"; the "little demon" quote; the bullet "Why losing naturalism means maths, science and logic become tenuous guides to reality".

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: Analytic idealism explains dissociative boundaries only qualitatively. It needs formal machinery to individuate alters and explain life.
  Resource: Integrated Information Theory together with Markov blankets
  Solution: Make analytic idealism "increasingly specific and sophisticated" by joining IIT and Markov blankets to it, so that dissociative boundaries, and life itself, get a formal account
  Confidence: Medium (the host's paraphrase of a stated hope; it repeats the direction of an existing PRS around line 639 of prs_triplets)
  Evidence: "perhaps joining with integrated information theory and the concept of Markov Blankets to explain dissociative boundaries and even life."

PRS-CANDIDATE-04:
  Problem: Can analytic idealism hold a metaconscious "mind at large", or God, without breaking naturalism?
  Resource: Chapters 1:41:19–1:56:19: a metaconscious mind at large; grace and spontaneity; "making room for the daimon"; dissociation as cosmic self-discovery; "purpose does not require metacognition"
  Solution: Keep teleology ("purpose") separate from metacognition, so that nature can have a purpose without a self-reflective God. Leave room for a daimon or grace as spontaneity
  Confidence: Speculative (chapter titles only, no quotes)
  Evidence: Chapter list; connects to *The Daimon and the Soul of the West* (2025) and the Hecht problem-of-evil card (2026-09-09).
