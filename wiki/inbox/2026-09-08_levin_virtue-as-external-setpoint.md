## The recasting

Virtues recast as embedded patterns of response driving activity toward goals
**stored outside the agent's cognitive light cone** and imparted by signalling
from an agent who holds them — with a correlative internal disposition toward
that external source as toward a beneficent provider of direction.

**Terminological flag, load-bearing.** Levin's cognitive light cone is the
spatiotemporal scale of goals a system can pursue and represent; it does *not*
require awareness. On Levin's own usage a bioelectrically stored target is
*inside* the collective's light cone. The recasting above uses a stricter,
awareness-indexed reading, under which the source of the goal is external. This
is a stipulation and must be declared, or the whole construction is read as a
misuse of Levin's term. The stipulation is what generates the theological
structure: a goal operative in the agent whose source is not cognitively
available to her.

## What it already is in the tradition

- **Aristotle**, NE VI.12 (1144a7–9): virtue makes the target right, phronesis
  makes what leads to it right. The end is set by character, formed by
  habituation before the agent could endorse it (NE I.4, 1095b). The Aristotelian
  *skopos* is already a stored setpoint the agent tracks without deliberative
  access to its installation.
- **Aquinas**, ST I-II q.68: the gifts of the Holy Spirit are dispositions by
  which a person is made readily movable by divine inspiration — needed because
  reason's own rule is insufficient for an end exceeding reason. The gifts are
  inseparable from charity (q.68 a.5). *Docilitas*, teachability, is a part of
  prudence (II-II q.49 a.3).

The recasting therefore does not overturn either. Its claim to novelty rests on
what follows.

## What Levin adds

1. **A manipulable substrate.** Transient disruption of gap-junctional signalling
   in regenerating planaria yields two-headed animals that regenerate two-headed
   in later rounds with the genome untouched. The target state is stored
   somewhere other than the code and can be rewritten from outside.
2. **A gradient where the tradition has a binary.** Acquired vs. infused becomes
   "where is the setpoint stored, and who has write access" — continuous and
   empirically investigable.
3. **Participation without representation.** No single cell represents the
   morphology the collective pursues. Applied to virtue: some goods are stored in
   the practice, not the practitioner, and are tracked by coupling rather than by
   representation. This is a mechanistic model of apprenticeship, and it supplies
   what DRA most conspicuously lacks — how a good held by a network becomes
   operative in a member.

## The three moments (revises the static-table framing)

1. **Reception.** A setpoint is written from outside the light cone —
   habituation, instruction, infusion, bioelectric signal. No merit, no choice.
2. **Ratification or refusal.** The agent turns on the source and judges it,
   using (a) reflection on the history of acquisition and (b) comparison with the
   aspirational states of other persons. Wickedness gets its *inside* description
   here: not "having wrong ends" but never having made the turn, or having made
   it and gone back.
3. **Authorship.** The ratified end is the agent's own; she is now a source, and
   can write to others' setpoints.

**Loughran, 2026-09-08, on the relation of 2 to 3:** moment 2 is extended in
time as meaning-of-life deliberation. Ignatian special moments occur but tend to
dissolve into, and be supported by, ordinary deliberation. The process typically
takes years. So 2 and 3 are not the same act, but 2 *matures into* 3 — as
continuous worship from free creatures matures into confirmation of the good for
St. Thomas. Diachronic convergence, not identity.

## Candidate finding (the falsifiable part)

**Rich cross-tradition exposure raises the rate at which agents revise their
ends.** Moment 2's revision mechanism — comparison with the aspirational states
of other persons — is intermittent, because its MacIntyrean trigger is
epistemological crisis and a crisis cannot be scheduled. Whether exposure raises
the revision rate is an empirical question, and instrumenting it is the
detector's proper job. If C2A2 can register revision events in a corpus, this is
measurable.

Where to look for confirmation or challenge, per Loughran: the psychometrics of
moral development. Starting points — Kohlberg and the neo-Kohlbergian line
(Rest's Defining Issues Test), identity-status research after Marcia
(moratorium/achievement), and quest religious orientation after Batson. **Not
verified for currency; treat as leads, not as a literature review.**

## Candidate bridge (rated above homology)

**A distributively held setpoint is genuinely in the agent and genuinely not only
in the agent, because the collective that holds it includes her.** If this holds,
the input/output complementarity — grace received, love authored — does not
require the temporal/eternal move to dissolve. It is what participation in a
distributed target *is*, described mechanistically. Aquinas's grace-as-
participation (I-II q.110) and MacIntyre's goods-internal-to-a-practice would
then be instances at different scales of one structure. This is the strongest
claim in the note and the one most worth attacking.

## Open problems

- **OPEN — normativity.** Levin's setpoints are descriptive. An induced
  two-headed target is a stored goal; cancer is reversion to a smaller light
  cone. The recasting describes vice exactly as well as virtue and must import
  its normative source from outside Levin (ergon and eudaimonia, or the ultimate
  end), or it is a theory of habit in virtue's clothing.
- **OPEN — bootstrapping.** Judging whether one's source is a tyrant requires
  standards that came from the source. The reflexive criterion is retrospective:
  available to one who already holds the right answer. MacIntyre's reply (crisis
  plus an encounterable rival with superior resources) converts this from a
  philosophical problem into an engineering problem about the availability of
  rivals — which is what C2A2 is for. Converted, not solved.
- **OPEN — comparison cuts both ways.** Exposure to rival aspirational states is
  the mechanism of conversion in both directions. Moment 2 is not
  self-certifying.
- **CAUTION — the modalist edge.** Language putting the agent in the Word's
  position ("we are the source"; "without us is not anything made") requires the
  secondary-cause qualifier to stay explicit, or the objection becomes the only
  thing discussed. Loughran's own compatibilism is the instrument here.

## New vice pair

Where the measure of a norm lies outside the agent, excess and deficiency are
replaced by **closure** (refusing a good source) and **captivity** (opening to a
bad one) — a two-sided failure of discrimination about sources rather than a
quantity of response. This is why the doctrine of the mean does not reach there,
and it predicts what to look for in the untabulated regions of Aquinas's and
MacIntyre's tables.
