## Summary
In the 4 May 2026 episode of *Ask NT Wright Anything* (with Mike Bird, Premier Unbelievable), Wright addresses the "lost" northern tribes of Israel, the theological meaning of exile, and how the New Testament reframes Israel's restoration — alongside whether Christians should read the Hebrew Bible or the Septuagint, and whether penal substitution reflects the biblical story. Wright's through-line: exile-and-return is the master-narrative of Israel's scriptures, and the NT presents Jesus as enacting the true end-of-exile and restoration of the people of God.
