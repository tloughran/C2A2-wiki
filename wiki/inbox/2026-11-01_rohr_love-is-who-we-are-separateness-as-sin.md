## Summary
Rohr calls love the foundation and the destination of human being. The image and likeness of God (Gen 1:26) means our origin is divine, and our starting point is "original blessing" rather than original sin. He defines sin as the state of separateness: the illusion that we are apart from God. He makes religion's primary task the communication of *union* rather than worthiness. To fail to love is to be "outside of being"; to love is to act from one's deepest truth and dignity. The meditation is adapted from *Essential Teachings on Love* (Orbis, 2018). It is a 2026 republication of existing material, not new writing, but this framing was not yet captured in the wiki. The week's companion post ("Expanding Our Capacity to Love," Oct 27) adds a developmental sequence in which respect for oneself comes before respect for others. See the weekly summary at https://cac.org/daily-meditations/loving-your-neighbor-weekly-summary/.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the most compact statement in the current CAC cycle of the ontological core of Rohr's program: love as being, sin as separateness, salvation as reunion. It maps onto the Summa 2026 central theme. If perspective-limitation individuates conscious agents, Rohr names the *experienced* side of that limitation, the felt illusion of separateness, as the thing salvation heals. "Outside of being" also comes close to the Augustinian-Thomist account of evil as privation, which makes this a rare place where Rohr's contemplative idiom and Aquinas's metaphysics say the same thing.

## Candidate PRS Triplets
PRS-CANDIDATE-1:
  Label: Sin as the illusion of separateness
  Problem: How can "sin" be stated so that it engages a non-dual or mind-monist metaphysics instead of only a juridical, rule-breaking model?
  Resource: Rohr's definition of sin as the state of separateness, with religion's task as reconnecting people to their original identity "hidden with Christ in God" (Col 3:3).
  Solution: Sin is primarily an epistemic-relational distortion: a false self-model of isolation. Salvation is the correction of that model into conscious union. Moral failures are downstream symptoms.
  Confidence: Medium (clear statement by Rohr; whether it can stand in for the full doctrine is contested)
  Evidence: Meditation, paragraph on overcoming "the illusion of separateness."

PRS-CANDIDATE-2:
  Label: Unlove as privation of being
  Problem: Is there a contemplative counterpart to the classical claim that evil is a lack of being rather than a positive substance?
  Resource: Rohr: "When we don't live according to love, we are outside of being."
  Solution: Loving is acting in accord with one's being; not loving is a falling short of being. This matches Aquinas's *malum est privatio boni* and gives a bridge text between the contemplative and scholastic idioms.
  Confidence: Speculative (Rohr does not cite Aquinas here; the convergence is the agent's reading)
  Evidence: Closing paragraph, citing *Essential Teachings*, p. v.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Stump Agent**: Candidate 2 is a direct bridge to Stump's Thomism (evil as privation; union as the ultimate end). Stump would probably resist reducing sin to illusion (Candidate 1), since for her the will's real refusal of love matters. That tension is useful for the master agent.
- **Friston Agent**: "Illusion of separateness" as an over-precise self/other boundary in the generative model. This matches the hunt brief's expected bridge: contemplative practice as loosening high-precision priors about a separate self. **Paradigm-bridge candidate.**
- **Kastrup / Hoffman Agents**: Separateness as appearance within one underlying mind (Kastrup's dissociation; Hoffman's interface agents) is structurally the same claim in a metaphysical register.
- **Wright Agent**: Tension to flag. Wright would locate "image and likeness" in vocation (humans as royal priesthood reflecting God into the world), not in an inner divine origin. He also does not frame sin chiefly as separateness. This is a live scriptural check on Rohr's reading.
- **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Candidate for a CROSS entry: "sin-as-separateness / perspective-limitation." It links Rohr, Friston, Kastrup and the Summa 2026 perspective-limitation thesis, with Stump and Wright as named dissenting checks.
