## Summary
A Perspective article in *Nature Reviews Physics* by Luppi, Rosas, Mediano, Boyd, Berta, Jensen, Gastpar and Carroll. It argues that "information" is used in physics and the natural sciences in several distinct technical senses that are often not told apart. It proposes a pluralist view with four "faces": engineering, statistical, thermodynamic and ontological. Each face has its own uses and limits, and none should be reduced to the others.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is a pluralism-of-descriptions move applied to the concept of information itself. That is the same structure as poetic naturalism: several vocabularies, each valid within its domain, and no forced reduction. It is also a published, peer-reviewed counterpart to Carroll's ongoing Mindscape interest in information, emergence and complexity (see the Biggest Ideas Vol. 3 proposal, 2026-09-04).

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: "Information" is used so widely across physics and natural science that it is unclear whether one coherent concept underlies all of these uses. Conflating its senses leads to misinterpretation.
  Resource: A taxonomy of four technical senses of information: engineering (Shannon/communication), statistical (inference), thermodynamic (entropy/work), and ontological (information as constitutive of reality).
  Solution: Adopt a pluralist stance. Disambiguate which face is in use and the scope over which it applies, rather than seeking one monolithic definition.
  Confidence: High (stated directly in the abstract)
  Evidence: The abstract says it "put[s] forward a pluralistic approach towards information, wherein multiple technical interpretations coexist."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: How can a physicalist who believes everything supervenes on quantum fields still treat information-talk in biology and cognition as real rather than metaphorical?
  Resource: The "complementarity of distinct, yet equally valid applications of an information lens for natural systems" (abstract).
  Solution: Treat the faces of information as level- and purpose-relative vocabularies, which is the poetic-naturalist template. This mapping is the proposer's reading; the abstract does not mention poetic naturalism.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Abstract only. The body needs to be checked.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Wolfram / Kastrup / Hoffman:** The "ontological face" (information as fundamental) is exactly the claim these programs make in different forms: the Ruliad, analytic idealism, and conscious agents. Carroll co-signing a paper that names it as one face among four, not the master concept, is a useful point of contrast for the C2A2 master wiki.
- **Friston:** The statistical and thermodynamic faces are the two senses that the free energy principle deliberately fuses. The paper's call to keep them disambiguated is a direct challenge for FEP to answer.
- **Levin:** Basal cognition relies heavily on the engineering and statistical faces. The taxonomy could sharpen what "information processing" means in bioelectric claims.
