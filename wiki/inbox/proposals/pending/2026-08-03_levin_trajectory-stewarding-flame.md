## Summary
84-minute interview launching Dan Faggella's *Stewarding the Flame* series on The Trajectory. Levin makes his strongest public statement yet of the containment ordering between cognition, physics, and life: cognition is the largest category, with physical systems and living systems nested inside it — "I think cognition is the biggest circle, and I think the space of physical things is within that and the space of living things is within that." He then names two research priorities for understanding the "flame" that distinguishes life from non-life, and argues that regenerative medicine leads unavoidably to "freedom of embodiment."

Full episode: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rcZsGNYjH14 (host's write-up with verbatim quotes fetched and used as the source for the extractions below; the transcript itself was not read).

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This inverts the standard emergence story that the wiki has recorded for Levin so far. Previously the program's claim was that cognition does not require neurons; here the claim is that cognition does not require *life* — biology is one especially good interface for hosting cognitive patterns, not their origin. That is a stronger metaphysical commitment and puts Levin's "Platonic space" line and the Kastrup/idealism traditions on the same board.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Standard science treats cognition as something that emerges once biological complexity is high enough, which leaves no principled way to ask about mind in physical or synthetic systems.
  Resource: A containment ordering — cognition as the outermost set, with physical systems and living systems as nested subsets — plus the framing of biology as an unusually capable *interface* for hosting cognitive patterns at multiple scales simultaneously.
  Solution: Reverses the explanatory direction: life does not produce cognition; life is one architecture that hosts and expresses it particularly well, so the question becomes which interfaces host which patterns.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Direct quote — "I think cognition is the biggest circle, and I think the space of physical things is within that and the space of living things is within that."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: We cannot recognize or communicate with minds in unconventional embodiments — Levin's "mind blindness" — and we have no taxonomy of patterns or of the interfaces that host them.
  Resource: Two named research priorities: (a) tools for recognizing and communicating with unconventional intelligences, (b) a taxonomy of patterns and their pattern-interface relations.
  Solution: Converts a metaphysical worry into a concrete research program with two deliverables; Levin makes these the prerequisites for bringing "more creativity, more life" into the world in the non-biological sense.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: "I think our mind blindness is pretty severe for sure. I think we have a really hard time detecting these things."

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: Personal and organismal identity is usually tied to material continuity, which regenerative medicine and prospective augmentation break.
  Resource: Target morphology as pattern memory held in the cell collective rather than in any persisting hardware (an explicit Ship-of-Theseus framing).
  Solution: "Freedom of embodiment" — continuity is carried by the organizing pattern, so the same advances that cure birth defects, injury, and cancer necessarily open up new embodiments; the two cannot be separated.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: "Your actual target morphology is the pattern memory in the collective of cells. And the hardware comes and goes." / "The path forward to solving birth defects, injury, cancer leads directly to freedom of embodiment. There's no way to separate it."

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Kastrup (strong):** cognition-as-outermost-set is structurally very close to idealist priority of mind over matter, arrived at from the biology side rather than the metaphysics side. This is the sharpest Levin–Kastrup convergence the wiki has logged; flag as a paradigm-shift candidate, but note the difference — Levin's "cognition" is functional/goal-directed, not necessarily phenomenal.
- **Hoffman:** if physical systems sit inside the cognitive set, spacetime-as-interface and biology-as-interface are the same move at different scales.
- **Stump / Aquinas:** pattern memory as the bearer of organismal continuity across total material turnover is a hylomorphic form claim in contemporary dress — form as the principle of identity through material change.
- **Wolfram:** "taxonomy of patterns and their interfaces" is a request for something like a rulial classification of what can host what.
- **C2A2/Loughran:** "we cannot steward what we do not understand" and the flame/torch distinction map onto the tradition-accelerator's premise that mature participation in a tradition must be achievable before traditions can be responsibly compared.
