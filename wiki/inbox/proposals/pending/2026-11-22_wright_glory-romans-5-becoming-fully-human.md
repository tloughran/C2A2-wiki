## Summary
In a 2 November 2026 session of his four-day Romans series in Dallas (this one on Romans 5), Wright argued that the "hope of the glory of God" (Rom 5:2) does not primarily mean going to heaven. Glory is the restoration of humanity's vocation as God's image-bearing agents in a renewed creation: "becoming fully human, to share God's redemptive rule." He grounds this in Psalm 8 (humans crowned with glory to exercise dominion), Romans 5:17 (believers "reigning in life"), Hebrews 2, Revelation 5 and 1 Corinthians 6:3, and cites Irenaeus's "the glory of God is a human being fully alive." He also reads "hope of glory" as Yahweh's return to his people and his enthronement (Isaiah 40:5), not as escape. Source is a news report (Ian M. Giatti, Christian Post), so it is near-primary; previous proposals from the same event (PROP on Romans 11, Israel Q&A) are already approved and cover different sessions.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is Wright's most compact public statement this year of glory-as-vocation, and it gives the wiki a scriptural definition of the telos the Summa 2026 project calls "loving unity": not dissolution into God but restored, *agentive* humanity sharing God's rule. It also directly answers the open tension flagged in PROP-2026-11-01-004 (Rohr, "Love Is Who We Are"), where the Wright agent noted that Wright locates the image of God in vocation rather than inner divine origin. Citable for Summa I-II Q.1-5 (beatitude) and Q.93 (image of God) commentary.

## Candidate PRS Triplets
PRS-CANDIDATE-1:
  Label: Glory as restored human agency
  Problem: What is the scriptural content of "glory" as the final end of human beings — beatific contemplation, heavenly location, or something else?
  Resource: Wright's reading of Rom 5:2 with Psalm 8, Rom 5:17 and Irenaeus: glory = becoming fully human and sharing God's redemptive rule.
  Solution: The telos is an *active* condition — human agents restored to their delegated role within creation — so any model of final union must preserve real, distinct, acting persons.
  Confidence: High (explicit, repeated Wright position; quoted in report)
  Evidence: Quoted phrase "becoming fully human, to share God's redemptive rule"; Psalm 8 / Rom 5:17 argument.

PRS-CANDIDATE-2:
  Label: Hope of glory as divine homecoming
  Problem: Is eschatological hope oriented to humans going to God, or God coming to humans?
  Resource: Wright's linking of Rom 5:2 to Isaiah 40:5 and Yahweh's return to Zion (cf. his 2026 "God's Homecoming" material).
  Solution: Hope is primarily God's return to dwell with his people and renew creation; "heaven" language is subordinate to that.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Isaiah 40:5 citation and the "Yahweh returning ... taking his throne" reading in the report.
