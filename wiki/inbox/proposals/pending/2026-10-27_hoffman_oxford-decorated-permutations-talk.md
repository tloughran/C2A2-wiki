## Summary
Oxford Philosophy talk abstract: Hoffman argues physicists reject fundamental spacetime, points to positive Grassmannians and amplituhedra as simpler structures, and presents conscious agents beyond spacetime whose dynamics project onto decorated permutations. This gives a dynamical reading of amplituhedra and lets agents construct spacetime as an interface. He also asks whether evolutionary dynamics could be a projection of agent dynamics. Only the abstract was read; no recording or text of the talk was found.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Decorated permutations are the bridge to the Arkani-Hamed program. The wiki already records decorated permutations (via Prentner and the Trace Institute material), so the novelty is the final question about evolutionary dynamics as a projection of agent dynamics, plus a dated, citable venue.
