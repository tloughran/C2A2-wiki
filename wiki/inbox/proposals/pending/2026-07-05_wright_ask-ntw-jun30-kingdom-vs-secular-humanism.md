## Summary
In this Ask NT Wright Anything episode (released ~June 30, 2026; date approximate from feed), Wright and Mike Bird take a listener question that cuts close to the bone: is "building God's kingdom" functionally the same project as secular humanism? Wright argues no — Christian hope differs from secular progress in both source and shape: the kingdom is God's act, inaugurated in resurrection and received as gift, in which humans participate rather than a human construction project extrapolated from present trends. The episode also covers Trinitarian prayer (whether prayer may be addressed to the Spirit) and practical advice on recovering biblical Greek.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The kingdom/secular-progress distinction is the live boundary question for any project (like C2A2) that translates theological claims into scientific frames: what survives the translation, and what is lost if "loving unity as computational telos" is read as mere progressivism? Wright supplies the scriptural ground for keeping gift-receivedness and cross-shape in the frame — the telos is not derivable from within the system's own optimization. Not yet captured in the wright wiki; citable for Summa syntheses on hope, providence, and the theological virtues.
