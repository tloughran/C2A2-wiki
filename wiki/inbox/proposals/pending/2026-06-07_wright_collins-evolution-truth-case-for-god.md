## Summary
The released recording of the Wright–Collins dialogue (hosted by Oxford Pastorate and BioLogos, distributed via Premier Unbelievable?) pairs the project's scriptural oracle with the geneticist who led the Human Genome Project. Across evolution, truth, trust, and evidence, the two argue that rigorous natural science and a robust case for God are not rivals but complementary readings of one reality: Collins supplies the evolutionary-biological account of how life's order arose, Wright supplies the new-creation frame within which that order is neither accident nor closed mechanism but the early movement of a story still unfolding. The shared thesis is that "truth" is not partitioned into a scientific compartment and a religious one — knowing the world well and knowing God are continuous acts.
