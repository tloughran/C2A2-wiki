## Summary
Wright contributes a chapter on the Hebrew *ruach* in the Psalter to a 2026 edited volume from Pusey House (James Clarke & Co), itself a sequel to the 2022 Pusey House conference proceedings on the third article of the creed — the communion of the Holy Spirit. The collection crystallises around the centrality of the sacraments, the necessity of yielding any possession of the Spirit, and the recognition of Christ *in* the Holy Spirit. Wright's chapter argues that *ruach* in the Psalter functions simultaneously as cosmic presence (the wind/breath that orders creation in Psalm 33, 104, 147) and personal presence (the breath that animates and indwells the individual in Psalm 51, 139, 143), and that the Christian doctrine of the Spirit inherits both registers integrally — the same Spirit that pervades creation is the Spirit that dwells in the believer. Reviewed in the Church Times (13 March 2026); Wright's chapter is one of several from a Pusey House register that includes contributors from the broader Anglican-catholic theological community.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Wright on pneumatology is rare and load-bearing for the network. His Christology, soteriology, and eschatology have all been extensively articulated; his pneumatology has been more dispersed (sections of *Paul and the Faithfulness of God*, scattered essays). This chapter is the first 2026 consolidated piece on the *ruach* doctrine in his own voice, and it sets up the cosmic-and-personal *both-and* that the C2A2 network's mind-monist members (Kastrup, Hoffman, Levin) need a scriptural pair for. It also supplies the Summa 2026 work on the Tertia Pars (sacraments, esp. confirmation and the indwelling Spirit) with a primary Wright text. Currently the wiki has Wright's Christology and eschatology well represented; pneumatology is the gap this fills.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: The Christian doctrine of the Holy Spirit is routinely fractured by modern interpreters into either a cosmic-immanence reading (Spirit as the world-soul of natural theology) or a personal-indwelling reading (Spirit as the gift to the individual believer). The Hebrew Psalter, which is the actual textual ground of both, doesn't make this split.
  Resource: Wright's reading of *ruach* in the Psalter as simultaneously cosmic and personal — one Spirit, two registers, no metaphysical seam. Critical-realist exegesis applied to the pneumatology texts that the Christian tradition has tended to read with one eye closed.
  Solution: A scripturally-grounded pneumatology that natively integrates the cosmic-immanence and personal-indwelling readings. The C2A2 syntheses can use this whenever a mind-monist claim (Kastrup's universal mind, Hoffman's conscious agents, Levin's nested cognitive light cones) is being paired with a scriptural pneumatology claim — no need to choose between cosmic and personal.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Wright's chapter content; broader Wright corpus on the Spirit (*Paul and the Faithfulness of God*, *Into the Heart of Romans*); the Pusey House volume's coherent framing.
