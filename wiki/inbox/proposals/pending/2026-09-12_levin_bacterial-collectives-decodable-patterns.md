## Summary

Sergeeva, Pigozzi, Varley, Comerci, Zhao, Brucker, Süel, Bongard and Levin show that
motile *Bacillus subtilis* populations reorganise their spatial and ionic collective
states in response to nearby *Xenopus* embryos and Xenobots. Bacteria in liquid culture
form autonomous, motility-dependent patterns; a living target redirects those patterns
into "attraction halos" that track the target's position **at a distance**. Extracellular
potassium amplifies the attraction, alters local potassium dynamics, and couples target
presence to the global complexity of the pattern.

**Provenance note.** Bibliographic metadata verified against Levin's own preprint index
(drmichaellevin.org, 2026-09-12); DOI 10.64898/2026.09.02.748853. The summary above is
from the bioRxiv abstract. Full text not read; no figure or statistic is quoted.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition

This is the clearest instance yet in Levin's corpus of one collective *reading* another
across a gap, with no contact and no shared genome — and reading it into a form the
experimenters could decode. The wiki's Levin material has plenty on bioelectric setpoints
held within a collective (planarian target morphology, FLIM patterning, xenobots) but
little on information crossing a species boundary in a substrate-independent channel. The
potassium dependence matters because it names the channel: this is the same ionic
vocabulary Levin uses inside tissue, now doing work between organisms.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Whether the presence of a multicellular system can be encoded into a separate
    living collective's emergent spatial organisation in a distributed, decodable way was
    an open question — the existing bioelectric work is all within a single collective.
  Resource: A two-collective assay — motile *B. subtilis* in liquid culture next to
    *Xenopus* embryos or Xenobots — plus manipulation of extracellular potassium as the
    candidate channel.
  Solution: The bacterial collective forms attraction halos that track target position at
    a distance, and potassium amplifies attraction and couples target presence to global
    pattern complexity. Presence is encoded distributedly and can be read back out.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Abstract — bacteria "reorganize their spatial and ionic collective states in
    response to nearby Xenopus embryos and Xenobots"; the autonomous motility-dependent
    patterns "were redirected by living targets into attraction halos which tracked target
    position at a distance"; extracellular potassium "amplified attraction, altered local
    potassium dynamics, and coupled target presence to global pattern complexity."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Levin's "cognitive light cone" is defined for a single agent or collective; it
    says nothing about what one collective can represent about another.
  Resource: A distributed, decodable representation of an external living system held in
    a bacterial population's pattern statistics rather than in any member.
  Solution: A candidate operationalisation of representation-without-representer at the
    inter-collective scale — the pattern is about the target, no cell is.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Inference from the abstract's "distributed and decodable" framing. The paper
    is not claimed to make this conceptual move; it is the wiki's extension, and should be
    checked against the full text before ingest.

## Cross-Tradition Signals

- **levin x hoffman.** A decodable pattern that is *about* a distant object without
  resembling it is close to Hoffman's interface framing — the halo is an interface icon,
  not a picture of the embryo. Whether that is a real connection or a pun turns on whether
  the encoding is fitness-indexed; not determinable from the abstract.
- **levin x friston.** A collective reorganising its state to track an external body at a
  distance is a textbook candidate for a Markov blanket with a non-trivial sensory
  surface. `synthesis/friston_levin_bridge.md` already exists and is the right home.
- **levin x macintyre (weaker).** Participation-without-representation (macintyre PRS-06)
  was imported from Levin's *intra*-collective work. If it also holds between collectives,
  the apprenticeship model gains a second, independent instance — which is exactly the
  kind of confirmation that note asked for.
