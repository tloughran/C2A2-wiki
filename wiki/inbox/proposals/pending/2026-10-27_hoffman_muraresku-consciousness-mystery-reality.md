## Summary
A public conversation between Donald Hoffman and Brian Muraresku (author of *The Immortality Key*), held Monday, October 5, 2026 in Austin, Texas, and listed by the Trace Institute as a past event. The Institute's own framing pairs Muraresku's question (were the ancient mystery traditions, from Eleusis onward, pointing at experiences that challenged ordinary reality?) with Trace's program of a mathematical framework in which consciousness may be fundamental and spacetime emergent: "From Eleusis to Trace, the question is strikingly continuous."

**Evidence limit (read before approving):** only the event page exists. No recording, transcript, or press account was found on 2026-10-27 (the Trace podcast page lists only Episode 1; a web search for the Austin conversation returned nothing). Everything below is drawn from the Institute's framing text, not from what Hoffman said on stage. This parallels PROP-2026-09-22-003 (Hopewell, event-page-only).

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It is the first time the Trace program has publicly framed itself as continuous with a religious/initiatory tradition rather than with physics or psychedelic science alone, which widens its outreach lineage from Gallimore (DMT) to historical mystery religion.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: How does a mathematical science of conscious agents relate to pre-scientific traditions that also claimed ordinary reality conceals something deeper?
  Resource: The Institute's stated thesis of continuity, "From Eleusis to Trace," staged as a Hoffman-Muraresku conversation.
  Solution: Positions the trace program as a rigorous restatement of an old human question, not a break from it (an institutional framing claim, not a theoretical result).
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Event page text: "The Trace Institute brings that same human quest into the language of modern science ... asking whether space, time and the physical world emerge from it."
