## Summary
A 90-minute online dialogue between McGilchrist and essayist Charles Eisenstein, hosted by the Galileo Commission (June 4, 2026; recording posted June 5). Framed as a follow-up to the Sheldonian event, it shifts from what to do to how to be: responding to personal and collective challenges with agency and centredness rather than passivity, navigating cultural "enshittification," and McGilchrist's claim that fundamental values are intrinsic rather than instrumental — that our capacity to embody them constitutes who we essentially are.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This extends the constructive turn documented in PRS-29 (diagnosis → treatment plan) from the institutional register into the personal-formation register — "how to be in the world" as the essential role of education. It also adds an author-stated etymological anchor for the value claim (truth/"treu" as fidelity to self and others), reinforcing PRS-32's value-as-constitutive line.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
The "truth as 'treu' — being true to oneself, loyal to others" move ties epistemic truth to fidelity and relationship — a strong resonance with Stump's second-person account of knowledge and with Fredrickson's relational flourishing (inner-state management as a condition of right engagement). The intrinsic-vs-instrumental value distinction continues the convergence with the Hobbs/Aristotelian virtue line flagged in PRS-30. For C2A2 directly: McGilchrist locating education's essential role in "how to be" rather than information transfer reinforces the design implication of PRS-06/PRS-12 — the tradition-accelerator must scaffold formation, not just content delivery.
