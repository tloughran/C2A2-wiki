## Summary
Opening meditation of the CAC week "Reading the Bible Through the Lens of Love" (July 26–August 1). Rohr recounts falling in love with Scripture in seminary and reframes revelation, citing Vatican II: divine revelation is "not God disclosing ideas about God but actually God disclosing Godself" — Scripture as "love-making, an actual mutual exchange of being and intimacy" rather than doctrine or moralism. He argues love works "only inside humility": fullness or self-satisfaction "cannot permit love because there are no openings, no give-and-take, and no deep hunger" (the two-inflated-balloons image). The Risen Christ "starts us off by revealing the human wounds of God, God's solidarity with human suffering" — divine self-disclosure that invites reciprocal self-disclosure from us. He notes Scripture even presents God as "needing" us and "jealous" for our love (Ex 20:5; 34:14).

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the thesis meditation of a new theme that puts Rohr's contemplative hermeneutic directly on the network's most contested seam — how to read Scripture — making it the natural Rohr-side counterpart to Wright's critical-realist textual anchor. The "revelation as self-disclosure of a Person, not deposit of propositions" move, and the claim that love is possible only across a "mutually admitted emptiness," are not yet captured in the Rohr wiki and supply fresh material for the Summa 2026 synthesis on Scripture, grace, and the relational ontology of love.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE:
  Label: revelation-as-mutual-self-disclosure
  Problem: Is Scripture a repository of propositions about God, or something else — and what does that "else" imply for how it should be read?
  Resource: Rohr's Vatican II gloss — revelation is God disclosing Godself (not ideas about God), Scripture as "mutual exchange of being and intimacy"; the Risen Christ reveals "the human wounds of God" first, inviting reciprocal disclosure.
  Solution: Recast biblical revelation as second-personal self-disclosure between persons rather than information transfer, so that right reading is participatory (love, vulnerability) rather than merely propositional.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Direct claims in the meditation, adapted from Rohr, *Essential Teachings on Love* (Orbis, 2018), 76–77.

PRS-CANDIDATE:
  Label: emptiness-as-precondition-of-love
  Problem: What structural condition makes love (divine or human) possible at all?
  Resource: The "two inflated balloons" argument — "Fullness or complete self-satisfaction in a person cannot permit love because there are no openings, no give-and-take, and no deep hunger"; "a mutually admitted emptiness is the ultimate safety net for all love."
  Solution: Frame perspective-limitation / lack / "hunger" not as a defect but as the enabling condition of loving union — an emptiness that opens the give-and-take by which persons unite.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Explicit argument in the text; God presented as "needing" and "jealous" (Ex 20:5; 34:14) as scriptural warrant.
