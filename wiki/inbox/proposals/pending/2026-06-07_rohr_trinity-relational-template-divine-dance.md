## Summary
Opening the Week-23 "Embracing the Divine Exchange" series, Rohr states his Trinitarian ontology in its strongest form: God is known only as "perfect giving and perfect receiving" — communion, receptivity, and generosity, "one hundred percent unhindered dialogue between three." This is not abstraction but "the foundational template of reality": being itself is "total, continual givenness and perfect, humble receptivity … the very source, pattern, and goal of reality." He claims contemporary science confirms it — "physicists and astronomers, looking through microscopes or telescopes, see this same pattern of utter relationship … if reality is anything, it's absolutely relational" — against a post-Enlightenment Western individualism that reduced faith to "getting the right information" and salvation to a private "how do I get to heaven" ego-question.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the most direct statement in the recent corpus of Rohr's relational ontology and his explicit science-confirms-Trinity move — and it lands squarely on the Summa 2026 central theme that loving unity is the telos and that relationship is constitutive, not derivative. The wiki carries the Universal Christ and non-dual awareness but lacks a node on the Trinity-as-relational-template-of-reality (the *Divine Dance* / perichoresis frame) tied to a relational reading of physics. It is high-value for the synthesis's Prima Pars Trinity loci and for the "loving unity as computational telos" thesis.
