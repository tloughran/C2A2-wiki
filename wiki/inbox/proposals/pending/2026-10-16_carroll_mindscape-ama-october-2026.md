## Summary
Carroll's monthly Ask Me Anything. Substantive answers on AI-generated mathematical proofs and what counts as understanding, the meaning of "measurement" in quantum mechanics, Boltzmann-brain cognitive instability, Gödel's theorems versus physics, black-hole horizons under Hawking evaporation, and whether philosophy makes progress. Note: only the first ~100k of ~127k characters of the transcript page was read (the tail, including the AI-risk and later answers, was not retrieved); the answers below are from the portion read.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Carroll's replies extend his poetic-naturalist stance: Boltzmann-brain "cognitive instability" ties physical cosmology to the reliability of knowers, and his claim that philosophy should advocate answers rather than map possibilities bears directly on how traditions mature. Not previously captured (Sept 2026 AMA and Mindscape #370 are already in the wiki).

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Why are cosmological models that predict Boltzmann brains unacceptable, if they are not strictly inconsistent?
  Resource: "Cognitive instability" criterion — a fluctuation brain has no reason to trust memories not causally linked to a real past
  Solution: Reject such models because they undermine the evidence used to support them (epistemic self-undermining)
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Carroll's AMA answer on Boltzmann brains and self-locating uncertainty; also states probabilities are fundamentally about knowledge.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: What is "measurement" in quantum mechanics, given textbooks rarely define it?
  Resource: Distinction between Dirac's description of what measurement does and a physical definition of it (Hermitian operators/eigenstates not a definition)
  Solution: Treat the lack of a definition as a genuine gap in standard pedagogy, motivating Everettian/many-worlds treatments
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: AMA answer on Dirac's measurement description and whether the Schrödinger time parameter is fundamental (second part only partly read).

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: Does progress in AI-generated/Lean-verified proofs (e.g. claimed Navier-Stokes blow-up) tell us anything about physical fluids or about "understanding"?
  Resource: Separation of mathematical result from physical-model validity (singularities mark failure of the fluid approximation)
  Solution: Treat such proofs as mathematical results with limited physical import; provenance/history of a work matters beyond its content
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: AMA answers on Navier-Stokes, Lean, and AI-generated Mindscape.
