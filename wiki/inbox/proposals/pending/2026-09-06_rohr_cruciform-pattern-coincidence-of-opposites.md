## Summary
Thursday of the "Becoming Disciples" week, adapted from *The Universal Christ* (Convergent, 2021), 147-148. Rohr's claim is that the crucifixion is not primarily a singular event but a statement that reality itself has a cruciform pattern: the Divine Mind transforms suffering by identifying completely with the human predicament rather than observing it. Jesus died at the collision of conflicting interests and half-truths, and that collision is the price of living in a world that is simultaneously broken and whole. Rohr names this structure with Bonaventure's and Cusa's "coincidence of opposites," cites Ephesians 1:10 (recapitulation of all things) as the one-verse summary of Franciscan Christology, and then generalizes: the people who hold contradictions and resolve them *in themselves* are the real agents of transformation, they exist in every tradition and sometimes with no faith at all, and many non-Christians accept this vocation more freely than Christians. The Monday meditation of the same week ("Standing in Solidarity") states the solidarity premise on its own.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The rohr wiki's Universal-Christ summary records Christ as the pattern present in all matter but does not record what the pattern *is*. This meditation names it: cruciform, a coincidence of opposites held rather than dissolved. That fills a gap in Active Research Question 1 (Universal Christ into mind-monist metaphysics) with something checkable, and it gives the Summa synthesis a Rohr anchor for the Tertia Pars passion questions (Q.46-49) that is conceptual rather than devotional. The "resolve contradictions in themselves" line also bears directly on the Summa central theme, since it locates the work of unity inside a single limited perspective.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-A:
  Label: reality-has-a-cruciform-pattern
  Problem: The Universal-Christ claim that Christ is present in all matter is a claim about extension; it does not say what structure that presence has, so it cannot be tested against any other account of the structure of reality.
  Resource: Rohr's statement that the cross is a statement that reality has a cruciform pattern: a coincidence of opposites (Bonaventure, Alan of Lille, Cusa) that is held rather than resolved, and that is simultaneously broken and whole.
  Solution: The cosmic Christ is given a definite structural content, contradiction-holding, which can be compared with other structural accounts (complementarity, hemispheric integration, opposed priors) instead of remaining a bare universality claim.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Stated directly and sourced to *The Universal Christ*, 147-148.

PRS-CANDIDATE-B:
  Label: contradiction-resolved-in-the-agent
  Problem: If unity is the telos, it is unclear where the work of unifying is done: in the whole, in an institution, or in the individual.
  Resource: Rohr's claim that those who hold the contradictions and resolve them in themselves are the only real agents of transformation, that they exist in every tradition and sometimes with no faith, and that the gospel is the wisdom of those who agree to carry their share of God's suffering.
  Solution: The locus of unification is the single agent's interior; unity at scale is the aggregate of contradictions carried by limited perspectives, not an operation performed on them from outside.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: The "saviors of the world" paragraph and the closing paragraph on non-Christians accepting the vocation.
