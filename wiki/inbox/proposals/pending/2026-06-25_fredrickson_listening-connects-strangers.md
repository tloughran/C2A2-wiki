## Summary
West, Huston, Chandler, Zhou & Fredrickson (in press, Communications Psychology) provide a behavioral test of the claim that high-quality listening enables positive connection between strangers. The study links observable listening behaviors to social connection in stranger interactions, specifying listening as a manipulable behavioral lever for positivity resonance with people outside one's existing ties.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The wiki already holds the *conceptual* claim that high-quality listening is a resonance enabler (P8, from the UBC colloquium and Zhou & Fredrickson 2023). This is the distinct, not-yet-captured *behavioral* paper that ties specific listening behaviors to connection between strangers — i.e., it operationalizes the lever. Stranger-connection is the empirical analogue of cross-tradition first contact, so this is unusually direct C2A2 grounding.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: High-quality listening is hypothesized to enable positivity resonance with strangers, but which concrete, observable listening behaviors do the work — and whether they actually produce connection between strangers — needed behavioral evidence.
  Resource: A behavioral study coding high-quality listening behaviors and relating them to measured social connection in stranger dyads.
  Solution: Specific high-quality listening behaviors are empirically linked to social connection between strangers, identifying listening as a trainable behavioral pathway to weak-tie / stranger positivity resonance.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: The paper's title claim and design — high-quality listening behaviors linked to social connection between strangers (Communications Psychology, in press).
