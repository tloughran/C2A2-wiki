## Summary

The sixth instalment of the *God's Homecoming* bonus series with Mike Bird. Wright takes the three New Testament texts most often used against his new-creation thesis and argues that none of them says what the popular reading takes it to say. "Today you will be with me in paradise" (Luke 23:43) cannot mean "heaven," on Wright's account, because on Good Friday Jesus had not yet ascended — *paradise* names a restful holding place, not the destination. The "many rooms" of John 14 are read as temporary lodgings on a journey rather than permanent celestial mansions. "Treasure in heaven" (Matthew 19) is read as treasure *kept safe* in God's space for a future that arrives here, not as a deposit the depositor must eventually travel to collect. The organising move is a three-term distinction Wright presses throughout: **death → intermediate state → final bodily resurrection**, with "heaven" reassigned from *humanity's final destination* to *God's space*.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition

The Wright tradition already holds inaugurated eschatology and bodily-cosmic resurrection (`traditions/wright/prs_triplets.md`, PRS-03 and PRS-04) and the homecoming arc at canonical scale. What it has not held is the **middle term**. Everything captured so far runs *now → not yet*, two states. This source inserts a third — the intermediate state — and, more importantly, makes it do work: it is the term that lets Wright deny the popular heaven-reading *without* denying the texts that the popular reading rests on. That is a different kind of claim from "the destination is renewed creation." It is a claim about the **topology of the interval**.

That matters for the Summa 2026 project specifically. Tertia Pars and the Supplement questions on the state of the soul between death and resurrection are exactly where the synthesis has had the least contemporary purchase, because the network's mind-monist wing has no obvious analogue for a disembodied interval. Wright here supplies a scripturally-grounded description of that interval which is neither "you are in heaven now" nor "you are annihilated until the resurrection" — and which is stated in terms of *location in God's space* rather than *substance persisting*. That is a formulation a monist can engage with. It is currently uncaptured.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Wright's new-creation thesis is standardly met with three proof-texts (Luke 23:43, John 14:2, Matt 6:20 / 19:21) that appear to teach a heaven-destination. Without an account of these, the thesis reads as exegesis by exception.
  Resource: The three-term sequence death → intermediate state → final resurrection, plus the reassignment of "heaven" from *destination* to *God's space*. Applied to each proof-text in turn.
  Solution: The three texts are re-read as compatible with, not exceptions to, the new-creation thesis: *paradise* is the interval and not the end (warranted by Jesus's own un-ascended state at the time of speaking), the "many rooms" are staging and not terminus, and "treasure in heaven" is safekeeping and not a destination requiring relocation. The thesis stops being an exception-riddled position and becomes a reading of the whole.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Publisher's episode description states the death / intermediate-state / final-resurrection distinction and the "heaven as God's space rather than humanity's final destination" formulation directly; chapter headings at 02:14, 04:27, 07:36 and 15:25 confirm the three texts are each treated. Medium rather than High **only** because the argumentation is behind a paywall — the theses are attested, the reasoning is not.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: The Summa 2026 synthesis has no contemporary handle on the state of the person between death and resurrection. The mind-monist wing (Levin, Hoffman, Kastrup) can describe persistence of pattern but not the *scriptural* interval; the Thomist wing (Stump) can describe the separated soul but in hylomorphic terms the monists reject.
  Resource: Wright's characterisation of the intermediate state as *location within God's space* rather than as *a substance surviving in a place*.
  Solution: Proposes that the interval be specified relationally (whose keeping one is in) rather than substantially (what part of one persists). This is a formulation both wings can take up without first conceding the other's metaphysics — the monist reads it as pattern held in a larger field, the Thomist as the separated soul sustained in being by God, and neither reading is excluded by the text.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: The relational formulation is attested ("heaven is better understood as God's space"); the claim that it is *neutral between* the two metaphysics is the agent's proposal, not Wright's, and Wright shows no sign of having the network's dispute in view. Rated Speculative on that account, not on the textual point.

## Cross-Tradition Signals

- **Stump (direct engagement, unresolved).** Wright's interval is described without a bearer; Stump's hylomorphism requires one — the separated soul is the *form* of a body it currently lacks, which is precisely why she treats the state as unnatural and incomplete. Wright's "paradise as rest" is compatible with that incompleteness reading, and the two could be joined. But Wright supplies no metaphysics of the bearer and Stump supplies no exegesis of Luke 23:43. Worth flagging as a place where the two traditions need each other and neither has reached across.
- **Levin / Hoffman / Kastrup (bridge candidate, weak).** "Heaven as God's space" invites the reading that the interval is a change of frame rather than of location — which is what a Hoffman-style interface account or a Kastrup-style dissociation account would say. Flagging it as *available*, not as *made*: nothing in the source engages consciousness studies, and the network should not credit itself with a convergence Wright did not make. The honest form of this signal is a question for the master agent, not a finding.

It is a **CROSS-NN candidate** on the narrow ground that it introduces a third term into an arc the network has been treating as binary, and that third term is where the Summa's Supplement questions live. Recommend the master agent consider promotion only if the audio or ch. 6 text is obtained — promoting a paywalled description would put an unverified argument into the cross-program index.
