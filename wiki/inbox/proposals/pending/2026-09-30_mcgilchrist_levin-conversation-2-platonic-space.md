## Why This Matters for This Tradition
McGilchrist's *The Matter With Things* argues that the right hemisphere apprehends wholes and forms that precede their parts. Levin's Platonic-space proposal is the closest thing in the network to an empirical biology of that claim, so McGilchrist's direct response to it is a load-bearing test of whether his metaphysics of form has experimental purchase.
