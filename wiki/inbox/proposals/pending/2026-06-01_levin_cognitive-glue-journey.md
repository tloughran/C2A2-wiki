## Summary
A first-person retrospective by Levin in the journal *Bioelectricity* tracing the arc of his research program from developmental bioelectricity to the "cognitive glue" thesis — bioelectric networks as the mechanism by which collective intelligence scales from physiology to mind. It consolidates two decades of work into a programmatic statement of how voltage-mediated signaling binds cells into goal-directed collectives whose competencies exceed those of any single cell.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is Levin himself naming the through-line of his entire program — "cognitive glue" — and framing it as the bridge from matter to mind. As an author's own retrospective it is unusually high-signal for mapping the spine of the tradition's PRS chain and for dating where the program now believes it stands.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: How does goal-directed cognition scale up from individual cells to integrated organisms — what physically binds many small agents into one larger agent with its own competencies?
  Resource: The "cognitive glue" thesis — bioelectric networks as the substrate-independent binding medium that scales selfhood and problem-solving from physiology to mind.
  Solution: Levin frames bioelectric signaling as the mechanism that enables evolutionary scaling of cognition, unifying his developmental, regenerative, and basal-cognition work under a single binding principle.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: The paper's central claim, restated in its title and abstract, that bioelectric networks are "the cognitive glue enabling evolutionary scaling from physiology to mind."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Is there a coherent narrative connecting Levin's empirical findings (planarian regeneration, xenobots, bioelectric memory) into one research program rather than scattered results?
  Resource: An author-authored programmatic retrospective spanning the development-to-mind arc.
  Solution: The retrospective supplies the program's own self-description of its track record and open frontier, useful as a canonical spine for the tradition wiki.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: First-person framing ("My Journey in Bioelectricity") explicitly organizing the program's history and trajectory.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
The "binding many agents into one larger agent" problem is a direct touchpoint with **Friston's** Markov-blanket / nested-agent formalism (what statistically individuates a higher-order self) and with **Hawkins** on how many cortical columns compose a single mind. The substrate-independence of the "glue" also speaks to **Wolfram's** computational-boundary question and bears on the **Kastrup/McGilchrist** consciousness debate about whether nested binding produces nested subjects. Strong candidate for a dispatch flagging the Levin-Friston binding/individuation overlap.
