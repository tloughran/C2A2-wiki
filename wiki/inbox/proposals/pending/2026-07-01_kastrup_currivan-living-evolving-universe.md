## Summary
A structured dialogue between Kastrup and cosmologist Jude Currivan (whole-world/"cosmic hologram" physics) on whether the evidence of purpose in a "living, evolving universe" reflects an instinctive, self-organizing tendency of universal consciousness or a planned teleology. Kastrup presses his analytic-idealist reading — that apparent cosmic purpose is the intrinsic behavior of mind-at-large, not an externally imposed design — against Currivan's cosmological framing of an informationally self-realizing universe.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the second Currivan–Kastrup dialogue of 2026 (after "A Worldview for Global Change," 27 Jan 2026) and the most recent item in Kastrup's own With Reality in Mind series.
