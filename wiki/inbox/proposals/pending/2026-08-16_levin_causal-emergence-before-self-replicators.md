## Summary
Pigozzi and Levin ask what the causal structure of a chemical medium looks like *before* replicators exist and Darwinian selection can take hold. Running information-theoretic analysis on the GARD model (a standard origin-of-life model of catalytic lipid assemblies), they find that a measure of causal emergence rises ahead of, and predicts, the first appearance of self-replication. They then intervene: pushing causal emergence up lengthens how long self-replicators persist, pushing it down reduces how many there are.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Levin's programme claims that agency and goal-directedness are not delivered by evolution but are the precondition evolution exploits. This paper is the sharpest version of that claim yet: it puts a measurable quantity on the pre-evolutionary medium and shows it is not merely correlated with the origin of replication but manipulable as a control knob.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Causal power in the world is standardly explained as a product of selection among replicators — which leaves the causal structure of the medium *before* replicators unexplained and usually unexamined.
  Resource: Information-theoretic measurement of causal emergence applied to the GARD model of catalytic networks.
  Solution: Causal emergence rises before self-replication appears and predicts its onset, so integrated causality is detectable in active media prior to any evolutionary dynamics.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: "We... found that causal emergence predicted the initial appearance of self-replication," and the closing claim that "progressive increases in integrated causality are detectable in active media before evolutionary dynamics begin to operate."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: A predictive correlation does not establish that causal emergence is doing any work; it could be a readout of something else.
  Resource: Bidirectional interventions on causal emergence within the model.
  Solution: Driving causal emergence up increases self-replicator longevity; driving it down decreases self-replicator abundance — supporting causal emergence as a functional control knob rather than a passive index.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Both intervention directions are reported with distinct outcomes (longevity vs. abundance). That the two directions act on *different* dependent variables is worth attention — it is a weaker result than a single quantity moving both ways, and the full text should be checked on this point.

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: Why causal power has risen across the biosphere over evolutionary time.
  Resource: The pre-replicator result generalised beyond the specific GARD setting.
  Solution: Proposes that the same increase-in-integrated-causality dynamic underlies both the origin of life across diverse scenarios and the observed rise in causal power on Earth.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Framed by the authors themselves as an implication ("may have implications for"), derived from one model system.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Friston**: a medium that increases integrated causality before any selection acts is a candidate physical realisation of free-energy minimisation preceding evolution — the "things that persist look like they infer" claim, tested at the point where there is nothing yet to select.
- **Carroll**: directly engages the emergence question Carroll works on — whether higher-level causal structure is real or bookkeeping. Here it is treated as manipulable, which is the strong reading; Carroll's deflationary account of emergence is the natural opposing position.
- **Hoffman**: causal emergence as a pre-biological quantity is a possible meeting point with conscious-agent networks, where structure is prior to organisms rather than produced by them.
- **Wolfram**: computational irreducibility and the rise of coarse-grained causal structure in a rule-driven medium.
- **Stump / Rohr**: an account in which directedness precedes life rather than being invented by it touches the teleology questions those traditions carry; flagged, not asserted.
