## Summary
Levin's keynote to the Artificial Life meeting in Japan, delivered by telepresence robot, frames scientific discovery as an active-inference-like loop and argues that studying unfamiliar intelligences requires openness to many levels of agency. It presents his multi-scale competency and "agential materials" ideas and an ICDO project with Josh Bongard and Doug Blackiston to build a robot scientist ("MomBot") that runs hypothesis-test cycles on Xenobots for synthetic morphology.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It is Levin's own statement of robot science as a translation tool between diverse intelligences, which bears directly on C2A2's question of how mature members of different traditions (including AI agents) can read one another.
