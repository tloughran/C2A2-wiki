## Summary
Mark Jeffery asked Wolfram how he thinks, meaning how he produces the ideas behind the Physics Project. The episode is Wolfram's answer in his own voice. The newsletter lists ten practices "derived from Stephen's answer": drill to foundations; experiment with your ideas; try to understand the findings; doubt your expectations; push ahead regardless; explain to others; discuss with others; learn when an idea will take flight; don't skip imagine → experiment → explain; visualize.
