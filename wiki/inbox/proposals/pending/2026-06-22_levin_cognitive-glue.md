## Summary
A first-person retrospective/review by Levin in the journal *Bioelectricity* (Sage) tracing the arc of his research program from developmental bioelectricity to the "cognitive glue" thesis — the claim that bioelectric signaling is the mechanism by which collectives of cells bind into higher-order agents with their own goals, memories, and problem-solving competencies. The piece frames bioelectricity as the through-line connecting morphogenesis to cognition and argues for a principled symmetry between how bodies build themselves and how minds build themselves.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is Levin's own consolidated statement of the program's intellectual trajectory, published within the last 30 days. As an autobiographical-review article it makes explicit the conceptual scaffolding (bioelectricity-as-cognitive-glue, scale-free cognition, the morphogenesis↔cognition symmetry) that prior wiki entries captured only piecemeal from individual empirical papers. It is a high-value "spine" source for the tradition wiki.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: There is no agreed mechanism explaining how a collection of competent sub-agents (cells) becomes bound into a single higher-level agent with unified goals and memory.
  Resource: "Cognitive glue" — the thesis that bioelectric signaling networks are the binding medium that scales individual cell competencies into collective, organism-level goal-directedness.
  Solution: Levin proposes bioelectricity as a substrate-general binding mechanism, positioning it as the explanatory link between physiological coordination and mind-like properties at higher scales.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: The paper's central organizing claim, named in the title and developed as the through-line of the research journey ("From Development to Cognitive Glue").

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: The relationship between morphogenesis (how bodies self-construct) and cognition (how minds self-construct) has been treated as analogy rather than as a shared formal structure.
  Resource: An explicit symmetry argument: the same control principles that let cell collectives navigate "anatomical morphospace" let agents navigate cognitive/behavioral spaces.
  Solution: Levin advances morphogenesis and cognition as two expressions of one substrate-independent competency, strengthening the continuity thesis already recorded in the wiki (PRS-09/PRS-10).
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: The retrospective frames the body-building/mind-building symmetry as the unifying lesson of the program; consolidates rather than newly demonstrates.
