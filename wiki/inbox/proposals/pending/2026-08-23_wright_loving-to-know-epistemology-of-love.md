## Summary
Wright's own framing of the epistemological project that has run under his exegesis since the 1980s. He states the aim plainly: to develop the notion of *love as the ultimate form of knowledge*, and to explore its relevance beyond biblical studies. The diagnosis he gives is disciplinary in origin and philosophical in cause. He was concerned by distortions in New Testament studies that trace to Enlightenment epistemology, and he names the specific mechanism: biblical scholars rarely examine their philosophical presuppositions, so they inherit a positivism that sorts claims into the supposedly objective ("the sure and certain results of scientific research") and the supposedly subjective (theological meaning, treated as pious speculation). The critical-realist alternative he has argued for elsewhere is here given its epistemological name — knowing that is answerable to its object precisely by being invested in it, rather than by standing apart from it.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The wright/ wiki holds Wright as the network's scriptural ground-truth oracle, which is a claim about *what he knows*. This source is about *how he claims to know it* — and that is the part the network actually needs, because the C2A2 accelerator's whole premise is that agents richly informed about one another's perspectives produce evidence, which presupposes a defensible account of knowing-through-engagement rather than knowing-by-detachment. Wright is not offering a devotional gloss; he is making a methodological claim inside a discipline that punishes methodological claims, and he names the Enlightenment fact/value split as the thing to be replaced. It is also the cleanest available answer to the standing worry that treating Wright as an "oracle" imports confessional commitments: his epistemology is stated, criticisable, and not merely assumed.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-A:
  Label: love-as-the-ultimate-form-of-knowledge
  Problem: Detached observation is the default warrant for knowledge claims, which leaves any knowledge constitutively requiring investment in its object — persons, communities, traditions — looking second-rate by construction.
  Resource: Wright's stated programme: love as the ultimate form of knowledge, developed since the 1980s as the epistemological ground of his critical realism.
  Solution: Knowing rises rather than falls with appropriate investment in the object, when the object is the kind of thing that discloses itself only to engagement. This does not license wishful thinking — the object still constrains — but it removes the presumption that distance is what makes a claim reliable.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Wright states the programme in his own words in the source. Confidence is Medium not High only because the summary states the thesis without the supporting argument; the full *First Things* article should raise or lower it.

PRS-CANDIDATE-B:
  Label: unexamined-positivism-in-a-humanistic-discipline
  Problem: Disciplines that never surface their epistemology inherit one anyway, and cannot see the distortions it introduces because the distortions look like rigour.
  Resource: Wright's specific diagnosis — biblical scholars plunge straight into texts, assuming a positivism that pre-sorts findings into objective results versus subjective theological speculation.
  Solution: A transferable audit: for any tradition in the C2A2 network, ask what its unexamined epistemology sorts *out* before inquiry begins. Wright's case is a worked example on a discipline that believed itself neutral.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Stated directly and specifically, with the mechanism named rather than gestured at.
