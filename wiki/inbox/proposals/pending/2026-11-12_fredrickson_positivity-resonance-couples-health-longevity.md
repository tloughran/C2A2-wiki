## Summary
Wells, Haase, Rothwell, Naugle, Otero, Brown, Lai, Chen, Connelly, Grimm, Levenson & Fredrickson (2022), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 123(5), 983-1003 (DOI 10.1037/pspi0000385). The team coded positivity resonance in 154 long-married couples during a conflict conversation, using three channels at once: shared positive emotion, nonverbal (behavioral) synchrony, and physiological synchrony. Higher positivity resonance predicted better health over 13 years and longer life over 30 years, after controlling for baseline health, demographics, health behaviors, and each spouse's *individually* felt positive affect. A simpler behavior-only measure gave the same result.

**Not a recent item.** It fails the 30-day window and is proposed under the "significant work not yet captured" clause: a search of `traditions/fredrickson/` and all of `inbox/proposals/` found no reference to it. It is the program's strongest outcome evidence that positivity resonance is a dyad-level phenomenon with long-run biological consequences, beyond what solo positive affect predicts. Observational design: associations, not causal proof; the authors call it one of the first studies of its kind.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It is the long-horizon outcome study the program's central thesis rests on: resonance (a shared state) predicts health over decades, beyond solo positive emotion. It also gives the existing measurement triplets (Positively in-sync, BIPR, "Does positivity resonance signify love?") a downstream consequence to point to.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Is positivity resonance more than a pleasant moment, and does a *shared* state carry health consequences beyond each partner's own positive affect?
  Resource: Multimodal latent measure of positivity resonance (shared positive emotion + behavioral synchrony + physiological linkage) coded from conflict conversations; 13-year health and 30-year mortality follow-up (N = 154 couples)
  Solution: Higher positivity resonance predicts better health trajectories and longer life, robust to individually experienced positive affect and baseline health. That places the active ingredient at the level of the dyad.
  Confidence: Medium (single longitudinal sample, observational; robustness checks reported)
  Evidence: Abstract and results: "higher rates of positivity resonance predicted better future health outcomes and longer lives"; the effect holds when solo positive affect is controlled, and when BIPR replaces the latent measure.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Can resonance occur, and matter, *during disagreement*, or only in easy moments?
  Resource: Conflict-discussion paradigm (Levenson marital-interaction lab) as the setting where resonance was measured
  Solution: Micro-moments of shared positivity *inside conflict* are what predicted health and longevity, so resonance and disagreement can share one interaction.
  Confidence: Speculative as a generalization beyond marriage; Medium for the study's own setting
  Evidence: Positivity resonance was coded from the couples' conflict conversations, not from neutral or pleasant ones.
