## Summary
Levy, Zeidman, Allen & Friston (bioRxiv preprint, not peer-reviewed) introduce Embodied Dynamic Causal Modelling: a closed-loop "Brainstem Cardiac Network" that jointly inverts simultaneous EEG and ECG. The model has four tiers: cortical canonical-microcircuit neural masses, five brainstem nuclei (sympathetic/vagal and baroreceptor pathways), a viscerosensory thalamic relay, and a sinoatrial-node pacemaker observed through the ECG. On EEG/ECG from an auditory Go/No-Go task, joint inversion reportedly gave higher model evidence and tighter posteriors than either signal alone, and Bayesian model comparison discriminated agranular-visceromotor from granular-sensory-relay hypotheses.

**Orchestrator caveat (read before approving):** every detail above comes from a secondary summary (theconsciousness.ai, 2026-11-07). The DOI did not resolve for the fetch tool, so title, author order, date and results are unconfirmed against the preprint. The summary gives no numbers. Verify on bioRxiv before approving.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It moves DCM from brain-only to brain-plus-body generative models, making interoceptive active inference empirically testable on standard EEG/ECG recordings. It continues the line already in the vault (insula-cardiac dysregulation in depression, 2026-09-14; interoceptive AI, 2026-08-31).

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Interoceptive active-inference accounts posit brain-heart loops, but standard effective-connectivity methods model the brain alone, so those loops cannot be estimated from data.
  Resource: Embodied DCM: a closed-loop cortical-brainstem-thalamic-sinoatrial generative model jointly inverted on EEG and ECG.
  Solution: Joint inversion yields higher model evidence and more precise parameter estimates than either signal alone, letting brain-heart coupling be estimated and compared across hypotheses.
  Confidence: Medium (secondary report; preprint unread)
  Evidence: Reported validation on an auditory Go/No-Go EEG/ECG dataset with variational Bayesian model comparison.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Competing cytoarchitectural accounts of visceral cortex (agranular visceromotor prediction vs granular sensory relay) have been argued anatomically rather than tested on physiological data.
  Resource: Bayesian model comparison inside the embodied DCM.
  Solution: The two hypotheses become comparable model structures whose evidence can be scored on EEG/ECG.
  Confidence: Speculative (direction of the result not stated in the summary)
  Evidence: Summary says comparison "distinguished" the hypotheses without reporting which won.
