## Summary
A week on contemplative practice mechanics, missed by prior runs. Rohr's central claim is a directional transformation: prayer that reaches the unconscious shifts one's whole construal of the world from fear to connection — from being fear-driven to being love-drawn — which he glosses as movement from ego consciousness to soul awareness. The method is explicitly non-suppressive: thoughts and feelings are neither clung to nor opposed but allowed to pass, with partial failure treated as itself instructive rather than as defect. Rohr adds that negative motivation is not fought but exposed under a compassionate gaze and falls away as counterproductive. The week's practice section, from spiritual director Therese DesCamp, refuses every instrumental justification for meditation (acuity, aging, blood pressure, stress, being a better Christian) as byproduct rather than aim, and relocates the test of practice from what is experienced during the sit to behavior across the remaining twenty-three hours.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the most mechanically explicit account of contemplative practice CAC has published in this cycle, and it is the piece the wiki most needs: the tradition's Research Question 4 asks what contemplation looks like computationally, and this week supplies three testable specifications — non-suppressive attention (neither clinging nor opposing), motivation-extinction-by-exposure rather than by force, and an externalized success criterion. DesCamp's relocation of the criterion to out-of-session behavior is methodologically important for the C2A2 accelerator, since it makes the claim assessable without any appeal to privileged first-person report.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

**PRS-CANDIDATE-A**
- **Problem:** Contemplative practice is standardly evaluated by the quality of in-session experience, which is unfalsifiable, self-reinforcing, and invites spiritual consumerism.
- **Resource:** DesCamp's inversion — practice is preparation, not performance; its true test is conduct in the twenty-three hours outside the sit, on the analogy of scales to concerto.
- **Solution:** An externalized success criterion for contemplative practice, assessable by third-party behavioral observation rather than introspective report. Makes contemplative claims admissible as evidence in the accelerator's tradition-comparison design.
- **Confidence:** High
- **Evidence:** DesCamp, *Hands Like Roots: Notes on an Entangled Contemplative Life* (Santos Books, 2025), 135–136, quoted at length in the Week Twenty practice section.

**PRS-CANDIDATE-B**
- **Problem:** How does a practice that neither suppresses nor endorses its own contents produce durable change in what an agent is disposed to do?
- **Resource:** Rohr's two-part mechanism — thoughts allowed to pass without clinging or opposing, and negative motivation exposed under a compassionate gaze until it "falls away as counterproductive and useless" — coupled with the fear-driven → love-drawn directional claim.
- **Solution:** Read contemplative practice as extinction-by-non-reinforcement rather than as inhibition: the practice withholds both suppression and engagement, so high-precision fear-weighted priors lose the traffic that maintains them, and the agent's default construal shifts from threat-modeling to connection-modeling.
- **Confidence:** Speculative
- **Evidence:** Sunday and Monday entries (fear-driven to love-drawn; passing thoughts without clinging or opposing) and Wednesday (negative energy exposed rather than fought). The active-inference gloss is the proposal's, not Rohr's.
