## Summary
In this live With Reality in Mind dialogue, Kastrup hosts Patrick Harpur (author on the daimonic and imagination) for a wide-ranging exchange in which Harpur presses the claim that the very notion of "the literal" is itself a metaphor — challenging consensus reality and arguing for an enchanted, depth-laden encounter with the world. Notably, Harpur critiques Kastrup's own "cockpit metaphor" (the dashboard/interface picture of perception) as too restrictive, and the two explore free will, how daimons mediate between the human and the beyond, and how to discern daimon from addiction.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is one of the few recent occasions where Kastrup's framing is challenged on its home turf by a sympathetic but divergent interlocutor. Harpur's critique of the cockpit/dashboard metaphor goes to the heart of how analytic idealism models perception (the "dials on a dashboard" picture Kastrup shares with Hoffman's interface theory), and Kastrup's response sharpens where analytic idealism stands on imagination, metaphor, and the reality of the daimonic. It tests the boundary between Kastrup's rigorous, argument-first idealism and a more romantic/imaginal idealism.
