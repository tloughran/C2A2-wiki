## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Arkani-Hamed's program argues that spacetime is not fundamental and that amplitudes are computed by combinatorial-geometric objects that never mention it. Celestial holography is the rival-adjacent route to the same destination: it keeps flat spacetime but reads physics off its asymptotic boundary, where the symmetry structure lives "at infinity." A lecture series in which Arkani-Hamed both co-organizes and teaches inside a celestial-holography-framed program is the clearest available signal of how the positive-geometry program means to relate to the boundary-symmetry program — whether as competitor, as complement, or as two coordinate systems on one structure. The wiki has captured the positive-geometry side (surfaceology, cosmohedra, hidden zeros, kinematic flow) but has no captured source on Arkani-Hamed's stance toward flat-space holography.
