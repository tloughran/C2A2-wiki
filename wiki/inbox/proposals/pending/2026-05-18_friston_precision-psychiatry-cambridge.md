## Summary
Cambridge Neuroscience has published the substantive abstract for a Karl Friston Distinguished Lecture titled "Precision psychiatry," scheduled for 28 May 2026. The abstract sets out a formal/computational approach to phenomenology, using psychopathology as a case study of aberrant perception, and centers on the neuromodulatory encoding of uncertainty (precision) within predictive-coding implementations of active inference. Friston argues neuromodulation selects the aspects of sensation that underwrite belief updating — and that aberrations in this precision-weighting underlie symptoms across the psychiatric spectrum.
