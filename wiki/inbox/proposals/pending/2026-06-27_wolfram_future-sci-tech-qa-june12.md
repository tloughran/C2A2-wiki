## Summary
Wolfram's biweekly unscripted Q&A (1h16m), the first Future-of-Science-&-Technology
livestream after the captured May 15 episode. Across the session he fields questions on
whether hyper-personalized technology could leave two people effectively "living in
different realities," whether AI agents will become sovereign quasi-jurisdictions, whether
minds are computable (whole-brain emulation, personal identity, brain-to-brain linking),
and where the real bottleneck on progress lies — lack of knowledge, lack of computation, or
lack of conceptual frameworks.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
These are Wolfram applying his standing apparatus — observer-dependent sampling of the
Ruliad, computational irreducibility, the rulial space of minds — to live questions about
mind-uploading, personal identity, and the structure of scientific progress. The
"different-realities" and "bottleneck on progress" threads are the program's two most
C2A2-relevant signals to surface since the April 3 Q&A (PRS-15/16).

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: If observers sample the Ruliad differently (PRS-05/17), what happens when
    technology actively widens the divergence between two human observers' samplings?
  Resource: Wolfram's "two people effectively living in different realities" framing — a
    technologically-amplified case of observer-dependent reality.
  Solution: Recasts hyper-personalization as a rulial-space divergence between observers,
    making "incommensurable effective realities" a consequence of the observer theory rather
    than a mere social worry — and naming a failure mode for any shared knowledge commons.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Q&A segment on personalized technology and divergent realities (per livestream
    description).

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: How should we model the rate-limiting step on scientific/technological progress —
    the question C2A2's whole measurement aim turns on?
  Resource: Wolfram's trichotomy of bottlenecks: lack of knowledge vs. lack of computation
    vs. lack of conceptual frameworks; plus his "person / team / AI-assisted computational
    process" framing of where the next breakthrough comes from.
  Solution: Supplies a concrete candidate taxonomy for what a progress metric must score — and
    foregrounds "conceptual frameworks" (new paradigms / new questions) as a distinct,
    possibly dominant bottleneck, which aligns tightly with the PRS-triplet thesis that
    progress is chained problem→resource→solution moves.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Q&A segment on whether the bottleneck is knowledge, computation, or conceptual
    frameworks (per livestream description).

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: Are minds computable, and if so what survives copying/uploading — i.e., where do
    persons sit in the rulial space of minds (PRS-03/05/17)?
  Resource: Wolfram's live treatment of whole-brain emulation, mind-computability, personal
    identity, and brain-to-brain linking as questions about an observer's path through the
    Ruliad.
  Solution: Extends the rulial-space-of-minds agenda from abstract observer-classes (PRS-17)
    to the concrete case of human personal identity under copying/merging, where identity
    becomes a question about continuity of a rulial path rather than substrate.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Q&A segment on whole-brain emulation, minds being computable, personal identity,
    and linking brains (per livestream description).
