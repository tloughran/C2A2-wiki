## Summary
Weekly summary of the Oct 4–9 series. Rohr presents creation as Francis's primary cathedral, which sent him back into the city's needs, and says the incarnation is to be met everywhere, not only in places marked as sacred. Guest voices extend it: Ilia Delio on the *Canticle of Creatures* as a lifetime's realization of kinship with the cosmos; Leonardo Boff claiming Francis intuited what genetics now shows, that living things are kin through shared code; Randy Woodley on contemplation leading to vulnerability and engagement rather than withdrawal. The practice (Delio, Warner, Wood) moves toward an "integral ecology" grounded in relationship.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Closes the Francis/nature series opened by approved PROP 2026-10-04 (God's Resting Place). New here: an explicit bridge from Franciscan intuition to empirical biology (Boff), and the claim that contemplation's proper fruit is engaged vulnerability. Citable for Summa 2026 on the relation of contemplative and active life (II-II Q.179–182).
