## Summary
The third and final volume of Carroll's *Biggest Ideas in the Universe* trilogy, subtitled *Complexity and Emergence*, is listed by the UK/Commonwealth publisher (Oneworld, ISBN 9780861549177) as a hardcover released 2 June 2026. Volume 1 (*Space, Time, and Motion*, 2022) covered classical physics and Volume 2 (*Quanta and Fields*, 2024) covered quantum field theory; this volume takes emergence and complex systems as its subject — "the extraordinary physics which governs complex systems… the astonishing science of emergence and its profound metaphysical implications," in the publisher's words. The trilogy's stated method is to present the actual equations rather than metaphors, assuming only high-school algebra.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Emergence is the single load-bearing concept in poetic naturalism — the doctrine that higher-level descriptions (life, mind, value) are real without being fundamental — and this is the first book-length, equations-included treatment Carroll has given it. Every open question in this tradition's wiki that turns on whether emergence is epistemic or ontological (Active Questions 3, 5, 9, 12) is a question this book is positioned to answer in Carroll's own considered voice rather than in podcast conversation.
