## Summary
A three-way panel at the Interdisciplinary Conference on Psychedelic Research (ICPR26), co-hosted by the OPEN Foundation and Essentia Foundation, in which Kastrup argues his analytic-idealist reading of psychedelic phenomenology against Anil Seth's predictive-processing/controlled-hallucination account and Christof Koch's IIT-informed position. The stated axis of disagreement is the *epistemic value* of psychedelic experience — whether it discloses anything about reality or only about the brain's generative model.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Kastrup's dissociation model makes a strong empirical bet on psychedelics: reduced cortical activity accompanying richer experience is his standard counterexample to production-based physicalism. Putting him directly against Seth (the predictive-processing case) and Koch (the IIT case) on that exact terrain is the sharpest live adjudication of that bet the wiki has an opportunity to capture, and it is a rival-traditions exchange of precisely the kind the accelerator exists to stage.
