three-week residential research program he runs at Bentley University (Waltham, MA),
28 June – 18 July 2026, in which participants complete an individual research project

## Why This Matters for This Tradition

Every other Wolfram source in this wiki is him addressing an audience he did not select:
Q&A viewers, podcast hosts, a debate opponent, a conference. This is him telling people who
have already bought into the paradigm *what to work on next*. That is a different kind of
evidence, and it is the kind MacIntyre's criterion of a living tradition actually asks for
— not what the founder believes, but what research program the paradigm generates and
whether it has a track record.

Concretely: the wiki's "Active Research Questions" list for this tradition now has sixteen
entries, several of which (3, 5, 15, 16) are flagged as awaiting a source in which Wolfram
addresses them on their own terms. A keynote whose whole function is to enumerate open
problems is the single most likely place for that list to be checked against his own.

The institute's five published tracks — Foundational Science; Ruliology & Pure Computation;
Applications of Computation; Educational Innovation; Philosophy & Strategy — are themselves
a taxonomy of the program, and the standing *Philosophy & Strategy* track is relevant to
Question 12 (McGilchrist's descriptive-adequacy challenge) and to CROSS-074
(computational metaphysics as method-export to philosophy).
