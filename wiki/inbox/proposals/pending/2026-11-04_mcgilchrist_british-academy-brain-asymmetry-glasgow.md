## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Why is brain asymmetry conserved across vertebrates, when popular "left brain / right brain" accounts have been widely discredited?
  Resource: Comparative evidence on lateralisation across species (birds, fish, reptiles, hominids), presented as the evolutionary origin of the divided brain.
  Solution: Asymmetry exists because it serves two incompatible kinds of attention at once: narrow, grasping attention to the known, and broad, vigilant attention to the whole. The pop-psychology version was wrong about *what* each hemisphere does, not about the fact that they differ.
  Confidence: Speculative (inferred from the abstract plus his published account. The lecture content itself has not been read.)
  Evidence: Abstract: "Across the animal kingdom, brain asymmetry is the rule rather than the exception… Many long-held assumptions about lateralisation have proven mistaken. This lecture explores the evolutionary origins of the divided brain, the distinct purposes each hemisphere serves…"
