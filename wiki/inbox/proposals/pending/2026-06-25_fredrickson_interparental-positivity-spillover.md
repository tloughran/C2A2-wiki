## Summary
Don, Simpson, Fredrickson & Algoe propose "interparental positivity spillover theory" in Perspectives on Psychological Science: a framework for how positive relational interactions *between* parents (including positivity resonance) transmit beyond the dyad to shape children's development. It extends broaden-and-build and positivity-resonance theory from a within-dyad mechanism to a multi-person, cross-generational transmission process.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The positivity-resonance program has been overwhelmingly dyadic. This is a theory-level extension to a *system* — how resonance in one relationship propagates to third parties who are not direct participants in the resonant moment. That propagation logic is exactly what C2A2 needs to model (how resonance inside one tradition-community radiates to observers/apprentices), so this is a high-value structural addition, not just another empirical study.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Broaden-and-build and positivity resonance describe effects within an interacting dyad, but offer no account of how the benefits of a resonant relationship reach people who are not parties to the interaction.
  Resource: Interparental positivity spillover theory — a model of the pathways (emotional, behavioral, relational-climate) by which positive interaction between two partners influences a third party (the child).
  Solution: Positive relational quality is reframed as a system-level resource with spillover pathways, establishing that positivity resonance has indirect, propagating effects beyond the resonant dyad.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: The paper's stated thesis and title — positive interactions between parents influence children via specified spillover pathways (Perspectives on Psychological Science, 2025).
