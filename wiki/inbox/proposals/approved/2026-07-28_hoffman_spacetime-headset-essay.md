## Summary
The Trace Institute publications page lists, under "Selected articles," an **unpublished 2025 long-form essay by Hoffman titled "Consciousness And Its Spacetime Headset."** The Institute describes it as arguing "that spacetime is a headset, not fundamental, and that physics, evolution, and the trace logic on conscious agents all point past it." This is the only item on the Institute's own canonical publications list that has **no counterpart anywhere in the Hoffman tradition wiki** (verified: zero matches for "spacetime headset" across the vault).

Its significance is structural rather than novel-result: it is apparently the single document where Hoffman runs all **three** independent argument lines — foundations-of-physics ("spacetime is doomed"), evolutionary game theory (Fitness-Beats-Truth), and trace logic on conscious-agent networks — as one convergent argument. The wiki currently holds each line separately (PRS on FBT/IOS from the Stevens' Handbook chapter; trace logic from the Levin dialogue and the whitepaper; spacetime-is-not-fundamental from the HOMeHOPe keynote) but holds no source for the *conjunction*.
