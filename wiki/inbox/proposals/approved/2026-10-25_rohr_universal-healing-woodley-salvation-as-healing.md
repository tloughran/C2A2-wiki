## Summary
Rohr presents Cherokee theologian Randy Woodley (*How Western Christianity Got It Wrong*, 2026), who argues that Western Christianity turned salvation into a doctrinal system that served power, with original sin functioning as a lever of control. Sin is recast as imbalance or broken harmony, and salvation as healing in the present, communal and extending to "the land itself" within the wider community of creation (shalom).

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It extends the Universal Christ frame toward a relational-ontology account of salvation (harmony restored across all creation), which is the closest recent Rohr-platformed text to the Summa 2026 theme of loving unity as telos. It also generates a live tension with Wright and Stump on original sin that the network should record rather than average away.
