## Summary
The week reads human homesickness as a "desirous dissatisfaction" grounded in original union with God, drawing us toward true self and God. Rohr reads death as "finally going home" and the Odyssey as return to true self; Brené Brown, Willie Jennings and Diana Butler Bass add homing as the Bible's central story. The practice (James Finley) suggests our longing echoes God's longing for us, and fulfilment comes when the two meet.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Extends the 11 Oct "Home is where we begin" proposal into a full series: teleology as homing, longing as God's own. Citable for Summa 2026's "loving unity as telos" and supplies the contemplative counterpart to Wright's exile-and-homecoming reading (see PROP-2026-10-18-001).
