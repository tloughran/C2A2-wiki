## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: The "meaning crisis" is usually diagnosed in cultural or economic terms, and those diagnoses do not explain why meaning drains away under conditions of material plenty.
  Resource: Hemisphere theory: left-hemisphere attention re-presents the world as decontextualised, usable parts, while right-hemisphere attention is the mode in which value, the living whole and the sacred show up.
  Solution: Treat the meaning crisis as a disorder of attention, not a lack of information or belief, and treat "recovering the sacred" as restoring the right-hemisphere mode of attending, not as adopting a doctrine.
  Confidence: Speculative
