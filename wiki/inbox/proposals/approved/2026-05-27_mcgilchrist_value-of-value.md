## Summary
McGilchrist's own Substack essay framing the question "what is true?" through the category of value — written as the discursive prelude to the May 2–3, 2026 Sheldonian "Good, Beautiful, True" event. The essay says explicitly that McGilchrist's own thinking has shifted recently, with value moving from a derived concept to a central one: value is not what minds project onto a neutral cosmos but a constitutive feature of reality available to right-hemispheric attention. It is shorter and more programmatic than the long-form trialogue captured in PRS-09 (Parallax interview, March 6, 2026), and it is a first-person McGilchrist text rather than a co-authored conversation.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The existing wiki captures value/sacred via PRS-09 (Parallax, with Gafni & Stein) and PRS-17 (Premier Unbelievable, with secondary interviewer). This essay is McGilchrist on the same theme without the interlocutors shaping the frame — and with an explicit "my thinking has changed" signal. That makes it the cleanest source for the program's current state of the value claim, and it is the canonical authorial statement that the Sheldonian lectures (PROP-2026-05-27-002) elaborate.
