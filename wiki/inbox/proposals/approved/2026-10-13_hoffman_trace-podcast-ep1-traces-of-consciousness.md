## Summary
This is the first episode of the Trace Institute's own podcast (1:25:41, hosted by Evan McGloughlin of Giants Shoulder). In it, Hoffman lays out the Institute's plan: start from observers rather than brains or particles, and rebuild physics from there. He explains what a "trace" is, how recursive trace logic builds on Markov chains (the "minimal observer"), why spacetime is "a headset" and "doomed" (positive geometries), and why he thinks consciousness science is stuck. He also describes how he and Andrew Gallimore plan to test claims about DMT entities. Listing page: https://traceinstitute.org/podcast/
