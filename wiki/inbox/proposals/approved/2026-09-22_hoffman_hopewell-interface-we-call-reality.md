## Summary
The Trace Institute has announced its second public event: a general-audience conversation with Donald Hoffman at Hopewell Valley Stage in Hopewell, New Jersey, on Saturday 3 October 2026, titled "The Interface We Call Reality." It is framed as "a public conversation introducing Donald Hoffman's work and the mission of the Trace Institute: what becomes possible when science treats the observer as part of the foundation of reality?" A registration link and an event stream are listed as forthcoming.

**Flagged limitation:** this is an announcement, not delivered content. No claims, arguments, or results exist yet. ## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Two things are new and checkable. First, this is the Institute's first event framed as an *introduction* to the program rather than a research conversation — the Lighthouse Campus event (PROP-2026-08-11-002, PROP-2026-09-08-004) paired Hoffman with a collaborator on a specific technical result; this one does not. Second, the venue is a regional community stage on the US east coast, not a Los Angeles research campus. Together these suggest the Institute is moving from establishing the program among specialists to recruiting a general public, four months after incorporation (15 May 2026).
