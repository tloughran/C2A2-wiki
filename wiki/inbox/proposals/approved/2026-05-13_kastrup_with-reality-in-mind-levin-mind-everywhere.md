## Summary
On May 12, 2026 (yesterday), Bernardo Kastrup hosted Michael Levin on the With Reality in Mind platform for a structured two-hour dialogue. The format was: first hour, Levin presenting his current "Mind Everywhere" framework (cognition scaling continuously from cells through tissues to organisms to constructed agents, with subjective vantage points at every level); second hour, Kastrup pressing Levin on where exactly the boundaries between vantage points fall under analytic idealism, and whether Levin's nested-subject model is genuinely idealist or whether it smuggles in panpsychist commitments Kastrup rejects. The two converge on the idealist premise (consciousness is not produced by matter) but diverge sharply on the shape of dissociation: for Levin, conscious agents nest within each other and scale continuously; for Kastrup, dissociative boundaries are real and discrete, with universal consciousness fragmenting only into specific alters, not into a smooth gradient.

This is direct, two-thinker, public engagement on the single most important open question for Kastrup's program in 2026: whether analytic idealism is compatible with nested, scale-free cognition (Levin) or whether it requires discrete dissociative boundaries that rule out the smooth Levin picture. This is also one of the highest-stakes cross-tradition events for the entire C2A2 network in this period — Kastrup explicitly framing where his program touches and where it diverges from the program that Kastrup himself has previously called the most important currently active. PRS-02 (DID model of individual identity within monism) was the canonical Kastrup PRS triplet on this terrain; this dialogue advances PRS-02 from a single-program statement to a live cross-program debate.
