## Summary
Closer to Truth (Robert Lawrence Kuhn's consciousness series, syndicated via Lifeboat News) released a short-form interview with McGilchrist on May 8, 2026 in which Kuhn pushes McGilchrist past the cultural-diagnostic level of hemispheric theory all the way to the fundamental-ontology question: what is consciousness ontologically? McGilchrist commits publicly to a position close to "consciousness is the stuff of the cosmos" — that mind does not emerge from a mindless physical substrate but is itself an ontological primitive, with the brain as a correlate and structurer rather than a generator of consciousness. He distinguishes his position from naive panpsychism (he is not claiming rocks are conscious in the way humans are) and from analytic idealism (he holds reality is genuinely relational rather than purely mental), while locating himself unmistakably inside the consciousness-as-fundamental family of views.

This is significant for C2A2 because it cleans up the question of what cluster McGilchrist actually belongs to (he is now firmly in the Hoffman–Kastrup–Stump cluster on ontology), and it cleans up the open question in wiki.md ("Does McGilchrist's program make a fundamental-ontology commitment, or only a cognitive-cultural one?") with a clear yes-to-fundamental-ontology answer.
