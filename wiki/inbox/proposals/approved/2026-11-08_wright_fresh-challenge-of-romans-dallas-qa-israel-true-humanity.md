## Summary
News report (ChurchLeaders, 3 Nov 2026; page 1 of 2 read) on Wright's four-day Romans lecture series in Dallas and its 2 Nov Q&A. Wright states his current one-sentence summary of Romans: the creator God has rescued, is rescuing, and will rescue the world through Jesus and the Spirit by enabling Jesus' followers to become the true humanity that reflects God's image into the world. In the Q&A he called the view that the modern state of Israel fulfils prophecy unwarranted and dangerous, doubted that the US or Britain can be called Christian nations, read the Beatitudes as a test Western Christians largely fail (justice wanted for ourselves, not others), and named Psalm 72 (the king who brings justice for the poor) as a core biblical political text. This is a secondhand report; the lectures themselves were not heard, and page 2 of the article was not read.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The three-tense "rescue through a true humanity that images God" formula is Wright's compact statement of salvation as vocation, not escape: the saved are a people with a job. That is the cleanest available Wright sentence for the Summa 2026 claim that salvation is inclusion in a united people, and it gives an image-bearing (outward-facing) reading the wiki does not yet hold. The Israel answer confirms his long-standing view that the "people of God" is redefined around the Messiah, not around an ethnic or national state; this bears on Research Question 3 (corporate substance). Citable only after a primary recording of the lectures is found (the preview video, youtube.com/embed/g7uG5o8xyQ4, is a lead).
