## Summary
A Sunday Daily Meditation from CAC's 2026 "Good News for a Fractured World" arc, adapted from Rohr's *Dancing Standing Still: Healing the World from a Place of Prayer*. The piece uses Archimedes' lever-and-fulcrum image to frame contemplative practice as the *fixed point* from which social transformation becomes mechanically possible. Rohr's claim: without a steady, centered, poised, rooted contemplative stance — some distance from "business as usual" — action moves the world reactively rather than transformatively. The fulcrum is not absence-of-action but the stance that *makes action a lever* rather than a mere push. The piece is short, but it gives a single-sentence-able operational definition of why CAC pairs Action *and* Contemplation in its name — the contemplative stance is the precondition for non-reactive transformation, not its alternative.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The "place to stand" image is exactly the operational figure the C2A2 network has been missing for the Rohr side — a clean, citable, geometrical metaphor for the contemplative stance that lets the Summa 2026 syntheses say *what the stance does* rather than *what it feels like*. Pairs cleanly with the project's central theme: perspective-limited agents who *find a place to stand* are precisely those who have stabilized their position in the cognitive light cone enough to act as a lever on the world rather than be levered by it. The image is also a clean bridge to Friston (a low-precision-prior reduction is, mechanically, a fulcrum that makes inference move the agent's behavior rather than thrash it) and to Levin (a stabilized somatic agent is one that has found its fulcrum in its own bounded boundary).

- **Summa 2026 (direct):** Useful for II-II Q.179–182, and for any synthesis where the contemplative-active complementarity is load-bearing.
