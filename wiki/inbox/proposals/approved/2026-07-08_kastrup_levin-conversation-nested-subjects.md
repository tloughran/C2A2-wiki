## Summary
A recorded dialogue between Bernardo Kastrup and Michael Levin. Both accept an idealist premise but diverge sharply on the topology of subjective vantage points: for Levin, conscious agents are nested (particles, cells, organs each retain a perspective while scaling into higher-order selves); for Kastrup, a smaller perspective cannot persist inside a larger one — it is automatically subsumed into a single integrated perspective.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is a direct, high-value stress-test of Kastrup's dissociation (DID) model against Levin's multi-scale competency architecture. It sharpens exactly where analytic idealism's account of individuation (alters of one mind-at-large) accepts or rejects nested subjectivity — a live, unresolved boundary question in Kastrup's program.
