## Summary
Wright's compact (nine-section, ~150pp) reading of Ephesians, published by SPCK (UK) and Zondervan Academic (US) in 2025, with a free first chapter circulated by Zondervan and a long-form December 2025 conversation with Russell Moore. The central thesis: Ephesians presents the church as a "small working model" of God's promised new creation — a heaven-and-earth community that is itself an advance sign of redeemed creation. Wright reads the letter as apocalyptic-but-not-partisan: the "principalities and powers" are real, but the church's vocation is to embody reconciled humanity (Jew and Gentile made "one new man," Eph 2:11–22) as the visible down payment of cosmic renewal. Surfaced this run as a foundational gap-fill: the wiki holds *God's Homecoming* and the eschatology corpus but lacks Wright's mature ecclesiology anchor.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
"The church as a working model of new creation" is the most direct scriptural statement available of the Summa 2026 central claim that loving unity is the telos — here, unity-in-distinction (Jew/Gentile reconciled without erasure) is itself the eschatological sign rather than a means to it. It supplies the ecclesiology the network has lacked: a primary Wright text on corporate humanity as a real composite that *anticipates* cosmic unity. It also gives the Tertia Pars syntheses a 2020s Wright source on the church as composite body.
