## Verification caveats

- **Source.** wiki/rc_document_explorer.html (page title "Resurrecting Civility — Document Explorer", 1.76 MB). It has three parts. (1) A 471-page document pane. Its pages are a ChatGPT transcript (the "ChatGPT Tome") flattened into paragraphs, with "— p.N —" markers. (2) Nine thinker panels (Levin, Hoffman, Carroll, Hawkins, Wolfram, Friston, Fredrickson, Kastrup, Loughran). Each has a model-written summary and PRS cards: 52 cards in total. Cards marked "(Text, p.N)" carry a page locator (27 cards). Cards marked "(Implicit)" carry none (25 cards). (3) An "Ask AI" chat and a "Solved Problems" grid that is built from the cards. The explorer was generated by a project pipeline. Its P/R/S wording is that pipeline's paraphrase, not any thinker's or Tom's.
- **Authorship tags.** "Verbatim" here means the exact words appear in the explorer's visible text (tags removed, HTML entities decoded, curly quotes normalised). The explorer's own card text is not evidence for itself. A passage is [MODEL] if it sits in ChatGPT or pipeline text.
- **Unreliable user anchors.** Fifteen transcript paragraphs carry a user-turn id (`u-tome-…`). At least two of them (pp.3 and 92) are model replies. Anchors therefore cannot establish authorship, and a paragraph that is tagged as user but reads as model text is tagged [UNKNOWN].
- **Criteria.** wiki/c2a2-prs-3d/testcorpus/CRITERIA.md (C1 to C6) is written for links between corpus entries. Inside one source none of them applies directly. C1 (naming) was looked for as an attribution by name. C2 to C6 (inheritance, placement, direction, asymmetry, coinage) do not apply. Tiers were assigned by hand on the High, Medium, Speculative scale.
- **Prior filing.** traditions/loughran/prs_triplets.md PRS-08 and PRS-09 carry explorer locators ("RC Text, p.141"; "RC Text, pp.399–401"). Their source note says they were re-homed from traditions/stump/prs_triplets.md, where they had been filed as Stump PRS-01 and PRS-03. Explorer-derived material has therefore been filed to a named thinker before.
- **Handoff premise.** handoffs/macintyre-crosslink-agent.md says no one has cross-linked MacIntyre's 3RV in this explorer. That is not quite so. "Three Rival Versions" or "3RV" appears on pp.102, 130, 387 and 389. All four are model text, except that p.389 holds Tom's footnote instruction. None of the mentions is a PRS card.
