## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It turns the announcement into actual primary material and extends idealism's psychopathology framework (open question 8) from mental illness to physical and immune illness. It also adds a limit Kastrup sets on his own view: idealism does not imply mental control over the body.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Why do childhood adversity, psychiatric illness and autoimmune disease cluster together? Dualist medicine treats them as separate systems.
  Resource: Analytic idealism's dual-aspect reading, where the immune system is the extrinsic appearance of psychological process; evidence that attention changes immune response (histamine skin test)
  Solution: Treat the immunological and psychological selves as two descriptions of one process, which supports integrated clinical models
  Confidence: Medium (host's framing; Kastrup's own wording not yet verified)
  Evidence: Page: "The immune system and the psychological self would be different accounts of the same thing."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Does idealism imply that illness is under voluntary mental control, with the blame-the-patient consequences that follow?
  Resource: Kastrup's distinction between "mental" and "under our control" (dissociation means most mental process is not accessible to the ego)
  Solution: Idealism widens what healing can mean without promising control. Mind-over-matter readings are blocked.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Host's paraphrase: "Believing everything is mental does not mean it's all under our control."
