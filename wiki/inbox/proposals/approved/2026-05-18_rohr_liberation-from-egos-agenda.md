## Summary
A Daily Meditation from CAC's 2026 "Good News for a Fractured World" theme arc in which Rohr argues that Jesus's central liberatory act is freeing us from the agendas of our inflated egos — what the contemplative tradition has called the false self. The meditation does light theological work but is conceptually substantial: Rohr explicitly identifies "what Jesus was liberating us from" with what we would now call "the ego or the false self," and frames detachment-from-personal-agendas-and-power as the operational shape of Christian discipleship. This continues Rohr's *Immortal Diamond* / *Falling Upward* line of work (true-self vs. false-self) but reframes it scripturally for the 2026 Bible-as-liberation-narrative theme of the year.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is a clean, recent (within the 60-day window), citable Rohr text on the *operational* meaning of liberation — the false-self/ego frame translated into scriptural-Jesus language. For C2A2 it (a) supplies the Rohr-side anchor for any synthesis where the question turns on the agent's relation to its own self-model, (b) bridges directly to Friston (the ego/false-self is the over-precision-weighted self-model that liberation reduces), Hoffman (the ego is the interface-self that universal consciousness is operating through), and Kastrup (the false self is the alter-mistaking-itself-for-the-ground), and (c) provides a 2026 Rohr statement of the perennial-tradition position on ego-as-construct that the network's mind-monist members need a contemplative-tradition pair for.
