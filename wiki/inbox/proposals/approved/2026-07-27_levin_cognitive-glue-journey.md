## Summary
A first-person retrospective essay by Levin (journal *Bioelectricity*, vol. 8 no. 2, 2026) tracing the arc of his research program from developmental biology to the "cognitive glue" thesis: bioelectric networks are the mechanism that binds the goal-directedness of individual cells into higher-order systems with larger cognitive capacity, scaling agency from physiology to mind across anatomical morphospace (development, regeneration, cancer suppression). It is a programmatic self-summary rather than a new empirical result.
