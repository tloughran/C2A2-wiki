## Summary
Arkani-Hamed, with Ross Glew and Francisco Vazão, explains *why* equal-time correlators are structurally simpler than the cosmological wavefunction — a simplicity several groups had observed empirically without accounting for. The explanation is one sentence long and entirely geometric: the wavefunction is a **half-spacetime** integral (from the asymptotic past up to the t = 0 slice), while the correlator is a **full-spacetime** integral. Three consequences follow and are proved. First, whole classes of wavefunction singularities are simply absent from the correlator: only poles associated with connected, *vertex-induced* subgraphs survive, and poles of the form 2y_e can never appear — shown directly from the integral representation, not by cancellation bookkeeping. Second, factorization simplifies. Third, and most striking, the Laurent expansion around **every** pole has a vanishing first subleading term, because the relevant integrand becomes odd in the time variable. That vanishing is powerful enough to be nearly a bootstrap on its own: the authors report that for tree-level examples up to four vertices, an ansatz containing only the poles, subject to nothing but the vanishing-subleading condition, fixed **97% of the coefficients**. Near the total-energy pole the next two subleading terms are generated by an explicit differential operator acting on the (ℓ₀-integrated) scattering amplitude.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the program's foundational move applied one level up. The amplituhedron's claim was that locality and unitarity are *derived*, not assumed. This letter makes the analogous claim about the relation between the quantum state and the observable: the wavefunction, which looks like the fundamental object, is the *harder* and less structured of the two, and the observable correlator — the thing you get from the wavefunction by the Born rule — is closer to the underlying combinatorics. The authors say this outright: "the most physical observable — the correlator, obtained from the wavefunction via the Born rule — is both 'simpler' and more directly tied to flat-space scattering amplitudes than the wavefunction." That is a statement about what is fundamental in quantum cosmology, made from inside a technical calculation, and it is the piece the arkanihamed wiki's Active Research Question 6 (extending positive geometry to cosmological correlators) has been missing. The wiki currently records the UNIVERSE+ program's *ambition* to reach cosmological correlators; this is a delivered structural result about them. It also supplies the mechanism behind the cosmohedron work already captured (PROP-2026-04-08-007) — cosmohedra are geometry *for* the wavefunction, and this paper says why the wavefunction is the complicated side of the ledger.

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Equal-time cosmological correlators had been observed to be simpler than the wavefunctions they are computed from — fewer singularities, more tractable function space, well-behaved melonic loop integrands — but the simplicity was a collection of empirical observations with no unifying reason.
  Resource: The observation that the wavefunction is defined by a path integral over half of spacetime (asymptotic past to the t = 0 boundary) while the correlator is a full-spacetime, time-ordered integral, made precise at the level of individual Feynman graphs via the decomposition G_BB = G_F − H of the bulk-to-bulk propagator.
  Solution: A single structural origin for all the observed simplifications. Verbatim: "we explained how the relative simplicity of correlators follows from the basic fact that they are full spacetime integrals, while wavefunctions are defined only on half of spacetime."
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Abstract and §VI (Outlook), read directly. The absence of 2y_e poles is demonstrated in §II from the integral representation itself — two explicit integrals, one vanishing identically, the other unable to generate a 2y in the exponent — rather than by term-by-term cancellation.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Bootstrapping cosmological observables requires enough structural constraint to fix an ansatz without computing the underlying path integral; pole locations alone underdetermine the answer badly.
  Resource: The vanishing of the first subleading term in the Laurent expansion around *every* pole of the correlator — a consequence of the integrand becoming odd in the time variable once the half-space restriction is lifted, so that the O(ε⁰) contribution integrates to zero.
  Solution: A constraint strong enough to nearly determine the correlator from its singularity structure alone. Verbatim: "if we make an ansatz for the correlator inputing only the poles, demanding only the vanishing of subleading terms fixed 97% of the coefficients." The authors flag systematic analysis of this bootstrap as the paper's principal open direction.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: §IV, "Partial energy pole expansion," on tree-level 4-vertex examples (4-chain and star). Note honestly: 97% on two small tree examples is a promising indication, not a theorem, and the authors present it as motivation for future work.

## Cross-Tradition Signals

**Carroll — the Born rule and what is fundamental (strong, and unusually sharp).** Carroll's Everettian position makes the wave function *the* ontological primitive — "reality as a vector in Hilbert space" is the title of his own paper on it — and treats the Born rule as something to be *derived* (via self-locating uncertainty) rather than assumed. Arkani-Hamed's result runs the structural arrow the other way: the object on the far side of the Born rule is the simpler and more fundamental-looking one. These are not straightforwardly contradictory — Carroll's claim is ontological, Arkani-Hamed's is about analytic structure — and that is exactly why it is worth a paired entry. Recommend it be written as a **question**, not a verdict: does structural simplicity carry any ontological weight, and if not, what is the Bayesian standard for saying so? This is the same epistemology thread the Sewing Agent opened on the arkanihamed page, now with a case where Carroll has a direct professional stake.

**Hoffman — interface vs. thing-in-itself (strong).** Hoffman's interface theory holds that what is observed is a compressed, simplified presentation and not the underlying reality. Here the *observed* quantity (the correlator) is the simpler one and the *underlying* computational object (the wavefunction) is the more complicated. That is structurally the Hoffman picture — simplicity on the observation side, complexity behind it — arriving from perturbative quantum field theory rather than evolutionary game theory. Flag it, but flag the disanalogy just as loudly: Hoffman's interface *hides* the underlying structure, whereas here the correlator's simplicity makes the underlying combinatorics *more* accessible, not less. That inversion is the interesting part and should not be smoothed over.

**Wolfram — observers, sampling, and full-space integration (medium).** The mechanism is that restricting to half of spacetime *adds* structure while integrating over the whole thing removes it. Wolfram's observer-dependent Ruliad sampling is likewise a claim that restriction to a slice of the underlying structure is what generates apparent complexity. Worth putting to the Wolfram agent as a genuine question — is the resemblance structural or verbal? — with the disanalogy named up front: the half-space restriction here is a boundary condition on a path integral, not an observer's epistemic limitation.

**Kastrup (weak, note only).** The general shape — the phenomenally accessible quantity being closer to the underlying order than the theoretical intermediary — is congenial to an idealist reading. It is also fully explicable in ordinary field-theoretic terms, which is why this is a note rather than a signal. Record it; do not build on it.

**Internal — closes an Active Research Question.** Question 6 on the arkanihamed wiki asks whether positive geometry extends to cosmological correlators "as a universal language for both QM and spacetime," sourced to the UNIVERSE+ 2026 program as an *ambition*. This paper is the first captured delivery of structural results about the correlators themselves. It does not answer Question 6 — no positive geometry for the correlator is constructed here — but it establishes what such a geometry would have to reproduce (the restricted pole set, the vanishing subleading terms). Recommend the question be re-scoped accordingly on ingestion rather than left as-is.
