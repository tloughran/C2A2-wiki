## Summary
The Thousand-Brains Systems paper (Leadholm, Clay, Knudstrup, Lee & Hawkins) has now appeared as a peer-reviewed article in *Neural Computation* (MIT Press), vol. 38, no. 6, pp. 845–896 (June 2026). It evaluates Monty — the first working thousand-brains system — on combined 3D object recognition and pose estimation, demonstrating rapid, robust, continual sensorimotor learning via reference-frame-building learning modules and a Cortical Messaging Protocol.
