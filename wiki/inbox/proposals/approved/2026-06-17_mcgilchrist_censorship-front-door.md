## Summary
In a short Substack post, McGilchrist reposts and endorses Mary Harrington's UnHerd article on UK proposals for device-level image scanning, mandatory age/ID verification, and digital ID, framing it with his own warning: "Whether or not you think you can trust this government, sooner or later there will be a government that we certainly should not trust. We must not give them a free pass to censoring our thoughts." His own contribution is the framing — freedom of thought and speech as "one of the most vital issues of our times."

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It extends McGilchrist's cultural-pathology diagnosis into the politics of speech and surveillance. The Harrington piece he selects characterizes the governing impulse as a retreat into "the only aspects of the country he understands: the dimension of words, and that of rules" — a description that maps almost exactly onto McGilchrist's account of the left hemisphere's domain (tokens, rules, control via re-presentation) divorced from relational trust.
