## Summary
Lyons, Pio-Lopez & Levin argue that in decentralized systems, alignment is always alignment *to a virtual governor* — an abstract governing entity that is not a physical object but is embodied in the coordinating relationships among agents (e.g., a bioelectric network's morphogenetic target, or a market's price system). Virtual governors are causally instructive: though non-physical, they control system behavior by pulling the parts toward higher-level goals. The paper frames alignment as a single problem spanning biological development, social systems, and engineered/AI systems.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This directly extends Levin's collective-intelligence program from morphogenesis into a general theory of coordination, and supplies the missing conceptual bridge between his bioelectric "cognitive glue" and the AI-alignment framing he has been developing (cf. Lyons & Levin, "From Cancer to AI Alignment," 2026-04). It reframes the anatomical attractor as one instance of a substrate-independent "virtual governor."

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: What is the target of "alignment" in a decentralized system that has no central controller and no physical locus of authority?
  Resource: The "virtual governor" — an abstract, relationally-embodied governing entity (bioelectric morphogenetic target, price system) that is causally efficacious without being a physical object.
  Solution: A unified theory in which alignment across biology, economics, and AI is always alignment to a virtual governor; coordination is achieved by parts converging on a higher-level goal-representation carried in their coupling, not in any component.
  Confidence: High
  Evidence: Authors state virtual governors "control the behavior of a system by aligning its parts toward higher-level goals" and are "causally instructive" despite being non-physical; alignment presented as central across "biological development to social and engineered systems."

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: Can AI-alignment be understood with the same formalism as developmental/biological coordination, rather than as a sui generis engineering problem?
  Resource: Substrate-independent generalization of Levin's collective-intelligence account to social and engineered multi-agent systems.
  Solution: Treating AI alignment as alignment-to-a-virtual-governor makes diverse-intelligence coordination a single tractable problem class, importing biological insights (homeostatic set-points, morphogenetic attractors) into AI governance.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Paper explicitly spans "biological development to social and engineered systems"; continues the trajectory of Lyons & Levin's homeostatic-externalities alignment work.
