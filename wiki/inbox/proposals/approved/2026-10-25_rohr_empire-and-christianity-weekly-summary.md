## Summary
Rohr's week-42 summary argues that the culture's operative identity has shifted from Descartes' "I think, therefore I am" to "I win, therefore I am," and that empire domesticated the gospel into a top-down, war- and wealth-tolerant religion. Jesus is read as exposing and inverting the pyramid; Christianity is "the religion of the wound," where the risen Christ keeps his wounds and the "ultimate loser" is the one God raises. The practical turn is shadow-ownership: healing comes from acknowledging wounds rather than projecting fear onto other races, religions, classes, or genders.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is conceptual work, not devotion: it gives the Universal Christ frame a political-psychological diagnosis (identity-by-winning as the false self at civilizational scale) and ties the descent pattern to resurrection-with-wounds. It is citable for the Summa 2026 project wherever the Tertia Pars treats Christ's passion and the status-reversal of the Beatitudes, and pairs with the prior pending/approved Rohr work on the myth of redemptive violence and power-with.
