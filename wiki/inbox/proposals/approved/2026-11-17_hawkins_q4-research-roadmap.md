## Summary
Recorded TBP research-planning session (presenter: nleadholm). After a quarter of theory work, Q4 focuses on unsupervised learning theory plus stage-2 compositional objects. The agenda includes benchmark issues, separate cortical and hippocampal systems for learning novel versus already-known objects, recovering from recognition errors, a capabilities map, and open questions on unsupervised learning and abstract versus concrete models.

**Provenance caveat:** TBP team session; Hawkins not a listed presenter. Distinct from the already-captured "2026/10 Q4 Roadmap and Q3 Review" (PROP-2026-10-23-003), which was the platform/program roadmap; this one is the research agenda.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The cortex/hippocampus split is a new architectural commitment for Monty, echoing complementary-learning-systems theory: fast hippocampal capture of the novel, slow cortical consolidation. The "abstract versus concrete models" item bears directly on Question 4 (mechanism of abstraction), which PRS-20 reframed.
