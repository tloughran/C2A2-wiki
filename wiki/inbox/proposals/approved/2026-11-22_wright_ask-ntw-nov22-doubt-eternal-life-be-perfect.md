## Summary
Wright and Mike Bird answer three listener questions. (1) A lifelong Christian finds resurrection and new creation hard to imagine; Wright answers that confidence about the future rests on Jesus's resurrection, and that prayer, worship, Eucharist and service sustain faith through doubt. (2) Matthew 5:48 ("Be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect") — whether Jesus demands moral perfection or points to something deeper. (3) If Christ has already won, should the world be steadily improving? Drawing on *Surprised by Hope*, Wright says Christians work for justice, mercy and beauty as signs of the kingdom without imagining they build it themselves. **Caveat:** this summary rests on the official episode description, not a transcript; the content of the Matt 5:48 answer is not stated there.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Two moves are useful for the Summa 2026 synthesis. First, Wright treats doubt as a condition *within* faith sustained by practice — a scriptural-pastoral analogue of "working out salvation in fear and trembling" under perspective-limitation. Second, the kingdom-is-not-built-by-us answer constrains any "computational telos" language: human work is real and lasting but does not itself generate the end state. Matt 5:48 (*teleios*) is directly relevant to the network's telos vocabulary and worth a transcript check before ingestion.
