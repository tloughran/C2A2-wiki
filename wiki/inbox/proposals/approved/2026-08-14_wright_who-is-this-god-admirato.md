## Why This Matters for This Tradition

Wright **Active Research Question 8** asks whether critical realism can hold Rohr's claim that revelation is the self-disclosure of a Person rather than a deposit of propositions (Rohr PRS-40) without losing the text's referential control. That question needs Wright talking about *who God is* rather than about what a text says — and the wiki currently has almost nothing of that kind in his own voice from 2026; the recent captures are exegetical (parables, 2 John, Ephesians, angels) or apologetic-in-dialogue (Collins). A doctrine-of-God broadcast is the right genre for the question, which is why the card is worth keeping open despite the failed retrieval.

Secondarily it may bear on **Question 5** (whether resurrection as a historical event with cosmological significance challenges or complements Carroll's physicalism), since a general-audience "who is this God" treatment typically has to say what kind of thing God is taken to be before any historical argument runs.
