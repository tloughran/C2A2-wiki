## Summary
Wolfram's biweekly unscripted livestream, four days after his pure-math-and-AI essay. Published segments: "AI, LLMs and the future of mathematics" (0:24), "The future of academic publishing and journals" (21:03), and "Detecting AI-generated images" (1:06:05). ## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The publishing segment is the new part: it asks how a research community records and certifies results once AI does much of the producing. That bears on the ruliology program (Wolfram's own non-journal publishing model) and directly on C2A2's question of how traditions keep a durable, trusted record.
