## Summary
In a 1h21m biweekly AMA, Wolfram recounts how his expectations about AI shifted: he expected "fuzzy pattern matching" to be the key,
explored neural nets around 1982-83, and judged the field unpromising by the 1990s, yet says "nobody expected" the 2011-12
image-identification results or ChatGPT in 2022. He now says much of the AI field is "more marketing than reality" and wants to
"do a little bit more science before I say more." He also describes NKS-era process constraints and several unfinished or unpublished
physics ideas (a 1977 cosmology paper; an unwritten Part 3 of a 1981 vacuum-properties series).

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It documents Wolfram's own account of why a ruliological/"more science" stance toward AI is pending rather than settled, which is
the setting for open question 3 (AI's location in rulial space). It adds no new technical claim.
