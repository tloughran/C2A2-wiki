## Summary
The full recording of McGilchrist's concluding Ralston symposium lecture, "Cognitive Freedom in the Age of AI," is now on YouTube (video ID mJ5oInI6klA). As with Lecture 1, the existing triplets drawn from it — PRS-64, PRS-65, PRS-66 — were written from Ralston's published event description, and PRS-66 is explicitly coded Speculative because "the falsifiability framing is an inference from the lecture summary, not a claim the summary attributes to McGilchrist."

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
PRS-64 records a Stapp-inflected quantum treatment of free will that the wiki itself annotates as "contested physics [that] should not stand unchallenged." That annotation was written without hearing how McGilchrist actually deploys Stapp. Whether he leans on quantum indeterminism as a mechanism or invokes it only illustratively is the difference between a serious vulnerability in the program and a passing reference — and the recording settles it.
