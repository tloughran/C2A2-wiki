## Summary
Pio-Lopez, McMillen, Pezzulo and Levin argue that niche construction (an agent changes its environment, and the changed environment then steers the agent) is not limited to ecology or genetics: it holds at any scale and on substrates with no ecology. The niche is recast as a computational scratchpad that agents write to and read from, offloading cognition into the environment. The new empirical claim is "active stigmergy": in wet-lab work, cells leave bioelectrically active traces that keep influencing neighbouring cells after the producing cell has moved on.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It extends Levin's cognitive-offloading and multiscale-agency program (cf. "Cognitive Offloading Is a Cognitive Universal", PROP-2026-08-03) from claims about agents to claims about the agent-environment loop, with a bioelectric mechanism offered as evidence.
