## Summary
Levin's "Platonic space / ingressing minds" framework proposes that, beyond genetics (hardware) and environment (boundary conditions), biological and cognitive forms are partly determined by a structured, non-physical space of patterns — ranging from mathematical truths to higher-agency "kinds of minds." Physical systems act as pointers/interfaces that "ingress" (pull into physical reality) pre-existing patterns from this space. Novel embodiments without evolutionary history (xenobots, anthrobots) are proposed as probes for mapping which adjacent patterns get ingressed. An ongoing online Symposium on the Platonic Space is developing the research agenda, with a contributor discussion on 2026-05-29.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is Levin's most ambitious recent conceptual move and is currently absent from the wiki. It reframes morphogenesis and basal cognition as the ingression of pre-existing patterns rather than purely emergent assembly, and bears directly on the Conscious Realism paradigm and on the hard problem (it is explicitly pitched as a "Platonic space background for questions in consciousness").

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Genetics plus environment under-determine the goal-directed, problem-solving forms observed in biology and cognition; conventional biology has no account of where genuinely novel adaptive patterns originate.
  Resource: "Platonic space" — a structured, non-physical space of patterns (mathematical through higher-agency "kinds of minds") that physical embodiments interface with — and "ingression," the process by which a physical system points to and instantiates such a pattern.
  Solution: A falsifiable research agenda: construct novel embodiments lacking evolutionary precedent (xenobots/anthrobots) and observe which adjacent patterns they ingress, thereby beginning to "map" the structure of Platonic space empirically.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Levin, "Ingressing Minds: Causal Patterns Beyond Genetics and Environment in Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments" (PsyArXiv, 2025); "Platonic Space: brief argument and research agenda"; Symposium on the Platonic Space, contributor discussion 2026-05-29.
