## Summary
A full-day in-person symposium (Saturday, May 9, 2026, 10AM–5PM) in which McGilchrist gave opening and concluding lectures and was in extended conversation with iconographer/symbolist Jonathan Pageau on the title theme: what artificial intelligence does to, and reveals about, the human soul. McGilchrist's now-public position — developed across his recent Closer to Truth (May 8/10) and Premier Unbelievable (March 26) appearances — is that AI operates in a purely left-hemisphere mode (analytic decomposition, re-presentation of the already-known) and is structurally barred from the right-hemisphere participatory attention through which meaning, beauty, and the sacred are disclosed. The Pageau pairing frames the question symbolically/theologically rather than only neuroscientifically. Recordings are issued to ticket holders after the event; public release date not yet established, but the event has occurred.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the most explicit application yet of McGilchrist's hemisphere theory as a *diagnostic instrument trained directly on AI* — extending the "AI as consequence-variable of one's theory of consciousness" move (Closer to Truth, 2026-05-10) into a sustained, soul-and-symbol-level treatment in dialogue with a non-neuroscientific interlocutor.
