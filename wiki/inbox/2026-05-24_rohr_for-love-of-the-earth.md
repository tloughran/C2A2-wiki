## Summary
A week of CAC Daily Meditations (19–25 April 2026, Earth Day week, within the 2026 "Good News for a Fractured World" cycle) on encountering God through nature, on healing the rift between our values and our daily lives by tending whatever "patch of ground" we have been given (Ragan Sutterfield), and on regarding wild creatures as "sacred others" — attention and conversation with the more-than-human world as "participation in the presence of Christ" (Victoria Loorz) — alongside Rohr's reflections on creation as a first incarnation. Constituent meditations include "Soul and the Natural World," "All that Breathes Gives Praise," "Recognizing God's Grace," and "Hospitality on Our Earth Home."

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the clearest recent articulation of Rohr's Universal-Christ-in-matter thesis — Christ as the eternal pattern of self-giving love present in all creation. For C2A2 it directly supplies the cosmic-Christ frame in which mind-monist claims (creation as the expression of a single underlying mind/consciousness) become lived spiritual practice rather than bare metaphysics. "Wild ones as sacred others" is a contemplative correlate of attributing interiority across the agential hierarchy.
