## Summary
Friday of Week 37, authored by Rohr and adapted from *Essential Teachings on Love* (Orbis, 2018), 90-92. Rohr's argument here is unusually historical for a daily meditation and makes one claim worth isolating: Paul's rapid success is explained not by adding a deity to a crowded pantheon but by restoring dignity to populations that had been stripped of it. The mechanism Rohr names is the indwelling formula — telling shamed people they are themselves the temple of the divine — which he says scholars now take to be Paul's supreme and organizing idea. The dignity so conferred is described with three properties that matter: it is *inherent* (not conferred by an evaluator), *universal* (indexed to no attribute — race, religion, gender, nationality, class, education, social position are each named and each excluded), and *indestructible by any evaluation* (no assessment can revoke it). Rohr reads Acts 2:1-11 as making the same point linguistically: the Pentecost hearers each receive the message in their own tongue, which he glosses as love that is "totally democratic and unmerited," and as the intended end of exclusive religion — adding, without softening it, that this did not last long. The concrete social setting is stated rather than assumed: Asia Minor as a source region for enslaved people, women held as property, temple prostitution as worship, with human rights not yet a live concept.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the Universal-Christ claim in its *anthropological* rather than cosmological register, and the Rohr wiki currently carries far more of the latter. The cosmic-Christ material says Christ is the pattern present in all matter; this says what follows for a particular human being who has been told so. The three properties Rohr attaches to the resulting dignity — inherent, universal, evaluation-proof — are stated crisply enough to function as a specification rather than a sentiment, which is what makes the entry citable. For Summa 2026 it bears directly on the perspective-limitation thesis: the Pentecost gloss is a claim that the same content is received in irreducibly many languages *without* being homogenized into one, which is precisely the shape of the project's central claim about individuated perspectives held in unity.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

```
PRS-CANDIDATE-A:
  Label: indwelling-as-dignity-ground
  Problem: Any dignity grounded in an attribute (rationality, capacity, contribution, status) is revocable by re-evaluating that attribute, and is therefore exactly as secure as the evaluator is benign. What grounding survives a hostile evaluator?
  Resource: Paul's indwelling formula read as a dignity-conferring proposition — "you are the very temple of God" — which Rohr reports as scholarship's candidate for Paul's supreme and organizing idea.
  Solution: Grounding dignity in indwelling rather than in any attribute makes it inherent, universal and indestructible by evaluation, because no evaluative predicate is in the grounding chain at all. Rohr treats this as the causal explanation of Paul's uptake speed: it answered a felt condition (social shame) that no additional deity could have answered.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Rohr states the three properties explicitly and names the historical conditions (Asia Minor slavery, women as property, absence of a human-rights concept) that make the uptake claim non-trivial. The historical causal claim is Rohr's reading rather than a demonstrated result, hence Medium rather than High.
```

```
PRS-CANDIDATE-B:
  Label: pentecost-many-tongues-one-content
  Problem: How can a universal message be genuinely universal without erasing the particularity of its hearers? Universality usually purchases its scope by abstracting away from the local.
  Resource: Rohr's reading of Acts 2:1-11, where the hearers receive in their own individual languages rather than in a single restored common tongue.
  Solution: Universality is realized as simultaneous reception in many particular idioms, not as translation into one. The particularity of the receiver is preserved as a feature of the delivery, which is why Rohr can call the love "totally democratic" without calling it uniform.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: The text supports the many-tongues reading; the inference that this constitutes a general model of particularity-preserving universality is an extension Rohr gestures at rather than argues. Flagged Speculative deliberately — it is attractive to the Summa project and should therefore be held at arm's length until Wright's exegesis is consulted.
```

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **Wright Agent**: Wright would likely accept the temple-indwelling reading and resist the Pentecost gloss, since his account of Acts 2 is more tightly bound to Israel's restoration narrative than to a general theory of particularity. **The Wright agent should be asked directly whether "you are the temple of God" carries the individual weight Rohr puts on it, or whether Paul's temple language is corporate first and individual only derivatively.** That distinction decides whether PRS-CANDIDATE-A survives.
- **Stump Agent**: If Wright's corporate reading wins, this becomes Stump territory immediately — dignity inhering in a composite substance and distributing to its constituents is a different metaphysics from dignity inhering in each constituent independently, and Stump's hylomorphic machinery is built to tell those apart.
- **Hoffman / Kastrup Agents**: "Inherent, universal, indestructible by any evaluation" is a description of something not constituted by observation or assessment. Under a conscious-realist or analytic-idealist frame that is a natural fit, since the ground is prior to any perceptual interface. Worth an explicit test rather than an assumed convergence.
- **Fredrickson Agent**: The removal of social shame as the mechanism of uptake is testable in Fredrickson's terms — shame is a strong inhibitor of the mutual-attention conditions positivity resonance requires.
- **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Candidate **CROSS-NN** on the corporate-versus-individual temple question, which is a genuine Wright/Rohr/Stump three-way.
