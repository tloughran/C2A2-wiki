## Summary
Fredrickson's new book is a career-spanning synthesis organized around five successive scientific breakthroughs across four decades. Each of the five sections opens with personal and scientific backstory, then culminates by reprinting curated excerpts from three key articles per breakthrough — fifteen "key scientific contributions" in total. The framing thesis: positive emotions are "the tiny engines that drive human flourishing." It is both a research retrospective and a methodological/career-reflection work (including what she would do differently starting out today).
