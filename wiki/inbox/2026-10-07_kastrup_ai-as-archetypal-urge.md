## Summary
A With Reality in Mind session (held Sunday 27 September 2026) in which Kastrup returns to AI, building on two earlier statements: last year's session ("Robots will be tools, not tyrants": AI will be intelligent but not conscious, and its lack of coherence makes a robot tyranny unlikely) and a more recent public clip (YouTube hYAuQPn5szA) arguing that AI development is driven by an *archetypal urge*, not by greed. The session was billed as covering "more recent developments and their implications."

**Evidence caveat:** the full recording is members-only. This card is built from the public session page and its framing; the specific claims made in the session are not verified. Reviewer may want to hold until a transcript or public clip is available.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
It joins two lines the wiki already tracks separately: Kastrup's archetype theory (archetypes as patterns that "always are but never were"; PROP-2026-05-05-008) and his intelligence-vs-consciousness split as the founder of the AI company Euclyd (PROP-2026-06-17-002, PROP-2026-08-05-006). Reading AI-building itself as an archetypal drive moves AI from an *object* of his metaphysics to an *expression* of it.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: Why is humanity building AI at a pace and cost that market incentives alone do not seem to explain?
  Resource: Kastrup's archetype theory applied to technology: AI development as the acting-out of an archetypal urge in collective mind, not primarily a product of greed
  Solution: Treat the AI drive as a psychological/archetypal phenomenon to be understood (and integrated) rather than only regulated as an economic one
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Session page: "he spoke about how AI development is motivated by an Archetypal Urge, not greed." Session content itself not verified (members-only).

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: If AI will be highly intelligent but, on analytic idealism, not conscious, what follows for its risks?
  Resource: Kastrup's intelligence/consciousness split plus his claim that AI lacks the *coherence* of a dissociated alter
  Solution: Danger comes from misuse and incoherent capability, not from an AI with its own will; a "robotic tyranny" is unlikely
  Confidence: Medium (stated in the prior session; the 27 Sept update is unverified)
  Evidence: Session page summary of the earlier "Robots will be tools, not tyrants" recording.
