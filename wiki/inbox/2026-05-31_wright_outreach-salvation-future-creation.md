## Summary
Drawing on his 2026 book *God's Homecoming: The Forgotten Promise of Future Renewal*, Wright reframes salvation, resurrection, heaven, and the kingdom of God as facets of a single coherent narrative: God's long-promised return to dwell with and renew all creation. Salvation is not extraction of souls to a disembodied heaven but inclusion in the renewed-creation people among whom God comes to dwell. "Heaven" is God's space overlapping and finally suffusing the world, not an escape destination; the kingdom is the present, partial form of that homecoming.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This interview is the most compact recent articulation of the precise claim the Summa 2026 project treats as its scriptural ground truth: salvation = inclusion in the covenanted people united with God in a renewed creation. It supplies a citable primary statement of "God's homecoming" as the integrating frame across salvation/resurrection/heaven/kingdom — currently scattered, not unified, in the Wright wiki. It is the scriptural anchor for the project's central theme that loving unity (not individual escape) is the telos.
