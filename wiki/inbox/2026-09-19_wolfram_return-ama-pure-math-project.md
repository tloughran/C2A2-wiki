## Summary

Wolfram returned to livestreaming on 2026-09-18 with a combined personal update and AMA,
ending a roughly seven-week public hiatus that followed his wife Elise Cawley's death
(memorialized in his 2026-08-04 post). The description names three substantive items: the
return itself, a **new "Wolfram Institute Pure Math Project"** that he began discussing at
**ICM 2026** (International Congress of Mathematicians, Philadelphia, 23–30 July 2026), and
the completion of the **2026 Wolfram Summer Research Institute with 50 original projects**,
many aligned with Wolfram Institute research directions.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition

A **new named research program** is the single highest-value signal this agent watches for,
because Wolfram's own criterion for a paradigm — and the C2A2 criterion borrowed from Levin —
is the research program a paradigm generates. "Pure Math Project" is the first new
Institute-level program named since the Physics Project, and it would be the operational
arm of the metamathematics thread that has sat dormant in this tradition since
*Metaphysics and the Ruliad* (Feb 2026) and the *P vs. NP* essay (Jan 2026).

It also **closes a real gap in the tradition's activity record.** Independent checks this

## Cross-Tradition Signals

**CROSS-017 / CROSS-024 (Wolfram ↔ Arkani-Hamed) — direct bearing, if the project is what
its name suggests.** Both entries turn on whether hypergraph rewriting and positive geometry
are windows on one pre-geometric structure. That is a *mathematical* question about the
relationship between two formal objects, and CROSS-024 notes Wolfram supplied the
metaphysical scaffolding (emes, the ruliad as ontological ground) without supplying the
mathematics. A standing pure-math program at the Institute is exactly the instrument that
could take up the mathematical half. **This connection is conditional on the project's
actual scope and should not be entered into the cross-program index until the recording is
heard.**

**Stump / Kastrup — dormant thread, possibly reactivated.** CROSS-024 flags that Wolfram's
Templeton "Computational Metaphysics" engagement opened dialogue paths with Stump and
Kastrup. The ontological status of mathematical objects is a question Thomism has a worked
answer to and analytic idealism has a different one. A Wolfram program on pure mathematics
puts a third answer on the table.

**Levin — research-program criterion, meta-level.** Levin's own standard is that a paradigm
is justified by the research program and track record it generates. The C2A2 network adopted
that standard. A newly named program is therefore a *measurable* event under it, and the
50-project count is a first data point. This is a signal about how to score traditions, not
a substantive claim connecting Wolfram and Levin.

standard?" — Status: Active, Open challenge).** Marked here only to note it was checked, not
