## Summary
Closer to Truth (Robert Lawrence Kuhn's long-running consciousness series, syndicated via Lifeboat News) released a short-form interview with McGilchrist on May 10, 2026 in which he is asked directly whether AI can become conscious. McGilchrist argues that the answer is structurally downstream of one's theory of consciousness: under physicalist accounts AI consciousness is in principle inevitable on sufficient compute; under hemispheric-attention and consciousness-as-fundamental accounts it is at minimum heavily constrained because consciousness is bound to embodiment, biological time, and a participatory mode of attention that current AI architectures do not instantiate. He resists both blanket "AI cannot be conscious" and blanket "AI will soon be conscious" framings, treating the question as a load-bearing variable in AI ethics, AI rights, and policy.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the second consecutive Closer to Truth segment in which McGilchrist makes a metaphysical commitment in a public-facing venue — the first (Life After Death, PROP-2026-05-05-006) located him near the consciousness-as-fundamental cluster on ontology; this one extends the same demarcation move to the AI consciousness question, the most policy-consequential corner of the consciousness debate in 2026. It also makes explicit a position that PRS-18 (Premier Unbelievable) marked as Speculative — that AI may be structurally limited to left-hemisphere modes of processing — moving it from speculative to clearly-stated by McGilchrist in a Kuhn-curated venue. For the C2A2 tradition-accelerator, this is the most direct possible signal: McGilchrist is now publicly defending principled limits on AI agency that map onto the human–AI division of labor C2A2's architecture already assumes.
