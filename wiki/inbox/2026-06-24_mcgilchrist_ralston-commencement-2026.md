## Summary
A short (~9 minute) commencement address delivered by McGilchrist in his capacity as Chancellor of Ralston College and published 21 June 2026. As a formal address to graduating students, it states what a McGilchristian education is *for* — the cultivation of attention, character, and the capacity to meet the world — at the threshold where students leave the formative community and enter the world.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is McGilchrist speaking not as cultural critic but as an institutional steward of an actual pedagogical community (Ralston). It is the clearest available instance of the hemispheric program *enacted* as an educational charter rather than argued as a thesis — the institutional counterpart to the personal "being in the world" pedagogy of PROP-2026-06-24-001.
