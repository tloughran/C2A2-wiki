## Summary
A recorded session (With Reality in Mind, May 26, 2026) in which Kastrup addresses seeing through the illusion of self and the question of "remembering life before birth" — i.e., what the dissociated alter's boundaries imply about experiential continuity before and beyond the individual life. ## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The program's identity story has two poles in the wiki — the alter as real-but-derivative (PRS-02) and the Daimon as principled individuation (PRS-08). A session explicitly on the *illusoriness* of self tests how Kastrup balances these: how much of personal identity survives analytic scrutiny, and what (if anything) is remembered or retained across the dissociative boundary. This bears directly on the soteriological-commitments question opened by the Spira dialogue (PRS-20).
