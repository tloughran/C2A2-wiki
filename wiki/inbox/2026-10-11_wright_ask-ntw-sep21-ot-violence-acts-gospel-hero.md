## Summary
Ask NT Wright Anything, three questions. (1) Mark's Gospel likely lost its original ending, and perhaps its beginning. (2) Acts is neither just church history nor a church-planting manual; its real hero is the gospel itself, travelling from Jerusalem to Rome. (3) On Old Testament violence, Wright weighs divine judgment and Israel's own failures, and argues these texts must be read inside the larger story that climaxes in Jesus and the cross.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
Item (3) is Wright's narrative-climax hermeneutic applied to the hardest texts: a whole-story reading of Scripture, as opposed to proof-texting or excising the Old Testament. The wiki needs this as the scriptural guardrail whenever network syntheses invoke "the God revealed in Jesus." Item (2) gives a new frame (the message as agent). Item (1) overlaps an existing Mike Bird substack proposal on Mark's ending; no new triplet for it.
