## Summary
The May 1, 2026 episode of *Everything Belongs* Season 4 — CAC's current podcast series, anchored on Rohr's *The Enneagram: A Christian Perspective*. The episode features Rohr in conversation with author and "urban hermit" Tessa Bielecki, walking through Type One ("Architects of the Ideal" / the Reformer / Perfectionist). The substantive theological move: the Enneagram is not a personality system but a sacred map of how *wounds become wisdom* — Type One specifically being the type whose gift (the capacity to make order from chaos) and shadow (perfectionism, self-judgment) emerge from the same root. Bielecki's contribution is autobiographical-contemplative: how trauma and betrayal shattered her need to fix everything, and how saguaro cacti became teachers in the desert. Theologically this consolidates Rohr's late-career framing of the Enneagram as a contemplative-developmental rather than typological tool — a move that has been latent in his work since *The Enneagram: A Christian Perspective* (1990, revised 2001) and is here being fully articulated for the contemplative-tradition reader.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
The Enneagram is, on Rohr's reading, the contemplative-tradition's *operational* account of how perspective-limited agents are differently structured and how those structurings become — through suffering and contemplative practice — pathways to integration rather than entrenchments. For C2A2 this is highly load-bearing: the project's central theme is that perspective-limitation individuates conscious agents, and the Enneagram is the contemplative-tradition's most articulate account of *how those individuations differ from one another systematically*. The Season 4 launch (Feb 13, 2026 — outside the 60-day window) anchored the methodological frame; this May 1 episode is the first within-window substantive type-walk. Pairs with the existing Rohr wiki anchor *Falling Upward* (the two-halves-of-life developmental frame), of which the Enneagram-as-integration-tool is the type-differentiated articulation.

## Candidate PRS Triplets

PRS-CANDIDATE-01:
  Problem: The C2A2 project's central theme — perspective-limitation individuates conscious agents — needs an articulation of *how perspectives differ systematically* across agents. Without this, the "loving unity is the telos" claim risks treating perspective-limitation as an undifferentiated phenomenon, when in fact agents are limited *in different characteristic ways*.
  Resource: Rohr's contemplative-developmental reading of the Enneagram: nine characteristic perspective-limitations, each with a distinctive gift-shadow structure, each with a corresponding contemplative path to integration. Type One is the worked example: order-from-chaos as gift, perfectionism-and-self-judgment as shadow, integration through embracing imperfection.
  Solution: A typology of perspective-limitations that the Summa-2026 syntheses can use whenever the question turns on *how* different agents fail or succeed in moving toward loving unity. Lets the network differentiate (e.g.) a Type-One failure mode (perfectionism) from a Type-Eight failure mode (domination) without collapsing them.
  Confidence: Medium-High
  Evidence: Episode content; Rohr's *The Enneagram: A Christian Perspective*; the broader Season 4 framing.

PRS-CANDIDATE-02:
  Problem: The Summa 2026 work on the vices and virtues (especially II-II loci on the cardinal and theological virtues, and the corresponding vices) needs a contemplative-tradition contemporary articulation that doesn't merely catalog vices but explains how a given person's *characteristic* vice and *characteristic* virtue are the same root expressed differently.
  Resource: Rohr's gift-shadow-from-the-same-root frame: Type One's perfectionism and Type One's ordering-gift emerge from the same underlying structure. The contemplative path is not eliminating the structure but integrating it.
  Solution: A contemplative-developmental reading of the virtues/vices that pairs with the Aquinas baseline. The Summa syntheses can use this to articulate why a virtue is not simply the absence of its corresponding vice but the integrated form of the same root capacity.
  Confidence: Medium
  Evidence: Episode content; Rohr's prior Enneagram work; structural compatibility with the Thomistic doctrine of virtues as habituated dispositions.

PRS-CANDIDATE-03:
  Problem: The cross-tradition synthesis work between Rohr's contemplative-developmental frame and the McGilchrist / Friston / Levin frames needs an explicit articulation of how *typological* differences in agent-structure relate to *biological-cognitive* differences in agent-structure. Without that bridge, the Enneagram looks like a folk taxonomy from the science side.
  Resource: The Bielecki-Rohr conversation's *embodied* framing — type structure as something that shows up in the body, in attention, in habitual response — which is the level at which McGilchrist's hemispheric account, Friston's predictive-processing account, and Levin's somatic-cognition account can engage.
  Solution: A bridge claim — typological differences are not merely psychological but cognitive-structural at the level the network's biological members operate. Lets the Summa-2026 syntheses pair Rohr's typology with the biological-cognitive frames without flattening either.
  Confidence: Speculative
  Evidence: Episode framing; coherence with Rohr's broader contemplative-developmental program; speculative bridge to be tested.

## Cross-Tradition Signals
- **McGilchrist (medium):** A typology of attentional-cognitive habits is the kind of object McGilchrist's hemispheric account would predict — different characteristic balances of right- and left-hemisphere attention. Worth flagging.
- **Friston (medium):** Each Enneagram type is, mechanically, a characteristic configuration of precision-weighting priors. Speculative but plausible bridge.
- **Levin (light):** Typological differences as somatic-cognitive structures rather than purely psychological labels. Light flag.
- **Stump (light):** Stump's analytic-Thomist account of virtues as habituated dispositions could pair with Rohr's typological-developmental account; the integration would be the gift-shadow-from-the-same-root frame.
- **Wright (light):** Wright's call-and-vocation frame (PROP-2026-05-18-002) pairs structurally with Rohr's gift-shadow-from-the-same-root frame — each agent is *called* into the integrated form of its characteristic structure, not into the elimination of its structure.
- **Summa 2026 (medium):** Useful for II-II virtue/vice syntheses and any synthesis where typological differences in agents matter. Not a primary anchor but a citable contemporary Rohr resource.
