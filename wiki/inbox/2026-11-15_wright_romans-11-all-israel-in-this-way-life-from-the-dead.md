## Summary
Lecturing on Romans 11 at the Dallas "Fresh Challenge of Romans" series, Wright argued that Romans 11:26 should read "*in this way* all Israel will be saved" (following the updated NRSV), not "and then." On his reading, the verse names the *manner* of salvation (Judeans and Gentiles brought in together through the Messiah), not a later event. He said there is "no sign at any point in Romans 9 to 11 that Paul envisages a large-scale, last-minute salvation of all ethnic Judeans," and that "all Israel" means the whole Messiah-people, Judean and Gentile, because Paul has opened the category of Israel to non-Judeans throughout the letter (ch. 2, ch. 4). He calls the present hardening "a divine strategy" that brings the nations in. Any Judean return to the Messiah would be "like the real return from exile ... life from the dead." He also warned against a "proto-Marcionite" split between the God of Israel and the God of the New Testament, and against any status claim based on ethnicity ("cutting off the very branch on which they claim to be sitting").
