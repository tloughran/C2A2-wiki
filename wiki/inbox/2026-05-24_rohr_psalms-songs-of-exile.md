## Summary
A week of CAC Daily Meditations from the 2026 cycle ("Good News for a Fractured World"; weekly summary dated 28 March 2026) reading the Psalms as songs of exile — Psalm 137's refusal to sing, exile as a metaphor for present-day displacement, and the call to humanize those experiencing displacement and to ground social action in contemplative consciousness. Includes Rohr's "Discovering Our Shadows in Exile," in which empires and nation-states are read as continuing generators of fear, violence, and oppression.

## Why This Matters for This Tradition
This is the contemplative register of the same exile/restoration motif Wright treats exegetically this run (PROP-2026-05-24-003). For C2A2 it supplies the "fractured world" pole of the central theme — exile as the lived condition of perspective-limited, displaced agents — and Rohr's move (contemplative consciousness as the ground of mending action) is the practice-side correlate of "loving unity as telos." It advances the Universal-Christ frame into the political/communal domain without leaving the contemplative tradition.
