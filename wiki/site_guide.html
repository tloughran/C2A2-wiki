C2A2 Explorer · Site Guide

How to read the Explorer

A guide to the C2A2 Explorer — part users manual on the surface (what each page is, and what happens when you click), part technical design report in the fold-down boxes (why the page exists and how it is built). Read the plain text straight through and you have the manual; open the boxes and you have the reasons.

A note on the two depths
Throughout this guide, a gold Deeper Dive box holds the design rationale a curious reader wants — the “why.” A green Deep Dive box nested inside it holds the implementation detail an engineer wants — files, data flow, guards. You can skip every box and still follow the main argument. Nothing load-bearing is hidden in a box; the boxes only add depth, never replace the surface.

CHAPTER 1

The shell — brand, Record, and the tab bar written

Every page you see lives inside one frame. The dark bar pinned to the top is the shell (explorer.html). On the left it carries the wordmark C2A2 over its functional subtitle, Community Dialogue Accelerator/Detector System. On the right sits a Record button that screen-captures a narrated walk-through. Between them run the chapter tabs.

Six chapters sit on the top row: Start here, Community Explorer, Community Education, Community Accelerator Tools, Community Interactions, and this Site Guide. Two of those chapters — Education and Accelerator Tools — open a second row of sub-tabs beneath them; the other four load as single pages. Wherever you wander, the pinned tab bar is your way back.

Why the Explorer is a shell around swappable pages
Each tool is a self-contained page. The shell hosts them one at a time in a single frame rather than bundling them together, so any one tool can be regenerated, opened on its own, or published independently without disturbing the others. The tab bar is the only shared chrome; a tool never has to know about its siblings.
How the frame swaps without breaking Back
Switching a tab calls location.replace(src + '?v=' + Date.now()) on the frame. The ?v= timestamp defeats the browser cache so an edited tool always loads fresh; location.replace (rather than setting src) avoids pushing a joint history entry, so the browser Back button can’t rewind the frame while the tab bar keeps its state. On restore-from-history the shell re-reads the frame’s actual URL and re-lights the matching tab, healing any desync. One caution lives here: a tab that loads its own separate .js/.css would get fresh HTML but cached assets — so a multi-file tab must either inline its assets or content-hash its includes. Single-file tabs (like this Site Guide) are immune because the whole file is the document.
CHAPTER 2

Start here — the front door written

The Start here chapter is the Explorer’s front door. It doesn’t argue that C2A2 is real; it walks you to three places where you can judge that for yourself — “about thirty minutes, three stops.” The page is three numbered steps.

The three steps

  • 1 · Orient — “What’s this?” A one-paragraph answer, then a door to all fifteen framings of what C2A2 is.
  • 2 · Decode the names — “Who’s who?” The community refers to its figures by last name; this step introduces the roster of eleven named thinkers (Levin, Friston, Hoffman, Hawkins, McGilchrist, Fredrickson, Stump, Carroll, Arkani-Hamed, Wolfram, Kastrup) plus the companion traditions (Aquinas’s Summa, MacIntyre, Rohr, Wright). Two doors: the full Who’s Who, and “meet them in the Sociogram.”
  • 3 · So what? Two ten-minute dives: Is a common mind forming? (the daily review cards) and What can that mind do? (the contemporary Summa commentary).

Why the front door shows rather than tells
C2A2’s claim is that alignment is worked out inside small communities of practice — groups that articulate shared goals and pursue them measurably. A front door that lectured would be self-refuting; one that hands you the evidence and lets you judge enacts the thesis. So the page is built as doors, not paragraphs.
How the doors know where they are
Each door carries a data-target. When Start here is embedded in the shell it postMessages the parent ({source:'c2a2-start-here', action:'navigate', target}) and the shell switches the right chapter/tab in place — so a door never navigates you out of the Explorer, it moves you within it. Opened on its own, the same link falls back to its plain href. The targets are fifteen, sociogram, review-cards, and summa-commentary; the Who’s Who link follows its href in both modes.

Behind door one: fifteen framings

The “What’s this?” door opens what_is_c2a2.html, which answers the question from fifteen angles — “a constellation of frameworks, each true, each revealing a different dimension.” In order: (1) fulfillment of MacIntyre’s vision; (2) an accelerator/detector for traditions; (3) a high-information-flow network of rich communities; (4) a semantic commons (with the Problem–Resource–Solution triplet2 as its grammar); (5) a social information-processing motherboard; (6) a bioelectric network (Levin); (7) “pixie dust” (Karpathy’s compiled-wiki1 seed, extended to human traditions); (8) a memory prosthetic for culture; (9) a university recovered — holistic inquiry and what it commits you to; (10) a courtroom, or better, an agon; (11) the Explorer as community; (12) an intelligent agent; (13) a mind; (14) a brain (Hawkins); (15) a living entity, placed last — the other fourteen are the body, the fifteenth asks whether the body has begun to live.

Behind door one’s appendix: the four lenses

At the foot of the fifteen-framings page sits a short Technical Appendix. It is a paragraph and two links — there are no diagrams on that page itself. The real technical figures live one hop further, on the “Services & codebase — four lenses” architecture page (architecture/lowlevel_architecture.html): four tabbed diagrams that are the closest thing C2A2 has to a blueprint. Here is what each one shows.

What each of the four architecture diagrams says
Lens 1 — Runtime / Service Topology. Three compute hosts (Mac Mini on launchd; the Cowork Linux sandbox, manual and read-only to the vault; GitHub Actions CI) read and write a shared data layer (open-story.db, the git repos, JSON indexes, the gitignored provenance/), which publishes through GitHub Pages to the browser, with external services (a Supabase broker, OpenAI, Tavily, Supabase Postgres) underneath. The design point: “the Mac pushes what the sandbox builds.”

Lens 2 — Code / Module Dependency Map. Six pipelines (Sociogram, Heartbeat, Review Log, Metabolism, Vault Sync, OpenStory, PRS-3D), each a bash wrapper calling Python/Node steps to an output artifact, with a star marking shared modules reused across pipelines. The point: orchestration is composable, and a handful of modules do the heavy shared work.

Lens 3 — Annotated Codebase Tree. The public repository laid out: root scripts, the CI workflows, the scripts/ pipeline modules, the wiki/ published app (with single-file vs. multi-file tabs marked) and its git-as-database vault of traditions and synthesis essays, and the gitignored local working files. The point: where everything lives, and which tabs carry the asset-caching caution.

Lens 4 — Daily-Run Execution Trace. The daily orchestrator (c2a2-wiki-agent-daily-run) as phases 0–6: ingest & preserve, validate inputs, tradition agents draft, integrate & detect, regenerate views, rebuild the review log, publish. Each phase lists what it runs, what it writes, and the guard that gates it — the last guard being a local visual review before any code/HTML push. The point: the system fails loud, with a checkpoint between every stage.

Reading a door in practice

Click “Meet them in the Sociogram.” Because Start here is embedded in the shell, the door doesn’t leave the Explorer — it messages the shell, which switches to the Community Accelerator Tools chapter and lights the Sociogram sub-tab. The full graph loads in the same frame; the pinned tab bar returns you to Start here whenever you like. That single click is the whole navigation model in miniature. Chapter 3 picks up exactly there — including a Sociogram reduced to just the Friston and Levin nodes, to show what a node-click and an edge-click each open.
