C2A2 · Dialectical Engagement of Traditions

Prepare the pair, then read the outcome

The Pilot in two acts. Act 1 (steps 1–12) prepares two traditions for interaction: each assembles its Mature Member Agreement as the standard, builds and aligns its tests, and forms a 2FL speaker. Act 2 (steps 13–18) interacts the pair once and reads the result, classifying it into one of five outcome types. The measure is never the speaker — it is the MMA, and a reading carries cross‑tradition force only when both standards concur. Click any stage to inspect it; click an outcome's ? for what it means. Glossary

DET — Dialectical Engagement of Traditions (the algorithm dramatized here; steps 1–18 = the Pilot)

C2A2 — Community Context for AI Alignment

MMA — Mature Member Agreement (the virtuous mature members of a tradition, together, are its standard or measure — Aristotle: the good person is the measure. It endures through an interaction; a 2FL speaker's reaction is evidence read against it, never itself the measure)

Dual MMA (a 2FL speaker's reaction carries cross-tradition force only when both traditions' MMAs concur — bilateral certification, which overcomes the incommensurability/relativism challenge)

PRS — Problem–Resource–Solution (the triplet each of a tradition's claims is traced through)

CCI — Community Concept Inventory (a plain-language test of the fundamental concepts of maturity, after the Force Concept Inventory)

AT (ATs) — Application Test (deploys those concepts in wider, harder contexts)

FL / 1FL — First Language (a mature member is, by definition, a first-language speaker of their home tradition)

2FL — Second-First-Language (a 1FL speaker also formed to mature competence in a second tradition)
