Read the mature-member action and AI rows together. Mature members act in three ways: model (exemplar an apprentice becomes), collaborate (rival traditions jointly producing the best account — incl. the interaction), and certify (agreement as the measure). Humans are irreducible in model and certify; AI can genuinely join collaborate (the PROBE core). So the 40 steps aren't 40 human-paced steps: PARALLEL recursions collapse, collaboration is AI-shareable, and only modeling + certifying hold humans fast.
