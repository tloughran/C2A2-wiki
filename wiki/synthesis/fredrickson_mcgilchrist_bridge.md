**Synthesis claim:** McGilchrist's "how to be" — managing one's inner state toward agency and centredness as a condition of right engagement with collective crisis — parallels Fredrickson's broaden-and-build: positive affect widens the action repertoire that constructive engagement requires. Inner-state management is the personal-formation hinge both traditions point to.
