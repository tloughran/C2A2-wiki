### 2026-08-09 — Dissent now requires founding an institution

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-08-04_hawkins_bbc-artificial-human-llm-dead-end.md` (PROP-2026-08-04-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** PRS-CANDIDATE-02 records the episode's framing: this is 'an increasingly common opinion among leading researchers who are setting up their own research labs to explore other approaches to AI despite the industry's focus on LLMs'. The Thousand Brains Project is the instance — spun out of Numenta in January 2025 as an independent nonprofit with a patent non-assert pledge.

**Synthesis claim.** C2A2 holds that a tradition becomes articulate when it acquires the means to state and develop itself. Here is that happening in the wild, with a date, a legal structure, and an intellectual-property posture attached, in a field where the dominant paradigm controls most of the capital. The observation upgrades the accelerator's thesis from a claim about knowledge to a claim about infrastructure: paradigm rivalry in a capital-intensive field is conducted through institution-founding rather than through journal argument, because the journals are downstream of the compute. That is a mechanism the architecture material does not currently name.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Does this generalize past capital-intensive fields? The contemplative and Thomistic traditions in the network are articulate without owning infrastructure of this kind, which suggests the institution-founding requirement is specific to domains where the means of inquiry are expensive and concentrated. If so, C2A2's own infrastructure requirements depend on which kind of field it is in — and that is a question about the accelerator's cost model, not just its theory.
