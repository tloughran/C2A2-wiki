## Building for a telos one does not oneself complete
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-12_wright_ask-ntw-everyday-work-waiting-for-return.md` (PROP-2026-07-12-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Wright's distinction between building *the* kingdom (God's act) and building *for* the kingdom (human vocation taken up into new creation) is the clearest recent statement of the Summa central theme's claim that present faithfulness participates in the loving-unity telos without being identical to it. He names data analysis explicitly among the kinds of work that qualify -- which is, incidentally, a description of the C2A2 accelerator's own labor.

**Synthesis claim:** A telos can be genuinely participated in by agents who neither complete it nor fully represent it, provided their work is *preserved and transfigured* rather than merely causally upstream. This is the strongest available answer to the accelerator's own legitimation problem: C2A2 cannot deliver the loving unity it aims at, but the claim is not that it delivers it -- the claim is that richly-informed cross-perspective contact is the kind of work that is taken up into it.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Wright's 'preserved and transfigured' is doing load-bearing work and has no secular analogue in the wiki. What would it mean, without the eschatology, for present work to be preserved rather than merely to cause? If nothing survives the translation, then the accelerator's participation claim is borrowing a warrant it has not paid for.
