# Levin x Stump — Identity Through Total Substrate Turnover

*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-19*

## Ship of Theseus at the species scale

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-06_levin_multi-scale-longevity.md` (PROP-2026-07-06-002, Pio-Lopez & Levin).

Levin's multi-scale longevity framework asks the Ship-of-Theseus question and answers it: species longevity will be *metamorphic* — continuity of pattern through transformation — rather than static repair of a fixed form. He treats the answer as obvious. It is not obvious, and the reasons it is not are Stump's.

**Synthesis claim.** Levin's framework requires a criterion of diachronic identity that it does not supply. "Persistence of a coherent large-scale informational pattern" is a *sufficient condition for something to continue*, but it is not yet a criterion for *the same thing* continuing, because informational patterns can branch, be copied, and be instantiated twice. Every classical objection to psychological-continuity theories of personal identity applies verbatim, one scale up. Stump's Thomistic commitment — that the bearer of identity is a substance with a form, not an information-theoretic structure — is not a decorative alternative here; it is the position that has already paid the cost of answering the duplication objection.

The productive move is not to adjudicate but to note what each buys. Levin's pattern-criterion is *operationalizable*: you can measure whether a bioelectric target morphology is being maintained. Stump's substance-criterion is *individuating*: it tells you which of two qualitatively identical patterns is the original. Neither has the other's property.

**Question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Levin's aging work implies that a well-maintained pattern degrades once its goal is met (set-point blurring). If the pattern is the bearer of identity, does its degradation constitute *the same individual aging* or *a gradual replacement by a similar individual*? A pattern-criterion appears to have no resources to distinguish these, and the distinction is the entire content of the question "will I survive?" — which is what the longevity program is ultimately funded to answer.
