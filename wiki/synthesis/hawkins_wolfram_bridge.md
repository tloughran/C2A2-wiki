### 2026-08-09 — Does the architecture matter, or does computational equivalence make it irrelevant?

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-08-04_hawkins_bbc-artificial-human-llm-dead-end.md` (PROP-2026-08-04-001) and `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-08-08_wolfram_mc0001-machine-thinking-ruliological-insights.md` (PROP-2026-08-08-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** Hawkins argues on national radio that LLM scaling is a dead end because the *architecture* is wrong: understanding requires a sensorimotor model indexed to reference frames, and no amount of parameters supplies one. Wolfram argues from computational equivalence and irreducibility that almost any sufficiently rich system is computationally equivalent to any other, which makes architecture a matter of convenience rather than of capability.

**Synthesis claim.** This is a disagreement, and filing it as one is the point — the file has stood empty since it was scaffolded. The strongest form of the Wolfram objection: if computational equivalence holds, a sensorimotor reference-frame architecture cannot be *necessary* for understanding, only efficient for it, and Hawkins' dead-end claim collapses into a claim about training economics. The strongest form of the Hawkins reply: computational equivalence is about what a system can in principle compute, and says nothing about what a given training procedure will actually find — the space of universal computers is not the space of reachable models. Both are correct about different quantifiers, and naming that is the resolution available today.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Is there a version of the dead-end claim that survives computational equivalence? It would have to be a claim about the reachability of certain models under gradient descent on text, not about representational capacity. Hawkins has not stated it that way and might not accept the reframing. Wolfram's MC0001 talk is the place he would have addressed machine cognition directly — and its content is unverified, so this pair cannot be closed until the recording is watched.
