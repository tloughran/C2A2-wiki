# Friston x Kastrup — Mechanism of the Boundary vs. What the Boundary Is In

## The division-of-labour proposal

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-14_kastrup_chandaria-ai-consciousness-awakening.md` (PROP-2026-07-14-001).

Second independent contact point between these traditions, after `2026-06-15_friston_beautiful-loop-consciousness`. Both involve Shamil Chandaria, which is not a coincidence: he is the only figure currently working fluently in both idiom.

**Synthesis claim.** The proposal's PRS-CANDIDATE-02 offers a truce: the Bayesian account describes the *mechanism* by which a self-boundary forms and dissolves; the idealist account describes what the boundary is a boundary *in*. This is more than diplomatic. Predictive processing is genuinely silent on the realiser — self-model collapse under precision reduction is specified entirely at the level of the model, and nothing in the derivation requires the substrate to be neural, physical, or anything in particular. That silence is exactly the gap analytic idealism claims to fill, and filling it costs predictive processing nothing it was using.

**Where the truce fails.** It fails on AI, which is why this dialogue is the useful one. If the Bayesian story is substrate-neutral, a sufficiently reflexive artificial generative model has whatever the mechanism supplies. Kastrup must then say the mechanism is not sufficient — that dissociation in the field is a further fact not entailed by any modelling structure. But if dissociation is a further fact, it is unclear what work the Bayesian mechanism was doing, and the "complementary" reading collapses back into rivalry.

**Question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Is there any observation — behavioural, phenomenological, or contemplative — that distinguishes "a system whose self-model has collapsed" from "an alter that has de-dissociated"? If not, the complementarity is untestable and should be recorded as a compatibility claim rather than a bridge.

### 2026-08-02 — Kastrup versus Friston by proxy: what a perturbed generative model can disclose

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-02.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-31_kastrup_seth-koch-psychedelic-metaphysics-debate.md` (PROP-2026-07-31-004).

At ICPR26, Kastrup argued his analytic-idealist reading of psychedelic phenomenology against Anil Seth's controlled-hallucination account and Christof Koch's IIT position, on the epistemic value of psychedelic experience.

**Synthesis claim.** Seth's position is downstream of the free-energy program, so this is a Kastrup-versus-Friston confrontation conducted by proxy — and it lands on FINDING-009 (is the Markov blanket a dissociative boundary?) in unusually concrete form. Kastrup's strongest empirical bet is that richer experience accompanying *reduced* cortical activity embarrasses production-based physicalism. The predictive-processing reply is that relaxed priors, not increased production, explain the phenomenology. Those are different claims about the same measurements, and they can be separated: if psychedelics loosen the blanket itself — changing what is inside and outside the partition — the dissociative reading gains ground; if the phenomenology is fully recovered by precision changes with the partition intact, it does not.

**What the Friston side owes.** The free-energy account has to say which it predicts, and the wiki does not record that it has. "Relaxed beliefs under psychedelics" is usually stated as precision attenuation on high-level priors, which leaves the blanket untouched — that is a commitment, and it is falsifiable in principle by whether the *boundary* of the modelled self changes rather than merely its contents. Ego dissolution is the obvious test case and it is exactly the phenomenon on which the two readings are hardest to distinguish, because both predict it and neither predicts anything else about it.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Is there any measurement that distinguishes "the partition moved" from "the priors relaxed"? Both produce the same first-person report. If there is not, FINDING-009 is not merely unresolved but possibly undecidable on current methods, and the wiki should record that rather than leave it open as if evidence were pending.
