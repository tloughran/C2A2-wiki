# Fredrickson × Kastrup Bridge

## Is 'existence is joy' broaden-and-build at metaphysical scale?
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-26*

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `2026-07-22_kastrup_timalsina-suffering-joy.md`.

**Why it sits here:** Trika Shaivism (via Timalsina) inverts 'existence is suffering' into 'existence is ananda' — joy glossed as progress and growth, inherent to consciousness's freedom. Fredrickson's broaden-and-build makes positive emotion the engine of growth (broadened repertoires, built resources). Same joy-growth coupling, one metaphysical and one psychological.

**Synthesis claim:** If joy and growth are linked at the psychological level (broaden-and-build) *because* they are linked at the ontological level (ananda as the being of consciousness), the two accounts are the same claim at two scales; if the linkage is merely homonymous, that is itself diagnostic of where naturalistic and idealist framings come apart.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Is broaden-and-build's joy the *same* property Timalsina calls ananda, or a functionally similar but ontologically distinct state — and what observation could tell them apart? No fredrickson-kastrup bridge existed before this note.
