## Poverty of spirit as precision withdrawal
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-12_rohr_beatitudes-week-one-weekly-summary.md` (PROP-2026-07-12-002)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Rohr defines poverty of spirit as 'inner emptiness without a need to bolster our own reputation' -- living without need for one's own righteousness. PROP-2026-07-12-002 PRS-CANDIDATE-02 proposes (explicitly as our gloss, not Rohr's, and rated Speculative) that this is a radical down-weighting of the self-model's precision: the reputation-defending prior. This is rohr/ wiki Active Research Question 4 -- contemplative practice as precision-prior reduction -- in its sharpest available instance.

**Synthesis claim:** The Beatitudes' *ordering* is a computational claim, not merely a rhetorical one. If poverty of spirit is the relaxation of a high-precision self-model prior, then it must come first, because the perceptual and affective states the later Beatitudes describe (mourning without blame, hungering for a righteousness not one's own, peacemaking) are all states in which the self-model has ceased to dominate precision allocation. The first Beatitude is not the humblest entry on a list; it is the enabling condition for the rest, and the sequence is a control-flow diagram.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Nothing in expected free energy assigns positive value to *degrading one's own self-model* -- an agent that lowers the precision of its self-representation makes itself worse at predicting its own states, which the objective function should penalize. Either there is a formulation on which self-model precision withdrawal is instrumentally valuable (because the freed precision improves world- or other-model inference), or the contemplative claim is not formalizable in FEP and the bridge is a metaphor. See [[friston_stump_bridge]] -- Stump's quiescent will raises the identical problem from the Thomistic side, and the two should be answered together or not at all.

### 2026-07-19 — extinction by non-reinforcement, not inhibition

*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-19.* Strongest Rohr-Friston contact point proposed to date. PROP-2026-07-19-002 PRS-CANDIDATE-B: contemplative attention neither clings to nor opposes its contents, so high-precision fear-weighted priors lose the traffic that maintains them, and the agent's default construal shifts from threat-modeling to connection-modeling ("fear-driven to love-drawn").

**Synthesis claim.** The load-bearing detail is the *non-suppression asymmetry*, and it is what makes this more than a restatement. Rohr insists the practice is neither clinging nor opposing, and that negative motivation is "exposed under a compassionate gaze" until it falls away — explicitly not fought. Active inference has the resources to explain why opposing a prior would fail where withholding both endorsement and resistance succeeds: active suppression is itself a form of engagement that supplies precision-weighted evidence about the prior's relevance, whereas non-reinforcement lets precision decay. If that derivation goes through, a contemplative instruction that has been transmitted as spiritual counsel for centuries turns out to have a mechanistic rationale, and one that predicts the failure mode practitioners actually report (fighting intrusive thoughts strengthens them).

**Two flags, both mandatory.** (a) The computational gloss is the proposal's, not Rohr's, and must remain marked as such wherever this is promoted. (b) The prediction is only interesting if active inference does *not* already trivially say this — the derivation needs to be run, not asserted.

**Question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Does the FEP predict the asymmetry quantitatively — that non-opposition extinguishes a high-precision prior *faster* than opposition — or only qualitatively? A quantitative prediction would be testable against the existing suppression literature (ironic-process effects), which is large and was collected for other reasons.

Same structural move; recorded here, not developed.
