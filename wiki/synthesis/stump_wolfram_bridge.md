Stump holds personal identity to be fixed hylomorphically: the person is this individuated form-matter composite, persisting per the metaphysics of substantial form, not by continuity of a computational thread.

**Synthesis claim:** The emulation/linking thought-experiment forces a clean confrontation between two rival criteria of personal persistence — *observer-coherence* (substrate-independent single thread through the multiway graph) versus *hylomorphic individuation* (this soul-as-form of this body). They agree that "identity is not what physicalist continuity-of-matter assumed" and disagree on what replaces it: a computational-structural property versus a metaphysical form.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Does substrate-independent observer-coherence survive a hylomorphic critique — i.e., can a copied or wire-linked "single coherent thread" be the *same person*, or does hylomorphism entail that observer-coherence tracks a functional duplicate but not a numerically identical individual?
