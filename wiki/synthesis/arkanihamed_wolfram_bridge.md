## What counts as progress for a pre-geometric program?

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Wolfram's June 17 history Q&A assesses string theory's evidential status after five decades without a distinguishing experimental confirmation (t=33m44s). The Post-Spacetime Cluster now contains three programs -- strings, hypergraph, amplituhedron -- all of which claim spacetime is emergent and none of which has produced an observation that would distinguish it from the other two.

**Synthesis claim:** The amplituhedron program has a structural advantage the other two lack, and the wiki should say what it is rather than treating the three as symmetric: positive geometry *reproduces* known scattering amplitudes and is therefore constrained by existing data, whereas the hypergraph program's contact with data is programmatic. That is not confirmation, but it is a different epistemic position -- Arkani-Hamed is doing recovery-of-the-known, Wolfram is doing derivation-in-principle, and only the first is currently falsifiable.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Does 'spacetime is emergent' have the same content in all three programs, or is the Post-Spacetime Cluster an artifact of shared phrasing? See [[arkanihamed_hoffman_bridge]] for the same worry raised acutely, where the identical slogan is reached from evolutionary game theory. The wiki has been clustering on vocabulary and should check whether it is clustering on claims.


### 2026-08-09 — Restriction adds structure — boundary condition or observer limit?

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-08-07_arkanihamed_correlators-simpler-than-wavefunctions.md` (PROP-2026-08-07-002)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** The mechanism behind the whole paper is that restricting a path integral to half of spacetime *adds* structure — extra poles, a larger function space — while integrating over the whole thing removes it. Wolfram's observer theory makes a formally similar claim: an observer's restriction to a slice of the ruliad is what generates the appearance of complexity, law, and time.

**Synthesis claim.** Name the disanalogy first, then see what survives it. The half-space restriction here is a boundary condition in the *definition* of the object: the wavefunction is defined on half of spacetime because that is what a wavefunction at time zero is, not because anyone is looking. Wolfram's restriction is epistemic — it is the observer's limitation, and a differently-bounded observer would slice differently. If anything survives, it is a weaker and still interesting claim: that partial integration over a structure is a general source of apparent complexity, independent of whether the partiality is definitional or epistemic. That is a mathematical statement and could in principle be made precise.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Can the general claim be stated formally? 'Marginalizing over part of a structure produces more singularities than marginalizing over all of it' is either a theorem with conditions or a coincidence of two cases. Nobody has tried to state it. Until someone does, this pair is a resemblance, and the entry should not be cited as more.
