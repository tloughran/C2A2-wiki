## Can a disembodied generative model do what the right hemisphere does?
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-26*

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `2026-07-22_mcgilchrist_ai-never-brain.md`.

**Why it sits here:** McGilchrist's 'in principle' argument is that AI is confined to the re-presentational, decomposable mode of left-hemisphere attention and lacks the embodied, participatory, temporally-embedded attention of the right. Friston's embodied active inference locates cognition in a Markov blanket *in a body*, coupled to the world in real time.

**Synthesis claim:** McGilchrist's demarcation is restatable in Friston's terms as a claim about what a *disembodied* generative model structurally lacks — real-time sensorimotor coupling and a body-scaled blanket — rather than an appeal to anything non-physical. That makes the 'in principle' barrier an empirical bet about embodiment, not a mystery.

**Open question:** If the barrier is embodiment rather than substrate, does an embodied, world-coupled active-inference agent (a robot) cross McGilchrist's line — or does he hold that even embodied machine attention stays left-hemisphere?
