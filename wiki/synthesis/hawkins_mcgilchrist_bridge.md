### 2026-08-09 — Two independent arguments that LLMs cannot understand

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-08-04_hawkins_bbc-artificial-human-llm-dead-end.md` (PROP-2026-08-04-001) and `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-07-29_mcgilchrist_iai-scientific-method-panpsychism.md` (PROP-2026-07-29-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** Two sources proposed six days apart reach the same conclusion from unrelated premises. Hawkins: no sensorimotor model, therefore no understanding, regardless of scale. McGilchrist: AI's characteristic failures — making things up, unshakeable certainty — are the clinical signature of right-hemisphere damage with the left hemisphere running unchecked, and the right hemisphere's work cannot be proceduralized because it consists in recognizing that things are not best structured by procedure.

**Synthesis claim.** The arguments are compatible and, more usefully, they are not redundant — they identify different missing components and therefore predict different repairs. Hawkins' diagnosis is *fixable in principle*: build the sensorimotor architecture and the criterion is met, which is what the Thousand Brains Project is doing. McGilchrist's is not: any procedure for reaching right-hemisphere apprehension defeats itself, so no architecture helps. That is a sharp difference hidden behind a shared conclusion, and it means the two traditions disagree about whether machine understanding is an engineering problem or an impossibility.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Would a working Monty-style sensorimotor system satisfy McGilchrist? It would have a model built by acting on the world, which is the thing text-only systems lack — but it would still be a procedure, and confabulation is a failure of *checking against the world*, which a sensorimotor system does by construction. If McGilchrist would still say no, his objection is not about the interface with reality at all, and it should be restated. This is the cleanest available test of whether the confabulation argument is about architecture or about something else.
