C2A2's detector work becomes, in Levin's terms, an empirical probe of which hybrid-cognition-space regions are actually constructible and stable.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Which void regions are *empty by contingency* (buildable but never built) versus *empty by constraint* (physically/informationally forbidden)?
