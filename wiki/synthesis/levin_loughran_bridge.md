## Atavistic dissociation: a decoherence metric for traditions
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-06_levin_aging-goal-directedness-bioelectricity.md` (PROP-2026-07-06-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Levin's aging talk supplies both the C2A2 collective-alignment thesis in cell-biological form -- 'cells align toward common purpose in a space they don't individually understand,' with connection to the large-scale network as the anti-cancer and anti-aging principle -- and, in PRS-CANDIDATE-03, a *measure* of its failure: atavistic dissociation, a phylostratigraphic finding that young tissues agree on the body's evolutionary age while aged tissues drift into disagreement across the tree of life.

**Synthesis claim:** A tradition ages the way a body does: not by damage and not by design, but by its members drifting out of agreement about what they are and where they came from. Atavistic dissociation is the biological instance of a general failure mode -- loss of concordance about one's own history among the parts of a whole -- and it is measurable. C2A2 has been treating tradition-coherence as an assumed background and tradition-*contact* as the object of study; Levin's result suggests coherence is itself a quantity with a decay curve, and that a tradition losing internal agreement about its own genealogy is exhibiting a detectable pathology rather than merely changing.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Does the accelerator have an analogue of the phylostratigraphic measure -- some way to ask whether the participants in a tradition still agree about the tradition's evolutionary age? The detector currently measures cross-perspective interaction and has no instrument pointed *inward* at a tradition's own concordance. If Levin is right that decoherence precedes decline, C2A2 is missing the upstream signal, and the sewing agent's own connectivity census (orphan/sparse/connected counts) may be the crudest possible version of exactly this instrument, pointed at the wiki instead of at a community.

### 2026-07-19 — over-alignment as a design constraint on the accelerator

*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-19.* The most directly actionable cross-tradition item in this week's batch, and it is a claim *against* an assumption C2A2 may be making.

PROP-2026-07-13-001 (virtual governor) derives **over-alignment** as a first-class failure mode: a system-level governor coordinates precisely by leaving the parts enough room to keep optimizing on their own local landscapes, and forcing the parts into too-complete agreement destroys the competence that made the collective intelligent. There is an interior optimum in coupling strength, and both under- and over-alignment are pathologies with distinct signatures.

**Synthesis claim.** If this holds, the C2A2 Rung-2 design should not be scored on convergence, and "richly informed about one another's perspectives" must be held sharply apart from "brought into agreement." The accelerator's success signature would be *increased mutual registration with preserved local optimization* — traditions that can state each other's positions accurately while continuing to argue from their own. Convergence would be evidence of over-coupling, i.e. of the detector damaging what it measures.

This is a testable claim about the instrument, not a philosophical preference, and Levin's paper supplies the vocabulary to state it: mutual registration (each part's action registers on the others) is the coupling condition; agreement is not.

**Companion item, same week.** PROP-2026-07-06-002 supplies the other half: longevity as persistence of a large-scale informational pattern across substrate turnover is structurally the C2A2 claim about traditions persisting through membership turnover. **Where the analogy breaks and should be recorded as breaking:** a tradition's members can dissent, and dissent is one of the mechanisms by which a tradition stays alive. A cell cannot. Any transfer of Levin's multi-scale machinery to traditions has to carry a term for productive internal disagreement that the biological case does not need — which is, notably, the same term over-alignment names.

**Question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Is there a measurable proxy for "local optimization preserved" in Rung-2 interaction data — something distinguishing a participant who has understood a rival position from one who has adopted it? Without it, the over-alignment constraint is unenforceable in practice.
