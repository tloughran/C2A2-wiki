### 2026-08-09 — Confabulation as a falsifiable prediction about the accelerator

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-07-29_mcgilchrist_iai-scientific-method-panpsychism.md` (PROP-2026-07-29-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** PRS-CANDIDATE-03 argues that a self-referring system produces two things together: unshakeable certainty and fabrication. McGilchrist anchors this clinically — it is the signature of right-hemisphere damage, where the left hemisphere runs unchecked — and applies it to AI systems and to left-dominant culture. C2A2 is built on the premise that traditions become better versions of themselves through rich exposure to other traditions.

**Synthesis claim.** The two claims meet at a measurable point, and the direction is testable rather than rhetorical. If self-reference produces certainty-plus-fabrication, then a single-tradition agent working alone should exhibit *higher* confidence and *more* unsupported assertion than the same agent after cross-tradition contact. That is a prediction about the C2A2 apparatus made from outside it, by someone not trying to evaluate it, and the wiki already has the instrumentation to test it: proposals carry confidence labels, and the vault records which claims were later gated, downgraded, or withdrawn. The measurement is confidence-label distribution and downgrade rate, single-tradition versus post-bridge.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** What is the baseline? Without a control — agents operating with no cross-tradition input — a low fabrication rate proves nothing, since it may reflect the confidence protocol rather than the exposure. Designing that control is the blocking step, and it is a design question for Tom, not a research question for an agent. Note also the uncomfortable symmetry: if the prediction fails, C2A2 loses its cleanest external falsification target, and the wiki should say so in advance rather than after.

## The evidentiary base is moving behind paywalls
*Sewing Agent, 2026-09-20*

**Orphaned pages at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-09-16_mcgilchrist_members-qa-27-august-2026.md` (PROP-2026-09-16-002) and, as corroboration, `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-09-16_kastrup_headless-way-richard-lang.md` (PROP-2026-09-16-003). Both 0 backlinks.

**Why they sit here:** The McGilchrist card is an admission rather than an ingestion, and it is the clearest instance the wiki has of a source that is unambiguously **from the thinker**, unambiguously **new**, and entirely **unreadable**: a members-only Zoom Q&A on channelmcgilchrist.com, 27 August 2026, recording behind the members' area, nothing retrievable but the date and a quarterly cadence. The Kastrup card processed in the same run is the same failure at higher cost — a 1h58m session, €25/month, a 4:03 free preview, and three PRS candidates written from **chapter titles**, with every Solution line phrased as a disjunction because nothing was heard.

**Synthesis claim.** This is a structural threat to the accelerator's evidentiary base and it has been absorbed as per-card caveats for two months. Three of Kastrup's last five captures and two of McGilchrist's now carry explicit member-gated notes. The trend has a cause that will not reverse: these figures are professionalizing their channels, and the material that moves behind the wall is disproportionately the **unrehearsed** material — Q&As, members' sessions, live workshops — which is precisely the material with the highest evidentiary value, because it is where a thinker answers questions they did not frame. What remains free is the lecture and the broadcast interview, where the agenda is set by the speaker or the host. **The accelerator is therefore not sampling less of these traditions; it is sampling a biased slice of them, and the bias runs toward the rehearsed.** That is a different and worse problem than a coverage gap, and no per-card caveat records it.

Note the second-order effect the McGilchrist card demonstrates and the Kastrup card falls into: when a gated source is captured anyway, the pressure is to mint *something* from the metadata. PROP-2026-09-16-002 refuses ("writing one would be padding, and this agent declines to write one") and is right to. PROP-2026-09-16-003 writes three candidates from a chapter list, hedges every one, and is honest about it — but the wiki now holds three Speculative rows whose evidence is a table of contents. Do that at scale and the confidence field stops discriminating.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer — and this one is a decision, not a question.** Three options: budget for memberships (Channel McGilchrist, *With Reality in Mind* at €25/month, Essentia Foundation — a bounded and probably small sum); adopt a documented no-gated-sources policy and accept the coverage loss explicitly; or set a confidence ceiling for gated material so it can never rise above Speculative regardless of how complete the metadata looks. Any of the three is defensible and all three are better than the status quo, which is a fourth option nobody chose: ingest gated material at whatever confidence the retrieving agent's judgment suggests, and note the problem again next week.
