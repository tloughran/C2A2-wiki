## The second person as the unit of formation
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-09_stump_infused-virtues-new-blackfriars.md` (PROP-2026-07-09-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Stump argues that for Aquinas the moral life is *first and foremost a matter of a right second-personal relationship*, not the acquisition of dispositions. C2A2's accelerator is built to produce second-first-language competence -- a relation to another tradition's perspective, not an accumulated stock of propositions about it. In both cases the formative unit is a relation to a second person, and in both cases the rival account (virtue-as-disposition; tradition-as-doctrine) is the one being displaced.

**Synthesis claim:** Formation is relational before it is dispositional, at both the moral and the epistemic scale: what is acquired in mature tradition-membership is not a set of beliefs but a capacity to be addressed by, and to answer, a particular other. This predicts that C2A2's detector should find richer cross-perspective interaction where participants engage a *named interlocutor* than where they engage a summarized position -- the second-personal encounter carries information the third-personal summary does not.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** If second-personal relation is the unit, what is the C2A2 analogue of *quiescence* -- the non-resisting state that makes the relation possible? Stump has an answer for grace; the accelerator has no account of what a tradition must stop doing in order to be addressed by another one, and no way to detect whether a participant has done it.
