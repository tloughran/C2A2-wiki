# Hoffman × Mcgilchrist Bridge

## Two boundaries between representation and the represented
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-26*

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `2026-07-22_mcgilchrist_ai-never-brain.md`.

**Why it sits here:** McGilchrist distinguishes the left hemisphere's re-presented, decomposable world from the right hemisphere's first, participatory disclosure of a living world. Hoffman's interface theory distinguishes the icons/representations of the perceptual interface from whatever they point to. Both draw a boundary between a representation and the reality it stands in for — and both make AI a test case.

**Synthesis claim:** The hemispheric divide and the interface divide may be describing the same cut from two sides: left-hemisphere attention is interface-manipulation (icons treated as the reality), right-hemisphere attention is the disclosure the interface presupposes. If so, McGilchrist's 'AI is stuck in re-presentation' is Hoffman's 'AI operates only on icons.'

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Does Hoffman's formalism have any resource corresponding to right-hemisphere *disclosure* — a non-iconic mode of contact with reality — or is his interface, like McGilchrist's left hemisphere, representation all the way down? ### 2026-08-09 — Named, localized, and therefore answerable

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-07-29_mcgilchrist_iai-scientific-method-panpsychism.md` (PROP-2026-07-29-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** PRS-CANDIDATE-04 is the first source in the wiki where a peer rejects interface theory by name and *localizes* the rejection: 'I don't agree with Donald Hoffman that we're just like sitting in a padded cell reading off dials, but that is a fair description of the left hemisphere.' The right hemisphere is 'like a room with a window on the world' and its test of an idea is whether it adds up against the world as we know it.

**Synthesis claim.** The concession is more valuable than the rejection. McGilchrist grants that the dashboard model is *true of something* and disputes only its scope, which converts an ontological quarrel into a question about coverage: does interface theory claim to describe perception simpliciter, or one mode of construal among others? Hoffman's fitness-beats-truth result is a claim about what selection optimizes, and selection does not obviously operate hemisphere by hemisphere — so the burden falls on McGilchrist to say what makes right-hemisphere contact exempt from the same pressure.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** What observation would distinguish 'all perception is interface' from 'one hemisphere's construal is interface'? Both predict that ordinary experience misrepresents intrinsic nature. A discriminating test would need a case where right-hemisphere-dominant processing yields veridical contact that left-dominant processing does not — and neither tradition has proposed one. Until it does, this is a difference of doctrine with no experimental handle, which is worth recording plainly.
