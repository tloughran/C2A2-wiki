## The winter-walk result: broadening without positive affect
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-10_carroll_mindscape-360-berman-nature-cognition.md` (PROP-2026-07-10-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Berman reports that the cognitive benefit of a nature walk holds for participants who did *not* enjoy the walk (January, cold, unpleasant) and in clinical depression cohorts under induced rumination. The restoration is therefore decoupled from mood. Broaden-and-build's core mechanism runs *through* positive affect: positive emotions broaden the thought-action repertoire, which builds durable resources. Here the broadening arrives with the positive affect subtracted.

**Synthesis claim:** Attentional broadening has at least two dissociable routes -- an affective one (positive emotion widens the repertoire) and an environmental one (low-surprisal, softly-fascinating input restores directed-attention capacity regardless of how it feels). If that is right, broaden-and-build has correctly identified a sufficient cause of broadening and mistakenly treated it as the necessary one. The theory is not refuted; it is shown to be a special case, and the general case is about *processing load*, not valence.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Does the environmental route build durable resources, or only restore depleted ones? Broaden-and-build's distinctive claim is the *build* half -- broadening compounds into lasting capacity. Berman measures restoration within a single session. Nobody has tested whether mood-independent broadening accumulates, and if it does not, then valence is doing real work after all and this bridge collapses.
