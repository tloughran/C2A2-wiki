**Synthesis claim:** The confirmation problem Wolfram identifies in string theory -- fertility without a distinguishing prediction -- applies with equal force to the Wolfram Physics Project, and Wolfram's willingness to state it against strings is evidence that he accepts the standard rather than rejecting it. That relocates a supposed paradigm clash into an ordinary empirical dispute -- which is the more tractable and more interesting result.

### 2026-07-19 — genealogy shifts a prior; it does not supply evidence

*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-19.* PROP-2026-07-18-001 (History of Science and Technology Q&A, 2026-06-03) traces the intellectual history of discrete space: who proposed it before Wolfram, why continuum methods displaced those proposals, and what changed.

**Synthesis claim, and it should be labelled partial.** The open question at line 198 of `master/cross_program_index.md` asks whether Wolfram Physics meets Carroll's Bayesian theory-confirmation standard. It does not, and a genealogy does not move it toward doing so — the standard asks for a distinguishing empirical prediction and history supplies none. What the genealogy *does* is repositions the program as a revived long-standing research option rather than an unmotivated idiosyncratic postulate, which is a change in the prior rather than in the likelihood.

That distinction is worth keeping sharp, because it is the one most often blurred in this dispute. A higher prior means the program deserves attention and funding; it does not mean the program is confirmed. Carroll's standard is indifferent to provenance by design, and rightly so — the amplituhedron program has a much thinner genealogy and a much stronger claim on physicists' attention.

**The reciprocal point.** If Wolfram's history correctly identifies *why* continuum methods won, it identifies what discrete approaches had to give up — and that is closer to being evidence, because it predicts where the current program should still be weakest. A genealogy that names its own tradition's unpaid debts is doing more than rehabilitation.
