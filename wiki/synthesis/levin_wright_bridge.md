## Building for a goal you cannot represent: cells and vocations
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-12_wright_ask-ntw-everyday-work-waiting-for-return.md` (PROP-2026-07-12-001)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Wright's building-*for*-the-kingdom (as against building *the* kingdom) describes agents whose labor contributes to a larger-scale goal they neither complete nor individually represent. Levin's multi-scale competency architecture describes exactly this: cells pursuing local objectives in a space they do not individually understand, whose collective effect is a target morphology stored at a scale above them. PROP-2026-07-12-001 flags the structural duality; PROP-2026-07-06-001 supplies the biological side in its strongest form ('cells align toward common purpose in a space they don't individually understand').

**Synthesis claim:** Goal-directedness at a scale above the agent is a general architecture, not a theological peculiarity: in both the biological and the vocational case, the lower-scale agent's competence consists in pursuing a local objective correctly *without* access to the global one, and the coherence of the whole is carried by the medium (bioelectric set point; tradition and its practices) rather than by any participant's representation. This is the deepest available warrant for C2A2's premise that a community can be steered toward a telos none of its members can state.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Wright claims the work is *preserved and transfigured* in the consummation, not merely causally upstream of it. Levin's cells have no analogue -- their contribution is exhausted by its effects. Is 'preservation' a real structural feature that the multi-scale picture is missing, or is it the point at which the theological claim exceeds the biological one? The answer decides whether this is a bridge or a false friend.
