## The weeping mode: a broadening negative affect
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-12_rohr_beatitudes-week-one-weekly-summary.md` (PROP-2026-07-12-002)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Rohr's Week 27 Beatitudes material names a 'weeping mode': grief that bears the world's pain *without* sorting it into perpetrators and victims. The claim is that this is a distinct processing mode which metabolizes moral information that blame-partitioning distorts -- i.e. a negative affect that *widens* perception rather than narrowing it. Broaden-and-build's foundational asymmetry is that negative emotions narrow the thought-action repertoire and positive ones broaden it.

**Synthesis claim:** Mourning-without-blame is a counterexample to the valence-broadening mapping and a friend to the broadening *function*. What narrows the repertoire is not negative valence as such but the *appraisal of threat and agency* -- the perpetrator/victim frame is what recruits the narrow, action-preparatory mode. Strip the frame and the grief broadens: it holds more of the world, not less. Broaden-and-build's real variable, on this reading, is not positivity but the *absence of a blame-assignment demand*.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Is there any measurable signature that distinguishes non-dual grief from ordinary sadness or rumination? The Rohr claim is currently phenomenological and the Fredrickson apparatus is biobehavioral; without a marker -- affective, physiological, or attentional -- the wiki cannot tell whether the weeping mode is a real third state or a virtuous description of a familiar one. Note the adjacent finding from PROP-2026-07-10-001: cognitive broadening occurs with positive affect entirely absent, which independently loosens the valence-broadening link.
