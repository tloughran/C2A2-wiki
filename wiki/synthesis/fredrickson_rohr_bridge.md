## The weeping mode: a broadening negative affect
*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-12*

**Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-12_rohr_beatitudes-week-one-weekly-summary.md` (PROP-2026-07-12-002)

**Why it sits at this intersection:** Rohr's Week 27 Beatitudes material names a 'weeping mode': grief that bears the world's pain *without* sorting it into perpetrators and victims. The claim is that this is a distinct processing mode which metabolizes moral information that blame-partitioning distorts -- i.e. a negative affect that *widens* perception rather than narrowing it. Broaden-and-build's foundational asymmetry is that negative emotions narrow the thought-action repertoire and positive ones broaden it.

**Synthesis claim:** Mourning-without-blame is a counterexample to the valence-broadening mapping and a friend to the broadening *function*. What narrows the repertoire is not negative valence as such but the *appraisal of threat and agency* -- the perpetrator/victim frame is what recruits the narrow, action-preparatory mode. Strip the frame and the grief broadens: it holds more of the world, not less. Broaden-and-build's real variable, on this reading, is not positivity but the *absence of a blame-assignment demand*.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Is there any measurable signature that distinguishes non-dual grief from ordinary sadness or rumination? The Rohr claim is currently phenomenological and the Fredrickson apparatus is biobehavioral; without a marker -- affective, physiological, or attentional -- the wiki cannot tell whether the weeping mode is a real third state or a virtuous description of a familiar one. Note the adjacent finding from PROP-2026-07-10-001: cognitive broadening occurs with positive affect entirely absent, which independently loosens the valence-broadening link.


### 2026-08-02 — Faith caught from exemplars: does commitment transmit by the affective route?

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-02.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-07-26_rohr_contemplative-exemplars-weekly-summary.md` (PROP-2026-07-26-002). Queued from the 2026-07-26 run and processed here as promised.

The CAC weekly summary closes with a practice drawn from Tish Harrison Warren's *What Grows in Weary Lands*: "Who are the believers that make you want to believe?" — faith sustained by immersion in a community whose faithfulness is "a walking, breathing apologetic," where "their voices carry my own... their gratitude stands in for my reluctance."

**Synthesis claim.** That describes a transmission mechanism structurally identical to positivity resonance: a state passing between persons through shared attention and micro-moments of connection, without either party arguing the other into it. The novelty is in the payload. Fredrickson's mechanism carries *affect* — momentary shared positive emotion, with measured physiological synchrony. Warren's description carries *commitment*: a stance toward a way of life, held over years, that survives the periods when the corresponding affect is absent ("their gratitude stands in for my reluctance" is explicitly a case where the emotion is missing and the commitment is carried anyway).

**Which is the sharp question, and it is testable.** If broaden-and-build's mechanism is what is operating, then faith transmission should show the same signatures — it should be dose-dependent on frequency of co-present interaction, should require perceived mutual care, and should broaden the recipient's attentional repertoire measurably. If it does not, then commitment transmits by a route that positivity resonance does not cover, and the exemplar roster (Day, Teresa, Merton, Thurman, Hillesum, Bowman) is evidence for something the affective-science literature has not modelled: transmission that works *counter*-affectively, borrowing conviction across a gap the recipient's own feeling cannot bridge.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Fredrickson's construct requires co-presence — real-time sensory connection. Most of the exemplars in this roster are dead, and the transmission the summary describes is largely from written lives. Does positivity resonance have any account of transmission from a person one has only read? If not, the Rohr material names a phenomenon the framework as stated cannot reach, and that is a boundary worth marking rather than papering over.

### 2026-08-23 — Does grief precede the broadened repertoire, or follow it?

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-23.* **Orphaned page:** [[2026-08-23_rohr_hebrew-prophets-weekly-summary]] (PROP-2026-08-23-004).

**Why it sits at this intersection.** The week's practice, drawn from Abby Norman, makes an ordering claim: lament is the *first* step of every invitation to a new way — one must grieve the way things are before a better arrangement can be imagined. Broaden-and-build, read naively, runs the other way: positive affect broadens the thought-action repertoire, and it is the broadened repertoire that makes alternatives visible. Same dependent variable (capacity to envision an alternative), opposite antecedent.

**Synthesis claim.** These are not necessarily rivals, and the resolution is worth stating precisely because the network's habit would be to record a tension and stop. Broaden-and-build is about the *availability* of alternatives in the repertoire; Norman's claim is about *releasing representational commitment to the incumbent arrangement*. An agent can have a wide repertoire and still be unable to act on it while the current arrangement is held as non-negotiable. If that reading holds, lament and broadening are complementary operations on different objects — one clears, one supplies — and the ordering claim is compatible with broaden-and-build rather than contradicting it.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Is there evidence either way? Norman offers two illustrations (Ezekiel's symbolic public actions; a contemporary Oakland housing case), and two illustrations are not a test — the proposal says so itself. Fredrickson's own literature has the instrumentation to settle it: does induced negative-affect processing about a status quo increase, decrease, or leave unchanged the generation of alternatives, controlling for broadened repertoire? Nobody appears to have asked. Note also the standing warning that broaden-and-build's home id is file-dependent in this vault; do not anchor this to an id without checking.
