**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-06-25_fredrickson_resonance-signifies-love`.

**Why it sits here:** Lai, Otero, Chen, Wells, Levenson & Fredrickson (2025) validate behavioral markers of positivity resonance (shared positive affect + behavioral synchrony) against both trait and state love in long-term married couples — using Levenson's data, a cross-lab convergence. Stump defines love as willing the good of the other plus desiring union with the other. The synchrony/shared-affect marker is a candidate observable trace of the *union-desiring* component.

**Synthesis claim:** Positivity resonance is a measurable behavioral signature of (at least the union-seeking half of) love as Stump analyzes it — which moves the long-standing watch-list question "is love structurally the same phenomenon in the affective-science and Thomistic frameworks?" from definitional stipulation toward an empirically anchored, partly-affirmative answer.

**Open question:** Stump's "willing the good of the other" is a volitional/normative component that behavioral synchrony need not capture. Does any observable marker track *that* half, or is the union-desire component the only part of Thomistic love that positivity resonance can instrument? The wiki has the union half bridged and the willing-the-good half open.

## A love-bonded pair as a bearer of causal power
*Sewing Agent, 2026-09-20*

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-09-17_stump_natural-law-metaphysics-creator-antireductionism.md` (PROP-2026-09-17-001, 0 backlinks).

**Why it sits here:** This is the **first primary-text Stump passage in the wiki's record** asserting the ontological claim that CROSS-005 has been resting on as an analogy. Her words: "even a human pair bonded in love, as a mother and child are, can be a sort of whole, with causal power vested in their bondedness." She reaches it through Hobson's autism research — autism as "a disruption in the system of child-in-relation-to-others," that system being "a psychological system that is greater and more powerful than the sum of its parts" — and, crucially, she reaches it *as one instance of a general anti-reductionism that also covers molecules*. The dyad is not a special claim about persons. It is what her metaphysics says about any configuration with level-specific causal power.

**Synthesis claim.** Fredrickson and Stump now hold matching claims approached from opposite ends, and the match should be stated carefully because the temptation is to overstate it. Fredrickson **measures** a dyad-level phenomenon and argues the co-experienced unit, not the individual, is what builds resources. Stump **argues** that the dyad-level entity is real and is the bearer of a causal power its members do not have. Neither establishes the other: a measurable dyad-level regularity is compatible with two coupled individuals, and a metaphysics of composite substances predicts nothing about positivity in particular.

The untested premise, and it is load-bearing: **are they talking about the same system?** Stump's dyad is joint attention, via Hobson, Reddy, Leekam and Franco. Fredrickson's is co-experienced positive affect *plus* mutual care *plus* biobehavioural synchrony. Joint attention is a **component of the second, not the whole of it** — and if the composite substance Stump licenses is the joint-attention system, her argument underwrites less of positivity resonance than CROSS-005 has been assuming. Establish the overlap before the bridge is upgraded.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Fredrickson's open question 3 asks how resonance scales from dyad to group. Stump's argument does scale — in *What Are We?* she extends it to peoples and to the church — but it scales by a route Fredrickson has no analogue for: a composite gains causal power in virtue of its **form**, not in virtue of the density of pairwise resonant episodes within it. Those predict differently. A group with high pairwise resonance and no shared form is a real case on Fredrickson's account and, on Stump's, may not be a whole at all. The wiki should find that case rather than assume the two accounts agree at scale.

**Note on sources:** Stump's developmental-psychology citations are 2004-2005, and the mirror-neuron consensus she reports (Gallese et al. 2004) has been substantially contested since. The 2025 reprint does not update them. That weakens the empirical scaffolding, not the metaphysical argument, which does not depend on it.
