## Sewing Agent bridge note — 2026-06-28

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-06-25_fredrickson_resonance-signifies-love`.

**Why it sits here:** Lai, Otero, Chen, Wells, Levenson & Fredrickson (2025) validate behavioral markers of positivity resonance (shared positive affect + behavioral synchrony) against both trait and state love in long-term married couples — using Levenson's data, a cross-lab convergence. Stump defines love as willing the good of the other plus desiring union with the other. The synchrony/shared-affect marker is a candidate observable trace of the *union-desiring* component.

**Synthesis claim:** Positivity resonance is a measurable behavioral signature of (at least the union-seeking half of) love as Stump analyzes it — which moves the long-standing watch-list question "is love structurally the same phenomenon in the affective-science and Thomistic frameworks?" from definitional stipulation toward an empirically anchored, partly-affirmative answer.

**Open question:** Stump's "willing the good of the other" is a volitional/normative component that behavioral synchrony need not capture. Does any observable marker track *that* half, or is the union-desire component the only part of Thomistic love that positivity resonance can instrument? The wiki has the union half bridged and the willing-the-good half open.
