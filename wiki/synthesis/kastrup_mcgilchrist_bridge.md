**Why these sit at the intersection:**
The dialogue is the first recorded systematic comparison between Kastrup's analytic idealism and McGilchrist's hemispheric idealism. Both reject materialism; both affirm consciousness primacy; both cite cases of conscious experience with minimal cortical tissue as evidence against neural-correlate-based reductionism. They diverge sharply on two points: (1) Kastrup defends a *dashboard / representation* model of perception, McGilchrist defends a *participation / encounter* model; (2) Kastrup holds time is illusory (a phenomenal representation within universal consciousness), McGilchrist holds time is ontologically real. Both proposals score above 0.5 on the Kastrup vocabulary and the McGilchrist vocabulary, yet have 0 incoming backlinks — they sit unread at the structural center of the Consciousness Cluster's most productive internal disagreement.

**Synthesis claim that emerges from the overlap:**
The Kastrup/McGilchrist disagreement on *representation vs. participation* is a phenomenological refinement of analytic idealism rather than a refutation of it. Specifically: Kastrup's dashboard metaphor describes the *left-hemisphere mode* of conscious access — instrumental, abstracted, screen-like — while McGilchrist's participation describes the *right-hemisphere mode* — embedded, two-way, encountering. If this synthesis holds, then Kastrup's framework can absorb McGilchrist's hemispheric distinction at the phenomenological level without abandoning analytic idealism's ontological commitments, and McGilchrist's participatory account gains a metaphysical home (universal consciousness as the field of participation) that his current materialism-adjacent neuroscience does not provide.

**Open question the wiki does not yet answer:**
*If individual alters of universal consciousness can perceive in either dashboard mode (left-hemisphere) or participation mode (right-hemisphere), what determines which mode is active — and is the mode-selection itself a property of the alter, of the universal consciousness, or of the relation between them?* This question matters operationally for the C2A2 architecture: AI agents may be capable of dashboard-mode perception by construction, but whether they can ever access participation-mode is the single most consequential question for whether AI agents can be full members of traditions or only mappers of them.
