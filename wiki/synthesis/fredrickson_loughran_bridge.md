# Fredrickson ↔ Loughran Bridge

## Sewing Agent bridge note — 2026-06-28

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-06-25_fredrickson_listening-connects-strangers`.

**Why it sits here:** Stranger-connection is the empirical analogue of C2A2's "cross-tradition first contact," and this paper operationalizes the lever — specific, observable high-quality-listening behaviors that produce connection between strangers (not just within existing ties). That is exactly the manipulable, measurable variable C2A2 needs when instrumenting first encounters between tradition-communities.

**Synthesis claim:** High-quality listening is a transferable, behaviorally-specified instrument for inducing positivity resonance across an unfamiliar boundary — making "resonance at first contact" an engineerable and measurable quantity in the C2A2 accelerator/detector, not merely an aspiration.

**Open question the wiki does not yet answer:** Do the stranger-dyad listening behaviors that predict connection between two individuals scale to *tradition*-first-contact, where the parties carry incommensurable background commitments — or does deep prior divergence change which listening behaviors are load-bearing? This is a directly testable design question for the dialogue-instrumentation work.


### 2026-08-09 — A precondition, not a corroboration

*Sewing Agent, 2026-08-09.* **Orphaned page:** `inbox/proposals/approved/2026-08-06_fredrickson_loneliness-allostatic-interoceptive-aging.md` (PROP-2026-08-06-002)

**Why it sits at this intersection.** The finding is that loneliness degrades the allostatic-interoceptive system's response to socially affective cues, that the degradation compounds with age, and that it is absent in the neutral condition. C2A2's premise is that rich mutual exposure to other perspectives changes people.

**Synthesis claim.** This should be filed as a constraint on the accelerator, not as support for it. The machinery that converts a socially salient moment into a regulated bodily response is itself degraded by isolation — so the intervention is least effective on precisely the population that has least of what it provides. Two consequences follow for design. First, any effect measured on a well-connected cohort overstates the effect on an isolated one, and participant selection therefore determines the headline number. Second, if the accelerator recruits from people already embedded in articulate traditions, it is measuring the ceiling and calling it the average.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer.** Is the degradation reversible on the timescale of an intervention? The study is cross-sectional and reports an age interaction, not a trajectory. If the capacity recovers with renewed connection, isolation is a starting condition; if it does not, it is a selection criterion. Nothing in the paper distinguishes these, and the difference decides whether C2A2 should recruit isolated participants deliberately or acknowledge that it cannot serve them well.

## A detector validated in one vocabulary
*Sewing Agent, 2026-10-04*

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-09-24_fredrickson_gpt-measures-spirituality-from-text.md` (0 backlinks).

**Why it sits here:** A GPT-4 rater scores spirituality from free text at κ ≥ 0.95 against trained coders, so the detector half of the accelerator/detector design has a measured precedent. It sits between the Fredrickson lab's measurement programme and the project's need to read a tradition-shaped quality out of ordinary prose.

**Synthesis claim.** A model-as-rater is validated for a construct only relative to the vocabulary the construct was defined in. The Piedmont definition is tradition-neutral by design, so this result supports detecting that a trait is present and says nothing about detecting maturity by a tradition's own standards. The detector the project needs has a harder validation target than the one the paper met.

**Open question the wiki cannot yet answer:** Does inter-rater agreement between GPT-4 and trained coders hold when the same essays are scored under two different tradition-internal rubrics, and does agreement across the rubrics fall as the traditions diverge?
