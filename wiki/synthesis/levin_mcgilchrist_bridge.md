## Sewing Agent bridge note — 2026-06-28

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-06-25_fredrickson_interparental-positivity-spillover`.

**Why it sits here:** Interparental positivity spillover theory (Don, Simpson, Fredrickson & Algoe, 2025) extends positivity resonance from a within-dyad mechanism to a multi-person, cross-generational *propagation* — resonance between two partners shaping a third party (the child) who is not a participant. Read through Levin: this is the scale-up question — how local coupling produces higher-order collective states, the behavioral analogue of signal propagation across a coupled tissue. Read through McGilchrist: the transmitted relational climate is an *inherited mode of attention*, the affective/attentional stance a developing person is formed to bring to the world.

**Synthesis claim:** A resonant relationship is a field, not an event: it propagates beyond its participants (Levin's local-to-collective scale-up) and what propagates is partly a mode of attention (McGilchrist), so spillover is simultaneously a coupling-propagation phenomenon and an attention-inheritance phenomenon.

**Open question the wiki does not yet answer:** Is the spillover pathway better modeled as signal *propagation* across a relational network (Levin) or as the *induction* of an attentional stance in the receiver (McGilchrist) — and would those two models predict different decay rates or different reversibility as the third party matures? C2A2 needs this to model how a tradition-community transmits its "feel" to newcomers.

### 2026-07-19 — mind-blindness as trained attentional narrowing

*Sewing Agent, 2026-07-19.* PROP-2026-07-13-003 introduces **mind-blindness**: professional training installs a prior about where minds can be, and that prior then functions as a perceptual filter rather than as a hypothesis — when accepted training says a phenomenon is impossible, the clinician stops looking.

**Synthesis claim.** Levin's construct is descriptive and McGilchrist's is causal, which makes them complementary rather than duplicative. Levin observes that trained experts systematically fail to recognize agency in unfamiliar embodiments and names the effect. McGilchrist has a standing account of *why* expert training produces exactly this pathology: a mode of attention that grasps, isolates, and re-presents rather than attending openly, and which characteristically mistakes its own map for the territory while being constitutionally unable to notice that it has. That account predicts the specific asymmetry Levin reports — the prior is not merely wrong but self-sealing, because the attentional mode that installed it also determines what would count as evidence against it.

**Question the wiki cannot yet answer.** If mind-blindness is attentional rather than propositional, then argument should be a poor remedy and exposure a good one — you cannot talk someone out of a way of attending. Levin's proposed intervention (diverse-intelligence *education* for clinicians) is propositional. McGilchrist's diagnosis predicts it will underperform, and predicts what would work better. Does any of the clinician-response data in the talk's comment thread bear on which remedy actually shifted practice?
