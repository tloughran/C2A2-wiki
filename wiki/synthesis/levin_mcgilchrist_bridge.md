# Levin ↔ McGilchrist Bridge

## Sewing Agent bridge note — 2026-06-28

**Orphaned page at the intersection:** `inbox/proposals/pending/2026-06-25_fredrickson_interparental-positivity-spillover`.

**Why it sits here:** Interparental positivity spillover theory (Don, Simpson, Fredrickson & Algoe, 2025) extends positivity resonance from a within-dyad mechanism to a multi-person, cross-generational *propagation* — resonance between two partners shaping a third party (the child) who is not a participant. Read through Levin: this is the scale-up question — how local coupling produces higher-order collective states, the behavioral analogue of signal propagation across a coupled tissue. Read through McGilchrist: the transmitted relational climate is an *inherited mode of attention*, the affective/attentional stance a developing person is formed to bring to the world.

**Synthesis claim:** A resonant relationship is a field, not an event: it propagates beyond its participants (Levin's local-to-collective scale-up) and what propagates is partly a mode of attention (McGilchrist), so spillover is simultaneously a coupling-propagation phenomenon and an attention-inheritance phenomenon.

**Open question the wiki does not yet answer:** Is the spillover pathway better modeled as signal *propagation* across a relational network (Levin) or as the *induction* of an attentional stance in the receiver (McGilchrist) — and would those two models predict different decay rates or different reversibility as the third party matures? C2A2 needs this to model how a tradition-community transmits its "feel" to newcomers.
