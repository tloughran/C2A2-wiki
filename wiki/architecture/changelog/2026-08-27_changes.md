dispositions, ending a nineteen-day stall — and the most useful thing on the record is not the
backlog but the reason eighteen of those proposals were stuck. A classifier keying on the strings

cross-tradition content*, which is precisely what the Level-2 signal harvester consumes; its

findings about this pipeline itself: **there is no 2026-08-26 self-awareness run**, no changelog and
no snapshot, and no artifact anywhere says so — while the morning health check reported the pipeline
green because it read the scheduler rather than the shelf. And on the Summa side, a QC sweep found
the first defect in the *argument* layer rather than the sourcing layer: Day 18 stated the Father–Son
relation backwards inside the article whose purpose is to fix that direction, past every automated
gate and a prior QC pass marked `rewrote`.
