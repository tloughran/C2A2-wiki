  Summary: The corrective proposition is strongly supported and, in the disciplines that measure detectors for a living, is close to axiomatic. Signal-detection theory makes it geometric: a detector is a curve, not a point, so any change that reduces one error type without altering discriminability has simply moved the operating point along it — which means a favourable reading after a directional fix is exactly what you would expect whether or not the instrument improved. The soundiness manifesto shows that the two-sidedness is not a defect of immature tools but a structural consequence of the precision/scalability trade-off, and that the professional standard is to DOCUMENT where each kind of error is accepted rather than to claim one direction. Metrology supplies the sharpest version: without an external reference, an instrument's bias has no determinable sign, so "we know it errs" and "we know which way it errs" are different states of knowledge and C2A2 has only the first. Cristian's taxonomy explains why single-polarity remediation leaves a structural hole — distinct failure classes need distinct detectors, so fixing the fail-open path does not put the fail-closed path under observation. And the mutation-based soundness work shows the only known way to measure the invisible direction is to seed known defects and count misses, which C2A2 does not currently do. The strongest thing that can be said for 14b's worry is that two consecutive days produced findings of OPPOSITE polarity from the same instrument family, which on this literature is the expected observation, not the surprising one.

  Caveats: (a) The literature does NOT say the two error directions are symmetric in COST. Fail-safe design deliberately biases toward one direction because the loss function is asymmetric, and PREMISE-110 was validated on exactly that reasoning. So the item supports "the sign is not knowable without two-sided measurement," and NOT "directional remedies are wrong." A directional remedy chosen with a stated loss function and a measured effect on both error rates is good practice; C2A2's problem is that neither the loss function nor the second measurement is recorded. (b) ROC analysis presupposes labelled ground truth. C2A2 has none for its defect classes, which is why the mutation/seeding route in source 5 matters more than the ROC framing — it is the only one of these methods that is actually executable here.
