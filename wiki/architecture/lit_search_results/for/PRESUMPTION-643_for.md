  Summary: The supportive direction returns nothing, and the safety literature is
  emphatic in the other direction. Near misses are treated universally as leading
  indicators of failure, not as evidence of a functioning barrier — the whole point of
  collecting them is that they signal where controls, training or supervision are already
  breaking down before an incident lands. A zero incident count alongside a high near-miss
  count is the canonical picture of an unprotected system that has been lucky. C2A2's case
  is a textbook instance: six recorded near misses caught by six *different* accidental
  mechanisms means there is no control, only six coincidences.
