  Summary: The FOR direction found one positive case and it is instructive rather than supportive. In
  aviation's Minimum Equipment List regime a logged defect genuinely is a contained defect — but the
  containment is performed by a regulator-approved list of what may be deferred, an operational
  restriction that changes conduct while the defect stands, and a rectification clock that starts on
  logging and grounds the aircraft on expiry. The record is the trigger for those three things, not a
  substitute for them. That is the third independent regime to converge on the same conclusion the
  register already reached twice: PREMISE-164 found the operative feature to be an enforced deadline,
  and PREMISE-183(1) found that closure requires an effectiveness condition defined at open time.
  Against the presumption as stated, the estate's own holdings are decisive and were obtained before
