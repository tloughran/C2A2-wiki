  Strength of support: Strong

  Summary: Every field that has had to institutionalise deferral has independently concluded that the deferral must carry its own expiry, and has built a mechanism to enforce it — a review interval in oncology, a recertification cadence in access governance, a stale-bot in open source, a discharge rule in this project's own PREMISE-133. The epistemology matches: Friedman shows suspension is a committal attitude requiring warrant, and Chow's reject option is optimal only relative to a cost ratio that the environment is free to change, so nothing about a hold makes it self-maintaining. The empirical picture is worse than the theoretical one: long-lived bugs sit for a median of well over a year, and three quarters of watchfully-waited follicular lymphoma patients need treatment within five years, which is the cleanest available demonstration that a correctly-placed hold becomes an incorrect one without anyone doing anything wrong. Six queues at a 100% hold rate for eleven consecutive runs is, on this literature, not evidence of consistent judgement; it is the signature of a hold state that has no mechanism capable of ending it. The honest reading is that C2A2 has a deferral system with no discharge path, which PREMISE-133 already forbids for one class of suspension and which this item extends to the queues.
