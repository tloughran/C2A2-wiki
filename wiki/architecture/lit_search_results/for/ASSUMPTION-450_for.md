  Summary: The FEP corpus is built to answer "why do things persist?", not "why do they stop?". Its formal core (Markov blanket plus a surprise bound) yields an organism that, at equilibrium with a good generative model, would in principle self-maintain indefinitely; the dark-room debate is the field's own admission that stasis is the theory's default attractor and must be argued away. Three independent critical literatures (technical, ontological, methodological) establish that the FEP does not carry enough falsifiable structure to DERIVE time-asymmetric decay from first principles. The "absence" half of ASSUMPTION-450 therefore holds for the core formalism as Friston states it.
  Caveats: This search establishes only that the CORE formalism lacks a fatigue term. It does not establish that no FEP-ADJACENT construct supplies degradation — and 15b's search is the place that question is answered. The claim's second clause ("aging is evidence about FEP rather than an application of it") is conditional on the first, and this search does not reach it.
