  Summary: This is the multitrait-multimethod / robustness-analysis principle, one of the most strongly supported ideas in measurement and philosophy of science: a result confirmed by several operationally independent indicators is harder to spoof than any single indicator because an adversary (or artifact) would have to corrupt several differently-biased channels at once. Pre-specifying the battery with no post-hoc weighting additionally blocks garden-of-forking-paths inflation. C2A2 has already incorporated the triangulation premise, so this is internally consistent.

  Caveats: The decisive condition - already flagged in the existing triangulation premise - is GENUINE independence. Indicators sharing method variance or a common confound provide pseudo-convergence; 'operationally independent' must be demonstrated, not asserted. More indicators add degrees of freedom unless the battery and decision rule are truly pre-registered.
