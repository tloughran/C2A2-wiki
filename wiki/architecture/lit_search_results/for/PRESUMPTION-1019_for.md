  Strength of support: Weak

  Summary: The literature supports the transfer more at the level of functional criteria than of the four named authorities as such. Records-management standards (ISO 15489), diplomatics as extended by Duranti, and PROV-O were each written or re-argued for non-adjudicative, internal or digital recordkeeping. So an internal wiki is within the intended scope of those frameworks. FRE 803(6) is by its text open to any "organization" and rests its reliability rationale on checking, regularity and institutional reliance, which are not specific to courts. Recent AI-agent work (Riedl & Desai 2025; South et al. 2025) shows researchers importing agency-law and delegation concepts into agent systems and treating that as a productive analogy. None of this shows that the specific transfer (803(6), §2.03, UETA §14 to wiki provenance markers) is valid without an argument, and no source found tests that claim.

  Caveats:
    - Support is for transfer of the criteria in general. No source addresses the four named authorities as applied to a single-designer, no-adversary, no-fact-finder wiki.

    - Duranti's extension and ISO 15489 are archival; they support the author/writer distinction, not the evidentiary-weight claim (HIGH weight, withdrawal of the novelty flag).
