  Summary: The literature supports the first half of ASSUMPTION-1297 and the estate already knew it —
  PREMISE-188(3) holds that per-claim attachment of a qualifier is the achievable design and GRADE is
  the working proof. What the search adds is a quantified version for the falsification case:
  Registered Reports, which fix the test in advance and bind a later gate to it, produce null results
  at roughly five times the standard rate, with 43.66% versus 96.05% first-hypothesis confirmation.
  That is a large, real effect and it is the reason this result is not NO-SUPPORT-FOUND. But the
  mechanism that produces it is in-principle acceptance — an editorial step whose own procedure
  requires the preregistered falsifier to be read. Strip that step out and the same practice performs
  badly: in the adherence literature, more than half of preregistered articles added or dropped
  hypotheses, and disclosure of the deviation was the exception. The structured-dissent literature
  points the same way from a different angle — its effect is measured where the adversarial role is
  ASSIGNED to a person who then argues, not where a record is labelled attackable and left for
  whoever arrives. So the honest reading is that the assumption is supported exactly to the extent that
  something outside the filing agent is obliged to test the falsifier before adoption, and the estate's
  own demonstrated failure mode this week — tagged records written and not read, PREMISE-107's defect
  named three times in one day — is the case where nothing is.
