Sculley et al. supply the systems-level frame and the mirror concept: undeclared consumers are readers with no registered writer; the case here is a writer with no registered reader, and both are couplings that exist in practice while being absent from the system's declared structure. The practical consequence, which the register does not currently state, is that the remedy shape "declare it more prominently" is wrong for this failure — the fix is to make the record an object the future executor's own procedure is required to read, which is what neighbouring fields do by promoting the dependency to a declared, consumer-triggering edge.
