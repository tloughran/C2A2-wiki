  Summary: The item's core is well supported and largely already held. Four decades of measurement
  establish that in corpora where every reference resolves, roughly one in nine citations
  misrepresents its source in a way judged *major*, and three-quarters of wrong quotations are of
  intermediate or high importance to the citing argument — an error class an existence check cannot
  see by construction. The audit literature supplies the vocabulary and the default: re-performance
  is a named procedure distinct from inspection of records, and the PCAOB's operating test is whether
  an experienced auditor with no prior connection could reconstruct the work from the file, with an
  unsupportable conclusion treated as a deficiency. The most useful new finding is the reliability
  gradient: with a published risk-of-bias instrument, trained reviewers reach κ ≈ 0.62 on the
  mechanically checkable domain and κ ≈ −0.04 on the judgement domain, median 0.19. That is a direct
  measurement of the substitution 1108 names — agreement is purchased by asking only the questions
  answerable by pattern-matching, and does not transfer to the questions that matter. Where the file
  stops short is the synchrony clause: nothing located distinguishes "never informative" from "went
  stale," and that distinction decides whether the remedy is a new instrument or a shorter interval.

  Caveats:

    (b) PREMISE-132'S NARROWING TRAVELS WITH IT AND CONSTRAINS THE REMEDY: the requirement is

        over-specifies and would push work into a channel with known near-zero throughput

    (c) THE κ EVIDENCE IS ABOUT HUMANS AND MAY NOT TRANSFER AS ASSUMED. Low human agreement is partly
        a variance problem a deterministic checker does not have. What transfers is the *gradient*,
        not the absolute κ; do not report a predicted κ for a C2A2 reviewer.
    (d) N = 4 AND THE DAYS WERE NOT SAMPLED. The four defective pass-marks are the ones that were
        looked at — the selection condition most likely to produce them. No rate can be read off
