  Strength of support: Moderate

  Summary: I scored the affirmative reading as the wiki's Friston×Levin bridge uses it — that FEP-conformity is a literal, substrate-independent property of the systems in question rather than an explanatory fiction. That reading is defensible and defended. Kirchhoff, Kiverstein and Robertson's BJPS paper is a direct, peer-reviewed defence of realism about the FEP that specifically dismantles the most common route to the instrumentalist conclusion, and Ramstead, Sakthivadivel and Friston make the same move from the theory's own side. More to the point for this particular bridge: Friston and Levin have co-authored a derivation of FEP-conformity for generic systems under weak coupling, so the wiki is not extrapolating an analogy that its two thinkers would disown — they built the substrate-independence claim together. The support is only moderate for two reasons. First, the position is contested rather than settled: Mel Andrews (2021, *Biology & Philosophy* 36:30, "The math is not the territory") argues the FEP designates a model *structure* to which construals are added, an explicitly deflationary reading, and the existence of a paper titled "the literalist fallacy" presupposes a live opponent. Second — and this is the sharper problem for the wiki — Friston's own framing of the FEP as unfalsifiable and "almost tautological," on analogy with Hamilton's principle of stationary action, does supporting work at the level of the principle but takes the empirical content out of any claim that a *particular* system is FEP-conformant. If everything meeting weak-coupling conditions minimises variational free energy as a matter of mathematical necessity, then "Vmem patterns are FEP-conformant" is not a discovery about Vmem. The wiki's bridge is safe in asserting literal applicability and unsafe in treating that assertion as an empirical finding about the Levin side.

  Caveats:
    - The realist reading is a defended position, not a consensus. Andrews (2021) and the broader "Markov blanket trick" critique literature run the other way; that is 15b's assignment and this file should not be read as though the question were closed.
    - I found no Friston statement that is deflationary about *ontology*. His deflationary statements are about *falsifiability* — that the FEP is a principle rather than a testable theory. Those are different deflations, and the wiki should not treat one as the other. If a wiki entry currently cites "Friston's own deflationary statements" as support for instrumentalism, that citation is likely mis-framed.
    - Source 3 is an arXiv preprint; I did not confirm a journal version this session.
    - The wiki's bridge already contains the correct falsification test (do the dose–response and time-course profiles of bioelectric and neuromodulatory precision-restoration match quantitatively?). That is the right way to convert the ontological question into an empirical one, and it is still unanswered. Recommend resolving ASSUMPTION-939 by running that test rather than by further literature search.
