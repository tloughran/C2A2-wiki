The Kuhnian mechanism — incommensurability makes emergent paradigms illegible to incumbent literature — supplies the theoretical basis, and the SB/Prince framework supplies the quantitative footing. I am recording Moderate rather than cycle 1's Strong, because on re-reading, what this literature supports is the weaker conditional (absence does not disconfirm) rather than the stronger claim implied by the item statement (absence is *expected*, and therefore not informative about novelty either way).
