  Strength of support: Moderate

  Summary: The literature broadly supports the risk the presumption describes. Corpus-linguistics and culturomics work (Kane et al. 2025, read in full) holds that LLM text admitted where human voice is presumed is, by definition, not evidence of that human or culture. It calls the resulting loop an Ouroboros. Model-collapse and MAD research (Shumailov et al. 2024; Alemohammad et al. 2023, abstracts only) gives theoretical grounding that self-consuming generative loops lose tail and diversity, with selection bias accelerating diversity loss. On marking, W3C PROV already separates Person from SoftwareAgent attribution, publishing ethics bodies reportedly require disclosure of AI contributions, and digital-humanities work proposes span-level provenance. So the recommended remedy, marking model-authored spans separately from the human interlocutor, has precedent. Support is by analogy rather than direct, because no source addresses a single model acting as source, carder and reviewer of one named thinker's corpus.

  Caveats:
    - Domain transfer: model collapse concerns training-distribution degradation over many generations. C2A2's case is one or a few model-generated arguments weighed by model reviewers, with no retraining. The mechanism is analogous, not identical, and the magnitude may be small.
    - The Kastrup–Claude dialogue is arguably a legitimate primary source for Kastrup's responses, since his replies are his voice. The contamination risk applies to the Claude-generated turns only if they are carded as Kastrup's position. Literature supporting span-level attribution fits this better than corpus exclusion.
    - Two key ML sources were read at abstract level only. The nature.com full text was blocked from fetch in this session.
    - The PROV-O, COPE/ICMJE and C2PA evidence is snippet-level and partly secondary. Primary texts should be checked before any of these is cited as authoritative.
    - Publication bias: much 2024-2026 commentary on "AI slop" contamination is opinion or brief communication rather than measurement.
