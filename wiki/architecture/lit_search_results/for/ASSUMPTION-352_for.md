    1. Simmons, Nelson & Simonsohn 2011. 'False-Positive Psychology.' Psychological Science. - Specifying analyses before seeing outcomes removes researcher degrees of freedom that otherwise manufacture spurious confirmation.

  Summary: The assumption states the core logic of pre-registration and severe testing precisely: a self-administered test escapes viciousness when the criterion that could fail it is fixed before the outcomes are seen. This is one of the best-supported methodological principles in the reform-of-science literature, and C2A2 has already incorporated a pre-registration premise, so the claim is both externally grounded and internally consistent.

  Caveats: Pre-registration is necessary but not sufficient: vague or non-exhaustive specifications leave residual maneuverability (Gassen 2023; Bakker et al. on preregistration specificity), and register-then-look does not by itself supply PERSONNEL independence (PRESUMPTION-394).
