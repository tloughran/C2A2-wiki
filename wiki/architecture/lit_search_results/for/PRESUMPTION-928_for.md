  Strength of support: Moderate — but strictly conditional. The support is for "deferral CAN be a
    genuine suspended state," not for "deferral IS a neutral state." Every supportive source achieves
    neutrality through an explicit mechanism; none finds it as a default property of waiting.

  Summary: The presumption is defensible as a design claim and undefended as a description. On the
  design side, 21 CFR 312.42 is a fully verified primary source showing that a deferral state can be
  made genuinely non-operative: while a clinical hold stands, subjects may not be dosed, no new subjects
  may be recruited, and the sponsor may not proceed until an affirmative external notification lifts the
  hold — with the state converting to "inactive" if it persists a year. Decision theory supports the
  companion claim that waiting is a distinct object with its own value rather than a covert vote for the
  incumbent: Bernanke's irreversibility framework prices the option to wait, and the choice-deferral
  literature (White et al.; Costa-Gomes et al.) treats "select no option" as a member of the choice set
  that can be modelled as rational rather than as bias. On the descriptive side, however, every one of
  these achieves its neutrality through machinery that PRESUMPTION-928 assumes rather than installs.
  The clinical hold is neutral because the default while held is STOP; nothing in the literature I found
  suggests neutrality arises where the default while held is CONTINUE. The C2A2 case as 14b describes it
  — four lit-search cycles executed while the binary stood open, all four behaving as though limb (b)
  had been chosen — is the CONTINUE default, and no source located supports neutrality under it.

  Caveats:
  (a) THE CONDITION IS THE WHOLE FINDING. The four features that make an FDA clinical hold effective —
      order not annotation, STOP as the default, affirmative external resumption, and expiry into a
      changed state — are absent from an escalation register that logs a carry and lets the run proceed.
      The support here is therefore an argument for CHANGING the mechanism, not for trusting it.
      Read narrowly, this file supports "a hold can be a control"; it does not support "the escalation
      register is one."
  (b) THE SOURCES ARE ABOUT HOLDS IMPOSED FROM OUTSIDE. The FDA hold is imposed by a regulator on a
      sponsor. C2A2's escalation carry is self-imposed by the same layer that continues to operate. The
      externality that makes the clinical hold binding is exactly what the routed question about
      "explicit escalation without resolution" puts in doubt, and I found nothing supportive on
      self-imposed holds.
  (c) FOURTEEN CONSECUTIVE CARRIES IS PRIMA FACIE OUTSIDE THE SUPPORTED REGIME. If the unverified
      figure in Source 2 is right (80% of holds lifted, mean 6.2 months), formal holds in a working
      regulatory system get resolved. A binary at 13 days and a fourteenth consecutive escalation carry
      is not evidence of a functioning hold; it is the pattern the option-value literature would call
      exercising by expiry.
  (d) STOP-WORK AUTHORITY WAS SEARCHED AND DID NOT YIELD. The obvious organisational analogue —
      stop-work authority in construction, offshore and manufacturing — returned only trade and
      practitioner sources this run (IADC, Airswift, LBNL EHS manual, OH&S magazine), with no
      peer-reviewed effectiveness measurement. Those same sources note that workers frequently do not
      exercise the authority they nominally hold. I record the null result rather than dressing the
      trade material up as evidence.
  (e) DIRECTIONAL HONESTY. I did not search the status-quo-bias, omission-bias, decision-avoidance,
      drift-into-failure or normalisation-of-deviance literatures named in 14b's Search hint — those are
      the AGAINST direction and are 15b's assignment. The reader should assume this file is only half
