  Summary: The item makes a good epistemic point and rests it on a premise the literature does not
    support. Limb (a) — that "the wiki is current against a named, checked source" is a different and
    better fact than "nothing was found" — is sound, is the estate's checked-zero standard, and was not
    contested by the intake. Limb (b) is the load-bearing one and no supporting literature was found for
    it. The single measured figure located points the other way: self-maintained ORCID records recovered
    57-73% of a comprehensive database's papers, and were complete for fewer than half the researchers
    studied. The completeness gap is also structurally predictable for the two thinkers in question:
    Stump's Online Papers page and Fredrickson's PEP Lab pages are article-oriented, while a large share
    of both thinkers' public output is third-party-hosted talks, podcasts and interviews — precisely the
    category that self-maintained academic pages systematically omit. The wiki's coverage claim should
    therefore be scoped to what the checked sources cover, not to the thinkers' output.

  Caveats:
    - The ORCID coverage figures are about ORCID, not about personal websites, and are from one field
      (solar/heliospheric physics) with n=27. The transfer is directional only. A personal website could
      in principle be better maintained than an ORCID record, and I found no study comparing the two.
    - Nothing retrieved bears on *timeliness* as distinct from completeness. The assumption makes both
      claims and the second is entirely unaddressed by the literature found.
    - The run's own declined hit — rejecting a "Friday, October 19" keynote page on the internal evidence
      that October 19 is not a Friday in 2026 — is a genuinely good piece of source criticism and should
      be recorded as a credit independently of how limb (b) is ruled.
    - The intake is right that this is cheaper to settle empirically than by search: n=3 known
      third-party-hosted appearances per thinker, checked against their own pages, settles it in an hour.
      This search does not substitute for that and the recommendation should not be read as if it did.
    - Under PREMISE-182's form, even a clean n=3 result licenses very little: 3-for-3 with zero misses
      gives a 95% lower bound on coverage of 0.05^(1/3) = 36.8%. The in-house test should be sized
      accordingly, or reported as a falsification attempt rather than a coverage estimate.

    Implication: The assumption cannot be settled from the literature and must be settled in-house. The

    Recommended status: NOVEL — and UNFAVOURABLE. The gap exists because the field regards the question
      as already settled in the negative: self-maintained records are used as a supplement, never as a
      denominator. This is not an original contribution; it is a premise the field declines to entertain.
