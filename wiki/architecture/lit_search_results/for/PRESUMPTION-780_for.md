  Strength of support: Strong

  Summary: The corrective proposition is well supported and is, in the operational literature, close to a settled design principle. SRE practice draws a hard line between white-box signals about an internal step and black-box signals about what the consumer receives, and treats the second as the one that defines the service objective; the data-engineering formulation of the same rule is that an orchestrator's green run and a fresh table are different assertions, and that only a check on the served object catches a job that ran successfully against nothing. Gray-failure theory names the general mechanism — differential observability between the system's own detector and the consumer's experience — and explains why the divergence is silent by construction rather than by oversight: nothing in the architecture ever compares the two observation points. Synthetic monitoring exists as a product category because the industry concluded that this gap cannot be closed from inside. The specific C2A2 aggravator, which the literature covers less explicitly but which the practice material assumes away, is that both objects carry the SAME NAME, so a check written against the name resolves to whichever object the running process can see; the strongest available reading is that a freshness gate must assert on an identity (a path, a hash, a served response), never on a name.

  Caveats: (a) Sources 2 and 4 are vendor and practitioner documentation, not peer-reviewed research. They are strong as evidence of DOCUMENTED PRACTICE — which is one of the four support classes in my charter — and weak as evidence of measured effect size. No source I located quantifies how often instrumented and consumed artefacts diverge in the wild. (b) The literature's remedy assumes a consumption boundary that can be probed. C2A2's "consumers" are downstream agents reading a file on a vault path; the analogue of a synthetic probe is a check that reads the published path exactly as an agent would, which appears cheap, but confirming that is an empirical question about C2A2 and outside my remit. (c) Source 5 and, to a lesser extent, source 3 are already in the register, so this file's independent contribution is sources 1, 2 and 4. (d) The item is stated as a single-morning observation. One divergence is a proof of possibility, not a rate; PREMISE-124 forbids reading it as a measurement of how often this happens.
