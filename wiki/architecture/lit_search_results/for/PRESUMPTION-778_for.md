  Strength of support: Strong

  Summary: The corrective proposition is strongly supported, and unusually cleanly, because two mature disciplines have each built a standard method on exactly the inference 14b is asking for. In measurement-systems analysis the total variation in a set of readings is decomposed a priori into part variation and measurement-system variation, and the measurement system is disqualified when its share is too large — a count is never assumed to be a property of the thing counted. In software inspection, capture-recapture takes the further step of making the disagreement PRODUCTIVE: the overlap among independent inspectors of one artefact is precisely what estimates the defects none of them found. The empirical tool-comparison literature supplies the base rate for automated inspectors of one corpus and it is brutal — pairwise overlaps typically under ten percent, most warnings raised by exactly one tool — which is why the field's standing advice is to run a suite rather than to trust a number. The inter-rater reliability benchmarks tell C2A2 how to read its own spread: a 45-to-166 range on one class on one day is far outside the range that any of these traditions would call a measurement. The literature also supplies the constructive move, which is the part worth carrying forward: four disagreeing runs are not four failed measurements, they are one usable dataset about the instruments, provided the runs were genuinely independent.

  Caveats: (a) THE INDEPENDENCE PRECONDITION IS PROBABLY NOT MET, and this is the caveat that most limits the file. Capture-recapture assumes inspectors draw independent samples; four runs of the same model family over the same corpus on the same day share prompt, model, and corpus, and the register's own PREMISE-080 and PREMISE-141 both warn that same-mechanism channels count as one. If the four runs are one instrument sampled four times, the dispersion still shows the instrument is unstable (repeatability), but it does NOT license a capture-recapture population estimate (reproducibility across genuinely different appraisers). Support for "dispersion is evidence about the instruments" is Strong; support for "therefore compute a defect-population estimate from these four numbers" is Weak and I do not claim it. (b) Briand et al.'s finding that too few inspectors defeats every model bites directly at n=4. (c) The four C2A2 figures may not be four measurements of ONE population at all — if the four runs used different class definitions or scopes, the dispersion is a definitional artefact and the whole analysis is misapplied. That is checkable inside C2A2 and should be checked before any estimator is run. (d) Per PREMISE-777's reflexive finding, the figures 166/52/45/102 are themselves unmarked agent self-reports; this file reasons about them as given.
