  Strength of support: Strong

  Summary: This is the most settled item in the batch after PRESUMPTION-770. Concurrency control has treated the lost update as the foundational hazard for fifty years, and the corrective proposition — that unmediated concurrent writes destroy work rather than duplicate it — is definitional rather than empirical. Two further points sharpen the finding. First, Kung and Robinson's distinction matters: optimistic concurrency is a *protocol with a validation step*, so describing C2A2's convention as an optimistic scheme flatters it — without a version check at write time it has no correctness argument at all, only a low collision probability, which two collisions in one day undermines. Second, Chubby's operational lesson is that advisory locking depends on universal cooperation and that large systems found this insufficient, which is the strongest empirical precedent against convention-only mitigation. The 2026 agent-coordination material adds a useful caution: locks alone address contention but not duplicated effort, so the correct instrument is a lock or lease *plus* shared completion state — meaning 14b's "redundancy versus contention" framing may be a false dichotomy, with both present and each needing a different mechanism.

  Caveats: (a) The database literature assumes a mediating transaction manager. C2A2 writes files, likely without one, so the applicable remedies are the weaker file-level ones — advisory lock files, compare-and-swap on mtime or content hash, or serialising writes through a single agent — each with different residual windows that this search did not compare. (b) Leases have a known residual failure mode (the stale-holder window) which any mitigation inherits; "install leases" is not a closure. (c) Two collisions in one day is a rate without a denominator: without knowing how many concurrent-run opportunities occurred, the collision probability is unknown, and the low-contention premise that would justify an optimistic approach cannot be evaluated either way. (d) The agent-specific sources are all unverified and partly promotional (agent-coherence.dev is a product site); their claims about lock-plus-state driving redundant rediscovery "to zero" should be treated as vendor assertion.
