  Summary: The corrective proposition is supported at the level of theory, of applied practice, and of empirical precedent. AGM belief revision establishes that consistency is a set-level property and that maintaining it requires operators over the set — which means no amount of per-item rigour can produce it, and the gap 14b identifies is structural rather than a lapse. Requirements engineering supplies the applied version and, usefully, a tractable architecture: nominate candidate conflicting pairs cheaply, confirm expensively. The Wikipedia inconsistency-detection work is the best existence proof that large curated bases carry contradictions that per-item review misses. Two further points are worth carrying forward. First, the paraconsistent literature shows that *tolerating* a contradiction is a defensible option — C2A2 need not resolve every conflict, but it cannot choose to hold one it has not detected. Second, the fact that the finder was an outbound agent is itself explicable in this frame: an outbound agent reads across items by the nature of its task, so it performs a set-level operation incidentally, while inbound instruments validate item-wise. That is an argument that the finding was lucky rather than that the instruments were negligent.

  Caveats: (a) Scaling is the real constraint and the literature is candid about it: pairwise consistency checking is quadratic in register size, and the requirements literature adopts two-stage filtering precisely because exhaustive comparison is infeasible. Any C2A2 instrument will therefore be a sampling instrument with a miss rate, which reopens the PRESUMPTION-768 problem (how do you know the consistency checker is working?). (b) AGM assumes a formal logical language with a consequence relation. C2A2's register is natural-language prose, where "contradiction" is often a matter of interpretation and the AGM machinery does not straightforwardly apply. This is the largest transfer gap. (c) Sources 3-6 are unverified; the strongest verified support (AGM, Priest) is the most abstract, and the most concretely useful support (two-stage conflict detection) is the least verified. (d) A single contradiction is not a rate. The literature establishes that contradictions are expected in sets of this kind; it does not establish that C2A2's register has an unusual density of them, and 14b's item does not claim otherwise.
