Part (ii) is where the search found something the register does not hold: functional-safety practice does not treat independence as a property a component either has or lacks, but as a quantity — the beta factor — estimated from a structured checklist, with published indicative ranges and a residual that never reaches zero. That matters for C2A2 in a specific way: the fleet's monitors are maximally coupled to their subjects on every axis the checklist asks about, so "the monitors are independent" is not merely unproven, it is the least defensible position available, and any future claim of unattended operation must carry the residual rather than assert its absence.
