  Caveats: The support is for entropy/thermodynamics as an *ordering principle*, not for the specific ladder in r292+. Three conditions weaken it. (1) Carroll's own result shows complexity is non-monotone in entropy, so "from highest to lower [entropy], from simplicity to complexity" bundles two axes that diverge; a ladder that uses both will not be a single order. (3) Chaisson's metric is disputed and Anderson's hierarchy is explicitly *not* reductive in the "effect follows from cause" sense ("more is different"), so the objective ordering it supports is one of complexity, not of derivation.
