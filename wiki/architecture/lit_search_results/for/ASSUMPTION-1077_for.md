  Summary: The item's central claim is correct and better supported than it states. Every field it names has, in fact, replaced per-item human adjudication with a rule declared in advance, and the rules are formal objects with published structure: ISO 2859-1 fixes an AQL, an inspection level, accept/reject numbers and switching rules before any lot is seen; PCAOB AU 350 / AS 2315 derives the extent of direct examination from three declared parameters (tolerable misstatement, allowable risk of incorrect acceptance, population characteristics) and explicitly refuses to name one acceptable level of sampling risk, making the parameter a stated design choice rather than a constant; lights-out machining pre-qualifies the unattended window by eliminating enumerated failure modes. **The most useful find is ISO 2859-3, because it is the case where the substitution is total — lots are accepted without being inspected at all — and it shows exactly what the standard demands in exchange: a lot-by-lot QUALIFICATION period first, evidence that the producer's own quality system can detect a shift, declared skip frequencies, and a defined INTERRUPTION state that reverts to full inspection on adverse evidence. A declared acceptance design is therefore not a licence written once and forgotten; it is a state machine with an entry criterion, a sampling rate, and a documented way back.** On P2, the override rate is a real and used metric with published benchmarks: below 5% is read as rubber-stamping, 10-20% as healthy, above 30% as a signal that the upstream process, not the gate, is the problem. The mechanism is automation bias, and its most relevant property is that complacency is greatest for aids of high and constant reliability — which is precisely the regime C2A2 believes itself to be in. **The proposed measurement is therefore sound in principle and the timing constraint 14a records is correct: the rate must be computed on the historical attended record, before the eleven-day backlog is adjudicated, because adjudicating a backlog under time pressure is the exact condition the access-review literature identifies as producing rubber-stamping — it would manufacture the low rate rather than measure it.**

  Caveats:
    (a) **THE SUBSTITUTION MOVES THE HUMAN; IT DOES NOT REMOVE HIM — AND THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING QUALIFICATION.** In every source located, the declared acceptance design is set by the party who bears the risk and is NOT set by the party whose output is being accepted. The AQL is the customer's. The tolerable misstatement and the allowable risk of incorrect acceptance are the auditor's, exercised on behalf of users of the financial statements. Skip-lot qualification is granted to the supplier by the inspecting authority, not claimed by the supplier. **This collides directly with PREMISE-096 (no self-produced artefact may certify itself; independence must draw on a genuinely disjoint evidence source). A declared acceptance design that C2A2 writes for C2A2 is a self-certification and is not the thing the literature validates.** What the literature supports is that Tom's role changes from per-item adjudicator to (i) author or signatory of the acceptance rule, (ii) periodic re-qualifier, and (iii) the authority who decides when to revert to full inspection. That is a very large reduction in attendance and it is real; it is not zero, and the item should not be carried forward as "validity without the human."
    (b) **THE MEASURAND FOR AN ACCEPTANCE RULE DOES NOT YET EXIST HERE.** Acceptance sampling presupposes a defined defect, a bounded lot, and an operating-characteristic curve — that is, a labelled notion of "nonconforming." PREMISE-158 records at validation that C2A2 has no labelled ground truth for any defect class, PREMISE-114/161 record that no instrument here has an external calibration, and PREMISE-101 records that counts over shared artefacts are properties of a reading. **Writing an acceptance design therefore requires first writing down what a defective item IS and what the lot is — which is a definitional act the register has repeatedly found this system has not performed. The acceptance-sampling frame does not supply that definition; it consumes it. This is a precondition, not an objection, but it is the expensive part and the item's "you could write it once" understates it.**
    (c) **THE OVERRIDE RATE IS AMBIGUOUS ON ITS OWN, AND THE LITERATURE RESOLVES THE AMBIGUITY WITH A SECOND SIGNAL, NOT WITH THE RATE.** A near-zero rate is consistent with ratification AND with genuinely good upstream output — the source that supplies the 5% threshold also supplies the >30% reading in which a high rate indicts the upstream process rather than vindicating the gate, which shows the metric is non-monotonic in gate quality. The published remedy is behavioural: time per decision, batch size, whether flagged-anomalous items were approved, per-reviewer quality scores. **If the historical attended record does not carry per-item timing or per-item disposition reasons, the override rate alone will not discriminate the two hypotheses, and the "decisive action" 14a records will return an uninterpretable number.** The cheapest strengthening available: alongside the rate, count what fraction of overrides carried a stated reason, and whether any item flagged by an agent as contested was approved unchanged.
    (d) **BASE-RATE AND POPULATION-COMPARABILITY PROBLEM.** An override rate computed on the last attended period is a statement about the items reviewed THEN. PREMISE-102/106 record that this system has a review channel with demonstrated near-zero throughput; if the historical denominator is small, the rate carries a confidence interval wide enough to span the 5% and 20% thresholds, and PREMISE-161's guard-band rule then forbids a decision inside it. **Compute and report the denominator and an interval with the rate, or the finding is not decidable.** Separately, eleven dark days may have changed what the agents produce, so a rate measured on the old population may not transfer.
    (e) **SOURCE-QUALITY ASYMMETRY, DECLARED.** P1 rests on three mature standards families read at snippet or index level, none read in full (two are paywalled). P2's decisive quantitative claim rests on ONE vendor-adjacent opinion article with unattributed statistics — that is a thin foundation for a threshold that would drive a disposition, and it is the weakest load-bearing point in this file. The lights-out leg has no research literature behind it at all. **I did not locate peer-reviewed measurement of override rates in AI-assisted review gates, nor any study relating override rate to downstream defect escape. If that measurement exists, it would replace source 4 and should be sought.**
    (f) **P3 IS A FRAMING, NOT A FINDING.** "The absence of a validity mechanism became visible" is a rhetorically strong and probably true reading, but nothing located supports it as an empirical claim, and the alternative reading — that the eleven days were BOTH a period of unvalidated output AND a period in which the gap became visible — is not excluded by anything here. The two are compatible, and treating the reframe as retiring the defect would be the error PREMISE-073's "HITL theater" caveat warns against.

    The disposition-worthy residual, stated so it cannot be confused with PREMISE-073: **073 holds that unattended high-impact actions must be reported rather than executed. This item holds the complementary and previously unheld claim that the REVIEW ITSELF is a designed object with declarable parameters — what is accepted, on what evidence, at what stated risk of incorrect acceptance, with what qualification period and what reversion trigger — and that the value of the existing gate is empirically measurable from its override rate.** The two cheapest actions both survive every caveat above: (1) compute the historical override rate WITH its denominator and, if available, per-item timing, and do it before the backlog is adjudicated; (2) write the acceptance rule's three parameters down — what counts as a defective item, what the lot is, and what risk of incorrect acceptance is tolerated — because caveat (b) shows that step is the precondition for everything else and it is the step the register shows this system has never taken.
