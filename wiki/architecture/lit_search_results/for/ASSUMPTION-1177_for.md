    FOR THE FRISTON HALF (overlap criterion):
    1. Friston, K. J., Parr, T., Heins, C., Constant, A., Friedman, D., Isomura, T., Fields, C., Verbelen, T.,
       Ramstead, M., Clippinger, J., & Frith, C. D. 2024. "Federated Inference and Belief Sharing."
       *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 156: 105500. — The strongest support for the attribution. This
       paper is explicitly about distributed intelligence emerging under *belief sharing* among agents who
       share a world and a world model; it reads communication as nested free-energy-minimising processes and
       gives "a mathematical image of communication as generalised synchrony," in which agents synchronise
       their internal narratives so that the segments they generate cohere as part of a single narrative.
       Belief overlap is not merely a by-product here; it is the quantity the account is about.
    2. Friston, K. J. & Frith, C. D. 2015. "A Duet for One." *Consciousness and Cognition* 36: 390–405. —
       Establishes the generalised-synchrony reading of communication in the two-agent case: minimising free
       energy under a shared generative model drives the agents' internal states into synchrony. This is the
       source of the "success = belief overlap" reading and it is a fair reading of that paper.

    AGAINST THE COMPLETENESS OF THE FRISTON ATTRIBUTION (found in the course of the FOR search, and reported
    here because it bears on whether the attribution is *entailed*):
    3. Friston, K. J., Rigoli, F., Ognibene, D., Mathys, C., Fitzgerald, T., & Pezzulo, G. 2015. "Active
       Inference and Epistemic Value." *Cognitive Neuroscience* 6(4): 187–214; and Parr, T. & Friston, K. J.
       2019. "Generalised Free Energy and Active Inference." *Biological Cybernetics* 113: 495–513. — Expected
       free energy decomposes into an *extrinsic* (pragmatic) term and an *epistemic* (intrinsic, information-
       gain) term. The epistemic term drives the agent to maximise the divergence between posterior and prior
       beliefs — that is, it rewards *moving away from* the current belief state, and it is what supplies
       novelty and salience in this framework. Friston's own formalism therefore already contains both a
       convergence pressure and an exploration pressure, and already supplies a principled arbitration between
       them (epistemic value is maximised until information gain is exhausted, after which extrinsic value
       dominates).

    FOR THE LEVIN HALF (emptiness criterion):
    4. Blackiston, D., Levin, M., et al. — the xenobot / "exploration vehicle" strand. See Levin, M. 2024,
       "Ingressing Minds: Causal Patterns Beyond Genetics and Environment in Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid
       Embodiments," and the Thoughtforms exposition of the same programme (drmichaellevin.org, retrieved
       2026-08-24). — Xenobots are described as "exploration vehicles" or "periscopes" into a latent space of
       forms, and are said to show that standard genomes and materials can access patterns *not directly
       selected in evolutionary history*. Here the object of interest genuinely is the region of the space
       that evolution has not occupied — an emptiness criterion in the vault's sense. Corroborated empirically
       by the transcriptomic finding that basal xenobots show significantly larger inter-individual gene
       variability than age-matched embryos, "suggesting increased exploration of the transcriptional space"
       (*Communications Biology* 8, 2025, "Basal Xenobot transcriptomics reveals changes and novel control
       modality in cells freed from organismal influence"; author list not verified).

    AGAINST THE COMPLETENESS OF THE LEVIN ATTRIBUTION:
    5. Levin, M. 2025. "The Multiscale Wisdom of the Body: Collective Intelligence as a Tractable Interface
       for Next-Generation Biomedicine." *BioEssays* 47: e202400196; and the multi-scale competency
       architecture literature (see also Levin, M. 2023, "Collective Intelligence: A Unifying Concept for
       Integrating Biology Across Scales and Substrates," *Communications Biology* 7, 2024; author list not
       fully verified). — Morphospace navigation *as a formalism* is homeostatic and target-directed. Cell
       collectives "navigate the space of possible anatomical outcomes (morphospace) … to achieve the correct
       species-specific target morphology even when circumstances, or their own parts, change," and every
       level of the competency architecture "traverses various spaces toward preferred regions." The
       navigation formalism is explicitly a setpoint-return formalism. Success in it is *arrival at a
       specified target*, not arrival at an unoccupied region.

  Strength of support: Moderate (Friston half: Strong for the attribution as stated, Weak for the attribution
  as *entailed*; Levin half: Weak-to-Moderate — the emptiness criterion is attributable to a different strand
  of Levin's corpus than the one named)

  Summary: The attribution survives at one end and does not survive at the other, and neither outcome
  dissolves the contradiction. On the Friston side, the overlap criterion is a fair and well-evidenced reading
  of the belief-sharing literature specifically: *Federated Inference and Belief Sharing* is about belief
  alignment, and *A Duet for One* casts communication as generalised synchrony. But the criterion is not
  *entailed* by the free-energy formalism as a whole, because expected free energy decomposes into pragmatic
  and epistemic terms and the epistemic term rewards maximal divergence of posterior from prior — the opposite
  pressure. On the Levin side, the named formalism does not support the attributed criterion at all:
  morphospace navigation is a homeostatic, target-morphology-seeking account in which success is arrival at
  the correct species-specific endpoint. The emptiness criterion belongs instead to the xenobot / latent-space
  strand, where the explicit object is patterns not selected in evolutionary history. The net effect is a
  relocation rather than a dissolution: the exploration/consensus tension is real, but it lives *inside*
  Friston's expected free energy, where it is already formally arbitrated, rather than *between* two traditions
  where it is not. That relocation is the substantive finding of this search and it is favourable to the
  vault's underlying diagnosis while unfavourable to its bookkeeping.

  Caveats: The Friston-side arbitration should not be over-read. The epistemic/pragmatic decomposition
  resolves exploration versus exploitation *for a single agent selecting a policy*; it is not a criterion for
  scoring a multi-agent synthesis system, and no source found extends it to that case. The Levin-side
  finding is a reading of a large and rapidly moving corpus in which the same author uses "navigation" both
  for homeostatic setpoint-return and, in the Platonic-space material, for exploratory ingression; the two
  usages are not always separated in the primary sources, so "misattribution" may overstate what is really an
  ambiguity in the corpus. The 2024–2025 Levin citations were verified by title and venue but not read in
  full; the transcriptomics paper's author list was not verified. Search scope: good on the Friston side
  (three distinct sub-literatures: belief sharing, generalised synchrony, expected free energy), moderate on
  the Levin side (morphospace navigation, multi-scale competency, xenobots/Platonic space) — did NOT cover
  Levin's bioelectric-pattern-memory papers, which is where a target-versus-novelty tension might be stated
  most explicitly.

  Recommendation: PARTIALLY-SUPPORTED
