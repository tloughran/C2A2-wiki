  Summary: Nothing in the FOR direction supports PRESUMPTION-944, and the search located the specific
  regime in which the exemption would have to exist if it existed anywhere. Where spoken reporting is
  permitted in a regulated safety-critical domain — the verbal-order regime in US hospital care — the
  spoken report is subjected to MORE identification discipline than a written one, must be transcribed
  into the record immediately with named giver and receiver, and must be authenticated within a stated
  window; the recognised exemptions are narrow, time-boxed, and exempt a procedural ordering rather
  than the record itself. The single FOR datum I could find is that CMS declined to write read-back
  into the regulatory text, which is a fact about one verification step and not about record-exemption.
  Against the presumption, the best experimental evidence is severe: Bhabra et al. report 2.5%
  information retention under verbal-only handover across five cycles against 99% with a printed
  handout. And the estate already holds the general case at High confidence in two places — PREMISE-195
  (voluntary self-report is not a detection control; detection must be evidenced by an artefact
  produced independently of the party assessed) and PREMISE-194 (an agent's recollection of its own work
  is not an independent copy of the record; corroboration must be cited rather than narrated). A spoken
  morning report is simultaneously the assessed party's own assertion AND an in-session recollection,
  which places it in the weakest evidence class the register recognises rather than in an exempt one.
  The one honest thing to say for the channel is PREMISE-116's: interactive delivery is the highest-
  efficacy way to change a recipient's behaviour, which is a reason to keep speaking to the designer
  and an aggravating factor rather than a mitigating one, since a false all-clear delivered well is
