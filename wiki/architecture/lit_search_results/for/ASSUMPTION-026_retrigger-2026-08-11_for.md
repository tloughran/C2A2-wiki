  Caveats: (a) "Genuine behavioral data" is the contested term throughout. The 2026 literature is consistent that LLM agents replicate human behaviour reliably only under specific conditions, with deviations becoming prominent in complex environments, and that they are "not yet suitable as universal substitutes for human participants." (d) Classical ABM derives agent rules from validated behavioural models; LLM tradition-agents derive them from text corpora with unvalidated fidelity, so "genuine" is asserted rather than demonstrated.
