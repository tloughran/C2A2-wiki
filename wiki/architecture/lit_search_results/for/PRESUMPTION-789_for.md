  Summary: The corrective proposition is strongly supported, and the diagnosis literature the queue entry pointed at turns out to have named every part of the C2A2 pattern separately. Graber et al. supply the base rate: in a hundred studied diagnostic errors, cognitive factors contributed in 74% and premature closure was the single most common cognitive cause — so failing to keep testing after a satisfactory explanation is the leading failure mode of diagnosis, not a marginal one. Croskerry supplies the two finer distinctions the register does not hold: search satisficing, which is precisely "confirmed only on cases where the explanation predicts what they found," and diagnosis momentum, which is precisely how one run's account became six runs' background assumption without any of them testing it. The software-engineering literature carries the finding into this system's own domain and adds the sharpest result for C2A2's purposes: confirmation bias in debugging leads programmers to misidentify the reasons for failure, and individual experience does not reduce it — the effective controls are detailed specifications and organisational structure, both of which are design variables rather than exhortations. Set against this, PREMISE-107 already holds the operative rule (run the discriminating test) and PREMISE-135 already holds the severe-test requirement, so what this file adds is not a new norm but the measured standing of the failure and two named sub-mechanisms the register does not distinguish.

  Caveats: (a) The transfer is by analogy in one respect that matters. Medical premature closure concerns closing on a diagnosis before verification within a CASE; the C2A2 analogue is closing on an explanation across a CORPUS, where the explanation's scope is the claim at issue. The analogy is good but is not identity, and the medical prevalence figures do not transfer as numbers. (b) PREMISE-069 is the register's own counter-instance and it is genuinely relevant: a universal anomalous reading in this very system was correctly attributed to a benign read-path artifact. (d) PREMISE-152 bears and should be read alongside: six homogeneous runs concurring is not six confirmations, because homogeneous unguided agreement does not outperform a single isolated pass. The "six runs attributed" figure is therefore weaker evidence than its size suggests, in BOTH directions.
