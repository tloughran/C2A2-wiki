    Item: ASSUMPTION-2141
    Strongest counterargument: Resonance requires a natural frequency, a coupling channel and a response that peaks when driving and natural frequencies match. A tradition has no identified period; persons are not driven at it; and what persists in traditions is better explained by selective reconstruction toward attractors than by entrainment. The one empirical literature that does show entrainment (interpersonal synchrony) works over seconds among co-present people. So "slow resonator" is a picture, and the three-scale link it suggests is borrowed from the image, not found in data.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: the wiki uses the phrase only as Rosa does (a relation of being addressed and responding) or as an explicitly labelled analogy, and keeps the cross-scale question open rather than answered.
