  Mitigations available: State an explicit membership criterion independent of folders; distinguish framework-suppliers (MacIntyre, possibly Loughran) from object-level traditions in the data model and in any count; decide deliberately whether MacIntyre is a member, the arbiter, or both (and if both, mark the self-reference); reconcile the roster against the criterion rather than against the directory listing (couples PRESUMPTION-353).
