  Summary: The checked-zero standard is sound and is not what I am challenging. The completeness limb is
    not sound, and there is a directly transferable number: in the one domain where single-source recall
    has been measured prospectively, a single source returns a median of roughly 71-76% of the relevant
    material, and adding any second independent source lifts that to 85-90%. There is no reason to expect
    a personal webpage to do better than a curated bibliographic database, and several reasons to expect
    it to do worse — personal pages are curated for publications, and the output types this estate tracks
    for Stump and Fredrickson are overwhelmingly third-party-hosted and third-party-announced. The run's
    own method concedes this: it checked the Thomistic Institute podcast, which is not Stump's page,
    because Stump's page would not have carried it. The timeliness limb is separable and untested — a
    page can be complete and six weeks stale, and nothing in the run distinguishes "no new entries" from
    "no recent edits." The repair is PREMISE-140's: name the metric by its channel. "Current against
    Stump's Online Papers page, his Video Lectures page, and the Thomistic Institute podcast feed, as of
    2026-09-10" is fully defensible and is what the run actually did.

  Specific risks: The specific risk is a false-negative that is invisible and self-reinforcing. A
    third-party-hosted appearance missed this week is not queued for next week — the checked-zero record
    asserts the week was empty, so no later run has a reason to revisit it. Over a quarter, at a plausible
    25% miss rate on a low-volume thinker, the estate accumulates a systematic under-representation that
    no internal check can detect, because PREMISE-189 holds that the artifact set alone cannot distinguish
    omission from absence. Worse, the *conclusion* is the thing that propagates: "both traditions are
    genuinely quiet this week" is a claim about the thinkers, derived from a claim about their webpages,
    and by PREMISE-140 that renaming is where the error enters. If it is later used to support a
    cross-tradition inference — a displacement measurement, a dissensus rate, an activity comparison
    between traditions with differently-maintained web presences — the miss rate becomes a differential
    bias, not just a gap.

  Mitigations available:
    - **Add exactly one independent second source per thinker.** Bramer et al. put the marginal gain at
      roughly 72.6% → 85-90%, and it is the largest single improvement available for the least work. For
      these two: a Google Scholar alert plus a YouTube/podcast-host search for Stump; a PubMed/Scholar
      alert plus a press-mention search for Fredrickson.
    - Rename the claim per PREMISE-140. This costs one line and removes the entire error class: report
      "current against [named channels]" rather than "current against their publication record."
    - Separate the timeliness limb: record each checked page's own last-modified date alongside the
      check. A page not updated since July does not certify a quiet week; it certifies a quiet page. This
      is one `curl -I` per source and it converts limb C from an assumption into a datum.
    - Run PRESUMPTION-947's n=3 test, which the intake note already proposes and which is cheaper than
      any search: take three known third-party-hosted appearances per thinker from the last 12 months and
      check whether each appears on the thinker's own pages. Six checks settles limb B per thinker.
    - Record the denominator per PREMISE-168: "zero proposals out of [N sources checked]" rather than
      "zero proposals."

    Strongest counterargument: The assumption is doing something more modest than the completeness
      reading attributes to it, and the modest reading is correct. The run is not claiming to have
      enumerated the thinkers' output; it is claiming that a *specific, named, repeatable* check returned
      zero, and that this is a materially better epistemic object than an unbounded search that happened
      to surface nothing. That is exactly right, and it is rarer in this estate than it should be: this
      was the day's one run whose null result is auditable at all. The systematic-review recall numbers
      do not transfer cleanly either — those measure recall against a *known* gold-standard set of
      included studies, which exists precisely because someone enumerated the space; here no such set
      exists or can exist, so "75% recall" has no denominator to be 75% of. Demanding completeness from a
      check whose virtue is boundedness would destroy the one auditable null result in the corpus and
      replace it with an unbounded search that certifies nothing. The run also declined a search hit on
      internal evidence (a "Friday, October 19" keynote page, when October 19 is not a Friday in 2026),
      which demonstrates the standard operating correctly in the other direction.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (1) the claim as *recorded* must be channel-named,
      not thinker-named — the run's prose already overreaches its method and that is the whole defect;
      (2) at least two independent channels per thinker, since the marginal value of the second is large
      and of the third is small; (3) page staleness must be recorded separately from content emptiness;
      and (4) no downstream cross-tradition comparison may use these counts until the per-thinker miss
      rate is known, because differential web-maintenance habits between traditions would bias any such
      comparison in an unknown direction.
    How to test: PRESUMPTION-947's n=3, and it settles this without any literature at all. For each
      thinker, identify three appearances from the last 12 months known from third-party sources
      (Thomistic Institute back catalogue, PEP Lab press coverage, conference programmes, podcast
      directories), then check each against the thinker's own maintained pages. The hit rate IS the
      completeness figure for that thinker. Six checks, one sitting, and it replaces this entire file
      with a number. If all six appear, limb B holds for these two thinkers and the assumption is
      vindicated per-thinker (which is the right scope — completeness is a property of a person's web
      habits, not a general truth). If they do not, the second-channel mitigation is mandatory.
