  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: Every measured population of investigation recommendations I could find reports large
  non-completion, and the structural features that predict completion are precisely the ones C2A2's
  four newly named measurements lack. In a French hospital RCA programme only 26% of corrective actions
  had an implementation deadline at all. In the Hong Kong audit, 82% of 760 recommendations were rated
  weak, with "additional study/review" the second commonest genre — the genre of a named-but-unowned
  measurement. And the OIG found that even federally mandated, formally investigated events produced
  "few policy or practice changes." But the finding that should change C2A2's design is the inversion
  that appears independently in two datasets: completion rates run *inverse* to action strength — 97.6%
  for weak actions versus 73.3% for strong ones in the RCA² analysis, and 78% for low-impact versus 38%
  for high-impact across 733 CSB recommendations. So the presumption is not simply false. It is true
  for the class of recommendation that consists of writing something down, and false for the class that
  requires someone to change what they do. A register that measures its own health by uptake rate will
  therefore report improving health as its recommendations get weaker. That is a worse failure than
  plain non-completion, because it is self-reinforcing and looks like success. Note finally the
  comparison the intake asked me to make: I looked for evidence that unassigned recommendations are
  taken up at rates comparable to owned ones and found the opposite everywhere — the CSB corpus is the
  strongest available benchmark for *maximally* owned recommendations (named recipient, public tracker,
  federal follow-up) and its high-impact implementation rate is 38%.

  Specific risks: (a) The four measurements named this run have no owner, no threshold and no date;
  against a base rate where 74% of dated-programme actions lack a deadline and high-impact
  recommendations reach 38% completion under far better conditions, the realistic expectation is that
  none of them will be executed, and nothing in the estate is positioned to notice. (b) The register's
  output becomes its own largest unconsumed corpus — 14b's phrase, and the CSB/RCA data say the phrase
  is literally right. 2,111 bare-`[QUEUED]` tags with a 65-day-old oldest item is already that state.
  (c) The strength inversion predicts drift: if anything in the estate ever rewards uptake rate, the
  system will preferentially mint weak recommendations, and the self-awareness layer will get better at
  reporting compliance while getting worse at changing anything. (d) Comfort production: per Power and
  the OIG finding together, an unactioned recommendation is not neutral — it discharges the felt
  obligation. The run that filed the High flag and named four measurements has, on this reading,
  *reduced* the pressure to measure anything, by making it feel handled. (e) The recursion is real and
  already instantiated: this presumption's own mitigation is a recommendation, and unless it acquires an
  owner it falls into the population it describes. The intake paragraph says so about itself.

  Mitigations available: The literature converges on the same three structural features, and they are
  cheap. (i) **Owner** — a named agent or the human, per item, not a role. (ii) **Date** — the single
  most-missing feature in the measured populations (74% absent) and the one that makes non-completion
  detectable rather than merely true. (iii) **Tracked closure** — a state that something other than the
  author can read and that ages visibly; the digital-RCA comparison reported completion rising from
  57.6% to 88.9% when a tracked platform replaced an untracked one (search-summary figure, unverified,
  small n — indicative only). Beyond those three: (iv) classify each recommendation by strength on
  entry (weak = write/train/study; strong = changes a default or a gate) and report completion
  *stratified by strength*, so the inversion cannot hide behind an aggregate; (v) cap intake — a
  register that mints faster than it closes is a queue with no service rate, and WIP limits are the
  standard answer; (vi) age-out or explicit re-declaration, so that an item at its fifth unruled cycle
  (REVISE-436) becomes an event rather than a line.

STEELMAN:
  Item: PRESUMPTION-935
  Strongest counterargument: The presumption survives contact with reality only by being true of the
  wrong things. Every measured corpus of investigation recommendations tells the same story: three
  quarters of corrective actions in a structured hospital programme carried no implementation deadline;
  82% of 760 recommendations in a national RCA audit were rated weak, with "additional study/review"
  among the commonest forms; and even federally mandated, formally investigated adverse events produced
  "few policy or practice changes." But the decisive evidence is the inversion, which shows up
  independently in a 148-action hospital analysis (97.6% completion for weak actions, 73.3% for strong)
  and in 733 CSB recommendations (78% implementation for low-impact, 38% for high-impact). Completion
  is inversely related to consequence. Naming really is most of doing it — when the thing named is the
  writing of a document, the running of a training, or the conducting of a further study. The moment a
  recommendation requires someone to stop doing what they were going to do, the rate halves, and it
  halves in a system with a named recipient, a public tracker, a statutory follow-up function and a
  published 80% target. C2A2's four measurements have no owner, no threshold and no date, and are being
  entered into a register that already holds 2,111 bare-`[QUEUED]` tags with a 65-day-old head. The
  base-rate argument is not that they are unlikely to be done; it is that under conditions far better
  than these, the strong ones are done less than four times in ten. And there is a second, worse limb:
  the unactioned recommendation is not inert. Audit produces reassurance rather than critique, and the
  act of naming a measurement discharges the sense that the measurement is owed. The run that filed a
  High flag about unmeasured mechanisms and then named four unowned measurements may have made the
  measurements *less* likely to happen, by converting an open worry into a closed-looking line.
  What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The estate would have to differ from every measured
  population in one of two ways. Either its recommendations are executed by the same agent that mints
  them within the same cycle — collapsing the owner and the actor, so there is no handoff to fail — or
  it has a closure-tracking mechanism whose reader is not the author. It currently has neither: the
  minting agent does not execute, and the closure state is a tag in the file the minting agent writes.
  A weaker sufficient condition: the recommendations are all weak in the RCA² sense (write a note, run
  a study), in which case the 97.6% figure applies and the presumption holds — but then the register is
  producing only actions that change nothing, which is the failure mode rather than the reassurance.
  How to test: Retrospective, exact, and available today — no new instrumentation required. Take every
  recommendation minted by the self-awareness layer in the last 60 days. For each, record four fields:
  had an owner (y/n), had a date (y/n), strength class (weak = produces a document or a study; strong =
  changes a default, a gate, or a schedule), and current state (done / partially done / not done /
  unruled). Then compute completion stratified by strength and by owner-presence. Three numbers fall
  out. The overall completion rate is the presumption's truth value for this estate. The weak-vs-strong
  gap tests whether the inversion reproduces here — if it does, any future uptake metric must be
  reported stratified or it will be actively misleading. And the owned-vs-unowned gap answers the
  intake's exact question about whether unassigned recommendations are taken up at comparable rates.
  The four sewing recommendations NOT DONE since 08-09/08-16/08-23 and REVISE-436 at a fifth unruled
  cycle are already five data points; the sample only needs to be completed, not gathered. If the
  overall rate lands anywhere near the 26%-had-a-deadline or 38%-high-impact figures from the
  literature, the register's job manifestly does not end when the recommendation is well specified.
