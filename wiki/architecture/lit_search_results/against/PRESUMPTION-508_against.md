  Strength of challenge: Weak-to-Moderate
  Summary: I searched for a professional convention that permits continuing to use an instrument after flagging a defect in it, and the metrology literature does not supply one in the form the pipeline used. ISO/IEC 17025 requires retrospective assessment of the impact of out-of-tolerance equipment on previously reported results, including notification where validity is in question — so the item's diagnosis is correct and the missing artifact is a retrospective impact analysis. Two challenges qualify it. First, the standard does not require quarantine or automatic invalidation; it requires containment, an impact judgement, a cause fix and a verification, and results may stand where the impact analysis shows they are unaffected. Continuing to use a flagged instrument is permitted under an assessment, and the item does not distinguish "continued without quarantine" from "continued without assessment" — only the second is a violation. Second, the item's risk statement says the six premises and every prior disposition are of "unknown quality," and this overstates. The defect the pipeline flagged has been measured: correlated-judge panels lose about three quarters of nominal independence and 8–22 points of accuracy. That is a large, specific, bounded degradation, and the correct response is a computable discount plus reweighting, not an admission of ignorance.
  Specific risks: If no retrospective assessment is performed, the 14 dispositions and the six validated premises carry an unpriced degradation, and the pipeline's own record of the defect makes the omission auditable against it later — the worst configuration, since the flag establishes that it was known. Symmetric risk: if "unknown quality" is adopted as the verdict, 14 dispositions and six premises are treated as worthless when the literature says they are degraded by a measurable amount, and the pipeline discards more than the defect warrants.
  Mitigations available: Perform the retrospective impact analysis rather than a quarantine — the ISO 17025 sequence is contain, assess impact on prior results, fix cause, verify. Attach a stated confidence discount to the affected dispositions using the panel literature's 8–22 point figure as the anchor, so the degradation is priced rather than either ignored or treated as total. Add the status value the item proposes ("produced under a self-flagged instrument") — that is a sound and cheap recommendation regardless. Run the 15a/15b source-citation overlap measurement to replace the borrowed literature figure with a house number.
