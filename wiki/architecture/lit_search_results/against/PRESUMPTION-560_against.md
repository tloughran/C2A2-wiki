  Strength of challenge: Moderate

  Summary: The presumption's strongest move - that abstention is itself an output of the suspect system - is correct and Friedman's account of suspension as a committed attitude supports it. But its inference from that to "abstention does not escape the circularity, therefore the refusal is defective" does not hold. Selective-prediction theory shows abstention has an asymmetric and characterisable error profile: it is a weaker commitment that reduces risk on the covered region rather than an equivalent assertion in disguise. Tarski's result and the layered-assurance literature both indicate that a system genuinely cannot settle claims about its own reliability from inside, so declining is conformant behaviour and the "permanently unadoptable by construction" property is a consequence of self-reference, not a fault introduced by 15c. What survives is narrower and still serious: suspension is only defensible if it keeps the question OPEN with a specified escalation route, and if no such route exists the abstention silently becomes a permanent verdict of non-adoption.

  Specific risks: If the presumption is over-read, C2A2 forces self-endorsement of a premise about its own reliability - the strongest form of the circularity, and worse than abstention. If under-read, MONITOR-486 and PRESUMPTION-556 become a permanent parking orbit: no rule exists that would ever move them, and the register's most consequential item is quietly excluded from the corpus while appearing to be "under monitor."

  Mitigations available: Write the escalation rule explicitly - name the external arbiter (human review, or a differently-trained model with no access to the corpus), the trigger, and the deadline after which continued suspension is itself reported as an unresolved HIGH-risk exposure. Adopt the selective-classification convention of reporting COVERAGE: state what fraction of high-risk items the pipeline abstained on, so abstention has a visible cost. Distinguish in the register between "suspended pending arbiter" and "declined," and put an aging clock on the former.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-560
    Strongest counterargument: Abstention is not the mirror image of endorsement. Chow's reject option and the risk-coverage framework establish it as a principled, cost-optimal choice with a different error profile - a weaker commitment that lowers risk on what it does cover - so the claim that 15c's refusal is "equally an output of the same model" and therefore equally suspect conflates a verdict with a declination. More decisively, Tarski's undefinability result implies that no sufficiently expressive system can settle claims about its own reliability from inside; if so, declining is the correct behaviour and the item's unadoptability is a theorem about self-reference, not a bug 15c introduced. Governance practice agrees, requiring exactly this escalation to a body outside the function's reporting line. Demanding that the pipeline adopt a premise about its own reliability would enact the circularity in its strongest form.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: the abstention is registered as inquiry-continuing (Friedman) rather than terminal - i.e. an external arbiter, trigger and deadline are named - and abstention rate on HIGH-risk self-referential items is reported as a visible coverage cost rather than absorbed silently.
    How to test: the in-house design question is decisive. Search the charters and registers for any written rule whose satisfaction would move MONITOR-486 out of MONITOR. If no such rule exists, the presumption is confirmed on its narrow form (abstention has become permanent non-adoption); if one exists, the abstention is a legitimate suspension and the presumption is refuted.
