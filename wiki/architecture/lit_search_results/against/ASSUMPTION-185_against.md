  Sources:
    1. Devezer, B. et al., 2021. "The case for formal methodology in scientific reform." Royal Society Open Science 8 — argues that contamination-mode frames developed for one research context do not automatically transfer; the relative weights of modes depend heavily on domain (e.g., quantitative psych vs prediction markets vs humanistic synthesis).
    2. Shrout, P. & Rodgers, J., 2018. "Psychology, science, and knowledge construction: Broadening perspectives from the replication crisis." Annual Review of Psychology — emphasizes domain-specificity of methodological remedies; humanistic / cross-tradition synthesis (the C2A2 case) is not the kind of work the open-science frames were primarily built for.
    3. Lakens, D., 2019 (preregistration value paper) — notes that pre-registration frames overdesigned for one domain can underperform or mislead in others; portability is not automatic.

  Strength of challenge: Weak-to-Moderate (the frame transfers in form; the weights may not transfer)

  Specific risks:
    - C2A2's bridge-claim work is interpretive synthesis, not quantitative prediction; "scoring-grain 0/0.5/1" may be inappropriate for claims like "Wright×Rohr bridge is substantive" where graded interpretation is needed.
    - "Author" contamination in cross-tradition synthesis has different structure than in social-science prediction work (here the "authors" are the original thinkers, not just the agents synthesizing).
    - Four-mode-portability assumes the four modes are exhaustive in C2A2's domain; cross-tradition work may have additional modes (e.g., tradition-coherence contamination, ecumenical-pressure contamination) the Pulte frame doesn't address.

  Mitigations available:
    - Adopt the four-mode frame but explicitly test for additional C2A2-specific modes during installation.
    - For interpretive bridge claims, supplement 0/0.5/1 with a qualitative "interpretive-confidence" tier.
    - Conduct one pilot pre-registration with the four-mode frame and audit which modes actually fired; revise weights based on observation.
