  Summary: The presumption that the two surfaces are PURELY reinforcing is well challenged. Status-tier and participation-inequality literature show visible earned status produces a predictable harm — lower-tier stigma and discouragement of the unseen majority — directly contradicting "each makes the other more truthful." Graph-presence functions as visible legitimacy, so graph-ABSENCE for the carded-only majority reads as deficiency rather than neutral. This is the visibility/value-smuggling family (couples PRESUMPTION-308 articulation-gated visibility and PRESUMPTION-314 curator-produced quality bar): a value-laden status hierarchy presented as a mutual good.

  Specific risks: The carded-only majority (the bulk of listed communities) is implicitly stigmatized as "not yet earned"; combined with the no-consent listing (313), communities are both listed without consent AND ranked by a visibility status they did not choose — compounding group-level harm and biasing the very evidence-about-traditions the system aims to produce (visibility, not merit, drives attention).

  Mitigations available: Drop or qualify the "earned/more truthful" framing (keep the validated complementarity, PREMISE-051); design against status reading — e.g., present graph membership as a data-coverage state, not a merit badge; measure whether carded-only communities are treated as lesser (engagement/representation asymmetry); route the visibility-value choice to Tom (shared remedy with 308/314).
