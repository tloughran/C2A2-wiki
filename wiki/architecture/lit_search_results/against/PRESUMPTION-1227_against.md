    Item: PRESUMPTION-1227
    The strongest form of the objection is MacIntyre's: every rational enquirer works within a tradition, so a master science of engagement is a tradition among traditions and its claim to adjudicate must be earned in its own terms. What would need to be true: a vantage from which rival traditions can be compared that is not itself a tradition. How to test: ask whether the master science's criteria can be stated without drawing on any one tradition's concepts.
