  Sources:
    1. Choi, Hecht & Tayler, 2013. "Strategy Selection, Surrogation, and Strategic Performance Measurement Systems." Journal of Accounting Research. — Experimental evidence that people treat the displayed measure AS the strategy, optimizing the metric even when metric-maximizing choices work against the underlying objective; surrogation occurs from mere measurement salience, incentives not required.
    2. Bentley, 2019 (working/pub. version). "Lost in Translation: The Effects of Incentive Compensation on Strategy Surrogation." — Single-measure prominence drives operational distortion; multiple-measure displays attenuate it — directly indicting a one-axis (commit-yield) live display.
    3. Campbell, 1979. "Assessing the Impact of Planned Social Change." Evaluation and Program Planning. — Campbell's law: the more a quantitative indicator is used for decision-making, the more it corrupts and distorts the process it monitors; mission-driven systems are the named context.
    4. Forsgren et al., 2021. "The SPACE of Developer Productivity." ACM Queue. — Activity/throughput is explicitly one dimension among five; systems instrumenting activity first "because it's measurable" are the framework's central cautionary case.
  Strength of challenge: Strong
  Summary: The surrogation literature challenges the presumption at its weakest joint: the display itself, not any incentive, is the mechanism. Choi/Hecht/Tayler show that making one measure salient causes decision-makers to substitute it for the strategy; a live metabolism dial showing tokens-in/commits-out makes artifact throughput the de facto constitutional aim for whoever watches it — human attention allocation, session prioritization, even what "counts" as a session worth running. "Interim" does not neutralize this: proxy-first instrumentation creates path dependence (dashboards persist, get cited in records, calibrate intuitions) such that PRS/peace dimensions arrive later into a decision culture already shaped around throughput. C2A2's constitutional aims (progress AND peace, per ASSUMPTION-309) are precisely the slow, hard-to-measure dimensions that Campbell's law predicts will be displaced by the fast, displayed one.
  Specific risks: Selection pressure toward sessions that emit commits over sessions that read, deliberate, or steelman (peace-work produces little exhaust); the wiki fills with commit-shaped artifacts; when PRS/peace metrics arrive they are judged against entrenched throughput intuitions ("yield dropped") rather than the reverse; combined with 307's gameable denominator, the instrument can show improving metabolism while constitutional progress stalls.
  Mitigations available: Display the proxy with an explicit placeholder slot for the missing dimensions (visible incompleteness, per Bentley's multi-measure attenuation finding); add a counterweight measure now even if crude (e.g., dyad/steelman events count); sunset clause: the commit-yield axis is removed or demoted on PRS integration, written into the instrument's spec; never cite the metabolism display in scheduling or evaluation records during the interim.
  STEELMAN:
    Strongest counterargument: With no metric at all, resource burn (tokens) is invisible and unaccountable, which is its own constitutional failure; a crude exhaust gauge is scaffolding, watched by one reflective human who authored the constitution and is maximally aware of its proxy status. Surrogation findings come from managers optimizing under organizational pressure, not from a single principal monitoring his own system, and measurable scaffolding is how the better metric gets bootstrapped.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The display's audience remains the aware principal only; it never enters allocation decisions or agent-visible feedback; the PRS/peace integration actually ships on a stated timeline; the proxy is visually framed as partial.
    How to test: Audit two weeks of scheduling/queueing decisions for any reference to the metabolism display; track session-type mix (artifact-producing vs deliberative) before/after the display's introduction — a shift toward exhaust-producing sessions is the surrogation signature.
