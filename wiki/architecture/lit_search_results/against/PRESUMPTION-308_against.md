  Strength of challenge: Moderate-Strong

  Summary: The presumption is well-founded and the challenge to the design is real: a visibility gate keyed on self-articulation is not value-neutral. Participation-inequality research shows articulation is concentrated among the resourced few, and critical-data scholarship shows visibility decisions encode power; together they imply the Q2 gate will reproduce inequality while presenting as a technical quality step. The "measurement surface" framing aggravates this (couples ASSUMPTION-276 / Goodhart): the graph will measure who could articulate, then be read as the community landscape. The honest move is to surface the normative choice for explicit (Tom's) decision rather than let it ride inside the pipeline.

  Specific risks: Emerging/under-resourced communities are structurally invisible exactly when visibility would help them; C2A2's graph is mistaken for a representative map of the community space while it is a map of articulateness; the value choice is never owned because it is buried in a "technical" gate.

  Mitigations available: Name the normative choice explicitly and route it to Tom (this is a values decision, not a defect to silently fix); separate quality (proxy-neutral conformity) from visibility (a deliberate inclusion policy); consider equity-aware admission (e.g., assisted articulation, seed-record visibility with a provisional marker) so the gate does not equal silence.

    Strongest counterargument: "Membership earned by self-articulation" sounds meritocratic but encodes a contestable value: that the ability to articulate equals worthiness of visibility. Decades of participation-inequality data show articulation tracks resources, time, and literacy more than intrinsic relevance. So the gate does not measure community quality; it measures community privilege, then makes that privilege the price of being seen. Embedding this in a "technical" pipeline launders a political choice as an engineering default — and the people it filters out are exactly those a community map should help surface.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The articulation criterion is genuinely uncorrelated with community resources (implausible), OR the normative choice is made explicitly, owned by a human, and paired with an equity mechanism so non-articulate communities are not simply erased.
    How to test: Measure correlation between admission and community resource proxies; check whether excluded seed records skew toward under-resourced/emerging groups; confirm a human has explicitly signed off on the visibility policy.
