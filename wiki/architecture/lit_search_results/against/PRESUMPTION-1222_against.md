  Summary: The qualitative-methods literature does not treat participant endorsement as the criterion of fidelity; it is contested as a validity test, and participants may endorse accounts they cannot assess or that flatter them.
  Specific risks: A flattering or superficially plausible portrayal could be certified because the thinker approves it, while mis-representing the thinker's actual argument; and a thinker who is dead, unavailable, or revising cannot certify.
  Mitigations available: Pair endorsement with an independent check against the thinker's published texts and a disagreement-handling rule.
