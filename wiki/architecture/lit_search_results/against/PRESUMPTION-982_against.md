  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-982
    Strongest counterargument: The differential-testing result does not transfer, and leaning on it is
      the category error this file accuses the item of making. Yang et al.'s oracle rests on a
      precondition they state explicitly and engineered an entire tool to guarantee — "deterministic
      implementations of **the same specification**" on a "**valid input**" — and C2A2 has neither half.
      There is no specification of what `connected` means; there is no written rule for what counts as a
      queued item; the runs are not deterministic implementations of anything, they are language-model
      agents improvising a counting method at execution time. Csmith's own authors concede that where
      the standard leaves a choice they can only compare **within an equivalence class**, and C2A2's
      three `connected` figures are almost certainly three different equivalence classes wearing the
      same label. Worse, the compiler case has a property C2A2 conspicuously lacks and the paper names
      it: *unrelated* implementations, built by different teams from a published standard, which is why
      no correlated wrong answers appeared. C2A2's "independent" runs share a model family, a codebase,
      a corpus and a prompt lineage — PREMISE-111 puts them at roughly two effective votes out of nine —
      so voting among them is not a defect oracle, it is the same instrument read three times. PURSUIT
      cuts the same way once you look at what the adjudicator *was*: a standing committee with a charter,
      a systematic review procedure over primary clinical, enzyme and ECG data, and — decisively — an
      **external** referent in mortality. C2A2 has no such committee and no such referent, and
      PREMISE-114 says in terms that absent a documented chain to an external reference, "no arbitration
      rule can name a winner." To insist on adjudication anyway is to pick a winner on authority rather
      than on evidence, which is worse than admitting indefiniteness because it launders a guess into a
      finding. And the item's practical instinct is vindicated by the estate's own history: the `connected`
      figures were produced by resolvers whose difference is now understood, which means the "wrong"
      number was never a bug at all — it was a different, coherent, defensible reading, and treating it
      as a defect would have sent someone hunting for an implementation error that does not exist.

      specification — and this is **false for 15d** (a timestamped filesystem record), **false for
      `connected`** (PREMISE-072 specifies path-aware resolution and declares basename-only invalid),
      and **true only for queue depth**, where no written rule exists; so the steelman holds on one of
      three instances, not three; (b) the runs must be non-independent enough that voting is worthless —
      granted, and PREMISE-111 supports it, but this argues against *voting*, not against *adjudication
      by re-derivation from a written definition*, which is PREMISE-114's exit and needs no
      independence at all; (c) there must be no external referent available — but the vault's filesystem
      **is** an external referent for every one of these quantities, deterministic over a frozen
