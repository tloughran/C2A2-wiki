    Strongest counterargument: The conclusion is likely correct and the citation supporting it is one of the most publicly discredited findings in social psychology. Decision fatigue is an application of ego depletion, and the 2016 multilab preregistered replication — 23 laboratories, over 2,000 participants — failed to find the effect, with subsequent commentary describing the evidence as neither clearly supporting nor debunking it and the construct as a central casualty of the replication crisis. Building the case for reviewer capacity limits on that foundation means the case can be dismissed in one sentence by anyone who knows the literature, and the conclusion would go down with the mechanism. This matters because the conclusion deserves better support and can have it: the operational literature on review queues measures exactly this phenomenon in the field, with override rates for safety alerts ranging from 49% to 96%, and with alert acceptance falling as workload rises, as complexity rises, and as the number of low-informational-value alerts rises. That is a directly analogous human review channel, measured rather than inferred, and it supports the item's claim with no reliance on a depletion construct. It also carries a refinement the item misses: acceptance degrades specifically with low-information items, which means the fix is triage by expected value, not merely fewer items. Cutting volume uniformly would discard high-value items at the same rate as low-value ones and would not restore acceptance.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: That the argument is re-grounded on override-rate evidence rather than depletion, and that any volume reduction is value-weighted rather than uniform.
    How to test: Run the one-query artifact the item proposes — every open item awaiting Tom, each with a time estimate, summed and reported in hours. Then compute the house non-action rate over the last 30 days (routed versus acted on) and compare it against the 49–96% override band. If the house rate sits inside that band, the phenomenon is present and quantified without any appeal to decision fatigue.
