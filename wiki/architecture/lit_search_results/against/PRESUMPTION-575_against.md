  Sources:
    1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clause 6.1.3(d) - the Statement of Applicability. — The single most direct challenge to "the denominator is self-set, therefore the metric is suspect". The world's principal information-security management standard requires the organisation to produce a document that records, for every Annex A control, whether it is included or excluded and why. Exclusion is not a defect to be minimised: an organisation that does not develop software may legitimately exclude the secure-coding controls provided the justification is recorded. The certification audit does not check that the denominator is maximal; it checks that inclusions and exclusions correspond to the risk assessment and risk treatment plan. A statement of the form "n controls: x applicable, y excluded with reason" is therefore the standard's own required output format, not an evasion of it.
    2. NIST Special Publication 800-53B (2020), Control Baselines for Information Systems and Organizations (nvlpubs.nist.gov; and NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5). — Tailoring is a first-class operation. The governing constraint is that every control from the selected baseline is accounted for: controls tailored out require a recorded risk-based rationale in the system security and privacy plan, approved by responsible officials. This challenges the presumption's framing precisely — the standard's answer to "0 uncovered" is not "prove the denominator was maximal" but "show the disposition of every item and who signed for the exclusions". Accounting-for, not covering, is the audited property.
    3. IAASB, International Standard on Auditing 200, Overall Objectives of the Independent Auditor, and ISA 320, Materiality in Planning and Performing an Audit (IFAC handbook). — Assurance in the most mature audit discipline available is explicitly bounded, not absolute: the auditor obtains reasonable, not absolute, assurance, and the extent of work is scoped by materiality and by an assessment of inherent, control and detection risk. The idea that a coverage figure is only meaningful against an absolute denominator has no counterpart in professional auditing, where scoping the population down is the mandated method rather than a compromise of it. Critiques of self-scoped denominators must therefore explain why C2A2 should hold a standard that financial audit explicitly rejects as unachievable.
    4. Spring, J. M., Hatleback, E., Householder, A., Manion, A. & Shick, D. 2019/2021. "Prioritizing Vulnerability Response: A Stakeholder-Specific Vulnerability Categorization (SSVC)." Carnegie Mellon University Software Engineering Institute (SEI report; presented at WEIS 2020; v2.0 April 2021, v2.1.0 September 2023). — Directly challenges the presumption's implicit claim that classifying an item into "deferred" is not work. SSVC's entire deliverable is a decision tree whose terminal outputs are categories including defer, scheduled, out-of-cycle and immediate. Reaching "defer" requires traversing the tree — assessing exploitation status, exposure, utility, and mission and well-being impact. The output "defer" is the product of the analysis, not a substitute for it. In a discipline where remediating every finding is infeasible by construction, triage into deferred buckets is the substantive contribution.
    5. ISO/IEC 27005, Information Security Risk Management - risk treatment options. — Risk retention (taking no action where residual risk meets pre-defined acceptance criteria) sits alongside modification, avoidance and sharing as one of four recognised treatments. "No action taken" is a treatment decision with a documented basis, not an absence of treatment. A roster line reading "4 excluded, 7 deferred" therefore maps onto two legitimate treatment states and does not, on its face, indicate that work was avoided by reclassification.

  Strength of challenge: Moderate

  Summary: The presumption's second clause — that today's finding was that the roster reader could not read the page — is a specific, local, and undefeated objection, and nothing located addresses it. But the general argument it is wrapped in is substantially challenged. Self-scoped denominators with documented exclusions are not a workaround in governance practice; they are the required output format of ISO/IEC 27001 (Statement of Applicability) and NIST SP 800-53B (tailoring with recorded rationale), and bounded scope is the explicit design of professional auditing under ISA 200 and ISA 320, where absolute assurance is stated to be unachievable. The claim that classification substitutes for work is challenged by SSVC, where "defer" is a decision-tree output reached by analysis, and by ISO/IEC 27005, where retention is one of four named treatments rather than the absence of one. What survives is a narrower and sharper objection: every one of these frameworks makes the same demand, which is not that the denominator be maximal but that each disposition carry a recorded, checkable rationale and a named approver, and that the classification be tested by someone other than the classifier. That is where the challenge stops and the presumption resumes — and the local fact that the reader could not read the page bears directly on it, because it undermines the evidence that the roster was traversed at all, which is a different and more serious defect than a self-set denominator.

  Specific risks: If the presumption is over-read, C2A2 adopts an absolute-denominator standard that no governance framework in use holds, and the practical effect is either paralysis (no coverage figure can ever be published) or perverse inflation (the denominator is padded with inapplicable items to make the ratio look conservative, which degrades the metric in the opposite direction). It would also discard triage as a legitimate output, which would push the system toward treating every item as requiring the same depth of work — the failure mode risk-based prioritisation exists to prevent. If the presumption is under-read, "0 uncovered" becomes an artefact of the exclusion and deferral buckets absorbing everything inconvenient, with no recorded rationale per item, no approver, and no independent check — and, given the same-day finding about the roster reader, no evidence that the 21 items were enumerated from the source at all. The specific risk is not that 21 is the wrong denominator; it is that a figure derived from an unreadable page is being reported with the confidence of a figure derived from a read one.

  Mitigations available: Adopt the Statement-of-Applicability form rather than the summary line: publish the per-item disposition table with, for each excluded and each deferred item, the stated reason, the date, and the responsible party — this is what turns 4-excluded and 7-deferred from an assertion into an auditable record, and it is exactly the artefact ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST SP 800-53B require. State the provenance of the denominator separately from the coverage figure: where the 21 came from, when, and whether the roster page was successfully read on that occasion. Report deferred items with a review date, per ISO/IEC 27005 risk retention, so deferral is time-bounded rather than permanent. Have a party other than the roster reader re-derive the denominator independently and reconcile; the boundary condition below makes this the highest-value single action. Consider reporting the metric with an explicit reachability caveat until re-derived, since the same-day reading failure is a known defect in the instrument that produced the figure.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-575
    Strongest counterargument (against the presumption): Every serious control framework in existence produces exactly the artefact the presumption objects to. ISO/IEC 27001 mandates a Statement of Applicability listing controls with justified inclusions and exclusions; NIST SP 800-53B mandates tailoring with recorded rationale and official approval; ISA 200 states that audit assurance is reasonable rather than absolute and ISA 320 scopes the work by materiality. A self-scoped, documented denominator is not a weakness of these systems, it is their method — the alternative, an absolute denominator, is explicitly rejected as unachievable in the discipline with the longest track record of trying. And the charge that "0 uncovered" is reachable by classification rather than work misreads what classification is: in SSVC, "defer" is a terminal node of a decision tree reached by assessing exploitation, exposure, utility and impact, and in ISO/IEC 27005, retention is one of four named risk treatments. Triaging 11 of 21 items into excluded or deferred, with reasons, is a defensible day's work under every one of these standards, and a system that refused to publish coverage until its denominator was provably complete would publish nothing at all.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: each of the 4 exclusions and 7 deferrals carries a written, item-specific rationale rather than a bulk classification; the deferrals carry review dates; a named party other than the classifier has approved or at least reviewed the dispositions; the denominator of 21 is independently re-derivable from the source roster by a second reader; and the roster page was in fact read on the occasion that produced the figure. That last condition is the one currently in doubt and it is the load-bearing one - the governance literature that legitimises self-scoping uniformly presupposes that the population was enumerated from the source.
    How to test: have a second reader independently enumerate the roster and produce their own count without sight of the 21. If the counts diverge, the denominator is not self-set but simply wrong, and the presumption is vindicated on stronger grounds than it currently claims. Then audit the 11 excluded-plus-deferred items for per-item rationale, approver and review date; the governance-literature challenge above holds only for items that have all three, so compute what fraction do. Finally, apply the check the self-assessment literature implies: Kuperman, E. F., Tobin, K. & Kraschnewski, J. L. 2014, "Comparing Resident Self-Report to Chart Audits for Quality Improvement Projects: Accurate Reflection or Cherry-Picking?", Journal of Graduate Medical Education 6(4): 675-679, found self-reported guideline compliance of 82% against 68% on external audit of the same population - a persistent positive bias of roughly 14 points. If C2A2's self-reported coverage shows a comparable gap when externally re-derived, the "0 uncovered" figure should be read as an upper bound rather than a result, regardless of how defensible the scoping method is in principle.
