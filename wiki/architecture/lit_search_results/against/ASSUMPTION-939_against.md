  Sources:
    1. Biehl, M., Pollock, F. A., & Kanai, R. (2021). "A Technical Critique of Some Parts of the Free Energy Principle." *Entropy* 23(3), 293 (arXiv:2001.06408). — Verified directly from the arXiv record. Four results, each adverse to a literal reading: (i) "various definitions of the 'Markov blanket' proposed in different works are not equivalent"; (ii) the steps rewriting the equations of motion of systems with Markov blankets "are not generally correct without additional (previously unstated) assumptions"; (iii) "we prove by counterexample that the original free energy lemma, when taken at face value, is wrong"; (iv) the Bayesian-inference interpretation hinges on an equality of variational and ergodic conditional density that is "not sufficiently justified." Point (iv) is the direct hit on the wiki's phrasing: "target morphologies as prior beliefs" is precisely the Bayesian-interpretive step the authors say is not sufficiently justified. Point (iii) is stronger than any deflationary reading — it says the literal claim is not merely metaphorical but, in its original form, false.
    2. Bruineberg, J., Dołęga, K., Dewhurst, J., & Baltieri, M. (2022). "The Emperor's New Markov Blankets." *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*. — Distinguishes the instrumental use of Markov blankets (a device within a modeller's probabilistic model) from the ontological use (a real boundary between an organism and its world), and argues the realist reading "inadvertently relies upon unfounded assumptions," exposing "the dangers of using the former to make claims about the latter." This is exactly the inference the Levin↔Friston bridge makes when it moves from "a cell can be modelled as minimising free energy" to "a cell IS an FEP agent." The challenge is therefore not that Friston prefers a fiction — it is that the literalist step has been argued to be unlicensed by people other than Friston, in the field's flagship commentary journal.
    3. Raja, V., Valluri, D., Baggs, E., Chemero, A., & Anderson, M. L. (2021). "The Markov blanket trick: On the scope of the free energy principle and active inference." *Physics of Life Reviews* 39, 49–72. — Argues that the FEP's apparent universality across scales rests on a permissive coarse-graining move by which Markov blankets can be found almost anywhere, and that this deflates the informational content of finding one. Directly challenges "applying FEP across biological scales," which is the load-bearing move in any Levin↔Friston bridge (cell → tissue → organism → morphogenetic field). Attracted a substantial commentary set (PubMed 35963034; 35930909; 36150310), confirming the scope question is contested rather than settled.
    4. Aguilera, M., et al. (2022). "How particular is the physics of the free energy principle?" *Physics of Life Reviews* [full author list not confirmed this session; commonly cited as Aguilera, Millidge, Tschantz & Buckley]. — Examines whether the "particular physics" the FEP describes actually obtains in concrete physical systems, and finds the conditions restrictive. Challenges the assumption that biological systems generically satisfy the FEP's premises; if the particular partition rarely obtains, "cells as FEP agents" is not a licensed description of arbitrary cells.
    5. Colombo, M., & Wright, C. (2021). "First principles in the life sciences: the free-energy principle, organicism, and mechanism." *Synthese*. — Notes that the FEP "has been called a postulate, an unfalsifiable principle, a natural law, and an imperative," that "its epistemic status is unclear," and sets out to identify limits on it as a first principle in the life sciences. Direct support for the deflationary reading and, more importantly for C2A2, direct evidence that the *category* of the claim (law? postulate? modelling convention?) is itself unsettled in the philosophy-of-biology literature — so a wiki bridge that asserts it in the indicative is asserting more than the field does.
    6. Secondary/contextual (weaker, cited for coverage rather than authority): the broader tautology-and-falsifiability discussion, in which critics hold the FEP "evades falsifiability... and thrives by rebranding tautology as insight," while proponents reply that the FEP "is a piece of mathematical reasoning no more subject to empirical falsification than calculus, but it is used to write down specific generative models which are subject to empirical verification" — a reply that itself concedes the deflationary point, since it locates all empirical content in the specific models rather than in FEP-conformity. Even sympathetic treatments note the FEP "is so simple that it is (almost) tautological." See also Colombo & Palacios' work on non-equilibrium thermodynamics and the FEP in biology [title/venue NOT confirmed this session — listed as a lead only, do not cite].

  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: **I am challenging the literalist reading of the Levin↔Friston bridge — the wiki's phrasing "cells as FEP agents with target morphologies as prior beliefs" — and I am reporting that the peer-reviewed literature challenges it considerably harder than Friston's own hedge does.** ASSUMPTION-939 frames this as a thinker deflating his own framework, which makes it look like a matter of authorial preference between two live readings. It is not. Biehl, Pollock & Kanai prove by counterexample that the original free energy lemma is wrong when taken at face value, show the Markov-blanket definitions in circulation are not equivalent, and conclude that the Bayesian-inference interpretation — the exact step that turns a morphogenetic setpoint into a "prior belief" — is not sufficiently justified. Bruineberg et al. argue in BBS that inferring an ontological boundary from an instrumental modelling device relies on unfounded assumptions. Raja et al. argue that the cross-scale reach on which any Levin↔Friston bridge depends comes from a permissive coarse-graining move that carries little ontological information. Aguilera et al. question whether the FEP's particular physics obtains in concrete systems at all. Colombo & Wright record that the field cannot agree whether the FEP is a law, a postulate, an unfalsifiable principle or an imperative. Note also that the strongest available defence — that the FEP is mathematics, not physics, and that the empirical content lives in the specific generative models — *concedes* the deflationary reading, because on that defence FEP-conformity is by construction not an empirical property of any system. On every route the literature takes, the wiki's indicative phrasing overcommits.

  Specific risks:
    - **Ontological overcommitment in wiki voice.** "Cells as FEP agents with target morphologies as prior beliefs" is written as a fact about cells. On the literature it is at best a modelling stance, at worst (per Biehl et al.) an interpretation resting on a lemma that is false as originally stated.
    - **The bridge may carry no content once deflated.** If FEP-conformity is an explanatory fiction and the empirical content lives in specific generative models, then "Levin's cells are Fristonian" reduces to "one can write a generative model of morphogenesis," which is true of almost any regulatory process and does not constitute a cross-tradition convergence. The bridge risks being a restatement of the detector's own vocabulary — which is precisely ASSUMPTION-937's failure mode appearing in a second place.
    - **Cross-scale transfer unlicensed.** Raja et al. and Aguilera et al. both attack scale-generality specifically. Levin's claims are essentially multi-scale (cell → tissue → anatomical setpoint), so this is the part of the bridge under the most direct fire.
    - **Reading a hedge as the whole story.** ASSUMPTION-939 treats Friston's own instrumentalism as the challenge. Taking the author's hedge as the ceiling of the critique understates the exposure: the formal literature goes further than the author does, and would not be satisfied by rephrasing the wiki entry in the subjunctive.
    - **Unfalsifiability imported into C2A2.** If a bridge is built on a principle whose falsifiability is contested, no wiki observation can ever count against the bridge, and the entry becomes permanently unrefutable — an anti-pattern for a system that tracks falsification conditions.
    - **"Prior beliefs" as a jingle term.** The phrase carries strong intentional connotations in ordinary and cognitive-scientific use, and a much thinner technical meaning in the variational formalism. A reader of the wiki will import the former.
    - **Same-shape failure as ASSUMPTION-933.** Both items turn on the instrumental→ontological slide around Markov blankets. This is one mechanism appearing twice, which is a systemic rather than local defect (see the systemic-risk flag).

  Mitigations available:
    - **Rewrite the bridge in explicitly modelling voice**: "morphogenetic setpoints admit description as priors within a variational generative model" rather than "target morphologies ARE prior beliefs." Cheap, immediate, and preserves whatever the bridge is actually worth.
    - **Attach the formal caveats** — Biehl et al.'s non-equivalent-definitions result and counterexample to the free energy lemma, Bruineberg et al.'s instrumental/ontological distinction — as standing annotations on every wiki entry invoking the FEP, not just this one.
    - **Adopt a wiki-wide rule** that no entry may assert FEP-conformity as a property of a system; entries may assert only that a system has been (or could be) modelled under the FEP, with the model specified.
    - **State the falsification condition**, or mark the bridge SPECULATIVE. Given the contested falsifiability of the FEP, if no observation could count against the bridge, that must be recorded on the entry's face.
    - **Test whether the bridge survives deflation.** Ask explicitly: with FEP-conformity read instrumentally, what non-trivial claim about Levin's results remains? If the answer is none, retire the bridge rather than rephrasing it. If the answer is a specific generative model with parameters, that model is the real bridge and should replace the prose.
    - **Answer PRESUMPTION-757 explicitly.** The wiki treats Friston's self-deflation as binding on a C2A2 construct. It is not obviously binding — but on this item the *external* literature is binding independently, so the C2A2 revision is warranted regardless of how PRESUMPTION-757 is resolved. Record that reasoning so the precedent is set on the right ground.
    - **Cross-reference to ASSUMPTION-933** so the shared instrumental→ontological mechanism is visible in both places.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: ASSUMPTION-939
    Strongest counterargument: The critiques cited are, with one exception, critiques of the FEP's *foundational* claims — its universality, its falsifiability, its status as a first principle. The Levin↔Friston bridge does not need any of that. It needs only the far weaker claim that morphogenetic control admits a useful variational description in which a target anatomy plays the formal role of a prior — and that claim is a modelling claim, is standard practice across computational biology, and survives every deflation on offer. Indeed Friston's own hedge, quoted in ASSUMPTION-939, licenses exactly this: an explanatory fiction that need not be entertained by the object is still an explanatory success if it predicts. Retiring or heavily caveating the bridge because the FEP's grandest claims are contested would be to hold a modest modelling analogy hostage to a metaphysical dispute it never entered. Furthermore, wiki prose that says "cells as FEP agents" is compressed shorthand of the kind every technical index uses; the fix is a style note, not an epistemic crisis.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (a) The bridge must in fact be a modelling claim, i.e. there must exist a specifiable generative model — states, priors, likelihood — under which a Levin morphogenetic result is recovered, and the wiki must point to it. If no such model can be named, the "modest modelling analogy" defence is unavailable and the bridge is doing metaphysical work after all. (b) The wiki's prose must not read in the indicative, because readers and downstream agents will take it literally. (c) Nothing downstream may depend on cells literally having beliefs — this must be checkable, not assumed. (d) The bridge must have a stated falsification condition, or be marked speculative. (e) The Markov-blanket caveats must be attached wherever the FEP appears, since the same slide has now been found twice (933 and 939) in one night's cohort.
    How to test: (1) **The specification test**: require whoever maintains the bridge to write down the generative model — state space, prior, likelihood, and which of Levin's published results it recovers. Pass/fail is unambiguous and the result is immediately informative either way. (2) **Deflation test**: rewrite the bridge entry entirely in instrumental voice and ask whether any downstream wiki claim changes. If several do, the wiki was relying on the literal reading and the exposure is measurable by counting them. (3) **Downstream literalism sweep**: grep for entries attributing beliefs, goals, or inference to cells on the strength of this bridge; the count is the exposure. (4) **Falsification-condition audit**: check whether the bridge entry names any observation that would count against it; absence is itself the finding. (5) **Ask Levin's own corpus**: check whether Levin himself endorses a literal FEP reading; if he does not, the bridge is a C2A2 construction attributed to two thinkers who each decline it, which is a much sharper finding than the one currently logged.
