  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: The literature does not support a general licence to raise confidence on agreement, and is unusually explicit that the licence depends on the *channel* of correlation rather than on the number of runs. Bovens & Hartmann's Bayesian-network results, and Claveau & Grenier's much broader failure map (98% of the independent-reliability parameter space admits reversals), show that under partial reliability the relation between source multiplicity and confirmation is not monotone — there are regions where one source confirms more than two. Knight & Leveson supply the engineering case that matters most here: independence of *process* does not deliver independence of *failure* when a specification is shared, and coincident failures arose even where the underlying logical errors were different. Song (2026) gives a contemporary, same-architecture measurement: independent parallel runs of the same model do buy a real but small precision gain (F1 0.376 → 0.393 at 2-of-3 majority), while inter-run F1 variance is small enough to show the runs are tightly correlated. The net finding is that C2A2's agreement signal is worth something, but far less than "independent corroboration," and the size of the discount is a function of how much of the variance the shared corpus and parser fix — a quantity C2A2 does not currently measure.

  Specific risks: If agreement between two runs on one corpus and one parser is treated as independent corroboration, every downstream confidence figure inherits an understated variance and an inflated Type I rate — the pseudoreplication signature. Concretely: (a) parser-induced artefacts get promoted to findings, because both runs see the same mis-parsed input and agree about it; (b) corpus coverage gaps become invisible, since neither run can disagree about material that is absent from both; (c) confirmed items accumulate a corroboration score that is monotonic in the number of runs, so the cheapest way to raise confidence becomes re-running rather than re-sourcing; (d) the estate's disagreement rate, which is currently the only visible diagnostic, will look reassuringly low for exactly the wrong reason. The three same-day instances suggest the pattern is already load-bearing rather than incidental.

  Mitigations available:
    - Vary the correlated channel, not the run count. Knight & Leveson (1986) implies the intervention is at the specification/corpus level; Song (2026, §6.4) shows parallel independent looks beat sequential ones, and that model *heterogeneity* is the factor that has been found to help in multi-agent settings (Zhang et al. 2025a, as cited therein).
    - Treat corroboration as calibration, not addition: Bovens & Hartmann (2002) distinguishes repeated measurement, multiple testable consequences, and instrument calibration as separate strategies with different yields. C2A2 could route agreement into the *calibration* channel (evidence about the parser's reliability) rather than the confirmation channel.
    - Estimate the shared-channel variance empirically before pricing agreement: a many-analysts design in miniature (Silberzahn et al. 2018) — hold the corpus fixed, vary the parser, and measure how much agreement survives.
    - Report an explicit correlation parameter alongside any agreement-based confidence, per arXiv:2502.03937's argument that the independence assumption must be relaxed and quantified rather than assumed away.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-859
    Strongest counterargument: Two runs sharing a corpus and a parser share the entire input-side variance, so their agreement carries information only about the residual — the stochastic component of the run itself. Knight & Leveson's result is the sharpest form of this: when the *specification* is common, failures coincide far more than independence predicts, and they coincide even when the proximate logical errors differ, so "different reasoning path" is no evidence of independence. Claveau & Grenier show that this is not a corner case to be waved away — across the parameter space of partially reliable sources, reversals of the expected confirmation ordering are near-ubiquitous. The practical consequence is that C2A2's agreement signal is being priced at the independent-corroboration rate while being generated at the repeated-measurement rate, and pseudoreplication theory says the resulting error is one-directional: variance understated, confidence overstated, Type I inflated. Because this is a presumption rather than a stated policy, no one has had to defend the exchange rate, and there is currently nothing in the system that would make the discrepancy visible.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (1) The dominant error mode is run-level stochasticity rather than corpus or parser artefact — i.e. most of the disagreement budget lives downstream of the shared channel. (2) The confidence increase applied on agreement is small and bounded, of the order Song (2026) measures for same-model parallel ensembling, not a step change to "corroborated." (3) Parser and corpus errors are separately detected by a mechanism that does not depend on inter-run agreement, so the shared channel has its own independent audit. (4) The corroboration score is not monotonic in run count, closing the re-run-for-confidence loophole.
    How to test: Empirically testable and cheap. Run a 2x2: hold the corpus fixed and vary the parser; hold the parser fixed and vary the corpus; measure the agreement rate in each cell against the baseline same-corpus/same-parser cell. The drop in agreement when the parser changes is a direct estimate of the parser's contribution to spurious agreement. Second test, following Knight & Leveson's design: seed a known set of parser-induced artefacts into the corpus and measure what fraction of them are agreed upon by both runs — under true independence that fraction should be the product of the single-run rates; any excess is the correlated-error term. Third, an inter-run disagreement rate should be logged and monitored as a first-class metric; if it is near zero the corroboration weight should be set near zero.
