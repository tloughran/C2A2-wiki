  Sources:
    1. Kahneman, D., Sibony, O., Sunstein, C., 2021. "Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment." William Collins. — Occasion noise: the same expert judging the same case at different times reaches different conclusions at "scandalously high" rates; documented across physicians, judges, forensic examiners, and graders. Temporal stability of a single expert is the default-false hypothesis.
    2. Hodgson, R., 2008. "An Examination of Judge Reliability at a Major U.S. Wine Competition." Journal of Wine Economics. — Expert judges re-scored identical wines identically only ~18% of the time (often only for the worst wines): a concrete quantification of within-expert instability under blind retest.
    3. Harik, P. et al., 2009. "An Examination of Rater Drift Within a Generalizability Theory Framework." Journal of Educational Measurement. — Beyond random occasion noise, raters exhibit systematic drift in standards over time in longitudinal programs; measurement designs are expected to detect and correct for it, not presume it away.
    4. Gilhooly, K., 2022. "To differ is human: A reflective commentary on 'Noise'." Applied Cognitive Psychology. — Critical engagement confirming the core occasion-noise findings while noting some intra-individual variation reflects genuine reconsideration — sharpening the relevant distinction (drift vs legitimate change of mind) that the no-retest design cannot draw.
  Strength of challenge: Strong
  Summary: The noise and rater-drift literatures contradict the stability presumption head-on. Within-expert disagreement across occasions is large, ubiquitous, and documented in domains with far tighter criteria than philosophical milestone assent (medicine, sentencing, wine, grading). Two distinct phenomena are both unhandled by an assent-once design: random occasion noise (mood, fatigue, framing, ordering) and systematic standard-drift over months. Worse, without retest the system cannot distinguish three different things a later disagreement would mean — noise, drift, or genuine conceptual development — and the last of these is supposedly what an integrative tradition undergoing active work EXPECTS to happen. Treating assent-now as durable fact therefore both overstates reliability and forfeits the ability to observe the tradition's own evolution, which is arguably C2A2's subject matter.
  Specific risks: PRS accumulates ratifications that the rater would no longer endorse, with no detection mechanism; contradictory certifications coexist silently across dates; external critics can void the corpus by demonstrating ordinary occasion noise; genuine intellectual development is indistinguishable from error in the record.
  Mitigations available: Periodic blind re-presentation of a sample of ratified items (test-retest protocol); date-stamp all assents and treat them as time-indexed, not eternal; when retest disagrees, require an explicit disposition (noise → re-adjudicate; drift → recalibrate; development → versioned supersession with rationale); anchor items held constant across sessions to separate rater change from item change.
  STEELMAN:
    Strongest counterargument: Occasion-noise findings come from perceptual/evaluative snap judgments (wine, essays, sentencing); deliberate, reasoned, documented assent to milestones of one's own long-developed framework is a different cognitive kind — closer to a philosopher's published positions, which are substantially stable over years. Moreover, treating assent as time-indexed-but-presumed-valid is how all scholarship works (papers are not re-ratified monthly); the presumption may be the normal and workable convention rather than a flaw.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: Within-rater retest agreement on ratified milestones is empirically high (this is testable, not assumable); assents are date-stamped so durability is a measured property; a supersession mechanism exists so change is recorded rather than hidden.
    How to test: Direct experiment: re-present 15-20 previously ratified milestones after 4-8 weeks, blind to original ratings, mixed with novel items. Retest agreement above ~90% substantiates the stability presumption for this rater; below that, drift detection becomes a standing requirement.
