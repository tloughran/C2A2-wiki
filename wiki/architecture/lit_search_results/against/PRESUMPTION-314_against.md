Construct validity / criterion contamination (Cook & Campbell; Messick on validity). — A quality measure produced by the same actor who selects the members, with no independent criterion, is construct-contaminated: it measures the curator's choices, not the communities' articulation. Challenges "articulated to a quality bar."
    2. Aspirational-vs-operative goals (organizational theory; Selznick). — Stated telos ("earned membership," "well-articulated seeds earn their place") diverges from the operative procedure (curatorial inclusion without community approval); the doc advances one while instantiating the other. Direct challenge.
    3. Bowker & Star, "Sorting Things Out" (classification & legitimacy). — Calling a curatorial selection a "quality bar the communities met" naturalizes a classification decision and obscures that the standard was applied BY the curator, not MET by the measured. Challenges the legitimacy framing.

  Strength of challenge: Moderate-Strong

  Summary: The presumption is well challenged. The "quality bar" language attributes to the COMMUNITIES a property (they articulated/earned their place) that was actually produced by the CURATOR (selection by fiat, "no community has approved its record"). That is a construct-validity error — the metric is generated by the measurer — and an aspirational-vs-operative gap: the doc's earned-membership telos is contradicted by the operative procedure that instantiates it. This is a validity-smuggling pattern: a value/quality claim presented as an established fact, the same family as PRESUMPTION-308 (articulation-gated visibility) and PRESUMPTION-310 (TF-IDF as relatedness).

  Specific risks: Downstream consumers (and Tom, and readers) take the 156 as quality-vetted/community-endorsed when they are curator-selected and unapproved; the telos ("earn their place") becomes self-certifying — the curator's choice is relabeled as the community's achievement — undermining the very evidence-about-traditions the system is meant to produce.

  Mitigations available: Relabel honestly — "curator-selected seeds (not community-approved)" rather than "articulated to a quality bar"; separate the curator's inclusion criterion from any future community-articulation signal; if "earned membership" is the telos, define an INDEPENDENT, community-side criterion and measure against it; route the value framing to Tom.
