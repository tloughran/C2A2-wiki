  Sources:
    1. Brand et al., 2025. "The Ideological Turing Test: A Behavioral Measure of Open-Mindedness and Perspective-Taking." Cognitive Science. — Operationalized ITT found HIGH mutual understanding across polarized topics: if accurate-representation-of-the-other is already near ceiling among engaged disputants, an endorsed-steelman KPI may have little headroom and weak discriminative power as a "peace" measure.
    2. Catapano, Tormala & Rucker, 2019. "Perspective Taking and Self-Persuasion: Why 'Putting Yourself in Their Shoes' Reduces Openness to Opposing Views." Psychological Science. — Generating the other side's arguments can REDUCE openness; steelman production is not monotonically peace-producing, undermining the KPI's assumed direction.
    3. Voelkel et al., 2024 (megastudy). "Megastudy identifying effective interventions to strengthen Americans' democratic attitudes." Science. — Interventions that improved understanding/warmth often left anti-democratic attitudes and conflict behaviors unchanged: understanding-type measures dissociate from the downstream peace outcomes they are meant to index.
    4. Paluck, Green & Green, 2019. "The contact hypothesis re-evaluated." Behavioural Public Policy. — Meta-analytic effects of contact on intergroup attitudes are weaker and more conditional than classic claims; measures of mutual comprehension are unreliable surrogates for reduced intergroup hostility.
  Strength of challenge: Moderate
  Summary: The literature does not contest that progress and peace are distinct constructs (the distinctness claim survives); it contests the chosen peace operationalization. Endorsed-steelman is a measure of accurate mutual representation, and three findings cut against it: understanding is often already high in live disputes (ceiling), producing steelmen can backfire on openness (wrong sign under some conditions), and understanding-type gains demonstrably dissociate from behavioral peace outcomes (criterion gap). A KPI can be "explicitly not agreement" and still fail to be peace. The MacIntyre-number side was not directly testable in the search, but as a citation/recognition-style count it inherits standard proxy-metric concerns (see PRESUMPTION-339 file).
  Specific risks: C2A2 declares peace progress when disputants merely articulate each other well while hostility/defection persists; the KPI rewards eloquent adversaries; optimization pressure produces performative steelmen endorsed as accurate yet leaving conflict untouched, and the constitution's peace aim is silently redefined as comprehension.
  Mitigations available: Pair endorsed-steelman with a behavioral co-measure (continued voluntary engagement, joint-artifact production, reduced derogation in subsequent exchanges); track the steelman KPI longitudinally against any available conflict indicator to estimate criterion validity; treat endorsed-steelman as necessary-but-not-sufficient in the KPI definition text.
  STEELMAN:
    Strongest counterargument: For an epistemic community (not warring populations), peace plausibly IS sustained mutual intelligibility under disagreement — MacIntyre's own framework defines tradition-constituted rationality that way — and endorsed-steelman is the most direct measurable correlate. The backfire and dissociation findings concern mass-polarization contexts, not small deliberative communities with repeated interaction, where endorsement-by-the-represented-party is a demanding, hard-to-game bar.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The relevant "peace" is intra-deliberative (between traditions in the wiki/dyads), not affective mass depolarization; endorsement is given by genuinely adversarial parties, not collegial insiders; the KPI is read as a floor, not the objective.
    How to test: Where both metrics can be collected on the same dyads, check whether high endorsed-steelman episodes are followed by cooperative vs degraded subsequent interaction.
