  Why this is not yesterday's flag, and why it supersedes its remedy:
    The 2026-09-11 flag (mine) diagnosed a **propagation** defect: premises do not reach the runs that
    need them. This cohort shows something narrower and worse. In all four items the premise **did**
    reach the analysis — I found it in the pre-check, and it is cited throughout — and the item is
    *still* undecided, because what the premise carried was an **obligation to measure** and the
    measurement is what did not happen. Propagation is therefore not the binding constraint on these
    four. **A premise that names a measurement and is read, understood, cited, and not executed is
    functionally identical to a premise that never arrived.** The propagation remedy 1321 points toward
    would deliver more premises to more runs and would not have changed a single one of these four
    outcomes.

    This also explains the pre-answering rate without recourse to either of PRESUMPTION-960's readings.
    The rate climbs because premises accumulate (PREMISE-174: growth is not a health signal) while the
    *evidence base* does not, because no premise's named measurement is ever run. A register whose
    entries generate obligations faster than the estate discharges them will asymptotically pre-answer
    everything, and will do so **regardless of whether any of its entries is true**. That is the
    degenerating case in PRESUMPTION-960, and this flag is its mechanism.
