    1. MacIntyre, 1988, "Whose Justice? Which Rationality?" — The recovery project concerns tradition-constituted *rationality* and rival *standards of justification*, whose incommensurability is about standards appearing unreasonable across traditions. A restatement-accuracy/understanding score does not measure justificatory commensurability, so it may not bear on the thesis at all.
    2. Hume's is/ought; Moore's naturalistic-fallacy tradition. — An empirical result (a positive understanding delta) cannot by itself constitute "evidence against" a normative/philosophical pessimism without a bridging argument; the inferential link is unestablished (level/category mismatch).
    3. Incommensurability scholarship (Kuhn/MacIntyre secondary lit). — "Understanding a paraphrase" is a weaker achievement than "rationally adjudicating between rival standards"; success at the former is compatible with the pessimist's claim about the latter, so it is weak evidence against pessimism.
    4. AI-in-the-loop caveat (links PRESUMPTION-436). — If the "traditions" are enacted by same-family AI agents rather than human tradition-holders, the demonstration's bearing on the *human* recovery project is further attenuated.

  Strength of challenge: Moderate-Strong

  Summary: The bearing claim overreaches. MacIntyre's pessimism (as characterized by rivals) is about commensurating rival standards of justification, not about whether a paraphrase can be understood; a positive understanding metric therefore may not touch the thesis, and inferring "evidence against pessimism" from it crosses an is/ought and construct-level gap. The achievement measured is real but weaker than the philosophical claim it is enlisted to rebut, and the AI-agent instantiation weakens the transfer to the human project.

  Specific risks: The study could be over-interpreted as empirically refuting a philosophical position it does not actually engage, lending false confidence to C2A2's foundational motivation.

  Mitigations available: Reframe as "consonant illustration that designed cross-tradition understanding is attainable," not "evidence against MacIntyrean pessimism"; make the bridging argument explicit and separate measured-understanding from adjudication-of-standards.

  Recommendation: PARTIALLY-CHALLENGED

  STEELMAN:
    Strongest counterargument: MacIntyre himself supplies mechanisms for cross-tradition rational engagement, so a demonstration that understanding rises under listening conditions is at least *consonant* with the achievability half of his project and mildly disconfirms the *strong* relativist pessimism that says traditions are mutually opaque. As modest, illustrative, defeasible evidence it is legitimate.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The claim is scoped to "achievability of understanding," not "commensuration of standards"; the is/ought bridge is stated; human vs AI-agent instantiation is distinguished.
    How to test: Have philosophers blind-rate whether the measured "understanding" instances involve adjudicating rival justificatory standards or merely paraphrase-comprehension; if the latter dominates, the anti-pessimism claim is unsupported.
