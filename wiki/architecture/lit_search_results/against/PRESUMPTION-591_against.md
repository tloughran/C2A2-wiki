  Sources:
    1. Dubois, D. & Prade, H. (2012/2014). "Three-Valued Logics for Incomplete Information and Epistemic Logic" (Springer LNCS 7519, JELIA) and Ciucci, D., Dubois, D. & Lawry, J. (2014), IJAR 55(9). — The sharpest challenge, and it strikes at the form of the proposal rather than its yield. Dubois & Prade argue that "void", the state of ignorance, is *not a truth value homogeneous with true and false* but a meta-evaluation, and that the truth-functionality assumption of three-valued calculi for incomplete information is questionable compared with non-truth-functional Boolean possibility/necessity pairs. If ignorance is a meta-level statement about the evaluator rather than a third value of the thing evaluated, then adding a third state to an intake convention is a category error dressed as a refinement — which is precisely the "rename" horn of 14b's question, reached by an independent route.
    2. Dubois & Prade, on the limited expressive power of three-valued logics in uncertainty management. — Direct challenge to the sufficiency of the proposed convention: three values are documented as too coarse to represent the distinctions that actually drive decisions under incomplete information, which is why the field moved to possibility/necessity pairs, imprecise probability and Dempster-Shafer. A system that adopts a third state and stops there has bought the criticism without buying the machinery that answers it.
    3. Cochran, W.G. (1953). *Sampling Techniques* — worst-case bounding for survey missing data, and its abandonment. — The most direct empirical challenge to the partition fraction being non-trivial. Cochran used the worst-case bounding idea and abandoned it because it routinely gave results too conservative to act on; the modern partial-identification literature repeats the finding that no-assumptions bounds are frequently wide enough to cover zero and so are practically uninformative. If the C2A2 partition fraction is small — and this is the historical base rate in the closest analogous practice — the third state is populated by almost nothing and the amendment is a rename in fact even if not in form.
    4. Rating-scale category literature: Preston, C.C. & Colman, A.M. (2000). "Optimal number of response categories in rating scales: reliability, validity, discriminating power, and respondent preferences." Acta Psychologica 104(1):1-15; and Lozano, L.M., García-Cueto, E. & Muñiz, J. (2008). "Effect of the number of response categories on the reliability and validity of rating scales." Methodology 4(2):73-79. — Challenging on the empirical "sharpens or weakens in practice" clause. Two- and three-point scales perform *poorly* on reliability, validity and discriminating power relative to scales with more categories; the improvement is monotone up to about seven. Transposed: if a two-state discipline is under-discriminating, three states is the literature's *worst* remedy short of doing nothing — it lands in the region measured to be weakest, and stops short of the range where reliability gains appear.
    5. Middle-category / satisficing literature: Krosnick, J.A. (1991). "Response strategies for coping with the cognitive demands of attitude measures in surveys." Applied Cognitive Psychology 5(3):213-236; with the experimental scale-length work in Journal of Well-Being Assessment (2020) on inappropriate category use. — The strongest single challenge on the mechanism 14b worries about. The documented failure of a midpoint category is that it becomes a refuge: selected disproportionately by respondents avoiding the cost of a judgment, which *increases* measurement error rather than capturing genuine neutrality. An intake convention is an instrument administered to a rater under time pressure — the C2A2 agents themselves. The predicted failure mode is that the intermediate state absorbs items that a two-state discipline would have forced to a decision, and the absorbed items are the inconvenient ones.
    6. Graydon & Holloway (2017) assurance-case confidence survey, read against the proposal. — Even the sympathetic literature documents that graded confidence states in assurance cases have repeatedly been adopted without a stated assignment rule and have then failed to discriminate. The proposal currently has a *type* for its settling quantity but no assignment procedure, and the amendment's own validating test measures something else (frequency).

  Strength of challenge: Strong

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-591
    Strongest counterargument: UNCALIBRATED already IS the third state. The register's two-state discipline is not valued/unknown — it is valued/UNCALIBRATED, and UNCALIBRATED was minted precisely to hold items whose cost cannot be entered as a number. Adding a "bounded" state above it therefore does not add a state to a binary; it splits an existing residual category on a criterion (computable non-trivial worst-case bound) whose satisfaction rate is unmeasured and, by Cochran's experience and by the wide-bounds finding in partial identification, is likely low. If the rate is low, the split's whole effect is to relabel a small minority while leaving the residual untouched, at the cost of a third decision every rater must now make at intake — and Krosnick's satisficing result predicts that the new state will not be populated by the items that meet its criterion but by the items whose rater did not want to adjudicate. Worse, the amendment relaxes the single rule (PREMISE-124) that currently blocks favourable self-produced numbers, and it does so on a warrant (boundability) that its own validating test (frequency of dark days) cannot confirm or refute. The convention would thus weaken a working constraint on the strength of an argument the system has not run.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (i) the partition fraction is materially above zero — a stated threshold, decided before measurement, below which the amendment is withdrawn; (ii) the bound admitted is *non-trivial*, i.e. strictly narrower than the logical range, tested per item rather than asserted; (iii) an assignment rule exists that a second rater could apply to the same item and reach the same state, with agreement measured; (iv) items in the new state receive a *different subsequent action* from UNCALIBRATED items, so the state is action-bearing rather than descriptive.
    How to test: Take the current UNCALIBRATED population. For each item, attempt to state (a) an outcome scale and (b) a worst-case bound on that scale. Record three outcomes: bound computed and strictly narrower than the logical range; bound computed but trivial; no scale statable. The partition fraction is the first count over the total. Pre-register the threshold. Then have the assignment repeated blind on a subsample to get an agreement rate. Both are readable from existing registers; neither requires new search.

  Specific risks: If the amendment is adopted and the partition fraction is near zero, C2A2 has relaxed PREMISE-124's prohibition — the one rule stopping favourable self-produced numbers from entering — in exchange for nothing, and has added a third bin whose predicted occupancy (per Krosnick) is items the rater declined to adjudicate. The register's calibration discipline degrades silently: entries that would have been forced to UNCALIBRATED now carry a "bounded" label that readers will treat as more informative than it is. Because the amendment's validating test measures frequency rather than boundability, the failure would not surface through the test that was specified to catch it.

  Mitigations available: (a) Do not adopt the third state until the partition fraction is measured on the existing UNCALIBRATED population — the measurement is in-house, needs no search, and is decisive. (b) Pre-register the threshold before measuring. (c) If adopted, require that entry to the state carry the computed bound *in the entry*, so an empty bound field is a schema violation rather than a judgment. (d) Consider the alternative the logic literature actually recommends — a possibility/necessity or interval pair attached to the existing two states — which adds expressiveness without adding a category for raters to satisfice into.

  Recommendation: CHALLENGED
