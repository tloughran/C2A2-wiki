  Strength of challenge: Strong
  Summary: The challenge is decisive at the THRESHOLD level rather than the mechanism level. Even granting best-case summarisation fidelity, an agent-mediated capture carries a measured error rate — 18-25% by the human quotation studies, worse for LLM attribution specifically — that is simply incompatible with the stakes described: overturning a months-standing classification and launching a downstream evidence channel. Greenberg's citation-distortion mechanism names the systemic risk exactly: an unverified secondary claim, once routed to a master agent, acquires authority through repetition rather than evidence, and every downstream artifact inherits it uncorrected and unmarked. The presumption is not that captures are useful — they are — but that they are SUFFICIENT, and nothing in the literature supports sufficiency at this stake level.
  Specific risks: THE RISK IS NOT HYPOTHETICAL — IT HAS ALREADY OCCURRED IN THIS RUN. 15b's independent search on ASSUMPTION-449 (same cohort) found that Kastrup explicitly affirms a "dissociative hierarchy" with sub-alters and layered internal dissociation — material that directly contradicts the "incompatible mereologies" framing the capture produced, and which Kastrup himself notes is "not to be found in anything I have written" (it appears in an interview, not the written corpus). The agent-mediated capture missed it precisely because it is not in the written corpus. A single primary-source pass caught what the capture missed, on the flagship item, on the first real use of the paradigm-shift channel. This is a live, in-run, measured instance of the exact failure PRESUMPTION-477 describes.
  Mitigations available: Gate the paradigm-shift channel behind a primary-text verification pass. Require every capture that triggers a reclassification to carry VERBATIM quotations with locators (page/section/timestamp). Run a FActScore-style atomic decomposition against the primary text. Sample-audit captures against primary sources and measure the misattribution rate; if it exceeds ~5%, the no-verification routing path is unsafe and must stay gated.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-477
    Strongest counterargument: For the presumption to survive, three conditions must hold. (a) The capture is EXTRACTIVE, not interpretive — the position is given in the thinker's own words with locatable citations, not paraphrased. (b) The decision is REVERSIBLE AND CHEAP — the reclassification is a hypothesis flag, not a committed edit propagated to dependents. (c) The error rate is TOLERABLE RELATIVE TO BLAST RADIUS. Under (b), agent captures are perfectly adequate as LEAD GENERATION, and C2A2 should keep using them that way — the failure is not in generating the lead but in routing it as a finding. The system built a channel for paradigm shifts and then fed it unverified secondary material, which converts its highest-signal pathway into its highest-variance one.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The paradigm-shift channel must be gated on primary-text verification, and captures must be labelled LEAD, not FINDING, until they pass that gate.
    How to test: Sample 10 existing captures that underwrite standing bridge classifications. Verify each against primary text with verbatim quotation and locator. Measure the misattribution rate. Given that the very first such check — ASSUMPTION-449, performed this run — returned a miss, the prior on that rate should be set high, not low.
