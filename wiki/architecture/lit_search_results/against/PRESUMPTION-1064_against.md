STEELMAN:
  Item: PRESUMPTION-1064
  Strongest counterargument: Even though the general disclosure-paradox literature is strong, it
  is drawn from human/organizational conflict-of-interest contexts (financial advisors, doctors,
  corporations), not from automated agent budget-breach logging — the mechanism (moral licensing,
  strategic exaggeration) presumes a motivated human discloser gaming trust, which may not
  transfer cleanly to an agentic system where "disclosure" is a mechanical log entry with no
  self-interested discloser to feel licensed. The presumption may be importing a human-psychology
  mechanism into a context where the analogous psychological step doesn't occur.
  What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: If C2A2's disclosure step has no agent "choice"
  or incentive attached to it (i.e., it's a forced, automatic log write with no discretion to
  exaggerate or a moral-licensing beneficiary), the human-psychology mechanism in the Cain/
  Loewenstein/Moore studies would not directly apply, even though the functional pattern
  (disclosure substituting for resolution) could still occur through a different route (e.g.,
  organizational/process inertia rather than individual psychology).
  How to test: Check whether, across the five-plus disclosed budget breaches, any design-level
  change was ever actually triggered by a disclosure, versus disclosures simply accumulating as
  logged/accepted without downstream design review — a direct empirical test of whether
  disclosure is functioning as discharge in this specific pipeline, independent of which
  psychological mechanism (if any) explains it.
