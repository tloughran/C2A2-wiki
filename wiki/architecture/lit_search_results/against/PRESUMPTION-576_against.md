  Original statement: [inferred] REVISE-251's proposed intake rule — a presumption must name the quantity that would settle it, or else be filed as a prompt to investigate that "does not consume a literature search" — presumes that the settling quantity is available to 14b at intake, i.e. before the search.   Sources:
    1. International Council for Harmonisation. 2019. ICH E9(R1) Addendum on Estimands and Sensitivity Analysis in Clinical Trials, to the Guideline on Statistical Principles for Clinical Trials. ICH Step 4, November 2019 (adopted as EMA/CHMP/ICH/436221/2017). - This is the decisive challenge, and it is a conceptual one: the addendum requires a priori specification of the *estimand* - the quantity the study intends to estimate - defined by five attributes (target population, treatment condition, endpoint/outcome variable, population-level summary measure, and the strategy for handling intercurrent events). None of the five requires knowing the estimand's *value*. The framework exists precisely because trials were being run and analysed without anyone having said what quantity was at issue, and the remedy adopted internationally was a pre-specification gate. PRESUMPTION-576 conflates naming a quantity with holding it: REVISE-251 asks for the former, and the estimand framework demonstrates at scale that the former is routinely satisfiable before any data exist. On this reading the alleged circularity dissolves - what 14b could have written at intake for PRESUMPTION-568 is "a rate with an ISA-450-style denominator, value unknown," which is a nameable estimand.
    2. Wittes, J. & Brittain, E. 1990. "The role of internal pilot studies in increasing the efficiency of clinical trials." Statistics in Medicine 9(1-2): 65-71. - A direct structural counterexample: the design quantity (the residual variance, a nuisance parameter) is only estimable from the very data the design must collect, which is exactly the topology PRESUMPTION-576 calls circular. The discipline's answer was not to abandon pre-specification but to make it provisional and revisable: commit to an initial value, recruit a fraction of the planned sample, re-estimate the nuisance parameter internally, and revise the design - in Wittes and Brittain's "restricted design" the final sample is the maximum of the initial and recalculated values, which preserves the error rate. The literature that grew from this (blinded sample-size re-estimation, adaptive designs) treats "the gate quantity is produced by the step it gates" as a solved engineering problem with known type-I-error properties, not as a defect that voids the gate. The transferable rule for C2A2 is: require a named quantity plus a provisional guess, and permit one documented revision after the search.
    3. Chan, A-W., Hróbjartsson, A., Haahr, M.T., Gøtzsche, P.C. & Altman, D.G. 2004. "Empirical Evidence for Selective Reporting of Outcomes in Randomized Trials: Comparison of Protocols to Published Articles." JAMA 291(20): 2457-2465. - Supplies the empirical cost of NOT gating, which PRESUMPTION-576 does not price. Comparing protocols to publications, Chan et al. found extensive discrepancy between pre-specified and reported outcomes, with statistically significant results markedly more likely to be fully reported - the canonical demonstration of outcome reporting bias. Transferred to C2A2, an intake regime that permits an item to consume a search without naming its settling quantity permits the settling quantity to be chosen after the evidence is in hand, i.e. it licenses picking whichever number the literature happened to supply as though it had been the target all along. PRESUMPTION-576's own examples are vulnerable to exactly this reading: that the ISA 450 denominator and the dependence beta *became* the settling quantities because the search returned them.
    4. Richardson, W.S., Wilson, M.C., Nishikawa, J. & Hayward, R.S.A. 1995. "The well-built clinical question: a key to evidence-based decisions." ACP Journal Club 123(3): A12-A13. - The founding statement of the discipline PRESUMPTION-576 treats as impossible: in evidence-based medicine the search is preceded, not followed, by a structured specification of what would answer the question (the PICO anatomy - population, intervention, comparison, outcome). Three decades of practice and instruction have been built on the claim that question formulation is a distinct, teachable, pre-search skill whose product is the search strategy itself. The outcome element of PICO is a named quantity type with no known value; naming it is what makes the search executable. This is a large body of documented practice in which a pre-search gate on "name the quantity that would settle it" is not merely feasible but is the mechanism by which searches become efficient rather than exploratory.
    5. Claxton, K. & Sculpher, M.J. 2006. "Using value of information analysis to prioritise health research: some lessons from recent UK experience." PharmacoEconomics 24(11): 1055-1068. - Bears on the specific worry that a pre-search gate misallocates search effort. Value-of-information analysis decides whether to commission new evidence *from current priors*, computing the expected value of perfect or sample information as the gap between the value of a decision made on current information and one made with better information. The whole apparatus is designed to answer "is this search worth running?" before the search exists, using an uncertainty characterisation the analyst already holds. It therefore refutes PRESUMPTION-576's implicit premise that nothing useful can be said about a search's yield in advance; and it also names the right gate quantity, which is not the settling value but the decision the value would change.
    6. van de Schoot, R., de Bruin, J., Schram, R. et al. 2021. "An open source machine learning framework for efficient and transparent systematic reviews." Nature Machine Intelligence 3: 125-133. - Bounds the cold-start problem the item invokes. In active-learning screening the cold start is real - the learner needs seed relevance labels before it can prioritise - but the empirically demonstrated penalty is small and bounded: a handful of seeds, or even a random start, is enough to reach recall levels that let screening stop after a small fraction of records. The lesson for C2A2 is that a cold start is a start-up cost measured in a few items, not a barrier that justifies removing the gate; and the ASReview design also shows the right shape for a fix, in which the specification is refined by the first few results rather than demanded complete on the first attempt.

  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: The presumption's load-bearing inference - that a rule requiring a named settling quantity filters on the output of the step it gates - rests on conflating the *specification* of a quantity with its *value*, and the challenge to that conflation is strong and multiply sourced. ICH E9(R1) institutionalises pre-specification of an estimand whose value is by construction unknown; PICO has done the same for pre-search question formulation since 1995; and Claxton and Sculpher's value-of-information machinery decides whether a search is worth running from priors alone. Where the structural problem PRESUMPTION-576 describes is genuine - the design parameter really is a product of the data - Wittes and Brittain's internal-pilot literature shows the standard remedy is a provisional commitment plus a documented revision, not abolition of the gate. Chan et al. then price the omitted side of the ledger: without a pre-specified target, the quantity that settles an item can be selected after the evidence arrives, which is outcome reporting bias with the labels changed, and 14b's own three examples are consistent with that reading. What survives the challenge is a narrower and more useful demand than the presumption makes: REVISE-251 should require a nameable quantity *type* with a provisional bound and an explicit permission to revise once post-search, and its sanction should be revision rather than the withholding of a search. What does not survive is the claim of circularity, and with it the inference that the rule would suppress the highest-value items.

  Specific risks: If PRESUMPTION-576 is over-read, C2A2 loses its only pre-commitment device at intake, and the failure mode is precise and documented (Chan et al.): every item's settling quantity becomes whatever the search returned, no item can be recorded as having failed to settle, and the pipeline's disposition record silently converts into a record of what the literature happened to contain. A second-order effect is that "prompt to investigate" ceases to be a meaningful category, so the intake queue loses its ability to distinguish a well-posed question from an unposed one - the cost is unbounded search effort on items nobody could say were answered. If PRESUMPTION-576 is under-read and REVISE-251 is adopted as a hard gate with no provisional-value path and no revision clause, the risk 14b names is real: items whose settling quantity is genuinely a nuisance parameter (dependence betas, base rates, cost coefficients) are the ones a search would most improve, and a rigid gate turns them away - the false-negative cost falls hardest on exactly the items with the highest expected value of information.

  Mitigations available: Rewrite REVISE-251 in estimand form rather than value form: require the five ICH E9(R1) attributes at intake (population of instances, the comparison or condition, the outcome variable, the summary measure, and how to handle the item's equivalent of intercurrent events), all of which are statable without a number. Add the internal-pilot clause: an intake entry may carry a provisional value with a stated basis, and exactly one post-search revision is permitted and must be recorded as a revision, which preserves the pre-commitment function while removing the circularity. Make the sanction proportionate - an item that cannot state its estimand is downgraded in search priority rather than barred, which is the value-of-information ranking rather than a gate. Instrument the cold start on ASReview's pattern: allow a bounded scoping pass (a handful of results) whose only permitted output is a sharpened estimand, logged separately from the substantive search. Finally, measure the in-house quantity 14b already proposed - for the last N dispositioned items, whether the settling quantity was nameable at filing time - but score *nameability of the type*, not availability of the value, or the measurement will confirm the presumption by construction.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-576
    Strongest counterargument (against the presumption): The presumption equivocates on "quantity." A gate that demands a value you can only get from the search is circular; a gate that demands a *specification* of the quantity whose value the search would supply is the ordinary discipline of every field that has taken pre-registration seriously, and it is the specification that REVISE-251 asks for. ICH E9(R1) requires the estimand a priori precisely because its value is unknown; PICO requires the outcome element before the search because that is what makes the search executable; value-of-information analysis ranks candidate searches before any of them are run. Where the nuisance-parameter topology genuinely bites, Wittes and Brittain showed thirty-five years ago that the answer is a provisional commitment with a licensed revision and a preserved error rate, not the removal of the gate - so the presumption identifies a real structure and draws the one conclusion the methodological literature rejects. And the cost of accepting the presumption is not neutral: Chan et al.'s outcome-reporting-bias findings show that when the target is not fixed in advance, the target migrates to whatever the evidence supported, which for a self-auditing pipeline means it can never record that an item failed to settle.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (i) the intake rule is read as requiring an estimand-style specification, not a value; (ii) a provisional value with a stated basis is permitted, and one recorded post-search revision is allowed; (iii) the sanction for an unspecified item is deprioritisation rather than exclusion, so the false-negative cost is bounded; and (iv) the pipeline retains a bounded scoping pass whose only output is a sharpened specification, logged as such.
    How to test: Take the last N dispositioned items and score each twice, blind to the other score: (a) was the settling *value* available at filing time, and (b) was the settling quantity's *type* - population, outcome variable, summary measure - statable at filing time from the item text alone. PRESUMPTION-576 predicts both are low; the challenge predicts (a) low and (b) high, and the gap between the two is the whole disagreement. A second test discriminates the false-negative worry: for the items where (b) was low, check whether the search that ran actually produced a quantity that settled them, or merely produced literature. A third, cheapest test: for the three examples 14b cites, attempt to write the pre-search estimand retrospectively from the original item text only. If it can be done for all three, the circularity claim fails on its own evidence; if it cannot be done for any, the presumption is vindicated and the gate should be softened to a priority weight.

  Recommendation: CHALLENGED

  Search scope note: Comprehensive on the pre-specification and adaptive-design literatures (estimand framework, internal pilots and blinded sample-size re-estimation, outcome reporting bias, PICO/question formulation, value of information) and on the cold-start problem in screening. Preliminary on two adjacent areas that would likely sharpen the false-negative estimate specifically: the sequential/two-stage screening design literature (Neyman-Pearson framing of screen-positive thresholds under uncertain prior information) and the triage literature on undertriage cost in emergency and disaster settings. A broader search of both is recommended if REVISE-251 is adopted as a hard gate rather than a priority weight.

SYSTEMIC-RISK-FLAG:
  Date: 2026-07-30
  Affected items: PRESUMPTION-576, PRESUMPTION-577, PRESUMPTION-588 (primary); PRESUMPTION-586 (secondary)
  Common vulnerability: All four items reason from the *absence of a measurement or a record* to the *absence of a bounded quantity*, and three of them do so in the same specific way - by treating "no matched comparison exists" or "the value is not in hand" as equivalent to "nothing can be said." PRESUMPTION-576 infers circularity from the settling value being unavailable at intake, when only the value and not the specification was unavailable. PRESUMPTION-588 infers that a missing input's cost "enters the record as zero because nothing measures it" and that UNCALIBRATED is therefore the honest entry, when worst-case bounds, sensitivity analysis and bias-threshold methods are all computable without the matched comparison it asks for. PRESUMPTION-577 asserts a salience effect of consolidation while itself supplying no measurement of it, and the psychological literature shows the effect is not even signed - unpacking can raise or lower judged force depending on component typicality. PRESUMPTION-586 shares the structure in the normative register: the missing artefact is a sign-off record, and its absence is read as an absence of legitimate authority rather than as an unmeasured question. The unifying defect is a default that converts unmeasured to unbounded, which in an auditing layer is not conservative - it systematically inflates the apparent severity of anything the pipeline has not yet instrumented, and it does so in the direction that generates more items.
  Literature basis: ICH E9(R1) 2019 (specification is separable from value); Wittes & Brittain 1990, Statistics in Medicine 9: 65-71 (the design quantity may legitimately be produced by the step it designs); Manski 1990, American Economic Review 80(2): 319-323 (worst-case nonparametric bounds require no matched comparison); Molenberghs, Beunckens, Sotto & Kenward 2008, JRSS-B 70(2): 371-388 (the missingness mechanism is unidentifiable from observed data, so sensitivity analysis, not measurement, is the discipline); VanderWeele & Ding 2017, Annals of Internal Medicine 167(4): 268-274 (bound the unmeasured by how strong it would have to be to change the conclusion); Sloman, Rottenstreich, Wisniewski, Hadjichristidis & Fox 2004, JEP:LMC 30(3): 573-582 (unpacking effects reverse, so aggregation effects on salience are not signed a priori).
  Risk level: High
  Recommendation: Adopt a single standing convention at 14a/14b/15* intake that separates three states which the current record collapses into one: UNSPECIFIED (the quantity type is not yet named), UNMEASURED-BUT-BOUNDED (type named, value unknown, worst-case or logical bounds stated), and UNIDENTIFIABLE (no observation could settle it, so a sensitivity analysis over stated assumptions is the terminal deliverable). Require that any item asserting a cost, a loss, or an effect either state a bound or name the method by which a bound would be obtained; forbid the entry "no consequence recorded" as a description of a quantity that has not been bounded, and forbid its converse - treating an unbounded quantity as high-risk by default. Route the four affected items back to 14b with the instruction to re-file each under one of the three states, and check the resulting distribution: if a large share of open presumptions are UNMEASURED-BUT-BOUNDED once the convention is applied, the pipeline's severity ratings need recalibrating, and that recalibration is itself a measurable and reportable quantity.
