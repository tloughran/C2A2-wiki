  Strength of challenge: Moderate

  Summary: The distinction survives in outline but not in the terms the assumption uses, and the crux
    the item stakes itself on cannot be settled from the literature because the literature has never
    posed the question. On the Hawkins side the vault's characterisation is accurate and if anything
    understated: the 2019 technical framework advances no non-veridicality thesis whatsoever — it is a
    theory of geometry-anchored object models scored against sensory prediction — so Hawkins's
    non-veridicality claims are confined to the popular book and are about secondary qualities in
    roughly the Lockean sense. On the Hoffman side the vault's word "total" is wrong in a way that
    matters. Hoffman's formal claims are structure-relative and asymptotic ("as the number of world
    states and payoff values go to infinity, the probability that a payoff function is a homomorphism
    goes to zero," for four named structures — Prakash, Fields, Hoffman, Prentner & Singh 2020) and
    carry an explicit exception (veridical strategies survive where fitness varies monotonically with
    truth). Bagwell (2023) argues the radical reading is self-defeating outright, which would pull
    Hoffman toward the indirect realism the vault assigns to Hawkins. Meanwhile the co-citation test
    returns exactly zero: no published work engages both frameworks, and Chris Fields is the only
    author I found who works formally on both sides of the vault's divide. So the assumption is right
    that a distinction exists and right that it has not been checked; it is wrong about the shape of the
    Hoffman pole, and its implied remedy — re-read every convergence entry — rests on a characterisation
    that would itself need correcting first.

  Specific risks: The item's architectural warning is sound and unaffected by any of this: a
    retrospective invalidation of an uncounted number of entries, with no agent authorised to count
    them, is an unbounded liability regardless of how the underlying distinction is drawn. The added
    risk from the challenge is that a re-reading pass executed against the stated distinction would
    propagate a second error: entries would be corrected to say "Hoffman's claim is total," which is not
    what Hoffman's formal papers claim, replacing one conflation with one misattribution. There is also
    a genre risk the item does not name — comparing Hawkins's trade book to Hoffman's peer-reviewed
    theorems is not a like-for-like comparison, and any convergence entry built on it is fragile whether
    or not the non-veridicality claims share a referent.

  Mitigations available:
    - Restate the Hoffman pole from the formal papers rather than from summary: structure-relative,
      generic, asymptotic, with a monotonicity exception (Prakash, Fields, Hoffman, Prentner & Singh
      2020; Prakash, Stephens, Hoffman & Singh 2020/2021).
    - Restate the Hawkins pole by source genre, separating what the 2019 Frontiers framework claims
      (nothing about veridicality) from what the 2021 book claims.
    - Use the shared-author bridge. Chris Fields publishes formally with Hoffman and with Levin; his
      2015 ITP commentary is the closest thing to a translation layer between these vocabularies that
      exists, and it is four pages long.
    - Scope the remediation before performing it. The count of affected entries is a mechanical query
      over the vault (entries tagged with both Hawkins and Hoffman) and should be run before any
      re-reading pass is authorised — the item explicitly notes this was never done.
    - Treat the absence of any published joint treatment as a standing caveat on every Hawkins x Hoffman
      entry, rather than as licence to adjudicate the comparison in-house.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: ASSUMPTION-1178
    Strongest counterargument: The core observation is very likely correct and my challenge is to its
      calibration, not its direction. Hawkins is a working neuroscientist proposing that cortical
      columns build location-based models of real objects using grid-cell-like machinery, tested by
      prediction error against sensation; Hoffman is arguing that natural selection almost surely
      prevents perceptual structures from being homomorphic to world structures, and elsewhere that
      spacetime itself is an interface artefact. These are not the same claim, they do not have the same
      referent, and a vault that filed them as convergent was filing a pun. The fact that zero published
      works cite both makes it more likely, not less, that the pairing was the vault's invention rather
      than a recognised connection. Where the assumption overreaches is the single word "total": on
      Hoffman's own formal statements the claim is conditional and structure-relative, and if Bagwell
      (2023) is right that the radical reading is self-defeating, then the tenable Hoffman is closer to
      Hawkins than the assumption allows — in which case some convergence entries may survive re-reading
      rather than all of them failing.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (a) the re-reading pass is scoped by an actual count
      before it is authorised, so the liability is bounded; (b) the corrected characterisation of Hoffman
      is taken from the Entropy and Acta Biotheoretica papers rather than from a one-word summary, so
      the correction does not introduce a fresh error; (c) convergence entries are graded rather than
      binary — "these two authors both deny naive realism, and differ on scope, mechanism and what
      survives the denial" is a defensible entry, whereas "Hawkins and Hoffman converge on
      non-veridicality" is not; (d) the absence of any published joint treatment is recorded on the face
      of each such entry. Under (a)-(d) nothing catastrophic follows from the conflation.
    How to test: Empirically and inside the vault. Step one: query the vault for entries linking Hawkins
      and Hoffman and produce the count the item says was never produced. Step two: for each, extract the
      specific proposition asserted to converge and classify it against three referents — (i) secondary
      qualities such as colour, where both authors deny veridicality and genuinely agree; (ii) spatial
      and geometric structure, where Hawkins asserts a real-geometry anchor and Hoffman's homomorphism
      results deny structural correspondence, so they genuinely conflict; (iii) the fundamentality of
      spacetime, where Hawkins is silent and no convergence claim is possible. The proportion falling in
      (i) is the proportion of entries that survive; the proportion in (ii) and (iii) is the true scope
      of the remediation. Step three, optional and cheap: check whether Fields (2015) supplies a bridge
      that makes any (ii) entries defensible.
