  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: Strong challenge on a load-bearing, designer-unaware presumption. The inference 'no broken bridge links => no synthesis bridges needed' is self-justifying: the only demand the graph can express is a link someone already gestured at, so 'no demand' is guaranteed wherever no one stubbed - regardless of true need. Link-prediction research shows warranted links are largely latent, and the most valuable cross-tradition bridges are precisely the non-obvious ones with no broken link. The signal is therefore systematically blind to under-connection, and treating its silence as completeness is unwarranted.

  Specific risks: The system could conclude its synthesis coverage is complete when it is not, leaving the most valuable (non-obvious) cross-tradition bridges permanently unbuilt because nothing flags them.

  Mitigations available: Enumerate warranted bridges independently of broken links (embedding link-prediction over the PRS connectome; structured cross-tradition pairing); treat broken-link demand as a lower bound on need, never as completeness.

  STEELMAN:
