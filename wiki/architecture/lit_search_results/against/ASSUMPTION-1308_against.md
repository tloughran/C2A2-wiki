       systematic review of trials with both blinded and non-blinded outcome assessors." *BMJ* 344:e1119
       (PMID 22371859). — SECONDARY (abstract content obtained via search summary; **direct retrieval of
       the PubMed record was blocked by a reCAPTCHA challenge which I did not attempt to bypass**, and
       the BMJ full text was not retrieved) — Effect estimates are exaggerated by an average of **36%**
       (pooled ratio of odds ratios ≈ 0.64) when based on non-blinded assessment of subjective binary
       outcomes; in the 12 trials with data on dependence between blinded and non-blinded assessments the
       pooled ROR was **0.76 (95% CI 0.61 to 0.94)**. **This is the decisive number against LIMB B.** The
       bias from non-blind coding is real, directional, measured, and roughly a third — which is a
       serious bias and a reason to blind, and is emphatically not "worthless." A quantity biased by a
       known factor with a known direction remains informative and is partially correctable; a worthless
       quantity is one with unknown sign and unbounded magnitude.
    2. Barriers-to-blinding literature (PMC4659325, "Barriers to blinding: an analysis of the feasibility
       of blinding in test-treatment RCTs"; PMC10826218 on manual-therapy blinding feasibility). —
       SECONDARY — outcome assessors were masked in only 22% of the trials examined and could have been
       masked in 66%; scarcity of blinding attempts reflected practical difficulty rather than
       negligence. Blinding is routinely unachievable, and designs proceed anyway with the limitation
       declared.
    3. O'Connor, C. & Joffe, H. (2020). "Intercoder Reliability in Qualitative Research: Debates and
       Practical Guidelines." *International Journal of Qualitative Methods* 19. — SECONDARY (abstract
       and summary retrieved; full text not retrieved) — ICR is contested in qualitative work; its
       benefits are framed as systematicity, communicability, transparency and team reflexivity rather
       than as validity per se. Campbell et al. (2013)'s "negotiated agreement" raised reliability from
       54% to 96% — a *non-independent* procedure that is nonetheless accepted practice — though the same
       literature warns that recomputing the reliability index on consensus-derived codes is "two bites
       at the apple." So: non-independent coding produces usable, publishable measurements; what it
       cannot produce is an honest reliability statistic.
    4. The reliability-vs-validity distinction as stated across the ICR sources above. — SECONDARY —
       independent coding measures RELIABILITY (agreement), not VALIDITY (whether displacement is what is
       being measured). Two coders can agree perfectly and both be wrong about what a "displacement" is.
       PREMISE-076 already names the requirement the assumption does not: a measured noise floor.
    5. LIMB C, in-house observation — VERIFIED (from the item's own context) — the two wikis are

       from format alone, so "independent" here can only mean *sequentially isolated*, not *blind*. That
       is a weaker control than the method note implies, and the note does not say which it means.

  Summary: The method is right and the rhetoric is wrong, and the rhetoric is load-bearing because
    "worthless" is what will license discarding a measurement that this study "almost never gets" in a
    window that is closing. The best measurement of exactly this bias — Hróbjartsson's systematic review
    of trials carrying both blinded and non-blinded assessors of the same outcomes — puts the
    exaggeration at about 36%, with a pooled ratio of odds ratios of 0.76 (0.61–0.94) in the subset with
    dependence data. That is a large bias and a good reason to blind; it is not a reason to call the
    unblinded number worthless. Second, blinding is frequently unachievable and the field's response is
    to proceed with the limitation declared, not to abandon the measurement. Third — and this is the
    limb the note does not address — the two wikis are distinguishable on sight, so a coder cannot be
    blind to source; "independently" can only mean sequentially isolated. Finally, the register already
    holds the sharper requirement: PREMISE-076 says a displacement rate is meaningful only above a
    MEASURED noise floor, which is a question about instrument reliability that blinding does not answer.

  Specific risks: The immediate risk is not a bad number; it is no number. FLAG-023's window is
    time-limited, the dialogue is paywalled, member access is the gating step, and **no owner and no date
    are attached to it**. A pre-registered method whose control is unachievable, attached to an
    unassigned access step, is a design that will expire unexecuted — and "the number is worthless" is
    exactly the sentence that will justify not taking a degraded reading in the last week of the window.
    The second risk is the mirror: if a non-blind number IS taken and the register treats it as clean
    because the method note was written, the ~36% exaggeration enters the estate unlabelled, and by
    PREMISE-188 the header caveat will not travel with the figure once it is quoted.

  Mitigations available:
    - Rewrite the method note to state the bias rather than the verdict: "a non-blind coding is expected
      to be biased in the direction of the coder's prior by roughly a third (Hróbjartsson et al. 2012);
      report it as such, do not discard it."
    - Take the degraded reading NOW and label it. A labelled biased measurement inside the window beats a
      clean measurement that never happens. Per PREMISE-117, publish-then-revise is codified practice.
    - Specify which control is actually meant: blind-to-source (probably impossible here), blind-to-
      hypothesis (possible — do not tell the coder what displacement is predicted), or merely sequential
      isolation (weakest). Blind-to-hypothesis is achievable and is the one worth specifying.
    - Satisfy PREMISE-076 first: establish a noise floor by double-coding a neutral control period with
      no known exchange. Without it, no displacement figure is interpretable however it was coded.
    - Attach an owner and a date to the member-access step today. This is the actual binding constraint
      and it is the REVISE-436 pattern: a named, cheap, unowned test.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: ASSUMPTION-1308
    Strongest counterargument: This measurement is unusual in that the hypothesis is known to the coder
      in advance and is highly specific — a displacement bracketed by two dated sources six days apart —
      which is the maximum-susceptibility case for confirmatory coding. Hróbjartsson's 36% is an *average
      across trials*, many with weak priors; a coder who knows the exact expected direction and
      magnitude sits in the tail of that distribution, not at its mean. And unlike a clinical outcome,
      this quantity has no external referent at all: there is no independent way to check the displacement
      afterwards, so the coding IS the measurement. Where the coding is the measurement and the coder
      holds the hypothesis, "worthless" is not hyperbole — it is the honest description of a number that
      cannot be distinguished from the expectation that generated it. Pre-registering the control before
      the data exist is the single strongest methodological act in this register's history and should not
      be softened.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (1) a blind-to-hypothesis arm is actually achievable
      — the coder can be given the texts without the predicted direction; (2) a noise floor is measured
      first (PREMISE-076); (3) the member access is obtained inside the window, which requires an owner
      and a date; and (4) whatever number results travels with its bias label attached to the figure, not
      to a document header (PREMISE-188).
    How to test: Cheap and in-house, and it settles the question in one pass. Take a *closed* prior case
      — any earlier cross-tradition displacement already coded in this estate — and re-code it twice:
      once by an agent told the hypothesis, once by an agent given only the texts. The difference between
      the two codings IS the local value of Hróbjartsson's ratio-of-odds-ratios for this instrument. If it
      is small, "worthless" is refuted and the FLAG-023 reading can proceed unblinded with a declared
      correction. If it is large, blinding is mandatory and the access step must be owned today. Either
      way the estate stops arguing about the adjective and has a number.
