```
    2. ARLIS/NA Cataloging Advisory Committee with VRA Data Standards Committee, n.d. (sources
       dated 2003–2005). "Attribution Qualifiers for Artists' Names." [fetched; read in full]
       — Direct challenge to the "drift from the thinker's own voice" framing. Art-library
       cataloguing has a controlled vocabulary for graded authorship: "Workshop of", "Studio
       of", "Office of" ("unknown individual working directly for the named master, probably
       under his supervision"), "Pupil of", "Circle of", "School of", "Follower of", "Style
       of", "Attributed to". "From Hawkins's team" corresponds to an established and legitimate
       category ("Workshop/Office of"). It is not a corruption of "from Hawkins". In this
       practice a corpus can widen from the master's own hand without being degraded, provided
       the qualifier travels with the item. Note (2) of the same document cuts the other way:
       a known workshop "should be listed as a separate 'corporate' creator and the nature of
       the activity of the individual recorded as a role". So the field already records the
       relaxation as structured data, not free text. That is the presumption's own remedy.
    3. [Authors not captured in fetch], 2020. "An assessment of the extent to which the contents
       of PROSPERO records meet the systematic review protocol reporting items in PRISMA-P."
       F1000Research (PMC7431973; PMID 32850123). [fetched; abstract and discussion passage
       read; full text not read sequentially]
       — Partial challenge: "Protocols are iterative documents and even after a review has
       started there may be legitimate reasons for amendments. Such changes should and can be
       reported in a registration record, with their justification and timing." In
       systematic-review practice, changing criteria mid-stream is not drift as such. It is
       legitimate when disclosed with a reason and a date. C2A2's per-card free-text disclosure
       meets "justification" and, implicitly, "timing". Counter-evidence I am recording for
       honesty: the same paper finds "reporting was more frequent for items required in
       PROSPERO than optional items". Structured, mandatory fields outperform optional free text.
       That supports the presumption's core worry.
    4. Cochrane Colloquium 2019 abstracts, "Did non-Cochrane systematic reviews in nursing follow
       protocols registered in [PROSPERO]" / "Inconsistencies are common between non-Cochrane
       systematic reviews and their [protocols]". [snippet only]
       — Base-rate challenge. In 43 PROSPERO-registered reviews, every review deviated from its
       protocol (changes to inclusion criteria among them), and only about 8% gave reasonable
       explanations for individual changes. Measured against that norm, C2A2's practice of
       disclosing every relaxation is above field practice. Disclosure alone is not the weak
       point. The weak point is the missing criterion-level record.
    5. Sunstein, C. R., 1996/2018. Legal Reasoning and Political Conflict. Oxford University
       Press. Also Tanner Lecture (1996). [snippet only, via search summaries; not read]
       — Theoretical objection. In common-law reasoning, new cases are decided by analogy to
       precedent "fixed points". Incremental, precedent-licensed extension with narrow, case-level
       reasons is the normal and defended way rules evolve. It is not a pathology. "Team
       meetings were approved before, so team material qualifies" is ordinary analogical
       reasoning. The snippets also give the boundary condition: such agreements are viable
       only under real adherence to precedent (stare decisis), so the precedent itself has to
       be recorded and retrievable. A student critique cited in the results (Fordham Urban Law
       Journal 34(5)) presses this point. I saw it as a snippet only.
    6. Archival appraisal and collection-development policies: Washington State Archives (2019,
       2022); Gwynedd Archives Service; Royal Armouries; Explore York v1.2. [snippet only]
       — Boundary condition. In archival practice, scope is held in place by documented
       individual appraisal decisions plus an annual policy review and periodic re-checking of
       holdings against criteria (York). Case-level documentation is accepted as legitimate,
       but always together with a periodic criterion-level review. C2A2 has the first and,
       on the item's own account, not the second.

  Strength of challenge: Moderate. The challenge is moderate against "without anyone deciding"
    and against framing "team" or "school" material as drift. It is weak against the core
    claim that free-text-only disclosure fails to record a criterion change.

  Summary: Normalization of deviance transfers only partly. Vaughan's mechanism depends on
    diffuse, structurally secret decisions and an unrecorded acceptance of risk. C2A2 has one
    human approver who sees each relaxation stated on its card, so the presumption overstates
    the claim that nobody decides. Three adjacent fields (art cataloguing's "Workshop/Circle/
    School of" qualifiers, PROSPERO protocol amendments, and common-law analogical extension)
    each treat precedent-licensed widening as legitimate criteria evolution, provided it is
    disclosed with reasons. By the PROSPERO base rate, C2A2 already discloses more than typical
    practice. However, every one of those fields legitimises widening through a structured or
    versioned record: a controlled qualifier, a dated registration amendment, a citable
    precedent, or a periodic policy review. That is exactly what the presumption says C2A2
    lacks. The literature therefore challenges the diagnosis (that this is deviance or
    unchosen drift) more than the remedy (record the criterion change).

  Specific risks: If C2A2 accepts the presumption as stated, it may (a) treat "team" or
    "catch-all" material as contamination and exclude legitimate workshop-level sources (lab
    papers, co-authored talks), thinning traditions in which the thinker works through a
    group; (b) chase a safety-culture cure (stopping relaxations) when a cataloguing cure
    (attribution qualifiers plus versioned criteria) would do. Risk the AGAINST search does NOT
    dispel: because C2A2 is low-stakes, no salient failure will ever force a review, so without
    a scheduled criterion audit, item-level approvals can accumulate into an unratified scope
    (Sources 1 and 6).

  Mitigations available: (i) Add a controlled attribution-qualifier field to every card,
    following ARLIS/CDWA (e.g., thinker | workshop/team-of | circle-of | attributed-to |
    unattributed-catch-all), so narration and synthesis can filter on "own voice". (ii) Keep a
    per-tradition criteria file with dated amendments, PROSPERO-style. When a card relies on
    precedent, require it to cite the amendment, or propose one for Tom to ratify at the
    criterion level. (iii) Periodic scope review (archival practice): list each tradition's
    holdings by qualifier and check the share that is not the thinker's own voice.
```

```
STEELMAN:
  Item: PRESUMPTION-1176
  Strongest counterargument: Widening by precedent is how every serious curation tradition
    evolves its criteria, so it is not deviance. Common law extends rules by analogy to settled
    cases. Systematic reviewers amend protocols mid-review and are judged on whether they
    disclose and justify the change. Art historians have accepted "Workshop of", "Circle of" and
    "School of" as legitimate parts of a master's corpus for generations. "From Hawkins's team"
    is a workshop attribution, and the team carries Hawkins's research programme. Normalization
    of deviance describes risk accepted silently inside a diffuse bureaucracy. In C2A2 a single
    named human sees each relaxation stated on its card and approves it. That person is
    deciding, and deciding case by case is how precedent is supposed to work. On the available
    base rates, C2A2's disclosure already exceeds normal systematic-review practice.
  What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (1) the relaxation text on each card is
    salient enough that Tom actually registers it as a scope change when approving; (2) the
    qualifier (team, catch-all, undated) survives downstream, so synthesis and narration do not
    voice workshop material as the thinker's own; (3) someone periodically reviews the
    aggregate share of non-own-voice material per tradition; (4) precedents are retrievable, so
    later cards cite the actual earlier approval and not a remembered gist.
  How to test: (a) For Hawkins and Hoffman, list approved items by authorship class (own /
    team / unattributed) over time and check whether the non-own share is rising. (b) Grep the
    tradition wikis and narration tracks for content from team or catch-all items and check
    whether it is attributed to the thinker by name. (c) Ask Tom, blind, to state the current
    admission criterion for each tradition, then compare it with what has actually been
    admitted. A gap between the two is unratified drift. Agreement means precedent is
    functioning as legitimate evolution.
```
