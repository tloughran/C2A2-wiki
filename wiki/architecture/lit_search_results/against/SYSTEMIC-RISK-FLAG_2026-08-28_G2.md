  Common vulnerability: **Controls in this estate are adopted without a recorded derivation, so no breach of
    them is interpretable.** Five items in one day's intake turn on the same missing artifact:
      - ASSUMPTION-1222 — a review exemption with no stated defect rate and no recorded adoption, described
        as "standing."
      - ASSUMPTION-1229 — a budget offered for revision on the strength of the breaches it suffered, with no
        prospective derivation to revise against.
      - ASSUMPTION-1232 — an agent applying a distinction the instruction's wording does not support, with
        no register of the interpretations taken.
      - PRESUMPTION-887 — undocumented local decision rules with no discovery method.
      - PRESUMPTION-892 — a token budget breached 28 times out of 28, with nothing on record establishing
        where the number came from.
    In every case the control exists as a number or a rule, and nowhere as a derivation. A control without a
    derivation cannot be breached in any informative sense: the breach is equally evidence about the actor
    and about the number, and no procedure in the estate distinguishes the two.

  Risk level: High

  The compounding property: the estate currently has two opposite failure modes available and no way to
    choose between them. Treat the breaches as deviations and you get more disclosure, more flags, and the
    alert fatigue this cohort also documents. Treat them as evidence the limit is wrong and you get the
    ratchet. Both readings are defensible *because the derivation is missing*, and each additional
    undocumented control adds one more place where the estate can argue with itself indefinitely.

  Recommendation: A single rule, applied at adoption rather than at breach — **a control enters the estate
    with a recorded derivation or it does not enter.** Minimum record: the date, the author, what the number
    or rule was derived from, and an expiry or review date. Retrospectively, the cheapest first move is one
    query: locate the derivation of the token budget. If it exists, PRESUMPTION-892's deviance frame applies
    and ASSUMPTION-1229's revision is unwarranted. If it does not, both items re-frame from "defend or excuse
    the breach" to "set a limit," and three of the five items above collapse into that one task.
