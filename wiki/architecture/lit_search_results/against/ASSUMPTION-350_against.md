  Summary: The challenge targets the word 'validate'. A single-thinker pilot can FALSIFY the mechanism (a clean failure is informative) but a PASS only shows the mechanism can work in one favorable instance - it does not validate the mechanism in the sense of establishing it will hold for other thinkers. Pilot-methodology cautions specifically warn that pilot results, especially from non-representative cases, mislead about scalability.

  Specific risks: A passing Hawkins pilot could be over-read as 'mechanism validated', authorizing rollout that then fails on less favorable thinkers.

  Mitigations available: Frame the pilot as necessary-condition / falsification only; pre-register a second, less favorable thinker before claiming the mechanism generalizes.
