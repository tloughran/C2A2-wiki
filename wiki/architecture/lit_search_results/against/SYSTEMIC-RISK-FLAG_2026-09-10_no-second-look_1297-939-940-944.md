  Common vulnerability: **The estate's controls terminate at the moment of production, and it has no
  control that operates at T+n.** In each of the four items the control is an act — writing a label,
  writing a FINDING, applying a fix, speaking a report — and in each case the act is treated as having
  changed the state of the world permanently, with no later re-reading by a party other than the
  author. The estate has verification AT the point of production and none AFTER it.

    - ASSUMPTION-1297: the falsifiers are attached at the moment the bridge is filed. Nothing reads
      them later. The best-measured analogue — a publisher-attached retraction notice — is
      acknowledged in 5.4% of the citation contexts that follow it, and roughly nine in ten
      preregistered studies deviate from a plan that the reviewers whose job it is to check do not
      open.
    - PRESUMPTION-939: the FINDING is the control, and the control is complete when written. No
      closure test, no owner, no adjudicator, no date. Under a mandated hospital programme with formal
      investigation the modal outcome of a logged defect was "few policy or practice changes."
    - PRESUMPTION-940: the fix is verified when applied and never again. The regulated world mandates
      two verification points precisely because one was found insufficient, and 6–10% of fixes are
      reopened even in projects with version control, review and regression suites.
    - PRESUMPTION-944: the report is delivered and is thereby discharged. It is the only channel with
      no later reader at all, and it is the one exempted from the discipline — while the controlled
      experiment on handover channels puts verbal-only retention at 2.5% after five cycles, with
      important facts lost at the same rate as trivial ones.

  The four therefore share a single structural defect: **every control in this estate is a speech act,
  and no speech act is re-read.** A label is written and not consulted. A defect is recorded and not
  revisited. A configuration is set and not re-checked. A report is spoken and not corroborated. In
  each case the estate's knowledge of the state is fixed at the instant of the act and thereafter
  decays at a rate it does not measure, in a direction it cannot see.

  Risk level: High

  Why it is systemic rather than four coincidences: The four items sit at four different control
  types — an epistemic marking (1297), a governance record (939), a system state (940), and a human-
  facing assertion (944) — and the same defect appears at each: the control's lifetime is one instant.
  That distribution is the signature of a design property, not of four lapses. Three further features
  make it worse than the sum of its parts. First, **the defect is invisible by construction**: a label
  unread, a finding unrevisited, a fix reverted and a report unchecked all produce exactly the same
  observable as their healthy counterparts, so the estate's confidence in each is bounded above by the
  moment of production and has no lower bound. Second, **they compose in one direction, toward the
  designer**: PRESUMPTION-940's unverified configuration state and PRESUMPTION-939's uncontained
  findings both feed the morning report, so file-level rigour applied upstream is discarded at the
  last hop by PRESUMPTION-944 — and that hop is the only one with a human at the end and no
  subsequent reader. Two consecutive false morning reports is what that composition looks like from
  outside. Third, **the estate already holds the governing rule and did not apply it**. PREMISE-183
  clause (1) states that verifying implementation is not verifying that recurrence stopped;
  PREMISE-188 states that a header-level qualifier does not govern the body once quoted; PREMISE-194
  requires corroboration by an artefact from a different process rather than narration; PREMISE-109
  requires health claims to be bound to a named artefact and timestamp. All four are ACTIVE. All four
  were read as governing files. The systemic finding is therefore not that the estate lacks the
  principle — it is that the principle is scoped to artefacts, and three of the four defects above
  live outside that scope.

  Recommendation (for 14a/14b/12 to consider; 15b does not make design decisions): Consider one
  cross-cutting change rather than four: **every control acquires a re-read, and the re-reader is not
  the author.** The regulated world's two-step corrective-action design is the off-the-shelf pattern
  and it is one field plus one scheduled question. Concretely, and cheapest first:
    (i)   **Scope-extend PREMISE-183 clause (1) from FLAGs to all four control types.** An
          effectiveness condition defined at production time, tested at a stated later point, with a
          failed check reopening rather than annotating. For a configuration fix the later point
          should be tied to the threatening event (the application's next relaunch), not to a calendar
          interval — a re-check at the wrong time measures nothing.
    (ii)  **Bind the discipline to the ASSERTION, not to the artefact type.** One clause stating that
          PREMISE-109 and PREMISE-194 govern regardless of delivery medium would close
          PRESUMPTION-944 outright, and it is a clause, not a subsystem. The corollary is that the
          morning report becomes a file that is read aloud rather than a narration — the printed-
          handout condition, at 99% against 2.5%.
    (iii) **Attach markings at the quotable unit.** PREMISE-188's clause (3) already specifies this
          and names GRADE as the working precedent; ASSUMPTION-1297 is the case that needs it. A
          provisional status that does not survive a copy-paste is not a guard.
    (iv)  **Put the second look in different hands wherever it is affordable.** Per Nemeth, a re-read
          by the producing party is the weakest form and may be counterproductive. The estate already
          owns the machinery for this — the 15a/15b independence architecture — so the extension is
          organisational rather than new build.
    (v)   **Instrument the decay before mandating the remedy.** Each individual result file names a
          cheap retrospective test (the 218-flag audit for 940; the luck-vocabulary grep for 941; the
          claims-without-evidence audit of 30 morning reports for 944; the marking-survival grep for
          1297). Running those four costs a few hours and would convert this flag from an argument
          into four numbers. Per the 2026-09-09 flag's TRIR caution, adding instruments reflexively is
          itself a failure mode; measuring first is the guard against it.
