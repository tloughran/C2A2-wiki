  Sources:
    1. Goodhart's Law / "When a measure becomes a target" (Strathern). — Once graph membership is a reward earned by self-articulation AND the graph is declared a "measurement surface," the criterion becomes a target communities optimize for, degrading the measure's validity. Direct challenge to "measurement surface" status.
    2. Participation-inequality literature (Nielsen 90-9-1). — Gated, articulation-based visibility systematically advantages resourced/literate participants; the gate reproduces inequality rather than measuring relatedness. Challenges the fairness and the measurement claim.
    3. YAGNI / speculative-architecture critique (Fowler). — Committing to P3 as "the correct target architecture" when its central join is unbuilt and doubted (PRESUMPTION-306) is premature; declaring a target correct before validation is the over-commitment anti-pattern.

  Strength of challenge: Moderate-Strong

  Summary: The data-pipeline analogy that supports the mechanism does not transfer cleanly to PARTICIPATION, and three challenges land. First, Goodhart: making the graph a "measurement surface" while membership is earned by self-articulation turns the criterion into a target, so the surface stops measuring what it claims. Second, the gate is normative, not neutral (couples 308): articulation correlates with resources, so the membrane encodes participation inequality. Third, declaring P3 "the correct target architecture" pre-commits to a design whose load-bearing join is unbuilt and newly doubted — premature per YAGNI. None of these says staged promotion is wrong; they say "correct target architecture" + "measurement surface" overclaim, especially before the join is validated.

  Specific risks: Communities game the articulation gate, hollowing the "measurement"; under-resourced/emerging communities are structurally invisible; C2A2 builds toward a "correct" P3 that the 306/311 doubts may invalidate, wasting the investment.

  Mitigations available: Drop or hedge the "measurement surface" claim while membership is reward-coupled (Goodhart); separate the quality gate (neutral conformity checks) from the visibility/normative decision; treat P3 as a candidate target with an explicit kill/validate criterion tied to the 306 join result.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: ASSUMPTION-276
    Strongest counterargument: A surface cannot simultaneously be a reward you earn and an unbiased measurement of the thing the reward is for. If self-articulation earns graph membership and the graph is then read as a measurement of the community landscape, you are measuring who articulated, not what exists — Goodhart in one move. And calling P3 "the correct target architecture" before its central join is even built or validated commits the system to a design that PRESUMPTION-306/311 suggest may be conceptually or practically impossible.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The quality gate measures something proxy-neutral (not resource-correlated articulation), the graph is NOT also read as an unbiased measurement of the population, AND the P3 join is validated before "correct target" is asserted.
    How to test: Check whether graph-membership rates correlate with community resources/literacy (inequality signal); audit whether any downstream claim treats the graph as a representative measurement; gate the "correct target" label on the 306 ER result.
