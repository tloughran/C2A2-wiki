  Summary: The assumption's *conclusion* (AI can't be conscious because inner life needs embodied biology) is one of the most actively contested claims in philosophy of mind, not a consensus. Functionalism, multiple realizability, and Chalmers' organizational-invariance argument all deny the biology requirement. So a "bridge" between McGilchrist and Kastrup that lands on this conclusion is a bridge into contested territory; the agreement of two thinkers does not settle a question the field treats as open.
