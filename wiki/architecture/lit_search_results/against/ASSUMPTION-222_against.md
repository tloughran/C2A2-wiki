  Sources:
    1. Campbell, D. T. & Fiske, D. W. (1959). "Convergent and discriminant validation by the multitrait-multimethod matrix." Psychological Bulletin 56(2), 81-105. — Names "method variance": correlations among measures sharing a method are inflated by the shared method, not by the construct. Convergence within one method is precisely the case the MTMM matrix is designed to *discount*.
    2. Podsakoff, P. M., MacKenzie, S. B., Lee, J.-Y. & Podsakoff, N. P. (2003). "Common method biases in behavioral research." Journal of Applied Psychology 88(5), 879-903. — Shared data-collection procedure produces spurious common-method variance that can masquerade as substantive convergence.
    3. Podsakoff & Organ (1986). "Self-reports in organizational research: Problems and prospects." Journal of Management 12(4), 531-544. — Foundational statement that same-source / same-occasion measurement systematically over-states relationships.

  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: The single strongest objection is the common-method-variance critique: three proposals surfaced in one week by one intake/sewing pipeline share a method and an occasion, so their thematic agreement is exactly the kind of co-variation that Campbell & Fiske and Podsakoff et al. warn is an artifact of shared method rather than evidence of a real shared structure. Promoting them "as a single unit because together they articulate one paradigm-bridge" treats mono-method convergence as construct convergence — the canonical validity error.

  Specific risks: If C2A2 unit-promotes a same-pipeline cluster as tradition-level homology, it over-claims structural unity (a credibility risk in the same family as the SUPER-BRIDGE concern, ASSUMPTION-020), and it bakes a method artifact into the network where downstream agents will read it as validated cross-tradition structure.

  Mitigations available: Require a cross-method / cross-occasion check before unit-promotion — e.g., confirm the convergence reproduces when the three sources are surfaced through different prompts, different days, or independent agents; or promote the items individually and let convergence emerge from independent routing rather than asserting it at intake.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: ASSUMPTION-222
    Strongest counterargument: The convergent-validity principle that licenses "convergence = shared construct" is *defined* by method independence. Same-week, same-pipeline intake violates that precondition, so the very framework invoked to justify unit-promotion actually disqualifies this instance. Absent a discriminant check, the cluster is indistinguishable from a batch/topic-model co-occurrence artifact.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: The three convergences reproduce under at least one genuinely independent method (different agent, occasion, or elicitation), i.e., they survive a minimal multitrait-multimethod-style discriminant test.
    How to test: Re-elicit each source's stance independently (separate sessions/agents/prompts) and check whether the "loving unity as telos" bridge still co-emerges; if it does, method-variance is ruled out and unit-promotion is warranted.
