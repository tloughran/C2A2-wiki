    1. Bruineberg, J., Dołęga, K., Dewhurst, J. & Baltieri, M. (2022). "The Emperor's New Markov Blankets." Behavioral and Brain Sciences. — The central objection to the assumption as stated. Distinguishes *instrumental* Markov blankets (Pearl blankets — a statistical tool in Bayesian networks) from *ontological* ones (Friston blankets — purported physical boundaries), traces a persistent conflation of the two through the FEP literature, and argues the equivocation "licenses considerable metaphysical overreach." ASSUMPTION-022's phrase "applies literally at every level" is precisely the ontological reading the paper targets.
    2. Raja, V., Valluri, D., Baggs, E., Chemero, A. & Anderson, M. (2021). "The Markov blanket trick: on the scope of the free energy principle and active inference." Multiple non-equivalent Markov blanket definitions across FEP papers; faulty lemmas in some derivations; conclusion that FEP is best understood as a modelling framework for "some properties of some systems," not a universal principle.   Summary: The literature has settled into a stable, well-documented critical position, and no 2026 movement was detected — appropriate for a debate that has been thoroughly aired across Physics of Life Reviews, BBS and BJPS. The core objection is unchanged and unrebutted: the Markov blanket is a statistical construct whose ontological status is contested, blanket placement is often a modelling choice rather than a discovered fact, and the FEP's scope is disputed by its critics and *limited by its own defenders*. ASSUMPTION-022 as worded — "fundamental shared structure" and "applies literally at every level" — is stronger than any position currently defensible in the peer-reviewed literature, including the pro-realist one. Note the interaction with ASSUMPTION-020: if blankets are instrumental at any of the three levels, the three-level unification unifies models rather than phenomena.

  Specific risks: This is the deepest and most load-bearing item in the batch. If false, the "inside/outside boundary" that C2A2 treats as the fundamental structure shared across all 11 programs is an artefact of choosing to model each level with the same formalism — which explains the apparent universality without any shared structure existing. Every cross-level finding then becomes a restatement of a modelling choice. Because the claim is architectural rather than local, it cannot be quarantined: it is the organising principle of the wiki. The failure mode is also self-concealing, since a formalism applicable to everything will always appear confirmed.

  Mitigations available: (a) Weaken the wording to what the literature supports: "the inside/outside distinction is a recurring and productive *modelling* motif across levels" — true, defensible, and preserves nearly all the project's usable content; (b) adopt Bruineberg et al.'s instrumental/ontological vocabulary explicitly and mark, per level, which reading is being used; (c) identify at least one level where the blanket is *not* merely instrumental, with an argument, rather than assuming uniformity; (d) look for a disconfirming case — a system in the C2A2 scope where no non-arbitrary blanket placement exists (extended/coupled systems are the obvious candidates); (e) cite the BJPS 2025 defence honestly, including its concession, rather than as blanket support.

  STEELMAN:
    Strongest counterargument: The instrumental/ontological dichotomy is itself contested — Kirchhoff's continuity commentary argues for a spectrum, and the literalist-fallacy paper shows that inferring instrumentalism from imperfect model-target mapping is a fallacy, not an argument. No scientific model maps perfectly onto its target; if imperfect mapping refuted realism, no theoretical physics would be real either. Furthermore, the recurrence of the inside/outside distinction across cell membranes, cortical columns, organisms and social groups is a genuine and striking regularity that predates FEP and does not depend on it; FEP supplies one formalisation of a boundary phenomenon that biology independently recognises. C2A2 could drop FEP entirely and retain the boundary insight.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (1) The project's substantive claims survive replacing "FEP applies literally" with "the inside/outside distinction recurs" — if they do, the risk is largely rhetorical and the fix is a wording change; (2) at least one level has an argued, not assumed, ontological reading; (3) no finding depends on the *literal* and *universal* form of the claim; (4) the project engages the Bruineberg critique explicitly rather than proceeding as though it did not exist.
    How to test: Deletion test. Take the five most important C2A2 findings that invoke Markov blankets. Rewrite each with the blanket language removed, using only "boundary" or "interface." If the finding survives intact, FEP was decoration and the strong claim can be dropped at no cost. If the finding collapses, it was resting entirely on a contested construct and must be marked CONTESTED. This is a one-session exercise with a clear decision rule and it resolves the practical exposure regardless of how the philosophical debate ends.
