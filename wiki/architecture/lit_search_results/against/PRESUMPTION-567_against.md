  Sources:
    1. Agrawal, A., Suzgun, M., Mackey, L. & Kalai, A. 2024. "Do Language Models Know When They're Hallucinating References?" Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EACL 2024 (arXiv:2305.18248). - The most directly on-point source located, because its task is C2A2's task: fabricated book and article references, used as a "model organism" of hallucination. The finding is that hallucinated references can be identified WITHOUT consulting external resources, by putting direct and indirect questions to the same model and applying consistency checks: models including GPT-4 produce inconsistent author lists for hallucinated references while accurately recalling authors of real ones. The authors conclude the model "knows" when it is hallucinating a reference. This is same-model checking, at zero model-level decorrelation, succeeding on exactly the citation spot-check that PRESUMPTION-567 doubts.
    2. Manakul, P., Liusie, A. & Gales, M.J.F. 2023. "SelfCheckGPT: Zero-Resource Black-Box Hallucination Detection for Generative Large Language Models." Proceedings of EMNLP 2023, Singapore. - Detects hallucinated content by sampling multiple stochastic responses from the SAME model and scoring cross-sample consistency (BERTScore, NLI, QA, n-gram, LLM-prompting variants), with no external database. The method's existence and measured performance falsify the strong reading of the decorrelation requirement, on which same-model instances carry no detection signal. If model-level correlation were total in the relevant sense, the consistency signal would be zero and SelfCheckGPT could not work.
    3. Wang, X., Wei, J., Schuurmans, D., Le, Q., Chi, E., Narang, S., Chowdhery, A. & Zhou, D. 2022. "Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning in Language Models." arXiv:2203.11171 (ICLR 2023). - Sampling diverse reasoning paths from a single model and marginalising over them yields large accuracy gains (reported +17.9 on GSM8K, +11.0 on SVAMP, +12.2 on AQuA). Same weights, same training data, same family - the entire gain comes from instance-level sampling variation. Establishes that instance-level decorrelation is a real and quantitatively substantial error-detection resource, not a nominal one, which is the exact thing the presumption treats as unwarranted.
    4. Littlewood, B. & Miller, D.R. 1989. "Conceptual Modeling of Coincident Failures in Multiversion Software." IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering 15(12): 1596-1614. - The assurance field faced this exact question and answered it against the binary framing. Eckhardt & Lee (1985) had shown that independently developed versions fail dependently - coincident failure exceeds the independence prediction - which is the software analogue of residual same-model correlation. The field's response was not to declare redundancy void but to model the dependence: Littlewood & Miller show that FORCED methodological diversity reduces the probability of simultaneous failure, and that versions exhibiting better-than-independent failure behaviour are theoretically obtainable. The operative variable is the achieved diversity gain, not the presence or absence of independence.
    5. Mosleh, A. et al. 1998. NUREG/CR-5485, "Guidelines on Modeling Common-Cause Failures in Probabilistic Risk Assessment." U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission / INEEL. - The reference standard for graded independence in high-consequence assurance. Dependence between redundant channels is a quantified parameter (beta factor, alpha factor, multiple Greek letter models), with coupling mechanisms - design, hardware, function, installation, maintenance, environment - enumerated as the source of common-cause vulnerability. Reported empirical beta values run roughly 0.03-0.22 with an average near 0.10, i.e. in acknowledged common-cause groups something like 78-97 percent of failures remain independent. Redundancy with known common-cause coupling is credited with a discount, not disqualified. Compare GAO, Government Auditing Standards (Yellow Book), whose conceptual framework for independence is explicitly a three-step graded process - identify threats, evaluate their significance individually and in aggregate, apply safeguards to reduce them to an acceptable level - with self-review and bias among the enumerated threat categories. Auditor independence is a level to be assessed and mitigated, never a binary a checker either has or lacks.

  Strength of challenge: Strong

  Summary: The presumption charges an inconsistency: crediting instance-level independence while carrying model-level correlation as an undischarged residual. The literature says this is not an inconsistency but the standard structure of assurance reasoning. Reliability engineering has treated inter-channel dependence as a quantified parameter since the 1980s - NUREG/CR-5485's beta factors of 0.03-0.22 mean most failure modes remain independent even inside an acknowledged common-cause group - and Littlewood & Miller's response to the demonstrated failure of the independence assumption in N-version programming was to model correlation and pursue diversity gain, not to void redundancy. Audit practice takes the same shape: the GAO conceptual framework grades threats and applies safeguards rather than certifying independence as present or absent. On the AI side the empirical case is stronger still and closer to home: self-consistency extracts large accuracy gains from sampling alone at zero model diversity, SelfCheckGPT detects hallucinations from same-model sample disagreement with no external resource, and Agrawal et al. show that consistency checks on the same model identify hallucinated REFERENCES specifically, which is C2A2's actual task. What the challenge does not license is the current form of the residual. The whole point of the beta-factor and threats-and-safeguards frameworks is that the residual is SIZED; "MONITOR-486 stands" names a residual without estimating it, and that gap - not the two-tier treatment - is the real defect.

  Specific risks: If the presumption is over-read, C2A2 discards a verification channel that the literature shows carries real signal on precisely this task, and either forgoes citation checking or defers it to an unavailable cross-family or human check, so unverified citations enter the corpus under a rule that was meant to protect it. The binary reading also has no stable stopping point: a different model family shares training corpora, evaluation benchmarks and architecture lineage, so it too fails strict decorrelation, and the requirement voids every practicable checker. If the presumption is under-read, the specific correlated failure modes go untracked - Zheng et al. (2023, "Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena," NeurIPS 2023) document self-enhancement bias, position bias and verbosity bias in LLM judges, and Huang et al. (2024, "Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet," ICLR 2024) find that intrinsic self-correction without external feedback can degrade rather than improve performance. A same-model checker sharing a fabrication prior will systematically miss the plausible-but-nonexistent citation, which is the highest-frequency failure in this batch and the one a naive pass is least likely to catch.

  Mitigations available: Size the residual instead of naming it. Estimate an effective beta for same-model citation checking by re-running the spot-check on the same 7 citations with a different model family and by hand - the in-house adjunct already queued - and use the disagreement rate as the empirical dependence estimate; that converts MONITOR-486 from an open flag into a number and makes PREMISE-132 auditable. Apply forced diversity in Littlewood & Miller's sense within the family, since diversity of the decision sequence is what buys the gain: vary the prompt framing, ask indirect questions (author list, venue, year, abstract) rather than "does this exist," and sample multiple times, which is the Agrawal et al. and SelfCheckGPT construction rather than a single re-read. Route the check through external ground truth wherever available - Huang et al.'s negative result is explicitly scoped to INTRINSIC self-correction absent external feedback, and citation verification is one of the few tasks with cheap external ground truth, which removes the failure mode their paper identifies. Adopt the GAO structure explicitly: enumerate the coupling mechanisms (shared pretraining corpus, shared RLHF preferences, shared plausibility prior over citation formats), rate the significance of each, and state the safeguard applied to each rather than carrying one undifferentiated residual.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-567
    Strongest counterargument (against the presumption): The two statements the presumption calls unreconciled are the textbook form of a correctly reconciled assurance judgement. Every mature high-consequence field treats independence as a graded quantity with a sized residual, never as a binary gate: nuclear PRA prices inter-channel dependence as a beta factor and still credits redundancy, N-version software conceded after Eckhardt & Lee that versions fail dependently and responded by pursuing measurable diversity gain rather than abandoning multiversion design, and audit standards replaced independence-as-a-fact with a conceptual framework of threats, significance and safeguards. Crediting instance-level decorrelation while flagging model-level correlation on a monitor is exactly that pattern, and calling it an inconsistency implies a standard - full decorrelation - that no verification arrangement available to C2A2, including a different model family or a tired human, can meet. The empirical case is decisive on the specific task: Agrawal et al. show that consistency questioning of the SAME model identifies hallucinated references, so the checker at issue is operating in a regime where same-model checking is demonstrated to work, not merely hoped to.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: the residual same-model correlation on citation existence is small enough that the check retains most of its power - i.e. the checker's miss rate on fabricated citations is materially below the generator's fabrication rate; the check is run in a diversity-generating form (multiple samples, indirect questions, varied framings) rather than as a single re-read; and it either consults external ground truth or is explicitly labelled as a screen rather than a verification when it does not.
    How to test: run the queued adjunct as a three-arm comparison on the same 7 citations - same-family instance, different model family, and manual check against the source of record - and compute pairwise disagreement rates. The manual arm supplies ground truth, so this yields both the same-model checker's true miss rate and a direct empirical estimate of same-family dependence: if the same-family and different-family arms agree on errors far more often than either agrees with the manual arm, correlation is high and the presumption is vindicated; if the same-family arm's miss rate is close to the different-family arm's, the model-level residual is small and the two-tier treatment was correct. Extend it by re-asking the same-family instance indirectly (name the authors, name the venue, quote the abstract) per Agrawal et al. and checking whether indirect questioning recovers errors the direct question missed - if it does, the operative variable was never independence but interrogation design, and the presumption has misidentified the failure mode.

  Common vulnerability: Both presumptions run the same inference - the instrument or procedure that produced X belongs to the same kind as one already known to be fallible, therefore X inherits that fallibility undiminished. Both accordingly treat a graded epistemic property as binary: warrant-from-iterated-generalisation in 566, independence-in-verification in 567. In both cases the relevant literatures hold that the categorical similarity is not decisive and that the correct move is to SIZE the residual - state the population and the termination criterion (Mitchell's version spaces, Mayo's severity), or estimate the dependence parameter (beta factors, diversity gain, threats-and-safeguards). Left unchecked, the pattern generates a regress in which no correction can be banked and no checker can be trusted, because every candidate belongs to some already-impugned kind.
