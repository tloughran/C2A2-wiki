  Strength of challenge: Moderate

  Summary: The complementarity core is sound (and already PREMISE-051), so the challenge is not to the dual-view design but to the OVERCLAIM: "purely mutually upbuilding, each makes the other more truthful." CMV's own Rule of Parsimony and empirical context-switching costs show coordinated views carry costs and can compete, not only reinforce. More importantly, the "directory feeds the graph / seeds earn their place" framing imports a tiered-visibility value claim that the CMV literature does not license and that has a documented harm side (lower-tier stigma). "More truthful" is a normative gloss/gain claim dressed as a design fact.

  Specific risks: Accepting the "purely upbuilding/earned/more truthful" framing as validated would launder a contestable visibility value choice (who is graphed = who is legitimate) into settled design rationale, and would understate the maintenance/attention cost of carrying two surfaces.

  Mitigations available: Keep the validated claim narrow (complementarity = PREMISE-051); treat "mutually upbuilding / earned place / more truthful" as a separate, contested claim routed with PRESUMPTION-314/316; apply Baldonado's non-absorption test (already folded into PREMISE-051) so the second surface stays justified by task, not by status narrative.
