  Sources:
    1. Norton, J.D. 2003. "A Material Theory of Induction." Philosophy of Science 70(4): 647-670; extended in The Material Theory of Induction, University of Calgary Press, 2021. - Directly attacks the presumption's load-bearing move. Norton's negative thesis is that there are no universal inductive inference schemas; his positive thesis is that inductive inferences are warranted by material facts that prevail locally, so "all inductive inference is local." On this account two inferences produced by the same surface procedure ("generalise from instances seen") can have radically different warrant, because the warrant does not come from the procedure at all - it comes from the domain facts that license the step. The presumption's inference "same procedure, therefore same epistemic standing" is exactly what the material theory denies. The correct question is not whether the fifth diagnosis was reached the same way, but which local facts about the page population license it.
    2. Lakatos, I. 1976. Proofs and Refutations: The Logic of Mathematical Discovery, eds. J. Worrall & E. Zahar. Cambridge University Press. - The canonical treatment of precisely the sequence the presumption finds suspicious: primitive conjecture, provisional proof, global counterexample, identification of the "guilty lemma," and lemma-incorporation building that lemma into the conjecture as a condition. Lakatos's whole point is that this iterated procedure constitutes genuine improvement - "successive improvement of creative hypotheses by attempts to prove them and by criticism of these attempts" - so the fifth conjecture in a refutation series is not on a par with the first. Being the latest member of a refutation series is an epistemic credential, not a disqualification.
    3. Mitchell, T.M. 1982. "Generalization as Search." Artificial Intelligence 18(2): 203-226. - Formal counterexample to the claim that successive generalisation from instances cannot converge. In the version-space / candidate-elimination framework each instance acts as a constraint that eliminates hypotheses, the version space contracts monotonically as instances arrive, and the procedure is guaranteed without backtracking to retain all hypotheses consistent with the instances seen. Convergence to a single hypothesis is the normal terminal state, and the algorithm has an internal, checkable criterion for having reached it (the general and specific boundary sets coincide). So "generalise from instances seen" is not a procedure that is intrinsically incapable of terminating - it is one that terminates when the surviving hypothesis set is a singleton, and whose progress is measurable.
    4. Mayo, D.G. 2018. Statistical Inference as Severe Testing: How to Get Beyond the Statistics Wars. Cambridge University Press. - Severity locates warrant in the test, not the origin: a hypothesis passes severely when it agrees with the data AND would with high probability have failed the test had it been false. The four superseded abstractions failed a test the fifth passed; on the severity account that is a real difference in warrant, and it is not erased by a shared generative history. Mayo also supplies the presumption's best foothold, and it is a narrower one than the presumption states: her objection is to after-the-fact hypotheses cooked up to fit the very data that refuted their predecessors, because such hypotheses have not been severely tested by that data. The challenge is therefore to double-counting, not to iteration.
    5. Hajek, A. 2007. "The reference class problem is your problem too." Synthese 156(3): 563-585. - Cuts against the presumption's asymmetry charge rather than for it. Hajek's thesis is that the reference class problem is not a defect peculiar to frequentism but afflicts the classical, logical, propensity and subjectivist interpretations alike. If the problem is universal, its presence in the fifth diagnosis is not a discriminating criticism - it is a condition every generalisation whatever must live with. A standard no candidate can satisfy cannot be used to rank the fifth against the four, and the demand that the replacement demonstrate freedom from it is unsatisfiable in principle. Compare SEP, "Scientific Discovery" (J. Schickore), on the discovery/justification distinction: the reconstruction on which the genesis of a hypothesis is separable from its defence is contested in detail but standard in outline, and the presumption assumes the opposite without argument.

  Strength of challenge: Moderate

  Summary: The presumption's residual caution is sound and nothing located refutes it: no stopping criterion is stated, so "terminal" is asserted rather than shown. But its central inference is challenged from three independent directions. Norton's material theory denies that a procedure confers warrant at all, so procedure-identity cannot entail status-identity; Lakatos describes iterated conjecture-and-refutation as the mechanism by which conjectures genuinely improve, making late position in a refutation series a credential; and Mitchell's version spaces show formally that generalisation from instances converges, contracts monotonically, and possesses an internal termination test. Hajek then removes the asymmetry charge's teeth: the reference-class problem is universal, so its presence in the replacement is not a distinguishing defect. What survives is a much more specific and more useful demand than the presumption makes - not "the fifth is as suspect as the four," but "state the population of page kinds over which 'read the controls' is claimed to hold, and the criterion under which it would be considered general." Mayo supplies the one genuinely sharp worry: if the fifth diagnosis was constructed to fit the four instances that broke its predecessors, it has not been severely tested by them and needs a fresh instance.

  Specific risks: If the presumption is over-read, C2A2 acquires a regress in which no correction can ever be adopted, because every correction is reachable by some generalising procedure and therefore inherits the suspicion - the moral "a generalisation is only as good as its worst-covered instance" becomes self-undermining, since it is itself a generalisation reached from instances. Diagnostic work would stall at permanent provisionality with no mechanism to bank a finding. If the presumption is under-read, "read the controls" is adopted as the covering account without anyone enumerating the page kinds it must cover, and the sixth failure arrives as another surprise - the specific failure mode being an unstated reference class in which the abstraction is general over the instances happened to be seen and silent on the rest.

  Mitigations available: Adopt the version-space discipline rather than the verdict: write down the population (the enumerable set of page kinds the roster reader must handle), treat each new page kind as an eliminating instance, and declare terminality only when the hypothesis set is a singleton over that stated population - which is the in-house adjunct already queued for this item. Apply Mayo's severity test by finding at least one page kind NOT among the instances that produced the fifth diagnosis and checking whether "read the controls" predicts its behaviour in advance; this is the only move that discriminates a genuine generalisation from a fitted one. Follow Norton in stating the material fact claimed to license the step (e.g. "every page kind in this system renders its state through a controls region"), because that claim, unlike the procedure, is directly falsifiable. Retain the moral, but apply it symmetrically and constructively: name the worst-covered instance of the NEW abstraction and go look at it.

  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-566
    Strongest counterargument (against the presumption): The presumption commits a genetic inference - it grades a claim by the procedure that produced it rather than by the tests it has survived - and the philosophy of induction has rejected exactly this move. Norton's material theory holds that no schema confers warrant, so two inferences sharing a surface procedure need not share a standing; warrant comes from local facts, which differ between the fourth and fifth diagnoses even though the method of arriving at them did not. Lakatos and Mitchell then supply the positive account the presumption lacks: iterated elimination is the standard mechanism by which conjectures genuinely converge, the hypothesis set contracts monotonically as counterexamples arrive, and late position in the series is earned rather than accidental. The presumption's asymmetry charge is also unfair on its own terms, because the defect it points at - an unspecified reference class - is, per Hajek, universal to every probabilistic and inductive claim whatever, so it cannot discriminate the fifth diagnosis from any possible replacement. Insisting otherwise makes the moral self-undermining, since "a generalisation is only as good as its worst-covered instance" is itself a generalisation from instances and would have to disqualify itself first.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: the population of page kinds the roster reader must cover is enumerable and enumerated; "read the controls" is licensed by a stated material fact about that population rather than by the run of instances that suggested it; and the fifth diagnosis makes a correct advance prediction about at least one page kind that was not among the instances used to reach it.
    How to test: enumerate the remaining page kinds. For each, record in advance what "read the controls" predicts, then check. Two outcomes discriminate cleanly. If it predicts correctly on kinds absent from the original four failures, the presumption's parity claim is refuted and the fifth diagnosis has passed a severe test in Mayo's sense. If it fails on a kind whose controls are absent, virtual, or rendered elsewhere, the abstraction is fitted rather than general, and the worst-covered instance has been located - which is what the moral, applied symmetrically, asks for. A third diagnostic is cheap and worth running first: check whether the four superseded abstractions were each falsified by a DIFFERENT kind of counterexample or by variations on one kind. Genuine eliminative convergence requires the former; the latter would show the series was narrowing within a single reference class and would substantially strengthen the presumption.
