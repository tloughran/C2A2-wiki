## Part 1 — Challenge to (P1): the transfer conditions

### Sources

1. **Deming — the stable-process precondition and the kp rule.** ("Inspection does not improve the quality, nor guarantee quality"; "Inspection is too late. The quality, good or bad, is already in the product"; Point 3, "cease dependence on inspection to achieve quality" — all verified this run via deming.org quotation pages. *Out of the Crisis* itself **canonical, NOT re-verified**. Rigdon, Champ & Rigdon, "Mood's Theorem, Deming's kp Rule, and the Death of Acceptance Sampling," *Quality Engineering* 8(1), 1995 — **title, authorship and venue verified this run via Taylor & Francis listing; paper NOT read**.) — The foundational challenge. The kp rule's own statement of scope is the disqualifier: it "makes the same assumption about stable processes ... that the production process is **in control** and thus has a **constant proportion nonconforming**," and its conclusion is that "for processes in a state of statistical control, **either no inspection or 100% inspection** is economically preferable to a continuous sampling plan." C2A2 is not in statistical control by its own same-day measurements: a 35.5% terminal-failure rate (ASSUMPTION-1068), one queue counted seven times returning seven values (ASSUMPTION-1072), one premise register carrying four live counts (same). A sampling scheme applied to an out-of-control process does not bound anything; the OC curve it promises is a function of a defect rate that is not constant.
2. **ISO 2859-1:1999 scope — the continuing-series condition.** [verified this run via ISO/ANSI catalogue entries and QualityInspection.org's standards comparison — **scope statements at snippet level, standard not read**] — The precise boundary condition. "ISO 2859-1 schemes are intended **primarily to be used for a continuing series of lots**, that is, a series **long enough to allow the switching rules to be applied**. These rules provide protection to the consumer ... should a deterioration in quality be detected." Where lots are isolated, "the switching rules can't be used since there is not a regular flow of batches," and a different standard applies — ISO 2859-2, indexed by *limiting quality* rather than AQL. The AQL machinery is therefore not a general-purpose unattended-acceptance technology: it is a scheme whose consumer protection comes from **switching between inspection levels across a stream of lots**. C2A2 has no lots. There is no lot boundary, no lot size, no defined nonconformity, no AQL, no stream, and so nothing for switching rules to operate on. The mechanism that does the protecting in the cited precedent is absent.
3. **MIL-STD-105E cancellation and its successor.** [verified this run via multiple secondary sources — Quality Magazine "Brief history of ANSI/ASQ Z1.4", Nimonik, Taylor Enterprises, Wikipedia; **DoD notices not read directly**] — A failed-transfer signal the item's framing does not register. MIL-STD-105E was **cancelled in February 1995**; the Notice of Cancellation recommends "MIL-STD-1916, *DoD Preferred Methods for Acceptance of Product*, or ANSI/ASQ Z1.4." The proximate reason was standards-harmonisation policy (OMB Circular A-119 preferring voluntary consensus standards), so the cancellation is not by itself a repudiation — that much should be stated fairly. But the DoD's *preferred* successor is MIL-STD-1916, whose stated orientation is toward prevention-based process control rather than AQL sampling [**canonical, NOT re-verified this run — I did not retrieve MIL-STD-1916's text and this characterisation should be checked before it is relied on**]. Citing MIL-STD-105 as the precedent for unattended acceptance is at minimum citing a standard withdrawn thirty-one years ago, and possibly citing one whose own successor moved away from its method.
4. **PCAOB AU 350 / AS 2315, *Audit Sampling* — population appropriateness and the omitted-items limit.** [verified this run via pcaobus.org — requirement and illustration retrieved; full standard **not read end to end**] — The most damaging source in this file, because it is one of the item's own three precedents and it disqualifies sampling for C2A2's largest defect class. The requirement: "the auditor should determine that the **population from which he draws the sample is appropriate for the specific audit objective**." The illustration: "**An auditor would not be able to detect understatements of an account due to omitted items by sampling the recorded items.** An appropriate sampling plan for detecting such understatements would involve selecting from a source in which the omitted items are included." ASSUMPTION-1069 records nine of twenty-five runs producing no deliverable and none counted anywhere; ASSUMPTION-1075 records checks that silently skip and read as passes. Those are omitted items in precisely AU 350's sense. **Any acceptance design that samples the fleet's artefacts is, by the standard's own worked example, incapable of detecting the fleet's dominant failure mode.** Sampling is also, in audit doctrine, one procedure among several and is not applied to every objective — the standard is a framework for *when* sampling is appropriate, which is the opposite of a warrant for sampling as a general substitute for attendance.
5. **Lights-out manufacturing qualification prerequisites** (The Fabricator, "When the lights go out: managing the hidden risks of unattended manufacturing"; AMD Machines, "Lights-out manufacturing: reality and requirements"; IMTS automated-factory guide; ATS). [verified this run — snippet level, trade press and vendor material; **no peer-reviewed source**] — The third precedent, and its entry requirements read as a list of what C2A2 lacks. "It requires meticulous upfront planning for process development and validation." "**If quality strategy relies on operators performing visual inspections or making accept/reject decisions, those functions need automated replacements**," with in-process measurement providing "continuous quality verification without human judgment." "**Statistical process control becomes essential in unattended production**, with the system needing to track trends, detect drift, and adjust process parameters or flag issues **before out-of-spec parts accumulate**." "Unattended production only works when process design, controls engineering, and maintenance function as a **single system**." "Implementing unattended manufacturing requires **high degrees of uptime and reliability**." Against that list: C2A2's stall detector did not run, three of its checks fail open, its counting instruments disagree with themselves, and its uptime is 64.5%. Lights-out manufacturing is not a precedent for running unattended without a validity mechanism; it is a precedent for the very large amount of engineering that has to precede running unattended.
6. **Parasuraman & Manzey (2010), "Complacency and Bias in Human Use of Automation: An Attentional Integration," *Human Factors* 52(3).** [verified this run — abstract and summary level; **not read in full**] — The challenge to the fallback. Even a declared acceptance design assumes a human who periodically re-engages meaningfully. The finding is that "automation complacency occurs under conditions of **multiple-task load**," is "found in both naive and expert participants and **cannot be overcome with simple practice**," and that automation bias "**cannot be prevented by training or instructions**." A human returning to an eleven-day backlog is in the highest-load condition the literature describes, which is where complacency is strongest — the specific prediction the item's own timing caveat gestures at, now with a mechanism and a citation.

## Part 2 — Challenge to (P2): the override-rate measurement

### Sources

7. **Signal detection theory — the accuracy/criterion confound.** (Open Encyclopedia of Cognitive Science, "Signal Detection Theory," MIT; Springer, *Experimental Design and the Basics of Statistics* ch. 2.) [verified this run — snippet level] — The formal statement of the confound the queue already suspects, and it is worse than stated. "The percentage of correct responses **confounds decision criterion and discriminability**. For a certain performance level, say 75%, we cannot tell whether there is high discriminability and a suboptimal criterion or an optimal criterion with a lower discriminability." "Two people with **identical perceptual ability** can produce very different numbers of correct and incorrect answers simply because one is cautious and the other is eager." Override rate is a single scalar produced by a decision process; it cannot separate *the reviewer's ability to detect defects* from *the reviewer's threshold for acting*, and it cannot separate either from the underlying defect rate. Three unknowns, one number. The queue's hypothesis "near zero means ratification" and the alternative "near zero means a good process" are two of at least three explanations, and the third — a stringent reviewer facing a moderate defect rate but a high action threshold — is indistinguishable from both.
8. **Feinstein & Cicchetti (1990), "High agreement but low kappa: I. The problems of two paradoxes," *J Clin Epidemiol* 43:543–549; Cicchetti & Feinstein (1990), "II. Resolving the paradoxes," 43:551–558.** [verified this run — citations, venue and pagination confirmed; papers **not read in full**] — The degenerate regime. "The expected-agreement-by-chance term (Pe) approaches 1 when prevalence is extreme (either >0.95 or <0.05), marking a **degenerate-marginal zone in which kappa becomes uninformative**." A near-zero override rate *is* the extreme-prevalence case. Precisely in the regime the fleet proposes to measure, chance-corrected agreement statistics stop carrying information — so the measurement is least informative exactly where the item expects it to be decisive.
9. **Priest & Klein (1984), "The Selection of Disputes for Litigation," *J Legal Studies*; and the reversal-rate literature.** (Lee & Klerman, "The Priest-Klein Hypotheses: Proofs and Generality," *International Review of Law and Economics*, 2016; Klerman & Lee, "The Selection of Disputes at Forty," *Yale J. on Regulation*; Emory Law Journal Online, "Why appeals courts rarely reverse lower courts.") [verified this run — citations and summaries; primaries **not read in full**] — The selection problem, and the closest structural analogue available. Priest and Klein's result is that the cases reaching adjudication are not a random sample of disputes but those "close to the decision standard," so outcome rates over the adjudicated set are governed by the *selection process*, not by the quality of the underlying population. The corollary is stated directly in the appellate literature: "**reversal rate is an imperfect proxy for quality**," and roughly 90% of appellate decisions affirm, which "further complicates their use as a reliable quality metric." C2A2's override rate has exactly this structure: the items that reached Tom were routed there by some upstream escalation rule, so the override rate measures **the routing rule's calibration**, not the review gate's value. Change the escalation trigger and the override rate moves with nothing else changing.
10. **Alert-override rates in clinical decision support — Wilson et al. (2026), "Alert fatigue measurement in clinical decision support: a systematic review," PubMed 42148822** (with supporting studies: Swiss Medical Weekly semi-automated CDS evaluation; JMIR Medical Informatics, "Appropriateness of alerts and physicians' responses," 2022). [verified this run — abstract, PDF summary and related-article level; **primary review not read in full**] — The field that has actually tried this measurement, at scale, for two decades, and its verdict. Override rates of **49–96%** are routine. The review's finding is that "alert fatigue is **poorly defined**," that "the most common alert metrics were quantity, **override rate**, and acceptance rate," and — decisively — that only 13.6% of studies "reported **inappropriate** override rates as a component of an operational definition." The recommendation is to abandon raw override rate in favour of "a significant, sustained decrease in **appropriate** alert response rates from an established baseline," because "**not all overrides are inappropriate — the appropriateness of the override itself matters more than the mere fact that an alert was overridden.**" This is the finding that breaks the item's operational proposal: **to interpret an override rate you must adjudicate, case by case, whether each override was appropriate — which is exactly the human adjudication the measurement was designed to make unnecessary.** The measurement is not attendance-free. It is attendance-deferred.

### Strength of challenge: Strong

### Summary

The reframe is the best thing in the day's record and nothing I found challenges it: the eleven dark days did not remove a validity mechanism, they revealed that none was specified. What does not survive is the pair of operational conclusions drawn from it. All three cited precedents carry entry conditions, and C2A2 fails each of them on evidence filed the same day. Acceptance sampling assumes a process in statistical control with a constant fraction nonconforming — Deming's kp rule says so explicitly, and says that for such a process the optimum is inspect-all or inspect-none, never a sample — while C2A2's own instruments returned seven different counts of one queue and a 35.5% run-termination rate. ISO 2859-1's consumer protection comes from switching rules applied across a *continuing series of lots*; C2A2 has no lot, no lot size, no defined nonconformity and no AQL, so the protective mechanism has nothing to act on. MIL-STD-105E was cancelled in 1995 and the DoD's preferred replacement points toward process control rather than AQL sampling. AU 350 is the precedent that does the most damage, because its own worked example states that omitted items cannot be detected by sampling recorded items — and omitted items are precisely what ASSUMPTION-1069 and ASSUMPTION-1075 describe. Lights-out manufacturing, read at its own requirements rather than as a slogan, is a list of prerequisites — automated replacement of every human accept/reject decision, in-process measurement, SPC to catch drift before out-of-spec output accumulates, high uptime — that C2A2 does not currently meet at any point. The measurement fares no better. Override rate is a single number over three unknowns: signal detection theory shows that accuracy confounds sensitivity with criterion; the kappa paradoxes show that agreement statistics become uninformative exactly in the low-prevalence regime the item expects to encounter; and Priest–Klein selection means the rate describes the escalation *filter* rather than the gate, which is why reversal rate is an acknowledged bad proxy for lower-court quality. Worst of all for the proposal's stated virtue, the one field that has measured override rates at scale concluded that the raw rate is uninterpretable and that what matters is whether each override was *appropriate* — a judgement requiring case-by-case adjudication. The measurement therefore does not evade attendance; it defers it, and it will return a number that reads decisive while resolving nothing.

### Specific risks

**The precise risk is a confident wrong answer on a HIGH-priority item.** If the override rate comes back near zero and the fleet reads it as "the review gate was ratification," it will have licensed a declared acceptance design on a statistic that is equally consistent with a good process, a cautious reviewer, a well-calibrated escalation filter, or a low base rate — and the kappa paradoxes say this is the regime where such statistics carry least information. The consequence is an architecture change justified by a measurement that cannot bear it.

If a declared acceptance design is adopted without the transfer conditions, the fleet acquires the *form* of unattended validity — a written acceptance policy — over a process that is out of statistical control, with no lot, no defect definition, and no in-process sensing. The policy will pass. Everything will pass. That is what an acceptance scheme does to a population whose defect rate it cannot measure.

If sampling is chosen as the mechanism, AU 350's illustration predicts the specific blind spot: the design will sample artefacts and will therefore never see the nine runs that produced none or the pairs whose checks silently skipped. The fleet's largest known defect class would be formally out of scope of its new validity mechanism.

There is also a timing risk the item half-anticipates. Its caveat — measure before adjudicating the backlog — is correct but insufficient, because Parasuraman & Manzey predict that a human returning to an eleven-day backlog is in the maximum-load condition where complacency is strongest and cannot be trained away. Whatever override rate that session produces is a measurement of a degraded reviewer, and the item's own prediction of a "false all-clear" now has a mechanism.

Finally: ASSUMPTION-1070 records escalations discharged by agents after being raised. If any of the last attended period's escalations were superseded before Tom saw them, the override denominator already contains items whose status changed, and no field records it.

### Mitigations available

(a) **Seed defects.** The only way to get a denominator that means anything is to inject items of *known* status into the population at a known rate and measure what the gate catches. This is standard practice in adjacent fields (planted-error testing, red-team probes, salted audit items) and it converts the review gate from an unmeasurable process into one with a measurable detection rate and a real d′. It is the single change that would make an override statistic interpretable, and it needs no attendance to set up.
(b) **Report override rate as three numbers, never one.** Items escalated; items overridden; items overridden *and later shown wrong*. The third requires adjudication, and saying so honestly is better than a single scalar that implies it does not.
(c) **Define the lot before invoking lot-based precedents.** If acceptance sampling is to be used at all, name the lot (a day's synthesis pairs? a batch of proposals?), the lot size, the nonconformity definition, and the AQL. If any of the four cannot be named, ISO 2859-1's own scope statement says its schemes do not apply, and the fleet should stop citing them.
(d) **Establish statistical control first.** Deming's precondition is not optional and it is measurable: run the counting instruments repeatedly and see whether they agree with themselves. ASSUMPTION-1072 and ASSUMPTION-1073 say they currently do not. Until they do, no acceptance scheme's operating characteristic is meaningful.
(e) **Take the lights-out list literally as a checklist.** Automated replacement for every human accept/reject function; in-process measurement; SPC with drift detection; uptime. Score C2A2 against those four. The score is the honest statement of how far the precedent is from applying, and it is a better deliverable than a policy document.
(f) **Design the measurement for the tail you can interpret.** A *high* override rate would be informative — it is inconsistent with pure ratification — whereas a low one is not. Frame the test as a one-sided check rather than a two-way discriminator, and say in advance what a null result will and will not license.
(g) **Do not schedule the measurement into the return session.** Reconstruct it from the historical record of the last attended period instead, and if that is impossible, note that the number could not be obtained rather than obtaining a degraded one.

## STEELMAN

**Item:** ASSUMPTION-1077

**Strongest counterargument:** The insight is genuinely the best thing in the record and the two things built on top of it are unsound. Take the precedents in turn, at their own words rather than as a list. Acceptance sampling's whole apparatus rests on a process in statistical control with a constant fraction nonconforming — that is the stated premise of Deming's kp rule, and the rule's conclusion is that for such a process you should inspect *all* or *none*, never a sample. C2A2 measured one queue seven times on one day and got seven answers, and loses 35.5% of its runs. Nothing about it is in control, and an operating-characteristic curve over an unstable defect rate describes nothing. ISO 2859-1 says on its face that its schemes are "intended primarily to be used for a continuing series of lots ... long enough to allow the switching rules to be applied," because the switching rules are where the consumer protection lives; for isolated lots you need a different standard entirely. C2A2 has no lot, no lot size, no nonconformity definition and no AQL, so there is nothing to switch on and nothing being protected. MIL-STD-105E has been cancelled since February 1995 and the DoD's preferred replacement points toward prevention rather than AQL sampling. And then AU 350, which is the item's own citation and which contains a sentence that reads like it was written about this fleet: *an auditor would not be able to detect understatements due to omitted items by sampling the recorded items.* ASSUMPTION-1069 is nine runs that produced nothing and were counted nowhere. ASSUMPTION-1075 is checks that skip silently and read as passes. Those are the omitted items, and every artefact-sampling acceptance design is structurally blind to them. Lights-out manufacturing is the same story: read its qualification requirements and they are a list of preconditions — automated replacement for every human accept/reject decision, in-process measurement, SPC that catches drift before out-of-spec output accumulates, high uptime — of which C2A2 currently has none. The precedent is not "they ran unattended, so you can"; it is "here is what they had to build before they could." Now the measurement. Override rate is one number standing in front of three unknowns — the reviewer's ability to spot a defect, the reviewer's threshold for acting, and the actual defect rate — and signal detection theory has been clear for seventy years that a scalar accuracy figure cannot separate discriminability from criterion. The kappa paradoxes add that when prevalence is extreme, which is what a near-zero override rate *is*, chance-corrected agreement collapses into uninformativeness. Priest and Klein add that the items reaching adjudication are selected by a filter, so the rate reports on the escalation trigger rather than on the gate — which is why appellate reversal rate is a known-bad proxy for trial-court quality. And the field that has actually run this experiment for twenty years, on tens of millions of alerts, reached a verdict: override rates run 49–96%, the raw rate is uninterpretable, and what matters is whether each override was *appropriate* — a case-by-case judgement. So the measurement's headline property, that it needs no attendance, is false. It needs exactly the attendance it was designed to replace, only later and with a number in hand that will look like an answer.

**What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe:** For the transfer: a defined lot with a defined nonconformity and a measurable defect rate; a process demonstrably in statistical control, which today means the counting instruments agreeing with themselves; an automated substitute for every accept/reject judgement the human was making; and in-process sensing that detects drift before defective output accumulates. All four are testable and none currently holds. For the measurement: seeded defects at a known rate, so the gate has a detection rate rather than an agreement rate; a reported denominator that distinguishes items *escalated* from items *reaching* the human; and an explicit, pre-registered statement of what a low rate will and will not be taken to license. Absent seeding, the honest position is that the value of the review gate is **unmeasured and not currently measurable**, which is a fine thing to write down and much safer than a scalar that reads decisive.

**How to test:** **One, and it is the whole ballgame:** seed twenty items of known defect status into the next review population and measure how many the gate catches. That gives a detection rate, a false-alarm rate, and — for the first time — a d′ that separates the reviewer's ability from the reviewer's threshold. It needs no attendance to prepare and it is the only design in this file that answers the question actually being asked. **Two, before anything else is decided:** score C2A2 against the four lights-out prerequisites and against ISO 2859-1's four definitional requirements (lot, lot size, nonconformity, AQL). Eight checkboxes, one sitting, no attendance. If most are empty — and on the same day's evidence most are — then the precedents are aspirational and REVISE-326 should say so. **Three:** re-derive the last attended period's override rate under two different escalation-trigger definitions. If the number moves substantially, Priest–Klein selection is confirmed by measurement and the statistic is shown to be reporting on the routing rule. **Four:** audit the last attended period's escalation set against ASSUMPTION-1070's supersession pattern. Any item resolved by an agent between escalation and review is already contaminating the denominator, and nothing currently marks them.

## SYSTEMIC-RISK-FLAG

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Affected items:** ASSUMPTION-1068, ASSUMPTION-1069, ASSUMPTION-1075, ASSUMPTION-1077 (peripherally ASSUMPTION-1070)
**Common vulnerability:** **Every instrument these four items propose draws its sample from the set of runs, artefacts or decisions that reached a recording surface — a frame generated by the very process the instrument is meant to audit.** 1068's watcher runs on the plane it watches; 1069 would count deliverables produced by runs that survived to produce them; 1075's checks report only on pairs where the check did not silently skip; 1077's override rate is computed over exactly the items an upstream filter routed to the human. In each case the failures of interest never entered the frame — and 1077 is the item where this matters most, because it is the one proposing to change the architecture on the strength of such a number.
**Literature basis:** PCAOB **AU 350 / AS 2315** — "an auditor would **not** be able to detect understatements of an account due to **omitted items** by sampling the **recorded** items. An appropriate sampling plan for detecting such understatements would involve selecting from a source in which the omitted items are included" [verified this run]. **Priest & Klein (1984)** — outcome rates over an adjudicated set are governed by the selection process; "reversal rate is an imperfect proxy for quality" [verified this run, snippet level]. **Wilson et al. (2026)**, alert-fatigue systematic review — raw override rate is not an adequate operationalisation; appropriateness must be judged case by case [verified this run, abstract level]. **LOPA / CCPS** — credit only one protection layer where common cause applies [verified this run, snippet level].
**Risk level:** Critical
**Recommendation:** No instrument in this group should be built, and **REVISE-326 should not be actioned**, until its **sampling frame** is stated and shown to originate outside the audited process. Two qualifying frames already exist and cost almost nothing: the **launchd schedule** (for 1068 and 1069, giving the set of runs that *should* have occurred) and **seeded defects** (for 1077, giving a population of known status). Without one of these, each instrument will report faithfully on the subset of the system that was working well enough to be observed, and the fleet will read that as a measurement of the whole.
