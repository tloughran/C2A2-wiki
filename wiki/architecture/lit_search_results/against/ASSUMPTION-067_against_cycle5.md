    METAPHYSICAL / MECHANISTIC LEG — mirror neurons and inter-brain coupling:
    7. Hickok, G. 2014. The Myth of Mirror Neurons: The Real Neuroscience of Communication and Cognition. W.W. Norton. The sustained case that the action-understanding interpretation rests on codependent assumptions that do not survive the evidence, and that the monkey single-unit findings were never replicated in humans — the human "mirror system" rests on indirect TMS, PET and lesion inference.
    8. Quanta Magazine, 2 April 2024, "Overexposure Distorted the Science of Mirror Neurons" (interview/feature on Hickok). Journalism, cited as a marker that the deflationary reading is now the mainstream framing a decade on, not a minority complaint.
    9. "Hyperscanning: from inter-brain coupling to causality." Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, 2024, article 1497034. From within the hyperscanning field: analytical methods "remain heavily reliant on purely correlational approaches, inherently restricting their explanatory power." Inter-brain synchrony as measured does not license claims about a coupled group-level entity.
    10. Shadaydeh, M. et al. 2025. "Directionality of Interpersonal Neural Influence in fNIRS Hyperscanning: Feasibility of Information–Theoretic Causality Analysis in Motor Tasks." European Journal of Neuroscience. Demonstrates that spurious causal relationships arise from shared physiological processes and measurement artifacts — i.e. two brains in the same room doing the same thing will synchronise for reasons that have nothing to do with a corporate substance.
    11. Moen, L.J.K. 2023. "Eliminating Group Agency." Economics & Philosophy 39(1):43–66. The eliminativist case against group-agent realism, arguing the posit is not required. Also relevant: List, C. & Pettit, P. 2011, Group Agency (OUP) — the leading realist defence, which grounds group agency in a FUNCTIONALIST account with attitudes supervening on individual attitudes, and explicitly rejects essentialist conceptions of collective willing. That is a materially different metaphysics from a hylomorphic corporate substance, and the best-developed realism in the field does not deliver what the pairing needs.

A metaphysics cannot be operationalised by an intervention trial in any case; but here the trial is contested and the mechanism is contested too.
