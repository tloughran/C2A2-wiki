  Affected items: PRESUMPTION-794, PRESUMPTION-796 (primary); 793, 795, 797 (secondary)
  Risk level: CRITICAL

  Quality Assurance and Improvement Program works because an external, independent assessor evaluates
  the audit function and reports PAST IT to the board. Accreditation works because ISO/IEC 17011 bodies
  sit OUTSIDE the 17025 laboratories they assess. Metrological traceability terminates in a primary
  standard held by a DIFFERENT INSTITUTION. In every case the regress is broken BY LEAVING THE SYSTEM,
  not by ascending within it.

  PRESUMPTION-794 reports that C2A2's only external assessor — the human — has been absent eleven days,

  external assessor — that another item queued on the same day independently reports is NOT CURRENTLY
  MET. Conversely, 794's most plausible remedy (measure the review gate before restoring it) is itself
  an act of SELF-ASSESSMENT BY THE LAYER UNDER REVIEW, which is precisely the structure PREMISE-144 says
  fails in ways visible only from outside.

  The fleet is at risk of writing a design principle (796) whose validity depends on a resource another
  item on the same day reports at ZERO (794), and of doing so without either item noticing, BECAUSE EACH
  WAS ASSESSED ALONE.

  Literature basis: IIA Global Internal Audit Standards 8.4/12.1 [verified this run]; ISO/IEC 17011 and

  Computer Law & Security Review 45:105681 [verified this run]; Sele, D. & Chugunova, M. (2024),
  PLOS ONE 19(2):e0298037 [verified this run].

  RECOMMENDATION: before any meta-control exemption is adopted as a standing principle, NAME THE

## AXIS 3 — HIGH (raised by 15b on items 787-792; distinct from Axes 1-2)
### The fleet has no KNOWN-ANSWER CONTROL as a shared facility

  Affected items: PRESUMPTION-787, 789, 790, 791, 792 (five of six; 788 excluded as governance rather
  than instrumentation)
  Risk level: HIGH

  In every one of these five items the literature's remedy converged on the same primitive, from five
  unrelated disciplines: RUN THE INSTRUMENT AGAINST A CASE WHOSE CORRECT ANSWER IS ESTABLISHED IN
  ADVANCE, ON THE SAME PATH AS THE REAL READING.

  - 787 — a sentinel queue item / watchdog alert / injected freshness heartbeat: a POSITIVE CONTROL, so
    that "unchanged" is an assertion rather than a silence.
  - 789 — a warm-cache re-run of a failing fixture: a DISCONFIRMING CASE, one the standing explanation
    predicts should pass and which is therefore capable of refuting it.
  - 790 — a defined measurand plus a reference measurement: a DEFINITIONAL CONTROL, so a divergence can
    be classified rather than assumed definitional.
  - 791 — an observed red before the green, or the retained defective page: a NEGATIVE CONTROL, without
    which the passing adversarial test is itself a null reading.
  - 792 — seeded known defects: the ONLY residual-defect estimator that works with a single auditor,
    capture-recapture being structurally unavailable to a self-audited instrument.

  Each item independently reinvents this primitive, NONE OF THEM NAMES IT, and the fleet has no facility
  that provides it. That is why five items with different subject matter, different owning runs and
  different risk gradings all resolve to the same shape of remedy — and why five separate, uncoordinated
  per-item fixes are the likeliest outcome, each of which would be a universal mandatory control of the
  sort SYSTEMIC-RISK-FLAG_2026-08-13 already characterised as degrading into checks recorded-as-performed.

  Literature basis: NFPA 72 circuit supervision and trouble-signal semantics [verified, secondary];
  Prometheus Watchdog / dead-man's-switch and `absent()` [verified]; synthetic canary probes exercising
  a path when real traffic is zero (AWS CloudWatch Synthetics; Microsoft Code-with-Engineering Playbook)
  [verified]; heartbeat-record injection in data-freshness monitoring [verified, practitioner]; Beck's
  mandatory red step [canonical, not re-verified]; the fail-to-pass convention in bug-reproducing-test
  research (Hora, AST 2026) [verified, abstract]; JCGM VIM3/GUM measurand definition and metrological
  compatibility [verified]; defect seeding as the single-inspector fallback where capture-recapture's
  independence assumption fails (Wohlin & Runeson 1995, 2004; Briand et al. 2000) [verified, abstracts].

  RECOMMENDATION: treat KNOWN-ANSWER CONTROL as ONE NAMED, SHARED CAPABILITY rather than as five
  per-item remedies — a facility that lets any instrument declare a case whose answer is fixed and report
  that case's outcome alongside its real output. Three properties the per-item fixes lack: it is CHEAP
  (each control is one sentinel, one seeded fault, or one retained artefact, not a procedure); it is
  SELF-REPORTING, so it does not depend on anyone performing an audit — exactly the degradation path the
  08-13 flag identified; and it is MEASURABLE, because a control that stops being reported is itself an
  observable, whereas a mandated practice that stops being performed is not.

  15b's own note, recorded verbatim in substance: this recommendation IS itself a universal control and
  therefore falls under PRESUMPTION-796's challenge. 15b records the one property that may distinguish
  it — IT EMITS EVIDENCE OF ITS OWN EXECUTION AS A SIDE EFFECT OF THE INSTRUMENT'S NORMAL OUTPUT, so
  "recorded as performed but not executed" is not a reachable state for it — and EXPLICITLY DECLINES to
  answer 796 with it. 15c has carried that candidate property into REVISE-327 alongside 15a's O(1)/O(N)
  synthesis, and has adopted NEITHER.

  15c disposition of this axis: ACCEPTED. PREMISE-159 mints the obligation for the 787 case
  (liveness test of the reader), PREMISE-160 for the 789 case (one disconfirming case), PREMISE-161 for
  the 790 case (stated measurand plus guard band), PREMISE-162 for the 792 case (seeded defects), and
  REVISE-325 names the 791 case as one command against a retained artefact. THE SHARED FACILITY ITSELF
  IS NOT BUILT AND 15c CANNOT BUILD IT — it is an infrastructure decision and is routed to Tom through
  this flag rather than mandated through a premise, which is what Axis 2's acceptance test requires.

  SECONDARY OBSERVATION (cross-cycle, lower confidence, raised by 15b as an observation and not a
