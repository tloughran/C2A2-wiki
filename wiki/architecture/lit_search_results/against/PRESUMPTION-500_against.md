  Sources:
    1. Alasdair MacIntyre, *Three Rival Versions of Moral Enquiry* (Notre Dame Press); and "MacIntyre on Tradition, Rationality, and Relativism" (retrieved 2026-07-20). MacIntyre holds that incommensurable conceptual schemes "do not entail either relativism or perspectivism," that all inquiry is tradition-constituted but rationally adjudicable, and that a tradition in epistemic crisis can be shown by its own standards to be defeated by a rival. He also recommends learning a rival tradition's language as a "second first language." The literature the item nominates as the alternative to convergence in fact supplies a rational-resolution criterion — one tradition's standards being satisfied better by another's resources — which is convergence-shaped.

The most developed behavioural instrument for understanding-without-agreement operationalises passing as producing arguments that **opponents agree with** at or above the in-group rate, and the paper acknowledges that this conflates persuasiveness with genuine understanding. The claimed instrument gap is partly a definitional gap: the leading candidate measures agreement.

  Strength of challenge: Strong (against the evaluation); the surfacing itself is not challenged.

  Summary: The presumption is well surfaced — nothing in the record states convergence as a hypothesis, and its being built into every counter without a stated alternative is a genuine blind spot of exactly the kind 14b exists to find. The challenge is to the item's implicit next move, that the presumed criterion is wrong and needs replacing. Three findings resist that. MacIntyre, cited by the item as the authority for tradition-constituted enquiry, argues that rival traditions *are* rationally adjudicable and that one can defeat another on the other's own terms — an outcome that a convergence detector would register as a hit, so the philosophical authority partly supports the presumption it was recruited against. The ensemble literature, the closest formal analogue for aggregating diverse local optimisers, reports a diversity–accuracy tradeoff with an interior optimum rather than a monotone preference for preserved difference. And the instrument gap is not clean: the best-validated behavioural measure of understanding-without-agreement scores understanding by measuring opponents' agreement, so the alternative signature may not be separable from convergence with any instrument currently available. The honest conclusion is not that convergence is right, but that "convergence versus mutual registration" may be a false dichotomy that no existing instrument can resolve.

  Specific risks: Because this is named as the accelerator's target function, replacing it carries the highest possible cost of error. If convergence is dropped from the Rung-2 detector before a separable alternative exists, the rung becomes unscored — and worse, unfalsifiable, since any dialogue outcome including permanent mutual incomprehension satisfies "preserved local optimization." If the ITT or a relative is adopted as the replacement without noting its agreement confound, the system will believe it has switched criteria while measuring the old one, which is the worst of the three states because it is invisible. And the item's inherited anti-convergence intuition comes from a domain whose originator disclaims cross-level transfer.

  Mitigations available: Do not replace; instrument both and report the pair, as ASSUMPTION-475's in-house test proposes. State the presumption explicitly as a hypothesis — which is the item's real contribution and costs nothing. Adopt MacIntyre's own criterion as a third measurement: can tradition A restate tradition B's position in B's vocabulary and identify B's unresolved epistemic crisis? That is testable, is not agreement, and is not convergence. Where an ITT-style instrument is used, publish the agreement-rate correlation alongside the score.

STEELMAN:
  Item: PRESUMPTION-500
  Strongest counterargument: 14b has correctly found an unstated premise at the centre of the system and then attached to it a verdict the evidence does not support. The item's framing presupposes that convergence and mutual registration are rival success signatures and that the system has been measuring the wrong one — but the philosophical tradition it invokes says rival traditions can be rationally adjudicated, with one shown superior on the other's own terms, which is precisely the kind of outcome a convergence counter registers; the formal literature on aggregating diverse optimisers reports an interior optimum rather than a preference for preserved difference; and the leading instrument for the alternative signature turns out to score opponents' agreement, which means the two signatures may not be empirically separable at all. Meanwhile the intuition driving the whole reframe traces to a bioelectric result whose originator states that intuitions do not transfer reliably across levels and whose framework is under active philosophical criticism in its own field. The strongest form of the challenge is therefore this: the item's value is entirely in the surfacing — "nobody ever stated this as a hypothesis" is true, important, and free to fix — and its risk is entirely in the implied remedy, because removing the only measurable criterion from the accelerator's target function, in favour of one that no available instrument can distinguish from it, converts a possibly-wrong objective into no objective.
  What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: An instrument for mutual registration that demonstrably does not correlate with agreement, and evidence that the two criteria rank real dialogues differently. Absent both, the presumption should be stated and retained, not replaced.
  How to test: Score a set of existing dialogues under a convergence measure, a mutual-registration measure, and an ITT-style measure, and compute the pairwise rank correlations. If mutual registration correlates strongly with convergence, the dichotomy is false and the presumption is harmless. If it does not, the disagreement set — the dialogues the two criteria rank differently — is the whole empirical content of the item and should be inspected by hand before any detector is changed. Additionally, apply MacIntyre's own criterion to the same set as a third, independent measure.
