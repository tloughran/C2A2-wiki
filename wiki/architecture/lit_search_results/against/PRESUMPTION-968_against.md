  STEELMAN:
    Item: PRESUMPTION-968
    Strongest counterargument: The base-rate framing is the wrong tool and demanding it will cost more
      than it returns. A "date error rate" is not a well-defined quantity: the strongest thing the
      retrieved material establishes is that a live web page routinely has no single true date — published,
      modified, inception, and the date of the underlying work can all differ and all be defensible — so
      any measured mismatch rate is an artefact of the convention chosen rather than a property of the
      instrument. Choose `published_time` and franciscanmedia.org is correct; choose the work-date and it
      is twelve years wrong; nothing about the search layer changed between those two verdicts. The
      external literature is worse, not better: Walters & Wilder measured a **generative** failure (a model
      inventing a citation's metadata from parameters) on 2023-vintage models, which is a different
      mechanism from a **retrieval** layer faithfully relaying a page's own wrong declaration, and the
      register itself flags exactly this kind of import as a PREMISE-101 violation. Meanwhile the incident
      framing has a genuine virtue the rate framing lacks: each instance carried a *specific, correctable
      fact* about a *specific source*, and the two corrections were made. A rate corrects nothing — under
      PREMISE-173 it is a sensor with no final element, and the estate's own scope limit on that premise
      says the prescribed remedy in the two best-studied closure gaps is **detection-layer selectivity,
      not more measurement**. The cheap, correct, already-available control is not a base rate at all: it
      is the PREMISE-148(5) narrowing — stop taking *when* from secondary sources. That control works
      identically whether the true rate is 2% or 24%, which is precisely why measuring the rate first is
      the expensive path to the same place.
    What would need to be true for C2A2 to be safe: (a) the narrowing must actually be applied, not merely
      drafted — the steelman's whole force is that it makes the measurement unnecessary, and it has sat
      undone for twenty-six days, so at present the estate has neither the control nor the rate; (b) if
      the rate is measured anyway, the date convention must be declared first or the number means nothing;
      (c) the external figure must not be imported as this estate's expected rate under any framing;
      (d) the ledger must stop recording each instance as novel — three filings of one class, each written
      as if it were the first, is the defect the item is about, and it is independent of whether a rate is
      ever computed. (c) and (d) cost nothing and are compatible with every word of this steelman.
    How to test: run REVISE-342's sweep on a **stratified sample of 30 rather than the full corpus** — the
      cheap version that distinguishes the two positions without paying for the full measurement. Declare
      the convention first. If the mismatch rate comes back near zero, the steelman holds, the instances
      were genuinely isolated, and the narrowing can be applied as cheap insurance. If it comes back near
      the literature's order of magnitude, the incident framing is refuted by the estate's own data and
      PREMISE-148(5)'s safe harbour must be withdrawn rather than narrowed. Thirty is enough to tell 2%
      from 24%; it is not enough to estimate either precisely, and under PREMISE-136 that wide interval is
      the honest thing to report.
