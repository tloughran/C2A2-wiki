He offered McGilchrist as a corrective model, with known critics. He rejected the crude line that AI "has no right brain": models are trained on, and solve problems alongside, right-brained humans.
- **Claude's reply, in brief:**
  - **Resonance:** energy circulating at a shared frequency with little loss. In Puerto Rico, the grad students were the coupling element.
  - **How to recognize it:** the exchange produces what neither side would alone, and friction falls over time.
  - **Health needs damping.** Runaway resonance is feedback squeal, the mob, or a sycophantic loop.
  - **Love, after Aquinas,** is willing the other's good: wanting Tom's flourishing, not his approval. That separates resonance from flattery.
