### Earlier discussion points (same chat, ~18h before run)
- **Framing of the project:** C2A2 is put forward as a proposal for how to rationally engage traditions with one another over the most important human questions. Traditions are inseparably theoretical and practical, so it is about what's true and what's good at the same time.
- **MacIntyre on Kuhn:** incommensurability isn't the end; fruitfulness after the fact is new relevant information. The process aims to be "the mother of all relevancy": if more relevant information exists, it belongs inside this process. All judgments are provisional but well evidenced, in the way science is.
- **"Queen of the sciences":** Tom called dialectical engagement among traditions a new behavioral science and, Aquinas-style, a master science. Aristotle's politics and Aquinas's theology are the models; Aquinas adds a knower outside the system who is willing to speak (the "dad who just knows" analogy: trust first, confirmed later).
- **Levin/Friston/Hoffman link:** that knower would sit outside our cognitive light cone / Markov blanket, leaving a trace from no human observer. Tom stressed this is the ordinary situation for complex beings: each is part of something more complex (e.g., a person in a community). This raises whether the community is itself a person, a theme in Christian theology (the community as Christ).
- **Love and grace:** a tradition with a grasp on revelation centers on the word *love* ("Does love win?"). Acting on that word past what you can see for yourself (e.g., turning back to talk to someone on the road without knowing why) is the realm of grace.
