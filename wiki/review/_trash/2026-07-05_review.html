BERNARDO KASTRUP · PODCAST · 2026-06-23
Overwhelming evidence for purpose, but is it instinctive or planned? — Jude Currivan & Bernardo Kastrup, exploring a living, evolving universe
https://www.withrealityinmind.com/instinct-or-plan/
SUMMARY
A structured dialogue between Kastrup and cosmologist Jude Currivan (whole-world/"cosmic hologram" physics) on whether the evidence of purpose in a "living, evolving universe" reflects an instinctive, self-organizing tendency of universal consciousness or a planned teleology. Kastrup presses his analytic-idealist reading — that apparent cosmic purpose is the intrinsic behavior of mind-at-large, not an externally imposed design — against Currivan's cosmological framing of an informationally self-realizing universe.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the second Currivan–Kastrup dialogue of 2026 (after "A Worldview for Global Change," 27 Jan 2026) and the most recent item in Kastrup's own With Reality in Mind series. It forces the instinct-vs-plan distinction onto the program's explicit agenda: whether analytic idealism entails any teleology at all, and if so, whether that teleology is immanent (a habit/tendency of consciousness) rather than designed. It extends the "evolving laws as patterns of dissociation" thread already captured (Rovelli/Smolin, PRS from 2026-04-29) toward cosmology.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P The universe shows what looks like overwhelming evidence of purpose and directional evolution — does analytic idealism have to posit a plan (teleological design), or can it account for apparent purpose without one?
R Kastrup's immanent-teleology move: purpose is the intrinsic, "instinctive" self-organizing tendency of universal consciousness (mind-at-large), not a goal imposed from outside; paired with Currivan's cosmology of an informationally self-realizing, evolving universe as an independent route to the same directionality.
S Apparent cosmic purpose is reframed as the natural behavior of a conscious cosmos unfolding its own dispositions — dissolving the design-vs-blind-chance dichotomy by locating directionality inside the one universal subject rather than in an external planner.
Evidence: Full dialogue "Overwhelming evidence for purpose, but is it instinctive or planned? … exploring a living, evolving universe," recorded 2026-06-23 in Kastrup's With Reality in Mind series.
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
Strong multi-tradition resonance. (1) **Wolfram**: an evolving, self-realizing universe with directional structure parallels the ruliad's observer-traversed computational unfolding — instinct-vs-plan maps onto whether the ruliad has intrinsic directionality. (2) **Levin**: immanent purpose as the "instinctive" tendency of mind-at-large echoes Levin's goal-directedness at every scale (the two were flagged as broadly aligned but divergent on discrete-vs-continuous nesting; this cosmological-purpose framing is a fresh test of that divergence). (3) **McGilchrist**: Jude Currivan is a shared interlocutor — she co-presents with McGilchrist at the Sheldonian "Good, Beautiful and True" event — making Currivan a concrete cross-tradition bridge node between the Kastrup and McGilchrist programs. (4) **Fredrickson/community**: the "living universe with purpose" framing bears on the individual-vs-collective-mind question. Flag for the master agent: Currivan is emerging as a bridge figure appearing in both the Kastrup and McGilchrist orbits.

SUMMARY
A 43-minute curated "masterclass in human nature" from the Freedom Pact channel, assembling McGilchrist alongside Robert Greene (power, strategy) and Robert Sapolsky (behavioral neuroscience, determinism) among others. McGilchrist's contribution places his participatory, right-hemisphere account of the person directly against a reductive-materialist and a strategic-instrumental account of human nature in the same frame.
WHY IT MATTERS
The value is the juxtaposition: McGilchrist's hemisphere theory is here staged against Sapolsky's hard-determinist neuroscience and Greene's instrumental view of human behavior — the exact rivals his program most needs to be read against. Capturing it lets the wiki hold a canonical instance of McGilchrist's view meeting reductive materialism on shared ground.

CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Speculative
P Is human nature best explained by reductive neurobiological determinism (behavior as caused-through-and-through) or by a participatory account in which attention and meaning are constitutive of the person?
R The multi-thinker masterclass format juxtaposing McGilchrist's right-hemisphere participatory realism with Sapolsky's determinism and Greene's strategic instrumentalism — three incompatible construals of "human nature" placed side by side.
S McGilchrist's move that reductive accounts describe left-hemisphere re-presentations of the person (decomposed, manipulable) while missing the right-hemisphere whole from which agency and meaning arise; determinism is a category-level artifact of the analytic mode, not a discovery about persons.

Thinking Class — John Gillam and Dr Iain McGilchrist: There Is A Great Deal Of Ruin In The Western World. This Is Why
https://channelmcgilchrist.com/thinking-class-john-gillam-and-dr-iain-mcgilchris…
SUMMARY
A 43-minute long-form dialogue between McGilchrist and John Gillam (Thinking Class) diagnosing the causes of Western civilizational decline through the hemisphere framework. McGilchrist argues that the "ruin" in the Western world is not an accident of policy but the predictable outcome of a culture that has handed authority to the left hemisphere's abstracted, manipulative mode of attention, severing itself from the right hemisphere's living, contextual grasp of reality.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is a fresh (June 2026) author-direct application of hemisphere theory to civilizational diagnosis, extending the "cause variable, not co-symptom" framing McGilchrist developed with Nate Hagens into an explicit account of decline. It is a new dialogue partner (Gillam) and a new venue, adding to the program's track record of cultural-pathology diagnosis.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P Why is the West in a state of visible civilizational decline, and why do internal reform efforts fail to arrest it?
R McGilchrist's hemisphere-as-cultural-diagnosis: left-hemisphere dominance ("ruin") is self-reinforcing because a left-hemisphere culture cannot diagnose its own pathology from within — it lacks the right-hemisphere vantage needed to see the whole it has fragmented.
S Decline is reframed as a structural attentional condition rather than a set of separable policy failures; remedy requires re-grounding attention in the right hemisphere's mode, not left-hemisphere fixes applied to left-hemisphere problems.
Evidence: Full 43:12 dialogue titled "There Is A Great Deal Of Ruin In The Western World. This Is Why," published on McGilchrist's own channel 2026-06-14.
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
McGilchrist's claim that a left-hemisphere culture cannot self-diagnose is a strong C2A2 signal: it is a claim that a *tradition skewed toward one mode of attention* is structurally blind to what a rival mode discloses — directly relevant to the "second-first-language" thesis that a tradition needs an outside vantage to see itself. Connects to McGilchrist's own left/right divide as a model for tradition-vs-tradition dialogue (a flagged core C2A2 signal), and resonates with Rohr's contemplative critique of purely analytic religion and Stump's Thomistic account of knowledge that exceeds propositional decomposition.
SEAN CARROLL · PODCAST · 2026-06-29
Mindscape 359 | Solo: Theories of Dark Energy
https://www.preposterousuniverse.com/podcast/2026/06/29/359-solo-theories-of-dar…
SUMMARY
The second of two connected solo episodes on cosmic acceleration (the sequel to Mindscape 358 on vacuum energy / the cosmological constant, already captured as PRS-45/46). The cosmological constant currently gives a perfectly good account of the observed acceleration, but it may not be the right one — and showing that would be "yet another foundational discovery." Carroll lays out what it takes to build a plausible theory of *dynamical* dark energy: the particle-physics ingredients (light scalar fields with a slowly varying equation of state), possible experimental tests that could distinguish dynamical dark energy from a constant Λ, and the rival option of modifying gravity rather than adding a new energy source to Einstein's equations.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is Carroll speaking directly in his home domain (cosmology, philosophy of physics) and sharpens the boundary-of-physics theme from PRS-45/46: he frames a live, testable fork in fundamental physics (dynamical dark energy vs. cosmological constant vs. modified gravity) as a candidate foundational discovery, exactly the kind of "tangible explanatory gain over established physics" his Bayesian theory-confirmation standard (PRS-03) demands before a novel framework is accepted.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P The cosmological constant fits the acceleration data but may not be the correct explanation; physics needs a way to construct and empirically distinguish a *dynamical* dark energy alternative in which the vacuum energy density evolves over cosmic time.
R Dynamical-dark-energy model-building — light scalar fields (quintessence-type) with a slowly rolling equation of state w(z) near but not exactly -1, constrained by particle-physics naturalness and targeted by equation-of-state / expansion-history measurements.
S A program for inventing plausible dynamical dark energy theories that make predictions distinguishable from a constant Λ, so that detecting w ≠ -1 (or its variation) would count as a foundational discovery rather than a parameter refinement.
Evidence: Episode synopsis — "what is required to invent a plausible theory of dynamical dark energy… includes considerations from particle physics, possible experimental tests."
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — Medium
P Cosmic acceleration admits two structurally different explanations — a new source of energy in the stress-energy tensor versus a modification of gravity itself — and the two are easy to conflate without a principled criterion for telling them apart.
R The "new energy source vs. modify-gravity" dichotomy Carroll draws explicitly, treating modified-gravity accounts as a distinct branch rather than a variant of dark energy.
S A framing in which the acceleration puzzle becomes an empirically adjudicable fork — different observational signatures for a dark-energy field versus modified gravity — keeping poetic naturalism honest by locating meaning-of-the-data disputes in testable structure rather than interpretation.
Evidence: Episode synopsis — "the option that we should modify gravity rather than adding a new source of energy to the equations."
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
Strong pairing with **[[09_arkanihamed_agent]]**: dark energy / the cosmological constant is the same vacuum-energy naturalness crisis that sits under Arkani-Hamed's hierarchy-problem and positive-geometry-cosmology work (cosmohedron, kinematic flow) — both traditions are asking what the smallness and possible dynamics of Λ demand of a fundamental theory. Secondary signal to **[[10_wolfram_agent]]**: the "modify the dynamics vs. add a new ingredient" fork echoes Wolfram's stance that apparent new physics can be a feature of the underlying computational rule. This continues the PRS-45/46 (vacuum energy) arc and is the direct sequel Carroll promised in Mindscape 358.
