DONALD HOFFMAN · PAPER · 2026-06-02
A Science of Reality: Observers, Interfaces, and Traces (The Trace Institute Whitepaper)
https://traceinstitute.org/papers/TRACE_INSTITUTE_WHITE_PAPER.pdf
SUMMARY
The Trace Institute's founding whitepaper, dated 2026-06-02, is the first single-document statement of the full research program: a four-step chain running Interface Theory of Perception → Conscious Agent Theory → Trace Logic → **Recursive Trace Logic (RTL)**, followed by a four-domain research roadmap (physics, neuroscience, metaphysics, technology). It gives the explicit matrix formula for the trace kernel Q_A, defines the conscious agent in streamlined form as C = ⟨(X, 𝒳), Q, n⟩, and — most importantly — publishes **eight named, falsifiable physics conjectures** that the program commits to proving.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the canonical program document for the Hoffman-Prakash / TRACE phase of the tradition, and the wiki currently has the TRACE Institute recorded only as an organizational entity (PROP-2026-05-05-005) with no capture of its actual scientific content. It also supersedes the April 2026 Levin-hosted talk as the authoritative statement of trace logic: the whitepaper introduces the Policy Level / Meta-policy Level hierarchy as the definition of RTL, which the existing PRS-08 records only informally as "recursive trace logic."
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P The trace-logic program has been criticized as unfalsifiable metaphysics because it has never committed to specific, checkable physical predictions.
R The Eight Conjectures (Table 1) — a numbered, domain-tagged list binding RTL to concrete derivation targets: (1) Minkowski space as the n→∞ limit of Markov n-cycles; (2) curved spacetime from non-cyclic chains; (3) cosmology from long samples of trace chains; (4) Planck-scale breakdown from energy growth with trace state-count; (5) quantum wavefunctions and the Born rule from asymptotics of enhanced Markov chains; (6) Standard Model particles as classes of Markov chains; (7) scattering amplitudes with ABHY associahedra as subpolytopes of the Markov polytope; (8) entanglement from disjoint traces of an ergodic chain.
S The program converts its own falsifiability problem into a published research contract — each conjecture either proves or fails on ordinary mathematical grounds, independent of anyone's intuitions about consciousness.
Evidence: "Proving these conjectures is a prime focus of the scientific program." (Table 1 and §5.1)
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — High
P The original conscious-agent formalism decomposed agents into perception, decision, and action kernels — an assumption that smuggles an internal/external split back into a framework meant to dissolve it.
R The streamlined agent C = ⟨(X, 𝒳), Q, n⟩ — a measurable space of potential experiences plus a single Markov kernel and a step counter, with the perception/decision/action decomposition explicitly withdrawn.
S The framework drops the dualist residue in its own foundations; the whitepaper compares the move to the limits on decomposability of Hilbert space in quantum theory, and notes the 2014 Hoffman-Prakash formalism is recoverable as a "sufficiently decomposed variant."
Evidence: Footnote 1, §3: "the present formalism adopts a more cautious stance regarding the decomposability of kernels and state spaces into respective internal and external constituents."
CANDIDATE PRS 3 — High
P Trace logic describes transitions *within* a fixed state space, but perception plainly involves changes to what the space of possible experiences even is — coarsening, fine-graining, combining with other agents.
R Recursive Trace Logic — a hierarchy of Markov kernels governing changes to the state space itself. At each step the process either transitions within X_n or "steps up" to the **Policy Level**, a kernel over admissible restrictions/expansions of X_n; a third **Meta-policy Level** is a kernel over policies, and the hierarchy extends to arbitrary depth.
S A formal account of an observer that reconstructs its own interface, with the trace order constraining which state-space changes are legal.
Evidence: §4, worked two-level and three-level example.
CANDIDATE PRS 4 — Medium
P Active inference derives perception from surprise minimization but presupposes a material substrate, an internal/external Markov-blanket split, and a true world state.
R RTL's surprise, defined as the difference between beliefs based on knowledge of the larger state space and the outcome realized on the trace.
S Surprise minimization is recovered **without** substrate, without a rigid internal-external decomposition, and without any true observable world state — positioning RTL as strictly weaker in assumptions than active inference while reproducing its dynamics.
Evidence: §4: "unlike the standard active inference models, in the case of RTL this minimization of surprise is defined without the assumption of a material substrate..."
CANDIDATE PRS 5 — Medium
P The framework's non-dual ontology aligns with contemplative traditions, but importing contemplative claims as data would be methodologically indefensible.
R The stated metaphysics protocol — "We do not import contemplative claims as data; we use the mathematics to clarify which questions are well-posed and how answers might be distinguished."
S A worked test case: whether the tension between Buddhist *anatman* and Advaita's *ayam atma brahma* is a substantive soteriological conflict or a semantic one, to be settled by whether the two correspond to distinguishable trace-lattice structures.
Evidence: §5.3.
CANDIDATE PRS 6 — Speculative
P Deep-learning architectures are enormously inefficient relative to brains, and attempts to borrow biological efficiency have largely failed.
R "Microchip epistemology" — treating the hidden agent network as a distributed, non-spatiotemporally constrained computational reservoir with tunable read-outs and small-world topology.
S A proposed computational paradigm claimed to be as transformative as attention architectures, motivated by the diagnosis that borrowing from the brain fails *because* the brain is an interface representation, not ground reality.
Evidence: §5.4, "Machine Intelligence"; cites Gauthier et al. 2021 on next-generation reservoir computing.

The whitepaper insists it does not abandon physics but proposes recovering known laws as limiting cases. Conjectures 1-2 (recovering Minkowski and curved spacetime) are precisely what Carroll's poetic naturalism would demand before taking the program seriously; they are now on the record as commitments. - **Kastrup** — §2 and §5.3 give a non-dual ontology reached by mathematical rather than argumentative route; the whitepaper cites Chalmers' "realist idealist" positioning. This is a direct, named invitation to the Thomistic tradition, and the framework leaves the question open rather than settling it. - **C2A2 methodological note** — §5.2's "dark matter of human experience," including "linguistic narratives and experiential point-of-views... not ultimately physically situated in spacetime measurements, but nevertheless amenable to rigorous probabilistic inference," describes almost exactly the object C2A2's inter-tradition instrument is built to measure. DONALD HOFFMAN · PAPER · 2024-10 (preprint; cited by the Institute as 2024 and 2025)
Traces of Consciousness (the Trace Chain Theorem paper)
https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202410.1305.v1
SUMMARY
Hoffman, Prakash & Chattopadhyay's **Traces of Consciousness** is the primary source for the Trace Chain Theorem — the result the Trace Institute takes its name from. For any subset A of a Markov kernel's state space, there exists a *unique* trace kernel Q_A giving the effective dynamics seen by an observer restricted to A. Tracing induces a partial order on kernels, and that order induces a logic that is locally Boolean, globally non-Boolean, and provably homomorphic to the Lebesgue logic of probabilistic belief.
WHY IT MATTERS
**This is a gap-fill, not a new discovery.** The wiki already carries trace logic across PRS-07 through PRS-10, but every one of those triplets is sourced either to the April 2026 Levin-hosted talk or — in one case — to a secondary popular explainer at mindbodysolution.org. The tradition's namesake theorem has no primary-source citation in the vault. Given that the Hoffman wiki now leans heavily on trace logic and the whitepaper (PROP-2026-07-21-001) treats the theorem as settled ground, the load-bearing citation should be the paper itself. Flagging a **provenance discrepancy for review**: the Trace Institute's publications page lists this as "Hoffman, D. D., Prakash, C., & Chattopadhyay, S. (2025)" while the whitepaper's own bibliography dates it 2024 (preprint deposited October 2024). The wiki should record the 2024 preprint date with a note, not silently pick one.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P An observer restricted to part of a system sees dynamics that mix the visible states with the influence of hidden ones; there was no canonical account of what dynamics such a restricted observer is entitled to say it is seeing.
R The trace kernel, given in closed form as Q_A = I_A Q [Σ_{k=0}^∞ (I_U Q)^k I_A], where U is the complement of A and I_A, I_U are indicator multiplications — the unique Markovian kernel on A induced by Q.
S A restricted observer's effective dynamics is a well-defined, unique Markov process. Perspectival limitation gets an exact mathematical characterization rather than being treated as noise or error.
Evidence: Theorem restated in the Trace Institute whitepaper §3 with explicit formula, crediting Revuz (1984) and Hoffman et al. (2024).
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — High
P Trace logic needed a connection to established formal work on belief, or it would remain an isolated construction.
R A proof that the trace logic is homomorphic to the Lebesgue logic of probabilistic belief — the logical principle previously identified as the basis of "observer mechanics" (Bennett, Hoffman & Prakash, 1989).
S The trace order is shown to be the same formal object as an existing, independently motivated theory of perception, closing a thirty-five-year loop back to Hoffman's own earlier work.
Evidence: Whitepaper §3, footnote 5.
CANDIDATE PRS 3 — Medium
P Reconciling incompatible observer perspectives is usually treated as a failure of communication or rationality, presuming a common frame must exist.
R The partial order's incomparability structure — "not all kernels are comparable... many pairs are strictly incomparable, and no universal order exists that holds for all kernels across the entire set of possible observations."
S Some pairs of observers are *formally* incomparable. Where no join exists in the trace order, there is no common refinement — a mathematical rather than merely psychological account of perspectival incommensurability.
Evidence: Whitepaper §3. Note this sharpens the wiki's existing Active Research Question 8, which asked precisely for the conditions under which two Markov chains have a join.
CANDIDATE PRS 4 — High
P If a trace is all an observer has access to, what can it infer about the reality generating it?
R The many-to-one structure of tracing — while only a restricted set of larger kernels are valid precursors to a given trace, "any given trace kernel could have been derived from an infinite number of larger, higher-dimensional kernels."
S Underdetermination is formally exact: the information about unobserved states is simply lost, and no amount of interface-side inference recovers it. This is a strong, precise version of the interface argument that does not depend on evolutionary premises at all.
Evidence: Whitepaper §3.
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **C2A2 — highest-value signal in this proposal.** PRS-CANDIDATE-03 gives a candidate formal criterion for when two traditions are genuinely incommensurable versus merely unreconciled: existence or non-existence of a join in the trace order. If that maps onto MacIntyre's incommensurability thesis, C2A2 acquires a mathematical test for a claim that has been argued philosophically for forty years. Recommend the Master agent and the Loughran tradition both receive this. Treat as a *conjecture worth testing*, not an established mapping — the burden is to show the trace order is the right formalization of a tradition's rational structure, and that is not yet shown. - **Friston** — PRS-CANDIDATE-01 is the technical basis for the trace-blankets-subsume-Markov-blankets claim (CROSS-032). The generalization from acyclic to cyclic graphs lives here. - **Wolfram** — Observer-restricted effective dynamics from a larger substrate is structurally the same move as Wolfram's observer-dependent coarse-graining of the ruliad. Both make the observer's computational boundedness constitutive of the physics it sees. Existing wiki entry CROSS-033 touches this; the primary paper strengthens it. - **Arkani-Hamed** — Feeds the positive-geometry bridge (CROSS-033), though the concrete associahedron claim belongs to the whitepaper's Conjecture 7, not this paper. - **Hawkins** — Worth noting as a genuine structural rhyme: a trace kernel is a complete-but-partial model built by an observer with restricted access, which is formally close to what a cortical column is in Thousand Brains Theory. Hawkins grounds this biologically and stays agnostic on ontology; Hoffman grounds it ontologically and treats the biology as interface. The productive tension already recorded in the Hawkins wiki (HTM's consciousness-agnosticism vs. the Consciousness Cluster) now has a shared piece of mathematics to argue over.

SUMMARY
Carroll's Mindscape 361 (published 2026-07-20) is a long-form interview with molecular biologist Bonnie Bassler on quorum sensing — the chemical signaling by which bacteria detect population density and coordinate group behavior, effectively acting as a multicellular collective. The conversation sits within Carroll's recurring poetic-naturalist theme of how genuinely agent-like, coordinated behavior emerges from simple physical/chemical substrates without invoking anything beyond the natural.
WHY IT MATTERS
Carroll's program uses concrete science cases to argue that higher-level descriptions (agency, communication, cooperation) are real and useful even when fully grounded in lower-level physics — his "poetic naturalism." Quorum sensing is a clean, empirically rigorous instance: "communication" and "collective decision" as legitimate emergent vocabulary over molecular signaling. It extends Carroll's emergence corpus (cf. "What Emergence Can Possibly Mean") into microbiology.

S A poetic-naturalist reading in which "communication" and "collective behavior" are real emergent descriptions fully compatible with the underlying chemistry — agency language earns its keep by capturing genuine coordination patterns, not by adding non-physical ingredients.

AI, consciousness & awakening — In a world of words... could AI wake up? (Shamil Chandaria in dialogue with Bernardo Kastrup)
https://www.withrealityinmind.com/ai-awakening/
SUMMARY
A long-form dialogue (recorded 2026-07-07) between Kastrup and Shamil Chandaria — an advisor to Google DeepMind and a consciousness researcher at Oxford, Cambridge and Imperial College. Chandaria presents perception as a predictive generative model (we "live inside our own simulation," a hallucination updated by a thin trickle of sensory data) and previews five proposed criteria for evidence of consciousness — spanning behaviour, algorithms, IIT's causal structure, living biology, and organism–environment coupling. Kastrup, sympathetic to IIT but firmly skeptical of substrate-independent consciousness, presses the question: if a computer is conscious, what exactly is the *thing* that is conscious — the microchips, the cooling system, the river feeding the coolant?
WHY IT MATTERS
This is Kastrup's most direct 2026 engagement with AI consciousness — a standing C2A2-relevant question — and it stages his dissociation/biology account against a rigorous computationalist-adjacent interlocutor from inside frontier AI research. It sharpens the program's boundary-of-the-alter criterion into an explicit empirical challenge ("what is the thing?") and connects idealism to predictive-processing accounts of perception.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P Computationalist arguments for machine consciousness assume consciousness is substrate-independent, but offer no principled account of which physical system is the bearer of the alleged inner life.
R Kastrup's dissociation model — inner life ("alters") emerges only in biology, which is the sole system exhibiting the clear boundaries a dissociative process requires; the rest of the "dashboard" (chips, cooling, water supply) has no such boundary.
S A demarcation challenge to computationalism: absent a bounded biological system, there is no identifiable *thing* to be conscious; the burden is on computationalists to name the bearer, which reframes the AI-consciousness debate around individuation rather than information-processing capacity.
Evidence: Article (2026-07-07) states Kastrup "is sympathetic to IIT, but suspicious of substrate independent consciousness... leans firmly towards biology as the place where alters emerge," and poses "what exactly is the thing that's meant to be conscious? The microchips? The cooling system? The river that supplies the cooling water?"
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — Medium
P If perception is a generative model built from sparse data (Chandaria: only the thumbnail-sized fovea receives sharp data; the rest is a continuously updated best guess), what is the relationship between that predictive model and the reality it purports to track?
R The convergence between predictive-processing neuroscience (perception as prior-driven generative modelling, priors discoverable and re-designable in meditation) and Kastrup's idealist claim that the perceived world is a phenomenal representation, not the thing-in-itself.
S A bridge claim — studying how machines build world-models from language alone may illuminate how human perception constructs its dashboard, without licensing the inference that either system's model is or contains consciousness.
Evidence: Chandaria's fovea/hallucination account and "we are living inside our own simulation... a generative model"; the piece's closing note that studying machine world-models "we might be learning more about how we do it, too."

Flag strongly.
BERNARDO KASTRUP · INTERVIEW · 2026-07-14
The freedom to transform suffering to joy — Sthaneshwar Timalsina & Bernardo Kastrup on the joy, horror & meaning of existence
https://www.withrealityinmind.com/suffering-meaning-joy/
SUMMARY
A two-hour structured dialogue (recorded 2026-07-14, moderated by Prof. Ravi S. Kudesia) between Kastrup and Sthaneshwar Timalsina, a scholar of Kashmir Shaivism (Trika). Timalsina argues that consciousness is not only fundamental but that freedom, creativity, and purpose are inherent to its being, and that multiplicity and suffering are expressions of that freedom — inverting the Buddhist premise that existence is suffering into the claim that existence is fundamentally joy (ananda, glossed as progress and growth). Kastrup, drawing on his own experience of catastrophic tinnitus, is sympathetic to the peace such a perspective affords but wary of declaring existence inherently blissful, which he worries insults those in unspeakable suffering.
WHY IT MATTERS
This stages analytic idealism against a second, independently-arrived-at consciousness-first tradition (Trika Shaivism) with a fully developed account of *purpose and freedom* — the very dimension Kastrup's dissociation-based idealism has been least developed on. It also puts on record where Kastrup will and will not follow a convergent tradition (he accepts consciousness-as-fundamental but resists inherent-bliss), which is exactly the kind of principled boundary-marking the C2A2 network needs to catalog.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P Analytic idealism establishes that consciousness is fundamental but has an underdeveloped account of whether purpose, freedom, and value are intrinsic to consciousness or emergent/contingent features.
R Trika (Kashmir Shaivism) doctrine that freedom, being, and will are fundamental qualities of consciousness, that "order is an expression of freedom," and that purpose can be instinctive rather than planned — a consciousness-first metaphysics with intrinsic agency built in.
S A comparative resource for idealism: a worked-out position in which self-determined, creative freedom is constitutive of fundamental consciousness (contrasted with Advaita's more passive consciousness), giving Kastrup's program a foil for developing its own account of purpose without importing inherent bliss.
Evidence: Timeline markers in the recording — "0:22:30 Fundamental qualities: freedom, being, will"; "0:57:00 order is an expression of freedom"; "0:46:00 Trika vs Advaita: consciousness is self-determined, not passive"; "0:18:50 Purpose can be instinctive, not planned."
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — Medium
P A consciousness-first metaphysics must say something about the meaning of extreme suffering, or it risks being a comfortable abstraction that fails precisely where lived stakes are highest.
R The two interlocutors' lived cases (Timalsina's near-fatal assaults during Nepal's Maoist uprising; Kastrup's assisted-dying-eligible tinnitus) tested against Trika's claim that the subject retains the freedom to give suffering another telos — "recognising within suffering the higher telos: fulfilment, upliftment."
S A cautious, non-triumphalist synthesis — freedom and meaning (not guaranteed bliss) are what a consciousness-first view can honestly offer in the face of horror; Kastrup's "meta-peace with everyday suffering" marks the boundary of what he will assert.
Evidence: The article's account of both men's histories and Kastrup's wariness of "claiming existence is inherently blissful... it seems an insult to those experiencing unspeakable suffering"; timeline "1:42:00 transforming horror & disgust to ecstatic bliss," "2:01:00 happiness & freedom is finding meaning in spite of the suffering."
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Fredrickson:** "Ananda/bliss really means progress & growth" and the freedom to transform suffering into meaning map onto positive-psychology accounts of flourishing and post-adversity growth — a convergence between a contemplative tradition and empirical flourishing research worth flagging. - **Stump:** Direct contact with Stump's Thomistic work on suffering, theodicy, and the good — both address how a consciousness/intellect-first metaphysics accounts for the meaning of affliction. Strong pairing candidate. - **Spira / non-dual cluster:** Continues the analytic-idealist ↔ non-dual convergence thread already in the wiki (Spira, May 2026); the closing line "the absolute itself is dialogical" is notable for a network premised on inter-tradition dialogue. - **C2A2 method note:** A clean instance of a mature member of one tradition (Kastrup) marking exactly where he does and does not adopt a convergent tradition's resources — the "second-first-language" boundary-testing the accelerator is designed to surface.

CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium

R McGilchrist's hemispheric account of attention: right-hemisphere attention is embodied, contextual, participatory, and lives in real temporal flow; left-hemisphere attention re-presents an already-abstracted, decomposed world. AI, processing by decomposition and recombination, instantiates only the latter mode.
S A structural (not merely technological) demarcation — AI can indefinitely extend left-hemisphere re-presentation but cannot access the right-hemisphere disclosure of a living whole, because that disclosure requires embodiment and lived temporality AI does not have.
Evidence: Title and framing "Why A.I. will never be able to do what the brain does"; 20:47 talk published 2026-07-14 on channelmcgilchrist.com; continuous with McGilchrist's stated Closer to Truth (May 2026) position that the participatory/embodied/temporally-real mode is structurally unavailable to AI.

IAIN MCGILCHRIST · TALK · 2026-06-21
Chancellor McGilchrist Commencement Address | 2026 (Ralston College)
https://channelmcgilchrist.com/chancellor-mcgilchrist-commencement-address-2026/
SUMMARY
A ~9-minute commencement address delivered by McGilchrist in his capacity as Chancellor of Ralston College and published 2026-06-21. As a formal address to graduating students, it states in prescriptive rather than diagnostic register what an education oriented toward the right-hemisphere mode of attention is meant to form in a person — the virtues, dispositions, and capacities a mature member of a tradition should carry out into the world.
WHY IT MATTERS
