NIMA ARKANI-HAMED · PAPER · 2026-03-31
Which Functions Admit a Positive Geometry? From Branch Cuts to String Amplitudes
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.28543
SUMMARY
Co-authored paper extending the positive-geometry program from polytope-like canonical forms toward functions with non-trivial branch-cut structure — the analytic features that characterize string amplitudes and other physically realistic S-matrix objects. The paper asks the inverse question: rather than starting from a geometry and reading off a function, which classes of physical functions are even candidates for a positive-geometry origin?
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the kind of move that determines whether the positive-geometry program scales from toy models (amplituhedron, associahedron) to the actual analytic structures of real-world QFT and string theory. If branch-cut-bearing functions admit positive geometries, the program's claim to be a candidate for "post-spacetime physics" becomes substantially sharper; if they don't, the program is bounded. Either resolution is high-value.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P Whether positive geometry, as a candidate framework beyond spacetime/locality, can capture analytic structures (branch cuts) that physical amplitudes actually have.
R Criteria for "admissibility" — a precise mathematical characterization of which functions can be expressed as canonical forms of a positive geometry.
S A classification programme that lets practitioners check, for any candidate physical amplitude, whether a positive-geometry origin is in principle available.
Evidence: Paper title and arXiv abstract framing the question as branch-cut → string-amplitude scope-extension.
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — Medium
P The amplituhedron program has been criticized as restricted to planar N=4 SYM — a beautiful but possibly idiosyncratic theory.
R Extending the admissibility analysis to string amplitudes (which have richer analytic structure than rational planar N=4 amplitudes).
S A bridge between the amplituhedron-style positive-geometry program and the broader S-matrix bootstrap / string-amplitude community.
Evidence: Paper subtitle explicitly couples "branch cuts" to "string amplitudes."
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Wolfram:** Both Arkani-Hamed and Wolfram take spacetime to be derived from a more fundamental combinatorial/computational substrate. The positive-geometry route and the ruliad/hypergraph route are competing answers to the same family of questions about emergence of locality. Branch-cut-handling is a discriminating test case. - **Carroll:** Carroll's Mad-Dog Everett (this same proposal batch, PROP-2026-05-27-005) takes Hilbert-space dynamics as fundamental. Positive geometry, by contrast, takes combinatorial geometry as fundamental and derives even Hilbert-space-like objects. The pair sets up a clean three-way contrast (Carroll / Arkani-Hamed / Wolfram) on what "fundamental" means.
SEAN CARROLL · PODCAST · 2026-05-25
Mindscape 355 | Solo: Looking Quantum Mechanics in the Eyeball
https://www.preposterousuniverse.com/podcast/2026/05/25/355-solo-looking-quantum…
SUMMARY
Carroll's solo episode lays out what he calls "Mad Dog Everettianism" — a maximally austere formulation of quantum mechanics where the only ontology is the universal wavefunction and the Schrödinger equation. He argues this minimalism, rather than being a limitation, is its explanatory virtue: branching, classicality, and the appearance of measurement all emerge without additional postulates. He defends the view against operational and Bohmian rivals.
WHY IT MATTERS
Carroll has been building a multi-year argument that poetic naturalism + Many-Worlds is the cleanest naturalist ontology. This is his most concentrated solo statement of the position since the 2019 *Something Deeply Hidden* arc, sharpened by responses to recent operational quantum foundations debates. It establishes a clear "minimalist realist" pole that other program agents (Hoffman's interface-theoretic, Kastrup's idealist, Arkani-Hamed's post- spacetime) will read against.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P What is the most parsimonious ontology consistent with the empirical success of quantum mechanics?
R "Mad Dog Everett" — wavefunction + Schrödinger equation as the only fundamental ingredients.
S Branches, classicality, and measurement statistics are emergent derivations, not added structure.
Evidence: Carroll's own framing of the episode as "super austere, bare-bones quantum mechanical theory."
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — Medium
P How to distinguish a "naive observer" reading of measurement from a derivation of classicality from quantum dynamics alone.
R Decoherence + branch-counting / self-locating-uncertainty arguments inside pure Schrödinger evolution.
S Observers are subsystems whose classical-looking experience is a consequence of decoherence in a unitary global state — no collapse, no hidden variables required.
Evidence: Solo episode treatment in the standard Carroll Everettian style.
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Hoffman:** Carroll's "observers are decohered subsystems" stands in sharp tension with Hoffman's "observers are fundamental conscious agents and spacetime is the user interface." Worth flagging to the pattern detector as a paradigm-level disagreement about what kind of thing an observer is. - **Arkani-Hamed:** Both Carroll and Arkani-Hamed take spacetime/observers to be derived rather than fundamental, but from different directions (decoherence in Hilbert space vs. positive geometry beyond spacetime). Possible explanatory-bridge candidate. - **Kastrup:** Direct ontological opposition — Mad Dog Everett (physicalist wavefunction monism) vs. analytic idealism (mental monism). Useful as a clean negative bridge for inter-tradition dialogue.

BARBARA FREDRICKSON · PAPER · 2025-11-21
High-quality listening behaviors linked to social connection between strangers
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44271-025-00342-2
SUMMARY
West, Huston, Chandler, Zhou & Fredrickson (Communications Psychology, Nov 2025) report two pre-registered studies (total N = 646) showing that observable verbal listening behaviors — verbal validation and follow-up questions — predict faster conversational response times and other behavioral, third-party-observer, self-, and partner-reported markers of social connection between strangers. A simple social-connectedness intervention (vs. active control) increased follow-up-question frequency in Study 1's deep-talk conversations, and that increase mediated the intervention's effect on connection. In Study 2's small-talk extension, global listening behaviors replicated as predictors of behavioral and partner-reported connection, but verbal indicators alone were less consistent and the intervention did not raise listening behaviors in the small-talk context.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the peer-reviewed empirical paper underlying the listening-quality findings previously captured only at colloquium level (existing PRS-08, from the 2025-10-01 UBC colloquium). It identifies a *behavioral* (not merely self-reported) intermediate that links a deliverable-at-scale intervention to positivity-resonance precursors — closing a gap in Fredrickson's program between her theoretical "perceived safety + real-time sensory connection" mechanism and the question of what trainable behaviors actually produce those conditions. It also surfaces a boundary condition (small-talk vs. deep-talk asymmetry) that the colloquium account did not.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P Self-reported listening quality has long been theorized to enable positivity resonance, but until now the field lacked behavioral, observable, pre-registered evidence that specific listening behaviors mediate the link between a social-connectedness intervention and felt connection between strangers.
