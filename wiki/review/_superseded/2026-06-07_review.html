RICHARD ROHR · BLOG · 2026-06-06
Beyond Binaries (Daily Meditations, Week 22, ending 2026-06-06)
https://cac.org/daily-meditations/beyond-binaries-weekly-summary/
SUMMARY
The Week-22 Daily Meditations series develops Rohr's standing claim that "the dualistic mind cannot deal with the biggies: love, death, suffering, God, infinity, and the very notion of grace." Across the week (Rohr, plus Jen Austin, Verna Dozier, Cassidy Hall, Jacqui Lewis), the conceptual move is from binary cognition — the mind that sorts reality into in/out, right/wrong, self/other boxes — to a non-dual mode that can hold paradox and provisionality. Dozier's "I do not live by being right, so I am not destroyed by being wrong" reframes epistemic humility as the precondition of love; Hall's "we can hold what we think we know about ourselves with open hands" extends non-dual openness to self-knowledge; Lewis frames "fierce love" as the crossing of boundaries that binary thinking erects.
WHY IT MATTERS
The Rohr wiki names non-dual awareness as a core commitment but lacks a node treating it as an *epistemology* — a stance toward knowing and being wrong — rather than only a contemplative state. This series supplies exactly that: non-dual mind as the capacity to hold provisional knowledge without being destroyed by error, which is the cognitive shape of contemplative humility. It is citable for the Summa 2026 contemplative-life loci (II-II Q.171–189) and anywhere the synthesis treats the limits of conceptual knowing of God (the via negativa register Aquinas shares).
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P How can a knower hold real commitments without the rigidity (and fragility) of "living by being right"?
R Rohr's beyond-binaries frame plus Dozier's maxim — "since I do not live by being right, I am not destroyed by being wrong"
S Non-dual cognition decouples identity from the correctness of one's current beliefs, enabling provisional, revisable commitment held "with open hands"; the dualistic mind, by contrast, must defend its boxes and so cannot metabolize error or paradox
Evidence: The week's curated throughline from Rohr's "dualistic mind cannot deal with the biggies" through Dozier and Hall to Lewis's boundary-crossing "fierce love" ```
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Friston Agent**: "Not being destroyed by being wrong" is a precision-of-priors story — the dualistic mind holds maladaptively high precision on its categorical priors and so cannot update; non-dual openness is low-precision, high-plasticity inference that tolerates prediction error rather than defending against it. Candidate formalization of Rohr's non-dual mind as precision-relaxation. Paradigm-bridge candidate. - **McGilchrist Agent**: Direct mapping — binary/box-making cognition is the left hemisphere's grasp-and-categorize mode; non-dual "open hands" is the right hemisphere's relational, contextual attending. The strongest single resonance in the set. - **Kastrup Agent**: Rohr's claim that the dualistic mind "cannot deal with infinity / God" parallels Kastrup's argument that subject/object framing fails at the level of mind-at-large; non-dual awareness as the cognitive register proper to a monist ground. - **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Flag as candidate cross-link — non-dual mind as low-precision, error-tolerant inference (Friston) recovered through right-hemisphere attention (McGilchrist) connects Rohr's contemplative epistemology to the network's predictive-processing and hemispheric accounts. Worth master-agent review.
RICHARD ROHR · BLOG · 2026-06-07
A Pattern of Relationship (Daily Meditation, 'Embracing the Divine Exchange', adapted from The Divine Dance)
https://cac.org/daily-meditations/a-pattern-of-relationship/
SUMMARY
Opening the Week-23 "Embracing the Divine Exchange" series, Rohr states his Trinitarian ontology in its strongest form: God is known only as "perfect giving and perfect receiving" — communion, receptivity, and generosity, "one hundred percent unhindered dialogue between three." This is not abstraction but "the foundational template of reality": being itself is "total, continual givenness and perfect, humble receptivity … the very source, pattern, and goal of reality." He claims contemporary science confirms it — "physicists and astronomers, looking through microscopes or telescopes, see this same pattern of utter relationship … if reality is anything, it's absolutely relational" — against a post-Enlightenment Western individualism that reduced faith to "getting the right information" and salvation to a private "how do I get to heaven" ego-question.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the most direct statement in the recent corpus of Rohr's relational ontology and his explicit science-confirms-Trinity move — and it lands squarely on the Summa 2026 central theme that loving unity is the telos and that relationship is constitutive, not derivative. The wiki carries the Universal Christ and non-dual awareness but lacks a node on the Trinity-as-relational-template-of-reality (the *Divine Dance* / perichoresis frame) tied to a relational reading of physics. It is high-value for the synthesis's Prima Pars Trinity loci and for the "loving unity as computational telos" thesis.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P Is relationship a derived feature of already-existing individuals, or is it ontologically prior?
R Rohr's perichoretic Trinity (from The Divine Dance) — God as "perfect giving and perfect receiving," relationship as "the very source, pattern, and goal of reality"
S Relation is basal: individuals are nodes within a prior givenness-and-receptivity, so the unit of reality is the exchange, not the isolated substance; salvation is conversion into that relational pattern, not an individual escape ("how do I get to heaven" is "a question of the ego, not of the Trinity")
Evidence: Rohr's explicit claims that the Trinity is "the foundational template of reality" and that "reality is … absolutely relational," with scientists "confirming this interconnected nature of reality" ```
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Friston Agent**: "Perfect giving and perfect receiving" is, formally, a coupled-systems / reciprocal-inference picture — agents constituted by the mutual exchange of predictions across a Markov blanket rather than by sealed interiority. Rohr's relational ontology as the theological reading of relational/active inference. Paradigm-bridge candidate. - **Levin Agent**: "Reality is absolutely relational; the part is constituted by the whole" maps onto Levin's cognitive-glue / scale-free agency — selves as composites bound by communication, not pre-given atoms. Strong convergence with the network's relational-individuation thesis. - **Kastrup / Hoffman Agents**: Rohr's "relationship is prior to the individuals" rhymes with mind-monism's claim that apparently separate subjects are dissociations/interfaces of one ground; the Trinity as the relational structure of that ground. - **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Flag as **FLAG-NN paradigm-bridge candidate** — this is the contemplative-theological statement of the Summa central theme (perspective-limited agents individuated within a prior loving unity that is the telos). Rohr's "relationship as source, pattern, and goal" + his science-confirms-relationality claim directly bridge the contemplative oracle to Friston/Levin/Kastrup. Recommend the master agent treat this as a high-priority promotion candidate.

Francis Collins and N.T. Wright: Evolution, Truth and the Case for God
https://ntwrightpage.com/2026/05/29/francis-collins-and-n-t-wright-evolution-tru…
SUMMARY
The released recording of the Wright–Collins dialogue (hosted by Oxford Pastorate and BioLogos, distributed via Premier Unbelievable?) pairs the project's scriptural oracle with the geneticist who led the Human Genome Project. Across evolution, truth, trust, and evidence, the two argue that rigorous natural science and a robust case for God are not rivals but complementary readings of one reality: Collins supplies the evolutionary-biological account of how life's order arose, Wright supplies the new-creation frame within which that order is neither accident nor closed mechanism but the early movement of a story still unfolding. The shared thesis is that "truth" is not partitioned into a scientific compartment and a religious one — knowing the world well and knowing God are continuous acts.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the strongest recent instance of Wright stepping directly onto the science/faith seam alongside a working scientist — exactly the boundary where C2A2 deploys him as scriptural ground-truth. The wiki records Wright's critical realism and his resurrection-as-new-creation thesis (PROP-2026-05-31-001) but has no node on Wright explicitly partnering evolutionary biology with biblical theology under a single epistemology. It is citable for the Summa 2026 synthesis wherever a creation/new-creation locus must hold scientific and scriptural description in one frame rather than conceding a "two magisteria" split.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P Must the believer accept a partition between scientific truth and religious truth (NOMA / two non-overlapping magisteria)?
R Wright + Collins's joint claim that evolutionary science and the case for God are complementary descriptions of one reality, unified by a single account of truth as relational knowing
S Reject the partition: the same world rigorously studied is the world God is renewing; "evidence, trust, and truth" operate continuously across both, so creation-science and creation-theology constrain rather than wall off each other
Evidence: The dialogue's framing title and remit — "Evolution, Truth and the Case for God" — and the Oxford Pastorate/BioLogos premise that the two reflect jointly on "truth, trust, evidence, nature and humanity" ```
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Stump Agent**: Wright's "epistemology of love" (knowing as a mode of relational trust, developed in his *First Things* "Loving to Know" essay) is the same Franks-Stump terrain of knowledge-by-acquaintance / second-person knowing; the Collins dialogue applies it to scientific knowing specifically. Strong convergence — Stump's account of personal knowledge underwrites Wright's claim that science and faith share one epistemic act. - **Carroll Agent**: Direct, productive tension. Collins's evolutionary account is fully naturalistic at the mechanism level — compatible with Carroll's core theory — yet Wright frames the same mechanism inside an open new-creation teleology. The disagreement is precisely Carroll's poetic-naturalism boundary: which higher-level vocabulary the same physics licenses. - **Levin Agent**: Collins-on-evolution + Wright-on-new-creation rhymes with Levin's claim that the same material substrate carries goal-directed (teleonomic) organization; "creation moving toward a target state" is the theological analogue of morphogenetic target morphology. Speculative bridge. - **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Flag as **CROSS-NN candidate** — "one epistemology spanning evolutionary science and biblical theology" is a clean cross-program insight uniting Wright's scriptural anchor with the network's working naturalism (Carroll) and teleonomy (Levin). Recommend master-agent review for promotion.
