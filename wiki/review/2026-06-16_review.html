RICHARD ROHR · BLOG · 2026-06-06
Beyond Binaries (Daily Meditations, Week 22, ending 2026-06-06)
https://cac.org/daily-meditations/beyond-binaries-weekly-summary/
SUMMARY
The Week-22 Daily Meditations series develops Rohr's standing claim that "the dualistic mind cannot deal with the biggies: love, death, suffering, God, infinity, and the very notion of grace." Across the week (Rohr, plus Jen Austin, Verna Dozier, Cassidy Hall, Jacqui Lewis), the conceptual move is from binary cognition — the mind that sorts reality into in/out, right/wrong, self/other boxes — to a non-dual mode that can hold paradox and provisionality. Dozier's "I do not live by being right, so I am not destroyed by being wrong" reframes epistemic humility as the precondition of love; Hall's "we can hold what we think we know about ourselves with open hands" extends non-dual openness to self-knowledge; Lewis frames "fierce love" as the crossing of boundaries that binary thinking erects.
WHY IT MATTERS
The Rohr wiki names non-dual awareness as a core commitment but lacks a node treating it as an *epistemology* — a stance toward knowing and being wrong — rather than only a contemplative state. This series supplies exactly that: non-dual mind as the capacity to hold provisional knowledge without being destroyed by error, which is the cognitive shape of contemplative humility. It is citable for the Summa 2026 contemplative-life loci (II-II Q.171–189) and anywhere the synthesis treats the limits of conceptual knowing of God (the via negativa register Aquinas shares).
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P How can a knower hold real commitments without the rigidity (and fragility) of "living by being right"?
R Rohr's beyond-binaries frame plus Dozier's maxim — "since I do not live by being right, I am not destroyed by being wrong"
S Non-dual cognition decouples identity from the correctness of one's current beliefs, enabling provisional, revisable commitment held "with open hands"; the dualistic mind, by contrast, must defend its boxes and so cannot metabolize error or paradox
Evidence: The week's curated throughline from Rohr's "dualistic mind cannot deal with the biggies" through Dozier and Hall to Lewis's boundary-crossing "fierce love" ```
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
- **Friston Agent**: "Not being destroyed by being wrong" is a precision-of-priors story — the dualistic mind holds maladaptively high precision on its categorical priors and so cannot update; non-dual openness is low-precision, high-plasticity inference that tolerates prediction error rather than defending against it. Candidate formalization of Rohr's non-dual mind as precision-relaxation. Paradigm-bridge candidate. - **McGilchrist Agent**: Direct mapping — binary/box-making cognition is the left hemisphere's grasp-and-categorize mode; non-dual "open hands" is the right hemisphere's relational, contextual attending. The strongest single resonance in the set. - **Kastrup Agent**: Rohr's claim that the dualistic mind "cannot deal with infinity / God" parallels Kastrup's argument that subject/object framing fails at the level of mind-at-large; non-dual awareness as the cognitive register proper to a monist ground. - **C2A2 master / paradigm flags**: Flag as candidate cross-link — non-dual mind as low-precision, error-tolerant inference (Friston) recovered through right-hemisphere attention (McGilchrist) connects Rohr's contemplative epistemology to the network's predictive-processing and hemispheric accounts. A Pattern of Relationship (Daily Meditation, 'Embracing the Divine Exchange', adapted from The Divine Dance)
https://cac.org/daily-meditations/a-pattern-of-relationship/
SUMMARY
Opening the Week-23 "Embracing the Divine Exchange" series, Rohr states his Trinitarian ontology in its strongest form: God is known only as "perfect giving and perfect receiving" — communion, receptivity, and generosity, "one hundred percent unhindered dialogue between three." This is not abstraction but "the foundational template of reality": being itself is "total, continual givenness and perfect, humble receptivity … the very source, pattern, and goal of reality." He claims contemporary science confirms it — "physicists and astronomers, looking through microscopes or telescopes, see this same pattern of utter relationship … if reality is anything, it's absolutely relational" — against a post-Enlightenment Western individualism that reduced faith to "getting the right information" and salvation to a private "how do I get to heaven" ego-question.
WHY IT MATTERS
This is the most direct statement in the recent corpus of Rohr's relational ontology and his explicit science-confirms-Trinity move — and it lands squarely on the Summa 2026 central theme that loving unity is the telos and that relationship is constitutive, not derivative. The wiki carries the Universal Christ and non-dual awareness but lacks a node on the Trinity-as-relational-template-of-reality (the *Divine Dance* / perichoresis frame) tied to a relational reading of physics. It is high-value for the synthesis's Prima Pars Trinity loci and for the "loving unity as computational telos" thesis.

S Relation is basal: individuals are nodes within a prior givenness-and-receptivity, so the unit of reality is the exchange, not the isolated substance; salvation is conversion into that relational pattern, not an individual escape ("how do I get to heaven" is "a question of the ego, not of the Trinity")

ELEONORE STUMP · PAPER · 2022 (book); review essay by Mark Wynn in Faith and Philosophy 42.2, 2026-04-01
The Image of God: The Problem of Evil and the Problem of Mourning
https://academic.oup.com/book/41739
SUMMARY
Stump's *The Image of God* (OUP) is the sequel to *Wandering in Darkness*: having argued there that suffering can be defeated through union with God, she here confronts the residue — the "problem of mourning." Even defeated suffering in a post-Fall world merits mourning, and the book asks how warranted mourning is compatible with an omniscient, omnipotent, perfectly good God. She defends a felix culpa view (the Fall was fortunate because there is an outweighing benefit to sufferers unavailable in a world without suffering) while insisting the loss mourned remains real. A substantive review essay by Mark Wynn just appeared in Faith and Philosophy 42.2 (April 2026), marking the book as an active discussion point. This major work is not yet captured in the Stump wiki (we have Wandering in Darkness, Grains of Wheat, and the Maritain Lecture, but not this).
WHY IT MATTERS
This completes Stump's theodicy arc — defeat of suffering (Wandering in Darkness) → narrative knowledge of suffering (Grains of Wheat) → the warrant of mourning what was defeated (Image of God) — and develops her accounts of the true self and of relationships as constitutive of flourishing, both load-bearing for the wiki's existing PRS-05, PRS-11, and PRS-12.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — High
P Even if suffering is defeated through union (the Wandering in Darkness solution), defeated suffering still merits mourning — and standard theodicies cannot explain how warranted mourning is compatible with a perfectly good God who permits what is mourned
R Stump's felix culpa account in The Image of God: the original sin is fortunate because there is an outweighing benefit to sufferers that could not be gotten in a world without suffering, joined to an account on which the mourned loss remains genuinely real, not explained away
S Mourning and theodicy are made compatible rather than rivals — the defeat of suffering does not erase its loss-character, so a perfectly good God can permit what warrants mourning; theodicy is completed by an ethics of mourning rather than by the elimination of grief
Evidence: The book's stated thesis addresses "the problem of mourning: even defeated suffering in the post-Fall world merits mourning" and defends the felix culpa view that the original sin carried "an outweighing benefit to sufferers that could not be gotten in a world without suffering"
CANDIDATE PRS 2 — Medium
P Accounts of human flourishing oscillate between individualist (flourishing as internal state) and relational pictures without a principled account of what constitutes a person's true self
R The Image of God's treatment of the role of relationships in human flourishing and of what constitutes one's true self, developed within Stump's hylomorphic psychology
S Flourishing is constituted by relationship — the true self is the self in union with others and with God — giving the Thomistic wing a worked-out relational anthropology rather than a merely asserted one
Evidence: The book "explores topics in philosophical theology to explain and evaluate the role of suffering in human lives by addressing... the role of relationships in human flourishing, and the question of what constitutes one's true self"
CROSS-TRADITION SIGNALS
Strong Fredrickson resonance: Stump's relational constitution of flourishing (PRS-CANDIDATE-02) is the metaphysical counterpart of positivity resonance as the engine of flourishing — this extends the established CROSS-005 (love/union as fundamental in Stump, Fredrickson, Kastrup) into the flourishing-anthropology register. KARL FRISTON · PAPER · 2025-09
A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0149763425002970
SUMMARY
Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria propose an active-inference theory of consciousness resting on three conditions: (1) the system simulates a world model; (2) there is inferential competition to enter that model; and (3) "epistemic depth" — recurrent, system-wide sharing of Bayesian beliefs, i.e., a self-referential loop in which the model is reflexively available to itself. Consciousness is identified not with any predictive process but specifically with this circular one, and the theory is used to explain altered states (meditation, psychedelics, minimal phenomenal experience).
WHY IT MATTERS
This is Friston's most developed extension of active inference into a full theory of consciousness, and it introduces "epistemic depth" as the proposed mark of the conscious. It is distinct from the Whyte et al. "minimal theory" already captured (PRS-09) and is not yet in the wiki; it pushes FEP from perception/action into awareness itself.
CANDIDATE PRS TRIPLETS
CANDIDATE PRS 1 — Medium
P Active inference / FEP explains perception and action but has lacked a principled account of why and when a predictive system is conscious rather than merely adaptive.
R Three conditions — world-model simulation, inferential competition to enter the model, and "epistemic depth" (recurrent, system-wide sharing of Bayesian beliefs forming a self-referential loop).
S Consciousness is identified with circular (self-referential) predictive processing that satisfies epistemic depth, yielding mechanistic explanations of meditative, psychedelic, and minimal phenomenal states.
Evidence: Laukkonen, R., Friston, K., Chandaria, S. (2025) "A beautiful loop: An active inference theory of consciousness," Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, doi:10.1016/j.neubiorev.2025.106296.

Top-down perspectives on cell membrane potential and protein transcription
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-31696-6
SUMMARY
Cervera, Levin & Mafe model the relationship between resting cell membrane potential (Vmem) and gene/protein transcription as a top-down process: rather than transcription being determined purely bottom-up by gene-regulatory networks, the cell's bioelectric state acts as a higher-order control variable that constrains and biases transcriptional outcomes. This is a peer-reviewed 2026 formalization of the central Levin thesis that bioelectricity is a genuine control layer "above" the genome. It supplies mechanism for the "bioelectric software" claim (PRS-07) by tying Vmem directly to transcription, and is not yet captured in the wiki.
