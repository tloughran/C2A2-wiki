What a lens is. Your lens is how you read this signal stream — it both filters (which sources and tags you see, and a minimum relevance) and ranks (how signals are ordered), plus your digest cadence and consent boundaries. It changes what you see, never the underlying data, so two readers can hold different lenses over the same heartbeat. Stored on this device by default; sign in to sync it across devices.
