## Research Program Overview
**Thinker:** Nima Arkani-Hamed (IAS Princeton, Harvard)
**Core claim:** Spacetime and quantum mechanics are not fundamental — they emerge from a deeper combinatorial/geometric structure; the amplituhedron demonstrates that physics can be reformulated without spacetime scaffolding
**Paradigm:** Amplituhedron, positive geometry, on-shell methods, post-spacetime physics

**Summary (from RC Pilot document):**

Nima Arkani-Hamed enters the project as the physicist whose work gives the post-spacetime turn its most mathematically rigorous form. The amplituhedron (Arkani-Hamed & Trnka, 2013) is a geometric object that computes particle-scattering amplitudes in N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory without reference to spacetime: locality and unitarity, normally treated as bedrock assumptions, emerge as *derived* consequences of a positive geometry rather than inputs. The result is technical, but the claim underneath it is metaphysical — that spacetime is not rock-bottom but a downstream appearance of a deeper combinatorial structure.

That is why the program matters to the synthesis. If spacetime and quantum mechanics are emergent, then the project's central wager — that the felt world is an interface rather than the thing-in-itself — has a working precedent inside fundamental physics itself. The RC Pilot records the speculative hypothesis that the amplituhedron's underlying structure might be related to, or derivable from, the mathematics of Hoffman's conscious-agent networks; were that to hold, physics would emerge from consciousness rather than the reverse. The project files this as a genuine open conjecture, not a settled result.

Arkani-Hamed's more recent UNIVERSE+ / Les Houches material (2026, the tradition's first dedicated primary source) widens the frame from the amplituhedron to *positive geometry* as a general language: the amplituhedron becomes one instance of a broader framework that reaches cosmological correlators, aiming at a single combinatorial account spanning both quantum amplitudes and spacetime structure. This raises one of the program's sharper methodological questions — whether "mathematical beauty," understood as the inevitability of a result, is a legitimate epistemic criterion in a regime that runs ahead of experiment.

The active questions track this arc: what is the complete structure underlying amplituhedron amplitudes, and can it be extended beyond N=4; is there a more general positive geometry of which the amplituhedron is a special case; and — the connection the project most wants to test — is there a relationship between positive geometry's combinatorial structures and Wolfram's rulial graph, two independent post-spacetime programs that may be converging on similar objects from opposite directions. Within the architecture, Arkani-Hamed is the most exact representative of the Post-Spacetime cluster (CROSS-002), paired with Wolfram's hypergraph approach and pointed toward Hoffman's agent mathematics. *(Note: this tradition is still partially sourced — four PRS triplets recorded, awaiting more primary material.)*
