## Research Program Overview
**Thinker:** Iain McGilchrist (Psychiatrist, neuroscientist, literary scholar)
**Core claim:** Left hemisphere re-presents known fragments; right hemisphere attends to the living whole; modernity suffers catastrophic left-hemisphere dominance; attention is world-constituting
**Paradigm:** Divided brain, relational ontology, attention as ontological act, embodied cognition

**Summary (from RC Pilot document):**

Iain McGilchrist is the tradition's phenomenologist and neurologist of culture, whose divided-brain theory supplies the empirical foundation for the project's insistence on relational ontology over analytic fragmentation. His core argument in *The Master and His Emissary* (2009) is that the right hemisphere perceives context, relation, and the living whole, while the left hemisphere abstracts, categorizes, and manipulates — and that the left hemisphere's operations are parasitic on what the right hemisphere first discloses. The right hemisphere is the master; the left its emissary, which in modernity has usurped control. The diagnosis is not metaphor but measurable hemispheric asymmetry in cognitive style, confirmed across his clinical and literary corpus.

This has direct methodological consequences for the C2A2 project. Genuine engagement with a rival tradition requires right-hemisphere modes of attention — inhabiting the whole before analyzing the parts — which is precisely what "second-first-language" competence demands. McGilchrist's corpus-callosotomy evidence also does double duty: split-brain patients exhibit two independent conscious agents that integrate when reconnected, giving neurological support to Conscious Realism's mathematical claim that multiple conscious agents can compose a single unified self.

His more recent Sophia Lectures (Ralston College, 2026) push the program further. Synthesis is not the addition of divided parts but a "union at a higher level" that produces a third thing qualitatively beyond its components — a claim the project reads as a possible neurological twin of Levin's morphogenetic integration, where coordinated cells produce a whole no single cell contains. The same lectures treat paradox not as logical failure but as ontological depth, marking genuine asymmetric relations between levels of reality — a stance the project sets beside Wolfram's observer-dependent sampling of the Ruliad as two routes to the co-presence of the finite and the infinite.

The open questions this program is generating run from the empirical to the architectural: is left-hemisphere dominance measurable at a *cultural* level; does right-hemisphere engagement ground all propositional knowledge; can McGilchrist's attention theory be formalized in terms compatible with Friston's precision-weighted prediction; and what institutional practices cultivate right-hemisphere openness inside academic discourse — a question that bears directly on how the C2A2 communities should be run, not just theorized. In the architecture of the project, McGilchrist imports a cognitive and neurological foundation for the relational ontology that the Conscious Realism cluster (Hoffman, Levin, Friston) theorizes mathematically, and he names the failure mode — decontextualized abstraction — that the contemplative voices (Rohr) and the narrative voices (Wright, MacIntyre) are each trying, in their own registers, to remedy.

14. Is there a common phenomenology of paradigm-level discovery across programs? McGilchrist's gestalt-not-method account, Wolfram's ruliad exploration, and Arkani-Hamed's structures-found-before-they-are-derived may be three descriptions of one thing.
15. Does the confabulation criterion (a self-referring system produces certainty *plus* fabrication) yield a falsifiable prediction about the C2A2 network itself — namely that a single-tradition agent without cross-tradition contact should exhibit that signature, and that the dialogue apparatus should measurably reduce it?
16. Is suffering-as-condition-of-a-fulfilled-life, reached here from embodiment and literature, the same conclusion Stump reaches from Thomistic theology — and if so, does independent arrival strengthen it or merely show a shared cultural inheritance?

*Added 2026-08-10 (from PROP-2026-08-05-002):*

17. Does "reality exceeds computation" say anything more than "computation is a description"? Wolfram can grant that a model is not the thing modelled and lose nothing. The question is whether McGilchrist's embodiment/receptivity/participation triad names a feature of reality that a computational ontology must *deny* rather than merely not mention — and if it does not, the disagreement with Wolfram is about emphasis, not ontology. This is the sharper form of open question 12.
18. Can a recorded rival-tradition encounter be *coded* for the competence C2A2 is trying to measure? The Ralston conversation is the first on-record, long-form exchange between two registered C2A2 traditions. If second-first-language competence means each party can state the other's position from inside it, that is in principle scoreable from the transcript — which would turn a promotional claim ("less a debate than an encounter") into a measurement. What would the coding scheme be, and does the transcript survive it?

*Added 2026-08-28 (from PROP-2026-08-14-011, PROP-2026-08-14-012):*

19. Does the metaphor-priority claim survive contact with people whose discipline is poetry? The claim that metaphor and poetic language are prior to, not derivative of, literal propositional language is load-bearing at PRS-55 and PRS-57, but every source in this wiki that carries it comes from a venue that already grants the hemispheric frame. The Poetry Foundation episode (PRS-72) is the first located instance of the other case and is **not yet heard** — so this question is opened as a retrieval task before it is a research task. The cheap sub-question is the checkable one: the "real word for sunshine" claim, if it is what the title suggests, would be the smallest falsifiable unit the impoverishment thesis has ever been pinned to, and a stronger test case for open question 12 than the Zen argument, because a poem's meaning is not separable from its exact wording and paraphrase destroys it.

20. Is the metacrisis diagnosis better argued at the level of institutions than at the level of metaphysics? PRS-70's exhibit list includes "the reverence for machine-like efficiency," which is open question 17's dispute with Wolfram — whether a computational ontology must *deny* embodiment or merely not mention it — restated as a claim about institutional practice rather than about the furniture of the world. If the institutional form is easier to adjudicate than the metaphysical one, that is a method result, not only a topic. **Do not adjudicate this from the McGilchrist side alone** (see the standing cross-file note in the 2026-08-10 log).
