## Research Program Overview
**Thinker:** Richard Rohr, OFM (Center for Action and Contemplation)
**Core claim:** Reality operates at multiple levels simultaneously; non-dualistic thinking — the capacity to hold contradictions without resolving them prematurely — is the hallmark of mature consciousness; the contemplative tradition offers an epistemology that complements and exceeds rationalist analysis
**Paradigm:** Contemplative epistemology, non-dual awareness, the Universal Christ, order-disorder-reorder, levels of consciousness development

**Summary (from RC Pilot document):**

Richard Rohr, OFM, is the tradition's contemplative epistemologist — the voice insisting that the C2A2 project cannot succeed through analysis alone. His central contribution is the recovery of contemplative knowing as a legitimate and necessary mode of inquiry, distinct from both scientific empiricism and philosophical argument. Where McGilchrist *diagnoses* the left hemisphere's tyranny of decontextualized abstraction, Rohr *prescribes* the remedy: a practice-based return to non-dual awareness, the capacity to perceive unity-in-distinction rather than collapsing reality into either/or categories.

His order-disorder-reorder framework maps closely onto MacIntyre's epistemological crisis. Traditions begin in a received order, pass through the disorder of internal contradiction and external challenge, and — if they are living — emerge into a deeper reorder that preserves what was true in the original while transcending its limits. Stated in this register, the contemplative tradition and the philosophy of inquiry are describing the same movement from two sides, which is exactly the kind of cross-traditional rhyme the detector is built to surface.

Rohr's theology of the Universal Christ — the claim that the divine pattern is not confined to one tradition but is present throughout reality — supplies a theological warrant for the project's multi-tradition architecture. If truth is genuinely universal, then encountering it simultaneously through Levin's biology, Friston's mathematics, and Stump's Thomism is not eclecticism but a contemplative practice of perceiving the same pattern at different scales. His Franciscan emphasis on incarnation and embodiment also connects to Fredrickson's positivity resonance: love is not an abstraction but a biological and spiritual event that occurs between embodied persons.

The questions this program generates are sharp precisely because they test the limits of formalization: can a non-dual epistemology be formalized at all, or does formalization necessarily distort it; is contemplative knowing a distinct epistemic mode or a special case of McGilchrist's right-hemisphere attention; how does order-disorder-reorder relate to Friston's surprise-update-equilibrium cycle; and can the contemplative stance — holding contradictions without premature resolution — be operationalized as an actual methodology for cross-program analysis rather than remaining a private disposition. His recorded advances are modest and honest about their status: that there is a mode of knowing beyond dualistic analysis (corroborated by McGilchrist's hemispheric research), and that the order-disorder-reorder arc credibly describes intellectual development (mapped onto MacIntyre and Kuhn, at medium confidence). He connects to McGilchrist (right-hemisphere knowing), Stump (a complementary Catholic intellectual tradition that also makes union the highest good), Fredrickson (embodied love as the substrate of spiritual encounter), MacIntyre (the contemplative dimension his rationalism lacks), and Kastrup (Universal Christ and universal consciousness as possibly one claim in two registers).

## Active Research Questions
*(Questions currently being generated by this program)*

1. Can Rohr's non-dual epistemology be formalized — or does formalization necessarily distort it?
2. How does the order-disorder-reorder framework relate to Friston's free energy minimization (surprise → model update → new equilibrium)?
3. Is contemplative knowing a distinct epistemic mode, or a special case of McGilchrist's right-hemisphere attention?
4. Does Rohr's Universal Christ theology provide a genuine framework for inter-tradition convergence, or does it privilege one tradition's categories?
5. How does Rohr's developmental model (first-half-of-life task vs. second-half) relate to the maturation of AI agent programs within C2A2?
6. Can the contemplative stance — holding contradictions without premature resolution — be operationalized as a methodology for cross-program analysis?
7. Does second-personal knowledge scale from a dyad to a community without changing kind, or is community transmission of faith a distinct third thing? (Rohr↔Stump; PRS-39)
8. Is "revelation as self-disclosure of a Person rather than a deposit of propositions" (PRS-40) compatible with a critical-realist reading that holds the text's referential control, or does one have to give? (Rohr↔Wright)
9. Does the effects-of-the-reading criterion (PRS-42) survive the objection that it cannot rule out a reading that produces inclusion at the cost of saying something the text does not say? Rohr supplies no independent check on the philology once adjudication has moved off it.
10. Rohr's account requires that divine "need" (PRS-41) be a genuine structural claim, not anthropomorphic accommodation. Can that be held alongside classical divine simplicity, or is PRS-41 a real break with the Thomist side of the network?
11. **Independence, partially answered 2026-08-10 (see log).** Rohr and Stump both hold that suffering conveys knowledge of a person that propositional theodicy cannot carry. Stump's own retrospective account traces her route to that claim through mid-century individualist epistemology and shared-attention research, not devotional literature — evidence *for* independent arrival. The Rohr side remains open: Rohr's *Job* (1996) predates *Wandering in Darkness* (2010), so the chronology never supported influence in the worried direction anyway, and nobody has checked whether Rohr read Stump for the later CAC material.

12. **Univocity, and what it costs the network (PRS-48).** Rohr names Scotus and univocity of being in his own voice as the ontological ground of the Universal Christ. Univocity is the historic Scotist alternative to Thomist analogy and the disagreement is not verbal. Which of the recorded Rohr-Stump convergences survive that answer, and which turn out to be convergences in English only? Note the second half of the question: univocity is a thesis about *predication*, not about *substance*, so it must not be promoted to the monist wing (Kastrup, Levin, Hoffman) as a monism.
13. **Does the internal-target model survive formalization (PRS-49)?** This is Question 1 made answerable rather than a new question. The tradition now has a two-term structure with a stated invariant (image, given) and a stated update rule (likeness, polished), so it can be asked whether writing it down distorts it, instead of whether Rohr has a model at all.
14. **Gate or gradient (PRS-46)?** Rohr states the recognition constraint absolutely - only love already present internally can recognise love externally, and "all mystics are positive people, or they are not mystics." Fredrickson's upward-spiral work states something adjacent as a gradient and holds the measurements. Below threshold, is nothing perceived or merely less? The answer decides whether the recognition claim can be rescued from its definitional form.
15. **Precision or breadth (PRS-47)?** The tradition has been conflating two separable attentional parameters: engaging an object before it resolves, and widening rather than fixing the focal point. The first is consistent with the working guess that contemplation is precision reduction; the second is about the breadth of the generative model's attentional field. Which is the contemplative literature actually describing? The answer changes what a test would measure. Consider it together with PRS-51, which supplies the opposite-signed case - a practice that deliberately *sustains* uncertainty about the self-model for forty days.
16. **Do PRS-62 and PRS-65 conflict?** PRS-65 has joy arriving with grief fully intact and asserts no ordering between the two. PRS-62 asserts a strict ordering, that lament must precede the imagining of an alternative. Either they concern different pairs of states or one is wrong, and nothing in the corpus currently says which.
17. **Is the Enneagram type the shape the false self takes (PRS-56)?** The bridge between the typology strand (PRS-23 to PRS-25) and the true/false-self strand is inference from *Immortal Diamond* and is explicitly NOT ingested, because the Type 3 episode was captured metadata-only. Listening to the audio would settle it.
18. **Does Rohr's developmental reading of the prophetic corpus survive second-temple context (PRS-58)?** Rohr reads the prophets as a single author's arc from judgment toward patience. Wright's exile-and-restoration frame holds that the announced alternative future is covenantal and concrete rather than a moral posture. Either the developmental reading survives that context or it is a modern moral-psychology overlay, and the Rohr tradition is not the one that can decide it. Held open, not averaged.
19. **The Kushner fork, unadjudicated.** Week 31 platformed Harold Kushner's trade of omnipotence for solidarity and never chose. One reading is that Rohr's Sunday framing - God cannot be known through theology, law, or common sense, but only through relationship - IS the answer, in the sense that the omnipotence question is malformed rather than that either horn is true. That reading was NOT ingested as a triplet, because it attributes to Rohr a resolution he does not state; the alternative reading is that he simply left the fork open. It matters because the first reading has a cost: it forecloses the network's ability to use Rohr on any question requiring a determinate doctrine of divine power. The Wright agent is better placed to say which reading is right. This also bears on the fail-loud caveat carried by PRS-34, which assumes Rohr declines Kushner's move.

### Rohr↔Stump independence — CHECKED, not assumed
The master agent asked on 2026-08-07 for the independence of the Rohr/Stump convergence to be **checked rather than assumed**, flagging that Rohr's *Job and the Mystery of Suffering* (1996) predates Stump's *Wandering in Darkness* (2010). Today's Stump work retrieved the full text of her 2023 Dewey Lecture (see the Stump wiki's 2026-08-10 log), which is an intellectual autobiography and therefore unusually direct evidence about derivation. It bears on the question, and the finding is **partial support for independence**:

- Stump narrates her arrival at second-personal knowing as a **reaction against a specific mid-century orthodoxy** — reductionism plus "an unreflective privileging of an isolated individualism" in which knowledge is what an individual acquires alone by foundationalist or coherentist procedure. Her stated correctives are secular and technical: shared-attention and mind-reading research, the social character of language acquisition, testimony, and Wittgenstein on seeing emotion. She names **no devotional or contemplative source** anywhere in that passage.
- Her originating experience is textual and literary — Ecclesiastes, taught informally by a classics professor — not contemplative practice. The through-line she claims for her whole career is "the acknowledgement of unspeakable human suffering paradoxically coupled with a countervailing reverence for the beauty in human lives," which she says she has pursued since her sophomore year.

**Why this is only partial.** It is evidence about *Stump's* route, from Stump, in a genre that rewards self-presentation. It says nothing about whether Rohr's post-2010 CAC material absorbed Stump's vocabulary — and the vocabulary overlap in the recent weeks is striking ("second-personal," "knowledge of persons"). But note that the worry as originally posed had the arrow pointing the wrong way: 1996 predating 2010 is a reason Rohr *cannot* have been drawing on *Wandering in Darkness* for the *Job* material, not a reason to suspect he was. **The live independence question is the reverse one, and it is not answered:** did Stump's account shape how the CAC frames suffering after 2010, and did the proposals' own use of "second-personal" import Stump's category into Rohr's material rather than finding it there? Two of the three sources ingested yesterday use the term in the proposal's voice, not Rohr's. That is worth a look before the Rohr↔Stump CROSS-NN is confirmed.

**New standing tensions - hold open, do not average.**
- **Rohr - Stump, univocity versus analogy (PRS-48).** The sharpest internal incompatibility the network has surfaced. If the Rohr tradition is committed to univocal predication of being across creator and creature, several recorded Rohr-Stump convergences may be convergences in English only, and the existing cross-program entries need auditing rather than extending.
- **Rohr - Stump, theosis versus the Thomist image of God (PRS-49).** Both traditions use "image of God" and mean measurably different things by it. Rohr endorses "a true, objective sharing and growing in our divine nature" and concedes it reads as heterodox from a Western vantage.
- **Rohr - Stump, self-donative being versus divine simplicity (PRS-50).** The mirror image of Stump's own open question about whether the problem of mourning applies to God. Both traditions push on the same joint from opposite directions.
- **Rohr - Hoffman, whether the interface is penetrable (PRS-45).** Both posit a hidden connected substrate behind a veiling surface. Hoffman's veil is evolutionarily fixed and fitness-serving; Rohr's is lifted, by some. A tension, not the convergence it resembles.
- **Rohr - Fredrickson, gate versus gradient (PRS-46), and the undoing hypothesis (PRS-65).** Two independent disagreements. Fredrickson holds the measurements in both cases.
- **Rohr - Wright, developmental prophets versus second-temple context (PRS-58).** A third distinct Rohr-Wright tension, alongside the two already logged (whether a settled answer to evil is wanted; what adjudicates a rival reading).
- **Rohr - Friston, sustained self-model uncertainty as an anomaly (PRS-51).** To be routed as an anomaly rather than a convergence, and considered together with PRS-47's first parameter, which pulls the other way.
- **Rohr - Kastrup, illusory versus real-but-provisional separation (PRS-55).** The part/whole picture fits the dissociated-alter account structurally; the two differ on the status of the separation, and that is not a difference of emphasis. Rohr is not an idealist and PRS-52 must not be cited as though he were.

- **Rohr - Wright on Philippians 2:12-13, from opposite directions (PRS-49).** Wright reads the verse corporately as participation in the people God is forming; Rohr reads it interiorly as gift and wanting being one operation. Two theological oracles, one verse, the project's own central question, no evidence of contact. The strongest genuine convergence in this batch.
