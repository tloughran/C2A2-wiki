## Triplets

PRS-01:
  Label: P1 — Dualistic knowing
  Problem: Western rationalism defaults to either/or categorization, fragmenting reality into opposing camps and making genuine integration impossible
  Resource: The contemplative tradition's practice of non-dual awareness — holding both/and without premature resolution
  Solution: Contemplative epistemology as a complement to analytical reason; mature consciousness perceives unity-in-distinction rather than collapsing into false dichotomies
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: Everything Belongs (1999); The Naked Now (2009)
  Confidence: High

PRS-02:
  Label: P2 — The disorder necessary for growth
  Problem: Intellectual and spiritual traditions resist disruption, treating challenges to received order as threats rather than opportunities for deeper understanding
  Resource: The order-disorder-reorder pattern observed across contemplative traditions, developmental psychology, and the history of science
  Solution: Epistemological crises are not failures but necessary passages; the reorder that follows genuine disorder is deeper than the original order because it has been tested and survived
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: Falling Upward (2011); parallels MacIntyre's epistemological crisis
  Confidence: High

PRS-03:
  Label: P3 — The Universal Christ
  Problem: If truth is confined to one tradition, inter-tradition dialogue is at best diplomatic compromise and at worst intellectual imperialism
  Resource: The Franciscan theological claim that the divine pattern (the Christ) is present in all of creation, not confined to any single tradition or institutional expression
  Solution: A theological warrant for genuine multi-tradition inquiry — encountering truth through biology, mathematics, philosophy, and contemplation simultaneously is not eclecticism but a recognition of the universality of the pattern
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: The Universal Christ (2019)
  Confidence: Medium

PRS-04:
  Label: P4 — Embodied love as epistemology
  Problem: Abstract theological claims about love remain disconnected from empirical observation and biological reality
  Resource: Franciscan incarnational theology — the insistence that the spiritual is always embodied, never purely abstract
  Solution: Love as a biophysiological event (connecting to Fredrickson's positivity resonance) and a contemplative practice, not merely a theological concept; the body is a site of knowing
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: The Universal Christ, Chapter 6; connection to Fredrickson Love 2.0
  Confidence: Medium

PRS-05:
  Problem: How does the false self become the true self — what is the mechanism of contemplative transformation, not just its endpoint?
  Resource: Rohr's Enneagram-as-sacred-map — the characteristic wound/compulsion is the very site from which a person's distinctive wisdom and vocation emerge
  Solution: Transformation is not the erasure of the wounded pattern but its redemptive re-reading; the limitation becomes the gift (order-disorder-reorder applied to the self)
  Date Added: 2026-06-07
  Source: Introducing the Enneagram with Richard Rohr (Everything Belongs, Season 4) — https://cac.org/podcasts/introducing-the-enneagram-with-richard-rohr/; PROP-2026-05-31-003
  Confidence: Medium



PRS-6:
  Problem: How can a knower hold real commitments without the rigidity (and fragility) of "living by being right"?
  Resource: Rohr's beyond-binaries frame plus Dozier's maxim — "since I do not live by being right, I am not destroyed by being wrong"
  Solution: Non-dual cognition decouples identity from the correctness of one's current beliefs, enabling provisional, revisable commitment held "with open hands"; the dualistic mind, by contrast, must defend its boxes and so cannot metabolize error or paradox
  Date Added: 2026-06-17
  Source: Beyond Binaries (Daily Meditations, Week 22, ending 2026-06-06) — https://cac.org/daily-meditations/beyond-binaries-weekly-summary/; PROP-2026-06-07-002
  Confidence: Medium

PRS-7:
  Problem: Is relationship a derived feature of already-existing individuals, or is it ontologically prior?
  Resource: Rohr's perichoretic Trinity (from The Divine Dance) — God as "perfect giving and perfect receiving," relationship as "the very source, pattern, and goal of reality"
  Solution: Relation is basal: individuals are nodes within a prior givenness-and-receptivity, so the unit of reality is the exchange, not the isolated substance; salvation is conversion into that relational pattern, not an individual escape ("how do I get to heaven" is "a question of the ego, not of the Trinity")
  Date Added: 2026-06-17
  Source: A Pattern of Relationship (Daily Meditation, 'Embracing the Divine Exchange', adapted from The Divine Dance) — https://cac.org/daily-meditations/a-pattern-of-relationship/; PROP-2026-06-07-003

PRS-18:
  Problem: Contemporary contemplative literature often describes contemplative practice phenomenologically (what it feels like, what one experiences) but rarely *operationally* (what the stance does, mechanically, in the agent's engagement with the world). The result is a contemplative tradition that reads as quietistic to outsiders and as unverifiable to scientists.
  Resource: Rohr's lever-and-fulcrum frame: the contemplative stance is the fixed point from which a small applied force becomes a transformative force in the world. Mechanically specifiable.
  Solution: A geometrically-clean account of what contemplation *does* — it provides the stability that turns reactive action into leveraged action. The Summa-2026 syntheses can use this whenever the contemplative dimension is being articulated, and it pairs cleanly with the active-inference frame (Friston) and the agential-boundary frame (Levin).
  Date Added: 2026-04-12
  Source: Finding a Place to Stand (Daily Meditation, adapted from *Dancing Standing Still*); PROP-2026-05-18-004
