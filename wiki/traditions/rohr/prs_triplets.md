## Triplets

PRS-01:
  Label: P1 — Dualistic knowing
  Problem: Western rationalism defaults to either/or categorization, fragmenting reality into opposing camps and making genuine integration impossible
  Resource: The contemplative tradition's practice of non-dual awareness — holding both/and without premature resolution
  Solution: Contemplative epistemology as a complement to analytical reason; mature consciousness perceives unity-in-distinction rather than collapsing into false dichotomies
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: Everything Belongs (1999); The Naked Now (2009)
  Confidence: High

PRS-02:
  Label: P2 — The disorder necessary for growth
  Problem: Intellectual and spiritual traditions resist disruption, treating challenges to received order as threats rather than opportunities for deeper understanding
  Resource: The order-disorder-reorder pattern observed across contemplative traditions, developmental psychology, and the history of science
  Solution: Epistemological crises are not failures but necessary passages; the reorder that follows genuine disorder is deeper than the original order because it has been tested and survived
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: Falling Upward (2011); parallels MacIntyre's epistemological crisis
  Confidence: High

PRS-03:
  Label: P3 — The Universal Christ
  Problem: If truth is confined to one tradition, inter-tradition dialogue is at best diplomatic compromise and at worst intellectual imperialism
  Resource: The Franciscan theological claim that the divine pattern (the Christ) is present in all of creation, not confined to any single tradition or institutional expression
  Solution: A theological warrant for genuine multi-tradition inquiry — encountering truth through biology, mathematics, philosophy, and contemplation simultaneously is not eclecticism but a recognition of the universality of the pattern
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: The Universal Christ (2019)
  Confidence: Medium

PRS-04:
  Label: P4 — Embodied love as epistemology
  Problem: Abstract theological claims about love remain disconnected from empirical observation and biological reality
  Resource: Franciscan incarnational theology — the insistence that the spiritual is always embodied, never purely abstract
  Solution: Love as a biophysiological event (connecting to Fredrickson's positivity resonance) and a contemplative practice, not merely a theological concept; the body is a site of knowing
  Date Added: 2026-04-29
  Source: The Universal Christ, Chapter 6; connection to Fredrickson Love 2.0
  Confidence: Medium

PRS-34:
  Problem: Theodicy is standardly posed as an explanatory problem — produce a proposition reconciling divine goodness and power with unjust suffering. Every such proposition is either false to the phenomenology of grief or defensible only at the cost of trivializing it. The problem appears to have no satisfying propositional solution.
  Resource: The book of Job read as a text that deliberately withholds explanation and satirizes the explainers (the comforters), while still producing a determinate effect on the reader; plus Kushner's abandonment of omnipotence (Wednesday) as the explanatory route Rohr declines to take.
  Solution: Relocate the problem. The demand on a response to suffering is not that it explain but that it *realign* — that it correctly reposition the sufferer's self relative to reality. Job satisfies this demand while failing the explanatory one, which shows the two demands are separable and that the explanatory framing was the wrong framing.

PRS-35:
  Problem: If the postmodern predicament is a crisis of the self — no stable standpoint from which to adjudicate anything — then no argument can repair it, because arguments are evaluated *from* a standpoint. What repairs a standpoint?
  Resource: Rohr's claim that "what both Job and Jesus seem to say is that truth is finally a person and an encounter — much more than a concept that can be argued," coupled with the gradual-unveiling condition: the encounter is neither quick nor easy and is experienced as loss of self-importance and self-control.

PRS-53:

PRS-58:

PRS-63:

PRS-64:

PRS-65:

PRS-66:
  Problem: The Rohr corpus is heavily weighted toward individual transformation - non-dual mind, two halves of life, Universal Christ - and holds almost nothing on how a community that carries those commitments should be *structured*.   Solution: Relationship density, not doctrinal specification, is the load-bearing transmission mechanism. Fidelity is preserved by the network's connectivity rather than by its rule set, which predicts that formalizing the rule set is a *symptom* of the network having thinned rather than a remedy for it - and it predicts the fourth-century centralization Rohr names as the inflection point at which the church stopped being countercultural and non-imperial. The five-part specification then gives the tradition a falsifiable self-description, and its last term - mission execution over statement recitation - is close to an auditable criterion.
