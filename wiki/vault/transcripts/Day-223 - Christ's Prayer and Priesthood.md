## Notes

- Q.21: prayer is 'the unfolding of our will to God' (and the raising of the mind to God, Damascene); Christ prays vocally to teach us and to show his humanity ('Father, I thank thee that thou hast heard me,' John 11).
- Q.22: Christ is both *priest and victim* — he offers himself; the mediator (sacerdos) 'gives the sacred,' reconciles, makes satisfaction by obedience unto death.
- Q.22 a.6: Christ is a priest 'according to the order of Melchizedek' — superior to the Levitical priesthood, 'without beginning of days nor end of life,' a priest forever (Heb 7).
- Flag-worthy: the mediator who unfolds the human will to God and reconciles the many to God is the bridge-figure / loving-unity structure; priesthood as permanent intercession = sustained union.
