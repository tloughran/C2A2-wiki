## Notes

- Q.25: an indulgence is a remission of temporal punishment drawn from the *treasury* of the merits of Christ and the saints, applied by the Church's authority to a member.
- The treasury (thesaurus meritorum) is a shared, super-abundant store; the Church, holding the keys, dispenses it to individuals — the communion of saints as a common resource.
- Q.26: who can grant indulgences — those with jurisdiction (chiefly the pope, and bishops within limits); the power again descends from the power of the keys.
- Efficacy defended: indulgences truly avail before God to the extent of the authority granting and the devotion receiving.
- Flag-worthy: the treasury/communion is a shared informational-merit field the whole can apply to a part — one super-abundant resource meeting many members' deficits.
