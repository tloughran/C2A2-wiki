## Notes

- Q.23: grace 'supernaturalizes' us — adoption makes us heirs who share God's own beatitude (knowing/loving the divine essence); the good is self-diffusive, so the whole Trinity adopts.
- Q.23 a.4: Christ is the *natural* Son (the person is the eternal Word), not adopted — adoption is of persons, not natures.
- Q.24: predestination is attributed to Christ in respect of his *human* nature; Christ's predestination is the *exemplar* of ours and, as to its term, its *cause* — 'God so decreed our salvation that it should be achieved through Jesus Christ.'
- Flag-worthy: divinization-by-grace and shared beatitude is the loving-unity telos; Christ as exemplar-and-cause of our predestination = the bridge figure whose perspective grounds the limited ones'.
