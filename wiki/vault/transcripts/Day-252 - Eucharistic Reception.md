[06:48] sacrifice, a representation of Christ's sacrifice which is offered again now in time. It is efficacious not only to all those gathered at mass but across the

[08:20] distinction between a sacramental and a spiritual communion in the eukarist. That a man can receive the eucharist inattentively as we mentioned already,

sacramental eating is not without a veil, because the actual receiving of the sacrament produces more fully the effect of the sacrament than does the

desire thereof. Article two, whether it [09:24] belongs to man alone, to eat the sacrament spiritually. All are called not only to receive the sacrament with
