## Notes

- Q.33: Christ's body was *perfectly formed and animated in the first instant* of conception (by the infinite power of the agent), unlike the gradual ensoulment Aquinas, following Aristotle, posited for humanity generally.
- Q.33 a.3: the flesh was assumed *in* its conception, not conceived first then assumed — else it would be its own person, missing the hypostatic union.
- Q.34: from the first instant Christ was sanctified, had the use of free will, could merit, and was a *perfect comprehensor* (the beatific vision from conception, Mystici Corporis).
- Flag-worthy: comprehensor *from the first instant* is the wayfarer-and-comprehensor keystone (cf. Day 220) — the perspective that already beholds the whole while inhabiting a developing, limited human body; meritorious free choice = limited agency under grace.
