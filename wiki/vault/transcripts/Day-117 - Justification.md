## Notes

- The headline claim: "God alone is the cause of grace" — the kindling analogy (fire alone causes ignition) is doing real work. No created cause can produce a quality that exceeds every created nature.
- Preparation paradox: man must prepare himself for grace, but the preparation itself must be moved by *prevenient* (actual) grace. Quotes Phil 2:12 explicitly ("work out your own salvation with fear and trembling") — directly anchors the project's central theme.
- The grace-given-according-to-measure article (Q.112 a.4) is a strong opportunity to bridge to information-theoretic bandwidth: how much "above-natural" perspective can a given substrate hold.
- Justification = motion *from* state of sin *to* state of grace; involves (a) infusion of grace, (b) movement of free will toward God, (c) movement of free will against sin, (d) remission of sin. The structure is a state-transition diagram.
- Merit: "when God crowns our merits, he crowns nothing else but his own gifts" (Augustine). The whole apparatus of merit is divine self-giving cycling back as receivable reward — a recursive teleology Levin's morphogenetic teleology can be read against.
