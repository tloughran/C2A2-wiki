## Notes

- Q.182 a.1: nine reasons the contemplative life exceeds the active — chiefly that it engages the highest faculty, is self-sufficient, continues into eternity, and is the GOAL rather than a means. Dominican disclaimer: in particular cases of present need the active is preferable ('better to be wise than rich, but for one in need, better rich').
- Q.182 a.2: merit's principle is friendship with God (adoptive sonship by grace). Generically contemplative merit (love of God) exceeds active (love of neighbor, which is FOR love of God), but one who leaves contemplation out of overflowing love of God to serve others may merit more individually. Decisive: merit tracks the degree of charity in the heart, REGARDLESS of state. Strong central-theme hook.
- Q.182 a.3: active life disposes to contemplative by quelling the passions that breed phantasms. Working definition of contemplation: humans cannot think without phantasms (the triangle example) UNLESS God infuses truth directly as with angels — John of the Cross: contemplation is 'a secret, peaceful and loving infusion from God which... enkindles the soul with the spirit of love.' Mediated-abstraction vs infused-immediate knowing again.
- Q.183 a.2: diversity of states is fitting because God's utterly simple essence 'requires a multitude of various and splendid things to manifest his goodness' (rocks to angels); plus the Pauline body (1 Cor 12: if all were an eye...); plus hierarchy/order. CENTRAL-THEME CORE: the simple whole manifested only through a multitude of limited perspectives, composed into one body.
- Q.183 a.3: offices differ by perfection (religious state), action (married + clerical), and beauty (order — 'due proportion, harmony, right order is essential to beauty'), with a graded hierarchy. Beauty-as-order bridges to mathematical-beauty/positive-geometry registers.
- Q.183 a.4: states track beginning/middle/term — beginners, proficient, perfect. Habituation in charity makes one a 'slave of righteousness'; vice run the other way reduces freedom. Developmental ascent toward a target form.
