## Notes

- Q.53 a.1 division of labor: passion removes evils; resurrection is 'the beginning and exemplar of all good things' — inauguration language, directly Wright's territory (new creation begun, not postponed).
- Q.53 a.3 perfect vs imperfect resurrection: Lazarus and Moses revived to mortal life; Christ first to attain life utterly immortal — a genuinely new mode of embodiment, not resuscitation.
- Q.54 a.1-3 same body, same soul, entire (flesh, bones, blood) but differing in glory — identity-with-transformation; the form persists, the mode changes.
- Q.54 a.4 scars as 'trophies' and perpetual intercession — the wound retained as glory, not defect; Rohr's wound-to-wisdom pattern in bodily key.
- Q.53 a.2 third-day timing balances proof-of-real-death against speedy confirmation of faith — evidential reasoning inside the fittingness argument.
