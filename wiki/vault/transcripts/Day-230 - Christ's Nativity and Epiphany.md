## Notes

- Q.35: *nativity regards the person* (the subject born), the nature as its terminus — therefore Mary is truly *Mother of God* (Theotokos, Ephesus), bearing the person who is the eternal Word.
- Q.35 a.5: only the *eternal* sonship is a real relation in Christ; the temporal sonship to Mary is a relation of reason — yet Mary is genuinely his mother in respect of the temporal nativity.
- Q.36: Christ's birth made known not to all but to representatives of every condition (Simeon/Anna, shepherds/magi — male/female, Jew/Gentile, poor/rich) 'that no condition of man was excluded'; faith preserved by partial manifestation (Heb 11).
- Flag-worthy: the manifestation to *every category of humanity* 'that none be excluded' is the radical-inclusivity / loving-unity telos (Fredrickson PRS-06, Rohr Universal Christ); faith-preserved-by-hiddenness is the perspective-limitation structure (we are not given the whole).
