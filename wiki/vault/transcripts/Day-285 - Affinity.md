## Notes

- Affinity: relationship by marriage as an impediment, and how affinity is contracted.
- Affinity arises from the one-flesh union, extending kinship to the spouse's blood relations.
- Hook: the union literally re-draws the boundary of who counts as kin — multiscale boundary redrawing (Levin/Kastrup).
- Rohr non-dual relational ontology.
