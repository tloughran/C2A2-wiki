## Notes

- Aquinas: indissolubility of marriage is of *natural law*, grounded in lifelong parental concern for offspring (Q.67 a.1) — the bond serves an end that outlasts any moment.
- The Mosaic bill of divorce treated as a *dispensation* from a secondary precept, tolerated to prevent a greater evil — same 'tolerated, not ideal' logic as the polygamy run (Day 290).
- Q.68 on illegitimacy: four birth-statuses (natural/legitimate etc.) and legitimization as removal of legal disabilities — a juridical, status-conferral mechanism, not a change in the child.
- Flag-worthy: legitimacy is a *socially conferred standing* the law can add or withdraw — a hook for thinking about identity/standing as relational rather than intrinsic.
