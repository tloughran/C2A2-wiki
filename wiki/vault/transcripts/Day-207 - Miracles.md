# Day 207 — Miracles

Context: Day 207 covers II-II Q.178 (the grace of miracles) and Q.179 (the division of life into active and contemplative). Austin Habash treats miracles as the gratuitous grace that confirms the preached truth, distinguishes genuine miracles (which require the authority of the Creator who alone can suspend the laws of nature) from the merely spectacular or illusory works of demonic power, and reiterates that without charity even mountain-moving faith is nothing ('I never knew you'). He then opens the active/contemplative division through Martha and Mary, grounding it in the twofold exhaustive activity of the intellect.

## Notes

- Q.178 a.1: miracles are a gratuitous grace 'unto the profit of others,' confirming proclaimed truth — 'just as the grace of the word is necessary that people may be instructed, so the grace of miracles is necessary that people may be confirmed.'
- Q.178 a.1: the 'lawless one' (2 Thess 2) works *pretended* signs — not real miracles, because 'only the legislator of the laws of nature can suspend or supersede those laws.' Demonic works are spectacular-but-natural (Pharaoh's magicians) or illusions to the external/internal senses. Sharp distinction: authority over the generative rules vs manipulation within them / of the senses.
- Q.178 a.2: the wicked can work wonders but 'I never knew you' — without charity, nothing (1 Cor 13). Charity (love of God to self-contempt) is the grace to pursue; wonders only to be desired if 'expedient for others.' Continues the charisms-subordinate-to-charity thread from Days 204-206.
- Q.179 a.1: 'the life of every man is that wherein he delights most' (basketball, money) — to follow Christ is to subordinate every prior chief aim. Martha (active service) and Mary (contemplation, purity of heart) as the two means to the one end.
- Q.179 a.2: the active/contemplative division is jointly exhaustive because it follows the twofold exhaustive activity of intellect — contemplating truth simply (speculative) vs entertaining truth for the sake of action (practical). Maps cleanly to perception/inference vs action/policy.
