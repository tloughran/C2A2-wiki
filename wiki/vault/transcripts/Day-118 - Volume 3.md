## Notes

- This is the volume-transition episode: 20 articles compressed, finishing Prima Secundae (Q.114, merit) and opening Secunda Secundae (Q.1, faith). Treat as bridge.
- Merit framework: there is no strict justice between creature and Creator; merit is possible only because God *wills* to make it so by elevating man to adopted sonship. Justice within the household of God, not justice between independent parties.
- Charity is the principle of merit — not the other virtues alone. "What we do out of love we do most willingly" — voluntariness is the qualifying condition. Direct Fredrickson hook: positivity resonance as biological substrate of the love that quickens merit.
- The first grace is not meritable; once given, increase in grace is. This sets up an asymmetry that maps cleanly onto active-inference learning curves where the prior cannot be earned by the agent but updates can be.
- Q.1 of II-II opens with the object of faith: faith's material object is varied (articles of the creed) but its formal object is *the First Truth*. The synthesis should mark this — faith's unity is in what it cleaves to, not what it inventories.
