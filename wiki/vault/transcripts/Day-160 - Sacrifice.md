## Notes

- Q.84: adoration = external manifestation of internal reverence; *latria* (to God alone) vs *dulia* (to creatures rightly honored); body and soul both adore — bodily prostration is fitting.
- Q.85: sacrifice — the *external* act proper to religion; offered to God alone; rooted in *natural law* (every people in every age has offered sacrifice).
- Aquinas: a sacrifice is whatever is *done for the honor properly due to God* in order to appease Him; the inner sacrifice (the offered will) is its substance, the outer sign is the manifestation.
- Augustine on sacrifice: "every work done that we may cling to God in holy fellowship" — the central-theme reading: sacrifice = enacted re-alignment of a part with the whole.
- Sets up Days 161+ (oaths, vows, tithing, the active life) — religion continuing to unfold as the *form* of justice toward God.
