## Notes

- Q.52 a.2 presence by effect vs presence by essence — Christ 'in' each hell differently; a precise theory of differentiated presence worth setting against field/interface models of locality.
- Q.52 a.5 the limbo of the fathers: justified individuals still blocked by a *common* race-level defect until satisfaction made — individual cleansing insufficient for the corporate barrier (mirrors Day 237 Q.49 a.5).
- Q.52 a.8 purgatory untouched: personal defect vs common defect cleanly distinguished — the descent removes only the shared barrier.
- Q.51 a.2 dense burial symbolism (garden ↔ Eden reversal; stranger's grave; new sepulchre ↔ virginal womb) — pattern-level correspondences doing theological work.
- Host's aside in Q.52 a.7 (naturally happy unbaptized children) is explicitly flagged by him as a passing speculation, not Aquinas.
