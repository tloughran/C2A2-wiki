## Notes

- Q.124 treats martyrdom: the supreme act of fortitude, witness unto death; the martyr stands firm in truth against the gravest danger.
- Q.125 treats fear/timidity (timor) as the vice of excess: fear that flees the good it should pursue, making one shrink from duty.
- Martyrdom as holding a long-horizon attractor (inclusion in the people of God / new creation) against the strongest immediate free-energy gradient — death (Friston; Wright on resurrection).
- Fear narrows; the martyr's freedom is openness to the whole (Fredrickson broaden-and-build inverse; McGilchrist).
