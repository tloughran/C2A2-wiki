## Notes

- Q.83 subtlety: the body's 'subtlety' is caused by the *soul's* grasp of its matter; the more fully the soul informs the body, the subtler the body — a property that scales with the depth of form's control.
- Two bodies in one place is naturally impossible (dimensive quantity distinguishes individuals) but possible *by divine power alone* — paralleled to accidents-without-substance in the Eucharist; Christ through closed doors, the virginal birth.
- Palpable by nature, impalpable to non-glorified bodies by God's power; the glorified body keeps its nature, gains glory (Gregory: 'unchanged in nature, changed in glory').
- Q.84 agility: ease/swiftness of local motion overflowing from the soul's perfection; motion is still *temporal* (no instantaneous motion — actualizing a potential), but imperceptibly fast, with bodies differing in speed.
- Flag: subtlety/agility = the body's responsiveness to the will/soul taken to its limit — a 'control overflows from the higher to the lower' picture worth reading against multi-scale agency.
