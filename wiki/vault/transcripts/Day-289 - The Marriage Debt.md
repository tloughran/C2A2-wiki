## Notes

- The marriage debt (debitum) — the mutual conjugal obligation, drawn from 1 Cor 7.
- Aquinas: the debt is mutual and equal; neither spouse has sole power over their own body.
- Hook: symmetric mutual self-giving = the paradigm of loving unity from two limited perspectives.
- Wright primary on the Pauline text (1 Cor 7); Rohr PRS-04 embodied love as epistemology; Rohr PRS-7 relationship prior.
