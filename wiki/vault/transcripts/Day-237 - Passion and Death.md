## Notes

- Q.49 a.1 the mystical body: the whole church reckoned 'as one person with its head' — redemption works through corporate incorporation, hand redeeming the foot. The strongest multi-scale-agency bridge in the run.
- Q.49 a.5 heaven's gates closed by a *race-level* debt plus personal sins — two distinct scales of fault, both addressed in the passion.
- Q.49 a.6 humility-exaltation reciprocity: stripping oneself justly merits recompense (Luke 14:11) — a conservation-like moral symmetry.
- Q.50 a.4 hylomorphic identity: Christ was not 'a man' during the three days, simply speaking — the soul as formal complement; form-based identity under interruption.
- Q.50 a.5 the dead body is 'identically the same' only via the person as subject — identity carried by the hypostasis, not by unchanged matter.
