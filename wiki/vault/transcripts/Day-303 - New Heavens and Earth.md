## Notes
- **Renewal, not replacement.** New heavens and new earth (2 Pet 3) — not a change of *species* but an added "perfection of glory," so the bodily senses too may partake of God via his corporeal effects. Cosmic renewal, world and man glorified "at the one same time."
- **Motion ceases.** Heavenly movement terminates in rest (rest nobler than motion, likeness to the immovable God); brightness of luminaries increased.
- **Austin flags Aquinas's cosmology as dated.** On whether plants/animals persist, Austin explicitly says Aquinas's argument leans on a medieval physics of celestial matter "which I don't believe he would hold if he was alive today" — a clean evidence-strength marker.
- **Direct informing of the mind.** Q.92: the created intellect cannot abstract God's essence from any creature; God must *supernaturally elevate the mind to be informed directly by his essence* — "knowing God by becoming God, at least in the mind." No representational mediation.
- **Vision without comprehension.** The blessed see God truly yet do NOT comprehend him / do not know all God knows — the finite intellect grasps the infinite really but not exhaustively. Perspective remains partial even in beatitude.
