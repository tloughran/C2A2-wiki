## Notes

- Q.7 a.1: Christ's soul has habitual grace — the created grace by which His humanity is sanctified — distinct from the grace of union itself; the finite humanity is graced, not absorbed.
- Q.7 a.9-12: whether Christ's grace was infinite and whether it could increase — the grace is the greatest possible for a creature yet remains a *creature's* grace; pertinent to perspective-limitation even at the summit.
- Q.8 a.1: Christ is the Head of the Church — capital grace flows from Him to the members as from a head to a body; the central image of a whole whose fullness is communicated to its parts.
- Q.8 a.5-7: Christ is Head of all men (in varying degrees) and even of the angels; the cosmic scope of headship — Rohr's Universal Christ register.
- Flag-worthy: 'capital grace' (gratia capitis) as the influx of a head into its members = a direct image of the loving-unity-of-perspectives telos and of Markov-blanket / positivity-resonance scale-bridging.
