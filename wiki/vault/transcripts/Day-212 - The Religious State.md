## Notes
- Numbering: the host corrects himself ('186 which I skipped last time') but then labels Q.189 as 'question 187'; the actual content is Q.186 (the three vows) and Q.189 (entrance). Loci recorded from content, not the host's spoken numbers.
- The three vows as 'holocaust'/total offering (Q.186): poverty removes lust of riches, continence the lust of the flesh, obedience the pride of life; obedience is chief because by it a man gives God his very will/capacity to choose. Strong material for the perspective-surrender/individuation thread.
- Q.189 a.1: religious life is for beginners AND the practiced — a school of perfection, not a club for the already-perfect (contrast with the episcopal state, the 'professorship of perfection,' Day 210).
- Q.189 a.10/a.12: the host's own Carthusian-to-active discernment surfaces (leaving one order for a 'more excellent kind'); and the striking closer — for an objectively good end like entering religion one need NOT deliberate long, only about *which* order. 'Religion is the sweet yoke of Christ.'
- 'Work out his salvation in a monastery' (a.7, parish priest) — note the Phil. 2:12 language appears verbatim in Aquinas's source, the project's central-theme verse.
