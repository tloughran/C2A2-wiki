## Notes

- Q.48 a.6 instrumental causality — Christ's humanity as the instrument of the Godhead (stone-and-stick illustration) is the episode's metaphysical center; pairs directly with instrument/interface framings in the synthesis.
- Q.48 a.2 atonement definition: offering something the offended one loves more than he detested the offense — superabundance via charity, extent of suffering, dignity of the life laid down. Stump's love-and-union atonement work is the contemporary anchor.
- Q.48 a.1 merit from the moment of conception, with the passion removing *obstacles on our side* — salvation already won, blockage removal as the operative frame.
- Q.47 a.5-6 the rulers knew the Messiah but not the godhead — culpable partial knowledge; perspective-limitation with moral weight.
- Q.47 a.2-3 obedience requires two wills; divine simplicity means one divine will — the Father delivers up Christ without tritheism of wills.
