## Notes
- **The dower (dowry) of the blessed.** "Everlasting adornment of soul and body adequate to life lasting forever in bliss," given to the Bride (the Church) on the marriage analogy (Eph 5). The light of glory is the dower-as-habit; beatitude itself is the act of vision.
- **Three dowries ↔ three theological virtues.** Vision/faith, comprehension/hope, fruition/charity.
- **The aureoles (halos/crowns).** Accidental rewards for *exceptional victories*: virgins (victory over the flesh), martyrs (over the world), doctors/teachers (over the devil — by expelling error "not from himself alone but from others also"). Differentiated reward for differentiated contribution.
- **The doctor's crown.** Teachers are crowned for *converting and confirming others* in truth — articulating and propagating right understanding. Directly resonant with a project about richly informing one another's perspectives.
- **Degrees within a kind.** Crowns vary even within one type (the flame red→yellow→blue analogy); intensity of crown tracks intensity of merit in the specific act. Individuation by the precise form and degree of one's victory.
