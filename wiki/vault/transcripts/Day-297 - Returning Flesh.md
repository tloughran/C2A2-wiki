## Notes

- Q.79: the soul returns to the *same identical body* — else it is 'not resurrection but the assuming of a new body'; identity of the person requires numerical identity of the body (Job 19 'in my flesh I shall see God').
- Decisive move (a.3, a.5): sameness is by 'totality of species' (quantity, shape, position, order of parts) — NOT 'totality of matter'. Not every past cell returns; the *form/organization* secures identity.
- Aquinas openly lacks the biology (Austin notes this) to adjudicate which matter returns — an explicit invitation to re-map the question onto modern accounts of bodily continuity.
- Q.80: all members, hair, nails, needful humors rise for the body's 'perfection'; no generative/nutritive fluids (no procreation/eating in the risen state, per Luke 20).
- Flag: 'same body = same form, not same matter' is almost exactly Levin's pattern-persists-through-turnover and the ship-of-Theseus answer — the central bridge for this day.
