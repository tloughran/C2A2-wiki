## Notes

- Q.86 (stain of sin -- macula): the stain is the privation of the soul's beauty (its proper order to God) that remains after the act of sin is past. Not a physical residue but a real metaphysical disordering that persists until restored by grace.
- Habash emphasizes that the stain is in the soul's relation to grace, not in any property the soul has 'in itself' apart from God. It's relational, not substantive.
- Q.87 (debt of punishment): every sin involves a disturbance of order; restoration of order requires punishment (poena). Aquinas distinguishes punishment as restoration of justice vs as medicinal correction vs as deterrent.
- Habash flags the merit/punishment economy: just as merits can be drawn on (treasury of merit), so the debt of punishment can be remitted, transferred, or compensated through participation in the body of Christ. Anticipates indulgence theology.
- Flag for synthesis: this is Stump PRS-05 territory (suffering and divine permission) -- the question of how persistent disorder in the generative model is restored, and whether the work of restoration can be communal (Stump PRS-14 corporate substance).
