## Notes

- Vestments, the rite of ordination, and impediments that bar or invalidate reception of Orders.
- Distinguishes worthiness from validity — an unworthy minister still confers a valid sacrament.
- Hook: role vs. agent; the office persists independent of the individual's epistemic state (ex opere operato).
- Bridges to Friston's mutual-modeling and the question of who may occupy a node in a collective.
