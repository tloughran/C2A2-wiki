## Notes

- Q.118 treats covetousness/avarice (avaritia): immoderate love of possessing; 'the root of all evils' (1 Tim 6:10) per Aquinas.
- Q.119 treats prodigality: the opposite excess — wasteful giving/spending; Aquinas holds it a lesser and more curable fault than covetousness.
- Covetousness as defection from the cooperative whole — strong bridge to Levin's cancer-as-defection (cells revert to selfish unicellular behavior, lose integration).
- Friston angle: covetousness as corrupted resource-estimate, over-provisioning against modeled future surprise.
