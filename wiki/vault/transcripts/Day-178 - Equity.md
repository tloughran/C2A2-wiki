## Notes

- Q.120 treats equity (epikeia): the higher justice that corrects the letter of the law by the legislator's intent when literal application would fail the law's purpose.
- Q.121 treats piety (pietas): reverence and duty owed to parents and country, a part of justice annexed to the worship of God.
- Equity = right-hemisphere attention to context/intent correcting left-hemisphere literalism (McGilchrist); laws as useful higher-level stories serving an underlying good (Carroll, poetic naturalism).
- Piety = recognition of the larger nested agent (family, polity) of which one is a part (Levin scale-up of self).
