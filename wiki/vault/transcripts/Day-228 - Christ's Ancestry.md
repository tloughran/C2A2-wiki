## Notes

- Q.31: Christ's flesh derived from Adam (to save Adam's race) and David's line (prophecy; the mingling of the priestly Levi and royal Judah lines in Mary); the Matthew/Luke genealogies reconciled (royal vs. priestly personality, Augustine).
- Q.31 a.4: by taking flesh from a woman Christ 'rounds out' all modes of human origin (Adam from earth, Eve from Adam, all others from man+woman, Christ from woman alone) — and ennobles the female sex.
- Q.32: the conception is attributed to the Holy Spirit (the personal Love of God; grace and adoption appropriated to the Spirit); Mary supplied only corporeal matter, not active seminal power.
- Flag-worthy: 'rounding out all modes of human origin' is a form-bearing-matter / hylomorphism theme (Levin); attribution of conception to Love-as-Spirit connects to the loving-unity telos and Fredrickson's love-as-coupling.
