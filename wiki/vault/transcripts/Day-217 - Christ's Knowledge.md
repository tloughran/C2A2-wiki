# Day 217 — Christ's Knowledge

> Episode: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gq7Samlh5DQ>
> Summa reference: *Tertia Pars (III), Q.9-10*

Context: Day 217 covers III Q.9 (Christ's knowledge in general — whether He had a knowledge besides the divine, and the threefold human knowledge: the beatific, the infused, and the acquired) and III Q.10 (the beatific knowledge of Christ's soul — whether His created soul comprehended the Word, knew all things in the Word, and knew the infinite). ## Notes

- Q.9 a.1: 'everything is for the sake of operation' (Aristotle) — a human intellect with no knowledge would be 'to no purpose'; Christ's human mind is a real, operative finite knower, not a divine-knowledge bypass.
- Q.9: the threefold human knowledge — beatific (vision of the divine essence), infused/imprinted, and acquired/empirical — three distinct modes the one soul possesses.
- Q.10 a.1: the created finite mind of the incarnate Word truly knows God 'as He is' (beholds the essence) yet does NOT *comprehend* it: 'the infinite is not comprehended by the finite' (Damascene). The keystone for perspective-limitation.
- Flag-worthy: beholding-without-comprehending is exactly the central-theme claim — even the highest finite perspective sees the whole truly yet not exhaustively; the limitation is constitutive, not a defect to be removed.
