## Notes

- Tale-bearing = repeating disparagement *to sever a friendship*; its formal object is the destruction of social bonds, not (as in detraction) of reputation.
- Derision = humiliating another by mocking their faults. Both are species of verbal sin against neighbor.
- Aquinas: tale-bearing is among the gravest verbal sins because friendship is the supreme external good (citing Aristotle's *Ethics*).
- The thread across 154-155: each verbal injustice attacks a different *axis* of community — honor, reputation, friendship, dignity — naming the distinct goods the wider whole protects.
