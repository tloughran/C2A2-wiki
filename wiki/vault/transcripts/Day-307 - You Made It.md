## Notes

- FINAL EPISODE of the Summa in a Year podcast. Austin closes: "this is the close of the Summa in a Year podcast... I cannot wait to see us both by the grace of God... in heaven."
- Locus: Supplement Q.99 (further on God's mercy and justice toward the damned), Appendix 1 Q.1-2 (the souls who depart with original sin only — the limbo of infants; and souls expiating actual sin or its punishment in purgatory), and Appendix 2 (two articles on purgatory).
- Three states beyond death are surveyed together here: the damned (fixed loss), those with original sin only (natural happiness without the beatific vision — limbo), and the purgatorial (temporary purification, ordered toward the vision). The whole eschatological map is laid out at the very end.
- Flag-worthy for synthesis: graded final states rather than binary; purgatory as a *process* of purification (becoming-fit for union) vs. the fixity of Q.97-98; limbo as natural-good-without-the-vision (a perspective complete on its own terms yet not united to the whole).
- The finale invites a whole-series closer: the entire Summa has been a map of the exitus-reditus — all things from God and all things returning to God — and the last word is union (heaven) reached through purification.
- Bridges: Suppl. last things -> Wright (primary: Surprised by Hope, resurrection/new-creation), Carroll (time), Kastrup (persistence/dissociation), Rohr (universal restoration, the cosmic Christ as telos of the return).
