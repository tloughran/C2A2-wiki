## Notes

- Impediment of consanguinity (blood kinship) and degrees of relation barring marriage.
- Aquinas grounds incest prohibition in both natural reverence and the widening of social bonds.
- Hook: kinship boundaries and the widening of community — Friston PRS-18 (widening a community).
- Central theme: marriage as a mechanism for extending loving union beyond the already-near.
