## Notes

- Q.116 treats quarreling/contention (contentio) as the vice opposed to affability by defect — abrasive contradiction that fractures civic bonds.
- Q.117 treats liberality (liberalitas): the right disposition toward external goods, giving freely; a part of justice, not its own debt-of-strict-right.
- Aquinas: liberality is loved because the generous person uses money for others' good, not self; the mean is internal (right affection toward wealth), not the amount given.
- Bridge target: liberality as expansion of the self/care-boundary (Levin); generosity as bond-building (Fredrickson); value of giving as a real higher-level pattern (Carroll).
