## Notes

- Q.29: Christ born of an *espoused* virgin for his protection, genealogy, and Mary's protection; the marriage of Mary and Joseph was 'absolutely true' in its first perfection (the inseparable union of souls) though unconsummated.
- Q.30 a.1: the Annunciation was needed so Mary might make a free *act of faith* — 'more blessed in receiving the faith of Christ than in conceiving the flesh of Christ' (Augustine); she receives the God-man on humanity's behalf.
- Q.30: a fitting reversal — a fallen angel brought news that downed mankind, a heavenly angel brings news of salvation; Mary's 'be it done unto me' (fiat) is free consent.
- Flag-worthy: Mary's *free consent on behalf of humankind* is the central-theme structure — a limited human perspective freely aligning with the One who sees the whole, and a community-representative act (incorporation, Wright).
