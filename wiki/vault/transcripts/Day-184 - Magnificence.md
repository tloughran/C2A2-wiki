## Notes

- Magnificence (Q.134) is the virtue of making/doing great works requiring great expenditure, done fittingly and for a worthy end — generosity at scale.
- Meanness / parvificence (Q.135) is the defect: stinting and penny-counting on great works one has the means and occasion to do.
- Aquinas distinguishes magnificence from liberality: it concerns the great work itself, not merely the right use of money — flag for the build-at-scale angle.
- The episode is out of broadcast sequence: locus Q.134-135 here, after Day 185's Q.132-133. Habash misspeaks the numbers as "132 and 133" in this episode and acknowledges the inversion in Day 185.
