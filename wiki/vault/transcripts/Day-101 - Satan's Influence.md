## Notes

- Q.80: the devil cannot directly cause sin -- only the agent's own will can move it. Satan acts only by suggestion / object-presentation, working on imagination and passions (which are material in us, hence reachable by an immaterial substance that can also move chairs).
- Habash's exegesis: only God can move the will from within; only the intellect can propose its object; Satan has access to neither. He can affect 'everything material about us' -- imagination and passions.
- Q.80 articles 3-4: not every sin is from the devil; some sins are sufficiently explained by fleshly passion or environmental occasion. Augustine and Damascene cited.
- Q.81 (cause on the part of man): introduces original sin via Adam -- first sin transmitted by generation, not imitation (Romans 5).
- Flag for synthesis: cleanest case in the Summa where a non-monist demonology is metaphysically load-bearing. Mind-monism reframes 'angelic intervention' as a question about the topology of mind, not the population count of immaterial substances.
