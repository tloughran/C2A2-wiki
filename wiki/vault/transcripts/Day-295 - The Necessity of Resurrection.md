## Notes

- Q.75 a.1: resurrection is *necessary* because perfect happiness is unattainable in this life and the soul is the *form of the body* — a disembodied soul is in an 'unnatural', incomplete state (Savonarola quoted to the same end).
- All rise, good and evil, because merit/demerit was earned as embodied persons and 'a separated soul is not, strictly, a man' — embodiment is constitutive of the human, not a husk.
- Resurrection is *not natural*: as the rational soul is created from nothing to inform matter, only God can reunite soul to body — agency that exceeds any creaturely power.
- Q.76: Christ's resurrection is cause of ours (equivocal as God, univocal as man, instrumental through his humanity); angels may gather the remains but only God reunites soul to body.
- Flag: 'soul as form, incomplete without body' is the hylomorphic keystone — the synthesis's chance to read form as Levin's anatomical target-state / Hoffman's interface, not a ghost.
