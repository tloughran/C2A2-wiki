## Notes

- Q.73: signs preceding judgment; Augustine's multi-sense reading (signs apply to both Jerusalem's fall and the end) — Scripture bears many senses in one statement.
- Q.74: the world must be *cleansed* by fire before the new heavens/earth — purification of a defiled-but-not-destroyed cosmos; elements changed in state, not annihilated ('substance not consumed').
- The same fire acts uniformly by 'natural virtue' (reduces all bodies to ashes) yet differently as 'instrument of divine justice' (pain to the wicked, none to the good) — one process, perspective-relative effect.
- Aquinas explicitly flags that much of the detail rests on 'an outdated cosmology' (Austin's words) — invites a clean re-mapping onto contemporary cosmology/thermodynamics.
- Flag: 'cleansing not annihilation' + 'set the heavens at rest' is a renewal-of-creation eschatology, not escape-from-matter — Wright's new-creation, against the gnostic read.
