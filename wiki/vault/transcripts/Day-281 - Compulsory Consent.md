## Notes

- Compulsory, conditional, and coerced consent — when fear or conditions vitiate consent.
- Coerced consent is no consent; the will must be free for the bond to form.
- Hook: agency under resource/constraint — active inference requires an unconstrained policy selection.
- Bridges: Friston on free policy selection; the central theme's 'fear and trembling' as the opposite of coercive fear.
