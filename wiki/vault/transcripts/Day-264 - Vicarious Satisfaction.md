## Notes
- Q.13 possibility of satisfaction + Q.14 quality (incl. vicarious satisfaction). A finite creature cannot render God infinite recompense by quantity, but can by a proportion/orientation (as with the command to love with whole heart) — finitude met by genuineness, not magnitude.
- Vicarious satisfaction: one can satisfy for another — corrective work distributed across agents (collective active inference; Wright on corporate solidarity / bearing one another's burdens; Rohr on the shared Body).
- Good deeds void punishment even more than omitting evil — positive corrective action, not mere avoidance (Fredrickson broaden-and-build resonance).
- Theme hook: the perspective-limited agent cannot match the infinite whole by quantity but is included by proportionate, and even vicariously shared, turning.
