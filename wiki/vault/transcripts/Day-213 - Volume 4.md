# Day 213 — Volume 4

> Episode: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4RZoxk1nScE>
> Summa reference: *Tertia Pars (III), Q.1-2*

Context: Day 213 opens the Tertia Pars (Volume 4 of the five-volume set) and the treatise on the Incarnation. It covers III Q.1 (the fitness of the Incarnation — whether it was fitting, necessary, conditioned on sin, ordered to actual vs original sin, the timing 'in the fullness of time,' and why not deferred to the end) and III Q.2 (the mode of union of the Word Incarnate — that the union is in the Person, not the nature; that there are two natures and one hypostasis; the rejection of mixture, accident, and form-matter accounts; union as created relation; union vs assumption; the greatest of unions; and whether union came by grace or merit). Austin Habash uses extended physical analogies — gravity, fire, chemical combination — to frame the self-diffusion of the good and the modes of union.

## Notes
- The 'good is self-diffusive' argument for fitness (Q.1 a.1) is delivered through physics analogies: mass-gravitation, the sun's reach to the Oort cloud, a bonfire making the room like itself, procreation as maturity. Bonum diffusivum sui rendered in field/thermodynamic imagery — rich for a Levin/Friston/Carroll bridge.
- Modes-of-union taxonomy (Q.2 a.1): arrangement (a chair), chemical combination (H2 + O2 -> water, where components are only virtually present), and form-matter — all rejected. The hypostatic union is union in the PERSON, 'two natures and one hypostasis' (Damascene). Central image for unity-in-distinction without confusion.
- Q.2 a.7: the union 'is not really in God but only in our way of thinking' — God is united to the creature with no change in Him; the union is something created, in time. Flag: asymmetry of the relation (cf. Hoffman interface / Kastrup mind-at-large).
- Q.2 a.10-11: the union is by grace (gratuitous, unmerited); no merit of the human nature preceded it, for from conception the suppositum was the Word. 'God was made man that man might be made God' (Athanasius via Augustine) cited.
- Host's ASR: 'Garrigou-Lagrange' (rendered 'Lrange/the grange'), 'Eutyches' (mixture heresy, rendered 'Udakis'), 'Nestorius' (accidental-indwelling heresy, rendered 'notorious'). Cleaned in text.
