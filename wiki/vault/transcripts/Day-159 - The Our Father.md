## Notes

- Q.82: devotion = the will to *promptly* serve God; cause of joy and (accidentally) of sorrow; chiefly caused by meditation on God's goodness and on our defects.
- Q.83: prayer — proper to the rational creature (we *bend* providence to our needs by asking, not by *changing* it); we should pray for definite goods; for our enemies; for others.
- The Lord's Prayer is treated as the *exemplar* of all prayer — it asks for the right things in the right order (God's honor first; then bodily and spiritual needs; deliverance from evil).
- Aquinas: prayer's causality is not "informing God of what He doesn't know" but *aligning us with the providence already at work* — direct anchor for the central-theme reading of prayer as perspective-calibration toward the whole.
- Sevenfold structure of the Pater: three God-ward petitions (name, kingdom, will) + four creature-ward petitions (bread, forgiveness, temptation, evil) — the same two-pole structure of self+God that ran through Day 131's charity treatise.
