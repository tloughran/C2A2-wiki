## Notes

- Q.78: usury = charging for the *use* of a thing whose use is its consumption (money). Aquinas's argument: selling something that does not exist.
- Q.79: the quasi-integral parts of justice — doing good / declining evil — the two-sided structure of any just act.
- Modern caveat the podcast itself raises: today money is no longer a pure measure but participates in productive capital (insurance, time-value, opportunity cost) — so the medieval bright line is more nuanced today.
- Substantive opening for the contemporary parallel: distinguish *productive interest* (genuine resource transfer) from *extractive usury* (rent-seeking on nothing).
