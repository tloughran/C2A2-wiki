## Notes

- Q.128 enumerates the parts of fortitude (magnificence, magnanimity, patience, perseverance) — the secondary virtues annexed to the cardinal one.
- Magnanimity (Q.129) is the right relation to one's own greatness and to honor: aiming at great things proportionate to one's real worth, in accordance with reason.
- It is explicitly NOT pride: magnanimity is calibrated to truth about oneself; its disorder lies in over- or under-estimation (presumption / pusillanimity).
- Aquinas ties magnanimity to confidence, security, and the right use of honor — flag for the accurate-self-model and resource-estimate angle.
