## Notes
- Q.33-34: whether unction may be repeated; the essence and parts of the sacrament of Order.
- Indelible character contrasted with repeatable sacraments.
- Bridge to Levin (morphogenetic memory — a stable imprinted target state) for indelible character.
- Friston: ordering = role-differentiation within the collective agent for joint inference.
- Rohr: orders as relational differentiation (perichoresis), not domination.
