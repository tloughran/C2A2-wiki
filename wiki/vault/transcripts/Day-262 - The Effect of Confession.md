## Notes
- Q.9 quality of confession + Q.10 effect of confession. Attrition (imperfect contrition) suffices for the sacrament — minimal disposition crosses the threshold (Friston evidence-accumulation; echoes Day 260).
- Confession should be entire; "wicked to hope for half a pardon" — the corrective effect is whole, not partial (completion of the repair loop).
- Forgotten sins are loosed with the rest when ignorance excuses — the effect covers what the agent cannot itself represent.
- Theme hook: the whole pardon meets the whole (even imperfect) disclosure; sufficiency is about genuineness, not magnitude.
