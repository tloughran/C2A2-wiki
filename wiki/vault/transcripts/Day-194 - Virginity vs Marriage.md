## Notes

- Virginity (Q.152): bodily integrity is accidental; the FORMAL element is the perpetual purpose of abstaining from venereal pleasure for the kingdom (a.1). Lawful (a.2) because not all are commanded to procreate — a division of labor for the common good (the 'army' analogy).
- A distinct virtue (a.3), as magnificence is distinct from liberality — adding an arduous aspect. More excellent than marriage (a.4, Trent) because it frees the agent for contemplation ('the unmarried is anxious about the Lord; the married, divided' — Paul). But a means: below the theological virtues and religion (a.5).
- Lust (Q.153) = venereal desire beyond right reason (a.1-3); venereal acts not per se sinful when ordered to procreation (a.2); a capital vice (a.4).
- Daughters of lust (a.5) corrupt the WHOLE practical-reasoning chain: blindness of mind, rashness, thoughtlessness, inconstancy (intellect: apprehension->counsel->judgment->command) and self-love, hatred of God, love of this world, despair of the future (will: end + means). A precise account of action-selection breakdown.
- Science/central-theme hooks: virginity's 'freedom for contemplation' = reallocating finite cognitive/relational resources toward the whole (perspective-widening); common-good/division-of-labor = distributed specialized agents (CROSS-069 corporate substance); the daughters map onto corruption of the active-inference perception->policy->action pipeline; self-love-as-disordered-end vs loving-unity-as-telos IS the central theme.
