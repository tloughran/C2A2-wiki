## Notes

- Q.84 (one sin as cause of another): covetousness (avarice) named 'root of all evils' (1 Tim 6:10); capital vices are the seven sins from which other sins arise. Habash organizes via the seven: pride/vainglory, envy, anger, sloth, avarice, gluttony, lust.
- Pride is named 'beginning' (Sirach 10:13/15) -- the root in turning away from God; the seven capital vices are species of disordered turning toward goods.
- Q.85 (effects of sin -- corruption of nature): sin diminishes natural good without destroying it. Original sin removed the gift of original justice; actual sin diminishes the natural inclination to virtue. Aquinas treats this incrementally -- nature's good is reduced, not annihilated.
