> Episode: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCp54DXC_tE>

Context: Day 211 covers II-II Q.187 (things competent to religious — teaching and preaching, secular business, manual labor, living on alms, begging, coarse clothing) and Q.188 (the different kinds of religious life — whether there is one order, orders for active works, soldiering, preaching, study, the comparative excellence of contemplative vs active orders, possessing things in common, and community vs solitary life). ## Notes
- The host's stated question numbers (186-187) are off by one; the actual content is Q.187 (things competent to religious) and Q.188 (kinds of religious life). He acknowledges the slip opening Day 212.
- Q.188 a.6 is the title article ('Better Religious Orders'): the teaching/preaching orders rank highest because nearest the episcopal perfection and the life of Christ and the apostles; contemplative second; corporal-works active third. Note the explicit ranking by *which form of charity-in-action* an order embodies.
- 'The religious state is a training school wherein one aims by practice at the perfection of charity' — the vows and works are means, not the end; perfection = charity (continuous with Day 210). Flag for synthesis: capability/role vs essence.
- Possessing-in-common (a.7): riches 'draw a man's affections away,' steal the heart, breed self-sufficiency — but common goods (houses, cars, computers) may be needed for the order's task. Judas and the common purse as the cautionary figure.
- Solitary vs communal (a.8): the host's first-person Carthusian testimony — solitude is 'a spiritual multiplier' that makes the best better and the unready worse; better in the abstract, not for everyone. The Aristotle 'beast or god' tag closes.
