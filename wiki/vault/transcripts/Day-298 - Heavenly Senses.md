## Notes

- Q.81: all rise at the perfect age (~30, the age of Christ's maturity) and at the stature they *would* have reached without nature's failures — God supplies what was lacking; diversity of stature kept for the beauty of the whole ('a chord').
- Both sexes rise (a.3); difference of sex remains without shame, since lust is gone — the body's form is retained, its disordered passibility removed.
- Q.82: glorified bodies are *impassible* — the soul, perfectly subject to God, so dominates the body that no external object can thwart the perfection flowing from soul to body.
- a.3-4: sensation remains, but the senses receive the *form* of their object 'without the material residue' (color without the matter, no burn from the hot stove) — perception purified to pure information-uptake.
- Flag: 'receive the form without the matter' is a near-verbatim interface-theory image — perception as form/info-reception, the glorified senses as a cleaned-up interface.
