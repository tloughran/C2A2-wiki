## Notes

- Q.55 a.4 'whatever is received is received according to the mode of the receiver' — the episode's philosophical core, and as clean a statement of perspective-limitation individuating reception as the Summa offers; Emmaus as worked example.
- Q.55 a.1-2 hierarchical manifestation: loftier mysteries reach lower ranks through higher; witnesses chosen, not broadcast — information cascade structure.
- Q.55 a.5 the resurrection cannot be demonstrated deductively; it is an article of faith with persuasive proofs (wounds, eating, scripture) — evidence-strength honesty inside the text itself.
- Q.56 a.1 causal propagation: every cause operates first on what is nearest (fire/air illustration) — locality of causal influence; the risen body as first node.
- Q.56 a.2 efficient AND exemplar causality of our resurrection, body and soul — pattern-replication (conformity to the exemplar) alongside efficient agency.
