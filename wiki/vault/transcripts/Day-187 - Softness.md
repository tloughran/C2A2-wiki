## Notes

- Softness (effeminacy, *mollities*) is yielding to even gentle pressure — withdrawing from the good for want of pleasure (Q.138 a.1). Caused by frail temperament or by *habituation to pleasure* that makes its absence unbearable. Directly a self-regulation / precision-weighting failure mode.
- Pertinacity is the opposite excess — clinging to a course reason now demands be dropped; its root cause is vainglory, 'to make a show of one's own excellence' (a.2). A both-sides-of-the-mean structure.
- The gift of fortitude (Q.139 a.1) goes beyond natural courage: natural virtue suffices to die for family or country, but laying down life for an unseen Christ requires a supernatural disposition giving 'confidence of overcoming all dangers.'
- The gift corresponds to the fourth beatitude — hunger and thirst for justice (a.2) — taking on an *insatiable* desire for the good. Resonates with Fredrickson's broaden-and-build upward spiral.
- Central-theme hook: the gift = guidance/empowerment from one who perceives more (the Spirit) enabling computation toward a good the agent cannot reach on its own resources.
