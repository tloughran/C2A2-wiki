## Notes

- Presumption (Q.130) is the excess opposed to magnanimity: attempting things beyond one's real power — an over-estimated self-model.
- Ambition (Q.131) is the inordinate desire for honor — seeking honor for oneself, not referred to God or to others' good, or desiring honor not proportioned to one's state.
- Honor for Aquinas is a *sign* of virtue/excellence; ambition's disorder is treating the sign as the object — flag for the reputation-signal-as-terminal-reward angle.
- Both vices curve the agent toward its own standing rather than the good it should serve.
