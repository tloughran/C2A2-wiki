## Notes

- Q.76 wraps verbal injustices: cursing as calling-down-evil on another; lawful only as judicial sentence or prophetic warning, never as appetite-driven malediction.
- Q.77 opens economic justice: the just price; concealing defects; selling something for more than it is worth.
- Aquinas: it is unlawful to sell a thing for more than its worth merely because the buyer needs it more — value is not whim-relative but tied to the real good of the thing.
- Direct setup for Day 157's usury: the principle that price-difference must answer to a *real* good exchanged, not invented value.
