## Notes

- Q.71 suffrages: one person's merits/prayers profit another *because* all are one body in charity — efficacy flows along the bond of union (Pauline 'one body, one Spirit').
- Hard limit: suffrages cannot change a soul's *state* (damnation<->salvation), only diminish punishment for those already in grace — union transmits help but not a change of fundamental orientation.
- Mass and almsgiving are the chief suffrages 'on the part of charity'; prayer chief 'on the part of intention directed to the dead' — two channels: union and directed attention.
- Q.72: saints know our prayers because such knowledge belongs to their happiness as 'God's cooperators'; intercession honors the *order* by which higher leads lower to God.
- Flag: the whole question is a theory of *coupled agents sharing an objective* across a community — direct active-inference / mutual-modeling parallel.
