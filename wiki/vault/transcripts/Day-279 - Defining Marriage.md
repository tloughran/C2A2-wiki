## Notes

- Defining marriage: betrothal, the espousals, and what the marriage bond formally is.
- Marriage defined by the indissoluble joining of consenting wills, not merely by consummation.
- Hook: consent as the constitutive computational act that forms the union.
- Kastrup individuation-within-monism; the bond as a real relation, not a derived feature.
