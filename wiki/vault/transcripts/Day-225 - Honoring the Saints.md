## Notes

- Q.25: one *person* receives one adoration (latria) though honored for several causes; images and relics are venerated because honor passes to the prototype (the divine person), not the matter.
- Q.25 a.5: the Blessed Virgin and saints receive *dulia/hyperdulia*, not latria (reserved to God); relics honored as members of Christ, temples of the Holy Spirit destined for resurrection glory.
- Q.26: Christ is *the* Mediator 'as man' — his humanity occupies the middle position uniting sinful humanity to the Godhead; clergy and the faithful are mediators by participation.
- Flag-worthy: mediation-as-uniting and honor-passing-to-the-person are the loving-unity/individuation-by-person structure; the mediator who unites the many to God is the central-theme bridge figure.
