## Notes

- Q.23: communication with the excommunicated — the faithful are to withhold ordinary fellowship (to mark the separation and prompt amendment), with allowances for necessity and for those bound by prior duty.
- Q.24: absolution from excommunication — the same authority that bound can loose; restoration reincorporates the member into the communion.
- Short episode (~5.5 min): the binding/loosing symmetry is the heart — what was excluded can be re-included by the same jurisdictional power.
- Directly stages re-inclusion: the negative of excommunication is undone by absolution, returning the member to the body.
- Flag-worthy: absolution = restoring a decoupled member to the collective field; the boundary is permeable by design, ordered to reintegration.
