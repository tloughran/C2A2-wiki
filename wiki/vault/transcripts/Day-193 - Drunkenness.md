## Notes

- Drunkenness (Q.150 a.1-2) = voluntarily throwing off the use of reason — the mark of the human. Mortal because 'one sins by discarding that which keeps him from sinning': willingly disabling one's own guide. Not the gravest (carnal < spiritual, a.3).
- Culpability tracks voluntariness (a.4): involuntary intoxication excuses; voluntary drunkenness is culpable for what follows. Austin's anesthesia case — loss of reason ordered BY reason (for surgery) is licit. Means-end ordering under a higher policy.
- Chastity (Q.151 a.1) = reason 'chastises' concupiscence, which 'like a child needs curbing.' A special virtue (a.2) for venereal pleasure, distinct from abstinence (a different pleasure of touch, a.3).
- Purity (a.4) governs the external signs — looks, kisses, touches — while chastity governs the act itself; purity is a circumstance of chastity, not a separate virtue.
- Science/central-theme hooks: reason = the agent's self-model/controller; voluntary self-incapacitation = deliberately degrading one's own predictive apparatus (Friston: dismantling the model that minimizes long-run free energy). The anesthesia case is active inference under a higher-order policy — the same loss of reason, opposite valuation, because differently ordered to the whole.
