## Notes

- Q.69: the 'place' of separated souls (heaven/hell/purgatory/limbo of fathers/limbo of children) defined by *spiritual state*, not literal location — Aquinas strains the word 'place' for non-spatial entities.
- Limbo of fathers = rest-without-vision before Christ (Abraham's bosom); a *temporal* eschatological state resolved by Christ's descent — inaugurated-eschatology hook.
- Q.70: sensitive powers remain in the separated soul only 'radically' (radix, as conclusions in a principle), not in act — identity/capacity persists through loss of the organ. KEY for personal-identity-through-death.
- How a separated soul 'suffers bodily fire': the soul is *detained* by fire as instrument of divine justice and knows it as hurtful — a relational/intentional account of punishment.
- Flag: 'powers retained radically' is a strikingly Levin-adjacent claim — the pattern/target-state survives the loss of the substrate that expressed it.
