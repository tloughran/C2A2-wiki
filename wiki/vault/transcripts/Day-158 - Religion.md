## Notes

- Q.80 enumerates the *potential* parts of justice — virtues annexed to justice that fall short of its strict definition (rendering due *according to equality*) because the debt is unpayable (religion, piety, observance, gratitude, truthfulness, friendship, liberality, epieikeia).
- Q.81: religion as the virtue by which we render to God the worship and reverence due to Him — strictly an act of justice, even though we can never pay back equally.
- Aquinas: religion is a *moral* virtue (not theological — its object is the *act of worship*, not God Himself); it is the highest of the moral virtues because its object is most excellent.
- Major pivot point: from interpersonal justice to justice-toward-God. The whole structure of religion (devotion, prayer, adoration, sacrifice) about to unfold in Days 159-160 is *this part* of justice.
