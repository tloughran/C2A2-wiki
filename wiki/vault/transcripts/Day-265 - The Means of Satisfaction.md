## Notes
- Q.15 means of satisfaction + Q.16 the subjects of penance. Means = penal works restoring equality of justice (the traditional triad: prayer, fasting, almsgiving) plus restitution exceeding the thing taken (the malice of will also repaid).
- Satisfaction as concrete corrective action instantiated — the satisfaction step of the repair loop made specific (Levin restoring balance; Friston action reducing divergence).
- Q.16: even the sinless (e.g. the angels/Christ in the limiting case) possess penance as a virtue though they have no occasion to exercise it — the habit/act distinction (echoes Day 255).
- Theme hook: the limited agent restores equality through proportionate action; the disposition can exist without the deviation that calls it forth.
