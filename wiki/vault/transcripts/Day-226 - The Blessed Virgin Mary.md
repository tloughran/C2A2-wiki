## Notes

- Q.27: Mary sanctified before birth and full of grace ('nearest of all to the Author of grace'); Habash notes Aquinas predates and differs from the defined Immaculate Conception dogma (preserved free from original sin from the first instant).
- Q.27 a.5: 'by bringing him forth she in a manner dispensed grace to all' — the title *mediatrix of grace*.
- Q.28: Mary virgin in conceiving, in birth, and ever-virgin ('the only-begotten of his mother as he is the only-begotten of the Father'); the 'brothers of Jesus' are close relations, not other children.
- Flag-worthy: 'full of grace, dispensing grace to all' and original-innocence language connect to the loving-unity telos and the order-disorder-reorder / limitation-becomes-gift pattern (Rohr); the development of the dogma is itself a tradition working out its insight over time (Wright's inaugurated eschatology / faithful improvisation).
