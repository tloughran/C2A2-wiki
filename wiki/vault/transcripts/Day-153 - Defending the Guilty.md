## Notes

- Q.70: who is bound to give evidence, when natural law overrides civil compulsion (the confessional seal), the gravity of false witness.
- Q.71: lawyers — bound to defend the poor; but cannot in conscience defend an unjust cause. The clean test: refuse a case you cannot in conscience win.
- Aquinas: an advocate may defend even one known to be guilty if there are extenuating circumstances or to mitigate the punishment — but never to procure an unjust acquittal.
- The whole procedural treatise centers on *truth* as the prior good of justice — a perspectival epistemology of social inquiry.
