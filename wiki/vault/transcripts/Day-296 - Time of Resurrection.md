## Notes

- Q.77: the resurrection is deferred to the end of the world and its time is *hidden* — deliberately, 'so that all may be on the watch' (Christ withholds the date even in his humanity).
- Mode: partly by angelic ministry (gathering remains, in time), partly immediately by God's power (instantaneous) — a two-tier causal account of the event.
- Q.78: all pass through death first (even the living are reduced to ashes by the cleansing fire) — universality of death before glory; 'as in Adam all die, so in Christ all made alive'.
- a.3: the ashes have *no natural inclination* toward the soul that will inform them — reunion is by divine ordination alone, not by any residue in the matter. KEY: identity is not carried by the matter.
- Flag: 'which parts of the elements, not others' are reunited by providence — the principle that selects *this* matter is extrinsic to the matter, a strong hook for pattern/target-state accounts of identity.
