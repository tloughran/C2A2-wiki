## Notes

- Hebrews 11:1 definition unpacked: faith is (a) the substance of things hoped for, (b) the evidence of things not seen — both intellect (evidence) and will (hope) implicated.
- Faith resides in the intellect (its object is First Truth, which is cognitive). Charity is the *form* of faith — i.e., charity quickens and perfects faith, making the difference between *living* (fides formata) and *lifeless* (fides informis) faith.
- Living vs lifeless faith: same habit in the intellect can persist when charity is lost (mortal sin). Faith can remain when love is gone; love cannot remain when faith is dead. Asymmetry worth flagging.
- The synthesis hook: charity as the *form* of faith is exactly the Fredrickson + Stump CROSS-005 territory — love as biological/metaphysical real, not sentiment; "form" not in a decorative sense but in the hylomorphic sense of what makes the thing what it is.
- Bridges to Wright: Pauline pistis as covenant-fidelity, not merely propositional belief. Aquinas's three-fold (Deum / Deo / in Deum) is closer to Wright's reading than to a propositionalist gloss.
