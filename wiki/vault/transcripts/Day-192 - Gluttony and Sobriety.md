## Notes

- Gluttony (Q.148 a.1) = disordered desire for food/drink at variance with reason; mortal only when it turns one to an end incompatible with charity (a.2); not the greatest of sins (a.3) since sins directly against God are worse.
- Five species (Gregory, a.4): hastily, sumptuously, too much, daintily, greedily. A capital vice (a.5) because the pleasure of the table is a 'counterfeit beatitude' that spawns other vices; daughters (a.6): dullness of mind, unseemly joy, loquaciousness, scurrility, uncleanness.
- Sobriety (Q.149) is a SPECIAL virtue about intoxicating drink specifically (a.1-2) — because wine is a 'special hindrance to reason,' a special virtue is required to remove that hindrance. Wine not evil in itself (a.3).
- Sobriety is 'more requisite in persons of greater standing' (a.4) — bishops, kings, those whose reason must instruct and rule others. Protecting the high-bandwidth perspective that a community depends on.
- Science/central-theme hooks: 'counterfeit beatitude' = reward-prediction hijack (the appetite's generative model crowding out the higher policy); 'special hindrance to reason' = a substance degrading the predictive cortex (Friston precision-weighting; Hawkins/McGilchrist on cortical model integrity).
