## Notes

- Fearlessness (Q.126) is treated as a *vice* — a defect of fear, not its absence as virtue: failing to fear what one ought (God, death, sin, real danger).
- Foolhardiness / audacity (Q.127) is the excess of daring — rushing into danger beyond reason. With timidity (Day 180) these complete the field around fortitude.
- Both are calibration errors on the same threat-tracking that timidity over-weights: fearlessness under-weights the threat signal; daring over-drives the approach response.
- Aquinas roots disordered fearlessness in pride and in inordinate love of temporal goods that dulls the fear of losing higher ones — flag for the self-model angle.
