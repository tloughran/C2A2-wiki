## Notes

- Q.68: accusation (vs denunciation) is for *punishment*; binding when a crime injures the common good. Calumny / collusion / evasion as the three perversions.
- Q.69: the defendant's sins — falsely denying truth, perjury, refusal to answer, escape from a just sentence.
- Aquinas defends a defendant's lawful self-defense (every man may defend his own life and reputation by all just means) but condemns evasion of *just* punishment.
- Procedural justice: roles in the courtroom map to the offices (accuser / defendant / witness / judge / counsel) — each tradition-bound and each correctable; matches the C2A2 accelerator/detector frame of distributed correction.
