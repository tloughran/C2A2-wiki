## Notes
- **The book of conscience.** Aquinas reads the "books" of Rev 20 as each person's conscience; at judgment every soul recalls the full moral character of all its acts, the recall *caused by divine power* ("the one divine power will cause all to recall their deeds"). Total self-legibility.
- **Mutual legibility.** Article 2: everyone will read everything in everyone else's conscience — the hidden made manifest "unto God's glory," so the justice of each sentence is publicly evident. Privacy of the interior dissolves entirely.
- **Asymmetry of the all-at-once glance.** The blessed see all merits/demerits simultaneously "in the Word"; the damned grasp them only successively, by divine power supplied *from outside*, not from any intrinsic gift. Flag-worthy: capacity tracks union.
- **General vs particular judgment.** Particular at death (Heb 9:27); a general judgment besides, so man is judged in body AND as a *member of the race*, not only as an individual — the social/corporate dimension.
- **Mode, time, place.** Judgment communicated mentally not vocally (speculative); time unknown (vigilance, "thief in the night"); place near Mount Olivet / valley of Jehoshaphat (probable, not certain). Austin marks the speculative articles as speculative.
