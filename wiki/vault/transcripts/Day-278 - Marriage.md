## Notes

- Opens the treatise on Matrimony: marriage as an office of nature and as a sacrament.
- Aquinas grounds marriage in natural teleology (offspring, fidelity) elevated to sacramental sign.
- Hook: the one-flesh union as a higher-order individual — Levin multiscale individuality; the central-theme telos of loving unity.
- Wright on the Pauline marriage-as-covenant (Eph 5); Rohr on relationship as ontologically prior (PRS-7).
