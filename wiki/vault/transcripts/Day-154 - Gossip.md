## Notes

- Transition from injustice-of-deed (theft, judicial wrong) to injustice-of-word — the verbal injuries proper to the *public* offices of communication.
- Q.72 reviling = face-to-face dishonor; Q.73 backbiting = damaging another's reputation behind their back.
- Both are *mortal sin* by Aquinas's reasoning — they destroy a real good (the *fama* / public good of reputation) which the victim cannot defend.
- Aquinas: backbiting can be more grievous than open hostility because it is *covert* and removes the chance of correction — exactly the perspectival-asymmetry failure the project is mapping.
