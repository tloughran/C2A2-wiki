## Notes

- Aquinas treats the 'keys' as a spiritual power of jurisdiction in the forum of conscience: authority to bind and loose, opening or closing the way to the kingdom (Matt 16:19, 18:18).
- The keys do not create grace but minister it; the priest is an instrument, the principal cause is Christ/God. The 'effect of the keys' (Q.18) is the remission of sin under the power that opens heaven.
- Nested-agency motif returns (cf. Days 253, 261): a delegated, ministerial authority operating inside a larger authority that is its source.
- Distinction between the power of order (sacramental) and the power of jurisdiction (over a subject) is in view.
- Flag-worthy for synthesis: the Church as a higher-order collective agent that has real top-down power over its members.
