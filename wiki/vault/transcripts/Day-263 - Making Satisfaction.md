## Notes
- Q.11 seal of confession + Q.12 satisfaction as to its nature. The seal is inviolable: God does not reveal our sins, neither may the priest; practically, the secrecy is what makes honest disclosure possible.
- The seal as the trust-condition of the error-sharing channel — a safe channel enabling truthful self-disclosure (McGilchrist on relational trust; Friston on low-cost communication).
- Satisfaction defined: compensation for an inflicted injury "according to the equality of justice"; giving God due honor — the corrective-action step restoring balance (Levin: return to set-point; control-loop closure).
- Theme hook: disclosure requires a held, trustworthy space; repair restores a disturbed equality.
