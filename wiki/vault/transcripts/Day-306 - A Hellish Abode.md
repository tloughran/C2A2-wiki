## Notes

- Locus: Supplement Q.97 (of God's mercy and justice toward the damned) and Q.98 (of the will and intellect of the damned). The penultimate episode of the series.
- Q.97 turns on whether the punishment of the damned is tempered by divine mercy and whether it is just — Aquinas holds the punishment real and unending, yet not exceeding the measure of justice.
- Q.98: the damned retain intellect and will but their will is fixed in evil and their understanding darkened — obstinacy of the will after death is the key Thomistic claim (the soul's orientation is sealed at death).
- Flag-worthy for synthesis: fixity/irreversibility of a final state; whether a perspective can become permanently closed; "torment of finding themselves deemed unworthy to see the glory of the saints" — privation-as-suffering, the loss of vision.
- Bridges locus: Suppl. last things -> Wright (primary), Carroll (time/cosmology), Kastrup (persistence of mind), Rohr (universal restoration — a live tension with the doctrine of eternal fixity).
