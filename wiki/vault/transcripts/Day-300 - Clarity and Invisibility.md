## Notes

- Q.85 clarity: the glorified body shines ('lightsome') as a visible manifestation of invisible spiritual glory; 'star differs from star in glory' (1 Cor 15) — degrees of clarity track degrees of charity/merit.
- Visible to non-glorified eyes (the Transfiguration as precedent); yet the glorified body can will to be *invisible* (Christ vanishing at Emmaus) — visibility becomes obedient to the will.
- Q.86: the damned rise incorruptible (cannot die again) and *passible* — bodies restored to full health precisely so they can feel and endure pain without limit; deformities that would *dull* pain are removed.
- Symmetry: as the body shared in merit it shares in reward; as it shared in sin it shares in punishment — embodiment is morally load-bearing on both sides.
- Flag: clarity as 'inner state made outwardly legible' and visibility-by-will close the whole eschatology on a theme of the body as transparent expression of the soul's perspective — the central-theme payoff.
