# Day 266 — Keys of the Kingdom (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (Suppl. Q.17-18) opens the treatment of the *power of the keys*: the spiritual authority, given to the Church on the words of Matthew 16 and 18, to bind and to loose — to open or close the way to the kingdom — together with the *effect* of that power, the remission of sin. The keys are a power of jurisdiction in the forum of conscience; the minister does not create grace but ministers it, the principal cause being Christ. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: what is it for a community to hold a *real* authority over the standing of its members — a power exercised by limited individuals but belonging to the whole — and what does it mean for such a power to *effect* a change in a member rather than merely register one?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how does a higher-order whole come to hold genuine top-down power over the members composing it, and how is that power wielded by a member without being owned by him?** Aquinas's keys presuppose exactly this — an authority that is the Church's, deposited in Christ, exercised by a priest who is its instrument. The contemporary question is whether collective agency of this kind is real causal structure or only a manner of speaking.

## Resource

Friston's program supplies the formal lead. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work, PRS-06 (multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling) asks precisely how individual agents "form coherent higher-order wholes without losing autonomy — how communities cohere," and answers that agents couple by reducing prediction error in one another's models until they constitute a single collective agent; love is named there as the mechanism of that synchrony. PRS-07 (the substrate-independence of the Free Energy Principle) then licenses the move across substrates: Markov-blanket conditions are substrate-agnostic, so social cognition falls under the same framework as cellular and organismal cognition. What supplies the *community's own* boundary is PRS-22 (the group-level Markov blanket) — a statistical boundary maintained by the collective as a whole, distinct from the blankets of its members; where a collective sustains one, it satisfies the formal conditions to be modeled as a single active-inference agent, and "one" and "many" become two consistent levels of the same generative model. The power of the keys, read through this lens, is the collective agent's authority over what falls inside that group-level blanket: to bind or loose is to alter a member's coupling to the whole.

Levin's program gives the biological instance. In Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work, PRS-08 treats cell collectives as problem-solving agents navigating anatomical morphospace, which is what opens them to top-down, information-level intervention rather than bottom-up molecular manipulation; the body is a collective intelligence whose morphogenetic field exercises top-down command over its cells — the whole really does set goals and enforce them on its parts. That a higher level holds causal power over its members is, on Levin's evidence, not metaphor but measurable biology; it makes the Church-as-agent claim continuous with how living wholes actually work.

Rohr keeps the keys from being read as a possession: on the Universal Christ, the authority ministered is a divine *pattern* present in all reality, not a private property of the office-holder — the minister opens a door he did not build. Wright situates the keys in the five-act drama: the church improvises faithfully with an authority handed to it, extending a covenant story it did not originate. The *institution* of the keys by Christ and their grace-bearing *effect* remain theological.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the power of the keys is the real top-down authority a higher-order collective agent holds over the members coupled into it — a delegated, ministerial power that opens or closes a member's standing in the whole, not a power the minister owns.** This *shares* with Aquinas the keys as jurisdiction (not order), the minister as instrument and Christ as principal cause, and the *effect* as a true change in the member's relation to the kingdom. It *reframes* "the Church's power" as the causal authority a collective sustaining its own group-level Markov blanket holds over its interior (Friston PRS-22, on the mutual-modeling coupling of PRS-06), continuous with the morphogenetic whole's command over its cells (Levin PRS-08). It *firms up* that collective agency is genuine causal structure, not a façon de parler. It *marks* that the resource explains the *form* of a binding/loosing authority — how a whole can act on its members — but not the *grace* the keys minister, the divine institution of the office, or its eschatological reference, all of which are theological (Rohr, Wright).

## The central-theme thread

The keys state the project's nested-agency motif in the register of jurisdiction (cf. Days 253, 261). A perspective-limited agent — one priest, in one place, seeing very little of the whole — wields a power that opens the kingdom, and the power is real precisely because it is *not his own*: it descends from a whole that perceives more of the pattern than he does, and from the One who perceives all of it. This is the central claim's social face. We work out our standing in the people united with God under guidance from "those who perceive more of the whole than we do, be they human or divine"; the keys are one institutional shape of that guidance — the whole's authority, ministered by a part, to keep a member oriented toward the loving unity it cannot see for itself.

## Where this leaves us

Day 267 ("Minister of the Keys") narrows from the power to its *wielders and subjects*: who can exercise the keys, and over whom. The collective-agent reading predicts the answer — the power reaches exactly as far as the membership coupled into the whole. Open thread: hold the keys as delegated, not owned; the collective-agency resource gives the *form* of the authority, not the grace it conveys.
