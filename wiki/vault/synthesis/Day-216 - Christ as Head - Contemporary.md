# Day 216 — Christ as Head (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Having settled *what* was assumed and in *what order* (Days 213-215), the *Summa* now asks what the union *implies* for the assumed humanity, beginning with grace. Q.7 treats the grace of Christ *as an individual man*: His soul has habitual grace (a.1) — the created sanctifying grace by which His humanity is holy, distinct from the grace of union itself — together with the virtues (a.2), the gifts (a.5), and the gratuitous graces or charisms (a.7); and the question presses whether this grace was the greatest possible, whether it was infinite, and whether it could increase (a.9-12). Q.8 then turns to the grace of Christ *as He is the Head of the Church*: Christ is the Head of the whole Church, the influx of grace flowing from Him to the members "as from a head to the body" (a.1), He is Head of all men in varying degrees and even of the angels (a.4-5), and — by contrast — the devil is not a head but only a perverse principle of the wicked (a.7). The two questions move from a graced *individual* to a graced *body*: the fullness in the one becomes the life of the many. The contemporary version of the question: *how does the fullness held in a higher, more complete perspective become the very life of the limited perspectives joined to it?*

## Problem

The underlying inquiry-problem shows up in both frames. Aquinas needs a single grace to do two jobs at once: to sanctify Christ's *own* humanity (habitual grace, Q.7) and to be the *source* from which the whole body's grace flows (capital grace, Q.8). The technical name *gratia capitis* — "grace of the head" — marks the puzzle precisely: how can a perfection that is fully an individual's also be the principle of a *corporate* life, such that what is in the Head is communicated to the members without ceasing to be the Head's own? In contemporary terms: by what mechanism does a fuller, more integrated agent become the organizing principle of a larger body of lesser agents, so that its fullness genuinely flows into them and knits them into one? Aquinas answers with the metaphysics of grace-as-quality and a juridical-organic image of headship. The problem he is really solving is the problem the whole project is built around: *the communication of a whole's fullness to its limited parts*.

## Resource

On the scriptural side, Wright reads salvation as incorporation into the people among whom God comes home, with Rohr's *Universal Christ* carrying the cosmic scope; but the *mechanism* of head-to-member influx is where the C2A2 set has fresh resources. The leading move comes from Tom's PRS-form record of Fredrickson. PRS-02 there reframes love as an *objective coupling mechanism* — positivity resonance with a measurable neurophysiological signature (vagal tone, oxytocin, neural phase-locking) — and PRS-03 proposes that this same coupling *scales*, bridging dyadic interaction to group resonance to, by extrapolation, communities and traditions. Read against Q.8, capital grace stops being only a juridical transfer and becomes a *coupling-and-resonance* picture: the Head as the agent whose fullness propagates through the body by real, measurable bonds of charity. Master CROSS-005 supports this directly — Fredrickson, Stump, and Kastrup converge on love/union as a *fundamental category*, not a sentiment: positivity resonance (biology), suffering-as-condition-for-union (Stump), and re-integration of dissociated alters (Kastrup) are three faces of one claim that union is basic. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston adds the architecture: the free-energy formalism (PRS-01) and the organism-as-active-modeler (PRS-04) describe agents whose Markov blankets *nest* — cells within tissues within organisms — so that a "body" is literally a higher-order agent built of lower ones. The body of Christ, on this reading, is a nested multi-scale agent whose Head is the highest-scale integrating principle. Rohr's register (PRS-03, the Universal Christ as the divine pattern present in *all* creation; PRS-04, embodied love as a real site of knowing) carries the cosmic scope of Q.8 a.4-5 — Head of all men and angels — and keeps the love incarnate, not abstract. PRS-05 in the Wright-tradition record (salvation as *incorporation into the people among whom God comes home*) names the goal the influx serves: the members are not merely helped but *brought into one body* united with God.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **capital grace is the influx by which a whole, fuller perspective communicates its fullness to the limited perspectives joined to it, knitting them into one body — and the mechanism, read through the emerging paradigm, is a coupling-and-nesting one: love as a measurable resonance (Fredrickson) propagating through a multi-scale nested agent (Friston) from its highest integrating principle (the Head) into its members, incorporating them into one people united with God (Wright).**

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps the two jobs of one grace — Christ's habitual grace sanctifying His own humanity *and* the same fullness flowing as capital grace to the members. It keeps the head-to-body image as more than metaphor: a real influx of life from a real principle. It keeps the asymmetry — the fullness originates in the Head and flows *to* the members, not the reverse. And it keeps the cosmic scope: the headship reaches all men and even angels.

*Where it reframes.* The influx, framed by Aquinas as grace-as-quality communicated through a juridical-organic headship, is reread as *coupling and nesting*: love as an objective, scalable resonance (Fredrickson PRS-02/03) and the body as a nested active-inference agent (Friston PRS-01/04). "From a head to the body" becomes "from the highest-scale integrating agent to the lower-scale agents it binds." The devil's non-headship (a.7) becomes intelligible too: a perverse principle *fragments* rather than couples — it raises free energy across the body rather than minimizing the members' distance from the whole, so it cannot be a true head.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* The coupling claim is *empirically firm at small scale* — positivity resonance is a measured phenomenon (Fredrickson PRS-02). Its *scaling* to communities and traditions (PRS-03) is empirically suggestive but extrapolated (medium). The nested-agent architecture (Friston) is a strong formal analogy but its application to a *grace*-bearing body is analogical (medium). The cosmic headship over angels (Q.8 a.5) is carried as theology, and the identification of this influx with the *grace* of Christ is theological throughout. The central-theme fit, however, is strong: capital grace is the clearest single image in the treatise of a whole's fullness flowing into limited perspectives.

## The central-theme thread

This is one of the most central-theme-resonant articles in the entire *Summa*. The project's wager is that conscious agents are individuated by the limitations of their perspective, and that *loving unity* — every limited perspective contained in the simple whole — is the telos toward which each agent computes under the resources it perceives. Q.8's *gratia capitis* shows that telos flowing in the opposite direction and meeting us halfway: not a limited member straining upward toward the whole, but the *whole's own fullness*, held in the Head who already contains it, pouring down into the members and binding them into one body. Capital grace is the loving-unity computation run from the side of the one who perceives the whole. Fredrickson's positivity resonance gives it a biological echo — love as a measurable coupling that really does knit separate nervous systems into momentary shared states, and (by extrapolation) scales toward the communal. Friston's nested blankets give it an architecture — a body that is genuinely *one* higher-order agent without dissolving the members who compose it. And Wright's salvation-as-incorporation (PRS-05) names the end: the members are *brought home into the people among whom God dwells*, which is exactly "inclusion in the people united with God." The "fear and trembling" of Phil 2:12 is the members' side — limited, working out their salvation under scarce resources — while capital grace is the Head's side: the fullness already given, flowing down, doing in the body what no member could do for itself. Rohr's Universal Christ widens the frame to its cosmic edge (Head of all men and angels), and his insistence that love is *embodied* (PRS-04) keeps the influx from evaporating into abstraction: the body of Christ is a real coupled body, and grace moves through it the way life moves through a living thing.

## Where this leaves us

Q.7-8 establish that the assumed humanity is not only complete (Day 215) but *full of grace* — and that the fullness is not sealed in the individual but flows, as capital grace, into the whole body. Tomorrow (Day 217, "Christ's Knowledge") the treatise turns from grace to *knowledge*, asking what the assumed humanity *knew* — and there the project's central claim meets its sharpest test: a created, finite mind that truly *beholds* the divine essence yet cannot *comprehend* it. Where Q.8 showed the whole's fullness flowing down into limited members, Q.9-10 will show the limited mind looking up — seeing the whole truly, yet, because finite, never exhaustively. The open thread to carry forward: if capital grace is the loving-unity telos flowing *down* from the Head, the beatific-but-non-comprehensive vision of Q.10 is the same telos seen from *within* a limited perspective looking up — and the project should watch whether the two directions are one motion described from two ends.
