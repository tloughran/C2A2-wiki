# Day 204 — Prophetic Causes (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.172 asks after the *cause* of prophecy and Q.173 after its *manner*. Aquinas's answers are unusually mechanism-rich. Prophecy is not natural — natural foresight reads cause-and-effect ("red sky at morning"), but the prophet knows "by seeing or knowing things in the future as if already present," which "is how God knows the future": "all of time lies bare before his knowledge." Revelation is conveyed *through the angelic hierarchy* ("God orders all things sweetly... through the chain of command"). It requires no natural disposition and no good life — it is a *gratuitous* grace, "given irrespective to the individual holiness of the possessor," and so "can be without charity." And the *manner* of conveyance is built on a complete theory of natural cognition: things are known by abstraction — "first to the senses, secondly to the imagination, thirdly to the passive intellect," the species "derived from the phantasms" and lit by "the active intellect" (the blue-ball example) — so prophecy is conveyed in three ways: by a stronger *light* (better abstraction), by *species impressed* directly (skipping abstraction, as with Paul), or by a new *coordination of images* (Jeremiah's boiling pot), the last requiring withdrawal from the senses. Finally, providence and freedom meet: the Spirit moves Caiaphas and the soldiers casting lots "so that they choose freely... yet certainly," and even so "the prophet's mind is a defective instrument." The contemporary version: *what are the cause, the channel, and the mechanism of a knowing that overflows the natural cognitive pipeline — and does the knower stay bounded?*

## Problem

Several linked inquiry-problems, all about *how information from beyond reaches a finite knower*. (1) **Atemporal knowledge of contingents.** Aquinas needs God to know future free acts with certainty without making them necessary, and his device is that all times are *present* to eternity. The problem is to make sense of a standpoint from which the temporally distant is not inferred but *seen*. (2) **Mediated conveyance.** Why should revelation run through a *hierarchy* (God → angels → us) rather than arriving directly? The problem is the role of layered mediation in transmitting knowledge across an ontological gap. (3) **The mechanism of conveyance.** Given a natural pipeline that builds knowledge by abstraction from the senses, how can a knowing that exceeds that pipeline nonetheless be *received* by it — and why does one mode (imagery) specifically require the senses to be switched off? (4) **Providence and freedom.** How can a choice be at once genuinely the agent's own deliberated act *and* certainly what the higher cause intends? (5) **Boundedness.** Why does even a correctly-driven prophet "know not all that the Holy Ghost means"? And — separately — why can this knowing exist *without charity*, decoupling cognitive capability from union?

## Resource

For atemporal knowing (1), the contemporary frame here is Hoffman's interface program. Tom's PRS-form record at PRS-03 and PRS-04 holds that spacetime is a *species-specific construction* — an interface, not the substrate; observer-dependence is fundamental, not incidental. On this frame, *time itself is part of the rendering*, a feature of the dashboard rather than of the underlying reality. Aquinas's claim that God knows the future "as if already present" then translates without strain: a standpoint *outside the constructed time-interface* does not infer the future from present causes but apprehends the whole temporal manifold at once. (Carroll's eternalism — the block universe in which past, present, and future are equally real — is the physics-side companion, treated here as the conservative naturalist version of the same "all-time-present" structure; CROSS-021 notes Wolfram's observer-sampled spacetime as a third route to time-as-constructed.) The contingency worry dissolves the way it does for Aquinas: seeing a free act laid out is not causing it to be necessary.

For mediated conveyance (2), the lead is Levin's nested-agency program: living and cognitive systems are *multiscale* — agents within agents — and information is routinely conveyed *through layers*, each level translating for the one below. Aquinas's angelic "chain of command" (his own CEO-to-manager analogy) is, structurally, layered multiscale conveyance: revelation reaches the human level through intermediaries that step the content down across the ontological gradient, much as a higher-order biological goal is conveyed to cells through tissue- and organ-level mediation. The claim that mediation is *fitting* ("God orders all things sweetly") matches the engineering point that robust cross-scale transmission generally runs through hierarchy rather than direct broadcast.

For the mechanism (3), the lead is the Friston/Hawkins account of cognition as a *hierarchical generative model*. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston (PRS-04 active inference; the hierarchical-model machinery) renders Aquinas's abstraction pipeline almost line-for-line: sensory input is passed up through layers (imagination ≈ intermediate representations) to intelligible structure (the "intelligible species" ≈ a learned latent), the whole driven top-down by a generative "light" (the active intellect ≈ the model's high-level priors that make the abstraction possible). Hawkins's reference-frame thesis (PRS-04, reference frames as the mind's modeling primitive) gives the "intelligible species" a concrete shape: the gist abstracted from many blue things is a reusable reference-frame structure. Now the three conveyance modes become three *entry points into this pipeline*: (a) a *stronger light* = boosting the top-down inferential capacity so the agent abstracts deeper truths from ordinary input (Daniel reading the writing); (b) *impressed species* = injecting a high-level latent *directly*, bypassing the bottom-up abstraction (Paul's gospel "not from man"); (c) *coordinated images* = injecting at the *imagery* layer (Jeremiah's pot). And Aquinas's striking detail — that mode (c) *requires withdrawal from the senses* — is exactly what a predictive-processing account would demand: injected imagery and externally-driven perception are confusable at the same layer, so to mark the input as revelatory rather than perceptual, live sensory precision must be suppressed (his own argument: losing hearing/touch/smell "clues us in that something abnormal is happening").

For providence-and-freedom (4), the lead is again active inference: a lower process can be *genuinely self-determining* (sampling, deliberating, settling) while operating under a *top-down prior* that shapes its outcome — top-down constraint is not override. The Spirit moving Caiaphas or the soldiers "so that they choose freely yet certainly" is the theological maximum of this structure: a high-level constraint under which the low-level inference still runs as real deliberation. (CROSS-008/019 record the converging point from the Levin/Stump side: final-causal goal-direction — the not-yet-realized end shaping present action — is compatible with, indeed constitutive of, the lower process's own activity.)

For boundedness (5), the resources converge on one claim: a finite generative model, *however well driven*, represents only what its structure can carry. This is the formal content of "the prophet's mind is a defective instrument."

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **prophecy's cause is a standpoint outside the constructed time-interface (so the future is seen, not inferred); its channel is nested, multiscale mediation (the angelic chain as layered conveyance); and its mechanism is entry into the agent's hierarchical generative model at one of three points — strengthening the top-down light (deeper abstraction), injecting an intelligible latent directly (bypassing abstraction), or injecting coordinated imagery (with sensory precision suppressed so the input is not mistaken for perception). Providence moving a free act "certainly" is top-down constraint shaping a genuinely self-determining lower process. And because the receiving model is finite, even a correctly-driven prophet under-represents what the source means — the boundedness is intrinsic.**

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps the natural/supernatural distinction (read cause-effect vs. see-as-present); keeps mediation as fitting; keeps his abstraction theory essentially intact and his *three* conveyance modes, including the sense-withdrawal requirement for imagery; keeps the compatibility of certain providence with real freedom; and keeps "knows not all" and "without charity" exactly.

*Where it reframes.* "All time present to eternity" becomes the view from outside the time-interface / the block universe; the angelic hierarchy becomes nested multiscale conveyance; the abstraction pipeline becomes a hierarchical generative model and the intelligible species a learned reference-frame latent; the conveyance modes become pipeline entry-points; providence-with-freedom becomes top-down priors over a still-active inferential process. What the reframe does *not* naturalize is the *identity* of the cause and the channel: Aquinas names God and angels, and these are carried as the theology, not re-derived.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* Eternalism/observer-dependent time is strong as physics, speculative in its divine application. Nested multiscale mediation is well-grounded biologically, analogical here (medium). The abstraction-as-generative-model mapping is a strong structural fit (medium-strong). The three-modes-as-pipeline-entry and the sense-withdrawal prediction are structurally satisfying (medium). Providence-as-top-down-constraint is a conceptual fit (medium). The "defective instrument" is a robust rendering of perspective-limitation. The divine source, the angelic ministry, and the theology of gratuitous grace are theological, presented as such.

## The central-theme thread

The project holds that agents are individuated by the limits of their perspective and that union with the whole is computed by each limited perspective under guidance. Today supplies the capstone statement of the limitation *and* a crucial qualification. The capstone: "since the prophet's mind is a defective instrument, even true prophets know not all that the Holy Ghost means by the things they see or speak or even do." The most exalted natural knower, *correctly driven by the source itself*, still under-represents what the source means — not from inattention or sin, but because a finite model cannot carry all that an infinite meaning contains. Perspective-limitation is thus shown to be intrinsic to finitude, not a remediable failure; the very mechanism of conveyance (injecting into a bounded generative model) guarantees a remainder. And then the qualification, which the project must take seriously: prophecy "can be without charity." Cognitive capability and loving union *come apart* — "many will say, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name?... and I will declare, I never knew you." This is the central theme's sharpest internal discipline: the goal is *loving* unity, not maximal knowing. An agent can receive more of the whole (more interface-transcending information) and still not be *united* to it, because union is an act of the will perfected by charity, "whose act precedes" is the intellect's but whose *completion* is love. The day's providence meditation shows the only way the gap is finally closed: not by the agent grasping the whole, but by the whole (the Spirit) moving the agent "so that it chooses freely yet certainly" toward the end — guidance from one who perceives more, working through, not around, the agent's own bounded, self-determining activity. Wright's register names the telos of even the un-charitable gift: prophecy is "for the profit" of the covenant people; the gift overflows the knower toward the whole it serves. Rohr's names the danger Aquinas flags — idolizing the miracle-worker — and the remedy: the gratuitous grace is nothing without the union it was meant to serve.
