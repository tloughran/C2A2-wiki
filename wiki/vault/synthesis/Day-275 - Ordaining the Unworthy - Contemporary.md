# Day 275 — Ordaining the Unworthy (Contemporary)

## Frame

Aquinas treats the effect of the sacrament of Order (Q.35) and the qualities required of those who receive it (Q.36). Behind the canonical detail sits one of the most consequential questions in sacramental theology: does the *effect* of a sacred act depend on the *worthiness* of the one who performs it? The Church's settled answer — that an unworthy or even sinful minister still validly confers grace (later crystallized as *ex opere operato*) — distinguishes the office from the man. The medieval question is theological: how can grace flow through an unworthy channel? The contemporary version under mind/conscious-realist monism is structural: when is a function fixed by a *role* in a system rather than by the interior state of the individual occupying it?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **the independence of a role's function from the bearer's interior state.** Aquinas needs to hold two things at once: that personal holiness genuinely matters (he lays down real qualities required of ordinands), and that the sacrament's efficacy does not hang on it (else no one could ever be sure their sacraments "worked"). The general problem — far beyond theology — is when and why a system's output is determined by structural position rather than by the private condition of the part filling that position. Money works in an untrustworthy hand; a true theorem proved by a scoundrel is still true; a load-bearing role functions regardless of the beam's feelings about it.

## Resource

The emerging paradigm gives this a precise formal home. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, PRS-07, establishes that the Free Energy Principle is *substrate-independent*: a system's status as an inference-performing agent is fixed by its Markov-blanket boundary conditions — the statistical separation of inside from outside — not by what it is made of or by any privileged interior essence. Function follows structural position. Extended to the collective agent (PRS-06), this says an office is a *role* defined by its place in the community's shared generative model; the role does what the structure specifies, with considerable independence from the private state of whoever currently occupies it.

the Levin-tradition wiki's PRS-01 offers a structural analogue from biology: the bioelectric *target-state* — the field-level specification of the body's form — operates above the "merit" or condition of any single cell. The field coordinates toward the target even when individual cells are damaged or unreliable; the pattern is carried at the level of the whole, not certified by the virtue of each part. This is the natural shape of a function that survives an unworthy bearer.

Rohr supplies the theological reading without dissolving the tension. His PRS-03 (the Universal Christ — the divine pattern present in and acting through all creation, including matter) gives the warrant for grace working *through* a channel rather than being generated *by* it: the channel need not be the source. And his PRS-06 (not living by being right; identity decoupled from the correctness of one's current state) names, at the level of persons, exactly the decoupling the doctrine requires — the office holds even when the man does not. Wright's accent on grace as gift and on covenant faithfulness that exceeds human worth grounds the whole in Scripture: the gift is God's, not the minister's.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel reading: **the validity of an unworthy minister's act is intelligible as the substrate-independence of a structural role.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: the firm distinction between the office and the man, and the refusal to let the gift's reliability hang on private virtue. The monist resources *clarify the mechanism* of that distinction — function fixed by structural/boundary conditions (Friston PRS-07), carried at the level of the whole rather than certified by the part (Levin PRS-01). Where it *reframes*: it shows that "the office exceeds its bearer" is not a special pleading invented to rescue the sacraments, but an instance of a general and well-understood pattern in how roles work in cooperative systems.

The seam must be marked honestly, and it is a sharp one. Substrate-independence explains how a *function* can be position-fixed; it does not establish that *supernatural grace* actually passes through the unworthy minister to the ordinand. That claim — the heart of Q.35 — is strictly theological, resting on the Church's understanding of Christ as the true minister of every sacrament. The science furnishes a clarifying analogy for the *structure* of the doctrine; it neither confirms nor could confirm its supernatural content. Likewise, the qualities Aquinas requires of ordinands (Q.36) are canonical and theological judgments, not findings. The monist reading actually preserves Aquinas's both/and: holiness matters (the part's condition is not nothing — a defected cell degrades the field), and yet the role's core function is structurally secured (substrate-independence).

## The central-theme thread

The central claim turns on our dependence, as limited perspectives, on guidance from those who perceive more of the whole. This article presses the uncomfortable corollary: that guidance can reach us through a conduit far more limited and compromised than we would wish — even through one who perceives little and deserves less. The grace channeled exceeds what the channel itself perceives or merits, because its source is the whole, not the part. There is consolation here for the central theme's "fear and trembling": our access to the whole does not rest on the perfection of the human mediators between us and it. And there is a warning: the very substrate-independence that secures the office's function is what makes personal worthiness a real and separate obligation rather than a precondition the system enforces automatically.

## Bridges

- **Friston PRS-07** (formal): substrate-independence — function fixed by Markov-blanket/structural conditions, not the bearer's interior. Why this pairing: it is the precise formal image of office-independent-of-man.
- **Friston PRS-06** (formal): the office as a role in the collective generative model. Why: it locates the priesthood's function structurally rather than in private virtue.
- **Levin PRS-01** (empirical, by analogy): the field/target-state operating above any single cell's merit. Why: it shows a function carried at the level of the whole, surviving unreliable parts.
- **Rohr PRS-03 / PRS-06** (theological): the Universal Christ acting through matter; identity decoupled from being right. Why: it warrants grace flowing through, not from, the channel, and names the decoupling at the level of persons.
- **Wright on grace-as-gift** (theological): covenant faithfulness exceeding human worth. Why: it anchors the gift in God's action rather than the minister's.
