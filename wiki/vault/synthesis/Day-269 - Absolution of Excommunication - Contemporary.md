# Day 269 — Absolution of Excommunication (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (Suppl. Q.23-24) treats the *limits of contact* with the excommunicated — the faithful withhold ordinary fellowship to mark the separation and prompt amendment, with allowances for necessity and prior duty — and *absolution from excommunication*: the same authority that bound can loose, restoring the member to the communion. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: what is it for a collective agent to *re-incorporate* a member it had excluded, and why is the boundary it drew reversible by the same power that drew it?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how is a severed member restored to the whole — and what makes the boundary a collective agent draws permeable rather than final?** The withholding of fellowship presses the same point from the other side: the social cut is maintained *as a signal*, ordered to amendment, not to permanent estrangement.

## Resource

Levin's program supplies the leading move, completing yesterday's. In Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work, PRS-07 (bioelectric reprogramming as a therapeutic paradigm) is the model of restoration: a defected cell is not destroyed but *normalized* — the field's signaling is repaired so the rogue cell re-couples to the body's collective goals. Absolution is exactly this in the ecclesial register: the loosing restores the member's coupling to the communion. The cut of Day 268 was medicinal precisely because this re-coupling was its point.

Friston gives the formal symmetry. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work, PRS-22 supplies the boundary itself — the group-level Markov blanket a collective maintains as a whole, distinct from the blankets of its members — so excommunication is a redrawing of that blanket to put the member outside, and absolution is the inverse operation: re-incorporating him inside it, restoring the mutual modeling (PRS-06: agents couple by reducing prediction error in each other's behavior) that constitutes belonging — noting that PRS-06 is marked (Implicit) at Confidence Medium in the register, so this leg is the network's reconstruction rather than a measured result, and it is the weaker of the two Friston records carrying the argument. The binding/loosing symmetry is structural: the same power that defines a boundary can dissolve it, because both are the one operation of the whole regulating its membership. The withholding of fellowship, on this reading, is the community holding the boundary salient — keeping the prediction-error of the separation legible to the member so it can move him.

Rohr names the shape. In Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr's work, PRS-02 sets out the order-disorder-reorder pattern and its key claim: the reorder that follows genuine disorder is *deeper* than the original order because it has been tested and survived. Excommunication is the disorder that interrupts a false order; absolution is the reorder that re-includes the member at a deeper level than before. Wright grounds the symmetry in the dominical grant — what is bound on earth and loosed on earth — and in the restoration of the offender to the covenant body once amended. That the withheld fellowship and the restored *sacraments and suffrages* are real spiritual goods, and that absolution conveys grace, remain theological.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **absolution is the re-coupling of a severed member to the whole by the same jurisdictional power that severed him — the collective agent re-incorporating into its boundary what it had excluded, with the interim withholding of fellowship held as a signal ordered to that return.** This *shares* with Aquinas the binding/loosing symmetry, the medicinal withholding of contact, and the restoration to communion. It *reframes* absolution as re-coupling to the collective field (Levin PRS-07) and re-incorporation into the group-level Markov blanket (Friston PRS-22/06), and the social cut as a salient, reversible signal. It *firms up* that the boundary a whole draws is permeable *by design* — restoration uses the same machinery as exclusion. It *marks* that the spiritual reality of the withheld and restored goods, and the grace of absolution, are theological; the resource gives the *form* of re-incorporation, not the grace it conveys.

## The central-theme thread

Absolution is where the central claim shows its direction. Excommunication individuated the member by hard separation; absolution dissolves that separation and returns him to the whole — the perspective-limited agent re-included in the people united with God. The boundary was never the goal; loving unity was. The same power binds and looses because both serve the one telos: to keep the member oriented toward, and finally restored to, the unity he could not maintain alone. Disorder resolves into reorder, and the door the keys can close they are made to re-open.
