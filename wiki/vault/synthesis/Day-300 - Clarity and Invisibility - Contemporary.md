# Day 300 — Clarity and Invisibility (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.85–86 close the qualities of risen bodies. **Clarity** is the glorified body's brightness ("lightsomeness") — the *visible manifestation* of the saints' invisible spiritual glory, "a beauty becoming to their reward." Its degrees track charity: "star differs from star in glory" (1 Cor 15); "the righteous will shine like the sun." This clarity is visible to non-glorified eyes (the Transfiguration is the precedent), yet the glorified body can also will to be *invisible* — Christ "vanished from their sight" at Emmaus — so visibility itself becomes obedient to the will. Then the dark mirror, Q.86: the **bodies of the damned** rise *incorruptible* (they cannot die again) and *passible* — restored to full health *precisely so that they can feel and endure pain* without the dulling that sickness or missing limbs would bring; the deformities that would *lessen* suffering are removed, while the body remains able to suffer. The symmetry is explicit: "as the body cooperated in merit, so it cooperated in sin," and is therefore rewarded or punished accordingly. The contemporary version, at the close of the Summa's anthropology: **what is a body that has become the fully legible expression of the soul it organizes — and what is a perspective restored to full capacity yet closed against the whole?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **the relation between an agent's inner state and its outward appearance — and whether that appearance is transparent, controllable, and morally weighted**. Aquinas's clarity is *inner state rendered outwardly*: glory made visible, gradated by charity, and partly under the agent's own control (visible or invisible at will). The damned are the same machinery inverted: a body restored to full expressive and sensory capacity, but expressing and feeling a closed, averted state. The contemporary sciences of mind and signalling reach the same structural place whenever they ask how an agent's internal state becomes an outward, readable signal — and how far that signalling is under the agent's control.

## Resource

This is the culminating image of the whole arc — the body as the *expression* of the form that organizes it — so the resources are drawn together. The Levin-tradition program supplies the empirical figure: PRS-01 (*morphogenetic control*) shows that a body's *internal organizing state* (its bioelectric pattern) *is* what determines its outward form and behavior — inner state is, quite literally, *made legible as outward configuration*. Aquinas's "clarity is the visible manifestation of invisible glory" is, structurally, inner organizing state rendered as outward appearance. The Friston-tradition program reads the *controllability and reading* of appearance, though at a grade below Levin's: PRS-06 (*multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling*, (Implicit)/Medium) has agents coupling through mutual model-building, so that one agent's state is something other agents model; PRS-04 (*organism as active modeler*, (Implicit)/Medium) supplies the agency side — the living system as an active hypothesis-tester whose action follows from its model. Neither record treats *self-presentation* as such: that an agent might govern how it is read is this commentary's own extension of the two, and it is that extension, not the records, which carries Aquinas's "visible or invisible at the will of the blessed." The Hoffman-tradition program grounds why appearance is *rendered and controllable*: PRS-03 (*perception fitness-tracking*) and PRS-04 (*observer-dependent spacetime*) make what is *seen* a function of the interface between perceiver and perceived, so a perfected agent's governing its own visibility is the limit-case of appearance being interface-relative rather than brute. The Rohr-tradition program supplies the theological consummation: PRS-21 (*Universal-Christ-in-creation* — "creation as the first incarnation") and PRS-04 (*embodied love as epistemology*) read clarity as the light of Christ shining *through* glorified matter — creation become transparent to its source — and the saints' graded brightness as love made visible. The Wright-tradition program (PRS-04, and 1 Cor 15) anchors the scriptural register: the risen body "sown in dishonor, raised in glory" is continuous-yet-transfigured, its glory the outshining of the new-creation life. For the damned, the honest note is that Q.86 is *theological projection*; what the synthesis can responsibly carry is the *structural symmetry* — a perspective restored to full capacity can be either transparent-and-open or fully-capacitated-yet-closed — leaving the doctrine of eternal punishment itself flagged as theological and contested (and read, with Rohr's universal-restoration hope held in tension, as a question the tradition does not close uniformly).

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **clarity is the body made transparent to the soul's state — inner glory rendered as outward brightness, graded by love, with visibility itself answering to the will; the perfected perspective closes the gap between what it is and what it shows.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: clarity as the visible expression of inner glory, its degrees tracking charity, its visibility obedient to the will, and the body's moral participation on both sides. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas frames clarity as a quality infused into the body; the form-first and signalling programs recast it as *inner organizing state made outwardly legible and partly self-governed* — which makes "the body manifests the soul" the *natural* terminus of the whole hylomorphic account rather than an added marvel. Where evidence is *firm*: that internal organizing state determines outward form is well-supported (Levin PRS-01, High). A full grade weaker: that agents couple by modeling one another, and that a living system acts from the model it maintains, rest on (Implicit)/Medium records (Friston PRS-06, PRS-04) — and the further step, that an agent *governs how it is read*, is this commentary's extension of those records rather than a finding in either. Where *theological*: that glorified matter *shines* with spiritual glory, and the entire condition of the damned, are theological projections beyond any current science — marked as such, with the universalist hope noted as a live intra-traditional tension.

## The central-theme thread

Day 300 closes the project's anthropology on its deepest note. The whole *Summa* has been building a single figure: the human person as a *form organizing a body*, a particular embodied *perspective*. Clarity is what that perspective looks like *perfected* — fully **transparent**, no gap between what it is and what it shows, its inner state of love rendered as outward light, graded exactly to how much of the whole it has come to love. This is the project's loving unity at the level of appearance: not perspectives dissolved, but perspectives made *mutually legible* — each viewpoint fully seen by the others in charity, "star differing from star in glory," distinctness preserved and made beautiful in the shared light. The damned are the photographic negative: a perspective restored to full capacity and full feeling yet *closed* — sealed against the whole, unable to die out of its self-enclosure. That contrast is the project's "fear and trembling" given its final stakes: the lifelong computation toward loving unity is the difference between a perspective that becomes transparent and is included, and one that retains all its powers but remains shut. Three hundred days in, the synthesis-in-progress arrives where it has been heading — the limited perspective's destiny is either to be made luminously legible within the loving whole, or to keep its light to itself.
