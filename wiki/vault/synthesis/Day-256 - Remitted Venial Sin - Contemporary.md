# Day 256 — Remitted Venial Sin (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.87) asks whether and how penance remits *venial* sin — the lesser disorder that wounds but does not sever the relation to God. His answer is graded: venial sins are remitted not all-or-nothing but through acts informed by charity (and through sacramental and devotional means), with the disposition's intensity bearing on the degree of remission. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: is the repair of a strayed agent a binary switch or a continuous, graded reduction of disorder?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **is corrective repair binary or graded?** If forgiveness were strictly on/off, venial sin would have no distinct treatment; Aquinas's graded account presupposes that disorder comes in degrees and is reduced in degrees. The contemporary question is what kind of process admits of degree in exactly this way.

## Resource

Friston's program supplies the leading move. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, active inference reduces free energy (prediction error against preferred states) *continuously* — there is no discrete "corrected/uncorrected" flag but a magnitude that falls as belief and action realign the system. Venial-sin remission as graded follows naturally: lesser disorder is lesser residual free energy, reduced incrementally by charity-informed acts rather than toggled.

Hawkins sharpens the structure. In Tom's PRS-form record of Hawkins' program, PRS-06 (the neocortex is heterarchical, not a strict hierarchy, with corrections propagating across many levels in parallel) gives an image of repair as corrections percolating gradually up and across a layered predictive memory — a disorder is unwound level by level, not erased at a stroke. This matches the medieval intuition that venial faults are cleared progressively as the whole disposition is reordered.

Rohr (PRS-27) keeps the depth-reading: the creature's inability to fully reciprocate an infinite love is generative rather than a defect — it is what sustains ongoing transformation, which is why "lesser" sins are dissolved in the continuing work rather than dispatched by a single verdict.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the remission of venial sin is graded because corrective repair is continuous free-energy reduction, with disorder unwound level by level rather than switched off.** This *shares* with Aquinas the degree-admitting structure and the role of charity's intensity. It *reframes* "degree of remission" as magnitude of residual prediction-error reduced (Friston) and as multi-level corrective propagation (Hawkins). It *firms up* the premise that repair admits of degree — a formal feature of these models, not a bare assertion. It *marks* that the *venial/mortal distinction itself* (what severs versus wounds the relation to God) is theological and not derivable from the formal gradient; and that grading the repair does not grade the *mercy*, which is given whole.

## The central-theme thread

The motif appears as incremental computation under limited resources. A perspective-limited agent does not leap to loving unity; it reduces its divergence from the whole by degrees, with the resources it perceives, lesser disorders dissolving continuously as the larger reordering proceeds. Venial-sin remission is the fine-grained texture of "working out salvation" — the small, continual corrections by which a finite perspective is kept oriented toward the whole it cannot yet fully see.

## Bridges

- **Friston PRS-01 (the Free Energy Principle; free energy as a minimized magnitude)** — *formal, well-developed.* Repair as graded magnitude, not a switch; sharpest resource.
- **Hawkins PRS-06 (hierarchical/heterarchical predictive memory)** — *empirical-in-domain (neuroscience).* Corrections propagate gradually across levels.
- **Rohr PRS-27 (the finite/infinite asymmetry as generative; continued growth toward union)** — *theological / contemplative.* Why the work of reordering is continued rather than completed at a stroke.
- **PRS-22 in the Stump-tradition wiki** (`traditions/stump/prs_triplets.md`) — *theological / moral-psychological.* The vices as predictable failure modes of an intellect-will system, each misordering a good that is itself real in object, manner, or *measure* relative to the agent's final end. The measure axis is what makes graded disorder — and therefore graded repair — intelligible. The register has no entry specific to venial-sin remission, so this receives the continuous-repair reframe rather than grounding it.

## Where this leaves us

Day 257 asks whether the virtues *lost* by sin are *restored* by penance, and in equal measure — moving from reducing disorder to *recovering a prior configuration*, where Levin's pattern-completion and Friston's path-dependence carry the analysis. Open thread: keep the venial/mortal content theological; the gradient models the repair's texture, not the sin's gravity.
