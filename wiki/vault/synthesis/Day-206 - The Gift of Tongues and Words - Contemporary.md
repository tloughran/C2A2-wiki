# Day 206 — The Gift of Tongues and Words (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas turns from prophecy to two further gratuitous graces, both ordered not to the recipient's own perfection but to *others*. Q.176 treats the gift of tongues; Q.177, "the gratuitous grace consisting in words." The gift of tongues, Aquinas says, was given the apostles first of all as the *reversal of Babel* — where "a diversity of tongues was inflicted by God as a punishment" for humanity's attempt "to make a name for itself instead of worshiping the one true God," tongues now re-gather "all of humankind again under the one name by which we are to be saved." Q.176 a.1 asks whether those who received the gift of tongues spoke in every language, and describes the mechanism precisely: the gift "regards the utterance of certain words which signify an intelligible truth," and the same content is heard by each listener in their own language — so much so that at Pentecost some heard the apostles and others, hearing only foreign sound, "deemed the apostles drunk." Then a.2 asks whether the gift of tongues is more excellent than the grace of prophecy, and ranks tongues *below* it ("the gift of prophecy regards the knowledge of things, which is more excellent than the knowledge of words"). The grace of the *word* (Q.177 a.1, whether any gratuitous grace attaches to words) is the above-natural aptitude to instruct — to teach so that wisdom is conveyed, attention aroused, and the hearer moved to love the teaching — yet, citing Gregory, "unless the Holy Ghost fill the hearts of the hearers, in vain does the voice of the teacher resound in the ears of the body." The contemporary version of the question: *what is it for intelligible content to cross from one limited perspective into another whose rendering apparatus differs — and what actually completes the crossing?*

## Problem

Two linked inquiry-problems sit under both the medieval and the contemporary frames. (1) **Babel: the opacity of scattered perspectives.** Different languages are mutually opaque encodings of the world; a perspective that masters one cannot, by that mastery, reach another. The problem is how a single intelligible content can be *delivered across* a manifold of perspective-specific encodings rather than being trapped inside one. (2) **The insufficiency of the utterance.** Aquinas insists, with Gregory, that the spoken word as such accomplishes nothing — "in vain does the voice resound" — unless something happens *within the hearer*. The problem is to say what the inner event is that turns received sound into grasped meaning, and why the gift is realized "more in the speaker" yet only *completed* in the listener.

## Resource

For (1), the leading move comes from interface theory together with Friston's program. Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman (PRS-03/PRS-04) makes every perception a *rendering through a species-specific interface*; a natural language is one such learned interface, a particular lossy encoding of a shared world. Aquinas's striking claim — that the content is "heard in the mind of others according to their own tongue" — reads almost exactly as *decoding through the receiver's own interface*: one intelligible truth, re-rendered into each hearer's encoding, so that those without the matching codec hear only noise ("drunk"). Tom's PRS-form record of Friston (PRS-06, multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling) supplies the process: communication is not the shipping of tokens but the *alignment of two generative models* — successful when speaker and hearer converge on a shared latent cause for the signal. For (2), this same resource answers the insufficiency-of-the-utterance problem directly: on the active-inference picture (Tom's PRS-form record of Friston, PRS-04, the organism as active modeler), meaning is *reconstructed on the receiver's side* by their own model; the utterance only supplies evidence that the hearer's prior must take up and update. McGilchrist names the qualitative side of the same event: Tom's PRS-form record of McGilchrist (PRS-09) holds that truth "arises through encounter, and in every genuine encounter both participants are changed," and (PRS-12) that the deepest transmission is not propositional transfer but participatory uptake — exactly Gregory's point that the word lands only when the hearer's heart is engaged. Rohr supplies the unifying telos: Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr (PRS-03, the Universal Christ as one pattern present across all traditions; PRS-01, non-dual unity-in-distinction) gives the Babel-reversal its shape — many tongues, one pattern; unity that does *not* erase the distinct encodings but reaches the same content through each. CROSS-003 (attention constructs reality — McGilchrist, Friston, Hoffman, Fredrickson) is the network locus.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **communication across individuated perspectives is the re-rendering of one intelligible content through each receiver's own perspective-specific interface, completed not by the utterance but by an inner reconstruction in which the hearer's generative model takes up the signal and aligns on the shared latent cause. The gift of tongues is the limit-case of this ordinary architecture — a content delivered intact across encodings that would otherwise be mutually opaque; the grace of the word is the limit-case of its completion — an instruction that reliably triggers the inner uptake.**

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps content "heard in each hearer's own tongue"; keeps the gift "more in the speaker" yet realized in the hearer; keeps interpretation as a distinct further grace; keeps the ranking of tongues below prophecy (knowledge of *things* exceeds knowledge of *words* — content exceeds codec); and keeps Gregory's insistence that the utterance alone is empty.

*Where it reframes.* "Signified by phantasms in an imaginary vision" becomes *decoding through a perspective-specific interface*; "the Holy Ghost perfects the work within" becomes *receiver-side reconstruction by the hearer's own model* — with the theological claim that the *source* and *efficacy* of that inner work are the Spirit's carried, not reduced. Babel becomes the formal fact of mutually opaque encodings; its reversal becomes one content re-rendered across them.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* That meaning is reconstructed receiver-side rather than transmitted as tokens is well-supported by predictive-processing and the mutual-modeling account of communication (empirical/formal, medium-strong). That a language is one lossy interface among many is structural (medium). The Babel-reversal-as-perspective-repair is a central-theme analogy with real yield (strong as analogy). The Spirit as the inner completer, and "the one name by which we are saved," are theological and carried as such.

## The central-theme thread

This day is the project's thesis told as a *founding myth and its repair*. The project holds that conscious agents are individuated by the limits of their perspective; Babel is precisely that individuation hardened into mutual opacity — perspectives so encoded that they cannot reach one another, and the cause named is self-deification ("to make a name for ourselves"), the perspective absolutizing itself. The gift of tongues is the first repair: not the *erasure* of distinct perspectives (each still hears in their own tongue) but one content reaching all of them — unity-in-distinction, Rohr's non-dual pattern. Yet the day also disciplines the claim, exactly where the project insists it must. Tongues is ranked *below* prophecy, and prophecy itself was ranked, two days ago, below charity ("if I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, but have not love"). The gift that crosses interfaces is *instrumental* to loving communal union, not that union itself — a re-gathering "under one name," which is salvation on the reading recorded at PRS-13 in `traditions/wright/prs_triplets.md` (inclusion in the covenanted people united with God, the homecoming frame; **Confidence High**) — inclusion in the one covenanted people, not a private virtuosity of cross-encoding. And the word's completion "within the hearer" is the project's computational telos in miniature: meaning, like loving unity, is not delivered ready-made but *worked out* on the receiver's side, under guidance — here, the guidance of one who perceives more of the whole and the Spirit who "fills the heart."

## Where this leaves us

The charisms continue their descent from knowledge toward act: from prophecy (knowledge of things), through tongues and the word (the delivery and inner completion of that knowledge across perspectives), toward the most public sign of all. Tomorrow Aquinas takes up *miracles* (Q.178) — the gratuitous grace that authenticates the word not in the hearer's understanding but in the world's matter. Carry forward the discipline established here: crossing the gap between perspectives is real and graced, but it is *for* loving, communal union; the codec is never the content, and the word is empty until it is taken up within.
