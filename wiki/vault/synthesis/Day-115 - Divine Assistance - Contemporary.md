# Day 115 — Divine Assistance (Contemporary)

## Frame

Q.108-109 close the law treatise and open the grace treatise. Q.108 lists what the new law contains — sacraments and the moral precepts of charity; instruction on interior actions; the evangelical counsels. Q.109 asks the fundamental questions about grace: what can man do without it? Without grace: he cannot fulfill the commandments (a.4), cannot love God above all things in his Fallen state (a.3), cannot merit eternal life (a.5), cannot prepare himself for grace (a.6), cannot rise from sin (a.7), cannot persevere (a.10). Yet — Trent's careful balancing claim — God commands not impossibilities; God's commands assume the divine assistance.

This is the climactic central-theme question of the entire law-and-grace section: *how can a perspective-limited agent reach a loving-unity end that exceeds the proportion of its own perspective?*

## Problem

The shared problem here is the *bootstrapping* problem. From a perspective-limited generative-model, how does the agent come to operate under a generative-model that exceeds the proportion of the limited one? Aquinas's answer: only by the import of priors that the agent does not generate. The contemporary version is precisely the bootstrapping problem in learning theory: a sufficiently impoverished prior cannot reach a target distribution without exogenous information.

A second nested problem: how to hold the *necessity of grace* alongside the *non-impossibility of the commandments*. This is Trent's careful position; Aquinas in Q.109 a.4 explicitly endorses it. The synthesis must hold both poles without collapsing either: the agent cannot do this *of itself*, and yet the agent *can* do this with divine assistance, and yet the assistance is gratuitous (not merit-earned), and yet the agent's response to the assistance is the agent's free response.

## Resource: bootstrapping under perspective-limitation; grace as imported prior

The whole convergent paradigm comes together here.

**Levin's morphogenetic field at the agent level** (PRS-04): an organism develops toward a target morphology under the dynamic of its bioelectric / cognitive system. When the system is sufficiently impoverished — through damage, mutation, environmental insult — the target cannot be reached without external intervention (recovery requires the import of additional informational and physical resources). This is Aquinas's Q.109 a.4 picture precisely: post-Fall human nature has the same target (the loving-unity flourishing) but the system is sufficiently impoverished that the target cannot be reached from within.

**Friston's active inference under resource-bounded agency** (FLAG-05): an agent's behavior follows from the priors of its generative model. *Updating* the priors requires either prediction-error information (which the agent must already be in some relation to the truth to receive) or the import of priors from elsewhere (other agents; trained corpora; explicit teaching; grace). The bootstrapping problem is the formal version of Q.109 a.6: "to prepare oneself for grace is, as it were, to be turned to God; just as whoever has his eyes turned away from the light of the sun prepares himself to receive the sun's light by turning his eyes toward the sun. Hence it is clear that man cannot prepare himself to receive the light of grace except by the gratuitous help of God moving him inwardly." The agent cannot turn its priors toward the target unless something already in relation to the target moves the priors.

**Kastrup's analytic idealism** (PRS-02): mind-at-large is the ontological primitive; individual minds are *alters* — dissociated centers within mind-at-large. Grace, on this reading, is the operation of mind-at-large *re-integrating* the alter's priors with the deeper structure. It is not a strange exception to natural psychology; it is the *default* dynamic when an alter's dissociation reaches the threshold where deeper structure becomes operative.

**Hoffman's interface theory** (FLAG-02): species-specific perspective is the constitutive form of agent-individuation. Q.109 a.5's proportion-argument ("acts conducive to an end must be proportioned to that end… everlasting life is an end exceeding the proportion of human nature… hence man by his natural endowments cannot produce meritorious works proportionate to everlasting life") is the precise inter-interface translation: a finite interface cannot operate on the deeper-pattern level without recalibration. Grace is the recalibration.

**McGilchrist's hemispheric integration** (FLAG-09): the integrated right-and-left hemisphere is the conscious-functional analog of the grace-and-nature integration. The right hemisphere's relational-contextual grasp must master the left hemisphere's rule-following capacity. When grace operates, this is the integrated functioning Aquinas's Q.109 a.9 describes: "first, a habitual gift whereby corrupted human nature is healed, and after being healed is lifted up so as to work deeds meritoriously of everlasting life; secondly, man needs the help of grace in order to be moved by God to act."

**Fredrickson's broaden-and-build** (FLAG-11): grace operates through the positive-affect-broadening dynamic that produces durable intrinsic motivation. Q.108 a.3's "the new law directs a man to seek the kingdom of God and His will above all things" is the affect-positive prior that broadens the agent's behavioral repertoire toward genuinely loving action — not the fear-narrowed compliance of an agent in chronic threat.

**CROSS-15 (master wiki, bootstrapping under limited resources)**: a robust finding across cognitive science, machine learning, and developmental psychology is that *no* agent can bootstrap from a sufficiently impoverished prior to a target prior; the import of structured information from outside is necessary. Aquinas's claim about grace is the medieval formulation of this empirical truth — though Aquinas locates the import metaphysically in the divine action.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

Grace is the *operation of mind-at-large* (Kastrup) / *imported prior-update* (Friston) / *recalibration of the value-interface* (Hoffman) / *master-hemisphere integration* (McGilchrist) / *broaden-and-build dynamic* (Fredrickson) / *morphogenetic field-restoration* (Levin) — all describing the same reality at different levels of explanation.

The reality is this: the agent's generative-model priors, post-Fall and pre-grace, are insufficient to track the loving-unity target. The target is real, the natural-law access to it is partially intact, but the agent's resources are not proportioned to actualizing it. Grace supplies the proportioning by importing priors from outside the agent's own generation. These priors are *personal* (in Aquinas's idiom, the indwelling Spirit; in Wright's reading, participation in the covenant community in whom Christ is the Lord); they are *sacramental* (in Catholic teaching, the new-law sacraments efficaciously communicate the grace they signify); they are *contemplative* (in Rohr's reading, the practice of seeing-the-Christ-in-everything that produces durable interior alignment).

The agent's role is the *receptive cooperation*: turning toward the source whose movement makes the turning possible. This is the Trent-Aquinas balance. The agent does not earn grace; the agent does not generate grace; the agent does receive grace and cooperate with it. The agent's salvation is genuinely the agent's salvation *and* genuinely gratuitous.

### What this shares with Aquinas

- The proportion-argument: a supernatural end requires a supernatural proportioning.
- The bootstrapping limit: the agent cannot prepare itself for grace by its own initiative.
- The cooperative structure: the agent's free will is *moved* by grace, not bypassed.
- The two-fold help (habitual + actual): the standing recalibration of priors plus the moment-to-moment movement to act.
- The grace of final perseverance: continued operation of grace throughout the agent's life, not a one-time event.
- The careful balance: God commands not impossibilities; the commands assume the assistance; the assistance is gratuitous; the agent is responsible.

### Where this reframes

- **Grace is not an *addition* to a complete nature; it is the *operation* of mind-at-large in the agent.** On the mind-monist reading, the strict nature/grace separation is softened. There is one mind, operating at multiple levels; the conscious-explicit operation in the agent (grace) is the same mind that is the deepest structure of the natural agent (nature). This is closer to Rohr's *Universal Christ* than to a strict two-tiered Thomism.
- **The proportion-argument is now empirically supported.** The bootstrapping problem in learning theory is the structural analog. The agent cannot reach a target prior with sufficient distance from its current prior except by importing structured information from outside.
- **The agent's "free will" is not violated by grace; the agent's will is *enabled* by grace.** This is Aquinas's careful position (Q.109 a.6 last sentence: "free will can only be turned to God when God turns it"). On the active-inference reading: the agent's actions are downstream of its priors; updating the priors is what enables the agent to act differently. The updated prior is the agent's, not an external constraint.

### Where this is firm vs. speculative

*Empirical*: Active-inference accounts of resource-bounded agency. Self-determination theory on intrinsic motivation. Fredrickson's broaden-and-build. McGilchrist's hemispheric data. Bootstrapping limits in learning theory.

*Empirical*: Levin's morphogenetic-field work on pattern-restoration under impoverished initial conditions.

*Formal*: The bootstrapping problem in machine learning and cognitive development is mathematically articulable.

*Theological*: Wright's Pauline soteriology. Rohr's sacramental ontology. Catholic teaching on grace, including Trent's precise formulations.

*Speculative*: The full integration of mind-monism with Catholic grace-theology is an ongoing theological-metaphysical project. Some classical Catholic theologians will resist the softening of the nature/grace distinction; others (Karl Rahner, Henri de Lubac in *Surnaturel*) have moved in compatible directions.

*Theologically hard*: The "grace of final perseverance" given to some and not to others (Q.109 a.10) is a difficult passage in the predestination tradition. Aquinas, Augustine, and the Reformed tradition have argued this; Wright's Pauline reading (and much subsequent Catholic theology) softens the apparent harshness through covenantal-corporate framing. The synthesis should hold the difficulty rather than flatten it.

## The Central-Theme Thread

This is the synthesis-day for the central theme of the project. Every element comes together:

**Conscious agents are individuated by the limitations of their perspective.** Q.109's entire treatment is structured around this. The agent's natural-law access is *real but limited*. The agent's *fomes*-corrupted priors are the inherited limitation. The agent's incapacity to bootstrap to the loving-unity end is the structural limitation.

**When all limitations are removed, the simple whole is seen, and every limited perspective is contained in it.** The eternal pattern is what the agent's perspective gives partial access to; the loving-unity end is the actualized perception of the pattern; grace is the operation by which the limited perspective is brought into actual alignment with the pattern.

**Learning to obtain the goal of loving unity from each perspective is a computational task, limited by the resources we perceive.** Q.109 a.5's proportion-argument is the medieval formulation. Friston's active inference is the contemporary formulation. The agent cannot generate the loving-unity end from its own resources; the resources must be supplemented.

**Therefore we "work out our salvation in fear and trembling" (Phil 2:12).** The agent's task is the *cooperative receptivity* with what is being done. The "fear and trembling" are the appropriate posture under recognition that the work exceeds the agent's resources. The "salvation" is genuinely worked-out by the agent (Wright: inclusion in the covenanted community is worked-out by the community's actual practice) *and* genuinely received as gift (Wright: justification is grounded in Christ's faithfulness, not the agent's faithfulness).

The whole law-and-grace treatise has been building to this: a careful articulation of how the loving-unity computational task is reachable by perspective-limited agents only through the operation of priors imported from outside their perspective. Aquinas's medieval formulation, Wright's Pauline-covenantal reading, Rohr's sacramental-contemplative reading, and the convergent monist science (Levin / Hoffman / Friston / Kastrup / McGilchrist / Fredrickson) all converge on the same structural claim.

The differences from Aquinas are not in the structural claim but in the *background metaphysics*: on a mind-monist reading, grace is not a strange addition to a value-neutral universe; it is the *default* operation of mind-at-large when an alter's dissociation reaches the threshold for re-integration. This is closer to Rohr than to strict two-tiered Thomism, and closer to the patristic theology of *theosis* (Aquinas's contemporary East-Christian counterparts) than to the strict scholastic separation of natural and supernatural.
