# Day 303 — New Heavens and Earth (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Two questions, both metaphysical. Q.91: will the world be *renewed*? Aquinas says yes — a new heavens and new earth (2 Pet 3), not a change of species but an added "perfection of glory," so that even the bodily senses may partake of God through his corporeal effects; heavenly motion ceases into rest, and the luminaries brighten. Q.92: can the created intellect see God *in his essence*? Yes — but only because God supernaturally elevates the mind to be "informed directly by his essence," since no creature could mediate the infinite; and yet the blessed, seeing God truly, do *not comprehend* him — they do not know all that God knows.

Austin does something unusual on-air: he flags Aquinas's argument about plants and animals as resting on a medieval physics of celestial matter "which I don't believe he would hold if he was alive today." That candor is a gift, because it marks exactly where the medieval cosmology is load-bearing and obsolete, and where the deeper structural claims survive.

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **the relation between the world as perceived and the world as it is — and what "seeing reality directly" could even mean for a finite knower.** Aquinas frames renewal so that the senses can finally reach what they always pointed at, and frames the beatific vision as direct acquaintance with the real, unmediated by any creaturely representation. The contemporary form: is perceived spacetime the fundamental reality or a species-specific rendering, and what would it be to know the underlying reality *as it is* rather than through the interface?

## Resource (metaphysics — Hoffman, Kastrup, Levin to the front)

The leading move is interface theory. Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman's program (PRS-04 in the Hoffman-tradition wiki, observer-dependent spacetime; PRS-16/18, the IOS theorem and "spacetime is doomed") argues that perceived spacetime is a fitness-tuned interface, not the fundamental ontology — a claim the network treats as theorem-grade for the fitness-beats-truth result, while its strong ontological reading remains contested (CROSS-002, "is spacetime fundamental?"; CROSS-014/QIT, matter as derivative of consciousness). Kastrup's analytic idealism supplies the substrate: a mind-at-large within which individuated minds are dissociated alters (PRS-02), with the persistence of memory and continuity a live question precisely because universal consciousness does not end (PRS-26). Levin's program adds that *form* is empirically real and reachable — morphological attractors in a Platonic space of possible bodies (PRS-16, PRS-03) — so that "renewal not of species but of glory" has a contemporary echo: the same attractor, more fully realized.

Read through these, Aquinas's two claims rhyme. "The carnal eye cannot attain the divine essence; it will see the Godhead in its corporeal effects" is the interface point exactly — the senses reach reality only through a rendering. And "the mind informed directly by God's essence" is what it would mean to drop the interface and know the substrate as it is.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary parallel proposes: the renewed creation is the interface *perfected rather than discarded* — the rendering made fully translucent to what it renders — while the beatific vision is the limit case of knowing the substrate directly. With Aquinas, it keeps that the body and the sensible world are *included*, not escaped (this is where Wright's new-creation theology is decisive, below). Where it reframes: it reads "world made new" not as a second cosmos bolted on but as the present one seen without the fitness-distortion of the interface — CROSS-062 in the network index already pairs Hoffman's non-veridical interface with the Thomistic priority of intellect over sense. Evidence strength, marked honestly: the fitness-beats-truth theorem is formal; the ontological "spacetime is doomed" reading is contested; analytic idealism is a serious but non-consensus metaphysics; and Aquinas's specific cosmology (immutable celestial matter, motion-to-rest) is, as Austin himself notes, obsolete physics — Carroll's account of the arrow of time (PRS-21, PRS-14) is the live replacement for "why motion ceases," and it does not deliver Aquinas's conclusion.

## The central-theme thread

The decisive beat is Q.92's third article: the blessed see God *truly* but do *not comprehend* him; "it is not necessary that he who knows a cause should know all its effects unless he comprehend the cause, and this is not in the competency of a created intellect." Even in beatitude, the perspective remains finite. The whole is *seen* — but not exhaustively *contained* by the one who sees it.

This is the project's central claim stated by Aquinas himself, and it is worth marking how it *differs* from a naïve reading of the synthesis-in-progress. The claim is that when perspective-limitation is removed the simple whole is seen and every limited perspective is contained *in it*. Aquinas's vision-without-comprehension keeps the asymmetry honest: the finite agent is contained in the whole and sees the whole, but does not become the whole — individuation is not dissolved into the infinite, it is fulfilled within it. The agent's desire is satisfied "by the mere fact of seeing God," not by acquiring God's omniscience. Loving unity is the telos; comprehension is not. That is the difference between union and absorption, and it is exactly the seam where Kastrup's open question about post-mortem continuity (PRS-26) becomes pressing rather than decorative.

"Fear and trembling" recedes here, as it should at the vision of the end — but the finitude that grounds it does not. The blessed still see *as creatures*, from a standpoint, with a desire that is filled rather than abolished.

## Bridges

- **PRS-04 + PRS-16/18 in the Hoffman-tradition wiki / CROSS-002, CROSS-062** — *perceived spacetime as interface; intellect over sense* (formal for FBT; ontology contested). Renders "the eye sees God only in corporeal effects" as the interface point, and direct vision as dropping the interface.
- **PRS-02 + PRS-26 in the Kastrup-tradition wiki** — *individuation as dissociation; persistence of continuity in universal consciousness* (philosophical/speculative). Keeps vision-without-comprehension from collapsing into absorption.
- **PRS-16 + PRS-03 in the Levin-tradition wiki** — *form as a real attractor in Platonic space* (empirical/formal). "Renewal not of species but of glory" = the same attractor more fully realized.
- **PRS-05 + PRS-06 in the Wright-tradition wiki** — *salvation as incorporation; resurrection as firstfruits of new creation, not escape* (theological, High). The strongest guard against reading "new earth" as world-denial.
- **PRS-21 + PRS-14 in the Carroll-tradition wiki** — *the arrow of time* (formal). The live replacement for Aquinas's obsolete "motion ceases" cosmology; marks where the medieval physics fails and the structural claim survives.
