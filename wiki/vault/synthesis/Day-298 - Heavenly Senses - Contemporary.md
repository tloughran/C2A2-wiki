# Day 298 — Heavenly Senses (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.81–82 describe the *quality* of risen bodies and the **impassibility** of the glorified. All rise at the perfect age (~30, Christ's maturity) and at the stature each would have reached "if nature had not failed," with diversity of size kept "for the greater beauty of the whole," like notes making a chord. Both sexes rise; sexual difference remains "without shame, since there will be no lust." Then the two key claims. **Impassibility**: because the soul will be "perfectly subordinated to God," the body will be "perfectly subject to the soul," so "no external object can thwart" the perfection flowing from soul to body — the glorified body cannot be changed contrary to its perfection. And yet **sensation remains**: the glorified senses receive "the form which is received without the material" — color without the matter of the colored thing, "without the material change that occurs in my hand when I touch a hot stove" — and *all* the senses are in act, perception perfected rather than abolished. The contemporary version: **what is sensation when it receives the form of its object without suffering the disordering material passion — and what is "impassibility" if not numbness?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **the nature of perception as form-reception, and the difference between receiving information and being disordered by it**. Aquinas is drawing a precise line: the *formal* content of sensation (the color, the sound) is retained and perfected, while the *material passion* (the burn, the bodily disturbance, the passion that "obscures the use of reason") is removed. The contemporary sciences of perception draw structurally the same line whenever they distinguish the *information* a system extracts from a signal from the *physical disturbance* the signal imposes on the substrate.

## Resource

Because perception and embodiment are at stake, the lead comes from the interface and active-inference programs. The Hoffman-tradition program supplies the central image, and it is close to verbatim. Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman's work (PRS-03, *perception fitness-tracking*; PRS-04, *observer-dependent spacetime*; PRS-01, *hard-problem transformation*) treats perception as the reception of *task-relevant form/information* through an interface — the perceived qualities are *renderings*, not transfers of the object's matter into the perceiver. Aquinas's "the senses receive the form without the material — color without the matter of the tree" is, structurally, exactly an interface picture: what is taken in is *form/information*, never the stuff. On that reading, glorified sensation is the interface *purified* — receiving the form of its object with none of the disordering material residue that, in this life, the interface also drags along. The Friston-tradition program sharpens "impassibility." PRS-04 (*organism as active modeler*) and PRS-09 (*minimal theory of consciousness in active inference*) describe perception as *active* model-updating rather than passive imprinting; impassibility, read through this, is not the cessation of perception but the limit-case in which the perceiving system is so well-ordered that incoming signals *inform without destabilizing* — the body "cannot be changed contrary to its perfection" because its generative model is perfectly poised. That is precisely Aquinas's "the senses receive their object, but not the material changes" — reception without disorder. The Levin-tradition program (PRS-01) grounds the metaphysics: "the soul's complete command of its matter" is the limit of a form's control over the body it organizes — impassibility as maximal formal command, not numbness. The Rohr-tradition program governs the theological register: PRS-01 (*dualistic vs non-dual knowing*) and PRS-04 (*embodied love as epistemology*) read glorified perception as the healed, non-dual seeing in which the knower receives the known without grasping or distortion — the contemplative's purified perception made bodily and permanent.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **glorified sensation is the perceptual interface purified — it receives the *form* of its object with full fidelity and none of the disordering material passion; impassibility is the limit-case of a form's complete command of its matter, the body informed without being destabilized, not a body gone numb.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: sensation retained and perfected (not abolished), the form-without-matter character of perception, and impassibility as the soul's perfected command of the body. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas frames form-reception in Aristotelian species-terms; the interface and active-inference programs recast it as *information-reception by an active model*, which makes "receive the form, not the material change" the *ordinary* structure of perception rather than a special glorified feat — what is special is the *purity* (no material residue) and *stability* (no disorder). Where evidence is *firm*: that perception extracts information/form rather than transferring an object's matter, and that a well-ordered perceiving system can be informed without being destabilized, are well-supported. Where *speculative-to-theological*: that a body could receive *all* sensory form with *no* material passion whatever, and be wholly impassible, is a theological projection beyond any current system — the synthesis claims the *structure* (form-reception, stability) and marks the *limit-case totality* as eschatological.

## The central-theme thread

A perfected perspective is one that receives *more* of the whole with *less* distortion. Glorified perception is that ideal realized at the level of the senses: the interface takes in the form of things with full fidelity, no longer dragging the self-obscuring material passions — the burn, the overwhelming emotion that "obscures the use of reason" — that, in this life, limit and warp every perception. This is the project's individuation-by-limitation addressed *at its perceptual root*: here the limitation is not removed by abolishing the perspective (the senses remain, in full act) but by *purifying its interface* so that the particular embodied viewpoint receives the whole more truly. Loving unity does not blind the senses into a uniform glare; it gives each perspective a clearer, undistorted reception of all that is — diversity of stature kept "for the beauty of the whole," each viewpoint perfected as itself within the chord.

## Bridges

- **Hoffman PRS-03 / PRS-04 (formal-to-speculative; load-bearing):** perception as reception of form/information through an interface — "color without the matter" is an interface picture.
- **Friston PRS-09 (formal):** impassibility as the limit-case of an active model informed without destabilization — reception without disorder. **PRS-04** (*organism as active modeler*) supplies the active-modelling base case only; its record is *(Implicit)* / **Medium**, so the formal weight rests on PRS-09 and not on it.
- **Levin PRS-01 (empirical figure):** impassibility as the form's complete command of its matter, not numbness.
- **Rohr PRS-01 / PRS-04 (theological):** glorified perception as healed, non-dual seeing — receiving the known without grasping or distortion.
