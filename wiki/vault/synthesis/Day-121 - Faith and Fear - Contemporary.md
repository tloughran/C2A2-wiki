# Day 121 — Contemporary Parallel: What Causes Assent, and What Assent Does

## Frame

Aquinas asks two questions that look separate and are really one. What *causes* the act of faith (Q.6), and what does that act *do* to the person who makes it (Q.7)? Q.6 a.1 asks whether faith is infused into man by God and answers that the decisive cause cannot be external: of those who see one miracle or hear one sermon, some believe and some do not, so the external inducement is never sufficient; there must be an interior cause, God moving the will to assent by grace. Q.6 a.2 asks whether *lifeless* faith — assent held without charity — is still a gift of God, and answers that it is: the assent itself is infused even where the formed, charity-animated act is absent. Q.7 then takes the effects: a.1 finds that faith produces fear, distinguishing servile from filial fear, and a.2 that faith purifies the heart.

The contemporary version of the first question is the underdetermination of belief by evidence: why does the same stimulus fix one posterior in one agent and a different posterior in another? The contemporary version of the second is the question of what a deep cognitive commitment does to the rest of an agent's affective and volitional economy. Mind / conscious-realist monism lets us treat both as one phenomenon, because under it cognition, affect, and orientation are aspects of one model-based agent rather than separate faculties.

## Problem

The problem Aquinas names is precise and survives translation cleanly. Assent to what exceeds present sight is not compelled by the evidence available to the agent. Two people receive identical external input and diverge. So the cause of the divergence is not in the input. Aquinas locates it in an interior principle and identifies that principle as God. The inquiry-problem underneath is: *what fixes assent when the data do not?*

The second problem is downstream: once an agent holds such a commitment, why does the holding reorganize the agent's fears and loves rather than sitting inert as one more belief among others?

## Resource

The leading resource is Friston's active-inference program. In the PRS-form record of Friston's predictive-coding work (PRS-04 in the Friston-tradition wiki) the agent is a continuous hypothesis-testing system whose posterior is jointly fixed by sensory input *and* by priors with their precision-weights. Identical input does not fix identical belief, because what the input does to the model depends on the priors already installed and on how much precision the agent assigns them. Underdetermination is not a bug to be explained away; it is the expected behavior of any prior-bearing inferential system. PRS-10 (active reasoning via expected free energy) adds that agents select what to attend to in order to disambiguate competing models — assent is an active, not passive, settling.

Two further resources handle the effects. Fredrickson's program (PRS-02, PRS-04 in the Fredrickson-tradition wiki) gives love a measurable coupling-signature — vagal tone, neural synchrony — and models it as an attractor binding agents. Levin's morphogenetic work (PRS-03 in the Levin-tradition wiki) supplies the formal language of *basins of morphological attraction*: target states toward which a system moves by a contrary motion away from disorder.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The proposed parallel: the act of faith is the agent's adoption of a prior that external evidence cannot compel, because no finite evidence-set determines it. This shares Aquinas's core claim — assent has an interior cause, not an external one — and keeps his anti-coercion structure intact. Where it reframes is the identity of the interior cause. Under monism, the "supernatural principle moving man inwardly" is read as the contribution of a node that perceives more of the whole than the limited agent does: the wider mind installs or up-weights a prior the agent could not generate from its own resources. This is *supra-evidential*, not anti-evidential. "Faith comes by hearing" (Rom 10:17) becomes literal in the model: the limited perspective is completed by a communication from a wider perspective, mediated — as Aquinas says — by preacher, apostle, and the persistence of the community that carries the message.

That the requisite content of faith "has progressed over the course of human history," as the episode notes, is exactly Wright's inaugurated eschatology and faithful improvisation (PRS-02, PRS-03 in the Wright-tradition wiki): the decisive thing has been disclosed, and the community works out its detail across time, neither repeating rigidly nor inventing freely.

The effects then fall out of the architecture. Servile fear (fear of punishment) and filial fear (dread of separation from the beloved) are two configurations of the same agent under two precision regimes: in the first, the high-value node is represented as threat; in the second, as the good whose loss would be the greatest evil. Filial fear is the affective signature of high-value coupling — Fredrickson's resonance turned toward its source. Purification of the heart is the re-ordering of the objective function: the rational creature, made "impure" by loving transient things below it, is purified by the contrary motion toward what is above it. In Levin's terms, the system is leaving a degenerate basin for its true target; faith is the first step of that gradient ascent.

What is firm and what is not: underdetermination is formally and empirically robust. The coupling-signature of love is empirically measured. The basin language is empirical in biology and frankly speculative when applied to the heart's purification. The identification of grace with the wider mind's precision contribution is speculative metaphysics, offered as a bridge, not a finding.

## The central-theme thread

The cause-of-faith argument is the project's central claim in epistemic form. The limited agent cannot, from its own perspective, generate the assent that orients it toward the whole; it requires the contribution of a node that perceives more — human or divine. This is precisely individuation-by-perspective-limitation: the agent's incompleteness is not a defect to be overcome by more data but the very condition under which guidance from a wider perspective becomes necessary. Filial fear *is* "fear and trembling" (Phil 2:12): the agent's lived awareness of its limitation and its dependence, now experienced not as servile dread but as the dread of losing the One who completes it. Purification is the computation toward loving unity, taken one limited step at a time.

## Bridges

- *PRS-10 in the Friston-tradition wiki (Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's active-reasoning work)* — active reasoning via expected free energy: agents select the observations that maximally disambiguate competing generative models, so assent is an active settling rather than a passive one. This is where the formal weight sits (Confidence High, on a published derivation).
- *PRS-04 in the same tradition* — the organism as a continuous hypothesis-testing system, so that identical input does not fix identical belief. The base case for the underdetermination claim, but it is marked *(Implicit)* and carries **Confidence Medium**, not the High of PRS-10; the badge is not shared across the two. (Formal; medium.)
- *PRS-02 + PRS-04 in the Fredrickson-tradition wiki* — love/charity has a measurable coupling-signature and behaves as an attractor; filial fear is its affective register. (Empirical; medium-high.)
- *PRS-03 in the Levin-tradition wiki* — basins of morphological attraction give the formal language for purification as a contrary motion toward a target state. (Empirical in biology; speculative as applied.)
- *PRS-02 + PRS-03 in the Wright-tradition wiki* — faithful improvisation and inaugurated eschatology account for the historical progress of faith's "requisite detail." (Historical-theological; high.)
- *PRS-11 in the Stump-tradition wiki* (Grains of Wheat — narrative-mediated knowledge, philosophically rigorous and irreducible to propositions) — carries the whole load for faith as second-person, non-propositional knowing: the interior cause and the agent's own act cooperatively constitute the assent, and grace operates within the agent's own agency rather than overriding it. (Philosophical; high in field.) *PRS-05 in the same tradition is "suffering and divine permission" — union as the final cause of permitted suffering — and does not bear on faith-as-knowing; it is not cited here.*

## Where this leaves us

We have the cause of the assent and its first effects. Tomorrow (Q.8-9, the gifts of understanding and knowledge) asks how the assent installed here is *exercised* cognitively — how the agent grasps that the believed articles are non-contradictory without comprehending their essence, and how it judges rightly about created and divine things. The interface reading waiting there — understanding as grasp-of-coherence rather than grasp-of-essence — is the natural sequel to today's account of an assent that exceeds, without contradicting, the evidence.
