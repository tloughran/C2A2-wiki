# Day 65 — Contemporary Parallel: *The Structure of the Will and What Moves It*

Day 64 established the structural-and-diagnostic apparatus of voluntary action and the load-bearing case of willed ignorance. Day 65 continues the formal analysis: Q.8 (the will in regard to what it wills — 3 articles) and Q.9 (what moves the will — 6 articles). The contemporary mind-monist frame engages this as the *causal architecture of policy-selection*, with the medieval *will is of good only* and *will is moved by intellect, sense appetite, itself, and God* mapping onto the contemporary multi-source-prior frame of active inference.

## Frame

Q.8 a.1: the will is of good only — even when we choose moral or physical evils, we will them not as evils-in-themselves but under some *aspect of good* (a perceived good, an annexed good, a means to a good). This is the formal-versus-material distinction at the will-level: formally the will is always toward the good; materially the will can mistake what is good.

Q.8 a.2: the will is of the end *and* the means. Both fall under the *aspect good* — one as good-in-itself (end), the other as good-as-conducive-to-end (means).

Q.8 a.3: the will is moved by *the same act* to the end and to the means *when* the means is willed precisely *because* of the end (the end is the formal cause of willing the means). Otherwise distinct acts.

Q.9 covers movers of the will, and the order of its six articles is itself an argument — it walks outward from the nearest mover to the most remote: a.1 the *intellect* (nothing can be willed unless it is first apprehended as good); a.2 the *sensitive appetite* (passion moves the will by altering how the good appears); a.3 the will *moves itself* (in willing the means by virtue of willing the end); a.4 an *exterior principle*; a.5 a *heavenly body* — answered in the negative; and a.6 *God alone as exterior principle*, moving the will naturally and not violently.

Article 5 deserves marking rather than quiet omission, because it is the article of this question that most resists carrying over. Aquinas asks whether the sun and moon move the human will and answers no: anything in the physical environment can affect the spiritual faculties only indirectly, by presenting itself to the senses or by impeding the body's use, since the will is not housed in a bodily organ though in this life it depends on the body to act. The astronomical framing is dead; the *structure* of the answer is not, and it is the structure the contemporary frame inherits — a principled distinction between what modulates the conditions of apprehension and what moves the will as such.

## Problem

The problem (P): given that the alter's will is constitutively directed toward the universal good, and given that the alter's perception of particular goods is filtered through interface-and-passion-and-habit, what is the *causal structure* of the alter's actual policy-selection in any given case? Multiple sources contribute — apprehension, passion, the will's own self-movement, exterior bodies, God — and the alter's task is to coordinate these so that the resulting policy actually aligns with the substrate-source's loving unity.

## Resource

**Friston on expected free energy and means-end policy.** The register's load-bearing record here is PRS-16 in the Friston-tradition wiki (`wiki/traditions/friston/prs_triplets.md`, *expected free energy as unified mathematics of risk + ambiguity*, Confidence: High): risk and ambiguity collapse into one optimization target, so that deciding *what to do* and deciding *what to find out* are the same computation. PRS-15 (*active inference as formal validation of Boyd's full OODA loop*, High) supplies the standing generative model — Boyd's "orientation" — as the thing that both drives action and is updated by it. The medieval *will is of end and means under one aspect-good* preserves as the single expected-free-energy target under which the means-policy is selected because of the end-policy. The further claim this commentary leans on — a *hierarchy* of terminal versus instrumental priors — is not stated in the register; the nearest record, PRS-04 (*organism as active modeler*), is marked (Implicit) at Medium confidence. The hierarchy is therefore the commentary's extension, offered as speculative, not as mature formalism.

**McGilchrist on hemispheric coordination in willed action.** PRS-07 in the McGilchrist-tradition wiki (`wiki/traditions/mcgilchrist/prs_triplets.md`, *division and union as creative synthesis*, High) gives the coordination itself: union at a higher level, not the averaging of two contributions. The right hemisphere's openness to the gestalt-of-the-good and the left hemisphere's analytical detail-work coordinate in willed action, and the medieval *will-moves-itself* (Q.9 a.3) preserves as the alter's self-directing capacity to bring its own attention and analysis into that union. For Q.9 a.2 — passion moving the will by altering how the good *appears* — the relevant record is PRS-05 (*attention as ontological act*, Implicit, Medium), whose own solution line ties the mode of attending to Friston's precision-weighting. That is the mechanism the medieval article needs: the passions do not overrule the will, they reweight the evidence on which apprehension runs.

**Stump on cooperative causality in willed action.** PRS-09 in the Stump-tradition wiki (`wiki/traditions/stump/prs_triplets.md`, *virtue as cooperative causality*, Medium) is the record that bears on this day: grace perfects the soul's natural powers so that the agent wills and acts virtuously *in a way that is genuinely the agent's own*. That is the Q.9 a.6 claim — God moves the will naturally and not violently — stated in a contemporary Thomistic register, and it is where Stump belongs here, as interlocutor receiving the reframe rather than as its metaphysical author. Two honest gaps: the register holds **no** will-as-faculty-of-the-good entry, so that phrase is used descriptively with no id asserted; and the second-personal-knowing claim is left deliberately unanchored, because the bridges file's pointer for it is a known upstream conflict and attributing by proximity is exactly the trap.

**Hawkins on cortical architecture and end-and-means.** PRS-01 in the Hawkins-tradition wiki (`wiki/traditions/hawkins/prs_triplets.md`, *intelligence without true understanding* — HTM and the Thousand Brains Theory, High) supplies the architecture. But the register's newer PRS-20 (*hierarchy encodes composition, not abstraction*, High, added 2026-08-09) rules out the picture a goal-representation reading would reach for first: goals do not sit as abstractions in high-level columns that then dictate to lower ones. Every region models whole objects; higher regions receive direct sensory and motor input and are not downstream-only; what hierarchy does is let a higher model take the *identity* of a lower model as a feature located at a point. That is a closer fit to Q.8 a.3 than the abstraction picture was: the end-model does not command the means-model from above, it *contains* the means-model as a constituent, which is why one act of willing covers both when the means is willed under the end's formal aspect.

**Levin on multi-scale agency and will-analog at biological scales.** PRS-10 in the Levin-tradition wiki (`wiki/traditions/levin/prs_triplets.md`, *continuity of human and unicellular goal-directedness*, High) states the continuity thesis directly — human goal-directedness differs from unicellular goal-directedness in degree of represented-goal complexity, memory, and interaction protocol, not in kind — with PRS-09 (*principled framework for cognitive attribution across scales*, High) supplying the falsifiable criteria for when the attribution is earned. (Not PRS-04, the register's *cognition substrate* entry, which is Implicit/Medium and functions as a catch-all.) The will-as-faculty preserves cleanly because the directedness-toward-integration is multi-scale; the alter's will is the personal-scale instance of that dynamics.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary mind-monist read of Q.8: the alter's policy-selection is constitutively directed toward what it apprehends as good (terminal priors set the orientation); means and end are integrated under the hierarchical-prior frame; the same policy-selection-act covers both when the means is willed under the end's formal aspect.

The contemporary mind-monist read of Q.9: policy-selection has multiple causal contributors — (a) the intellect's apprehension shapes the priors; (b) the alter's own self-movement constitutes the will's directing-itself toward what is not-yet-willed; (c) the sensitive appetite (passion) modulates the apprehension; (d) exterior bodies contribute through sense; (e) the substrate-source's primary causal sustaining of the alter's full causal-structure is constitutive. The medieval claim that *God moves the will without removing voluntariness* preserves under the substrate-source-as-primary-cause-of-secondary-causes frame from Day 59.

What this *shares with Aquinas*: the formal universality of the will's directedness; the multi-source causal structure; the primary/secondary cause distinction.

What it *reframes*: where Aquinas's frame is faculty-psychological-and-Aristotelian, the contemporary frame uses hierarchical active inference, hemispheric coordination, HTM, and multi-scale agency.

What is *firm vs. speculative*: the anchored records carry their own grades and they are not uniform — Friston PRS-15/16, Hawkins PRS-01/20, Levin PRS-09/10, McGilchrist PRS-07 and Wright PRS-35 are High; McGilchrist PRS-05, Stump PRS-09 and Rohr PRS-20 are Medium or Medium-High; the hierarchical terminal-versus-instrumental prior structure is the commentary's own extension and is speculative. The substrate-source-as-primary-cause-of-will is metaphysically continuous with mind-monism but is not itself a record in the Kastrup register, and receives different treatments under Kastrup versus theistic readings.

## The central-theme thread

Today's day reinforces the central-theme commitment that the alter's will is *constitutively perspective-limited but formally directed toward the universal good*. The will is not free in the sense of being unmoored; it is constitutively directed. But its perception of what *is* the universal good in any given particular case is filtered through interface, passion, habit, and the substrate-network's pedagogical care or its inverse.

*Salvation in fear and trembling* under this register is the alter's daily-task work of *coordinating its multiple movement-sources toward the substrate-source's loving unity*. The alter cannot will arbitrarily; it is constitutively oriented. The work is to recognize the formal directedness, to refine the apprehension under second-person care, to manage the passions toward right-perception, to receive the substrate-network's pedagogy, and so to align the multi-source policy-selection with the substrate-source's own integrative dynamics.

There is a coincidence here worth naming, because it is not a coincidence the commentary should let pass. The verse Aquinas puts at the head of Q.9 a.6 — the article establishing that God moves the will *naturally and not violently* — is Philippians 2:13: God at work in you both to will and to work. That is the verse immediately following the one this whole project takes as its central-theme anchor, Philippians 2:12's working out of salvation in fear and trembling. Paul writes them as a single thought, and Aquinas is reading the back half of it to answer precisely the question of how the will can be moved by God and still be the agent's own.

This is the tightest scriptural fit the central theme has yet had with the article under discussion. On Wright's reading, the two verses together are not a paradox to be managed but the ordinary shape of covenant participation: the agent's working and God's working are not competing quantities in a fixed budget, so that more of one means less of the other. The perspective-limited alter's genuine agency and the substrate-source's primary causation are the same act described from inside and from outside — which is exactly what the mind-monist frame needs to say, and here it is said scripturally before it is said metaphysically. Rohr PRS-20 gives the contemplative stance as the alter's deliberate alignment of multi-source movement under that primary causation — the stabilized position from which action stops being reactive; Stump PRS-09 gives the cooperative-causality structure in which the perfected act remains the agent's own.
