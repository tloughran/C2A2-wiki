# Day 243 — Passion & Sacrament (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.60-62) holds that the sacraments derive their power from Christ's Passion: the historical event works through later sensible signs as through instruments. The medieval puzzle is causation across time and through matter — how a past event empowers present rituals, and why God uses sensible things at all. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: how does a determinate *form* (the pattern of a once-occurred event) propagate its causal efficacy across substrate and time, and why must the channel be sensible?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how is causal power carried by form across material and temporal gaps?** Aquinas needs the Passion to be efficacious "now" without being repeated; the sign must be an *instrument* transmitting a power it does not originate. The contemporary frame meets the same problem wherever an outcome is governed by stored or distal pattern rather than by local push-contact.

## Resource

Levin is the leading resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's program, PRS-01 (morphogenetic control) and PRS-08 (cells as cognitive agents navigating an anatomical morphospace) describe how *form* — a target pattern, not a substance — exercises real causal influence over a system, with information carried across substrate boundaries and persisting through total material turnover. This naturalizes the structure Aquinas needs: a pattern (the Passion's saving form) can be the operative cause through later instruments without being materially "in" them.

PRS-07 (FEP substrate-independence) in Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program reinforces that the relevant causal regularities are formal — definable independently of any particular material realizer — which is exactly the property an instrumental sacramental causality requires. PRS-03 (perception fitness-tracking) in Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman's program answers the "why sensible?" question: limited agents have no direct access to the deep order; sensible signs are the only available interface through which the pattern can be presented and engaged.

Rohr supplies the theological ontology: if the Universal Christ is already the pattern indwelling matter, sensible signs are not arbitrary — matter is natively capable of bearing the saving form.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the Passion's efficacy reaches the present as form-borne causation — a determinate saving pattern propagated through sensible instruments that limited agents can engage because the interface is the only access they have.** This *shares* with Aquinas the instrumental realism (the sign truly transmits, does not merely remind). It *reframes* instrumentality as information/form-propagation rather than Aristotelian moved-mover mechanics. It *marks* the limit: Levin's form-causation is empirical in biology, and its transfer to a singular salvific event is analogical, not demonstrative; the claim that the Passion *is* such a form is theological (Wright/Rohr ground truth).

## The central-theme thread

Why sensible signs? Because agents are individuated by perspective-limitation and perceive only the interface (Hoffman). The sacraments meet agents *at the limit of their perception*, letting them engage a saving pattern they cannot directly see. The whole condescends to the resource-bounded perspective — which is the project's recurring shape: loving unity is computed toward with the resources we perceive, and grace supplies a sensible foothold for that computation.

## Bridges

- **Levin PRS-01 + PRS-08 (morphogenetic control; agents in morphospace)** — *empirical-in-domain, analogical transfer.* Form as real cause carried across substrate and time.
- **Friston PRS-07 (FEP substrate-independence)** — *formal.* The operative regularities are substrate-independent, as instrumental causality requires.
- **Hoffman PRS-03 (perception fitness-tracking)** — *formal-speculative.* Why the channel must be sensible: the interface is the only access limited agents have.
- **Rohr (Universal Christ in matter)** — *theological / contemplative.* Matter natively able to bear the saving form. Primary on sacraments.
- **Wright (the Passion as historical, world-changing event)** — *theological, high within method.* Guards the realism of the once-for-all event the signs transmit.
