# Day 286 — More Impediments (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.57–58 catalogue the remaining diriment impediments — among them solemn **vows** (a prior total self-gift to God), **abduction** (raptus, which removes the freedom and safety consent requires), the impediment of **crime**, and related bars. What unites this miscellany is structural: each names a *prior commitment or condition* that makes a valid new coupling impossible — the candidate is already bound, or is not free, or has acted in a way that disqualifies. The contemporary version: **what are the general boundary-conditions under which two agents *cannot* form a new coupling — when does a prior binding, or a defect in freedom, preclude a new union from being constituted at all?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is the **preconditions of coupling**. Marriage (Days 279–281) is constituted by a free, expressed, mutual act; the impediments of Q.57–58 are the systematic ways those preconditions fail. A solemn vow means the self is *already fully committed* (no remaining capacity to give); abduction means the freedom-condition fails; crime means a disqualifying prior act. The contemporary sciences of coupled agency face the same: two systems cannot form a new coherent unit if one is already saturated in a binding coupling, or if the coupling attempt bypasses free policy selection, or if a prior action has destroyed the trust the mutual model requires. The impediments are a taxonomy of failed preconditions.

## Resource

The Friston-tradition program supplies the leading frame. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work (PRS-06, *multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling*) makes valid coupling depend on two free agents each able to model and be modeled by the other; PRS-18 (*widening a community*) makes admission to a shared model depend on coherence-preserving conditions. Read onto Q.57–58: a solemn vow is *prior full coupling* — the agent's modeling capacity is already wholly committed, so no further constitutive self-gift is available (this is the structural meaning of "a vow of continence voids a subsequent marriage"). Abduction is the *freedom-condition failure* from Day 281 — a clamp, not a selected policy. The crime impediment is *trust-precondition failure* — a prior act (e.g., machination against a spouse) that corrupts the mutual model before it forms. The Levin-tradition program supplies the licensing precedent: PRS-08 (*cells as cognitive agents in morphospace*) shows that the whole *licenses* members into roles and that there are real conditions under which a role cannot be taken — the biological analogue of "diriment impediment." Kastrup's PRS-23 (*how individuals separate and join*) gives the metaphysics of why a prior total commitment forecloses a new boundary-relaxation: an alter already wholly joined elsewhere has no free boundary to relax. Rohr's PRS-7 (*relationship prior*) keeps the analysis from treating the bonds as mere contracts.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the further impediments are a taxonomy of failed coupling-preconditions — prior saturation (vow), freedom-failure (abduction), and trust-failure (crime) — each of which makes a new constitutive act impossible rather than merely illicit.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: the classification of these as *diriment* (voiding, not merely forbidding) and the recognition that they touch the very possibility of the bond. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas lists them under diverse natural and ecclesiastical rationales; the monist reframe unifies them as three structural ways the preconditions of coupling fail, which clarifies *why* each voids rather than merely prohibits. Where evidence is *firm*: the dependence of coupling on freedom, available capacity, and mutual-modeling trust is well-defined in the formal frameworks. Where *speculative*: the mapping of specific canonical impediments onto the three failure-types is interpretive, and several impediments are frankly historical-ecclesiastical conventions whose force does not follow from the formal account — that should be marked, not unified away.

## The central-theme thread

The project's loving unity is built only from free, available, trusting perspectives freely electing union. The impediments name the conditions under which that election cannot occur — and so they protect the seriousness of the central theme's central act. A union extracted from an unfree, already-bound, or betrayed agent would be a counterfeit of the very unity the project seeks: configuration without the self-giving computation that alone makes two into a real one.

## Bridges

- **Friston PRS-06 / PRS-18 (formal; juridical mapping speculative):** valid coupling needs free, available, mutually modeling agents — the structural reading of vow / abduction / crime impediments.
- **Levin PRS-08 (empirical at tissue scale):** the whole licenses roles and there are real bars — the biological analogue of a diriment impediment.
- **Kastrup PRS-23 (metaphysical, speculative):** an already-fully-joined boundary has none free to relax — why a prior total vow forecloses a new union.
- **Rohr PRS-7 (theological):** the bonds are constitutive relations, not contracts.
