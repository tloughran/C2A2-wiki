# Day 278 — Marriage (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

The treatise on Matrimony opens (Q.41–42) by asking what marriage *is*: whether it is an office of nature, whether it falls under the natural law, whether it is a sacrament, and how the sacramental marriage relates to the natural institution. Aquinas's answer is layered — marriage is rooted in nature (ordered to offspring and to the partnership of life), elevated by Christ into a sacrament, and in either register it is fundamentally a *joining*: two become, in the biblical phrase, one flesh. The contemporary version of the question is the one mind/conscious-realist monism finds most congenial of the entire Supplement: **can two distinct conscious agents form a genuine higher-order individual — a real unity that is more than the sum of two cooperating parties — and if so, what is the nature of the bond that constitutes it?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is the **ontology of union**: is a "one flesh" a real entity, or a manner of speaking about two entities who coordinate? Aquinas clearly wants the former — the marriage bond is a real relation with real effects, not a contractual fiction — but the metaphysics available to him locates reality primarily in *substances* (individual persons), which makes any genuine two-in-one ontologically awkward. Exactly the same problem recurs across the contemporary sciences of life and mind: a multicellular organism is a real individual composed of cells that were once, evolutionarily, free-living individuals; a colony, a holobiont, a coupled pair of brains in joint action — each presses the question of whether the higher unity is a real agent or only an aggregate. The marriage question is the human, covenantal instance of biology's deepest open problem: **the reality and emergence of higher-order individuality.**

## Resource

The Levin-tradition program is the leading resource, and on this locus the project's mind-first frame and the topic coincide. Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work (PRS-32 in the Levin-tradition wiki, *how goal-directed cognition scales up from individual cells to integrated organisms*; PRS-09, *a principled framework for cognitive attribution across scales*; PRS-10, *continuity of goal-directedness*) is precisely a research program on how lower individuals become a higher individual with its own goals, its own competencies, its own coherent agency. Levin's central empirical finding — that cells coordinate via a shared bioelectric "cognitive glue" into a collective that pursues a target morphology no cell represents alone — is the best available naturalistic model of *two-or-more becoming a real one*. The Friston-tradition program supplies the dynamics of the dyad specifically: PRS-06 (*multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling*) and PRS-05 (*distributed cognition across scales*) model a couple as two generative models that come to share a Markov blanket, so that the pair minimizes free energy *as a unit*. Theologically, the Rohr-tradition program goes furthest: PRS-7 in the Rohr-tradition wiki (*is relationship a derived feature of already-existing individuals, or is it ontologically prior?*) directly answers the medieval substance-bias — relationship is not built on top of finished individuals but is constitutive of them. Wright's program grounds the scriptural register: the Pauline reading of marriage (Eph 5) as a "great mystery" signifying Christ and the Church places the one-flesh union inside covenant theology, not mere contract.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **marriage is the covenantal, sacramental case of a higher-order individual genuinely emerging from two — and the bond Aquinas calls real is real in exactly the way that an organism is more than its cells, or a coupled pair of agents is more than two coordinating agents.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: the realism about the bond, the rootedness in nature, and the layering of a sacramental meaning onto a natural institution. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas's substance-metaphysics makes the two-in-one a puzzle to be managed; the monist reframe — relationship-as-ontologically-prior (Rohr PRS-7) plus multiscale-individuality (Levin) — makes the higher individual *expected*, the natural next rung on a ladder that runs from cells to organisms to societies. The one-flesh union stops being a metaphor straining against the metaphysics and becomes a clean instance of a general principle. Where evidence is *firm*: multiscale individuality, with lower agents composing higher agents that have their own goals, is well-attested across biology. Where *speculative*: that two adult human persons form a higher individual in the *same robust sense* as cells forming an organism is an analogy whose disanalogies (persons retain full agency and can dissolve the union; cells largely cannot) must be marked, not smuggled past.

## The central-theme thread

This article sits at the heart of the project's claim. Conscious agents are individuated by the limits of their perspective; loving unity is the goal toward which limited agents compute. Marriage is the most concrete human school of exactly that computation: two perspectives, each radically partial, undertake to form a single life in which each sees through the other what neither sees alone. The "one flesh" is not the abolition of the two perspectives but their union *without erasure* — which is precisely the project's eschatological picture of the many limited perspectives contained in the one whole without being dissolved into it. Marriage is the central theme rendered at the scale of two.

## Bridges

- **Levin PRS-32 / PRS-09 / PRS-10 (empirical at biological scales, speculative for human dyads):** the science of how lower individuals become a real higher individual; the strongest naturalistic model of "one flesh."
- **Friston PRS-06 / PRS-05 (formal / speculative):** the couple as two generative models sharing a Markov blanket, minimizing free energy as a unit — the dynamics of union.
- **Rohr PRS-7 (theological):** relationship is ontologically prior to the related individuals — the direct answer to substance-bias.
- **Wright (scriptural, theological):** Eph 5 places one-flesh inside covenant and the Christ–Church mystery, not contract.
- **Kastrup PRS-02 / PRS-25 (metaphysical, speculative):** individuation and the joining of dissociated alters within one mind — a monist substrate in which union of perspectives is natural.

## Where this leaves us

Q.43–44 (tomorrow) move from *what marriage is* to *how it is formally constituted* — betrothal, espousals, and the definition of the bond — which presses the next question: if the union is a real higher individual, what *act* brings it into being? The thread to carry: the disanalogy between freely dissoluble human unions and obligate biological individuality is not a defect in the parallel but the very place where consent, covenant, and the central theme's "fear and trembling" enter.
