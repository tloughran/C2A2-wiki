# Day 251 — Accidents Remaining (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.77) takes up the hardest Aristotelian residue of the Eucharistic doctrine: after transubstantiation, the *accidents* — the appearance, taste, dimension, weight of bread and wine — remain, but they remain *without a subject*, since the substance they once inhered in has been wholly converted. For an Aristotelian this is nearly a contradiction in terms: accidents are by definition what exists *in* a substance. Aquinas's solution is technical — quantity (dimensive extension) is sustained directly by divine power and itself serves as the quasi-subject for the other accidents. The contemporary version of the question under mind / conscious-realist monism: what is it for sensible appearances to persist when there is no underlying material thing for them to be appearances *of*?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **can appearances exist with no material substrate carrying them?** On the naive metaphysics in which the appearance is grounded in, and resembles, an underlying material object, the answer is no — pull the object and the appearance must collapse. Aquinas keeps the appearances and pays the price of a metaphysical exception. The contemporary question is whether appearances were ever the kind of thing that *needed* a material substrate in the first place.

## Resource

This is the locus where Kastrup's move is decisive. In Tom's PRS-form record of Kastrup's program, PRS-02 (identity within monism) makes the reality of any thing a configuration *in mind*, not a parcel of mind-independent matter. On that ontology, the "accidents" — what shows up to a perceiver — are perceptual states, excitations of mind-at-large as registered by a localized perspective. They never required a material substrate, because matter was never the substrate of appearance; mind was. "Accidents remaining without a subject" stops being an exception and becomes the ordinary case stated plainly: appearance is a mental state, and the material "subject" the Aristotelian sought was a posit that did no real work.

Hoffman supplies the parallel from the perceptual side. In Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman's program, PRS-03 (perception is fitness-tracking, not truth-tracking) and PRS-04 (observer-dependent spacetime) give an argued evolutionary basis for the claim that the perceptual icon is systematically decoupled from whatever it tracks. The bread-icon can persist as a stable interface element while the reality it once indexed is gone or changed — exactly the structure of accidents persisting without their former substance.

Levin gives the empirical hinge from biology. In Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's program, PRS-03 (a formal language for target morphology) plus the demonstrated persistence of form through total material turnover shows, in living systems, a pattern that floats free of the specific matter realizing it. Appearance and organization need not be welded to a fixed substrate.

Rohr (Universal Christ in matter) and Wright (bodily, new-creation realism) remain the theological ground truth for the *content*: the reality now present beneath the conserved appearances.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the accidents remain without a subject because appearances never needed a material substrate to begin with — they are perceptual states in mind, and "the subject" was a redundant posit of the older physics.** This *shares* with Aquinas the insistence that appearance persists while underlying reality is converted, and shares his refusal to let the appearance dictate the reality. It *reframes* his hardest move: where Aquinas must invoke divine power to sustain accidents in the *absence* of their natural subject, the monist denies that the natural subject was ever doing the sustaining — mind sustains appearance always, here as everywhere. It *firms up* one premise Aquinas could only assert: that appearance can be decoupled from underlying reality is, in Hoffman's program, an argued evolutionary result. It *marks* honestly that Kastrup's idealism is contested, that this renders the doctrine legible rather than proven, and that the *content* (what the conserved appearances now veil) is theological, not derivable from the metaphysics.

## The central-theme thread

The motif of the project surfaces precisely at the seam. A perspective-limited agent perceives only the icon — the remaining accidents — and the limitation is not lifted: the appearances persist exactly as before. Yet the whole is fully present beneath them. The persistence of the accidents *is* the persistence of the agent's finitude; the conversion of the substance *is* the whole offering itself within, and not despite, that finitude. To receive under the conserved appearances is to be included in a reality one's perspective cannot see — "salvation as inclusion in the people united with God" (Wright) enacted under the very limits that individuate the perceiver.

## Bridges

- **Kastrup PRS-02 (substance-as-mental-reality)** — *metaphysical, contested.* Appearances are mental states; the material substrate was never required.
- **Hoffman PRS-03 + PRS-04 (interface theory; FBT)** — *formal with an argued evolutionary basis.* The icon persists while its referent is converted.
- **Levin PRS-03 (target morphology; form through turnover)** — *empirical-in-domain.* Pattern floats free of fixed matter.
- **Rohr (Universal Christ in matter)** — *theological / contemplative.* The reality veiled by conserved appearance.
- **Stump (Thomistic interlocutor)** — receives the monist reframe of "accidents without a subject" in place of the divine-power-sustains-quantity solution.
