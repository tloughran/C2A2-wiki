# Day 128 — Contemporary Parallel: The Two Failures of the Goal-Prior

## Frame

Aquinas treats the two vices opposed to hope. Despair (Q.20): it is a sin (a.1), can exist without unbelief (a.2), is in a real sense more dangerous than unbelief or hatred of God (a.3), and arises chiefly from sloth and lust (a.4). Presumption (Q.21): it trusts inordinately either in one's own power or in the divine mercy (a.1), is a sin (a.2), is more directly opposed to fear than to hope (a.3), and arises from pride and vainglory (a.4). His definition of presumption is exact: "an inordinate trust in the divine mercy or power, consisting in the hope of obtaining glory without merits, or pardon without repentance."

The contemporary version is the natural completion of the last two days. If hope is the goal-prior and fear is the precision on divergence, then despair and presumption are the two ways that control system breaks — and mind / conscious-realist monism describes both with unusual precision.

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: *what are the characteristic pathologies of an agent governed by a goal-prior and an error signal, and why are they so resistant to repair?* Aquinas's pairing is not accidental. Despair and presumption are not random vices; they are the symmetric distortions of one rightly-ordered movement. And both have the disturbing property of being self-stabilizing — the despairing agent will not reach for help it has judged useless, and the presumptuous agent will not heed correction it has judged unnecessary. Why would the two opposite errors share that immunity to update?

## Resource

The leading resource is Friston's program, and in particular Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's clinical work (PRS-14, symptoms as Bayesian self-trapping: locally-optimal but globally-suboptimal predictive states that resist revision because *updating itself raises short-term free energy*). This is the mechanism behind both vices.

Despair is the collapse of the goal-prior (PRS-03). The agent ceases to weight the preferred state — loving union with the whole — as reachable, and so stops acting toward it. Crucially, this is self-stabilizing in exactly the PRS-14 way: once the target is judged unreachable, the evidence that would revise that judgment is precisely the evidence the agent no longer seeks, because seeking it would mean re-opening a painful gap. This is why Aquinas can rank despair, in its genus, as more dangerous than unbelief (a.3): it removes the very restraint — hope — that draws the agent toward the good and holds it back from evil. Day 125 already named this: despair removes hope as a restraint, and "with no holds barred" the agent plunges. In control terms, kill the goal-prior and there is no longer a target against which any divergence registers as something to correct. The embodied etiology (a.4 — despair from sloth and lust) fits the affective-gating pattern this volume keeps surfacing: a sluggish or appetite-captured system down-weights the spiritual good until it no longer reads as attainable (Fredrickson on the absence of upward spirals, PRS-01; McGilchrist on narrowed attention, by theme).

Presumption is the mirror image: not the collapse of the goal-prior but the *pathological down-weighting of the divergence signal* (PRS-16, mis-set precision). The presumptuous agent treats the target as already secured — "glory without merits, pardon without repentance" — and so mutes the error signal that would tell it how far off it is. This is why Aquinas says presumption is opposed more directly to *fear* than to hope (a.3): fear is the precision on divergence, and presumption is precisely the failure to weight divergence at all. Where despair is the agent that stops seeking because it judges the target unreachable, presumption is the agent that stops correcting because it judges itself already arrived. Both are forms of a frozen model; both refuse the update; both are, in PRS-14 terms, locally comfortable and globally lost. And a.4's diagnosis — presumption from pride — is the same point in the register of the self-model: the agent overweights its own state-estimate against the incoming evidence.

Wright and Rohr give the two failures their temporal shape. Wright's already/not-yet (PRS-03) is the structure both vices reject: despair denies the *already* (the down-payment, the reachability), presumption denies the *not-yet* (the distance still to be crossed). Rohr's order-disorder-reorder (PRS-02) names the passage both refuse: despair refuses to believe the reorder is coming and stays in disorder; presumption skips the disorder and claims a reorder it has not undergone. In each case the agent has stepped out of the live, in-progress computation into a frozen verdict.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The proposed parallel reads despair and presumption as the two failure modes of a single control system: despair is the collapse of the goal-prior (the target abandoned as unreachable), presumption is the pathological down-weighting of the divergence signal (the target falsely judged already held). This shares Aquinas's structure exactly — the two are symmetric distortions of one rightly-ordered hope, despair opposed to hope's reaching and presumption to fear's weighting — and it explains his otherwise surprising ranking of despair's danger (remove the goal-prior and nothing downstream can be corrected) and his pairing of presumption with fear rather than hope.

Where it reframes: Aquinas treats the two as distinct vices with distinct objects. The parallel reads them as two settings of one mechanism gone wrong — under-confidence and over-confidence in the reachable good — which makes vivid both their symmetry and their shared immunity to repair (PRS-14: each is a locally-optimal attractor that resists the update which would dislodge it). The structural lesson is that rightly-ordered hope is a *calibration*, not a quantity: not "as much confidence as possible" but confidence correctly weighted against honest divergence — the exact midpoint the last two days described.

Evidence strength. The self-trapping account of update-resistant states is formally strong and clinically supported (Friston PRS-14/PRS-16: high as model, medium-high clinically). Despair's special danger is theological (high in field) and coheres tightly with Day 125. The embodied etiology is empirically plausible (medium). The reading of the two vices as symmetric mis-calibrations of one control system is offered as illumination — structurally firm, theologically speculative.

## The central-theme thread

Both vices end the computation toward loving unity from the inside, and in opposite directions. Despair abandons the whole as unreachable and stops seeking; presumption pretends the whole is already held and stops correcting. Each is a refusal of the central theme's posture — that loving unity is a real but not-yet-possessed target, sought under acknowledged limitation, with dependence on those who perceive more of the whole. The despairing agent denies the *reachable*; the presumptuous agent denies the *not-yet* and the *dependence*. Between them they mark the two cliffs on either side of the narrow road that hope and fear, together, keep an agent on: confident enough to keep moving, honest enough to keep correcting, dependent enough to keep leaning on what sees more.

## Where this leaves us

The treatise on hope closes on its two failures — and on the recognition that rightly-ordered hope is a calibration held between them. Tomorrow (Q.22-23) Aquinas gives the precepts of hope and fear and then opens the great treatise on charity, defining it as *friendship with God* and naming it the form of all the virtues. The synthesis turns from the goal-prior and its guardian signal to the bond that is their proper end: charity as the coupling by which limited agents are bound into the loving whole the whole project has been computing toward.
