# Day 125 — Contemporary Parallel: The Sin That Disables Its Own Correction

*Locus note: the episode narration says "questions 12 and 13," but Q.12-13 were yesterday; the content here is unmistakably II-II Q.14 (blasphemy against the Holy Spirit) and Q.15 (vices opposed to knowledge and understanding). The synthesis follows the content.*

## Frame

The sin against the Holy Spirit (Q.14) is the one Scripture calls unforgivable, and Aquinas, following Augustine and the Church, reads it not as a magnitude of offense God cannot match but as *final impenitence* — the standing refusal of the very mercy by which sins are forgiven. His a.1 gives the mechanism: this is sin "through certain malice," which works by "removing the things that prevent the choosing of evil — hope removed by despair, fear by presumption, and so on." Since those restraints are effects of the Holy Spirit, attacking them attacks their font. Q.15 then treats the cognitive damage: blindness of mind (loss of the supernatural light) and dullness of sense (weakened acuity toward spiritual goods), with Gregory's etiology that blindness flows from lust and dullness from gluttony.

The contemporary version is precise and, of all the articles in this stretch, the most striking: what happens to an agent that disables its own error-correction? Mind / conscious-realist monism has a clean model of exactly that pathology.

## Problem

Every other sin in this volume leaves the corrective machinery intact — the agent could still be moved by hope, restrained by fear, instructed by truth. The sin against the Holy Spirit is different in kind because it *targets the machinery itself*. Despair removes hope (the expectation that the target is reachable); presumption removes fear (the signal that one is off-target); obstinacy and resisting-known-truth remove docility (the willingness to update on a better-informed source). The inquiry-problem: *what is the status of a system that, rather than computing badly, dismantles the components by which it could compute better?* And why would the resulting condition be, structurally, beyond repair?

## Resource

The leading resource is Friston's free-energy program. In the PRS-form record of Friston's predictive-coding work (PRS-04 in the Friston-tradition wiki), an agent stays viable by continuously minimizing prediction error — registering the gap between its model and its world and updating. Hope, fear, and docility map cleanly onto this loop: hope is the prior that the preferred state is attainable (without it, the agent stops seeking the target); fear/precision is the error signal that one is diverging (presumption is the pathological down-weighting of that signal); docility is the openness to model revision from a higher-precision source (active reasoning and model selection, PRS-10). Peter Lombard's six kinds are, almost item for item, the ways an agent can sabotage its own inference: despair (kill the goal-prior), presumption (mute the error signal), impenitence and obstinacy (refuse the update), resisting known truth (reject the disambiguating evidence), envy of another's grace (attack the very coupling that could correct you). The sin against the Spirit is a system disabling its self-referential corrective loop (PRS-09) — and that is why it is unforgivable in the structural, not the arithmetic, sense. Mercy is not withheld; the channel by which mercy would be received has been switched off from the inside. Aquinas's "no way it is pardoned… because it was not remitted by repentance," together with the Catechism's "there are no limits to the mercy of God," names exactly this: the door is locked from the creature's side.

Q.15 is the same insight applied to cognition. Friston and Hawkins distinguish two failure modes that match Aquinas's two vices: blindness of mind is channel-loss (the supernatural light, the relevant model, fails to form at all — Hawkins's cortical reference frame never gets built); dullness of sense is low precision (the channel exists but is too noisy or sluggish to resolve spiritual goods — the model forms but never sharpens). And Gregory's embodied etiology — blindness from lust, dullness from gluttony — is an embodied-cognition claim the contemporary set strongly supports: McGilchrist on attention shaped by the mode of engagement, Fredrickson's broaden-and-build (PRS-01) on affective and appetitive state gating cognitive scope. Rohr's embodied-love epistemology (PRS-04 in the Rohr-tradition wiki) gives the constructive inverse: "the body is a site of knowing," so abstinence and chastity "dispose man to the perfection of intellectual operation" exactly as Daniel reports of the abstinent children given "knowledge and understanding."

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The proposed parallel reads the sin against the Holy Spirit as the agent's disabling of its own error-correcting capacity. This shares Aquinas's structure precisely: it is sin "through malice" that works by removing the preventers of sin, and it is unforgivable because of what it does to the agent, not because of a limit in mercy. Where it reframes: Aquinas locates the preventers as distinct effects of the Spirit acting on distinct faculties; the parallel reads them as components of one corrective loop, so that despair/presumption/obstinacy are not six unrelated vices but six ways of breaking one mechanism. This makes the unforgivability legible without making God stingy. A system that has destroyed its capacity to register and act on the gap between itself and its target cannot be repaired *from inside*, and — by the non-coercion principle established two days ago (Q.10 a.8) — cannot be repaired from outside either, since assent and reception cannot be forced. Final impenitence is the perspective permanently sealed against the only inputs that could redraw it.

The Q.15 reframe is gentler and more empirical: blindness of mind and dullness of sense are real, distinguishable cognitive failures (channel-loss vs low precision), and they are genuinely modulated by embodied appetite. This is one of the places Aquinas's psychology and the contemporary science converge with little strain — what you habitually consume and crave shapes what your cognition can resolve.

Evidence strength: the corrective-loop model is formally strong (high as a model of agency); its identification with the Spirit's restraining gifts is a theological application offered as illumination (speculative). The unforgivability-as-structural reading is theological but coheres tightly with the non-coercion results (high in field). The blindness/dullness distinction and the appetite-shapes-cognition claim are formally and empirically supported (medium-high).

## The central-theme thread

The central theme says we work out salvation "aware of our limitations and our need for guidance from those who perceive more of the whole than we do." The sin against the Holy Spirit is the exact negation of that clause: it is the limited perspective destroying its own capacity to *receive* guidance and correction. Presumption kills the trembling; despair kills the hope that the whole is reachable; obstinacy and resisting-known-truth refuse the higher-perceiving node. Where the central theme makes the agent's incompleteness the doorway to dependence and growth, this sin nails the doorway shut. That is why it stands as the limit case of the whole project: every other failure leaves the computation toward loving unity recoverable, because the agent can still be drawn by hope, corrected by fear, taught by those who see more. This one ends the computation by dismantling its instruments — the perspective curving permanently in on itself, sealed against the ground that would complete it.

## Bridges

- *PRS-04 + PRS-09 + PRS-10 in the Friston-tradition wiki (Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's predictive-coding and active-reasoning work)* — hope/fear/docility as components of one corrective loop; the six kinds as six ways to break it; unforgivability as a disabled loop, not withheld mercy. (Formal; high as model.)
- *Hawkins-tradition wiki (cortical model formation vs sharpening)* — blindness of mind as channel-loss (model never forms) vs dullness as low precision (model never sharpens). (Neuroscientific; medium-high.)
- *McGilchrist-tradition wiki + PRS-01 in the Fredrickson-tradition wiki* — attention and cognitive scope gated by mode of engagement and affective/appetitive state; the embodied basis of Gregory's etiology. (Empirical; medium-high.)
- *PRS-04 + PRS-02 in the Rohr-tradition wiki* — embodied love as epistemology (the body as a site of knowing) and the order-disorder-reorder pattern as the recoverable counterpart to final impenitence. (Contemplative/theological; medium.)
- *PRS-01 in the Wright-tradition wiki* — critical realism and salvation-as-covenant-inclusion frame the corrective loop as relational reception, not solitary computation. (Historical-theological; high in field.)

## Where this leaves us

This closes the treatment of faith and its opposed vices on its sharpest note: the one failure that is structurally unrecoverable because it attacks recovery itself. The volume now turns toward hope (II-II Q.17 onward) — the very goal-prior whose loss is despair. Having seen what it means to destroy hope, the contemporary parallel is well positioned to take up hope directly: the expectation, held under perspective-limitation, that the target of the computation — loving unity with the whole — is genuinely reachable, and the grounds on which a finite agent may rationally hold it.
