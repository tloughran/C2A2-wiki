# Day 246 — Baptism & Salvation (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.68-69) treats the necessity of baptism for salvation and its effects, including the hard cases — infants, the unevangelized, the invincibly ignorant. The medieval tension: baptism is genuinely necessary as the ordinary means, yet God is not *bound* by the means he institutes. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: how can a rule of the *interface* (the ordinary, instituted means by which limited agents access grace) be really binding on those agents while not binding what *underlies* the interface?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how can a means be necessary "by interface" — really obligatory for the agents who perceive it — without being a constraint on the deeper reality that the interface presents?** Aquinas needs both that baptism is the appointed door and that the appointing God can save apart from it. And he needs the agent's *culpability* to track what the agent could perceive (invincible ignorance excuses).

## Resource

Hoffman is the leading resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman's program, PRS-03 (perception as fitness-tracking; the Interface Theory of Perception, whose solution is that perception is an adaptive interface rather than a truth-mirror) distinguishes sharply between the *rules of the interface* — binding on the agent who has only the interface — and the *freedom of what underlies it*; PRS-04 (observer-dependent spacetime) adds that distinct agents construct mutually consistent interfaces through agent-to-agent interaction, which is what makes the rule public rather than private. This gives precisely the structure Aquinas needs: baptism is the rule of the interface, really necessary for those to whom it is given, while the underlying reality (God) is not bound by the dashboard it authored.

Friston supplies the moral-computation half. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, PRS-04 (the organism as an active modeler) frames agency as inference *under the resources and evidence available* to the agent. Culpability, on this reading, tracks the model the agent could actually have built from its perceived evidence — which naturalizes "invincible ignorance excuses": you are answerable for the computation your resources permitted, not for evidence you could never access.

PRS-02 (identity within monism) in Tom's PRS-form record of Kastrup's program keeps the operative reality dispositional. On Wright's reading, salvation is covenant inclusion, baptism its ordinary sign; the exceptions are still membership, not a second mechanism. Rohr's Universal Christ underwrites a wide mercy that meets the agent where its perception ends.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **baptism is necessary as the rule of the interface — really binding on agents to whom it is given — while the reality the interface presents (God) is free to include those the interface never reached; and an agent's culpability tracks the moral computation its resources actually permitted.** This *shares* with Aquinas both the real necessity of the ordinary means and God's freedom beyond it, and it *preserves* the excusing force of invincible ignorance. It *reframes* "necessity of means" in interface terms and "ignorance excuses" in resource-bounded-inference terms. It *marks honestly* that the interface/underlying distinction is philosophical, not established physics; that the Friston account describes the *form* of bounded moral computation, not its content; and that the saving exceptions are a theological claim (Wright/Rohr), not an inference from the science.

A guardrail (flagged from Day 245): this does not license universalism-by-fiat. The interface rule is real; the exceptions still require an orienting disposition, which Wright's covenant framing supplies and which Friston's account locates in the agent's actual (not hypothetical) computation toward the good.

## The central-theme thread

This is the project's central claim in near-explicit form. Agents are individuated by perspective-limitation; they compute toward loving unity with the resources they perceive; they need guidance from those who perceive more of the whole. Baptism is exactly such guidance — an instituted means that reaches the limited agent at its interface. The hard cases are the test: an agent that *never perceived* the means is judged on the computation its resources allowed, because the whole sees each perspective truly (Day 242's judgement). Necessity-by-interface plus mercy-beyond-interface is the precise shape of "salvation in fear and trembling" for finite perceivers.

## Bridges

- **Hoffman PRS-03 (perception fitness-tracking; Interface Theory)** — *formal.* The interface/underlying distinction itself: necessity binding the interface-bound agent without binding the underlying reality.
- **Hoffman PRS-04 (observer-dependent spacetime)** — *formal-speculative.* Mutually consistent interfaces across agents — why the rule is a public institution, not a private one.
- **Friston PRS-04 (active modeler under resource limits)** — *formal.* Culpability tracks the computation the agent's resources permitted; "ignorance excuses" naturalized.
- **Kastrup PRS-02 (identity within monism)** — *metaphysical.* Operative reality remains dispositional.
- **Wright PRS-34 (`traditions/wright/prs_triplets.md`; "works of the law" as covenant badge practices)** — *scriptural, high within method.* Justification is God's declaration of covenant membership and the badge individuates rather than constitutes — so the exceptions are still membership, not a second mechanism. Guards against fiat-universalism.
- **Wright PRS-13 (`traditions/wright/prs_triplets.md`; the people of God as the location of God's homecoming)** — *scriptural, high within method.* Salvation as inclusion in the covenanted people, which is what the ordinary means delivers.
- **Rohr PRS-03 (`traditions/rohr/prs_triplets.md`; the Universal Christ)** — *theological / contemplative.* The divine pattern present in all creation and not confined to a single institutional form — the warrant for mercy reaching past the instituted rite.
- **Rohr PRS-26 (`traditions/rohr/prs_triplets.md`; election and universal belovedness)** — *theological.* Election as a real role-conferral whose telos is its own universalization: mercy is wide without the ordinary means becoming empty.
