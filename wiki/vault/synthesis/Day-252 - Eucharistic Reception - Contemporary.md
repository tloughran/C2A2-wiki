# Day 252 — Eucharistic Reception (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.79–80) turns from what the sacrament *is* to what it *does* in the one who receives it, and to the difference between receivers. The same consecrated species is offered to all, yet its effect varies: the well-disposed receive grace, the indisposed receive to their condemnation; there is sacramental eating (taking the species) and spiritual eating (receiving the reality with faith and charity), and reception "by desire" can obtain the res without the sacramentum. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: how can one and the same offered reality produce opposite effects in differently disposed agents — and what is the disposition that does the sorting?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **why does an identical input yield divergent outcomes across receivers?** If the cause is the same and the effect differs, the difference must lie in the receiver's state, not the offering. Aquinas locates it in the disposition of faith and charity. The contemporary question is what kind of thing a "disposition" is, such that it can determine whether the same offered whole is assimilated or refused.

## Resource

Friston's program is the sharpest resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, PRS-04 (the organism as active modeler — a formal account of goal-directedness within naturalism, against a biology that had no room for it) makes an agent's *prior* — its generative model and preferences — the thing that determines how any incoming signal is interpreted and what it does to the system. The same sensory evidence updates two differently primed models in opposite directions: confirming and integrating for one, generating large prediction-error or being rejected for the other. PRS-14 sharpens the mechanism of refusal: it accounts for states that are *locally* optimal but globally suboptimal — Bayesian attractors that resist update because revising them raises free energy in the short term. A receiver whose priors are so trapped does not assimilate the offered reality merely by contact; the disposition is the operative variable, and a hardened prior can take in the species while refusing the res. Two honest qualifications. PRS-14 is recorded as an account of *symptom*-formation and its clinical resolution; using its self-trapping structure for a culpable spiritual indisposition extends it beyond the domain in which it was stated, and no claim is being made that unworthy reception is a pathology. And the formalism is well-developed as formalism — it is not a measurement of what it is here being used to model.

Kastrup supplies the ontology of *reception itself* — with a provenance qualification the entry itself insists on. PRS-09 in the Kastrup-tradition record (participatory encounter) makes receiving the reality a participation in it rather than a reading-off of the icon, so "spiritual eating" and reception "by desire" are intelligible as the dispositional, participatory uptake of a reality that sacramental eating only contacts. The operative reality is the dispositional state, which is why desire can obtain the res. The qualification: PRS-09 is not settled Kastrup program content. It records *McGilchrist's* April-2026 counter-proposal against Kastrup's dashboard-of-perception metaphor — the objection that representation implies a one-way data-reading relation and misses genuine two-way encounter — and is filed at Confidence: Medium with a Solution line saying Kastrup's analytic idealism "may need refinement," distinguishing a left-hemisphere representational mode from an ontologically primary right-hemisphere participatory one. The participatory reading this synthesis leans on is the *proposed refinement*, not the program's own stated position.

Levin gives the in-domain hinge: in Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's program, PRS-03 (target-state specification) shows a system's outcome governed by its set-point rather than by the raw stimulus — the same intervention drives different morphological results depending on the bioelectric prior the tissue holds.

Rohr (PRS-03, the Universal Christ — the divine pattern present in all of creation) and Wright (PRS-05, salvation as incorporation into the people among whom God comes home rather than a relocation of souls) remain the theological ground for the *content*: that the reality offered is Christ, and that worthiness is a matter of charity, not merit ledgered.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the one offered whole is received according to each perspective's readiness, because the disposition is the prior that determines whether the offered reality is assimilated, refused, or received to harm.** This *shares* with Aquinas the single cause / divergent effect structure and the location of the difference in the receiver's faith and charity. It *reframes* "disposition" as the receiver's generative model and preferences (Friston) and reception as participatory uptake (Kastrup), which gives a mechanism for why the same species can be eaten sacramentally without the res, and why desire alone can obtain the res. It *firms up* one premise: that identical input yields disposition-dependent outcomes is, in active inference, a formal and empirically productive claim, not a bare assertion. It *marks* that the *worthiness content* — what charity is, what condemnation means — is theological; that Kastrup's participatory ontology is contested; and that the framework explains the *form* of differential reception without settling its *substance*.

## The central-theme thread

Here the project's central motif takes one of its clearest sacramental forms: the *same whole* is received *according to each perspective's readiness*. The whole is not rationed — every receiver is offered the entire reality — yet each limited perspective assimilates it only to the measure of its disposition. The limitation is not a withholding by the giver but the finite receiver's own prior, the resource-bounded model through which the whole must pass. To grow in charity is to revise the prior toward the loving-unity attractor, so that more of the always-fully-offered whole can actually be received. "Working out salvation in fear and trembling" is, on this reading, the lifelong revision of the disposition by which the unchanging gift is taken in.
