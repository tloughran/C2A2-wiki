# Day 291 — Bill of Divorce (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.67–68 turn from the *essence* of marriage to two juridical questions: the **indissolubility** of the bond (and the Mosaic *bill of divorce* as a dispensation from it) and the status of **illegitimate children**. Aquinas grounds indissolubility in natural law: a cause is concerned for the perfection of its effect, and since offspring "never cease to be the effect of their parents," the parental bond is ordered to an end that runs the *whole life* of the child — so the union is meant to be lifelong and inseparable. The Mosaic permission to divorce is read as a *dispensation* from a secondary precept, tolerated (like the polygamy of Day 290) to forestall a greater evil, never as an ideal. Illegitimacy, in Q.68, is a *legal* disability attaching to birth outside true marriage, removable by legitimization. The contemporary version: **what makes a bond rightly indissoluble, and what kind of thing is the "standing" that legitimacy confers or withholds?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **the source of a bond's normativity and the nature of conferred standing**. Aquinas's instinct is that the marriage bond's permanence is not a brute rule but follows from the *temporal horizon of its end*: an objective that spans a whole life underwrites a relation meant to last that long. And legitimacy, tellingly, is not a property *in* the child at all — it is a *standing* the community's law assigns and can withdraw or restore. Both halves press the same question the project keeps meeting: are the normative facts about persons grounded in something intrinsic and isolated, or in a relation to a wider, longer-horizon whole?

## Resource

The Friston-tradition program supplies the leading structural move. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work (PRS-06, *multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling*; PRS-16, *expected free energy as a unified objective*) lets us read indissolubility precisely: a bond becomes a single coupled agent when two parties adopt a *shared objective whose horizon is long*. Expected-free-energy accounts of agency are explicit that the planning horizon shapes the commitment — an objective indexed to the whole life of a third party (the offspring) underwrites a coupling meant to persist across that whole horizon. Dissolving it mid-horizon is not merely rule-breaking; it abandons an objective the bond was constituted to pursue. This gives Aquinas's "ordered to the lifelong good of the offspring" a structural rather than merely customary reading. The Wright-tradition program governs the scriptural register and does the load-bearing work on legitimacy: PRS-02 (*faithful improvisation*) and PRS-03 (*inaugurated eschatology*) frame biblical status-language as membership in a *covenanted people* — "salvation" in Wright's sense is precisely *inclusion in the people united with God*. Read through that lens, legitimacy is the small-scale civic analogue of covenant membership: a *conferred standing within a body*, not a metaphysical mark on the individual — which is exactly how Aquinas treats it (a disability the law adds and legitimization removes). The Levin-tradition program supplies the biological precedent that an end can span generations: PRS-01 (*morphogenetic control*; bioelectric circuits encode and hand on anatomical target-states) shows form being *transmitted and conserved across the parent–offspring boundary* — the empirical face of Aquinas's "offspring never cease to be the effect of their cause." PRS-04 in the Rohr-tradition wiki (*embodied love as epistemology*) keeps the analysis personal: the bond is known from within the practice of love, not deduced from outside it.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **a bond is rightly indissoluble when it is constituted by a shared objective whose horizon is a whole life; and "legitimacy" names a standing conferred by membership in a community, not a property intrinsic to the child.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: indissolubility as flowing from the lifelong end of the union, divorce as a tolerated dispensation rather than an ideal, and legitimacy as a removable legal standing. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas grounds the verdict in natural-law precepts; the reframe grounds the *core* of indissolubility structurally (a long-horizon shared objective underwrites a long-horizon coupling), and reads legitimacy through Wright's covenant-membership lens, which is both more honest to the texts (which treat it as conferred) and more humane — the disability is in the *standing*, never in the worth of the person. Where evidence is *firm*: that planning horizon shapes the durability of a coupling is a clean structural point; that biological form is conserved across generations is empirical. Where *speculative*: the move from "long-horizon coupling" to a normative verdict on the permanence of human marriage is theological-conceptual, not derivable from the model; and the analogy between civil legitimacy and covenant membership must not be pressed into any denigration of children — the whole point is that standing is *conferred and restorable*, never a measure of a person's value.

## The central-theme thread

The project's loving unity is *inclusion* — every limited perspective held within the one whole. Wright's "salvation as inclusion in the people united with God" gets its first explicit rehearsal here, at the human-law scale: legitimacy is membership conferred by relation to a covenanted body. A child's standing is not read off the child in isolation but off the child's place in a community — and the law's power to *legitimize* is a power to widen inclusion. This is the loving-unity computation in miniature: the worth that matters is the worth of being *included*, and the work of grace (and of just law) is to extend that inclusion to those the rules had placed outside it. The indissoluble bond, in turn, is the elementary long-horizon commitment out of which such communities of inclusion are built.

## Bridges

- **Friston PRS-06 / PRS-16 (formal; load-bearing):** indissolubility as a long-horizon shared objective underwriting a long-horizon coupling — the structural reading of "ordered to the lifelong good of the offspring."
- **Wright PRS-02 / PRS-03 (scriptural/theological):** legitimacy as conferred covenant-membership standing, not an intrinsic mark — salvation-as-inclusion at the civic scale.
- **Levin PRS-01 (empirical, biological):** anatomical target-states conserved across the parent–offspring boundary — the empirical face of "offspring never cease to be the effect of their cause."

From here the Supplement turns to the *last things* proper: Day 292 opens the questions on the state of separated souls — where the soul is after death and how it persists.
