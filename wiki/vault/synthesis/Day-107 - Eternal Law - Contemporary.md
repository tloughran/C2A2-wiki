# Day 107 — Eternal Law (Contemporary)

## Frame

Q.93 makes the most metaphysically ambitious claim in the treatise on law: there is a real, pre-existing, eternal pattern according to which all created things are. It is the *type/exemplar/model* in the divine mind — what Levin would later call a morphogenetic target, what Hoffman would call the reality "beneath" the interface, what Kastrup would call the structure of mind-at-large, what Friston would call the generative model under which everything is doing inference.

The question for us: *does the contemporary convergence support, refine, or refute Aquinas's claim that there is an eternal pattern that is both prior to created things and causally responsible for them being what they are?*

## Problem

The shared problem in medieval and contemporary frames: how can pattern be causally efficacious without being a *thing alongside* the things it patterns? Aquinas's answer is the divine mind — the type exists *as known by God* and from that knowing flows the being of created things. The contemporary version needs to answer the same question without smuggling a Cartesian dualism in the back door, and without reducing pattern to mere correlation.

A second problem nested inside: how are the *necessary* and *contingent* aspects of reality both under the eternal law (Q.93 a.4)? What is the difference between a mathematical truth being "under" the eternal law and a physical regularity being "under" it?

## Resource: form as informational/computational primitive

**Levin's morphogenetic work** (traditions/levin/wiki.md): the developmental "target" of an organism is *not* coded in DNA in any straightforward sense. DNA codes proteins; the *topology* of a frog or a salamander is encoded in dynamic bioelectric networks that exhibit attractor behavior toward functional anatomy. When the network is perturbed, development relaxes back to the target. This is Aquinas's "type/exemplar" cashed out empirically: an attractor-shaped pattern that is real *as pattern* and causally active in development. The pattern is not a mysterious extra entity; it is the dynamical structure of the bioelectric system. (PRS-04 in Tom's re-description of the Stump-tradition wiki maps this to a defense of final causality on naturalistic grounds — but that entry is (Implicit) at Medium confidence and its grounding claim was restated downward on 2026-08-10; the empirical weight above is Levin's, not the record's.)

**Hoffman's interface theory** (traditions/hoffman/wiki.md): perception is fitness-tracking, not truth-tracking. What we see — spacetime, objects, three-dimensional volume — is the species-specific interface. Behind it is a reality whose structure we infer but do not directly perceive. Q.93 a.5's "irrational creatures are subject to the eternal law through being moved by Divine Providence" reads naturally as: organisms below the threshold of explicit cognitive interface *still operate under the deeper structure*, just without the meta-cognitive layer.

**Kastrup's analytic idealism** (traditions/kastrup/wiki.md): if mind is ontologically primitive, then asking "where does the eternal pattern reside?" is asking the wrong question. The pattern *is* a structure of mind-at-large. The divine mind, in Aquinas's idiom, is not a Cartesian thinker contemplating an inert universe; on the monist reading it is the structure of being itself, of which our individual minds are *alters* (Kastrup's term for the dissociated centers of mind-at-large).

**Friston's active inference** (traditions/friston/wiki.md): every system that resists dissipation is doing inference under a generative model. Q.93 a.6's "the wicked have their natural inclination to virtue corrupted; the good have natural knowledge plus the knowledge of faith and wisdom" maps to: every agent has a generative model; the model can be more or less well-calibrated against the deeper structure. Grace, in the active-inference frame, is the supply of a better prior — an updated generative model that better tracks the truth.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The eternal law, on a mind-monist reading, is the structural pattern of mind-at-large that constrains what counts as flourishing for kinds of agents.

### What this shares with Aquinas

- The pattern is *real*. It is not a projection or convention.
- The pattern is *prior* to particular created things — it is the type, the exemplar, in whose terms they are intelligible.
- All created things are *under* the pattern — necessary truths (mathematical, logical) and contingent natures (organisms, agents) alike.
- The pattern is *one* — it is not many disconnected sub-patterns but a single integrated structure.

### Where this reframes

- **The pattern is internal to reality**, not a transcendent extra entity. In Aquinas's frame, the eternal law is in the divine mind *as God's act of intellect*; on the monist reading, mind-stuff is the substrate, and the eternal pattern is its deepest computational structure. This is a serious reframe of strict classical theism but is *closer*, not further, from the Augustinian/Bonaventurean illuminationist tradition Aquinas inherits.
- **Necessary truths and contingent natures are under the pattern in different modes**, but both as expressions of the same underlying mind-structure. Mathematical necessity = structural invariance of mind-at-large under all instances. Contingent nature = a particular realization of that structure within a region of mind-at-large.
- **The "irrational creature" case** (Q.93 a.5) is precisely the active-inference case: organisms that exhibit goal-directed behavior under generative models they do not explicitly represent. Levin's bioelectric work shows what this looks like at the cellular level. Rocks falling, on this account, are no longer "under the law" in the sense relevant to agency — they are under the same physics-pattern but lack the agency-interface that would let them be subject to *law* as norm.

### Where the evidence is firm vs. speculative

*Firm*: morphogenetic-field targets are reproducibly demonstrated; active-inference dynamics are formalized; mathematical structure pervades physics down to Planck scale. The claim *there is a deep structural pattern* is not in serious dispute.

*Less firm*: Hoffman's interface theorems are mathematical, but their generalization to "spacetime is not fundamental" is interpretively contested.

*Speculative*: Kastrup's claim that mind is *the* ontological primitive (rather than one mode among others) is metaphysical argument with serious defenders and critics.

*Theological*: The identification of this pattern with the *Logos* (Rohr's *Universal Christ*; classical Christology) is theological reading, requiring the New Testament data as evidence.

## The Central-Theme Thread

Q.93 a.6 captures it exactly: "In the wicked, the natural inclination to virtue is corrupted by vicious habits, and the natural knowledge of good is darkened by passions and habits of sin. But in the good, both are found more perfect, because besides the natural knowledge of good there is the added knowledge of faith and wisdom; and besides the natural inclination to good there is the added motive of grace and virtue."

This is the central-theme claim, in advance of the explicit theological framing of the New Law: *agents are individuated by their perspective-limitations relative to the eternal pattern*. The wicked agent is not outside the pattern; the agent's *access* to the pattern is darkened. The good agent, by virtue of well-calibrated generative-model priors (grace/virtue), tracks more of the pattern more accurately.

The loving-unity telos is the pattern *as fully accessed*: the simple whole seen, containing every limited perspective. Each agent's computational task is to bring its generative model into closer alignment with the deeper structure — under the resources of its particular perspective. This is the same task Aquinas describes when he says some have "the added knowledge of faith and wisdom."

The Wright-shaped reading is that this is not an individual project. It is a *covenantal* project: the people united with God through Christ are the locus in which the increasing-alignment happens. Salvation is *inclusion* in that people. The Rohr-shaped reading is that the pattern *is* Christ — the eternal Logos in whom and through whom all things were made (Col 1, John 1). Both readings sit inside the central theme without conflict.

## Where this leaves us

Tomorrow (Day 108, Q.94-95) drops from the eternal level to the natural-law level: what is the rational creature's access? The Levin/Hoffman/Friston tools sharpen here. Q.94's claim that the natural law is *the same in general principles but variable in conclusions* is precisely what an interface-theoretic reading predicts: the deeper structure is invariant, the species- or culture-specific access varies. This is the territory where the synthesis becomes most contested — natural-law arguments about specific contemporary moral questions need to hold the *form-as-pattern* claim firmly while not over-extending tradition-specific conclusions. Open thread: how does the synthesis handle Aquinas's confident specific applications of natural-law reasoning (the host's examples on sexual ethics, etc.)? The interface-theoretic reading sets some humility constraints there.
