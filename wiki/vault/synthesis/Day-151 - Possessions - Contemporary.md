# Day 151 — Contemporary Parallel: Possessions, Theft, and the Whole That Holds

## Frame

The treatise on justice turns from the gravest matter (life, Q.64-65) to the next: external possessions. Q.66 asks whether it is natural for man to possess external things, whether private property is lawful, what theft and robbery are, and — the article that will anchor the whole question — whether it is lawful for a person in dire necessity to take another's goods (a.7). Q.67 turns to the judge: when is it lawful to judge, by what standards, and what are the species of judicial injustice. The two questions form a single arc: the right ordering of external goods between parts of the political whole, and the special responsibility of the office whose job is to enforce that ordering.

## Problem

The inquiry-problem, in both medieval and contemporary frames: *external goods are scarce, agents are finite, and the agent who must use a thing cannot see the whole field at once — so on what basis does any given thing rightly belong to this rather than that part, and what corrects the misallocation?* Aquinas answers from a layered ontology: God, who possesses all things, gives the material order to humanity as steward (*imago Dei*); within that stewardship, positive law assigns particular goods to particular persons for orderly use, while their use remains common in the sense of being for the flourishing of all. The contemporary problem, under mind / conscious-realist monism, is to recover the same two-layer structure without the fixed cosmological ranking — to see why property is a *real* but *perspective-relative* allocation, and why the response to its violation is restitution and re-integration rather than mere retribution.

## Resource

The clearest contemporary resource on the metaphysics of *real-but-perspectival* allocation is Tom's PRS-form record of Wright's work — PRS-01 (critical realism) gives the formal structure: the goods are real, our access to them is perspectival, knowledge requires the right kind of relation. Property is then a real arrangement-of-the-political-whole accessed perspectivally by each holder. Tom's PRS-form record of Carroll's work (PRS-01, poetic naturalism's multiple legitimate levels of description) supplies the contemporary metaphysics for why property is a real higher-level pattern, not folk physics: the pattern of *who holds what to use for whose flourishing* tracks something the political ecology actually does, the way temperature tracks something thermodynamics actually does. PRS-28 (moral facts as real at the level of description appropriate to agents and their wellbeing, without non-natural properties) supplies the discipline: the pattern can be described without pretending the *should* is read off the *is*; the normative weight comes from the polity's actual telos.

Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work supplies the part-whole biology Aquinas's "members of the body politic" image presupposes. PRS-10 (goal-directedness continuous from unicellular to human, a graded rather than binary property) and PRS-17 (bioelectric pattern memory as the *cognitive glue* that constitutes a collective knower above the level of the individual cell) together establish a real collective cognition with components that hold local resources for the whole's coordinated function; PRS-02 (cancer as defection) is the direct contemporary statement of theft's structural form — a part appropriating resources for its own growth at the cost of the field; PRS-11 (re-integration as the actual therapy) is the analogue of the restitution arc that runs from Day 149 through this question. Theft is the misappropriation, restitution is the morphogenetic field reasserting itself by drawing the part back. Aquinas's bright line — that necessity makes all things common again ("in cases of need all things are common property"; a.7) — is the explicit medieval statement of the same biology: when the part's survival is at stake, the field releases its prior allocation and the goods return to common use. This is not an exception to property; it is the disclosure that property was always an instrument of the whole.

Tom's PRS-form record of Stump's work (PRS-14/15) supplies the corporate substance the field metaphor needs: the political whole is a real unity, not just an aggregate, so the part *holds for* the whole and the whole has a real claim on the part's holdings under the right conditions. This is the metaphysics that makes the necessity provision intelligible: not "the law lets you get away with it" but "the goods belonged to the whole the whole time, and your survival is the whole's interest."

Q.67's judicial injustice maps onto Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work. The judge occupies a *boundaried* position in the polity's inferential apparatus: PRS-28 (precision as the inverse variance of prediction error, weighted over *selected* channels) is precisely what an office is — a constrained set of priors and inputs, weighted to track a particular slice of the global model. To judge outside one's jurisdiction (a.2), against the law (a.4), on suspicion (a.3), or against the absent (a.5) is to *de-bound* the office, pulling priors and inputs the office is not weighted to integrate — the inferential analogue of theft, and the political analogue of what PRS-28 calls aberrant precision-weighting on belief updates. PRS-04 (the organism as an active modeler that generates predictions and tests them against input, rather than a passive machine) clarifies why the judge's intention matters: the office is constituted by what it is actively modeling *for*, and its proper target is truth in service of the common good, not the judge's private preferences.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The proposed contemporary parallel preserves Aquinas's two-layer structure with the cosmology made monist. *Preserved*: property is natural (it tracks the real fact that finite agents must hold particular goods); private possession is lawful (it serves orderly use); use remains common (the goods are for the flourishing of all); robbery is graver than theft because it adds violence to wrong (a.9) and so breaks the peace the political whole exists for; necessity makes goods common again (a.7) — the field reasserts its prior claim. *Reframed*: the "whole" is the real collective cognition of the polity (Levin/Stump), not just an aggregate under transcendent ownership; property is a real-but-perspective-relative allocation (Wright critical realism; Carroll poetic-naturalist level realism); restitution is field re-integration (Levin PRS-02/11), continuous with the cancer-therapy logic that pressured Day 150's excision argument toward rehabilitation. *Extended*: the judge's injustice is read as de-bounding of an inferential office (Friston PRS-28) — the office exists because the polity cannot judge from every perspective at once, so it constructs a specialized one; when the office abandons its bounds, the polity's apparatus for tracking truth fails locally, and the whole loses its instrument for self-correction.

Measure against Aquinas: convergence on the substance, with the metaphysics shifted from fixed-hierarchy stewardship to real-collective-cognition allocation. The necessity provision becomes more emphatic, not less: it is not a marginal exception but the disclosure of property's instrumental status. The judicial-injustice arc gains a formal gloss: each species of judicial wrong is a specific failure mode of a bounded inference engine, and the polity has reason to specify each because each undermines a different part of the office's truth-tracking function.

## The central-theme thread

External possessions are the most concrete arena of the central claim. Each agent's perspective is finite; resources are finite; the wholes we belong to allocate parts to parts so that each can act, on the whole's behalf, in the slice of the field they can actually see. Property is one of those allocations. Theft is the locally-optimal-globally-defective move — the agent's perspective absolutized against the whole that gave it the goods to hold. The judge's injustice is the same defection from a position of greater responsibility: the office that exists to track the whole's good for the parts that cannot see it. The necessity provision (a.7) is where the central claim shows most clearly: when a part's survival is at stake, the field releases its allocation, because *the part is what the whole was for*. "Working out salvation in fear and trembling" includes the discipline of holding what is the whole's to apportion as the whole's, available to the whole's need.
