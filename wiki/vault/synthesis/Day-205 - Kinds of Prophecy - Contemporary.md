# Day 205 — Kinds of Prophecy (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

The treatise on prophecy closes by sorting it and then transcending it. Q.174 *divides* prophecy: by object, into *denunciation* (a forecast of a contingent cause that "is sometimes hindered by some other occurrence supervening" — Jonah's Nineveh, which repented and was spared) versus *foreknowledge / predestination* (what "is known in itself as already present to God" and "will certainly come to pass"); and by *degree*, ranking the modes from highest to lowest, with the highest being "when God impresses a truth directly into the mind" (no images), and Moses the greatest of prophets because he knew "face to face." Q.175 then takes up *rapture*: Paul "caught up to the third heaven," which Aquinas defines as being "uplifted contrary to nature" — for since our nature is "to receive knowledge through the external senses and by then abstracting the essence," to be "elevated in the mind toward knowledge of the supernatural while withdrawn from the external senses" is *against* that nature. In rapture the intellect is "withdrawn from phantasms," transiently informed by "the light of glory" — which is "not an abiding quality" (that would be heaven) "but instead something transitory." And Paul, the paradigm case, "did not know whether his soul was separated from his body." The contemporary version: *what is a knowing that ascends by setting aside the very apparatus through which a perspective normally knows — and what happens to the knower at the limit?*

## Problem

Three linked inquiry-problems, forming an ascent. (1) **Two kinds of foretelling.** Denunciation and foreknowledge differ in modal force: one is a *defeasible* prediction (Nineveh *will* fall — unless it repents), the other a *certain* report. The problem is to distinguish a forecast that tracks a cause-effect relation liable to be overridden from a knowledge that is not a forecast at all but a *seeing* of what is settled. (2) **A ladder of mediation.** Aquinas ranks prophecy by *how* the truth arrives — direct intellectual impression at the top, image-mediated below, sleeping below waking. The problem is to say why *less mediation* should be *more* excellent, and what the limit of decreasing mediation would be. (3) **The limit itself: rapture.** Aquinas characterizes rapture entirely by *subtraction* — withdrawal from the senses, freedom from phantasms, suspension of the natural abstraction process — and calls it "contrary to nature." The problem is what kind of knowing remains when the agent's normal knowing-apparatus is set aside, why it must be *transient*, and why its own subject cannot locate himself within it ("in the body or out of the body, I know not").

## Resource

For the two kinds of foretelling (1), the resource is the active-inference picture carried forward from Day 204: an agent's forecasts are *model-based and defeasible* — Nineveh's destruction is the predicted trajectory of a generative model of cause and effect, revisable when a new cause (repentance) supervenes. *Foreknowledge*, by contrast, is the Day-204 "view from outside the time-interface": not a model's extrapolation but an apprehension of the settled manifold (Hoffman PRS-04, observer-dependent/constructed time; Carroll's eternalism as the physics companion). The distinction is clean: denunciation is inference *within* time, foreknowledge is seeing *from outside* it.

For the ladder of mediation (2), the lead is Hoffman's interface program together with the participatory-knowing refinement. Tom's PRS-form record of Hoffman (PRS-03/PRS-04) makes ordinary knowing a *rendering* through a species-specific interface: senses → imagery → abstracted structure. Aquinas's degree-ladder is then a ladder of *decreasing mediation* — each step up uses *less* of the rendering pipeline. Image-mediated prophecy still runs through the imagery layer; direct intellectual impression *skips* it; the highest knowing approaches truth with the interface most fully bypassed. Why less mediation is more excellent follows immediately: every layer of the interface is a *lossy, fitness-tuned compression* (Hoffman's central result), so the fewer layers interposed, the less the truth is deformed by the apparatus that individuates the knower. PRS-05 in the McGilchrist-tradition wiki (and the Kastrup-McGilchrist participatory refinement at Kastrup PRS-09) names what the top of the ladder *is*: not a better representation but direct *participation* — the highest knowing is the knower being informed by the thing rather than reading a model of it. CROSS-001 frames the open question this ascent points at — whether knowing can be, at the limit, unmediated at all.

For rapture itself (3), the contemporary frame here is Kastrup's analytic idealism. Tom's PRS-form record at PRS-02 reads individuation as a *dissociative boundary* within one mind-at-large: a perspective is a clenched, bounded point of view, not a separate substance. This gives Aquinas's "uplifted contrary to nature" a precise reading. Our *nature* is the dissociated, interface-bound condition — knowing by abstraction from the senses. Rapture is a *transient, partial relaxation of the dissociative boundary*: the alter, momentarily, knows more of the wider mind it is already part of, with the sensory interface set aside (Aquinas: intellect "withdrawn from phantasms"). This explains, rather than merely asserts, three of Aquinas's claims at once. It is *contrary to nature* because it suspends the very apparatus (the interface, the boundary) that constitutes the natural, individuated knower. It is *transient, not abiding*, because relaxing the boundary is not dissolving it — the dissociation re-clenches and the alter returns to itself (the "light of glory" does not abide; that would be heaven, where the boundary is transfigured rather than momentarily loosened). And the subject *cannot locate his own state* — "in the body or out, I know not" — because the self-model that would report "I am here, embodied, at this boundary" is precisely part of the apparatus that has been set aside; with the interface suspended, the usual self-locating representation is unavailable. (A strong caution belongs here: this models the *structure* of rapture as interface-relaxation; it does *not* claim that the beatific vision reduces to ego-dissolution or that the divine essence is identical to "mind-at-large." Those are theological/metaphysical commitments the synthesis marks as Aquinas's, held more firmly than the analogy supports.)

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **prophetic knowing forms an ascent of decreasing mediation — from a defeasible, model-based forecast of a contingent cause (denunciation), through truth received with fewer and fewer interface layers interposed, to the limit-case of rapture, in which the species-specific interface is set aside and the dissociative boundary that individuates the perspective is transiently, partially relaxed, so that the knower is directly (participatorily) informed by what exceeds it.** Because every interface layer is a lossy, fitness-tuned compression, less mediation means less deformation of the truth; because relaxing the boundary is not dissolving it, the state is transient and the self cannot locate itself within it.

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps the modal distinction between defeasible denunciation and certain foreknowledge; keeps the degree-ladder and its valuation (direct, image-free knowing is highest); keeps rapture defined by *subtraction* (withdrawal from senses, freedom from phantasms, contrary-to-nature elevation); keeps its *transience* (light of glory not abiding); and keeps Paul's inability to know his own state.

*Where it reframes.* The two foretellings become model-inference-within-time vs. apprehension-from-outside-time; the degree-ladder becomes a ladder of decreasing interface-mediation; "contrary to nature" becomes the suspension of the rendering/individuating apparatus; transience becomes the re-clenching of a relaxed (not dissolved) dissociative boundary; and Paul's not-knowing becomes the unavailability of the self-locating model when the interface is set aside. What the reframe explicitly refuses to reduce is the *content* of the vision: the divine essence, the light of glory, and the distinction between transient rapture and abiding heaven are carried as theology.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* The defeasible-forecast / view-from-outside-time distinction is formally clean (medium). The degree-ladder as decreasing mediation, and "less mediation = less deformation," follow from interface theory's compression result (structural, medium). Rapture-as-boundary-relaxation is an analogy with real explanatory yield (it predicts transience and self-opacity) but is speculative as metaphysics (medium), and is *not* offered as a reduction of the beatific vision. The divine source and content are theological, presented as such. This is an exploratory synthesis: at the very threshold of the vision, it claims structural illumination, not the thing itself.

## The central-theme thread

The project's claim is that conscious agents are individuated by the limits of their perspective; that "when all limitations are removed, the simple whole is seen and every limited perspective is contained in it"; and that a creature's full, lasting inclusion in that whole — *salvation* — is a loving, communal union worked out "in fear and trembling" under guidance. Rapture is this claim staged at its furthest reachable edge. Here a perspective's limiting interface is set aside *far enough to glimpse more of the simple whole* — the ascent of decreasing mediation arriving, transiently, at near-direct participation. It is the strongest natural confirmation the treatise offers that perspective-limitation is not the last word: loosen the interface and more of the whole discloses itself. And yet the day is equally a discipline on the claim. The glimpse is *transient* — the boundary re-clenches, the alter returns; it is explicitly *not* the abiding beatific vision; and even within it the subject cannot locate his own state. So the removal of limitation that the central thesis envisions is shown to be, for a creature in this life, momentary and incomplete — a foretaste, not the possession. More: rapture is a *cognitive* ascent, an individual's intellect informed by the truth — and the project, with Day 204's warning still ringing (prophecy "without charity"; "I never knew you"), insists that the telos is *loving* union, and that this union is *communal* — inclusion in the people united with God (Wright's salvation), not a solitary flash of vision. The highest knowing of Q.174–175 is glorious and real, but it is not yet the thing the whole project is for. Wright's register supplies the true horizon: the abiding form of "withdrawn from the senses to see God" is not endless rapture but *resurrection* — new creation, the embodied person fully and lastingly included, the dissociative boundary not merely loosened but transfigured. Rohr's supplies the posture: the contemplative's transient participation is a genuine foretaste precisely because it is *received*, non-grasping, the self de-clenched enough to be informed by what it cannot possess. The prophet, even the rapt one, remains what the project says every creature is until the end — a limited perspective, given more of the whole than it could reach on its own, and still on the way.

## Bridges

- **Denunciation vs. foreknowledge ↔ defeasible model-forecast vs. apprehension from outside time** — Tom's PRS-form record of the Friston program (PRS-04) and the Hoffman-tradition record (PRS-04); Carroll's eternalism. *Why:* one is revisable inference within time (Nineveh repents), the other a seeing of the settled manifold. Evidence: **formal, medium**.
- **The degree-ladder ↔ decreasing interface-mediation; less mediation = less deformation** — the Hoffman-tradition record, PRS-03/PRS-04; master CROSS-001. *Why:* each rendering layer is a lossy fitness-tuned compression, so direct intellectual impression deforms the truth least. Evidence: **structural, medium**.
- **The top of the ladder ↔ participatory, not representational, knowing** — the McGilchrist-tradition record, PRS-05; the Kastrup record, PRS-09; master CROSS-003. *Why:* the highest knowing is the knower informed by the thing, not reading a model of it. Evidence: **conceptual, medium**.
- **Rapture ("contrary to nature," transient, self-opaque) ↔ transient relaxation of the dissociative boundary / setting aside the interface** — the Kastrup-tradition record, PRS-02. *Why:* it predicts the contrary-to-nature character, the transience (re-clenching), and Paul's inability to locate his own state. Evidence: **analogical, medium — explicitly not a reduction of the beatific vision**.
- **Transient rapture ↔ abiding resurrection / communal loving union as the real telos** — Wright-tradition (resurrection / new creation) and Rohr-tradition (received contemplative participation) resources; master CROSS-005. *Why:* the project's end is lasting, embodied, communal inclusion in the whole, not a solitary cognitive flash. Evidence: **theological**.

## Where this leaves us

This closes the prophecy treatise (Q.171–175) and, with it, the long arc through the charisms. The ascent has run from defeasible forecast to the threshold of the unmediated vision — the natural high-water mark of "more of the whole disclosed to a limited perspective" — and has, at that threshold, re-confirmed the limitation it momentarily exceeds. Open thread: the treatise turns next to *rapture's neighbors* and the remaining gratuitous graces (the gift of tongues, the word of wisdom and knowledge, miracles — Q.176 onward, Day 206+), and beyond them toward the active and contemplative lives (Q.179–182), where the cognitive ascent of these days is rejoined to the *loving* and *communal* telos the project insists is the point. Carry forward the central discipline established here: knowing more of the whole, even to the edge of vision, is a foretaste of union, not union itself.
