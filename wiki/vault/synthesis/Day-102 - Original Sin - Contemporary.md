# Day 102 — Original Sin (Contemporary)

## Frame

Aquinas now turns from the agent of sin to the *patient*: what kind of thing is original sin, and what is its proper subject? In Q.82 he insists it is not an actual sin but a *habitual disorder* of the soul — a privation of the original rectifying gift (original justice) under which the soul's lower powers were ordered by the higher. In Q.83 he locates the subject of that disorder not in any single power but in the essence of the soul itself, because original sin is transmitted by *generation* — the form is received already disordered. Habash's exegesis underlines the move: humanity is "one in essence and many in person" with Adam as the principle of the species.

The contemporary version of the question is: what is the actual structure of inheritance that produces, in every newborn agent, a pre-volitional tilt of priors away from the trajectory of loving unity — and what makes that tilt properly its *own*, not just an external constraint imposed on it? Under mind / conscious-realist monism, this becomes the question of how a single mind-at-large can transmit perspective-limitation across instances of dissociation, and how empirically tractable inheritance mechanisms (genetic, epigenetic, developmental, cultural) realize that transmission in the bodies that the interface presents.

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem across medieval and contemporary frames:

> Every agent enters the world with a non-chosen starting condition that systematically biases its priors away from the very telos it is structurally ordered toward. The bias is universal, structural, and pre-volitional. What kind of entity is "humanity" such that this bias can be transmitted, and what is the proper site of the bias in any given agent?

Aquinas needs both halves at once: (i) a real composite "humanity" through which Adam's loss can be propagated, and (ii) a primary subject within each agent (the essence of the soul) where the disorder properly resides. Without (i), original sin collapses into mere imitation, which Paul has already ruled out (Romans 5). Without (ii), the doctrine cannot explain why every power is affected even though no power has yet acted.

The contemporary statement keeps both halves. Without a real supra-individual entity that humans constitute, intergenerational transmission of structural distortion has no proper subject. Without a primary site in the individual where the inherited tilt is realized, the doctrine cannot survive contact with developmental biology. Both halves are exactly what contemporary work on collective substance and on developmental priors is now in a position to supply.

## Resource

Two convergent moves from the C2A2 network supply the reframe.

The first is **Stump's collective-substance hylomorphism**, recorded in PRS-14 of the Stump-tradition wiki (the 2025 Maritain lecture "What are we?" in *Religious Studies*). Stump argues that mirror-neuron coupling, neural synchrony, and shared affective-cognitive substrate provide the empirical anchor for treating supra-individual wholes — peoples, communities, the church-as-body-of-Christ — as genuinely formed composite entities, not aggregates. PRS-16 extends this to traditions themselves. This is the closest contemporary move to what Aquinas's Q.83 requires: humanity as a real hylomorphic composite with Adam as its formal principle. The reframe drops the juridical-inheritance reading and keeps the metaphysical one.

The second is the **Levin / Friston / Fredrickson tri-axis on inherited developmental priors**. Levin's morphogenetic-pattern transmission (bioelectric scaffolds inherited through development), Friston's account of priors as developmentally laid down by sensory-affective history, and Fredrickson's broaden-and-build research on caregiver-mediated attachment patterns together describe four pre-volitional inheritance channels in any newborn agent: genetic, epigenetic, bioelectric, and affective-relational. None of these is fully volitional for the child; all are inherited from the supra-individual entity (family, community, lineage, species) that PRS-14 names as a real composite. This is Aquinas's "generation, not imitation" specified at empirical resolution.

To these the network adds **Wright** on Romans 5, where Adam-typology is read not as crude juridical-inheritance but as a structural-representative account: Adam stands for the species in the same way Israel stands for the nations in the Pauline corpus. Wright preserves the universality of the bias while shifting its register from forensic to vocational. And **Rohr** on the *first-half-of-life* developmental tilt: the necessary ego-formation in early life is itself the perspective-limitation that contemplative practice later loosens — original sin reframed as the structural starting condition of any limited perspective rather than a juridical stain.

## Solution (proposed, speculative where speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response:

1. *(Metaphysical, mid-evidence)* Humanity is a real supra-individual composite with form-persistence across generations, in the technical sense PRS-14 supplies. The doctrine of original sin does not require a juridical legal-fiction; it requires a real corporate substance through which structural distortion can be transmitted by generation. PRS-14's empirical anchor (collective-neuroscience findings on mirror-neuron coupling) is mid-strength; the metaphysical extrapolation is speculative but principled.

2. *(Empirical, well-supported)* The bias every agent inherits is realized through four well-evidenced channels: genetic (heritable variation in stress-response and attentional systems), epigenetic (developmental imprinting of caregiver stress and trauma), bioelectric/embodied (Levin-style pattern inheritance), and affective-relational (Fredrickson-style attachment dynamics). Each is independently empirically grounded; their joint contribution to the universal pre-volitional tilt is parsimonious and testable.

3. *(Theological, aligned with Wright and Rohr)* The primary subject of the bias in the individual is the agent's *initial generative model* — the priors-and-meta-priors with which it begins predictive engagement with the world. This maps cleanly onto Aquinas's "essence of the soul" as the site of disorder: not any single power, but the formal-causal organization from which all the powers operate.

**Difference from Aquinas, explicitly.** Aquinas's account requires a privation: the *loss* of an original gift (original justice) that rectified the soul's powers under the higher. The mind-monist reframe does not need to posit a historical prelapsarian gift to do the work. The structural starting condition of any perspective-limited agent already includes the bias toward narrower attention, fear-weighted priors, and self-protective closure. What contemplative tradition and the resurrection-shaped vocation supply is *the broadening*, not the *restoration* of a prior state. This is a real divergence: Aquinas's account is historical-juridical (a loss to be restored); the contemporary reframe is structural-developmental (a limitation to be relaxed). Both preserve the universality, the pre-volitional character, and the genuine need for outside help.

## The central-theme thread

The Q.82–83 locus is where the project's central claim about *individuation-by-perspective-limitation* meets the question of inheritance. Each agent is individuated by the perspective-limitation it inherits — and that inheritance is not optional; it is constitutive of being this particular agent at all. The "tilt" that classical theology names original sin is just the inevitable consequence of perspective-limitation propagated through a real corporate substance. Loving unity is the goal toward which the limited agent computes — *under* the very inheritance that makes the computation hard. Wright's reading of "salvation" as inclusion-in-the-people-of-God matters here because the response to inherited limitation is not solitary purification but vocational incorporation into a community whose form is being re-ordered. The "fear and trembling" of Philippians is the recognition that the inherited tilt is real and pervasive — and that the computation toward loving unity is genuinely costly because it runs against the grain of one's own developmental priors.
