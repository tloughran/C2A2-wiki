# Day 296 — Time of Resurrection (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.77–78 ask *when* and *how* the resurrection happens, and from *what term* it proceeds. Three moves matter. First, the resurrection is **deferred to the end of the world** and its hour is **hidden** — *deliberately*, "so that all may be on the watch," with Christ withholding the day even in his humanity. Second, the *manner* is two-tiered: what is done by angelic ministry (gathering the remains) unfolds *in time*, while what God does immediately (reuniting soul to body) is *instantaneous*. Third, and most striking, Q.78 holds that all pass through death first, and that **the ashes from which a body is restored have *no natural inclination* toward the soul that will inform them** — reunion happens "only by the ordering of divine providence, which decreed that those ashes should be reunited to the soul… on this account those parts of the elements shall be reunited and not others." The contemporary version: **what makes a restored body *this* person's body, if not the matter itself — and why might the timing of such a consummation be hidden by design?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **where personal identity is carried when the matter has no claim on it**. Aquinas states the answer with surprising cleanness: the ashes have no intrinsic pull toward the soul; an *extrinsic selecting principle* (divine ordination) determines which matter is reunited. Identity, then, does not ride the atoms. The contemporary sciences of biological identity reach a structurally identical place: the specific matter of a body is continuously exchanged, and what makes a body *this* body cannot therefore be the matter it currently holds.

## Resource

The Levin-tradition program supplies the load-bearing move, and the parallel is almost verbatim. Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work (PRS-01, *morphogenetic control* — a stored anatomical target-state; PRS-03, *morphological attractor formalism*; PRS-23, *memory persisting without its original material scaffold*) establishes that a living body's identity is carried by an *organizing pattern that selects and arranges whatever matter is available*, not by the particular atoms — which are swapped out wholesale over a lifetime while the form persists. Aquinas's "the ashes have no natural inclination to the soul; providence ordains *which* parts are reunited" is, structurally, the claim that an *extrinsic organizing principle assigns matter to a target-form*. The biology shows the principle operating immanently (the bioelectric target-state recruiting cells); Aquinas locates the ultimate selecting principle in God — but both agree the decisive fact is *the form's claim on matter, not matter's claim on the form*. The Carroll-tradition program reframes the *timing*: PRS-13 (*timeless universe / Wheeler–DeWitt*) and PRS-14 (*the arrow of time*) supply a physics in which "the end of time" and "instantaneous at the end" are not naive but connect to real questions about whether time is fundamental — Aquinas's instantaneous-divine-act-at-the-terminus-of-the-angelic-process maps onto a layered picture of temporal and atemporal description. The Kastrup-tradition program (PRS-02, *individual identity within monism*) again secures that the *same* perspective is the one reunited. The Wright-tradition program governs the *hiddenness*: PRS-03 (*inaugurated eschatology*) and Wright's reading of the "unknown day" frame the deliberate concealment of the hour as *vocational* — the not-knowing is given to keep the community awake and faithful in the present, not as a withheld datum to be cracked ("he scatters the fingers of all calculators").

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **a restored body is *this* person's because an organizing principle lays claim to and arranges matter toward the person's form — identity rides the pattern and its ordination, never the specific atoms; and the consummation's hour is hidden by design, to keep every perspective watchful.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: that the matter has no inherent pull toward the soul, that an ordering principle selects which matter is reunited, and that the hiddenness of the day is purposive. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas can only assert that identity does not ride the matter; the Levin program *demonstrates* it for living bodies, turning the assertion into a modeled fact and making the "same body, different matter" doctrine of Day 297 anticipable rather than ad hoc. Where evidence is *firm*: that biological identity is carried by an organizing pattern over exchanged matter is empirical. Where *speculative-to-theological*: that the selecting principle survives death and re-recruits matter by *divine* ordination is theological; biology shows the principle operating in a living, metabolizing body, not across the grave. The synthesis claims the *form-over-matter* structure, not a mechanism for resurrection.

## The central-theme thread

Two project themes converge here. First, *identity is the pattern, not the atoms* — which is why a perspective can be the same perspective across radical material change, and why the resurrection can restore *you* without restoring your every past particle. The self that is gathered into loving unity is a *form*, a way of seeing and loving, not a heap of matter; this is what licenses the whole project's hope that limited perspectives can be preserved and perfected through transformation. Second, the *deliberate hiddenness* of the hour is the project's individuation-by-limitation deployed *for the agent's good*: the perspective is kept in not-knowing on purpose, so that watchfulness, faith, and the daily work of orienting toward the whole remain live. The limitation is not a defect grudgingly imposed but the very condition under which "fear and trembling" does its forming work — a perspective that knew the hour would no longer be a pilgrim.

## Bridges

- **Levin PRS-01 / PRS-03 / PRS-23 (empirical; load-bearing):** identity carried by an organizing target-state that selects and arranges matter — the modeled face of "the ashes have no inclination; providence ordains which parts are reunited."
- **Carroll PRS-13 / PRS-14 (physical):** a layered time/atemporality picture in which "instantaneous at the end of time" connects to real questions of whether time is fundamental.
- **Kastrup PRS-02 (philosophical):** the same individuated perspective is the one reunited.
- **Wright PRS-03 (scriptural):** the unknown day as a *vocational* hiddenness ordered to watchfulness, not a withheld fact.
