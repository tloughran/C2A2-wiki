# Day 253 — Eucharistic Minister (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.82) asks who can confect and dispense the sacrament, and on what the efficacy depends. His answer turns on instrumental causality: the priest acts *in persona Christi*, as an instrument through which Christ is the principal agent. Two consequences follow — the sacrament is valid *ex opere operato*, by the work worked rather than by the worker's holiness, so an unworthy minister still confects validly; and the power is a participated, delegated one, not the minister's own. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: how can a finite agent be the locus of an act whose true author is another and larger agency — and why does the act's outcome not depend on the instrument's own state?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how can one act be genuinely performed by a limited agent yet be principally the act of a larger agency working through it, with the outcome robust to the instrument's defects?** Naively, an agent's acts are its own and bear the marks of its quality. Aquinas needs an account on which the act is really the minister's *and* really Christ's, the latter principally, and on which the minister's unworthiness does not corrupt the effect.

## Resource

Friston's program supplies the structural resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, PRS-06 (how individual agents form coherent higher-order wholes without losing autonomy) and the nested-Markov-blanket picture make agency genuinely *multi-scale*: a smaller agent, retaining its own boundary and dynamics, can be enrolled as a component through which a larger-scale agent acts, so that one event is correctly described at two scales at once — the minister's act and the higher agency's act. PRS-04 (goal-directedness within naturalism) lets the higher-scale goal be realized *through* the lower-scale instrument. This is a formal resource for "in persona Christi": not a metaphor of representation but a real composition of agencies, the lower nested within and conscripted by the higher.

Levin gives the empirical hinge for *ex opere operato*. In Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's program, PRS-03 (target morphology) plus the robustness of morphogenetic outcomes shows target states reliably reached *despite* noise, damage, and variation in the substrate — the outcome is governed by the set-point of the larger pattern, not by the quality of the particular cells implementing it. Channel noise (an unworthy minister) does not corrupt the signal when the outcome is anchored in the higher-scale target rather than the instrument.

Kastrup (PRS-09, participatory encounter) keeps the act a participation in the principal agent's reality rather than a transfer of the instrument's own. Rohr (Universal Christ; one presence acting through many) and Wright (apostolic commissioning, the body acting as Christ's) hold the theological *content*. Stump appears as the Thomistic interlocutor on instrumental causality, receiving the multi-scale-agency reframe.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the minister acts in persona Christi as a nested agent through which a larger-scale agency acts, and the sacrament's efficacy is anchored in that higher-scale target, which is why the instrument's own worthiness does not govern the outcome.** This *shares* with Aquinas instrumental causality and the principal/instrumental distinction. It *reframes* "instrument" as a Markov-blanketed sub-agent composed into a larger agency (Friston), and *ex opere operato* as the robustness of a target-anchored outcome to substrate noise (Levin). It *firms up* a premise Aquinas asserted: that genuine multi-scale agency — real lower-level autonomy enrolled in a real higher-level act — is a coherent and increasingly formalized notion. It *marks* that *who* the principal agent is (Christ), and that the act is salvific, is theological; that the analogy of "channel noise" must not trivialize the moral seriousness of an unworthy minister; and that the composition-of-agencies story explains the *structure*, not the *authority*, of the priestly act.

## The central-theme thread

The motif appears here as the dignity and the limit of the finite agent. The minister is a perspective-limited part who does not see or supply the whole, yet the whole acts through him — loving unity realized not by the part's sufficiency but by its enrollment in a larger agency. That the effect does not depend on his worthiness is the consoling face of perspective-limitation: the whole is not hostage to any single limited node. And the call to holiness remains, because the agent included in the larger act is summoned to conform his own prior to it. Salvation as inclusion (Wright) is mirrored in ministry as inclusion: to act for the whole is already to be drawn into it.

## Bridges

- **Friston PRS-06 + PRS-04 (nested agency; multi-scale goal-directedness)** — *formal, developing.* "In persona Christi" as real composition of agencies; sharpest resource.
- **Levin PRS-03 (robust target outcomes through noisy substrate)** — *empirical-in-domain.* Ex opere operato as outcome anchored in the higher-scale set-point.
- **Kastrup PRS-09 (participatory encounter)** — *metaphysical, contested.* The act as participation in the principal's reality.
- **Rohr (Universal Christ; one presence through many)** — *theological / contemplative.* The one acting through the many.
- **Stump (instrumental causality)** — Thomistic interlocutor receiving the multi-scale-agency reframe.
