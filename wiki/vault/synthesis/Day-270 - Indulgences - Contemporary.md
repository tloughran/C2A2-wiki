# Day 270 — Indulgences (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (Suppl. Q.25-26) closes this run with *indulgences*: a remission of the temporal punishment due to sin, drawn from the *treasury* of the superabundant merits of Christ and the saints and applied to a member by the Church's authority, together with the question of *who can grant* them (those holding jurisdiction — chiefly the pope, and bishops within limits). The treasury (the *thesaurus meritorum*) is a common, more-than-sufficient store; the Church, holding the keys, dispenses from it to individuals; the indulgence truly avails before God to the measure of the granting authority and the receiving devotion. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: what is it for a whole to hold a *shared store* on which its members may draw — a surplus accumulated by some and applied to the need of others — and what kind of structure is a resource that meets many members' deficits at once?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how can a collective hold a common resource that exceeds any single member's contribution, such that the whole can apply it to a member who has not himself produced it?** Aquinas's treasury presupposes exactly this — a store filled by Christ's infinite merit and the saints' superabundance, held by the Church, dispensed to the deficient member. The contemporary question is whether there is a principled account of *shared, pooled* resources in a collective agent — and of the peculiar economy in which one source meets many needs.

## Resource

Two programs converge here, and a third concept of Tom's names the structure they share.

Friston's program gives the formal sense of a *pooled* resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work, PRS-06 (multi-agent coherence via mutual modeling) describes how members, by reducing prediction error in one another's models, come to share a single generative model — a common store of expectations, competences, and corrections that no member built alone and on which each can draw. A collective agent of this kind *holds* something its members severally do not: the pooled model is a real, super-individual resource. The treasury of merit, read through this lens, is the limit case of such pooling — a shared store of the whole's accumulated good, held by the collective and applicable to any member coupled into it.

Levin's program gives the biological instance. In Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work, the body's collective morphogenetic field is exactly a *shared informational store*: the pattern toward which the whole organizes is held at the level of the field, and individual cells draw on it — a damaged or deficient region is repaired by signaling from the surrounding field, which carries the target pattern the local cells have lost. PRS-07 (bioelectric reprogramming / normalization) shows the mechanism: the field supplies to a deficient part what that part cannot supply itself. An indulgence has precisely this form — the whole's stored good applied to a member's deficit by the authority that holds the store.

And here the deep structure is one of Tom's own concepts. The *Synergistic Coil* — Tom's term for a configuration in which a single resource solves more than one Problem-to-Solution transition — names exactly the economy of the treasury. The merit of Christ is *one* resource that meets the deficits of *indefinitely many* members; the saints' superabundance is a surplus that, once produced, can be applied across the body without being used up. This one-R-serving-many-Ps structure is the formal signature of the *thesaurus meritorum*: not a ledger in which each member pays his own balance, but a coil in which a single superabundant resource discharges into many distinct needs. (The Synergistic Coil is Tom's concept, with the lineage Kuhn + MacIntyre + Levin-via-James; it is not any tradition's self-description.)

Rohr keeps the treasury from collapsing into accounting. On the Universal Christ, merit is participation in one divine pattern present throughout reality, not a quantity of credit transferred between ledgers; to "draw on the treasury" is to be conformed to the pattern the saints already embody, not to receive a payment. Wright grounds the shared store in the communion of saints and the body of Christ — a single shared life in which the members are knit together, so that the good of one is genuinely available to the others. That the treasury is *supernatural*, that it is filled by Christ's infinite and the saints' superabundant merit, that the keys *dispense* it, and that the indulgence *avails before God*, all remain theological — and contested even within the Christian traditions; the synthesis notes the medieval doctrine without smuggling confidence about its supernatural economy.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **an indulgence is the collective agent applying a shared, super-abundant store to a member's deficit — the communion of saints as a common field (Levin) pooled by mutual modeling (Friston PRS-06), in the one-resource-meets-many-needs structure Tom calls the Synergistic Coil — dispensed by the authority that holds the store (the keys).** This *shares* with Aquinas the treasury as a common superabundant store, its application to the member by the Church's authority, and the limit of granting power to those holding jurisdiction. It *reframes* "the treasury of merit" as a shared resource of the collective whole — formally, a pooled generative model (Friston); biologically, a field that supplies to a deficient part what it cannot supply itself (Levin) — and recognizes its peculiar economy as a Synergistic Coil, one resource discharging into many deficits. It *firms up* that shared, pooled resources in a collective agent are real structure, not bookkeeping fiction, and that "one source, many beneficiaries" is a coherent and instantiated form. It *marks* sharply that the *supernatural reality* of the treasury, the infinite merit of Christ, the dispensation by the keys, and the efficacy *before God* are theological claims — and ones the wider Christian world disputes; the resource explains the *form* of a shared store applied to a member, not the grace such a store contains or whether the medieval economy of temporal punishment obtains.

## The central-theme thread

Indulgences bring the central claim to a quiet climax. The synthesis's governing thought is that each agent is individuated by the limits of its perspective, and that we "work out our salvation in fear and trembling… aware of our limitations and our need for guidance from those who perceive more of the whole than we do, be they human or divine." The treasury of merit is the institutional shape of that very need. It is the *surplus of those who perceived more* — the saints who saw farther into loving unity and lived it superabundantly, and Christ who sees the whole — made available to the members who perceive less. The deficient member cannot, from his own limited perspective and resources, close the gap; the whole holds a store, accumulated by those nearer the unity, and applies it to him. The morphogenetic image is exact: a cell that has lost the target pattern is restored by the field that still carries it. Loving unity, on this reading, is not a resource each agent must mint alone; it is held in common by the body, carried in surplus by those who have advanced toward it, and ministered to those still on the way. That is what it means for salvation to be *inclusion in a people* — the people is a shared field, and its treasury is the perceived-more of its members poured into the perceived-less.
