# Day 228 — Contemporary Parallel: Form-Bearing Matter, and Love as the Active Principle

## Frame

Q.31-32 ask what Christ's body was made *from* and *by what active principle*. The matter: derived from Adam's race (Q.31 a.1, "he came to save the race descended from Adam") and specifically David's line (a.2, "descended from David according to the flesh," Rom 1:3) — with Aquinas reconciling the Matthew and Luke genealogies (royal vs. priestly personality) and noting the mingling of the priestly Levi and royal Judah lines in Mary. A.4 carries a striking claim: by taking flesh from a woman alone, Christ "rounds out" every mode by which a human can come to be — Adam from earth, Eve from Adam, all others from man-and-woman, and now one from woman alone — completing the combinatorial space of human origins. The active principle (Q.32): the *informing of the matter* in Mary's womb by the soul of Christ, "which did not pre-exist the conception but came to be with the conception," is attributed to the *Holy Spirit* — "the love of God as represented personally" — appropriately, because the Incarnation comes "through the love of God," by grace, for our adoption (Gal 4:4-6). Mary "supplied only corporeal matter," not "any human seminal virtue or power." The contemporary version: *what is it for form to come to matter — for a soul to inform a body — and what does it mean that the active principle of this informing is Love?*

## Problem

Two inquiry-problems, both metaphysical. (1) **Form-bearing matter.** The conception questions are a hylomorphism case: the soul *informs* matter, making this matter a human body; matter alone is potency, form is what makes it *be* a particular thing. The problem is whether form is *empirically real* and causally efficacious — whether "the soul informing matter" names a real feature of nature or a medieval abstraction. (2) **The active principle as Love.** Aquinas attributes the informing not to a material seminal power but to the Holy Spirit *as Love*. The problem is whether "love" can be the *active principle* of an organizing event — whether love names anything with the causal-organizing role the doctrine assigns it, or is merely devotional.

On form-bearing matter, the leading move is Levin's morphogenetics. Tom's PRS-form record of Levin (PRS-01, morphogenetic control; PRS-03, the morphological attractor formalism; PRS-06, the continuum from bioelectricity to mind) is the most direct contemporary vindication of hylomorphism in the set: Levin's bioelectric work shows *form* functioning as a real, top-down, causally efficacious organizer of matter — cells navigating toward a target anatomy stored as a bioelectric pattern, *form* shaping which body the matter becomes. "The soul informing matter so that there is a human body" finds, in Levin, an empirical analogue: a form-bearing field imposing organization on otherwise-potent matter, demonstrated in planaria and xenobots. Master CROSS-006 (FEP as a substrate-independent law of self-organization) and Tom's PRS-form record of Friston (PRS-03, morphogenesis as free-energy minimization) give the formalism: the informing of matter can be modeled as a system minimizing the gap between matter's state and a target form — a Lagrangian path toward a morphological attractor. And master CROSS-008 records the philosophical bridge explicitly: *Thomistic final causality provides the framework Levin's morphogenetic teleology requires* — and Tom's PRS-form record of Stump (PRS-04, final causality and modern biology) records the same convergence from the Thomistic side: Aquinas's hylomorphism is not refuted by modern biology but, on this reading, confirmed by it, "teleonomy becoming teleology properly understood." For the *active principle as Love*, Tom's PRS-form record of Fredrickson (PRS-02, love reframed as "an objective, biologically-grounded coupling mechanism among conscious agents") gives "love" a causal-organizing role: love as *coupling* — the mechanism by which agents are bound into coherent higher-order unities — is not sentiment but an objective binding force. That the active principle of the Incarnation is the Spirit-who-is-Love thus reads as the loving-unity telos operating as the *organizing coupling* at the origin of the God-man. The scriptural anchor (Tom's PRS-form record of Wright on the Pauline corpus) is the descent "from David according to the flesh" (Rom 1:3) and "born of woman... so that we might receive adoption" (Gal 4:4-6) — form taking flesh *for* incorporation.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the informing of matter at Christ's conception is a case of form-bearing matter — form as a real, causally efficacious organizer scaling onto potent matter, the way Levin's morphogenetic fields impose a target anatomy (PRS-01/03/06), modelable as minimization toward a morphological attractor (Friston PRS-03, CROSS-006), and philosophically continuous with the very Thomistic final causality the wiki records as framing Levin's work (CROSS-008, Stump PRS-04). And the active principle being the Spirit-who-is-Love means the organizing power at the origin of the Incarnation is *coupling-love* (Fredrickson PRS-02) — the loving-unity telos operating as the binding force that brings form to flesh.**

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps the soul *informing* matter (form making this matter a human body), with matter as potency and form as actuality. It keeps the active principle as *not material* — "no human seminal virtue," the informing attributed to the Spirit. And it keeps the genealogical realism (Adam's race, David's line) and the "rounding out" of the modes of origin.

*Where it reframes.* Aquinas's hylomorphism rests on Aristotelian metaphysics; the contemporary reframe supplies *empirical* warrant that form is real and causally efficacious (Levin) and a *formalism* for the informing (free-energy minimization toward an attractor). And it reads "the active principle is Love" not as devotional gloss but as a claim with a coupling-mechanism analogue (Fredrickson) — love as the objective binding that organizes.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* Hylomorphism and the informing-by-the-Spirit are theological-metaphysical (Aquinas's frame). Form as real, top-down organizer of matter is *empirical as biology* (Levin's data) but applied to the Incarnation by analogy — medium. The free-energy formalism is formal. Love-as-coupling is empirical *as physiology* (the coupling signatures are measured) but speculative in its application to the conception. The Pauline anchors are scriptural (Wright, strong).

## The central-theme thread

The project writes from "form as empirically real and scaling (Levin)" as one of its convergence-pillars, and holds that loving unity is the telos toward which all of this computes. Q.31-32 let both pillars surface at the *origin* of the Incarnation. The informing of matter — form coming to flesh — is the hylomorphic event the project's Levin-pillar was built to vindicate: form is not an abstraction laid over inert stuff but a real organizer, scaling from bioelectric pattern to anatomy to, the project ventures, mind. And the attribution of the active principle to the *Spirit who is Love* places the loving-unity telos not at the end of the story but at its *beginning*: the organizing power that brings form to the flesh of the God-man is Love — coupling, the binding force that the whole project identifies as the telos of every limited perspective. There is a quiet elegance in a.4's "rounding out" of the modes of origin: the Incarnation completes the combinatorial space of how a human can come to be, as if the whole, in becoming flesh, fills in the one remaining cell of the table — the limited form of human origin made complete by the one who contains all forms. Form-bearing matter, organized by Love, is the central theme operating at the molecular threshold of the Incarnation.
