# Day 163 — Adjuration & Hymns (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas pairs two questions that look unrelated. Q.90 treats adjuration — calling another agent (a fellow human, a demon, even an irrational creature) by the divine name to bind it to some action. Q.91 treats praise of God with lips and song. The placement is not accidental. Both are *invocations of the unlimited witness* turned in different directions: adjuration aims the invocation outward at another agent, praise aims it inward as self-modulation. Aquinas notices something about song in particular — we praise God with our lips not because God needs information but because *we need the act on ourselves*.

Under mind/conscious-realist monism, the contemporary version of the question is: *what does it mean for a perspective-limited agent to use the unlimited witness as a modulation handle — outward as compulsion of another limited agent, inward as transformation of its own state?* Aquinas reaches for the reverence of the divine name and the stirring of devotion. The contemporary parallel reaches for predictive entrainment (Friston), the embodied prosody of praise (McGilchrist), the positivity-resonance literature on group song (Fredrickson), and the New Testament's startling use of *hymns* as primary christological literature (Wright).

## Problem (P)

The problem is the **state-modulation problem**. A limited agent cannot will herself into a state she is not in. She cannot decide to feel devotion the way she decides to lift her arm. The problem is not new in the Summa — it is the same difficulty that the whole tradition on the passions and the affections circles — but Q.91 surfaces it cleanly. *How does the agent, from inside her current state, change her own state?* The same problem, mirrored across the membrane of self, becomes Q.90: *how does the agent invoke a binding on another that her own words alone cannot deliver?* The two articles are the inward and outward face of one perplexity.

## Resource (R)

The C2A2 paradigm supplies four converging moves.

**Friston** supplies the formal core: vocalization, especially patterned vocalization, is a form of precision-weighted self-prediction. When the agent sings, she generates a strong proprioceptive and acoustic prediction; the resulting closed-loop tightens the generative model around the content she is voicing. Habash's intuition that *we praise God for our own sake* is the active-inference principle: the act of formulating "the Lord is good" *aligns the agent's generative model with the content*. The Aquinian claim that song "moves a heart to resolution" is now mechanistic.

**McGilchrist** supplies the hemispheric reading. Music engages the right hemisphere more deeply than language; the right hemisphere is the hemisphere of holistic reception, openness, and embodied presence. Augustine's intuition — quoted by Aquinas — that song stirs the faint-hearted to devotion is McGilchrist's principle that the right hemisphere's mode of receiving is the mode in which the agent is most permeable to what exceeds her current frame.

**Fredrickson** supplies the empirical anchor for the collective case (see her positivity-resonance work and the line of group-vocalization studies). Communal song produces measurable physiological coupling — heart-rate variability synchronization, oxytocin elevations, shared affective trajectories. The Aquinian intuition that praise can stir devotion *in our neighbor as well* is empirically real and measurable. The same dynamics underlie the modulation effect that adjuration aims for in Q.90: a speech-act delivered with the right charge in the right collective field has real entraining power on another agent.

**Wright** supplies the New Testament's strongest evidence that early Christianity *knew this*. Wright argues — in *Paul and the Faithfulness of God* and across his work — that the earliest christology is found not in propositional treatises but in hymns: Philippians 2:6–11, Colossians 1:15–20, the prologue of John, fragments in 1 Timothy 3:16 and Ephesians 5:14. The earliest church did not first decide what Christ was and then set it to music. It *sang* what Christ was, and the singing was how the community came to know it. This is the Aquinian point about Q.91 at the strongest possible historical resolution: praise is not ornamental theology, praise is theology's primary mode of installation in the praising body.

**Kastrup** supplies the metaphysical reading. If mind is ontologically primary and individuated by perspective-limitation, then resonance — entrainment of one limited mind by another, or by the patterned voicing of the unlimited — is not a metaphor for something more fundamental. It *is* the more fundamental. Hymn-singing is the dissociated alters of the One Mind being brought into measurable coupling.

**Rohr** completes the move: in the contemplative tradition song is the first form of contemplation, the form available to those who have not yet learned silence. The transition Habash notes at the end of the episode — that perfect prayer is silent infused contemplation transcending sound — is Rohr's progression from cataphatic to apophatic.

## Solution (S, proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: *adjuration and hymn are two directions of a single resonance-modulation protocol*. Outward (adjuration), the limited agent invokes the unlimited as guarantor of a binding her own credibility cannot supply. Inward (hymn), the same invocation tightens the agent's own generative model around the content voiced — and across the collective, couples the singers physiologically into a shared affective field. The medieval virtue of religion gathered both under one virtue because they are one mechanism with two valences.

**What it shares with Aquinas:** the recognition that praise is *for our sake*, not for God's information; that song moves the heart; that the passions, well-formed, serve the moral life rather than oppose it (against the Stoic refusal); that mature prayer transcends song into silence.

**Where it reframes:** Aquinas grounds the effect in the reverence due to the divine name and the natural responsiveness of the soul to music. The contemporary parallel grounds the effect in measurable precision-weighting (Friston), hemispheric mode-shift (McGilchrist), and inter-bodily resonance (Fredrickson) — all converging from different evidence streams on the same phenomenon Aquinas was tracking.

**Where evidence is firm vs speculative:** the neurobiology of group song is strong empirical territory. The active-inference reading of vocalization-as-self-prediction is formal and accumulating empirical support. The hemispheric reading is suggestive-clinical. Wright's hymn-as-primary-christology reading is strong within New Testament scholarship. The Kastrup metaphysical reading is speculative-integrative and depends on the larger monist program.

## The central-theme thread

Notice how cleanly the adjuration/hymn pair instantiates the central theme. The limited agent cannot, from inside her perspective, change her perspective. Her words alone cannot bind another agent whose perspective is also limited. So she invokes — outward and inward — the unlimited witness. Adjuration says: *what I cannot bind, the One who sees the whole can bind through my words*. Hymn says: *what I cannot transform in myself, the One who sees the whole reshapes through the praise I voice*. Both are perspective-limited acts that route the work of transformation through an anchor that exceeds the limited perspective. Salvation worked out in fear and trembling — inclusion in the people united with God — proceeds in part through this resonance-modulation loop, in which limited singers and limited speakers participate in the loving unity that is computing them.

## Where this leaves us

Tomorrow's question — superstition — turns from *rightly-ordered invocation of the unlimited* to *wrongly-ordered invocation*. The same machinery we have just described can be aimed at the wrong target, by the wrong means, or with the wrong model of the cosmos. Aquinas's three species (idolatry, divination, observances) all *use* the same resonance-modulation loop we have described — they just route it through false addresses. Watch for the structural pattern that *the mechanism is invariant; the question is the ontology of the target*.
