# Day 254 — The Sacrament of Penance (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (III Q.84) opens the treatise on Penance, which is at once a *sacrament* and a *virtue*. As sacrament it has matter and form; as the act of the penitent it has three integral parts — contrition (sorrow with purpose of amendment), confession (truthful disclosure), and satisfaction (the repairing act) — ordered to the remission of sins committed after baptism. The deep description is *the restoration of a disturbed order*: sin disorders the relation between agent and God, and penance returns it. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: what is it for an agent that has strayed from its proper order to detect, disclose, and repair the deviation — and what kind of process is the three-part penitential structure?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **how does a goal-directed agent that has departed from its proper state register the departure and return to it?** A system with a preferred configuration needs a way to sense deviation, represent it, and act to reduce it. Aquinas names the proper state (right relation to God), the deviation (sin), and the return (penance in three parts). The contemporary question is what mechanism realizes "return to a disturbed order" in a perspective-limited agent.

## Resource

Friston's program is the leading resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, the Free Energy Principle (PRS-01) — and, as a reconstruction rather than a mature result, PRS-04 (the organism as active modeler) — describes agents as maintaining themselves near preferred, low-surprise states by minimizing prediction error: deviation generates an error signal, and action and belief-revision drive the system back toward its preferred set-point. Read through this, penance is *error-correction returning the system to its preferred states*, and its three parts map onto a control loop with striking exactness: **contrition** is the registration of prediction error against the preferred state (sorrow *is* the felt error signal plus the commitment to revise); **confession** is the explicit representation and externalization of the deviation, making the error model-available and shared; **satisfaction** is the corrective action that actually reduces the divergence. PRS-14 (how disordered states form, persist, and resist *direct* correction) explains why repair is structured and effortful rather than a wish — misaligned priors do not yield to mere intention, which is exactly why penance has integral *parts* and not just a sentiment.

Rohr supplies the depth-content. In Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr's program, PRS-05 (the mechanism by which the false self becomes the true self — contemplative transformation, not just its endpoint) frames penance as the very process of that transformation: not the punishment of the false self but its dying-into the true. Levin (PRS-03, the morphological-attractor formalism) gives the image of a system actively re-approaching its set-point after perturbation — a formal modeling language for target-form seeking, and it should be read as that rather than as an experimental result about restoration. Wright grounds the *content*: in Tom's PRS-form record of Wright's program, PRS-05 (salvation as incorporation into the people among whom God comes home, rather than the relocation of souls) makes repentance a return to covenant membership — restoration of a real relation rather than a private mood.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **penance is the agent's error-correcting return to its preferred state — contrition the error signal, confession the explicit error-representation, satisfaction the corrective action — by which a strayed perspective is re-aligned toward the loving-unity attractor.** This *shares* with Aquinas the "restoration of a disturbed order" frame and the three integral parts. It *reframes* the parts as a control loop under active inference (Friston) and as the false-self-to-true-self transformation (Rohr). It *firms up* a premise: that goal-directed systems detect and reduce deviation from preferred states is a formal, well-developed account, lending the threefold structure functional rationale. It *marks* that the *preferred state* here (right relation to God) and the agency of grace in the repair are theological; that "error-correction" must not reduce sin to mere mismatch — the moral and relational weight is not captured by the formal loop; and that the analogy illuminates the *mechanism* of return, not its *forgiveness*, which is given, not computed.

## The central-theme thread

The motif appears as re-alignment. The agent is individuated by the limits of its perspective; sin is a deviation in which the limited perspective pulls further from the whole, and penance is the resource-bounded computation by which it turns back toward loving unity. The threefold loop is precisely the work of an agent that *cannot* see the whole correcting its course toward it under guidance — felt error (contrition), shared disclosure (confession), and amending action (satisfaction). "Working out salvation in fear and trembling" is this very loop, run not once but as the ongoing labor of a perspective re-including itself in the whole it has strayed from.

## Bridges

- **Friston PRS-01 (the Free Energy Principle)** — *formal, well-developed (High in the register).* Penance as error-correcting return; the three parts as a control loop; sharpest resource.
- **Friston PRS-14 (symptom-formation as Bayesian self-trapping)** — *High in the register.* Why misaligned priors resist direct correction, and so why repair has integral parts rather than being a wish.
- **Friston PRS-04 (the organism as active modeler)** — *stated separately because its standing differs: the entry is marked (Implicit) at Confidence Medium — the network's reconstruction, not the mature standing of the active-inference literature it draws on.* The goal-directed framing of the loop.
- **Rohr PRS-05 (false-self to true-self transformation)** — *theological / contemplative.* Penance as the mechanism of transformation, not punishment.
- **Levin PRS-03 (the morphological-attractor formalism)** — *formal, biologically motivated — a modeling language, not a measured result.* Active re-approach to a set-point after perturbation. The empirical, material-continuity half of Levin's target-form work sits at PRS-82, which this day does not lean on.
- **Wright PRS-05 (salvation as incorporation into the people among whom God comes home, not relocation of souls)** — *scriptural, high within method.* Repentance as restored membership: a real relation re-established, not a private mood.
- **PRS-26 in the Stump-tradition wiki** (`traditions/stump/prs_triplets.md`) — *theological / philosophical-psychological.* Moral transformation as cooperative causality: the agent's natural powers are genuinely operative while being elevated and directed by grace working within them, so the turn is neither self-effort alone nor imposed from outside. Searching the whole of that register — all 35 numbered entries — for contrition, confession, satisfaction, penance, penitent, repentance, absolution, remission, and venial returns nothing: the register carries no penitential entry at all, so PRS-26 is the nearest anchored resource rather than an approximate match to a closer one. It supplies the account of how a natural power can be genuinely the agent's own while being moved by grace; it does not supply the control-loop reading of the integral parts, which comes from Friston.
