# Day 236 — Contemporary Parallel: How the One Act Works Through Many Agents

## Frame

Yesterday closed with a warning: Day 236 would need to handle agency at multiple scales without flattening it. Aquinas obliges immediately. Q.47 asks *who* brought about the passion and answers with a crowd: Christ was slain by others, yet was the cause of his own death by not preventing it; he died out of obedience, which requires two wills in one person; the Father delivered him up, yet there is only one divine will; the Gentiles executed what the rulers handed over; and the rulers knew he was the Messiah promised in the law but not the mystery of his godhead. Q.48 then asks *how* the one event works, and answers five ways at once: merit, atonement, sacrifice, redemption, and efficiency — closing with the stone-and-stick image of instrumental causality: "Christ's humanity is the instrument of the Godhead... therefore all Christ's actions and sufferings operate instrumentally in virtue of his Godhead for the salvation of men."

The contemporary question: **how can one event be the act of many agents at many scales without any of them being erased — and how does an act at one node change the state of every node incorporated with it?**

## Problem

The inquiry-problem common to both frames is **causal attribution in nested agency**. The medieval version: if the Father delivered Christ up, are the executioners absolved? If Christ caused his own death, is it suicide? If the humanity is an instrument, is it a puppet? Aquinas threads each needle by refusing single-scale attribution — the same event is voluntary obedience at one level, judicial murder at another, eternal love at a third, and the truth of the event is the *stack*, not any layer alone. The contemporary version is the standing problem of multi-scale systems: when a behavior is produced jointly by molecular machinery, cellular goal-directedness, organismal intention, and group dynamics, the question "which level really acted?" is malformed. What is needed — in both frames — is a principled way to attribute agency at each scale on its own terms.

## Resource

**Cognitive attribution across scales.** Tom's PRS-form record of the Levin program carries the closest formal resource: PRS-09, a principled framework for attributing cognition at whatever scale the goal-directedness actually operates, and PRS-03, the morphological attractor formalism — a target state encoded in the system toward which development reliably converges, such that intervention often works not by *constructing* the outcome but by *removing the barrier* that blocks convergence. The empirical base (bioelectric pattern repair, regeneration toward stored targets) is in-domain and strong; its use here is structural analogy, marked as such.

The analogy earns its keep at Q.48 a.1, the article the episode rightly flags as strange: "from the beginning of his conception Christ merited our eternal salvation; but on our side there were some obstacles whereby we were hindered from securing the effect of his preceding merits. Consequently, in order to remove such hindrances, it was necessary for Christ to suffer." Salvation is not manufactured at Calvary; it is *already merited*, and the passion removes what blocks limited perspectives from an attractor already in place. That is precisely the logic of barrier-removal toward a stored target — and it quietly reorders the whole satisfaction debate: the question is not how suffering generates a good, but why the blockage is on *our* side and what it costs to clear it.

**Atonement as union.** PRS-05 in the Stump-tradition wiki (suffering permitted as a condition for the deepest union love seeks) supplies the contemporary philosophical reading of Q.48 a.2's definition — atonement as offering what the offended one loves *more* than he detested the offense. On the union account, the superabundance is not arithmetic overpayment but the disclosure of a love that exceeds the breach, which is what makes reunion rather than mere acquittal possible. The Wright-tradition record (PRS-05, salvation as incorporation; the PROP-2026-05-31-002 dispatch) holds the corporate frame ready: those saved are "all those incorporated in him" — Aquinas's own phrase in a.1 — and incorporation is what next day's mystical-body article will make explicit.

**Action at the interface.** The interface program (Hoffman-tradition wiki) gives the reframe a way to say what "instrument of the Godhead" means without puppetry: what acts in spacetime is the rendering of an agent whose reality exceeds the interface. The instrumental humanity is not less real for being instrumental — the stick really moves the stone — but the act's full description lives at more than one level of the stack.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

**(a) Shared with Aquinas:** one event, many true agents; attribution distributed without dilution — the executioners fully culpable, the obedience fully free, the Father's delivering-up fully loving; salvation operative by merit already complete and obstacles genuinely removed; the humanity a true instrument of a principal cause it does not rival.

**(b) Reframed:** where Aquinas secures the stack with the metaphysics of the hypostatic union and divine simplicity, the monist reframe states the general principle those doctrines instantiate: agency is real at every scale at which goal-directedness is real, and the scales are nested, not competing. The two wills of Q.47 a.2 are the limit case of every agent's situation in this project's frame — a limited perspective whose freedom consists in aligning, or refusing to align, with a more comprehensive will that contains it without erasing it. And the deicide verdict of a.5-6 is perspective-limitation with moral weight: the rulers saw the signs, knew the Messiah, and chose not to see further. Culpability tracks not what an agent could not see, but the limitation it *elected to keep* — which is why the common folk are excused and the rulers are not. Salvation-as-barrier-removal then says: the attractor (loving unity, merited from conception) stands; what the passion does is clear, at the costliest possible price, the obstruction that limited perspectives had built between themselves and it.

**(c) Evidence strength:** the multi-scale competency framework is formal and empirically grounded in its own domain; its application to Q.47-48 is structural analogy, not derivation. The union reading of atonement is serious contemporary philosophy, theological in force. The interface gloss on instrumentality is speculative metaphysics consistent with the program's published case. The crucifixion is historically secure; that it *works* as merit, atonement, sacrifice, redemption, and efficient cause is theological confession, and the five-fold "how" should be read as Aquinas's own admission that no single causal frame exhausts the event.

Difference from Aquinas made explicit: Aquinas multiplies causal vocabularies (merit, satisfaction, sacrifice, redemption, efficiency) to honor the event's excess; the reframe proposes that the excess is what nested agency *always* looks like from inside one layer, and that the passion is the maximal case — the act in which every scale of agency in the created stack, from betrayal to empire to obedient human will, is taken up into the act of the unlimited perspective without one freedom being overridden.

## The central-theme thread

The central claim holds that conscious agents are individuated by the limitations of their perspective, and that learning to obtain loving unity from each perspective is a computational task. Q.47 is that claim rendered as a courtroom: every agent in the passion acts from a bounded view — Judas from his, Pilate from his, the rulers from a view they deliberately kept bounded — and the one agent whose view is unbounded acts *through* their bounded acts without forcing any of them. The fear and trembling of Philippians 2:12 belongs exactly here: we work out our salvation inside a stack we cannot see the top of, guided by those who perceive more of the whole than we do, responsible precisely for the seeing we refuse.
