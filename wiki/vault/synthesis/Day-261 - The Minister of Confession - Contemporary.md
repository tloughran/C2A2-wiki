# Day 261 — The Minister of Confession (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas (Suppl. Q.7–8) treats the *nature* of confession and its *minister*. Following Augustine, confession "lays bare the hidden disease by the hope of pardon" — the penitent voices the sin to the priest, who knows and absolves it by the power of the keys. Confession is an act of virtue, and its minister acts not on his own authority but on Christ's delegated power. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: what does *disclosure* do for a perspective-limited agent's repair, and what is it for one agent to absolve by an authority that is not its own?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **why must the deviation be made explicit and externalized, and how can the corrective authority be exercised by a delegated agent?** A purely internal recognition of error might seem to suffice; Aquinas insists on *voicing* it to a minister. The question is what externalized disclosure accomplishes that private acknowledgment does not, and how the loosing power is genuinely the minister's act and yet Christ's.

## Resource

Friston's program is the leading resource for the disclosure half. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, confession reads as the *explicit representation and externalization* of prediction-error: the hidden deviation, kept private, is harder to model and correct; voiced into a shared field, it becomes model-available, jointly represented, and therefore correctable. "Laying bare the hidden disease" is exactly the move from a latent, unarticulated error to an explicit one that the larger system (penitent-plus-minister) can act on. For the minister half, PRS-06 (how individual agents form coherent higher-order wholes without losing autonomy) gives the power-of-the-keys its structure: the priest is a nested agent through whom a larger agency acts — the same multi-scale composition developed on Day 253.

McGilchrist supplies the relational condition. In Tom's PRS-form record of McGilchrist's program, PRS-06 (traditions transmit through embodied, relational practice, not bare propositions) frames confession as disclosure *to a person* within a held relationship, not a data-dump — the embodied, attentive presence of the minister is part of what makes truthful self-disclosure possible.

Rohr (PRS-05) reads confession as the surfacing of the false self so that it can be transformed rather than hidden. Wright grounds the *content*: absolution as real re-inclusion in covenant community, exercised through delegated authority, not a private therapeutic act.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **confession is the externalizing of a hidden deviation into a shared field where it becomes correctable, and the minister looses it as a nested agent through whom a larger authority acts.** This *shares* with Aquinas the definition of confession as laying bare the hidden for pardon, and the delegated (keys) structure of the minister. It *reframes* disclosure as making prediction-error model-available and jointly representable (Friston), within a relational context that enables truthfulness (McGilchrist), and the minister as multi-scale nested agency (Friston PRS-06). It *firms up* the premise that externalized, shared representation aids correction — a structural feature of joint inference, not a bare claim. It *marks* that the *absolving authority* and what absolution effects are theological; that "shared field" must not reduce the sacrament to mere catharsis; and that the resource explains the *function* of disclosure and delegation, not the *forgiveness* conferred.

## The central-theme thread

The motif appears as the move from private limitation to shared correction. A perspective-limited agent harbors a deviation it cannot fully see or repair alone; confession brings that hidden state into a relationship where another, who sees more, can help loose it. This is the project's claim enacted in miniature — the limited perspective needs "guidance from those who perceive more of the whole than we do, be they human or divine." Voicing the hidden is the finite agent's act of seeking inclusion through the very acknowledgment of what separated it.

## Bridges

- **Friston (confession as externalized error-representation; PRS-06 nested agency)** — *formal, well-developed.* Disclosure makes the deviation jointly correctable; minister as multi-scale agent.
- **McGilchrist PRS-06 (relational/embodied disclosure)** — *humanistic, well-evidenced.* Truthful confession depends on a held relationship.
- **Rohr PRS-05 (surfacing the false self)** — *theological / contemplative.* Disclosure as condition of transformation.
- **Wright (absolution within covenant; delegated authority)** — *scriptural, high within method.* The content of the loosing.
