# Day 259 — Continual Contrition (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas, in the Penance material the Supplement resumes (Suppl. Q.2–3), treats contrition — sorrow for sin with the purpose of amendment — and the question of its duration and renewal. Contrition is not exhausted in a single moment of remorse; the penitential disposition is *ongoing*, a sorrow to be renewed and sustained rather than spent once. The contemporary version under mind / conscious-realist monism: what is it for an agent to hold a *continual* corrective orientation — not a one-time error correction but a persistent, course-keeping signal?

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem: **is corrective sorrow a discrete event or a sustained signal?** A single contrition would correct once and lapse; continual contrition is the standing readiness to register and reduce deviation across a life. The question is what kind of process is *persistently* corrective rather than episodically so.

## Resource

Friston's program is the leading resource. In Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's program, PRS-04 (goal-directedness) and active inference describe an agent that does not minimize prediction error once and stop but *continuously* monitors and revises its model against its preferred states. Continual contrition maps onto a persistent prediction-error signal driving sustained model revision: the penitent keeps the divergence-from-the-good in view as an ongoing input, not a closed episode, and the disposition is the standing sensitivity that keeps correcting. The strength of this resource needs stating precisely: the continuous-correction claim itself rests on the Free Energy Principle (PRS-01), which the register carries at High confidence, whereas the goal-directed agent-modeler framing this day actually leans on is PRS-04, marked (Implicit) at Confidence Medium — the network's reconstruction of Friston's position rather than a mature result. The formal machinery is well developed; the particular entry doing the work here is not the entry that carries that weight.

Rohr supplies the depth-shape, across two records that should not be collapsed. In Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr's program, PRS-02 (the order-disorder-reorder pattern, from *Falling Upward*) is what gives continual contrition its rhythm: the reorder that follows genuine disorder runs deeper than the order it replaced, so the recurring passage through failure is a passage rather than a relapse. PRS-05 (the mechanism of contemplative transformation) adds what happens to the *self* in that rhythm: the wounded pattern is redemptively re-read rather than erased, so the true self is continually re-emerging. Together they give the non-dual form: not chronic self-punishment but a recurring falling-and-rising. Continual contrition is the contemplative tradition's name for a sustained, gentle, course-keeping awareness — which guards against reading "continual" as anxious scrupulosity.

Wright grounds the *content*: Philippians 2:12 — "work out your own salvation in fear and trembling" — is the scriptural charter of exactly this ongoing posture, with salvation as continuing inclusion in the people united with God, a relation kept rather than a verdict banked.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **continual contrition is the agent's persistent corrective signal — sustained model revision toward the good — taking the contemplative shape of ongoing falling-and-rising rather than one-time remorse.** This *shares* with Aquinas the claim that contrition is a renewed, enduring disposition. It *reframes* "ongoing sorrow" as a persistent prediction-error signal driving sustained revision (Friston) and as the non-dual rhythm of continual transformation (Rohr). It *firms up* the premise that course-keeping is sustained rather than episodic — a structural feature of active-inference agents. It *marks* that the *object* of the sorrow (sin against God) and the agency of grace in sustaining the disposition are theological; and that the formal "persistent signal" must be read through Rohr's gentleness, lest continual contrition collapse into the very scrupulosity the contemplative tradition warns against.

## The central-theme thread

This day states the project's governing scripture directly. "Working out salvation in fear and trembling" (Phil 2:12) *is* continual contrition: the perspective-limited agent, aware it cannot see the whole, keeps a standing corrective orientation toward loving unity, sustained across a life rather than achieved at a stroke. The continual character is the signature of finitude — a perspective that fully saw the whole would have no further course to correct; the ongoing sorrow is the felt form of a limited agent computing its way, under guidance, toward a unity it does not yet possess.
