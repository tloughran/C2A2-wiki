# Day 190 — Beauty & Disgrace (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas closes this stretch with the two integral parts of temperance — the parts a temperate life *requires*. Shamefacedness (Q.144) is "fear of a base action," the recoil from disgrace. Onestum (Q.145) — Aquinas keeps the Latin, distrusting "honesty," "nobility," "decency" — is the positive pole: the spiritual *beauty* of a life whose conduct is well-proportioned to reason and reason to God. Shame pushes the agent back from the disgraceful; onestum draws it forward toward the beautiful. The contemporary version of the question, under mind / conscious-realist monism: *what is the social signal that regulates an agent away from disgrace, and what does it mean for a life to be "beautiful" — to shine clearly?*

## Problem

Two linked inquiry-problems. The first is **social self-regulation by attestation**. Aquinas is precise that shame is not just about being bad but about being *seen* as bad: "honor and disgrace denote the attestation, the witness from another." We are more shamed before those "more closely connected with us" and "people of the better sort" — those whose judgment is both most *accurate* and most *consequential* (Q.144 a.3). The question is how another mind's assessment becomes a regulatory force inside this one. The second is a **metaphysical** problem flagged by the text itself: onestum is identified with *beauty*, and beauty is defined (via Dionysius) as the convergence of "clarity and due proportion" (a.2). Spiritual beauty is conduct in which "the idea of man" — "what God had in mind when he made man" — shines clearly through. What is it for a finite perspective to become *transparent* to the whole it expresses?

## Resource

For social self-regulation, the leading resources are the Fredrickson- and McGilchrist-tradition wikis (CROSS-003's attention cluster and CROSS-005's love-as-fundamental sit directly underneath). Shamefacedness is the negative-valence pole of a fundamentally *intersubjective* regulatory loop. The Fredrickson program models love as positivity resonance — a measurable biophysiological coupling between agents, mediated by vagal tone and neural synchrony (PRS-06, love as a universal physical phenomenon). Shame is the same coupling running with reversed sign: the agent's physiology tracks the (anticipated) attestation of connected others and down-regulates the behavior that would rupture the coupling. Aquinas's observation that the attestation of "those among whom we live" and of "the better sort" carries the most weight is, in this frame, a claim about which couplings are most load-bearing for the agent's model of itself. McGilchrist's attention-as-ontological-act (PRS-05) supplies the mechanism by which another's regard is *constitutive*, not merely informational: to be attended to is to be partly disclosed, so being seen-as-disgraceful is a real modification of the self, not just data about it.

For the beauty problem, the metaphysical guardrail points to the Hoffman- and Kastrup-tradition wikis. Aquinas's beauty = clarity + due proportion is startlingly close to interface theory read in reverse. Hoffman's program holds that a perceived form is an *interface* — and an interface can be more or less transparent to what underlies it. Onestum, "the idea of man shining clearly through" the well-proportioned life, is a perspective that has become *transparent to the whole it expresses* — an interface tuned not for the usual fitness-distortion but for clarity. Kastrup's analytic idealism gives the same move its ontology: the individuated agent is a dissociated alter of mind-at-large; spiritual beauty is the alter become diaphanous, letting the larger mind show through with minimal distortion. Disgrace, on both readings, is the opposite — the interface dimmed and opaque, "the light of reason dimmed," the whole no longer legible in the part.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: shamefacedness is the social-attestation arm of self-regulation — an intersubjective coupling that steers the agent away from rupturing its most load-bearing relationships — and onestum is *clarity*: the state of an individuated perspective that has become transparent to the whole it expresses, so that "the idea of man" shows through it.

*What it shares with Aquinas.* The synthesis preserves his subtle ranking of shame: it is *not* a virtue in the perfected agent (who is "above" the fear because the relevant failures are no longer live options for it), but it is genuinely good and useful in the ordinary agent as a regulatory passion (a.1, a.4). That gradeation — shame as scaffolding that the mature agent outgrows — is exactly what a developmental account of self-regulation would predict, and the synthesis should keep it. It also keeps the deep identification onestum = virtue = beauty, and the claim that beauty is clarity-plus-proportion.

*Where it reframes.* "Attestation" becomes the regulatory signal of an intersubjective coupling (positivity resonance with reversed sign); "people of the better sort" becomes the agents whose models are both most accurate and most consequential for the self. The metaphysically interesting reframe is on *beauty*: Aquinas's "idea of man shining through" becomes the transparency of a limited interface to the whole — clarity as low-distortion disclosure of mind-at-large through an individuated perspective. This is where the central theme and the medieval text touch most directly: the simple whole is "seen" precisely where a perspective's distortions are minimized. Beauty is what partial transparency to the whole looks like from inside the interface.

*Evidence strength, honestly.* The shame-as-social-attestation reading rests on real empirical work in social regulation and positivity resonance (medium-high), though the precise mapping to Aquinas's shamefacedness is interpretive. The beauty-as-interface-transparency reading is frankly metaphysical and speculative — offered as the most fruitful contemporary parallel, not as a result. Onestum = virtue = beauty is philosophical-theological.

## The central-theme thread

This article gives the project's metaphysics its aesthetic face. The central claim is that when the limitations of a perspective are removed, the simple whole is seen and every limited perspective is contained in it. Onestum is the partial, in-via version of that: a life in which enough distortion has been cleared that the whole begins to show through — a perspective on its way to transparency. Disgrace is the opposite vector, the perspective contracted and dimmed (yesterday's "childish vice" that "dims the light of reason"). Working out salvation in fear and trembling is, on this reading, the slow clearing of an interface toward clarity — and shame is the early-stage social signal that keeps the clearing on course. Wright's register: human beings are image-bearers made for *glory* — Aquinas's "clarity" is the Pauline doxa, the creature reflecting and refracting the Creator; salvation is the image restored to clear-shining within the covenant people. Rohr's: the Universal Christ is the pattern present in all things, and onestum is that pattern made legible in a single life — clarity as the part transparent to the cosmic whole.

## Bridges

- **Shamefacedness ↔ social-attestation self-regulation** — the Fredrickson-tradition wiki (positivity resonance, PRS-06); CROSS-005. *Why:* shame is the reversed-sign pole of the intersubjective coupling that binds agents. Evidence: **empirical, medium-high** (mapping to Aquinas is interpretive).
- **Attention as ontological act ↔ being-seen as constitutive** — the McGilchrist-tradition wiki, PRS-05; CROSS-003. *Why:* another's regard partly *discloses* the self, so disgrace is a real modification, not mere information. Evidence: **phenomenological, medium**.
- **Beauty-as-clarity ↔ interface transparency** — the Hoffman-tradition wiki (interface theory). *Why:* onestum is a perspective tuned for clarity rather than fitness-distortion — transparent to the whole. Evidence: **metaphysical, speculative**.
- **Spiritual beauty ↔ the diaphanous alter** — the Kastrup-tradition wiki (dissociation/idealism). *Why:* gives beauty-as-clarity an ontology — the individuated alter letting mind-at-large show through. Evidence: **metaphysical, speculative**.
- **Clarity ↔ glory / image-bearing** — the Wright-tradition wiki and the Rohr-tradition wiki. *Why:* Aquinas's "clarity" is the Pauline doxa and Rohr's cosmic pattern made legible in a life. Evidence: **theological**.
