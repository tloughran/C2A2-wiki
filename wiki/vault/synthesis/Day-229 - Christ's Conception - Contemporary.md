# Day 229 — Contemporary Parallel: The Whole-Seeing Perspective at the First Instant

## Frame

Q.33-34 complete the conception. Q.33: Christ's body was "perfectly formed and animated in the first instant" — unlike the gradual development Aquinas, following Aristotle's embryology, posited for humanity generally (the body "successively formed and disposed for the soul," receiving "first an imperfect soul... afterwards a perfect soul"). For Christ, "on account of the infinite power of the agent," the body "was perfectly disposed instantaneously," receiving "a perfect form, that is the rational soul," at once. Habash flags that Aquinas's general account here reflects a biology "we do not hold today." Crucially (a.3), the flesh was *assumed in* its conception, not conceived first and assumed later — else "it would be its own person," missing the hypostatic union. Q.34 then crowns the conception with the Day-220 keystone restated at the threshold of the human life: from the first instant Christ was *sanctified*, had the *use of free will*, could *merit*, and was a "perfect *comprehensor*" — "hardly was he conceived in the womb of the mother of God when he began to enjoy the beatific vision" (*Mystici Corporis*; cf. 1 John 3:2, "we shall see him as he is"). The contemporary version: *can one mind already behold the whole at the very first instant of inhabiting a limited, just-begun body — and what is meritorious free choice from that same first instant?*

## Problem

Two inquiry-problems. (1) **Whole-vision at the threshold of limitation.** This is the wayfarer-and-comprehensor figure (Day 220) pushed to its origin: a perspective that *already* beholds the divine essence (comprehensor) at the *very first instant* of inhabiting a developing human body (the most limited, just-conceived condition). The problem is the simultaneity at its sharpest — the whole-vision and the most-limited embodiment coinciding from instant one, without fragmenting into two subjects (a.3's worry). (2) **Form complete from the start.** Aquinas's "perfectly formed in the first instant" sits against his own gradualist embryology for humanity generally — raising the question of whether form is assembled piece by piece or present-as-whole from the beginning. (3) **Free agency from the first instant.** Q.34 a.2-3: Christ had the *use of free will* and could *merit* from conception — limited agency, under grace, computing toward the good from the very start.

## Resource

For whole-vision-at-the-threshold, the resource is the same one that carried Day 220. Tom's PRS-form record of Kastrup (PRS-02, individuation as a dissociated point of view) gives the structure: one consciousness can inhabit a genuinely bounded, located perspective *while remaining the whole* — dissociation-without-fragmentation. Applied to the first instant, this is exactly the comprehensor-in-the-just-conceived-body: one mind beholding the whole (mind-at-large) while occupying the most limited embodiment, without splitting into two persons (answering a.3). Master CROSS-013 (conscious agents integrate into a higher-order unified agent, with McGilchrist's split-brain data as the empirical leg) and CROSS-001 (substrate-independence of consciousness) support the coherence of one agent spanning the comprehensive and the limited at once — and CROSS-001's substrate-independence speaks directly to the claim that the rational soul (mind) is fully present from the first instant rather than waiting on bodily maturation. For *form-complete-from-the-start*, Tom's PRS-form record of Levin (PRS-03, the morphological attractor) reframes Aquinas's gradualism productively: the target form is present *as an attractor* from the beginning, guiding the matter — so "perfectly formed in the first instant" is less anomalous than it sounds, in a frame where form is the whole pattern that organizes the development rather than a property assembled stepwise. (This also vindicates Habash's correction: modern embryology does not need Aristotle's serial ensoulment.) The theological-scriptural anchor is the beatific vision from conception (*Mystici Corporis*) and 1 John 3:2 ("we shall see him as he is"), with Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr (PRS-03, the Universal Christ) keeping the comprehensor cosmic in scope and Tom's PRS-form record of Wright (PRS-05) keeping the end in view: the comprehensor at the first instant is already the one who will incorporate the many.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **the comprehensor-from-the-first-instant is the wayfarer-and-comprehensor keystone (Day 220) restated at the origin of the human life — one mind already beholding the whole at the very first instant of inhabiting the most limited, just-conceived body, held together by dissociation-without-fragmentation (Kastrup PRS-02, CROSS-013): the whole consciousness inhabiting a bounded, located point of view while remaining the whole, without splitting into two persons. The "perfectly formed in the first instant" is legible where form is the whole pattern present as an attractor from the start (Levin PRS-03), not assembled stepwise — which also vindicates the correction of Aquinas's Aristotelian embryology. And meritorious free choice from the first instant is limited agency, under grace, computing toward the good from instant one.**

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps the simultaneity — beatific vision *and* genuine limited embodiment at once, from the first instant. It keeps a.3's safeguard: the flesh is assumed *in* conception, one person, not a human person later absorbed. And it keeps free will and merit present from the start.

*Where it reframes.* Aquinas secures the instant-perfection by "the infinite power of the agent" within Aristotelian embryology; the contemporary reframe offers a *structure* for the simultaneity (dissociation-without-fragmentation) and a *frame* in which form-complete-from-the-start is natural rather than anomalous (form as attractor). It also explicitly accepts Habash's flag: the gradual-ensoulment biology is corrected, and the corrected picture (form present as organizing whole) actually *fits* the Christological claim better.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* The perfect-formation, the assumed-in-conception safeguard, and meritorious-free-will are theological/formal. The beatific-vision-from-conception is theological (with 1 John 3:2 scriptural). The dissociation structure and conscious-agent integration are speculative-formal, with CROSS-013's empirical leg — medium. The form-as-attractor reframe is empirical-as-biology applied by analogy.

## The central-theme thread

This is the keystone figure (Day 220's comprehensor-and-wayfarer) pressed to its limit and found to hold: a perspective that *already beholds the whole* at the very first instant of inhabiting the most limited possible embodiment. If the central theme is right that "the simple whole is seen and every limited perspective is contained in it," then Q.34 is the most extreme demonstration — the whole-vision and the most-limited perspective coinciding from instant one, in one person. And Q.34's free will and merit from the first instant make the same point about agency: the limited perspective, even at its origin, freely turns toward the good and "computes" toward loving unity under grace ("at the very moment of Christ's conception, his act of willing God merited"). The project holds that obtaining loving unity from each perspective is "a computational task, limited by the resources we perceive" — and here is the limit case where the resource-limitation is maximal (a just-conceived body) yet the whole-vision is already complete, because this is the bridge figure in whom the comprehensive and the limited are *given together*. The corrected embryology is itself on-theme: form is not assembled piecemeal but present as the organizing whole from the start — a small instance of the project's wager that the whole is prior to and contains its limited unfoldings.
