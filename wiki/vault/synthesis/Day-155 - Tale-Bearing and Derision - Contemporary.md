# Day 155 — Contemporary Parallel: Tale-Bearing, Derision, and the Bond as Substrate

## Frame

Q.74 distinguishes *tale-bearing* (*susurratio*) from backbiting: the backbiter aims at the victim's reputation, the tale-bearer aims at *severing friendships* between the victim and the listener. Q.75 takes up *derision* (*derisio*): humiliating another by mocking their faults. Both are verbal sins against neighbor; both target distinct goods. Aquinas cites Aristotle (Ethics IX) — friendship is *the supreme external good* — and concludes that tale-bearing is among the gravest verbal sins because the bond it destroys is among the highest of created goods.

## Problem

The inquiry-problem, in both frames: *some verbal injuries target not the victim's standing in general but specific relational structures (the dyadic bond, the self-image) that are themselves load-bearing for the agent and the community — so what is the moral structure of these targeted attacks?* Aquinas's answer turns on the *good attacked*: friendship (a supreme external good) and personal honor (a real debt of esteem). The contemporary problem is to recover the same weight by recognizing that the bond and the self-model are not abstract goods but the cellular forms of the very substrate the central claim names as the telos.

## Resource

Tom's PRS-form record of Fredrickson's work makes the bond a measurable thing. PRS-03 (positivity resonance) is shared positive emotion plus biobehavioral synchrony plus mutual care — the empirical operationalization of friendship. PRS-08 (broaden-and-build) shows the cumulative effects: a long-running bond builds enduring resources (perspective-taking, resilience, expanded action-repertoire). Tale-bearing is the targeted attempt to dissolve exactly that — the susurratio is *specifically calibrated* to lower each partner's prior on the other so that the next attempt at resonance fails. Aquinas's medieval intuition (friendship as supreme external good) lands cleanly on the empirical work: positivity resonance is one of the most consequential modifiable variables in human flourishing.

Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work supplies the collective-cognition reading. PRS-30 makes friendship a dyadic small-scale collective cognition — two agents deeply coupled, sharing high-mutual-prior-weight information, the smallest unit at which loving unity is actually performed. PRS-02 (defection) names tale-bearing as defection at this layer: the third party exploits their position to corrupt the priors that hold the dyad together.

Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work formalizes derision. PRS-06 (shared generative models) makes mocking a kind of *enacted prior-installation* — the mockery is the public performance of a lowered self-model, and the witnesses' mirroring is what gives the performance its installation force. PRS-04 (intention as model-target): the derider's target is precisely the victim's self-model and its standing in the witnesses' perceptions; the wound is to the *prior the victim and others now carry of the victim*. PRS-16 (precision-weighting) explains why the public setting matters — the witnesses' assent up-weights the false prior.

Tom's PRS-form record of Stump's work supplies the philosophical ground. PRS-11 (second-personal / Franciscan knowledge) makes friendship the paradigm second-personal relation: the knowledge two friends have of one another is irreducible to third-party propositional content. Tale-bearing attacks that very mode of knowing. PRS-14/15 (corporate substance) extends the social ontology: the friendship is a real dyadic node of the community, with its own causal weight in the polity's life.

Tom's PRS-form record of McGilchrist's work names the cognitive form of derision. PRS-04 (right-hemispheric attention to whole persons) makes mockery's mode left-hemispheric reduction: the victim is collapsed to a stereotyped trait worth ridicule, the rest of the person screened off. Derision *is* a particular cognitive failure mode performed in public — the reduction of a whole person to a target.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The proposed contemporary parallel preserves Q.74-75 substantially, with the metaphysics of friendship and personal honor given empirical scaffolding. *Preserved*: tale-bearing is among the gravest verbal sins because the bond it attacks is the supreme external good (Q.74 a.2 — Aquinas's citation of Aristotle); derision is grave because it injures the victim in the very mode (self-image, dignity) that affects how they hold themselves in every subsequent encounter. *Reframed*: friendship is dyadic deep coupling — positivity resonance plus shared generative models plus second-personal knowing; tale-bearing is the targeted defection at that layer (corrupting both partners' priors on each other); derision is enacted-prior-installation of a lowered self-model with witness amplification. *Extended*: the empirical work on positivity resonance and on cognitive modes (Fredrickson; McGilchrist) gives Aquinas's medieval intuitions measurable correlates and clarifies why these specific verbal sins are structurally graver than general verbal injustice.

Measure against Aquinas: convergence on substance and on the relative weighting (tale-bearing as among the gravest verbal sins; derision as proportionate to the standing of the one mocked). The contemporary frame intensifies the readings by giving the goods attacked (bond, self-model) empirical substance — friendship is not a sentimental good but a measurable substrate of flourishing; dignity is not abstract but the prior an agent carries of themselves that shapes every subsequent perception and action.

## The central-theme thread

Tale-bearing and derision are the central claim in cellular form. The dyadic bond is the smallest scale at which loving unity is actually performed: two limited perspectives so well coupled that each holds the other in their model with the weight the central claim names as the telos. Tale-bearing targets that coupling directly; derision targets the perspective that the coupling is between. Both are precision-tuned to defeat the substrate the central claim depends on. The Day-154 reading of *fama* attacks extends here: the bond and the self-model are where loving unity is grown, and the verbal sins that target them are the central claim's negative image at the most consequential scale.
