# Day 161 — Tithes & Oblations (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Aquinas's question, surface-level, is canonical: are Catholics under precept to give to the church, what to give, and to whom does it go? Beneath the surface is something more general — a structural question about *resource flow inside a multi-agent system whose agents perceive only locally*. Each Christian household sees its own table; the parish sees its altar; the diocese sees its territory; Rome sees the universal church. None of these levels can fund itself from inside its own perceptual horizon. Tithe-talk is the medieval church's protocol for *price-of-coordination* between scales of perception.

The contemporary version of the question, under mind/conscious-realist monism, is: *given that we are individuated by the limits of our perspectives, by what mechanism do limited agents pool resources so that the larger agent they jointly constitute can act at all?* Aquinas reaches for natural law plus positive ecclesial determination. The contemporary parallel reaches for multi-scale agency and the cooperation-or-defection dynamic that sustains it (Levin), the conditions under which coupled agents constitute a single inferring collective (Friston), and the Pauline doctrine of the church as one body whose members are not independent (1 Cor 12; 2 Cor 8–9).

## Problem (P)

The problem is the **scale-coordination problem**. A limited agent — call her Maria — can only see her own household's needs. Maria's parish can only see what its envelopes contain on Sunday. The diocese can only see what its parishes remit. The universal church can only see what its provinces remit. At every level, the agent above is dependent on flows from agents below who cannot themselves see the level-above's needs. If everyone optimizes locally, the higher-scale agent starves and the whole composite ceases to function as a single body.

This is the *same* problem Aquinas is solving in Q.86–87 — and the *same* problem cells solve when they release morphogens into the bioelectric gradient that organizes the tissue, that a worker solves when she pays taxes, that a node in an active-inference hierarchy solves when it sends precision-weighted prediction errors upward. The medieval ecclesiastical answer (precept + positive law fixing quantity and recipient) is one particular implementation of a general resource-gating protocol.

## Resource (R)

The C2A2 paradigm contributes three converging moves drawn from the wiki's PRS-form records of these programs.

**Levin** supplies the central biological case (see PRS-02 in the Levin-tradition wiki on cancer as *defection* from the body's cooperative morphogenetic field, and PRS-07 on the bioelectric layer that coordinates cell behavior; PRS-04 supplies the wider basal-cognition framing that extends goal-directed processing across scales). Tissue-level form depends on cells releasing resources — bioelectric, biochemical, mechanical — that they could "selfishly" keep. Aggregate form fails when those releases fall below the threshold the higher-scale agent needs, and the failure has a name in the program: the defecting cell. Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work treats cooperation-or-defection as a *generic feature of any composite agent*, not a curiosity of biology.

**Friston** supplies the formal frame (see PRS-06 in the Friston-tradition wiki on how individual agents couple into coherent higher-order wholes *without losing autonomy*, and PRS-22 on the condition under which a collective is one active-inference agent rather than merely a crowd — that it sustains a group-level Markov blanket). The composite exists only where the parts sustain the shared boundary. The further step — reading the tithe-fraction as belief-mass or precision released upward, a fixed share of local capacity paid as the price of membership in a larger inferring agent — is this synthesis's own extension of that formalism, not a claim the register carries; it is offered as an analogy whose appeal is that it makes the medieval fraction look like a coordination parameter rather than a tax.

**Wright** supplies the scriptural anchor. The Pauline collection (2 Corinthians 8–9, Romans 15:25–27) is the New Testament's central financial argument, and Wright reads it as covenantal *koinōnia* — the Jerusalem church and the Gentile churches are *one body*, and a body whose members do not share resources is not a body. Wright's primary work here is *Paul and the Faithfulness of God* and his commentary on 2 Corinthians; his point is that Paul is not asking Corinth for charity but reminding them what membership *is*.

**Rohr** completes the theological move: in the Universal Christ frame, every act of giving across the membrane of self is participation in the cosmic Christ-body that includes the giver, the gift, and the recipient. Tithing is sacramental in the sense that it makes visible what is already ontologically the case.

## Solution (S, proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: *tithing is the symbolic protocol by which a perspective-limited agent participates in the larger composite agent it is already part of*. The agent cannot see the whole; the protocol substitutes for the seeing.

**What it shares with Aquinas:** the recognition that natural law commands *that* one returns to God of one's own, while the particulars are determined positively. The shape "render of your own to the larger order" is invariant; the percentage and the recipient are determined by the body in question.

**Where it reframes:** Aquinas treats tithing chiefly as an act of the virtue of religion ordered to *honoring God*. The contemporary parallel reframes it as an act of *participation in the larger agent that one is computationally a part of*. These are not opposed — for a monist ontology where God is the unlimited inclusion of every perspective, honoring God and participating in the larger body are two descriptions of one act. But the reframe surfaces a different evidence base. We can measure, with Levin and Friston, the cost of *not* releasing resources upward in a multi-scale system. We can measure, with Fredrickson's positivity-resonance research, what happens to the giver's own well-being when she gives habitually inside a recognized community. The reframe puts religion-as-resource-protocol on the same evidence floor as the rest of multi-scale biology.

**Where evidence is firm vs speculative:** the multi-scale dynamics in tissue and in active inference are empirically and formally robust. The neurobiology of prosocial giving (Fredrickson and successors) is empirical but tradition-thin. The claim that *theological tithing is the same protocol* is integrative-speculative — a hypothesis about what the medieval form was tracking, not a controlled experiment.

## The central-theme thread

Notice Q.86 a.3 — whether a man may make oblations of whatever he lawfully possesses: an oblation is *not* lawful when it sabotages a prior natural-law duty to parents or dependents. The agent's perspective on her own household is *protected* even from the demands of the higher body. This is not an exception to the perspective-limitation principle — it is the principle made explicit. Each limited perspective has a domain in which it sees more clearly than the levels above; the loving unity toward which the whole computes does not require obliterating the local view, it requires the local view to give *only what it can spare without becoming derelict to what is directly entrusted to it*. The corban reproach in Mark 7, which Aquinas cites, is the Lord himself protecting limited-perspective duties against ecclesial overreach. Salvation — inclusion in the people united with God — is worked out in fear and trembling precisely because the agent has to judge, from inside her limits, what is hers to release and what is hers to keep.

## Where this leaves us

Tomorrow's question — vows and oaths — turns from *what we render across the membrane of self* to *what we promise across the membrane of time*. The same perspective-limitation logic will reappear: a present self cannot see the future self that will be asked to honor the promise. The vow is the protocol by which the limited present binds the limited future for the sake of the loving unity neither alone can compute. Tithing pools resources across agents; vows pool resources across the temporal stages of one agent. Watch for the same structural move at the new scale.
