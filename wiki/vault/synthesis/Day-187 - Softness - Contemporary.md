# Day 187 — Softness (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Yesterday named the competence; today Aquinas names how it breaks. Softness — *mollities*, what the translation also renders "effeminacy" — is the vice opposed to perseverance (Q.138 a.1). Its mark is precise: a soft thing "gives way to even gentle pressure." Not the forceful blow that overwhelms patience, but the small, ordinary friction of delay and discomfort. Aquinas pairs it with its opposite excess, pertinacity (a.2): the stubborn clinging to a course reason now says to drop. Then he turns to the *gift* of fortitude (Q.139) — the Spirit-given firmness that natural courage cannot reach. The contemporary version of the question: *what are the characteristic failure modes of a self-regulating agent, and what does it take to exceed the limits of its own resources?*

## Problem

The inquiry-problem is **regulatory failure on both sides of the mean**. An agent maintaining a trajectory can fail by yielding too easily (softness) or by failing to yield when it should (pertinacity). Aquinas's etiology of softness is the load-bearing detail: it arises "either by a frailty of temperament" or — the interesting one — "through being accustomed to pleasure, which then becomes all the more difficult to bear the absence of because of that custom." That is a closed-loop account of how a regulatory system degrades itself: each indulgence raises the expected baseline, so the *absence* of pleasure registers as a larger and larger error signal, until even gentle pressure is unbearable. Pertinacity is the inverse pathology, and Aquinas roots it in vainglory — clinging "to make a show of one's own excellence." Both are perspective-narrowing failures: the soft agent's horizon shrinks to the immediate discomfort; the pertinacious agent's shrinks to the defense of its own standing.

## Resource

The leading move comes from the active-inference framework recorded in the Friston-tradition wiki, read together with the broaden-and-build program in the Fredrickson-tradition wiki. Aquinas's "accustomed to pleasure" is, in the contemporary register, *runaway precision on appetitive priors*: the system's predicted reward baseline drifts upward with each indulgence, so deviations below it carry inflated precision and dominate policy selection. The agent is not choosing the small pleasure over the great good in a clear-eyed way; its model has been retuned so the small loss *looks* large. The Fredrickson program supplies the affective mirror: just as positive affect broadens and builds in an upward spiral, the habituation Aquinas describes is a downward spiral — narrowing attention, eroding the durable resources (PRS-01's "disconnection and decline"; the broaden-and-build track record on how affective state gates what the agent can attend to). McGilchrist's attention-as-ontological-act (PRS-05 in that wiki) adds that softness is finally a *failure of attention*: the agent stops attending to the whole and lets the fragment — this pressure, this absent pleasure — fill the field.

For pertinacity, the resource is the same precision story turned on the self-model: vainglory over-weights the prior "I am right / I am excellent," so disconfirming evidence cannot update the policy. The Stump-tradition wiki is useful here for keeping vainglory analyzed as a relational distortion — a misvaluing of others' attestation — rather than a mere cognitive bug.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: softness and pertinacity are the two precision pathologies of a self-regulating agent — under-weighting the goal-prior (yielding to gentle pressure) and over-weighting the self-prior (clinging past reason). The gift of fortitude is the claim that the agent can be moved, from beyond its own resources, to a firmness it could not generate.

*What it shares with Aquinas.* The both-sides-of-the-mean structure is preserved exactly: virtue is the regulated middle, flanked by defect and excess. And the synthesis keeps his sharpest empirical insight — that habit is the mechanism of degradation, that "custom not resisted became a necessity" (the line he will quote tomorrow from Augustine) is literally how the loop closes.

*Where it reframes.* "Frailty of temperament" and "accustomed to pleasure" become, respectively, a prior set-point and a learned baseline drift — both tractable in principle, neither requiring a faculty-psychology of the will. The deeper reframe is the gift of fortitude (Q.139 a.1). Aquinas is explicit that natural virtue *suffices* for a man to die for family or country, but that laying down life for an unseen Christ requires a supernatural disposition giving "confidence of overcoming all dangers." Translated into the project's idiom: there are goods a limited perspective cannot compute its way to on its endowment alone, because the relevant evidence (the unseen Christ, the not-yet whole) is not available to that perspective. The gift is input from a perspective that perceives more — exactly the structure the central theme insists on. The fourth-beatitude correspondence (a.2) — fortitude as an *insatiable* hunger and thirst for justice — is the upward-spiral signature: a good that, the more it is approached, the more it draws.

*Evidence strength, honestly.* The habituation-as-runaway-precision reading is formally suggestive but speculative as applied to virtue (medium-low). The downward-spiral / broaden-and-build mirror is empirically supported (medium-high). The gift of fortitude is theological and offered as such.

## The central-theme thread

Softness is what it looks like when an individuated agent's perspective collapses to its own immediate resources and loses the whole. The cure Aquinas names is not more grit but a *gift* — being moved by one who sees and wills more than the agent can. This is "fear and trembling" read from the side of our insufficiency: we cannot self-rescue from the downward spiral, and the loving-unity goal is reached only with guidance from beyond the limited frame. Wright's register: the gift of fortitude is the Spirit empowering faithful improvisation toward a new-creation end the agent has not seen — courage *for* a story still being completed. Rohr's: softness is the first-half-of-life ego protecting its comfort and its standing (vainglory is its other face); the gift is what carries the self through the necessary disorder rather than letting it flee back to a smaller order.

## Bridges

- **Habituation-to-pleasure ↔ runaway precision on appetitive priors** — the Friston-tradition wiki, PRS-04. *Why:* gives a mechanism for Aquinas's "custom makes the absence unbearable" without invoking weakness-of-will as a primitive. Evidence: **formal, speculative**.
- **Downward spiral ↔ softness** — the Fredrickson-tradition wiki, PRS-01 + broaden-and-build track record. *Why:* the affective mirror of broaden-and-build; narrowing attention erodes durable resources. Evidence: **empirical, medium-high**.
- **Attention as ontological act ↔ softness as attentional failure** — the McGilchrist-tradition wiki, PRS-05; CROSS-003. *Why:* the soft agent lets the fragment fill the field and loses the whole. Evidence: **phenomenological, medium**.
- **Vainglory as misvalued attestation ↔ pertinacity** — the Stump-tradition wiki. *Why:* over-weighting the self-prior is a *relational* distortion, not just a stuck posterior. Evidence: **philosophical/theological**.
- **Gift of fortitude ↔ empowerment from beyond the frame** — the Wright-tradition wiki (Spirit, faithful improvisation). *Why:* courage for goods the perspective cannot yet see. Evidence: **theological**.
