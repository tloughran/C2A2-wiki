# Day 295 — The Necessity of Resurrection (Contemporary Parallel)

## Frame

Q.75–76 reach the hinge of the whole Supplement: **why must the body rise**, and **by what cause**. Aquinas's argument is hylomorphic to its core. The soul is *the form of the body*; a separated soul is therefore in an "unnatural," *incomplete* state, and "all things tend toward their perfection," so the soul's desire is unsatisfied until reunited with the body (Savonarola is quoted to the same end). Perfect happiness is unattainable in this life and even in the separated soul, because "a separated soul is not, strictly speaking, a man." Hence *all* rise — good and evil alike — since merit and demerit were earned as embodied persons and must be answered in the body. The resurrection is *not natural*: as the rational soul is created from nothing to inform matter, only God can reunite soul to body; Christ's own resurrection is its cause (equivocal as God, univocal as man, instrumental through his humanity), with angels gathering the remains but God alone reuniting soul to body. The contemporary version: **if the "soul" is the organizing form of an embodied perspective, in what sense is that perspective incomplete without a body — and what would its completion require?**

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem is **whether embodiment is constitutive of a personal perspective or incidental to it**. Aquinas's whole case rests on answering *constitutive*: the human person just is a soul-informing-a-body, so disembodiment is a privation and the person's perfection requires re-embodiment. The contemporary sciences of mind and form face the same fork: is the agent its substrate, a free-floating informational pattern, or — Aquinas's answer — a *form that is real and constitutive precisely as the organization of a body*?

## Resource

This is the metaphysical keystone, so the lead comes from the mind-first and form-first programs. The Levin-tradition program makes hylomorphism *empirical*. Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work (PRS-01, *morphogenetic control* — bioelectric circuits store an anatomical target-state as memory; PRS-03, *morphological attractor formalism* — the body's target form as a basin of attraction; PRS-04, *substrate-independent basal cognition*) gives "the soul is the form of the body" a contemporary referent: an organizing pattern that *is empirically real, causally potent, and constitutive of which body this is* — not a ghost added to inert matter, but the form *by which* the matter is this living whole. On that reading Aquinas's claim that a separated form is in an unnatural, incomplete state is not mysticism but the natural description of a target-state with no substrate currently expressing it: a pattern that "wants" (in the project's resource-bounded sense) re-expression in matter. The Hoffman-tradition program reframes what re-embodiment *is*: PRS-04 (*observer-dependent spacetime*) and PRS-01 (*hard-problem transformation*) treat the body as the *interface* through which a perspective acts and is acted upon — so completion of the perspective requires restoration of its interface, the contemporary gloss being "without the body the soul's knowing is diminished." The Kastrup-tradition program secures continuity of the *who* across the gap (PRS-02, *individual identity within monism*): the same individuated perspective persists to be re-embodied. And the Wright-tradition program carries the scriptural register here: PRS-04 (*resurrection as historical and cosmological*) and *The Resurrection of the Son of God* establish that the Christian hope is *bodily* resurrection — not the immortality of a disembodied soul — exactly Aquinas's refusal to let beatitude rest in the separated soul. Wright's historical case that the earliest Christians meant *bodily* resurrection is the scriptural ground truth under Aquinas's metaphysics.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **a personal perspective is constituted as a form organizing a body; disembodied, it is genuinely incomplete; its completion therefore requires the form to be re-expressed in matter — resurrection, not flight from matter.** What it *shares* with Aquinas: soul as form, the incompleteness of the separated state, the universality of the rising, and the insistence that only a power exceeding nature can reunite form to matter. Where it *reframes*: Aquinas asserts hylomorphism against a Platonizing pull; the Levin program *exhibits* a real, scaling, substrate-constituting form, turning a metaphysical posit into something with an empirical model. Where evidence is *firm*: that biological form is a real, memory-bearing, causally potent organizing pattern is empirical. Where *speculative-to-theological*: the leap from "organizing form" to the *survival* of that form through death (Day 292) and its *re-expression* by divine power is philosophical and theological, not derivable from morphogenesis — Levin's forms are conserved through *biological* turnover, not through the death of the organism. The synthesis claims a *structural analogy* (identity by persisting form, not by retained matter), not that biology demonstrates resurrection. That only God reunites soul to body remains, with Aquinas, explicitly theological.

## The central-theme thread

The project's loving unity is the union of *embodied, perspectival* agents — and this article is where that becomes non-negotiable. The hope is not that limited perspectives dissolve into a bodiless absolute, but that they are *completed as the embodied perspectives they are*. Aquinas's "a separated soul is not, strictly, a man" is the project's refusal of the escape-from-perspective story: the goal is not to stop being a particular embodied viewpoint but to have that viewpoint perfected and gathered into the whole. Re-embodiment is the precondition of the consummated communion of Day 293 — you cannot have a *body* of perspectives without the perspectives having bodies. "Salvation in fear and trembling" is therefore worked out *in the flesh*, and answered *in the flesh*: the same embodied agent that computed toward loving unity under limitation is the one raised to enjoy it.

## Bridges

- **Levin PRS-01 / PRS-03 / PRS-04 (empirical; load-bearing):** form as a real, memory-bearing, substrate-constituting organizing pattern — hylomorphism with a working model.
- **Hoffman PRS-04 / PRS-01 (formal-to-speculative):** the body as the interface a perspective needs to act and know — completion requires its restoration.
- **Kastrup PRS-02 (philosophical):** the same individuated perspective persists across death to be re-embodied.
- **Wright PRS-04 (scriptural; load-bearing):** the earliest Christian hope is *bodily* resurrection, not a disembodied afterlife — the ground truth under Aquinas's "no beatitude in the separated soul."

## Where this leaves us
