# Day 143 — Contemporary Parallel: Prudence at Every Scale, and the Case the Rule Cannot Hold

## Frame

Q.50 takes the *subjective* parts of prudence — its species, distinguished by their ends. Because "different kinds of goods intended make for specifically different kinds of means," there is a prudence of the individual, a *domestic* prudence ordered to the household's common good, a *political* prudence by which subjects govern themselves in obeying their rulers, a *regnative* prudence proper to the ruler who must direct all subjects to the common happiness, and a *military* prudence ordered to the common welfare secured in war. Then Q.51 names the connected (potential) virtues that feed prudence's chief act of command: *eubulia*, taking good counsel; *synesis*, judging well "according to the common rules"; and *gnome*, judging "according to higher principles" when the common rule fails to fit the case. Under the mind / conscious-realist monism this project writes from, Q.50 is prudence scaling across nested agents, and Q.51 a.4 is the moment a learned rule meets a case it cannot hold.

## Problem

Two inquiry-problems. First: *how does practical wisdom scale?* The individual is part of the household, the household part of the city, the city part of the kingdom; Aquinas insists each level needs its own species of prudence, because each has a distinct common good as its end. The problem is to say how the same competency can run at the level of one agent and at the level of a community of agents — and what changes as it scales.

Second, and sharper: *what does judgment do when the rule it has learned does not fit the case?* Aquinas's example is exact. Normally, if a man entrusts you a deposit, you return it on request — this is good judgment by the common rule, *synesis*. But if, between the giving and the asking, the man becomes "a known enemy and traitor of the state," returning his weapon-money would betray the common good. The common rule fails the singular case, and a higher judgment is required. The problem is the limit of every learned rule: it is trained on "the majority of cases" and will eventually meet one it was not trained for.

## Resource

For scaling (Q.50), the resource is agency across levels. On Tom's PRS-form record of Levin's work, cognition and goal-directedness are not confined to one scale but recur — cells compose tissues, tissues organisms, organisms collectives — each level a competent agent navigating its own problem-space toward its own target. Aquinas's individual / household / city / kingdom is the same nesting in the social register: each a perspective ordering itself toward the common good of the larger whole it belongs to. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work formalizes the join: nested Markov blankets, and the multi-agent coherence (PRS-06) by which agents "become a coherent collective agent" by synchronizing their models. Political prudence is precisely this — Aquinas says the subject, "though moved by the commands of others, moves himself by his free will," so that he "directs himself in obeying his superiors": the part computing *for* the whole while remaining a genuine agent. Tom's PRS-form record of MacIntyre's work supplies the human texture (PRS-01): practical reason is constituted within a *polis* and a tradition, not exercised by an abstract agent in the void — which is why Aquinas can treat political and domestic prudence as real species rather than mere applications.

For the rule that fails (Q.51 a.4), the resource is out-of-distribution judgment. *Synesis* judges by the common rule — the learned model applied within its training distribution. *Gnome* is invoked exactly when the case falls outside it: the agent recognizes that its settled rule does not cover this state and reaches to a more universal principle. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston's work (PRS-16) frames the recognition as model-mismatch — the situation whose ambiguity the current model cannot resolve — and Tom's PRS-form record of MacIntyre's work names the larger version of the same event: the *epistemological crisis* (PRS-02), when a tradition's own rules fail and it must reconstruct its judgment from higher resources. The deposit-to-a-traitor case is a crisis in miniature. Tom's PRS-form record of McGilchrist's work locates the capacity to make the move in attention to the living particular that the general rule cannot anticipate (PRS-05): the right apprehension of *this* case, against which the rule is measured rather than merely applied. And the move is structurally the one Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr's work calls non-dual — passing from the letter to the spirit *without collapsing the distinction*, holding the rule and its exception together rather than discarding either. It is also exactly what Tom's PRS-form record of Wright's critical realism (PRS-01) describes: knowing that is perspectival and revisable, tested against the reality of the case rather than read off a rule from nowhere.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The proposed parallel reads the subjective parts as *one practical-reasoning competency instantiated at nested agent-scales* — individual, household, city, kingdom — each a limited perspective computing toward the common good of the whole it belongs to (Levin's agency-across-levels; Friston's nested blankets and multi-agent coherence). And it reads *gnome* as *out-of-distribution judgment*: when the common rule (synesis) fails the singular case, the agent escalates to a higher principle, the letter yielding to the spirit. With Aquinas: the species are real, distinguished by their ends; the connected virtues genuinely feed command; and gnome is a *higher* virtue than synesis, not a license to ignore rules but the capacity to judge rightly where rules run out. Where it reframes: the social scales become nested blankets rather than a hierarchy of substantial communities, and gnome becomes the recognition of model-mismatch rather than the intuition of a separate faculty. Where it stays theological: the deposit case is resolved by *justice* — the common good outranks the private claim — and that ordering is carried by Aquinas's moral framework, not by the formal one.

Evidence, named: the scaling analogy is formal at the social level and empirical at the cellular (Levin), medium overall; the political-prudence-as-part-for-the-whole reading is interpretive but well-grounded; the gnome-as-out-of-distribution reading is a strong formal analogy joined to MacIntyre's epistemological-crisis account; and the substantive resolution of the hard case is theological.

## The central-theme thread

Two threads of the central claim cross here. The first is that loving unity is computed *at every scale*: Q.50 shows the same practical competency running in the individual, the household, the city, and the kingdom — each a limited perspective ordering itself toward a good larger than itself, the part working for the whole it is part of. The project's wager that union is real at collective scales, not only individual ones, is exactly Aquinas's insistence that political and domestic prudence are real species and not metaphors. The second thread is sharper and is gnome's. The central claim says each perspective is incomplete; Q.51 a.4 shows where the incompleteness bites in practice — the rule one has learned from the majority of cases meets the case it cannot hold, and one must reach past the settled model toward a principle one sees only partly. That reach is "working out salvation in fear and trembling" at the resolution of a single decision: acting rightly in the case the rule did not anticipate, by a judgment answerable to more of the whole than the rule contained, and trusting that the higher principle one is reaching toward is really there.

## Where this leaves us

Prudence has been scaled across the nested communities and pressed to the point where its learned rules run out. Tomorrow Aquinas raises the stakes to the limit: the *gift* of counsel, given because "human reason is unable to grasp the singular and contingent things which may occur," so that man must be "directed as though counseled by God who comprehends all things" — and, against it, the species of imprudence. The gnome problem of today (the rule that fails the case) becomes the deepest statement of the project's central claim: a perspective that cannot survey all contingents, needing guidance from one who can.
