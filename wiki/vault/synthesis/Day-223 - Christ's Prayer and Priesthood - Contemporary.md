# Day 223 — Contemporary Parallel: Prayer as Alignment, Priesthood as Union

## Frame

Q.21 asks the strange question of why the one who already beholds the divine essence (the comprehensor of Day 220) would *pray*. Aquinas's definition is the hinge: prayer is "the unfolding of our will to God that he may fulfill it" — and Damascene's, "the raising up of the mind to God." Because Christ has two wills (Q.18), it is becoming for him to pray, "not only in the sense of contemplating his divinity with his human intellect, but also for the sake of unfolding his human will to his divinity in petition." He prays vocally to teach us, to show his humanity, and to show "that what he asked was granted" (the raising of Lazarus, John 11). His prayer "was always heard" when it followed his deliberate will rather than the natural shrinking of the sensitive appetite ("not as I will, but as thou wilt"). Q.22 then takes up the priesthood: the priest (*sacerdos*, "the one who gives the sacred") "stands as a mediator of divine things to man," prays for the people, and reconciles them to God by making satisfaction. Christ is at once *priest and victim* — he offers himself; his priesthood "expiates sin," removing both the stain and the debt of punishment; and it "endures forever," "according to the order of Melchizedek" (Heb 7), a priest "without beginning of days nor end of life." The contemporary version: *what is prayer, when a limited perspective unfolds its will to one who sees more — and what is the priesthood that permanently holds the many in union with the whole?*

## Problem

Two inquiry-problems, both load-bearing for the project. (1) **Why would a perspective that already sees the whole petition the whole?** The puzzle of Christ's prayer is the puzzle of the central theme inverted: ordinarily the limited petition the One who sees more; but the comprehensor *already* sees. Aquinas's answer is that prayer is not primarily about acquiring information the petitioner lacks — it is "the unfolding of the will," the alignment of a will to what is willed by the One, and a *teaching* of how the limited ought to relate to the whole. (2) **What sustains union, once it is achieved?** Q.19 (Day 222) established incorporation into one mystical person; Q.22 asks what *holds* that union — and answers: a *permanent* mediation, a priesthood that "continues forever" and "always lives to make intercession" (Heb 7:25). Loving unity, on the project's terms, is not a one-time event but an ongoing computation; the problem is what agency sustains it.

## Resource

For prayer-as-unfolding, the leading move is active inference. Tom's PRS-form record of Friston describes the agent as an *active modeler* (PRS-04) whose perceiving, reasoning, and acting are unified as "expected free energy minimization" (PRS-10) — that is, as the continual bringing of the agent's own generative model into accord with the way things are and the way things are willed to be. Aquinas's "unfolding of the will to God that he may fulfill it" maps onto this with surprising exactness: prayer is the limited agent's act of *aligning* its generative model to a superordinate model — making its "will" explicit before, and conformable to, the One who sees more. On this reading prayer is not magic petition but the structured act by which a limited perspective seeks and receives guidance — the central-theme posture itself, performed. Tom's PRS-form record of Rohr (PRS-01) supplies the complementary, participatory reading: prayer is non-dual *encounter*, "knowing as participation," not data-transfer — which is why the comprehensor can pray even while already seeing (the prayer is union-in-act, not information-seeking). For the *priesthood* — the permanent mediation that binds the many to God — the resources are the integration and coupling programs from Day 222: master CROSS-013 (conscious agents integrate into a higher-order unified agent, with McGilchrist's split-brain data as the empirical leg) gives the structure of one mediator holding many in one body, and Tom's PRS-form record of Fredrickson (PRS-03, resonance as the scale-bridging coupling mechanism) gives the binding that must be *sustained* rather than achieved once. The scriptural anchors are strong and specific: Christ's high-priestly prayer ("I am praying for them... that they may be one, even as we are one," John 17) is the priesthood and the prayer together, and Heb 7:23-27 grounds the *permanence* — "he holds his priesthood permanently, because he continues forever." Tom's PRS-form record of Wright (PRS-05) names the end: the priest's intercession *incorporates* — salvation as inclusion in the people among whom God comes home. And Rohr's Universal Christ (PRS-03) keeps the priesthood cosmic in scope: the mediation is not parochial but the holding-together of all creation in the divine pattern.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response: **prayer is the limited perspective's act of unfolding and aligning its will to the One who sees the whole — on the active-inference reading, the structured bringing of a generative model into accord with a superordinate model (Friston PRS-04/10), and on the contemplative reading, non-dual participatory encounter (Rohr PRS-01). Priesthood is the permanent mediation that sustains the union of the many in the whole: the one mediator who, having integrated the many into one body (CROSS-013), continually holds them there by a coupling that must be renewed (Fredrickson PRS-03) — "always living to make intercession" (Heb 7:25).**

*What it shares with Aquinas.* It keeps prayer as the *unfolding of the will*, not the informing of an ignorant petitioner — which is why the comprehensor can genuinely pray. It keeps Christ as both *priest and victim*, offering himself, and the priesthood as *expiation* (removing stain and debt). And it keeps the *permanence*: the mediation endures, "according to the order of Melchizedek."

*Where it reframes.* Aquinas grounds prayer in the two-wills faculty psychology and the priesthood in sacrificial satisfaction; the contemporary reframe reads prayer as model-alignment-under-guidance (the central-theme posture performed) and priesthood as the *sustaining* of agent-integration by ongoing coupling. The "always lives to make intercession" stops being only a Christological fact and becomes the general truth that loving unity is maintained, not banked — a computation that must keep running.

*Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.* Priest-and-victim and expiation are theological. The high-priestly prayer (John 17) and the Melchizedek/permanence material (Heb 7) are scriptural (Wright, strong). Prayer-as-model-alignment is speculative-formal (medium) — a coherent analogue, not a demonstration that prayer *is* active inference. The permanent-mediation-as-sustained-coupling reading inherits CROSS-013's empirical leg for the integration claim but is speculative in its application to intercession.

## The central-theme thread

The project says the limited "work out [their] salvation... aware of [their] need for guidance from those who perceive more of the whole than we do." Q.21 shows the perfect instance: even the comprehensor *prays* — unfolds his human will to the One — and in doing so *teaches the limited how to seek guidance*. Prayer, on the active-inference reading, just *is* the central-theme posture in action: a perspective making its will explicit before, and aligning it to, what the One who sees more wills ("not as I will, but as thou wilt"). And Q.22 answers the question of what *sustains* the loving unity the project is computing toward: a *permanent* priesthood, a mediation that "always lives to make intercession." This matters because the project's telos is not a state reached and held automatically but a union that must be continually maintained — the limited perspectives, left to themselves, drift; the priest holds them in the whole. John 17 fuses the two: the high priest's prayer is *that they may be one* — prayer and priesthood are the same act of holding the many in the unity that contains them. The order of Melchizedek, "without beginning of days nor end of life," signals that this holding is not a historical episode but a permanent feature of the way the whole keeps the limited included.
