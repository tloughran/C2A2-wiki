# Day 103 — Capital Vice (Contemporary)

## Frame

Q.84 asks whether one sin can be the cause of another, and answers in two registers. Materially, prior acts produce dispositions (habits) that make subsequent acts of the same kind easier; formally, *covetousness is the root of all evils* (1 Tim 6:10) in the sense that disordered turning toward created goods (avarice broadly understood) is the structural condition out of which the other vices grow. Aquinas then organizes the moral phenomenology under the *seven capital vices*: pride (or vainglory), envy, anger, sloth, avarice, gluttony, lust — each "capital" because it is the head of a family of further disordered acts. Q.85 turns to effects: sin diminishes the natural good without destroying it; original sin removed the rectifying gift of original justice, and actual sins reduce the natural inclination to virtue incrementally rather than absolutely.

The contemporary version of the question is: *what are the characteristic attractor basins into which a perspective-limited agent's predictive model collapses when the broadening practices fail?* If active inference describes the agent as a generative model under resource constraint, and broaden-and-build research describes the affective-cognitive substrate of broad attention, then the medieval seven is a candidate empirical taxonomy of the dynamical pathologies that recur in such systems. The reframe asks whether the same seven still pick out real attractor basins, what unifies them at the dynamical level, and where the evidence is firm or speculative.

## Problem

The shared inquiry-problem across medieval and contemporary frames:

> Disorder in a perspective-limited agent is not arbitrary. It collapses repeatedly into a small number of characteristic patterns, each of which propagates daughter pathologies, and each of which is harder to exit the longer it persists. What are these patterns, why these in particular, and what unifies them as a family?

Aquinas's answer is the *capital* structure: each capital vice is a habitual disorder of one of the soul's powers in respect of a particular kind of object, and from each habitual disorder a characteristic tree of further disordered acts unfolds. Pride is named "beginning" (Sirach 10:13–15) because turning-away-from-God under the guise of self-sufficiency is the most root-deep distortion; covetousness is named "root of all evils" because turning-toward-created-goods is the broadest pattern. The capital-vice tree is the taxonomy; the unifying signature is that each names a stable pattern of misappraisal that the agent's other powers then accommodate.

The contemporary statement does not soften this; it specifies the dynamical signature. Under active inference, the agent's policy-selection is sensitive to precision-weighting on its priors. When attention narrows under stress, threat, isolation, or fatigue, certain priors become hyper-weighted and the agent's posterior collapses around fewer hypotheses. The system loses *metastability* — Friston's term for the healthy capacity to inhabit multiple attractors flexibly. The capital vices, on this reading, are the characteristic attractors a metastability-collapsed agent falls into: pride (rigid self-model), envy (other-as-threat), anger (instant precision on injury-priors), sloth (collapse of policy-energy), avarice (acquisition as anxiety-reduction), gluttony (consumption as affect-regulation), lust (objectification under narrowed attention).

First, **Fredrickson's broaden-and-build research**, together with the *Love 2.0* finding that positivity resonance is an empirically measurable coupling event — the latter recorded as PRS-02 in the Fredrickson-tradition wiki, whose resource line names vagal tone, oxytocin, and neural phase-locking as its objective signatures. The broadening claim itself is cited to Fredrickson's published work rather than to an id: the Fredrickson register has no triplet for attentional scope, and the nearest candidate (PRS-34, on why the capacity to be built declines) is a different claim marked speculative, so no id is forced onto it here. Broaden-and-build's empirical signature is *bidirectional*: positive affect broadens attentional scope and builds personal resources over time; negative affect narrows attention and contracts resources. The capital-vice tree is the catalog of contracted-attention patterns that propagate when the broadening collapses. Empirical strength here is high: the broaden-and-build effect replicates robustly across paradigms; vagal-tone measurement is mid-precision but reliable in trend.

Second, **Friston's active-inference account of metastability** (PRS-13 in the Friston-tradition wiki, paired with CROSS-076 in the network's `cross_program_index.md`, which records that loss of spatial Vmem organization in senescing keratinocytes is "structurally analogous to Friston's metastability collapse" and proposes aging-as-metastability-loss as a single dynamical formalism spanning Levin's bioelectric and Friston's neurodynamic accounts of degeneration). A healthy agent inhabits an attractor landscape with many shallow basins it can move between under shifting context; a pathological agent collapses into a few deep basins it cannot escape. The capital vices, under this reading, are the deep attractor basins that perspective-limited agents specifically fall into — not arbitrary, but predicted by the structure of the model under high-precision threat-priors and narrowed attention.

Third, **McGilchrist's hemispheric account** (PRS-01 hemispheric asymmetry; PRS-05 attention as ontological act). Left-hemispheric closure under stress narrows the field to decontextualized affordances; the perception of the *whole* (right-hemispheric, contextual, relational) falls away. Each capital vice, on McGilchrist's reading, is a characteristic left-hemispheric collapse: pride as self-as-fragment-mistaken-for-self-as-whole; envy as other-as-fragment-mistaken-for-other-as-whole; lust as object-as-fragment-mistaken-for-person; avarice as good-as-quantifiable-fragment-mistaken-for-good-as-relational. Empirical strength on within-individual hemispheric-attention narrowing under stress is high; the cultural-scale extrapolation McGilchrist also defends is mid and contested.

**PRS-09 in the Stump-tradition wiki** (virtue as cooperative causality) gives the contrastive frame — virtues are the broadening attractor states that the Holy Spirit cooperatively constitutes in the agent's natural powers. Vices and virtues are not symmetric: vices are attractor *collapses* the agent falls into; virtues are attractor *expansions* the agent cannot achieve unaided. The asymmetry matters for the central-theme thread.

**Wright** on Galatians 5:19–21 supplies the scriptural pedigree: Paul's catalog of "works of the flesh" is structurally the same family Aquinas inherits, and Wright's reading of the Pauline contrast (works of the flesh / fruit of the Spirit) maps onto the broaden-narrow polarity. On that reading the capital vices are the characteristic shapes the false self constructs to maintain itself under perceived threat — pride the architecture, the others its furnishings.

## Solution (proposed, speculative where speculative)

The contemporary-parallel response:

1. *(Speculative but principled — resting on an empirical base.)* The medieval seven is a candidate taxonomy of the characteristic attractor basins into which perspective-limited agents collapse when broadening practices fail. Two things must be kept apart here. That attention narrows under stress, threat, isolation, and fatigue, and that narrowing contracts the agent's behavioural repertoire, is *empirical and well-supported*. That the resulting patterns sort into these seven, in this arrangement, with these daughter relations, is *speculative*: it is a principled mapping — each capital vice is read as a stable misappraisal pattern under narrowed attention and high precision on threat- or appetite-priors — but no study has tested the seven-fold partition itself, and the taxonomy is inherited from the tradition rather than recovered from the data. The claim is that the grouping is not arbitrary, not that it has been confirmed.

2. *(Formal, mid-evidence)* The unifying signature beneath the seven is *metastability loss* — the agent's reduced capacity to move flexibly between attractor states. The capital-vice tree is the catalog of deep basins that emerge when the attractor landscape is flattened by chronic precision-mis-weighting. This unifies Aquinas's "covetousness is the root" (the broad pattern of object-binding) with "pride is the beginning" (the meta-prior that drives the precision-mis-weighting) in a single dynamical framework.

3. *(Theological, aligned with Wright and Rohr)* Q.85's claim that sin diminishes natural good without destroying it survives as: attractor-basin capture is partial, not total. The agent retains the *capacity* for broadening; it has lost the *facility*. This is what makes the diminution real and the recovery possible — and why outside help (grace, tradition, community, contemplative practice) is needed to restore the metastability the agent cannot recover unaided.

**Difference from Aquinas, explicitly.** Aquinas locates the unifying root in two places — pride as "beginning" (the soul's turning away from God) and covetousness as "root of all evils" (the soul's turning toward created goods). The mind-monist reframe unifies these *under* a third: the loss of metastability that makes the soul prone to both turnings simultaneously. Pride and covetousness are not two unrelated roots; they are two characteristic surface presentations of the same underlying dynamical collapse — high precision on self-model integrity (pride) and high precision on acquisition-priors (covetousness) are both signatures of an attractor landscape flattened by chronic narrowed attention. This is a genuine reframe: Aquinas's *moral* roots become *dynamical* roots, while preserving the structural-classificatory work the medieval account does.

The reframe is also more empirically demanding. Aquinas can describe the seven by appeal to common moral experience; the dynamical reframe predicts specific signatures (vagal-tone collapse, attentional-scope narrowing, precision-weighting shifts in active-inference paradigms) that should be measurable under conditions where the relevant capital vice is operative. This makes the contemporary version *more* testable, not less.

## The central-theme thread

The capital-vice question is one of the cleanest illustrations of *salvation as a computational task under resource scarcity*. Each capital vice is a way perspective-limitation cascades into deeper perspective-limitation: the agent's narrowed attention produces further narrowing, the daughter vices reinforce the parent, the attractor basin deepens. *Loving unity* is the goal; the computation toward it is *exactly* what the capital-vice attractor steals — the broadening of attention, the relaxation of precision, the willingness to take seriously perspectives the threatened self cannot incorporate. "Fear and trembling" is the recognition that one's own current attractor basin may be exactly the kind of capital-vice collapse one is least equipped to perceive — and that the way out is communal. Wright's reading of "salvation" as inclusion-in-the-people-of-God is on point here: the broadening signal that the captured agent cannot generate is supplied by belonging to a community whose practices re-open attention.
