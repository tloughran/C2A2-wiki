# Day 80 — Sorrowful Remedies (Contemporary)

## Frame

Q.38–39 close the affective tract of the Prima Secundae with a remarkable two-part movement. Q.38 catalogues the *remedies* of sorrow: pleasure of any kind, tears, sympathy of friends, contemplation of truth, sleep and baths. Q.39 then asks whether sorrow itself is evil, virtuous, or useful — and at a.4 makes the surprising claim that *the greatest evil is not pain or sorrow but being too evil to sorrow*. The contemporary synthesis recognizes the second part as the more radical: under perspective-limitation, the agent who has collapsed its model so as not to register present evil is in worse condition than the one whose sorrow is acute. The remedies of Q.38 are then read as the *concrete computational resources* by which a limited agent re-opens its model to receive evidence and integration.

## Problem

The shared problem is *how a limited agent recovers from unintegrated sorrow without dissolving the perspective that makes it an agent*. Aquinas catalogs five remedies. Each is a real and ordinary practice — drink something pleasant, weep, sit with a friend, contemplate, sleep, take a warm bath. None requires special grace; all are available across cultures and conditions. The puzzle Aquinas does not fully articulate but the contemporary frame can: *why* does each work, and *what* do they have in common that lets us call them remedies of the same condition?

The contemporary frame supplies the unifying answer: each remedy re-opens the agent's generative model — either by *introducing* new evidence (a friend's perspective, contemplated truth) or by *resetting* the precision-weighting on existing prior models (sleep, somatic comfort, externalization through tears). The remedies share a structural property: they all *re-admit* what the perspective-limited agent had narrowed away.

## Resource

**Fredrickson's program** is the empirical anchor for nearly every remedy on Aquinas's list.

- *Pleasure as remedy* (Q.38 a.1) — PRS-01 in the Fredrickson-tradition record states the mechanism: shared positive experience, deliberately cultivated, raises vagal tone and generates upward spirals of resilience out of a disconnected baseline. Even minor pleasures — warmth, food, music — shift vagal tone out of sympathetic dominance. (*Broaden-and-build* proper is carried on Fredrickson's published theory rather than anchored: it is `wiki.md` PRS-05 only, and `prs_triplets.md` PRS-05 is a different problem.)
- *Tears as remedy* (Q.38 a.2) — emotional disclosure literature (Pennebaker, Frijda) shows that *expressing* affect, including in writing or speech, reduces its physiological load and improves health outcomes over weeks.
- *Sympathy of friends* (Q.38 a.3) — PRS-08 (high-quality listening, sensory connection, perceived safety) and PRS-09/10 (weak-tie connection produces intellectual humility, prosocial belief, civic efficacy). Aquinas's "friends bear the burden with us" is the empirical co-regulation finding: synchronized vagal tone in close emotional contact (Fredrickson Love 2.0).
- *Sleep and baths* (Q.38 a.5) — parasympathetic restoration, HPA-axis return to baseline, glymphatic clearance of stress metabolites. Each is mechanistically substantive.

**Friston's active inference** frames *contemplation of truth* (Q.38 a.4) as the most powerful remedy because contemplation expands the model itself. The agent that can be remedied at all is PRS-04's — a continuous hypothesis-testing system generating, testing and updating predictions; each remedy intervenes on that loop, feeding it new evidence or altering the weight on what it already holds. Sorrow at a present evil is computed against a model of preferred states; contemplating divine truth (Aquinas's frame), the cosmic-Christ pattern (Rohr), or the resurrection-oriented future (Wright) widens the model so the present evil is no longer its terminus. The expected-free-energy calculation changes — PRS-16 records that quantity as one optimization target combining *risk* (divergence of predicted from preferred outcomes) with *ambiguity* (volatility of the predictive distribution): what was unactionable in the smaller model becomes integrable in the larger one. This is the formal home of "in the midst of tribulations men rejoice in the contemplation of divine things and future happiness."

**Levin's bioelectric framework** lets the synthesis name what sleep and baths are doing at the somatic-collective scale. That the body is itself a community of cells is PRS-17, which treats bioelectricity as *cognitive glue* constituting a collective knower above the individual cell. Sustained sorrow disrupts this coherence (Day 79's PNI literature); restorative physical practice (sleep, warmth, vagal stimulation) restores the bioelectric field at the tissue scale — PRS-02's resource, bioelectric coherence returning cells to the body's collective morphogenetic field — which is the substrate of any further psychological recovery.

**McGilchrist** explains *why* contemplation works as Aquinas says — and why it is the most powerful remedy. Contemplative practice cultivates right-hemispheric receptive attention (PRS-11): world-as-gift rather than world-as-objects-to-manipulate, a mode propositional content cannot substitute for, whose loss is an *epistemic* loss. The narrowed left-hemispheric model under which sorrow has become a cage is interrupted by right-hemispheric openness to the larger context — the asymmetry PRS-01 records. The Carthusian remark in Aquinas's text — "frostbitten, hungry, tired, alone, and happy" — is precisely the right-hemispheric receptivity that holds bodily privation within a larger unchanging frame.

**Kastrup's analytic idealism** lets the synthesis articulate what Q.39 a.4 means metaphysically. PRS-02 supplies the base move — one undivided consciousness experiencing multiple dissociated points of view — and PRS-07 the pathological case, where disorder is *dysregulation of the dissociative boundary* rather than brain malfunction. The agent whose model has collapsed to *not register* present evil — the desensitized — is the alter whose dissociative boundary has hardened beyond access to the larger field. The agent in acute sorrow, however painful, is *still in contact* with mind-at-large through the registration of loss. Q.39 a.4's "greatest evil is to be evil" then receives an articulation Aquinas could not have given: the greatest evil is the alter so dissociated from its substrate that it cannot even register the privation. The integrated-sorrow agent has a route home; the desensitized agent does not.

**Wright and Rohr** complete the theological articulation. Wright's lament tradition (Psalms, Lamentations, Romans 8 "the whole creation groans") establishes scripturally that the path to resurrection-hope runs *through* lament, not around it — suffering *inhabited cruciformly* rather than explained (PRS-29). That is Q.39's structure in scriptural key: sorrow is useful without ever ceasing to be sorrow. Rohr's *Falling Upward* makes the same claim developmentally (PRS-02, order-disorder-reorder): the second half of life requires integration of loss into a larger meaning, and the contemplative-non-dual mode (the universal-Christ pattern, PRS-03) is the stable ground that lets this integration happen without collapse.

**Stump's *Wandering in Darkness*** belongs here: her contention that suffering is integrable into second-person love of God (and of other persons) coheres with everything above on its own Thomistic terms. The book makes its case within a classical Thomist metaphysic rather than the monist frame explored here, and the synthesis reads the two as compatible accounts of the same phenomenon.

## Solution (proposed, speculative)

**(a) Shared with Aquinas.** Each of Aquinas's five remedies is preserved as a real, ordinary, effective resource. The unity Aquinas names but does not fully explicate — that pleasure, tears, friends, contemplation, and sleep all remedy *the same* condition — is preserved and given its mechanism. Q.39 a.4's startling claim that the greatest evil is desensitization to evil is preserved and amplified.

**(b) Where it reframes.** Aquinas's "contemplation of divine things" is, in the medieval Thomistic register, the soul's union with a numerically-distinct God. Under conscious-realist monism the same contemplative practice is the alter's re-opening of its dissociative boundary to mind-at-large. This is not Aquinas's exact metaphysic. Wright's *en Christō* (being-in-Christ as participation, not external union) and Rohr's universal Christ (cosmic pattern in which creation participates) are theological articulations that *do* reframe the medieval external-object reading without abandoning Christian orthodoxy. The contemporary synthesis aligns with these. Q.39 a.4's "greatest evil is to be evil" gets a precise mechanism: the dissociation that has hardened beyond access to substrate is the *most* perspective-limited state, and thus the *least* able to compute its way toward loving unity.

**(b2) Where it supersedes.** Q.38 a.1 does not merely report that pleasure assuages sorrow; it explains why, and the explanation is a mechanism rather than a distinction. Following Aristotle, sorrow is driven out by pleasure "by a contrary pleasure, and by any other, provided it be intense," and the argument runs on contrariety — the working image, as the episode puts it, is heat and cold: to drive cold from a room, add heat. That mechanism is retired. PRS-35 in the Fredrickson-tradition wiki reports that affective conditions are distinguished by network *configuration* — participation coefficient across five intrinsic networks, within-module degree within them — rather than by dedicated affect centers, supporting a constructionist account in which emotions emerge from flexible network states. Positive and negative affect are therefore not two magnitudes on a single axis that sum to a residue; they are different configurations of the same networks, and PRS-36 and PRS-37 add that the two do not even behave as a matched pair on the tradition's own metrics. Adding pleasure does not subtract sorrow the way heat subtracts cold. Aquinas's *conclusion* survives intact and is if anything better supported than he could have known — a pleasure of any kind, however ignoble, does assuage sorrow — but it survives on a different account: the remedy works by reconfiguring the state and resetting the precision the sorrowful model is holding, not by adding a quantity of the opposite sign. The same retirement reaches back and unpicks the contrariety framing the tract has been carrying since Q.35 a.3, where pleasure and sorrow were set as contraries because their objects are; the objects may be contrary while the states are not opposed magnitudes. *Evidence: PRS-35 is **High** — a published N=73 within-subject result. The step from "affect is network configuration" to "the contrariety mechanism is false" is this commentary's inference and not the record's own Solution; what the record establishes is the constructionist premise, and the conclusion follows only if the medieval mechanism is read, as it plainly reads, as quantitative cancellation on one axis.*

**(c) Where evidence is firm vs. speculative.** *Firm*: each remedy's mechanism (vagal stimulation, emotional disclosure, social co-regulation, contemplative attention, parasympathetic restoration) is empirically grounded. *Firm*: PRS-08/09/10 on listening and weak-tie connection (all High). *Medium*: PRS-11 on inflammation-gating — graded Medium in the record on a single IL-6 study, as the Bridges bullet states; listed separately here so this section does not contradict it. *Suggestive but mechanistically robust*: active-inference framing of contemplation as model-widening. *Theological, well-attested*: Wright's lament-to-resurrection tradition; Rohr's contemplative integration. *Speculative metaphysical*: the Kastrup-style reading of Q.39 a.4 as dissociative hardening.

## The central-theme thread

This is the day where the central theme becomes clinical. *Conscious agents are individuated by the limitations of their perspective. Loving unity is the computational task — and the agent works it out in fear and trembling, aware of its limitations.*

Q.38's five remedies are precisely the *cooperative computational resources* by which a limited agent extends its perspective: pleasure restores the body's capacity to receive; tears externalize what the inward model holds rigidly; the sympathy of friends imports another's perspective (PRS-08 listening; PRS-09/10 weak-tie humility); contemplation of truth holds the present sorrow within an unchanging larger frame (Rohr, McGilchrist, Friston); sleep and baths return the somatic substrate to coherence (Levin PRS-02). Fredrickson PRS-11 sharpens why that is social and not merely physical: inflammatory state gates the *radius* of resonance, narrowing it toward the already-familiar, so somatic restoration is a precondition of the perspective-widening the other four remedies perform.

Q.39 a.4's "greatest evil is to be too evil to sorrow" is the central theme's negative pole. The agent who has *collapsed* its perspective so as not to register present evil has shut off the computational route home. Aquinas's claim — that being-evil is worse than feeling-pain — is, in our frame, the claim that *narrowing* the perspective is worse than *suffering* within it. The grief that contains delight (Day 77), the inward pain that exceeds outward (Day 78), the somatic cost of unintegrated sorrow (Day 79) — all of these remain on the road home. The desensitized agent has stepped off it. The remedies of Q.38 are not luxuries; they are the *means* by which an agent under the central-theme conditions actually does the work of salvation, understood in Wright's covenantal sense as inclusion in the people united with God.
