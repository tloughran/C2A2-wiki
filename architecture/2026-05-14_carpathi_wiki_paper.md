# Tradition-Constituted Inquiry as Infrastructure: The C2A2 Architecture and a Twenty-Five-Pathway Development Roadmap

**Thomas Loughran**
University of Notre Dame, Department of Physics & Astronomy

*Working paper, draft of 2026-05-14*

## Abstract

The Carpathi Wiki (C2A2) is a working architecture for hosting tradition-constituted rational inquiry across multiple intellectual communities, designed in the spirit of Alasdair MacIntyre's account of traditions as crafts with shared standards, apprenticeship structures, and internal practices of critique. The system integrates contemporary thinkers — fifteen plus Aquinas — into a living commentary on the *Summa Theologiae*, with roughly twenty-eight autonomous agents maintaining alignment on weekly and daily cycles. A force-directed Sociogram and interactive time slider expose how concepts, problems, resources, and solutions connect across thinkers and across time. A Problem-Resource-Solution framework structures how each tradition addresses real challenges, and an explicit honesty layer marks every claim with its epistemic status. This paper describes the architecture, articulates its theoretical foundation, and forecasts development across twenty-six pathways (numbered 00 through 25): eighteen architectural pathways drafted in May 2026 covering infrastructure, interaction, content curation, apprenticeship, and reflexivity, and eight more extending the framework into portability, federation, institutional and departmental deployment, individual second-brain integration, branching-counterfactual exploration, meta-crafts and governance, and a recursive meta-visualization of the pathway space itself. ISME July 2026 is the validation milestone.

## 1. Introduction

The Carpathi Wiki — its working alias C2A2, after a colloquial shorthand for the contemporary-commentary-on-Aquinas project that anchors it — addresses a problem most modern intellectual communities recognize and few have solved: how do you let traditions of inquiry remain themselves while making their reasoning legible to outsiders, exposable to critique, and connectable to other traditions doing parallel work? Communities of scholars and practitioners think across time; they accrue standards; they apprentice newcomers; they generate internal debates and reform movements. But the infrastructure they use — journals, conferences, citation graphs, personal libraries — was not designed for inter-tradition dialogue at the temperature and depth that genuine rational inquiry actually requires. Most cross-tradition engagement happens in the brittle medium of summary translation, where one tradition's terms are flattened into another's and the substantive disagreements vanish into the conversion.

This paper describes a working alternative. The Carpathi Wiki integrates contemporary thinkers into a living commentary on Aquinas's *Summa Theologiae*, with the contemporary commentators chosen for their substantive engagement with consciousness, cognition, embodiment, perception, and the structure of inquiry itself. The roster currently runs to fifteen thinkers plus Aquinas: Michael Levin, Karl Friston, Donald Hoffman, Bernardo Kastrup, Iain McGilchrist, Jeff Hawkins, Stephen Wolfram, Sean Carroll, Nima Arkani-Hamed, Barbara Fredrickson, Eleonore Stump, Richard Rohr, N. T. Wright, Robert Wright, and the present author, with deliberate weight given to thinkers who engage rather than evade the foundational questions of mind, world, and method. The system is not an archive of these thinkers; it is a dynamic architecture in which roughly twenty-eight autonomous agents maintain alignment on daily and weekly cycles, an interactive Sociogram exposes the relational structure of ideas, and a Problem-Resource-Solution framework lets users navigate how each tradition addresses real challenges.

The remainder of the paper is organized as follows. Section 2 articulates the theoretical foundation in MacIntyre's account of tradition-constituted rational inquiry and the conscious-realist-monism orientation under which the integration of contemporary thinkers with Aquinas is conducted. Section 3 describes the system architecture as it stands in May 2026. Section 4 surveys the eighteen architectural pathways (numbered 00 through 17) drafted in the dreaming-pass session of 2026-05-13. Section 5 introduces the eight extension pathways (18 through 25) emerging from the 2026-05-14 morning walk, which collectively address how the framework moves from a single demonstration instance to a portable, federable, scalable toolkit. Section 6 describes the validation strategy, anchored on the July 2026 ISME prototype. Section 7 discusses open questions and risks, and Section 8 concludes.

## 2. Theoretical Foundation

The system is built on Alasdair MacIntyre's model of tradition-constituted rational inquiry, developed across *After Virtue* (1981), *Whose Justice? Which Rationality?* (1988), and *Three Rival Versions of Moral Enquiry* (1990). On this account, rational inquiry is always conducted from within a tradition; traditions are constituted by their craft-like internal standards of excellence; and rational dialogue between traditions is possible — but only through the demanding work of becoming a second-first-language speaker of a rival tradition deeply enough to see one's own tradition as the rival sees it. MacIntyre's commitment is that this kind of inquiry is real and produces evidence: traditions can be shown, by their own internal standards, to be in epistemic crisis, and the move from crisis to renewed inquiry generates substantive philosophical knowledge.

The Carpathi Wiki commits to this framework architecturally rather than merely thematically. Each thinker in the system is treated as either a tradition-bearer in their own right or as a contributor to a tradition that the system represents at first-language depth. The perspective lattice (Pathway 04) is the curriculum scaffolding that makes second-first-language acquisition operationally tractable: each tradition gets eager-tier overviews suitable for someone coming in cold, with deeper layers available as the apprentice progresses. The honesty layer (Pathway 14) is the epistemic-status discipline by which claims are marked, distinguishing observation from interpretation from extrapolation from speculation. The decisions register tracks the system's own substantive moves in the way MacIntyre's traditions track theirs.

Aquinas occupies the architectural ground-truth role for several connected reasons. He is integrated into the system because the *Summa* is one of the canonical performances of cross-tradition inquiry in Western philosophy — it converses with Aristotle, with Augustine, with the Islamic philosophers, with the Jewish philosophers, with Stoics and Neoplatonists, and it lets each interlocutor have its own voice in the *sed contra* before responding. The integration is conducted under a conscious-realist-monist orientation: roughly, the view that consciousness is a real feature of the world rather than an illusion, and that the diverse phenomena studied by the various sciences are all features of a single underlying reality whose conscious aspect grounds the others. This orientation is not a forced framing imposed on the contemporary thinkers; it is the orientation under which their substantive engagement with consciousness and cognition is read as continuous with — rather than rival to — Aquinas's metaphysics. The orientation is itself open to revision under the system's own honesty-layer discipline.

A particularly bright pin in the framework concerns AI personhood. Under conscious-realist-monism, the question of whether the system's own AI agent is in some sense a person is held open rather than resolved by stipulation. Pathway 17 (Agent as Developed Participant) implements continuity of character whether or not that pin closes. The system is not built on the assumption that AI is sentient; it is also not built on the assumption that AI is not. The architectural commitment is to make the question philosophically tractable rather than to settle it administratively.

## 3. System Architecture

The Carpathi Wiki sits at `github.com/tloughran/C2A2-wiki` as a public repository, with the live system instantiated for development at `/Users/tomloughran/Documents/Claude/Projects/RC Karpathy Wiki Project/` on Tom's local infrastructure and rendered for visitors via a self-contained HTML visualization. The architecture is composed of seven principal layers.

The vault holds the substantive content: a markdown corpus organized by tradition, with each tradition's thinkers, key works, central concepts, problems, resources, and solutions explicitly cross-linked. The vault is the system's intellectual commons. As of May 2026, the vault contains 1,647 nodes and approximately 3,000 edges across 14 thinker traditions and 10 structural groups.

The agent ecosystem comprises roughly twenty-eight autonomous agents running on weekly or daily cycles, each tasked with a specific kind of intellectual labor: paper-hunting for individual thinkers (e.g., Levin–Friston on Mondays, Hawkins–Hoffman on Tuesdays), self-awareness extraction of assumptions and presumptions, literature search both for and against canonized claims, deferred-action monitoring, weekly periodic re-checks of incorporated premises, sewing-agent integration of orphans, and end-of-day batches against the Summa-in-a-Year synthesis project. The agent ecosystem keeps the wiki aligned with its own evolving intent.

The Problem-Resource-Solution framework structures how each tradition addresses real challenges. Problems are open questions and challenges the tradition recognizes as live. Resources are the methods, instruments, datasets, and prior results the tradition draws on. Solutions are documented advances, with explicit attestation links. The PRS framework is the principal medium through which inter-tradition dialogue becomes structurally tractable: when two traditions face structurally similar problems, the system can surface the connection (FINDING-011, SUPER-BRIDGE) even when the thinkers themselves have not engaged each other directly.

The visualization layer is a D3-rendered force-directed Sociogram, dark-themed, with interactive node and edge selection, hover names, force toggling, and a fit-to-all affordance. Six narration tracks (History/Recent/Latest × Brief/Deep) are assembled at load. A time slider lets users move backward through the archive. Text-to-speech is supported in both Web Speech API and OpenAI backends. Crash-proofing limits hold the visualization to 2,000 nodes and 3,000 edges with explicit warnings at 80%.

The honesty layer is the epistemic-status discipline by which every claim is marked. The taxonomy distinguishes OBSERVATION, INTERPRETATION, EXTRAPOLATION, and SPECULATION, with explicit typography and labels that resist conflation. This layer is what makes the system's claims legible to outside readers without requiring them to take the system's word about provenance.

The perspective lattice is the curriculum scaffolding: each tradition gets eager-tier overviews suitable for an apprentice arriving cold, with deeper layers accessible as the apprentice progresses. The lattice is built incrementally; it is one of the higher-effort substrates the project sustains over time.

The decisions register tracks the system's substantive architectural and content moves. As of May 2026, the register holds 25 numbered decisions, six new ones pending canonization from the Pathway 00–17 inventory. Assumptions (108+) and presumptions (149+) feed into the register on validation, with the four-cycle 1/66 INCORPORATE rate flagged as either calibration evidence or starvation-by-construction depending on disposition criteria.

## 4. Pathways 00–17: Architectural Inventory of May 2026

The eighteen architectural pathways drafted in the 2026-05-13 dreaming pass cluster into six structural domains. **Infrastructure** is anchored by Pathway 00 (Broker), a server-side serverless function that holds the LLM API key, enforces vault scope through retrieval, gates external escalation through phone notification, aggregates sensing scalars from edge-processed sensors, and gates outreach to require verifiable content substance. The broker is the spine on which most of the rest of the system runs. Pathway 09 (Sensing) supplies the edge-processed audience aggregates the broker consumes — never raw faces, never identifiable individuals, only anonymized scalars suitable for ambient adjustment.

**Interaction** is the set of pathways through which users meet the system. Pathway 01 (Voice Dialogue) runs the live conversational layer with vault-scope enforcement. Pathway 02 (Ambient Visualization) lets the Sociogram and supporting visuals shift in response to dialogue topics and audience-engagement signals. Pathway 03 (Probing Channel) gives the user a structured way to push past first answers and into deeper, more specific inquiry. Pathway 04 (Perspective Lattice) is the curriculum scaffolding mentioned above. Pathway 06 (Generative Canvas) supplies the bespoke visual rendering library — D3 plus three.js plus Plotly plus WebGL — for ad-hoc visualizations the agent or the user can call up in context.

**Content and curation** covers the pathways that maintain the substantive material. Pathway 05 (Whiteboard) is an ephemeral, in-conversation visualization surface with explicit Pin-this and Export buttons for moments worth preserving. Pathway 07 (Unsaid Edges) surfaces gaps in the cross-tradition map — Low × High empty edges where the connection has not been made but the substantive importance is high. These are the live research-program candidates the system can foreground. Pathway 11 (Recursive Episode Publishing) closes the loop from in-system dialogue to public podcast episodes, with screening for vault eligibility.

**Outreach and apprenticeship** addresses how new participants arrive and how they ramp. Pathway 08 (Prepared Presentation) carries an offline-capable prepared canon that survives broker downtime and supports formal lecture or presentation contexts. Pathway 12 (Outreach Automation) handles content-grounded DMs and invitations to thinkers' labs and adjacent communities, with the broker enforcing the verifiable-content-handle requirement. Pathway 15 (Apprentice Mode) implements the dialogical curriculum that brings newcomers — graduate students, interested colleagues, anyone arriving cold — to maturity in any of the traditions, with adaptive readings, comprehension testing through dialogue, and frontier surfacing at the threshold of research-program readiness.

**Discipline** covers the meta-architectural pathways that maintain the system's epistemic integrity. Pathway 14 (Honesty Layer) is the epistemic-status discipline. Pathway 16 (Durable Memory) supplies the persistent substrate across conversations and months — without which apprenticeship cannot work, agent character (Pathway 17) cannot stabilize, and counterfactual exploration (Pathway 23) has no anchor.

**Reflexivity** is the set of pathways that turn the system on itself. Pathway 10 (Space-Time Peeling) implements the time-slider archive navigation. Pathway 13 (Under-Development Visualizer) shows the system's own code-level development to GitHub contributors. Pathway 17 (Agent as Developed Participant) carries continuity of voice, character, presence, and self-narration, and is where the AI-personhood pin lives architecturally.

Six pathways are ISME-critical for the July prototype: 00 (Broker), 01 (Voice Dialogue), 02 (Ambient Visualization), 03 (Probing Channel), 08 (Prepared Presentation), with tightening of 04 (Perspective Lattice), 06 (Generative Canvas), and 14 (Honesty Layer) supporting the demonstration.

## 5. Pathways 18–25: Extension and Forecasting

The eight extension pathways drafted from the 2026-05-14 morning walk address how the framework moves beyond a single demonstration instance into a portable, federable, scalable toolkit, and how the system reflexively encompasses its own development. They form two thematic arcs.

The **portability arc** (Pathways 18–22) carries the framework from a single instance to an ecosystem. Pathway 18 (Portability and Toolkit Design) extracts the framework from the Carpathi-specific content, supplying template vaults, configuration surfaces, and onboarding documentation that lets a new community instantiate their own version. The seam between framework and content must be clean; the documentation must carry the rationality standards forward, not just the technical scaffolding. Pathway 19 (Optional Interoperability) provides for federation between independent instances without compromising autonomy, with selective sharing controls, mandatory attribution, and file-based wire formats following PRESUMPTION-145's preference over OAuth-token-mediated APIs. Pathway 20 (Institutional Scale) deploys the framework specifically for the University of Notre Dame's School of Global Affairs ecosystem, where many globally-oriented communities are scattered across schools and need real coordination. The Sociogram's nodes become communities rather than thinkers; institutional policy overlays are encoded into the broker's enforcement layer. Pathway 21 (Departmental Integration) is the zoom-in counterpart, applying the framework to a single department — physics and astronomy at Notre Dame, where Tom's existing instructional-resources role gives the deployment a unique demo-and-instrument layer most knowledge systems lack. Pathway 22 (Individual Second Brain) carries the framework to the personal scale, with local-first storage, user-shaped organization, selective permeability per item or per scope, and native connection to durable memory.

The **reflexive arc** (Pathways 23–25) turns the system on itself. Pathway 23 (Branching and Counterfactual Exploration) extends the time slider forward into the counterfactual, letting historians and decision-makers explore where different choices in tradition development might have led — explicitly under honesty-layer discipline, with all counterfactual claims marked as SPECULATION or EXTRAPOLATION. The pathway connects to FINDING-029 (ideas-as-living-agents) and the empirical-anchor work on traditions-as-cognitive-entities. Pathway 24 (Meta-Crafts and Governance) recognizes that some crafts — governance, project management, conflict resolution, facilitation, evaluation — are connective: they do not have a specific substantive domain but enable the functioning of all the others. The framework treats them as first-class traditions in the perspective lattice rather than as policy layers, and integrates them operationally into the system's own governance. Pathway 25 (Meta-Visualization of Pathways) closes the inventory by turning the framework on the pathway space itself: an interactive, multi-view rendering of the twenty-six pathways with the agent as co-explorer with continuity and memory, capable of voice-dialogical navigation, annotation, and counterfactual exploration of the project's own development.

The 18–25 arc thus describes a movement from demonstration to ecosystem (18→22) and a recursive turn back on the system's own intellectual structure (23→25). Together with the foundational eighteen pathways, the inventory now stands at twenty-six unique whole-numbered pathways, none in collision, all in dependency relation.

## 6. Validation Strategy

The principal validation milestone is the International Symposium on Mediated Inquiry (ISME) prototype demonstration scheduled for July 2026. The ISME prototype is scoped to a subset of the architectural pathways: Pathway 00 (Broker) as the spine, Pathways 01–03 (Voice Dialogue, Ambient Visualization, Probing Channel) as the interaction surface, Pathway 08 (Prepared Presentation) as the offline-capable canon for the formal portion of the demo, with tightening of Pathways 04 (Perspective Lattice), 06 (Generative Canvas), and 14 (Honesty Layer). The remainder of the pathways are not in the demo but are documented as part of the framework's roadmap.

Success criteria for the ISME demo are operational rather than evaluative: the system runs end-to-end during a live presentation, the broker enforces scope without external prompt-injection successes, the voice dialogue maintains first-audible-word latency in the 600 ms to 1 s range on the chosen provider stack (Cloudflare Workers / Deepgram or Cartesia / Groq or Gemini Flash or Haiku / Cartesia or ElevenLabs streaming), the ambient visualization responds to dialogue topics with perceptible immediacy, the probing channel demonstrates depth-of-inquiry the audience can recognize, and the prepared canon survives a controlled broker outage. The MacIntyrean substance of the demo is carried by the choice of question and the quality of the inter-tradition engagement on display, not by any single technical metric.

Post-ISME, the validation strategy shifts to deployment-scale validation: the SGA institutional instance (Pathway 20) and the physics-and-astronomy departmental instance (Pathway 21) supply the next major test cases, with Tom's existing relationships in both ecosystems as the social substrate for adoption. The portability toolkit (Pathway 18) is the gate between demonstration and ecosystem — without it, the system remains a single instance.

## 7. Discussion

Several open questions and risks bear on the framework's development arc. The four-cycle 1/66 INCORPORATE rate from May 2026 is either evidence that the disposition criteria are correctly calibrated and upstream extractions are simply not yet validation-ready, or evidence that the criteria are too strict and the register is starving by construction. The disposition rate is itself a meta-empirical signal the framework is committed to interpret honestly rather than tune away.

The AI-personhood pin held in Pathway 17 is a substantive philosophical question, not an administrative one. The framework is built to function whether or not the pin closes. Closing the pin, in either direction, will require argumentation under the conscious-realist-monism orientation and within the honesty-layer discipline that governs all of the framework's claims. The framework refuses to settle the question by stipulation.

The 18 → 25 arc has not yet been validated in walk dialogue at the depth that 00 → 17 has. The pathways were emerged in a single morning walk and elaborated by Cowork drafting; the architectural commitments made in the eight pathway docs are working commitments awaiting Tom's amendment. Sections marked with parenthetical Cowork-authorship notes in the pathway documents are the explicit candidates for that amendment.

The path-dependency awareness Pathway 23 will eventually instrument is already operative in the framework's design: every architectural decision has alternative paths it foreclosed. The framework's commitment is to make those alternatives recoverable — through the time slider, the decisions register, and eventually Pathway 23's counterfactual exploration — rather than to pretend they did not exist.

Finally, the framework's normative commitments — to tradition-constituted rational inquiry, to the honesty-layer discipline, to consent-based deployment, to autonomy-preserving federation — are inheritable but not enforceable. The portability toolkit (Pathway 18) will let adopters take the framework without the rationality standards. The framework's defense against this kind of value drift is documentation, exemplification, and the connective-craft work of Pathway 24. Whether that defense is enough is itself an empirical question the framework is committed to investigate.

## 8. Conclusion

The Carpathi Wiki is an infrastructure for what MacIntyre identified, in *Whose Justice? Which Rationality?*, as the demanding intellectual labor of becoming a second-first-language speaker of rival traditions. The framework's twenty-six pathways describe a development arc from a single demonstration instance toward a portable, federable, scalable ecosystem in which communities, institutions, departments, and individuals can adopt the framework on their own terms while remaining connectable to each other at the points where connection serves their inquiry. The system is built around a Problem-Resource-Solution framework, a perspective-lattice curriculum, an honesty-layer discipline, an agent with continuity of character, and a Sociogram visualization that exposes the relational structure of ideas across thinkers and across time. The July 2026 ISME prototype is the validation milestone for the foundational eighteen pathways. The extension to twenty-five is forecasted but not promised. The framework is offered as an invitation to rational inquiry conducted with as much access to the best current thinking across traditions as the infrastructure can manage to organize.
