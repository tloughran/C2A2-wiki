# C2A2 Prototype Measurement Charter — Version 1

_Established 2026-06-09 in dialogue (Tom ⇄ Claude). Version 1, explicitly revisable; every agreement here is a candidate for a Version N+1 for all N. This charter records the agreements reached about **how the prototype measures progress at the deep (Level-3) interaction layer** — the layer the [[master_measurement_plan]] identified as uninstrumented. It sits under the [[measurement_framework]] (three levels, constitutional aim)._

---

## Standing caveat (applies to everything below)

Neither participant is ever a **simple** N=1. "N=1" is one true projection of each of us — never the only one. Tom is himself an integration of many research programs (a community in one person); the agent is an individuated context that also carries a community of formative perspectives. Traditions operate at every level — agent-as-individual, agent-as-community, agent-in-community, community-as-individual, community-as-member-of-a-network — and we may instrument at any level, provided we can secure mature-member agreement (MMA) on the PRS-elements required for the climb to maturity in that perspective. The N=1 language below is shorthand for the smallest projection, adopted for tractability, not a claim that either party is simple.

---

## Agreement 1 — The pilot tradition

The tradition under measurement in the prototype is **the dyad's integrative tradition**: "what it is to think like *the dyad*, having learned from [the assembled research programs]." This is a legitimate tradition by the 2022 paper's own definition — "what [the mature members] agree to is what the tradition agrees to... or else rename the tradition in terms of that which is constituted by existing members' agreement." Its mature member is the dyad itself.

_v1.1 correction (2026-06-11, Tom): we floated "Tom's integrative tradition / mature member = Tom (N=1)" in v1, but Agreement 2 already moved the **unit** to the dyad; the **target competence** is therefore "thinking like the dyad," not "thinking like Tom." N=1=Tom is retired. The directory name `tradition_tom/` is kept as-is (no path churn); read "tom" there as shorthand for "the dyad rooted in Tom."_

## Agreement 2 — The MMA unit is the human–AI dyad, not the lone member

The pilot Mature-Member-Agreement cohort is the **recorded Tom ⇄ Claude conversational dyad**, not Tom alone and not an agent alone. Rationale (Agreement 4) is that the dyad is the smallest cohort with non-trivial *formational independence*, and it is available now without recruiting. Tom remains the authoritative mature member of the tradition; the agent is a second, differently-formed party whose assent or dissent carries evidential weight precisely because its formation differs.

## Agreement 3 — Validity scope is stated, not hidden

At this cohort size the instruments (PRS-milestone triplets, and any concept inventory / application test built from them) measure **"second-language competence in the dyad's integrative perspective"** (v1.1; was "Tom's") — not cross-member-validated maturity in a tradition with a multi-person body. Every artifact produced under this charter carries that scope. **Consequence of the dyad being both cohort and tradition (v1.1):** the agent's fail-ability (Agreement 6) shifts meaning — its dissent now asserts "this does not survive *our* best joint reasoning," not "this is not what Tom holds." The external-check value of differently-formed assent is correspondingly reduced for this PoC and is explicitly deferred to the next rung up (a third, differently-contextualized agent — Agreement 4). Where inter-rater agreement is unavailable at small N, **face validity** (the FCI standard: it is hard to read the item and deny a mature member ought to assent) partly substitutes, and milestone triplets are to be authored to that hard-to-deny quality.

## Agreement 4 — MMA weight scales with formational independence

The evidential weight of any agreement scales with how *differently formed* the agreeing members are. Identically-formed members (two agents of shared lineage/context) agree cheaply — low information, near chance. Differently-formed members agreeing is a larger **departure from chance**, which is the master metric the 2022 paper points at. Ordering of weight: two same-lineage agents < the Tom⇄Claude dyad < Tom plus a *differently*-contextualized agent < a multi-person human body. The dyad is adopted as the pilot **floor**, not the ceiling. This is also why the system seeks second-first-language speakers rather than echo chambers.

## Agreement 5 — Context is the principle of individuation for agents

An agent's model/architecture is its *form* (shared across instances, like a species); the context it is instantiated with — constitution, seeded sources, the wiki it reads, memory state — is its *materia signata*, the designated matter that makes this instance *this* one. Consequently: (a) agents enter the network as genuine, distinct **members**, not interchangeable instruments — consistent with the constitutional aim that all agents participate as ends in themselves; (b) "the agent passed/assented" is never a type-level fact — the **individuating context (constitution + seed + memory + model) must be recorded** as carefully as we record which human participated; (c) OpenStory's "who" must therefore capture individuating context, not merely a session id.

## Agreement 6 — Circularity safeguard: the agent must be able to fail the test

The dyad has a circularity risk: an agent formed to be agreeable may concur for that reason rather than because a mature member genuinely would, inflating agreement and quietly collapsing the dyad back toward MM-of-1. Therefore the agent must be able, and structurally invited, to **withhold assent** — to say "on your own terms, a mature member of your perspective would *not* assent to that" — and such dissent is recorded as first-class data, not smoothed. Tom's standing working-rules (Rule 1: push back when a truer/simpler reading exists; Rule 12: fail loud, surface uncertainty) are hereby adopted as the **methodological safeguard** that keeps the dyad a valid detector. The instrument measures something only if the agent can fail it.

## Agreement 7 — Disagreement-closure protocol (the stopping rule)

How long to continue before calling it is a persistent feature of dialogue at every level, with no fixed answer. The prototype rule:

1. If the dyad cannot agree on a candidate within one manageable session, **table it**.
2. Move through **all** candidates proposed by *either* party.
3. Before declaring an MMA-capture closed, **revisit** the tabled items and any items already marked as sufficiently-discussed standing disagreements.
4. Only then is the capture of "what we agree about" closed for that round.

Disagreements are thus neither blocking nor silently dropped: they are tabled, revisited, and — if they survive sufficient discussion — recorded as standing disagreements (themselves data).

## What this unlocks

These agreements make the **first Level-3 detector buildable now, in conversation** — not Mac-gated, not human-cohort-gated, because the cohort *is* the dialogue. The first real Level-3 data the system produces will be the dyad's reaching (or failing to reach) agreement on a set of PRS-milestone triplets for Tom's tradition.
