# "What's it doing?" — 15 function clauses (draft 1, 2026-08-05)

One added line per existing card in `what_is_c2a2.html`. No card text rewritten.
Pattern: **the structure is already the card's title; this is what that structure buys.**
Test applied to each: *does this name a capability nothing else on the list would buy?*
Where two clauses came close, I pushed them apart rather than letting both blur.

| # | Structure (existing title) | …so it can |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fulfillment of MacIntyre's Vision | **adjudicate between rival paradigms without a neutral referee** — disagreement becomes productive rather than terminal. |
| 2 | Accelerator / Detector System | **instrument the collision** — traditions are brought to contact energy *and* the encounter is measured, producing evidence rather than opinion about it. |
| 3 | High-Information-Flow Network of Rich Communities | **move a discovery from one thick community into another without stripping the context that made it a discovery.** |
| 4 | Semantic Commons | **hold Friston's vocabulary and Stump's in one space without translating either into the other** — comparison without conquest. |
| 5 | Social Information-Processing Motherboard | **make the topology an editable parameter** — which tradition can reach which, at what bandwidth, is designed rather than inherited from who happens to know whom. |
| 6 | Bioelectric Network | **remember what each tradition is trying to become, and propagate that goal across parts that never talk to each other directly.** |
| 7 | Pixie Dust | **spread without permission** — it moves by being picked up and recombined, so it needs no mandate, budget line, or institutional host to keep going. |
| 8 | Memory Prosthetic for Culture | **carry tacit craft across a generational gap** — what mature members know but cannot state outlives the people who held it. |
| 9 | University (before its corruption) | **admit a newcomer as an apprentice rather than a customer** — a reader can be *changed* by the encounter, not merely informed by it. |
| 10 | Courtroom — or better, Agon | **stage a contest everyone leaves richer** — claims get tested and located without anyone having to lose for the exchange to have been worth it. |
| 11 | The Explorer as Community | **enrol you by being used** — there is no outside vantage, so looking is already joining, and the tradition grows by being examined. |
| 12 | Intelligent Agent | **act between your visits** — it pursues its goals overnight, so the system you come back to has moved without you. |
| 13 | Mind | **say things none of its parts said** — the whole generates contributions not ascribable to any agent inside it. |
| 14 | Brain | **hold many incompatible models at once and still act coherently** — no column has to be overruled for the system to reach judgment. |
| 15 | Living Entity | **be measured for life** — metabolic keying turns "is it alive?" into an empirical question with an intake and a yield you can read off. |
| … | *the sixteenth* | *— yours.* |

## Notes on the draft

- **1, 9 and 10 crowd each other.** All three are about answerability under
  conditions of real disagreement. I split them by *what the answerability is
  for*: #1 adjudication (an epistemic result), #9 formation (a change in the
  person), #10 location (finding where you actually stand). If that reads thin,
  the cleanest fix is to let #1 be the roundtable card instead — which is where
  yesterday's other thread wants to go anyway.
- **12, 13, 14 are a ladder,** and I've kept them one: acts → exceeds its parts →
  stays coherent while internally plural. Read in order they earn each other. If
  the page ever gets reordered, keep these three adjacent.
- **7 is the odd one and should stay odd.** Every other clause is a capability of
  the system; #7 is a capability of the system's *spread*. That asymmetry is the
  honest thing about it, per the card's own text.
- **15 ends on a question in the source card,** and I've made its clause a
  promise of measurability rather than a claim of life. Deliberate — overclaiming
  there would undercut the whole page.

Ready to be cut, swapped, or overruled. Nothing is committed to `wiki/`.
