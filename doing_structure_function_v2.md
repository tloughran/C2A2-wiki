# "What's it doing?" — structure/function pass 2 (2026-08-05)

**Tom's test, applied to every card:** *structure = agents in arrangement; function = their
goal-directed behavior.* Anything called a structure must clearly BE one. Radical rephrasing allowed.

This supersedes draft 1, where the titles were left verbatim and only a clause was added. Draft 1's
premise — "the existing 15 framings already ARE structure claims" — is **wrong for at least six of
them**, and card 1 is the clearest failure: *fulfillment of a vision* is a relation between the
project and a thinker's programme, not an arrangement of agents.

Verdict column: **PASS** = already names agents in arrangement, keep the title.
**FAIL** = names a relation, a substance, a device, or an effect. Retitle.

## Consequences of retitling — read before approving

1. **Card 1's body survives the change, and gains from it.** It is about the Gifford Lectures, i.e.
   about *where this structure came from*. Demoting MacIntyre from the title to the provenance is
   the same move already agreed for the roundtable: first among equals, not sole author.
   The Pathway 20 link is already removed (done this session).
2. **Card 7 loses the joke unless it's kept in the body.** "Pixie dust" is the most playful card on
   the page and the body says so. Recommendation: new structural title, phrase preserved in the
   first line of the body. Flag if you want it in the title instead.
3. **Card 8's retitle breaks a cross-reference.** `what_is_saying.html` card 4 names
   *"the existing framing #8, Memory Prosthetic for Culture"* in its note, and distinguishes itself
   from it. That note needs the new wording, or the argument goes dark.
4. **Cards 12-13-14 are a deliberate ladder** (acts → exceeds its parts → stays coherent while
   internally plural). Retitling 12 to "a standing crew" keeps the rungs legible; retitling 13 or 14
   would not. That's why they're left alone.
5. **Six retitles mean six body rewrites, not just six headings.** Each body opens by explaining its
   title. I have NOT touched any body text — that is the next decision, not this one.
6. **§1's Doing door lists examples**: *"a wiki, a mind, a brain, a community, a courtroom, an
   accelerator/detector."* All six survive this pass. No patch needed there.
